# Form 982 — Reduction of Tax Attributes Due to Discharge of Indebtedness (and Section 1082 Basis Adjustment)

**Status:** Phase 1 (Part I reporting) built 2026-09-17. Phase 2 (Part II attribute reduction) specified
here, not built — see §9.

*Authorities: IRC §108(a) (exclusions), §108(a)(2)(C) (QPRI/insolvency ordering), §108(a)(3) (insolvency
cap), §108(b) (attribute reduction), §108(b)(3)(B) (33⅓¢ credit rate), §108(b)(4)(A) (reductions made
AFTER the year's tax), §108(b)(5) (basis-first election), §108(d)(3) (insolvency defined), §108(h) (QPRI),
§1017 (basis reduction); Form 982 and its instructions; Pub. 4681.*

---

## 1. Line identity

Form 982 is a **filed attachment**, not a retained worksheet. Its instructions are explicit: *"File Form
982 with your federal income tax return for a year a discharge of indebtedness is excluded from your
income."* A return that excludes COD income without it claims the exclusion unsubstantiated.

| Part | Lines | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| I | 1a–1e, 2, 3 | Which exclusion, how much, and the §1221(a)(1) election |
| II | 4–13 | Reduction of tax attributes — the price of the exclusion |
| III | — | Corporate consent under §1082(a)(2); unreachable from a Form 1040 |

Semantic assets: `pdfs/f982_semantic_labels.pdf`, `pdfs/f982_field_map_semantic.csv` (27 fields, one
page), generated by `us-tax-be/scripts/generate-semantic-982.js`.

## 2. Formula

```
insolvency            = max(0, total_liabilities_before − fmv_assets_before)          [§108(d)(3)]
qpri_exclusion        = min(qpri_elected_amount, gross_cod)                            [§108(a)(1)(E)]
remaining_cod         = gross_cod − qpri_exclusion
insolvency_exclusion  = min(insolvency, remaining_cod)                                 [§108(a)(3)]

line2                 = qpri_exclusion + insolvency_exclusion
schedule1_line8c      = max(0, gross_cod − line2)
```

QPRI is applied **first** and insolvency to the remainder, which is the statutory default: §108(a)(2)(C)
disapplies QPRI to the extent of insolvency *only if the taxpayer elects* insolvency treatment.

## 4. Decision tree

```
gross COD > 0 ?
├─ no  → no Form 982
└─ yes → any exclusion elected?
         ├─ no  → whole COD taxable on Schedule 1 line 8c; no Form 982
         └─ yes → compute line 2
                  ├─ line 2 = 0 → no Form 982 (election made but nothing excluded, e.g. solvent)
                  └─ line 2 > 0 → PRODUCE Form 982
                                  Part I: 1b / 1e checked, line 2 = excluded amount
                                  Part II: reduce attributes (Phase 2)
```

The gate is **line 2 > 0, not Schedule 1 line 8c > 0**. A fully-excluded discharge nets line 8c to zero,
Schedule 1 disappears entirely, and Form 982 becomes the *only* evidence the discharge happened.

## 5. Part II — attribute reduction (Phase 2)

§108(b)(2) order, consuming the excluded amount until exhausted. Dollar-for-dollar except the three
credit attributes, which absorb **33⅓ cents per dollar excluded** — so $30,000 of exclusion consumes
$90,000 of credit carryover.

| Line | Attribute | Rate | Persisted source in this app |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | Net operating loss | $1.00 | `out_form_172.nol_carryforward_to_next_year` |
| 7 | General business credit carryover | 33⅓¢ | `out_schedule_1.add_inc_gbc_carryforward` |
| 8 | Minimum tax credit | 33⅓¢ | **none persisted** — skip |
| 9 | Net capital loss + carryover | $1.00 | `out_schedule_d.next_year_{short,long}_term_capital_loss_carryover` |
| 10a | Basis of property | $1.00 | **advisory only** — §1017 reaches depreciation assets |
| 10b | Basis of principal residence | $1.00 | `codHomeBasisBeforeReduction` (QPRI, §108(h)(1)) |
| 11a–c | Farm basis | $1.00 | out of scope with qualified farm indebtedness |
| 12 | Passive activity loss/credit carryovers | $1.00 | `add_inc_rental_passive_loss_carryforward` (+ AMT twin) |
| 13 | Foreign tax credit carryover | 33⅓¢ | `out_form_1116.carryforward_to_next_years` |

**Ordering exceptions:** line 1d (QRPBI) and the §108(b)(5) election both reduce basis *first*.

### ★ Why this fits the bridge architecture exactly

**§108(b)(4)(A): the reductions are made *after* determining the tax for the year of discharge.** They
therefore do not touch the current return at all — they reduce the carryforwards travelling *out* to next
year. Five of the fourteen existing cross-year bridges hold precisely the attributes §108(b) names, so
the implementation is: **reduce the value at the point it is persisted, and next year's import reads the
reduced figure with no further change.**

## 6. Compute order

1. `computeOtherIncomes` → gross COD, `computeCodSection108Exclusion` → line 2, Schedule 1 line 8c.
2. *(Phase 2)* after the year's tax is final, `computeSection108AttributeReduction(line2, …)` walks
   lines 6→13 and reduces each persisted carryforward before it is written.
3. `computeForm982(...)` near the end of `prepare()`, where `personalForms` and `header` are in scope.

## 8. Validations

- Line 2 never exceeds gross COD.
- Insolvency floors at zero (assets ≥ liabilities ⇒ no exclusion).
- Exclusion is **opt-in**: without an election the whole discharge is ordinary income under §61(a)(11).
- *(Phase 2)* the sum of lines 6–13 must equal line 2 unless attributes are exhausted first; any
  unabsorbed remainder is simply forgiven and must not be carried anywhere.

## 9. Out of scope / deferred

- **Part III** (§1082(a)(2) corporate consent) — not reachable from a 1040; rendered but never populated.
- **Line 8, minimum tax credit** — no MTC carryforward is persisted anywhere in the app, so there is
  nothing to reduce. Skipping is correct; inventing storage for it would not be.
- **Line 10a, general property basis** — §1017 reaches the depreciation-asset subsystem. Deferred to an
  advisory naming the required reduction, mirroring how the §931/§933 FTC reduction is handled.
- **Lines 11a–c, farm basis** — only with qualified farm indebtedness (line 1c), itself not captured.
