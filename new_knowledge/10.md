# Form 1040 line 10 — how the app does it

IRS rules: `C:\us-tax\new_lines\10.md`. Flowchart: `C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\10.md`.
Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`.

---

## 1. Status

Line 10 is computed: `computeIncomeAdjustments` (`TRCS:32742-33302`) builds every Schedule 1 Part II line from the two
"Income adjustments" personal forms (taxpayer / spouse), the Form 8889 and Form 8853 computations, Schedule SE and the
self-employed deductions form, and three statement types (1099-INT, 1099-OID, 1098-E). Line 26 is copied to Form 1040
line 10 (`buildAdjustments`, `TRCS:18833-18874`). Caps and gates exist for lines 11, 12, 13, 14, 19a, 21, 24b, 24c, 24h,
24i; the IRA deduction (line 20) is **typed by the user** and then checked — phase-out applied after AGI is known
(`TRCS:4483-4703`), compensation limit raised as a blocking flag (`TRCS:4720-4770`). The student-loan MAGI phase-out,
the Olympic AGI ceiling and the performing-artist $16,000 test run on a second compute pass.

**16 differences from the IRS rules** (D-10-1 … D-10-16). The most serious: Forms 1098-E are summed for the whole
household with no person filter, so another person's 1098-E puts a **non-overrideable** student-loan blocker on an MFS
leg or on a dependent's own return (D-10-2); Schedule 1 line 18 does the same with 1099-INT / 1099-OID penalties (wrong
amount, D-10-1); a typed student-loan amount and an uploaded 1098-E are added together (D-10-3); line 26 / AGI use the
full half-SE-tax deduction while line 15 shows the territory-prorated one (D-10-4); K-1 (Form 1041) box 11 code A is
never read although the form says it is imported (D-10-5); the typed IRA deduction is never compared with the
contributions entered (D-10-6).

## 4. How it is computed

Methods:

- Pre-processing in `prepare`: Form 3903 code P `withForm3903CodeP` (`TRCS:3585-3588`, `:10367`); line 12 eligibility
  `withLine12Eligibility` (`TRCS:3590-3594`, `:10298-10357`); Form 8889 injection `injectHsaDeduction` (`TRCS:2930-2937`,
  `:10249`); Form 8853 injection `injectMsaDeduction` (`TRCS:2969-2976`, `:10703`); TCJA-adopted alimony removed
  `withoutTcjaAdoptedAlimony` (`TRCS:4335-4338`, `:53587`); Schedule SE / self-employed deductions (`TRCS:4232`,
  `computeSeDeductions` `:29722`); territory SE fraction (`TRCS:4411-4418`, `:35716`).
- Main: `computeIncomeAdjustments` `TRCS:32742-33302`, called `TRCS:4419-4436`.
- IRA phase-out and compensation cap: `TRCS:4483-4770`; `runPub590aCoordination` `:35513`; `applyPub590aIraPhaseout`
  `:35245`; `computeIraCompensation` `:41140`.
- Second passes: Olympic ceiling `TRCS:7252-7279`; student loan phase-out `TRCS:7281-7315`,
  `computeStudentLoanInterestPhaseout` `:42412`; performing artist `TRCS:7533-7547`.
- Line 10 / AGI: `buildAdjustments` `TRCS:18833-18874`; Schedule 1 output `applySchedule1Adjustments` `TRCS:38125`.

On an MFS return the spouse's income-adjustments and standard-deductions forms are set to null inside the method
(`TRCS:32782-32786`), so every spouse amount below drops out. **Statement lists are not filtered by person** on any
filing status (`TRCS:3114`, `:3250`, `:3257` load them for the whole account; `withCommunityPropertySplit` returns them
unchanged unless MFS + community property, `CommunityProperty.java:37-48`).

Steps, mirroring `new_lines/10.md` section 6:

1. **Line 11.** Taxpayer and spouse `educatorExpensesLine11` each capped at $300 (`clampToEducatorCap`,
   `TRCS:33464-33480`; `ReferenceData.EDUCATOR_EXPENSES_CAP_PER_PERSON`), with advisory
   `SCHEDULE1_LINE11_EDUCATOR_EXPENSES_CAPPED_<side>`; summed (`TRCS:32849-32853`). No eligibility question (K-12,
   900 hours) and no reductions asked (D-10-12).
2. **Line 12.** `form2106Line10` per person (`TRCS:52379-52409`): lines 1–4 less non-meal reimbursement, plus meals less
   meal reimbursement × 50% / 80%, rounded. Earlier, `withLine12Eligibility` removes the amounts when the group's test
   fails (advisory `SCHEDULE1_LINE12_NOT_ELIGIBLE_<side>`) or blocks when a test answer is missing
   (`SCHEDULE1_LINE12_ELIGIBILITY_ANSWER_REQUIRED_<side>`); performing-artist AGI test on pass 2. The reservist
   per-diem / standard-mileage limit is not checked (left to the Form 2106 file).
3. **Line 13.** Form 8889 line 13, when computed, overwrites the typed amount (`TRCS:10249-10255`); a typed amount
   without Form 8889 is capped at $9,550 (`cappedHsaDeductionEntry`, `TRCS:10264-10280`, advisory
   `SCHEDULE1_LINE13_HSA_DEDUCTION_EXCEEDS_MAX_<side>`).
4. **Line 14.** `gatedMovingExpense` (`TRCS:10410-10440`): no active-duty Yes → amount dropped + blocking
   `SCHEDULE1_LINE14_MOVING_EXPENSES_NOT_ACTIVE_DUTY_MILITARY_<side>`; with W-2 code P, line 5 = costs − code P (advisory
   `FORM_3903_CODE_P_SUBTRACTED_<side>`), excess to line 1h (`TRCS:40206`). The storage-only answer is saved but unused;
   the line 14 box is never checked (D-10-14).
5. **Line 15.** `scheduleSE.halfSeTaxDeduction` (else legacy typed field) (`TRCS:32872-32874`). The output line 15 is
   prorated for an American Samoa / Puerto Rico excluder (`TRCS:33264-33265`), but **line 26 adds the unprorated value**
   (`TRCS:33217`) (D-10-4).
6. **Lines 16 / 17.** `seDeductions.line16Total` / `line17Total` (`TRCS:32878-32883`): per person, earned-income ceiling
   = net SE profit − trade share of ½ SE tax; line 16 bounded by plan limits and $70,000; line 17 = premiums (none for
   12 employer-plan months; LTC age limits) ≤ ceiling − line 16; Pub. 974 Marketplace coordination with advisories
   `SE_HEALTH_MARKETPLACE_COORDINATION_APPLIED` / `_ADVISORY` (`TRCS:29722-29830`, `:32829-32841`). No Schedule C / F
   → nothing (`TRCS:29726-29728`); a more-than-2% S-corporation shareholder has no path (D-10-9).
7. **Line 18.** Typed (taxpayer + spouse) vs. automatic = the two never-written `imported…` keys + **the sum of
   `earlyWithdrawalPenaltyAmount` over every 1099-INT and 1099-OID in the account** (`TRCS:32885-32890`); when both are
   non-zero the larger is used with advisory `SCHEDULE1_LINE18_MANUAL_VS_IMPORTED_MISMATCH`, else they are added
   (`TRCS:32900-32913`). No person filter (D-10-1).
8. **Lines 19a/b/c.** Per side; an agreement dated after 2018-12-31 zeroes that side with a blocking flag
   (`TRCS:32922-32946`); a TCJA-adopting modification removes it earlier (`TRCS:4335-4338`). 19b/19c come from the
   larger payer; both paying → advisory `SCHEDULE1_LINE19_OTHER_RECIPIENT_STATEMENT` (`TRCS:32949-32970`). A blank
   date is treated as pre-2019 (no flag); the UI marks 19b/19c required.
9. **Line 20.** `iraDeductionLine20` taxpayer + spouse as typed (`TRCS:32971`). Then in `prepare`:
   - Phase-out runs when a deduction is claimed and either person is covered (form answer or W-2 box 13)
     (`TRCS:4483-4510`). MAGI = rebuilt line 9 − (line 26 − line 20) + line 21 + Form 2555 exclusions + Form 8815 +
     Form 8839 line 30 (`TRCS:4517-4586`, `:35576-35590`) — the worksheet's line 9 − (11…19a, 23, 25), plus Pub. 590-A
     add-backs **except the Form 2555 line 50 housing deduction** (D-10-7). With social security benefits the
     Pub. 590-A loop recomputes 6b (`runPub590aCoordination`, `TRCS:35513-35620`; D-6b-2 for the MFS base flag).
   - Ranges and rounding (`TRCS:35181-35191`, `:35262-35310`): $79,000–$89,000; MFJ / QSS covered $126,000–$146,000;
     not-covered MFJ spouse $236,000–$246,000; MFS lived together $0–$10,000; allowed = limit × (top − MAGI) ÷ range,
     rounded up to $10, $200 minimum, ≤ claimed. A reduction rewrites line 20, line 26 and the Social Security subset,
     with advisory `SOCIAL_SECURITY_IRA_COORDINATION_DEDUCTION_REDUCED_BY_PHASEOUT` (`TRCS:4602-4688`).
   - Compensation limit (`TRCS:4720-4770`): per person W-2 box 1 (by SSN) + code Q combat pay + pre-2019 alimony + (net
     SE earnings − ½ SE); MFJ uses combined compensation less the other spouse's claim; over the limit → blocking,
     overrideable `IRA_DEDUCTION_EXCEEDS_COMPENSATION_<side>`; the amount is not reduced. Compensation misses lines 1b,
     1g and Form 4852 wages (D-1b-6, D-1g-2, D-1z-1), counts W-2 box 11 NQDC pay and does not subtract line 16
     (D-10-8).
   - The typed deduction is never compared with `traditionalIraContributionAmount` (D-10-6).
10. **Line 21.** Typed (taxpayer + spouse) **plus** the sum of `studentLoanInterestReceivedAmount` over **every 1098-E in
    the account** (`TRCS:32972-32974`) (D-10-3, D-10-2). If > 0: MFS → 0 + non-overrideable
    `SCHEDULE1_LINE21_STUDENT_LOAN_INTEREST_MFS_DISALLOWED`; claimed as a dependent (`someoneCanClaim…` Yes and
    `someoneIsClaiming…` not No, `TRCS:25938-25940`) → 0 + non-overrideable
    `SCHEDULE1_LINE21_STUDENT_LOAN_INTEREST_DEPENDENT_DISALLOWED`; else capped at $2,500 (`TRCS:32994-33024`). Pass 2:
    MAGI = AGI + line 21 + Form 2555 exclusions + Form 4563 + Puerto Rico; phase-out $85,000–$100,000 / $170,000–$200,000
    (QSS with single), ratio rounded to 3 places (`TRCS:7290-7315`, `:42412-42430`).
11. **Line 22.** Always null (`TRCS:33032`).
12. **Line 23.** Typed amount, else Form 8853 line 5 (put-if-absent, `TRCS:10703-10711`); advisory
    `SCHEDULE1_LINE23_ARCHER_MSA_DEDUCTION_LIMIT_ADVISORY` whenever non-zero (`TRCS:33033-33042`) (D-10-10).
13. **Lines 24a–24k.** Typed per side and added (`TRCS:33044-33148`). 24b capped at line 8l (advisory
    `SCHEDULE1_LINE24B_EXPENSES_EXCEED_8L_INCOME`). 24c dropped to 0 on pass 2 when AGI + 24c > $1,000,000 / $500,000
    MFS (`TRCS:33059-33075`, `:7259-7279`); not capped at line 8m (D-10-13). 24e = typed Trade Act repayment (the
    unemployment parameter is always null, `TRCS:4407`). 24h / 24i capped at the income basis (advisories) and
    **blocked, non-overrideable**, when the basis is blank. 24j typed only — Form 2555 Part IX is not computed
    (D-10-16). 24k typed + never-written import key (D-10-5).
14. **Line 24z.** Every non-blank description / non-zero amount pair, labelled "Taxpayer:" / "Spouse:"
    (`TRCS:33378-33412`) — no restriction (D-10-11).
15. **Line 25** = 24a … 24k + 24z (`TRCS:33193`); **line 26** = lines 11 … 23 + line 25 (`TRCS:33217`); Social Security
    subset = 11 … 20, 23, 25 (`TRCS:33229-33231`). If nothing is non-zero the whole result is null (`TRCS:33233-33257`).
16. **Rounding.** Each output line is `roundMoney` (half-up) of its own unrounded value, and line 26 is the rounded sum of
    the unrounded values (`TRCS:33260-33288`), so line 26 can differ by $1 from the sum of the printed lines when
    entries carry cents (cosmetic; not listed as a deviation).
17. **Form 1040 line 10** = round(line 26); line 11a = line 9 − line 10, negative allowed (`TRCS:18849-18864`).

## 8. Compared with the IRS rules

| # | IRS rule (source) | What the app does | Result |
|---|---|---|---|

## 9. Limits and out of scope

- Lines 15–17 detail (Schedule SE, Pub. 560 plan limits, Form 7206) belongs to their forms files; only the line-level
  routing is covered here.
- Form 2106, 8889, 3903, 8853, 2555 detail likewise.
- The IRA deduction is a typed figure by design (the app checks it rather than computing the worksheet's line 12);
  Form 8606 nondeductible contributions are separate inputs.
- The "imported…" keys and the statement-upload gating flags are dead code (section 3, 5).
- Line 10 is null when no adjustment is non-zero (`TRCS:33249-33257`; `knowledge/canonical-null-zero-semantic.md`).
