# Form 1040 line 11a / 11b — how the app does it

IRS rules: `C:\us-tax\new_lines\11a.md`. Flowchart: `C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\11a.md`.
Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`.

---

## 1. Status

Line 11a is computed once as line 9 − line 10, rounded, with no floor (negative AGI is kept), and line 11b is the same
value. A legacy third field (`adjustedGrossIncome`) holds the same number. AGI is then **patched in place twice**:
up by the Form 8814 child income (`TRCS:5122-5131`) and down by the Form 4972 lump sum (`TRCS:6075-6085`). Neither
patch refigures what was already built from AGI, and the Form 4972 patch floors AGI at zero.

There is no shared "MAGI" field. The app builds **about 25 separate AGI / MAGI figures** (section 4.3). Most follow
their IRS definitions; the problems are (a) **when** they run relative to the two patches and the adoption phase-2
change, and (b) a few formulas that rebuild AGI from pieces or miss an add-back.

**Differences recorded here** (D-11a-1 … D-11a-8), plus references to D-9-2, D-9-3, D-5b-11, D-1f-1, D-1f-2,
D-1z-1, D-1z-2, D-1z-4 and D-1a-9 where the root cause is already recorded. The most serious: the §469 rental MAGI is
rebuilt from five income lines and leaves out business income, Schedule 1 income and the adjustments (D-11a-3), and
AGI-based deductions are figured before the Form 8814 amount is in AGI (D-9-3). All silent.

## 2. Where the user enters it

Nothing is entered for lines 11a / 11b. Line 9 comes from `new_knowledge\9.md`; line 10 is Schedule 1 line 26
(10 file). Inputs that only shape a MAGI (Puerto Rico excluded income, Form 4563, Form 2555, typed MAGI overrides on
Forms 8815, 8828, 8839, 8936, 8962) are documented with those forms.

## 3. Where it is stored

No input storage. Output only (section 6).

## 4. How it is computed

### 4.1 The base computation

Method: `buildAdjustments` `TRCS:18833`, called from `buildForm1040` (`TRCS:13626`), which `prepareReturn` calls at
`TRCS:4839`.

1. **Line 10.** `roundMoney(incomeAdjustments.line10FromSchedule1Line26())` (`TRCS:18849`). Before this, the Pub.
   590-A IRA coordinator may already have lowered the IRA deduction and line 26 (`TRCS:4609-4623`), so line 10 is
   consistent with the IRA phase-out.
2. **Line 9.** `roundMoney(income.getTotalIncome())` (`TRCS:18850`).
3. **Line 11a.** `line9 == null ? null : roundMoney(subtractNonNegativeAllowNegative(line9, line10))` (`TRCS:18860`;
   helper `TRCS:26505-26510`): a null line 10 counts as 0; the result keeps its sign. If line 9 is null, AGI is null
   even when line 10 has an amount (D-11a-1).
4. **Line 11b.** The same `BigDecimal` object (`TRCS:18862`).
5. **Store.** If line 10 and line 11a are both null, no `Adjustments` object is created (`TRCS:18863-18865`);
   otherwise `adjustmentsSchedule1`, `line11aAdjustedGrossIncome`, `line11bAmountFromLine11aAdjustedGrossIncome`
   and the legacy `adjustedGrossIncome` are set (`TRCS:18866-18871`).

The Form 8814 patch (`TRCS:5122-5131`) only runs when an `Adjustments` object already exists and AGI is not null; when
the parent had no other income, line 9 becomes the Form 8814 amount but AGI stays blank (D-11a-2). The Form 4972
patch reads `line11a` (falling back to the legacy field), subtracts with a floor at zero and writes all three AGI
fields (`TRCS:6077-6085`). The re-run triggers recompute the whole return, so the figures they guard end up
consistent; nothing else is refigured after (b), (c) or (d).

None on lines 11a / 11b. MAGI-specific flags (for example `FORM_8815_MAGI_ABOVE_CEILING`,
`ADOPTION_MAGI_MANUAL_VS_AUTO_MISMATCH`) belong to their forms.

## 9. Limits and out of scope

- The legacy `adjustedGrossIncome` field duplicates line 11a; some readers use it (Form 8960, performing artist,
  sales-tax table, Form 4684 output). Same value, so not a deviation.
- AGI is null when there is no income by design (`knowledge/canonical-null-zero-semantic.md`), which is what D-11a-1
  disputes only for the case where line 10 has an amount.
- Form 8962 line 2b (dependents' MAGI) and the Form 8936 add-backs / prior-year MAGI are typed by the user.
- The NOL, Form 8828, Form 2210 and Pub. 974 figures (§4.3 #21, 22, 26, 28) are left to their own files.
