# Form 1040 line 1c — how the app does it

IRS rules: `C:\us-tax\new_lines\1c.md`. Flowchart: `C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\1c.md`.
Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`.

---

## 1. Status

Line 1c is computed, per person, from the **Tips** form (one row per employer: tips received, tips reported,
W-2 box 8 allocated tips, an adequate-records claim, tips under $20 a month, noncash tips). For each employer the
unreported amount is tips received − tips reported, replaced by W-2 box 8 when box 8 is larger, or by the
substantiated amount when the person claims adequate records showing less than box 8. Line 1c = the sum of those
amounts (Form 4137 line 4) + noncash tips, taxpayer plus spouse (spouse left out on married filing separately),
rounded once. If the Tips form has no rows and was not answered "No", the person's W-2s with box 8 are turned into
rows automatically. W-2 box 8 is read only for W-2s whose box a matches the person's SSN, so a dependent's box 8 does
**not** reach the parent's line 1c (the line 1a root cause D-1a-1 does not repeat here).

**5 differences from the IRS rules** (D-1c-1 … D-1c-5). The most serious: W-2 box 8 allocated tips are dropped
from line 1c with no flag when the person answers "No" to the Tips screening question, removes the W-2's row, or
adds the W-2 after the Tips form was saved with other rows (D-1c-1).

## 4. How it is computed

Methods:

- Load: `TRCS:2695-2696` (`personalForms.get("tip-income-taxpayer" / "-spouse")`); MFS test `TRCS:3548-3549`;
  call `computeTips(...)` `TRCS:3561`.
- `computeTips` `TRCS:42555-42578` — taxpayer, then spouse unless MFS; adds the two.
- `computeTipsForPerson` `TRCS:42580-42792` — per person: rows, line 1c, Form 4137, flags.
- `buildTipEntriesFromW2` `TRCS:52320-52354` — W-2 box 8 auto-rows.
- Assembly: `buildIncome` rounding `TRCS:18437`, output `TRCS:18584-18586`, line 1z `TRCS:18487-18488`.
- Return scoping: MFS spouse leg renames `-spouse` forms to `-taxpayer` and swaps identities
  (`us-tax-be/.../multireturn/MfsFormScoper.java:678-679`); dependent's own return builds only identity / filing
  status / standard-deduction forms (`us-tax-be/.../multireturn/DependentOwnFormScoper.java:148-215`), so it has no
  Tips form.

Steps, mirroring `new_lines/1c.md` section 6:

1. **Whose tips.** The taxpayer's Tips form, plus the spouse's unless the return is MFS (`TRCS:42570-42572`). On the
   MFS spouse leg the spouse's form becomes the filer's form. W-2 box 8 is attributed only through box a = the
   person's SSN (`TRCS:52329-52340`); a W-2 without box a, or a person without an SSN, gives no auto-row.
2. **Screening.** If `hasUnreportedTips` is `false`, the person contributes nothing — no rows, no W-2 box 8, no
   Form 4137 (`TRCS:42592-42595`). Nothing checks whether the person has a W-2 with box 8 (D-1c-1).
3. **Rows.** The saved `tipsByEmployer` rows are used. Only if there are **none** does the backend build rows from
   the person's W-2s with box 8 > 0 (`TRCS:42596-42599`). A saved row list that misses a W-2 with box 8 (row removed,
   W-2 added later) is not completed, and a saved row keeps the box 8 value copied when the form was last opened
   (D-1c-1).
4. **Per employer — unreported cash and charge tips** = `totalTipsReceived − totalTipsReportedToEmployer`, floored
   at 0; null when received is blank (`TRCS:42622-42627`). Tips under $20 a month are expected to be inside
   received − reported; they are not subtracted here.
5. **Per employer — allocated tips** (`TRCS:42629-42651`), only when box 8 > 0:
   - adequate records = Yes and substantiated < box 8 → the substantiated amount;
   - otherwise → the larger of (received − reported) and box 8.
   The substantiated amount is ignored when it is not below box 8, and received − reported is ignored when the
   substantiated amount is used (D-1c-2). Adequate records = Yes with a blank substantiated amount raises a blocking
   flag (§5).
6. **Form 4137 line 4** = Σ per-employer amounts (`TRCS:42655`, `:42670`). Form 4137 line 2 and line 3 are Σ
   received and Σ reported (`TRCS:42653-42654`), so when box 8 is used line 4 ≠ line 2 − line 3 on the printed form
   (D-1c-3).
7. **Noncash tips** — Σ `nonCashTipsFmv` (`TRCS:42658`); line 1c for the person = line 4 + noncash
   (`TRCS:42688`). Noncash tips do not create a Form 4137 (`TRCS:42752-42755`: Form 4137 only when line 4 > 0 and
   either line 6 > 0 or box 8 > 0).
8. **Spouse** — added with `addNonNull` (`TRCS:42573`); per-person values are kept as `taxpayerLine1c` /
   `spouseLine1c` for Form 2441 (`TRCS:42576-42577`).
9. **Round** — `roundMoney` (half-up, whole dollars) on the household total (`TRCS:18437`); cents are summed first.
10. **Output** — set on `Income.tipIncome` only when not null (`TRCS:18584-18586`); a person with no rows and no
    W-2 box 8 leaves line 1c null (blank), not 0.

Not modelled: the community-property split on MFS (D-1c-5; `CommunityProperty.java` splits W-2 boxes only), tips
of a dependent filing their own return other than W-2 box 8 (D-1c-4), the December/January timing and tip pooling
(left to what the user types; the help text for "Total tips received" says "Include all cash and charge tips you
received from this employer, whether or not you reported them" without mentioning either).

## 5. Flags and advisories

| Code | Blocks? | When | Tells the user | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ADEQUATE_RECORDS_MISSING_SUBSTANTIATED_AMOUNT_TAXPAYER` / `_SPOUSE` | Yes | Adequate records = Yes, box 8 > 0, substantiated amount blank (line 1c would use box 8) | Enter the substantiated amount or answer No | `TRCS:42641-42643`, `:42696-42711` |
| `MISSING_EMPLOYER_EIN_FORM_4137_TAXPAYER` / `_SPOUSE` | Yes | A Form 4137 is produced and any employer row has no EIN | Enter the EIN from W-2 box b | `TRCS:42777-42788` |
| `MISSING_SS_W2_DATA_TAXPAYER` / `_SPOUSE` | Yes | Form 4137 line 6 > 0 and neither a matching W-2 nor the form gives SS wages/tips (boxes 3 + 7) | Only Medicare tax was computed | `TRCS:42730-42747` |

None of these concerns the line 1c amount itself except the first. There is no flag for a W-2 box 8 that never
reached line 1c (D-1c-1). The EIN help text in the UI still says "Recommended … but not required"
(`form-tip-income-taxpayer.component.ts:1229-1231`) although compute blocks without it.

## 8. Compared with the IRS rules

| # | IRS rule (source) | What the app does | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Line 1c = tips not in W-2 box 1 (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 24`) | received − reported per employer (`TRCS:42622-42627`) | Match |
| 2 | Include W-2 box 8 allocated tips "unless you can prove that your unreported tips are less than the amount in box 8" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 24`; `f4137--2025.pdf p. 2`) | Box 8 reaches line 1c only through a Tips row. "No" to the screening question drops it (`TRCS:42592-42595`; UI saves no rows, `form-tip-income-taxpayer.component.ts:846-873`); backend auto-rows only when the row list is empty (`TRCS:42597-42599`), so a W-2 whose row was removed or that was added after rows were saved is skipped; a saved row keeps an old box 8 copy. No flag in any case | **Not supported D-1c-1** (silent; line 1c understated) |
| 3 | Adequate records showing less than box 8 → the smaller proven amount; otherwise box 8; actual unreported larger → actual (`p531.pdf p. 5`) | Substantiated < box 8 → substantiated; else max(received − reported, box 8) (`TRCS:42644-42650`). A substantiated amount ≥ box 8 is ignored (box 8 or received − reported used), and received − reported is ignored when it exceeds the substantiated amount | **Deviation D-1c-2** (only with inconsistent inputs; low) |
| 4 | Column (c) includes allocated tips; line 4 = line 2 − line 3 (`f4137--2025.pdf p. 1–2`) | Line 2 = Σ received only; line 4 = Σ per-employer amounts including box 8 (`TRCS:42653-42655`, `:42758-42760`). Line 1c amount is right; the Form 4137 as printed does not foot | **Deviation D-1c-3** (cosmetic for line 1c) |
| 5 | Tips under $20 a month are income (`f4137--2025.pdf p. 2`) | Stay in received − reported; only Form 4137 line 5 subtracts them (`TRCS:42685-42686`) | Match |
| 6 | Noncash tips on line 1c, not on Form 4137 (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 24, p. 112`) | Added to line 1c (`TRCS:42688`); not on Form 4137 | Match |
| 7 | Joint return: both spouses; separate Form 4137 each (`f4137--2025.pdf p. 1`) | Two per-person computations summed (`TRCS:42564-42577`) | Match |
| 8 | Separate returns: each spouse's own tips (`f4137--2025.pdf p. 1`) | Spouse skipped on MFS (`TRCS:42570-42572`); spouse leg uses the spouse's form | Match |
| 9 | A child's earnings are the child's (`p17.pdf p. 10`, as in line 1a) | W-2 box 8 matched by box a = filer SSN (`TRCS:52337-52339`); no Tips form on a dependent tab, so nothing of a dependent's reaches the parent's line 1c | Match (D-1a-1 does not repeat) |
| 10 | A dependent who must file reports their own unreported and noncash tips (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 24`; Chart C `p. 11`) | Dependent's own return has no Tips form (`DependentOwnFormScoper.java:148-215`; `shell.component.ts:1006-1010`); only W-2 box 8 auto-rows (person SSN = dependent) | **Not supported D-1c-4** (silent) |
| 11 | Community property, MFS: follow state law, Form 8958 (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 23`) | `CommunityProperty` splits W-2 boxes only; line 1c is never split, although the advisory says "half of the community wages … is reported on each separate return" | **Not supported D-1c-5** (silent; low) |
| 12 | Timing: December tips reported timely in January belong to the next year; late-reported tips to the year earned (`f4137--2025.pdf p. 2`) | Left to the user's figures; no question or help text | Match (input-dependent; no guidance) |
| 13 | Tip pooling: only tips kept (`p531.pdf p. 4`) | Left to the user's figures | Match (input-dependent; no guidance) |
| 14 | Self-employed tips → Schedule C (`p531.pdf p. 5`) | Tips form is per employer; business income entered elsewhere | Match |
| 15 | Rounding: add with cents, round the total (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 23`) | `roundMoney` on the summed total (`TRCS:18437`) | Match |
| 16 | Form 4137 required for allocated tips or unreported tips ≥ $20/month (`f4137--2025.pdf p. 1`) | Form 4137 built when line 4 > 0 and (line 6 > 0 or box 8 > 0) (`TRCS:42752-42770`) | Match |

## 9. Limits and out of scope

- **Form 4137 tax side** (lines 5–13, Schedule 2 line 5, Form 8959 line 2, the 50% penalty statement) belongs to the
  Form 4137 / Schedule 2 documentation, not to line 1c.
- **RRTA-covered tips**: the IRS says not to use Form 4137 for them; the app has no way to mark a row as railroad
  work (tax side only; line 1c unaffected).
- **Daily tip record** is not collected; the adequate-records answer and the optional description are trusted.
- **Null vs 0**: line 1c is null (blank) when there is nothing to report; never forced to 0
  (`knowledge/canonical-null-zero-semantic.md`).
