# Form 1040 line 1e — how the app does it

IRS rules: `C:\us-tax\new_lines\1e.md`. Flowchart: `C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\1e.md`.
Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`.
`CCE` = `us-tax-ui/src/app/forms/form-childcare-expenses.component` (`.html` / `.ts`).

---

## 1. Status

Line 1e is computed. The app sums W-2 box 10 of the filer (and the spouse on a joint return) by SSN, runs Form 2441
Part III lines 12–26 inside `computeDependentCareBenefits`, and puts line 26 on `form1040.income.dependentCareBenefits`.
Grace-period use, forfeitures, the "already in box 1" part, a plan limit, deemed income for students / disabled
spouses, the married-filing-separately limits and a §129(d) "plan failed testing" switch are all modelled. One
blocking flag forces the Child and dependent care form to be completed when box 10 is present.

**11 differences from the IRS rules** (D-1e-1 … D-1e-11). The most serious: when the "total care expenses" field
is left blank, line 16 is estimated as out-of-pocket expenses **plus the benefits themselves**, so benefits that
exceeded the care actually bought are excluded instead of taxed (D-1e-1); and the SSN match that finds box 10 is
format-sensitive, so a W-2 saved as `123456789` against an identification saved as `123-45-6789` drops the
benefits — and line 1e — with no flag (D-1e-2). Earned income (lines 18 / 19) misses self-employment earnings and
some line 1z items and wrongly keeps NQDC distributions and Form 2555-excluded wages (D-1e-3, D-1e-4).

Dependent W-2s (the D-1a-1 root cause) do **not** leak into line 1e: box 10 is filtered by the filer's / spouse's
SSN on every filing status (see §8 row 2).

## 4. How it is computed

Methods:

- Form merge (joint return): `mergeChildcareForms` `TRCS:38424-38498`, called `TRCS:2824-2826`.
- Part III and Part II lines 3–6: `computeDependentCareBenefits` `TRCS:38771-39263`, called `TRCS:3599-3600` (after
  tips, line 1g and line 1h are computed).
- Assembly: `buildIncome` — `line1e = roundMoney(…)` `TRCS:18439`, line 1z `TRCS:18487`, output `TRCS:18590-18592`.
- Part II lines 7–11 later: `finalizeForm2441PartII` `TRCS:44415` (does not touch Part III).
- Helpers: `sumW2DependentCareBenefitsForSsns` `TRCS:41243`, `sumW2DependentCareBenefits` `TRCS:41221`,
  `sumW2WagesForSsn` `TRCS:41652`, `sumW2WagesAfterCarveOuts` `TRCS:41055`, `countQualifyingPersons` `TRCS:41709`,
  `validateQualifyingPersons` `TRCS:41776`, `hasAnyChildcareData` `TRCS:42132`. Limits:
  `us-tax-be/.../microservices/ReferenceData.java:149-152`.

Steps, mirroring `new_lines/1e.md` section 6:

0. **Joint-return merge.** On the joint (primary) return the taxpayer and spouse forms are merged: amounts in
   `dependentCareBenefits` (box-1 part, lines 13, 14, 16, plan max) are **added**; deemed-income answers and
   `additionalEarnedIncome*` are taken **only from the taxpayer form** (`TRCS:38430,38484-38487`). Anything the
   spouse answers about themselves on the spouse tab (student months, other earned income) is dropped (D-1e-10).
1. **Is Part III needed?** `totalBenefits` = box 10 of W-2s whose `employeeSSN` equals the taxpayer's SSN (and the
   spouse's unless MFS) (`TRCS:38790-38796`). If neither SSN is saved, all W-2s in the account are summed
   (`TRCS:38797-38799`). The comparison is on trimmed text, not digits (`TRCS:41252`), so `123-45-6789` and
   `123456789` do not match (D-1e-2). If box 10 > 0 and the "Did you receive …" answer is not Yes, the blocking
   flag `DEPENDENT_CARE_BENEFITS_FORM_REQUIRED` is raised (`TRCS:38825-38832`). With no box 10 and no form data the
   method returns null — line 1e stays empty (`TRCS:38841-38844`). With form data but no box 10, Part III runs on
   zeros and line 1e becomes **0** (D-1e-11).
2. **Line 12** = `max(0, totalBenefits − alreadyIncludedInBox1Amount)` (`TRCS:38920-38921`). Nothing is added for
   sole-proprietorship or partnership benefits (D-1e-7).
3. **Lines 13–15.** Line 15 = `max(0, line12 + line13 − line14)` (`TRCS:38910-38911, 38922-38923`). Match.
4. **Line 16.** If `line16QualifiedExpensesForBenefits` is entered it is used. Otherwise, if the user did not answer
   No to care expenses and line 15 > 0, line 16 = **sum of the per-person expenses + line 15** (`TRCS:38936-38943`);
   else the per-person sum. Because the per-person figure is entered net of benefits (`CCE.ts:804`), the estimate
   assumes every benefit dollar bought qualified care. When benefits exceeded the care actually incurred (or there
   is no qualifying person at all) line 16 is overstated and the excess is excluded instead of taxed (D-1e-1).
   Provider eligibility (spouse, parent of the child, dependent, child under 19) is not checked (D-1e-9).
5. **Line 17** = `min(line15, line16)` (`TRCS:38944`). Match.
6. **Lines 18 / 19 — earned income** (`TRCS:38956-39073`):
   - Base per person: raw W-2 box 1 of that SSN (`sumW2WagesForSsn`, `TRCS:41652-41671`) less that person's
     inmate wages (`TRCS:38962-38965`). Raw box 1 keeps the W-2 box 11 NQDC distribution (a pension, not earned
     income) — D-1e-3. Statutory-employee box 1 stays in, which matches the Schedule C line 1 rule. If no W-2
     matches either SSN, the taxpayer gets the household total after carve-outs (`TRCS:38966-38968`).
   - Plus `additionalEarnedIncome*` typed by the user (`TRCS:38977-38982`), computed line 1c tips
     (`TRCS:38989-38994`), line 1g Form 8919 wages (`TRCS:39000-39005`) and the earned part of line 1h
     (`TRCS:39006-39011`; disability pay, excess deferrals, foreign wages not on a W-2, moving-payment excess,
     clergy housing excess — `TRCS:40223-40226`).
   - Not added: net self-employment earnings (Schedule SE line 3 less Schedule 1 line 15) and a self-employment
     loss, line 1b household wages, line 1d taxable Medicaid waiver payments, strike benefits, the combat-pay
     election (D-1e-4). Not subtracted: the Form 2555 line 43 exclusion (D-1e-3).
   - Deemed income: $250 / $500 a month (500 when two or more substantive qualifying persons, `TRCS:39014-39016`)
     × months, used as a floor on the **annual** figure: `max(earned, months × rate)` (`TRCS:39057-39058`) instead
     of month by month (D-1e-6). The taxpayer's deemed income applies on **every** filing status
     (`TRCS:39017-39018`; the UI shows the question to everyone), not only on a joint return (D-1e-5). Overlapping
     months of both spouses are split so the total is at most 12, with advisory
     `DEPENDENT_CARE_BOTH_SPOUSES_DEEMED_INCOME_SPLIT` (`TRCS:39023-39051`).
   - Line 18 = taxpayer earned income. Line 19 = spouse's on a joint return; on MFS without the exception the typed
     `mfsSpouseEarnedIncome` when > 0, else line 18 with an advisory (D-1e-8); otherwise line 18
     (`TRCS:39062-39073`).
7. **Line 20** = smallest of 17, 18, 19 (`TRCS:39074`). Match.
8. **Line 21** = $2,500 if MFS without the exception, else $5,000; the lower plan maximum if entered
   (`TRCS:39076-39080`). Match. (On a joint return both forms' plan maximums are added before the `min`.)
9. **Lines 22–24.** Hard-coded: line 22 "No", 0; line 23 = line 15; line 24 = 0 (`TRCS:39123, 39240-39244`).
   Correct for employees; no self-employed path (D-1e-7).
10. **Line 25 / 26.** Normally line 25 = `min(line20, line21)`, line 26 = `max(0, line15 − line25)`
    (`TRCS:39090-39091`). If the §129(d) "plan failed testing" answer is Yes, line 25 = 0 and line 26 = line 15
    (`TRCS:39083-39088`) — see §8 row 17.
11. **Part II effect.** Line 28 = 24 + 25, line 29 = max(0, 27 − 28), line 31 = min(29, 30), line 3 = line 31 when
    benefits exist (`TRCS:39123-39132`). Line 26 is not affected by Part II.
12. **Round and output.** `roundMoney(line26)` (`TRCS:39262`) and again `roundMoney` in `buildIncome`
    (`TRCS:18439`); stored when non-null (`TRCS:18590-18592`).

## 9. Limits and out of scope

- The Child and dependent care form is one per return; on MFS each leg uses its own copy.
- `planMaxExcludable` and `line16QualifiedExpensesForBenefits` are **added** across the two forms on a joint
  return; if both spouses type the same household figure it is doubled (behaviour by design of the merge,
  `TRCS:38480-38483`; not tested).
- Part I providers are display-only; more than three providers are signalled by a checkbox.
- Part II (the credit) is documented with Form 2441 separately; only lines 27–31 depend on Part III.
