# Form 1040 line 1g — how the app does it

IRS rules: `C:\us-tax\new_lines\1g.md`. Flowchart: `C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\1g.md`.
Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`.

---

## 1. Status

Line 1g is computed. Each of the taxpayer and the spouse has an "Uncollected Social Security" form (the Form 8919
intake) with one row per firm; the app adds column (f) of every row into that person's Form 8919 line 6, adds the two
line 6 amounts into line 1g (the spouse's is skipped on a married-filing-separately return), and also figures Form 8919
lines 7–13 (tax to Schedule 2 line 6). Blocking flags cover an invalid reason code, a missing IRS date for codes A / C,
code H without a 1099, and a missing firm federal identification number. No statement feeds line 1g. Unlike line 1a
(D-1a-1), a dependent's data never reaches the parent's line 1g: only the taxpayer and spouse have the form, and line 8
reads W-2s by the person's SSN.

**9 differences from the IRS rules** (D-1g-1 … D-1g-9). The most serious: Form 8919 wages are not IRA compensation,
so an IRA contribution backed by them is charged the 6% Form 5329 excess-contribution tax (D-1g-2); a 1099-NEC from
the misclassifying firm is auto-filled into Schedule C gross receipts when the person has one business with a blank
gross-receipts field, counting the same pay twice (D-1g-3); and the Form 8919 tax is left in the Form 2210 penalty
base (D-1g-4). The line 1b finding D-1b-2 (household rows marked "paid as a contractor" are forced onto Form 8919)
is seen from this side in §8 row 15; it is not repeated here.

## 4. How it is computed

Methods:

- Load: `TRCS:2699-2700` (`personalForms.get("uncollected-ss-medicare-taxpayer" / "-spouse")`).
- Orchestrator: `computeUncollectedSSTax` `TRCS:40348-40379`, called at `TRCS:3572` after `computeTips` (Form 4137) so
  Form 4137 line 10 is available.
- Per person: `computeForm8919ForPerson` `TRCS:40381-40554`; row filter `hasAnyForm8919Inputs` /
  `hasAnyForm8919RowInput` `TRCS:42228-42251`; `normalizeReasonCode` / `isValidReasonCode` `TRCS:42253-42262`.
- Line 8 helpers: `sumW2SocialSecurityWagesAndTipsForSsn` `TRCS:52174-52196`, `sumW2RrtaCompensationForSsn`
  `TRCS:52269-52289`.
- Assembly: `buildIncome` `TRCS:18441` (round), `:18487` (line 1z), `:18596-18597` (output).
- Cross-check with line 1b: `validateContractorHouseholdHasMatching8919` `TRCS:14242-14299`, called `TRCS:3536-3537`.

Steps, mirroring `new_lines/1g.md` section 6:

1. **Does Form 8919 apply?** Asked only in the UI (screening question). The backend trusts the saved rows: any row
   with any field filled is a Form 8919 row (`TRCS:42240-42251`). A person with no such row gets no Form 8919 and a
   null line 6 (`TRCS:40392-40395`, `:40476-40479`).
2. **Reason code per row.** Upper-cased and trimmed; anything other than A, C, G, H raises the blocking
   `FORM8919_REASON_CODE_INVALID_<PERSON>` (`TRCS:40411-40418`). A or C without a date → blocking
   `FORM8919_IRS_DATE_REQUIRED_<PERSON>` (`:40419-40426`). G → non-blocking `FORM8919_SS8_REQUIRED_<PERSON>`
   (`:40427-40434`). H without "1099 received = Yes" → blocking, non-overrideable `FORM8919_CODE_H_1099_<PERSON>`
   (`:40435-40462`; `NonOverrideableFlags.java:134-135`). Code H does not check that a W-2 from the same firm exists.
3. **Columns (a), (b), (e).** Copied to the output row. A blank firm id on any row → blocking
   `MISSING_FIRM_FEDERAL_ID_FORM_8919_<PERSON>`; any text, including "unknown", passes (`TRCS:40518-40533`).
4. **Column (f).** `wagesNoFicaNotW2` parsed per row; each row's printed amount is `roundMoney(wages)` (`:40470`).
5. **Line 6** = sum of the unrounded column (f) amounts of all rows (no 5-row limit), then rounded
   (`TRCS:40473`, `:40509`, `:40553`).
6. **Line 1g** = taxpayer line 6 + spouse line 6 (`addNonNull`, `TRCS:40373`). On MFS the spouse's form is ignored:
   `computeForm8919ForPerson` gets `null` data, `null` Form 4137 and `null` SSN for the spouse (`TRCS:40368-40370`;
   `isMfsReturn` from `TRCS:3548-3549`). On an MFS leg the other spouse's `-spouse` forms are dropped and the leg's own
   form becomes `-taxpayer` (`us-tax-be/.../multireturn/MfsFormScoper.java:339`, `:375`, `:678-679`).
7. **Round.** `roundMoney` (half-up, whole dollars) in `buildIncome` (`TRCS:18441`) — already whole dollars per
   person. Stored only when not null (`TRCS:18596-18597`).

Tax part (lines 7–13, `TRCS:40481-40516`, for reference — the result is Schedule 2 line 6):

- Line 7 = `ReferenceData.SOCIAL_SECURITY_WAGE_BASE` = 176,100 (`ReferenceData.java:12`).
- Line 8 = this person's W-2 box 3 + box 7 + `rRTACompensationW2Box14` (filtered by the person's SSN) + this person's
  Form 4137 line 10. Form 4852 substitute-W-2 social security wages / tips are not included (D-1g-5). With a blank
  SSN the W-2 filter is skipped and every W-2 in the account is summed (D-1g-6). RRTA is not capped at $176,100; this
  has no effect, because RRTA above that amount already makes line 9 zero.
- Line 9 = max(0, 7 − 8); line 10 = min(line 6, line 9); line 11 = line 10 × 0.062; line 12 = line 6 × 0.0145;
  line 13 = line 11 + line 12 — each computed from unrounded values and rounded only when stored (D-1g-9).
- Schedule 2 line 6 = taxpayer line 13 + spouse line 13 (`TRCS:40375`, set at `TRCS:42838`); line 7 subtotal
  `TRCS:42845`.
- Non-blocking `FORM8919_LINE1C_COLLISION_<PERSON>` when the same person also has a Form 4137 (`TRCS:40535-40550`).

**Copies of line 1g elsewhere.** `UncollectedComputation` carries the household total (`line1g`) and the per-person
amounts (`taxpayerLine1g`, `spouseLine1g`, `TRCS:55820-55826`); all are MFS-guarded at the source.

## 9. Limits and out of scope

- **Who must file** is asked only in the UI; the backend does not test the four conditions — it trusts the rows.
- **Form SS-8** is not produced or tracked (filed separately by the worker).
- **Section 218 state / local workers** and **Form 1040-NR / 1040-SS** filers are not modelled.
- **Statement link:** a 1099-NEC / 1099-MISC from the firm is not tied to the Form 8919 row; column (a) / (b) are
  typed.
- **UI-only fields** `screening.appliesToFiler` and `hasIrsLetter` are not persisted (see §2).
- **The tax itself** (Schedule 2 line 6, Form 2210 annualized line 30) is documented with Schedule 2 / Form 2210, not
  here.
