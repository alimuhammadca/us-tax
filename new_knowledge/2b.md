# Form 1040 line 2b — how the app does it

IRS rules: `C:\us-tax\new_lines\2b.md`. Flowchart: `C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\2b.md`. Tax-exempt interest (line 2a),
computed by the same methods: `C:\us-tax\new_knowledge\2a.md`.
Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`.

---

## 1. Status

Line 2b is computed. Per person, `computeInterestForPerson` adds 1099-INT box 1 + box 3 less box 11 + box 12 per
statement, 1099-OID box 1 + box 8 less box 6 + box 10 per statement (plus box 5 under a §1278(b) election), the
taxable part of the pooled 1099-OID box 2, hand-entered interest (with and without a 1099), §7872 imputed interest on a
gift loan and seller-financed mortgage interest, then subtracts the hand-entered adjustments (accrued interest,
nominee, premiums, savings-bond interest reported before, frozen deposits). At return level it adds the two persons,
the §54A tax-credit-bond credit and Schedule K-1 interest, and subtracts the Form 8815 exclusion on a second compute
pass. Schedule B Part I is produced when the total is over $1,500 or any listed condition applies.

**8 differences from the IRS rules** (D-2b-1 … D-2b-8). The most serious: Schedule K-1 interest has **no person
filter** outside MFS — a dependent's K-1 interest lands on the parent's line 2b, and the parents' K-1 interest lands on
every dependent's own return (D-2b-1, same root cause as D-1a-1, high); a 1099 with a blank recipient TIN is counted on
every return of the household (D-2b-2); 1099-INT box 10 market discount is never read (D-2b-4); Form 8815 gives a
qualifying surviving spouse the joint-return thresholds (D-2b-5).

**D-1a-1 check (a dependent's 1099-INT on the parent's return):** for 1099-INT and 1099-OID the repeat does **not**
occur when the statement carries the dependent's SSN as recipient TIN: `belongsToPerson` keeps only the taxpayer's or
spouse's TIN (`TRCS:36027-36033`) and the UI pre-fills the dependent's SSN into a statement created on the dependent's
tab (`us-tax-ui/src/app/service/statement-entry-state.service.ts:26-35`). It **does** occur for Schedule K-1 interest
(D-2b-1) and for a blank TIN (D-2b-2). Side finding outside this line: Schedule 1 line 18 sums the early-withdrawal
penalty of every household 1099-INT / 1099-OID with no person filter (`TRCS:32887-32890`) — same root cause, for the
line 10 owner.

## 4. How it is computed

Methods:

- Load: 1099-INT `TRCS:3114-3118` (MFS community-property split of boxes 1, 3, 8 and withholding only), 1099-OID
  `TRCS:3250`, K-1s `TRCS:3244-3248`.
- Before the call: §54A credit gross-up `TRCS:3703-3704`; K-1 interest items `TRCS:3709-3713` (helper
  `k1PortfolioInterestItems` `TRCS:36048-36074`, MFS filter `filterK1EntriesForMfs` `TRCS:31667-31680`).
- `computeInterestIncome` `TRCS:18875-19160`; `computeInterestForPerson` `TRCS:19162-19522`; attribution
  `belongsToPerson` `TRCS:36021-36040`.
- Form 8815: `computeForm8815` `TRCS:8911-9110`, applied through the second pass `TRCS:7511-7531` and
  `form8815ExclusionAppliedThisPass` `TRCS:8868-8870`.
- Schedule B: `buildScheduleB` `TRCS:36163-36173`.
- Output: `buildIncome` `TRCS:18450`, `:18634-18636`; line 9 `TRCS:18571`.

Steps, mirroring `new_lines/2b.md` section 6:

1. **Collect the statements that belong on this return.**
   - 1099-INT / 1099-OID: `belongsToPerson` (`TRCS:36027-36039`) — a filled TIN must equal `you.ssn` (taxpayer pass)
     or `spouse.ssn` (spouse pass); anything else, including a dependent's SSN, is dropped silently (D-2b-3 for a TIN
     that is merely wrong). A blank TIN goes to the taxpayer pass unless only the spouse answered Yes to "Did you have
     any interest income". On MFS the spouse form and SSN are nulled (`TRCS:18910-18911`, `:18949`); on a dependent's
     own return `you` is the dependent (`us-tax-be/.../multireturn/DependentOwnFormScoper.java:155-164`) and no interest
     form exists. So a blank-TIN statement is counted on **every** return of the household (D-2b-2).
   - Schedule K-1 interest: no attribution at all except dropping the **other spouse's** K-1 on MFS
     (`TRCS:3709-3713`, `:31667-31680`). On single / joint / HOH / QSS returns a dependent's K-1 interest is added to the
     parent's line 2b, and on a dependent's own return the parents' K-1 interest is added to the child's (D-2b-1).
   - Seller-financed rows, hand-entered amounts and the gift loan belong to the form they are entered on.
2. **List the items (gross).** Per 1099-INT: `subtractNonNegative(box1 + box3, box11 + box12)` (`TRCS:19208`) — the
   statement premium is netted **inside** the payer line, floored at 0 per statement; any excess is kept for
   Schedule A line 16 (`TRCS:19212-19218`, applied `TRCS:4919-4948` with the Reg. 1.171-2 history limit and the
   `BOND_PREMIUM_CARRYFORWARD` advisory). Box 10 is not read (D-2b-4). Per 1099-OID:
   `subtractNonNegative(box1 + box8, box6 + box10)` (`TRCS:19253-19256`); box 5 added only when
   `marketDiscountSection1278bElection` is Yes (`TRCS:19268-19276`). Box 2 is pooled per person; its taxable part is
   the override if entered, otherwise the pool less the tax-exempt part (`TRCS:19334-19340`). Then hand-entered taxable
   interest (`TRCS:19342-19349`), §7872 imputed interest (`TRCS:19351-19355`, helper `:19533-19564`, rate 4.22% from
   Rev. Rul. 2025-13 in the code — that ruling is not in the IRS library used here, unverified), interest with no 1099
   (`TRCS:19358-19364`), and seller-financed rows (`TRCS:19456-19488`, kept in a separate list).
3. **Subtract the adjustments.** Accrued interest, nominee, the three hand-entered premiums (ignored when "already
   netted" is Yes, `TRCS:19390-19398`), savings-bond interest reported before and frozen deposits are summed and
   subtracted from the person's total, floored at 0 (`TRCS:19403-19430`); each becomes a negative Schedule B item with
   its own label (`TRCS:19433-19439`). Hand-entered taxable / Treasury premium above the remaining interest goes to
   Schedule A line 16 (`TRCS:19418-19428`).
4. **Schedule B line 2.** Return level: taxpayer + spouse (`TRCS:18999-19002`), + §54A credit gross-up
   (`TRCS:19008`), + K-1 interest items (`TRCS:19010-19014`) = `taxableBeforeExclusion` → Schedule B line 2
   (`TRCS:36171`).
5. **Schedule B line 3 (Form 8815).** First pass: nothing excluded. `computeForm8815` then tests the claim: not on MFS
   (`FORM_8815_MFS_BLOCKED`, `TRCS:8924-8930`), user "specific circumstance" block (`TRCS:8934-8935`), modified AGI
   (`TRCS:8872-8893`) against the ceiling (`TRCS:9035-9038`; manual override `TRCS:8962-8967`), phase-out
   (`TRCS:9040-9062`). Thresholds: `isMfj` is true for **married filing jointly and qualifying surviving spouse**
   (`TRCS:8941-8948`, constants `us-tax-be/.../microservices/ReferenceData.java:813-818`) (D-2b-5). If line 14 > 0 and
   line 2b > 0, `prepare` runs again with line 14 injected (`TRCS:7511-7531`) and `computeInterestIncome` subtracts it
   (`TRCS:19040-19041`), floored at 0. Line 14 is not capped at the savings-bond interest actually in line 2.
6. **Schedule B line 4 = line 2b** (`TRCS:19041`, `:36173`).
7. **Schedule B required?** `TRCS:19088-19101`: line 2b > $1,500, or accrued interest, nominee, OID adjustment (Yes
   to the OID question or a hand-entered OID premium), bond-premium adjustment (Yes to the premium question or a
   hand-entered taxable / Treasury premium), Form 8815 claimed, seller-financed rows, savings bond previously reported,
   frozen deposits, §1278(b) election, dividends over $1,500 / nominee, foreign account, foreign trust. **Premium taken
   from the statements (1099-INT box 11/12, 1099-OID box 6/10) is not a trigger** (D-2b-6). Part I order:
   seller-financed (taxpayer, spouse), other items (taxpayer, spouse), K-1 items (`TRCS:19116-19122`).
8. **Round.** Person totals rounded (`TRCS:19514`), return totals rounded (`TRCS:19144-19146`), and again in
   `buildIncome` (`TRCS:18450`) — whole dollars, half-up (D-2b-8: cents are not carried to the final total).
9. **Output.** `Income.taxableInterest` set when not null (`TRCS:18634-18636`); added into line 9 (`TRCS:18571`).

## 9. Limits and out of scope

- Contingent payment debt instruments, stripped bonds and inflation-indexed (TIPS) refiguring are not modelled; the
  user enters adjustments by hand.
- Market discount without a §1278(b) election is left to the sale (Form 8949), as the IRS requires.
- The 1099-OID box 2 tax-exempt / taxable split is one figure per person, not per statement.
- Form 8815 line 14 is not capped at the Series EE / I interest actually included (only line 2b is floored at 0).
- Deliberate design: three-state value — null (no input), 0 (input nets to zero), positive (`TRCS:19133-19141`).
