IRS rules: `C:\us-tax\new_lines\3a.md`. Flowchart: `C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\3a.md`. Companions: `3b.md`, `3c.md`.
Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`.

## 1. Status

Line 3a is computed in the same pass as line 3b. Per person (taxpayer, and spouse on a joint return) the app sums
1099-DIV box 1b of the statements whose recipient TIN is that person's, adds the hand-entered qualified dividends,
subtracts the hand-entered exceptions (nominee, 61/121-day and 91/181-day holding periods, short-sale related
payments, payments in lieu, surrogate foreign corporation, non-treaty foreign corporation), and caps the result at
the person's line 3b. At the return level it adds Schedule K-1 qualified dividends (each capped at that K-1's
ordinary dividends) and Form 8814 line 9, caps line 3a at line 3b with an advisory, rounds, and stores
`form1040.income.qualifiedDividends`. A Form 4952 line 4g election does not reduce line 3a (correct).

**No difference of its own.** Line 3a shares the line 3b differences that change which statements are counted —
D-3b-1 (K-1 dividends not attributed to a person, same root cause as D-1a-1), D-3b-2 (a person not answered Yes
loses his or her 1099-DIVs) and D-3b-3 (blank-TIN 1099-DIV) — and the Form 8814 election differences D-3c-1 …
D-3c-3. The D-1a-1 check: a dependent's 1099-DIV does **not** reach the parent's line 3a directly (TIN
attribution); only through Form 8814 line 9.

## 4. How it is computed

Methods: `computeDividendForPerson` `TRCS:19910-20081`; `computeDividendIncome` `TRCS:19695-19908`;
`k1PortfolioDividends` `TRCS:36079-36106`; `buildForm8814` `TRCS:24610-24712`; output `buildIncome`
`TRCS:18451`, `:18637-18639`.

Steps, mirroring `new_lines/3a.md` section 6:

1. **Same statements as line 3b.** Attribution by recipient TIN and the per-person `hadDividendIncome` gate are
   shared (`3b.md` §4 step 1). A dependent's 1099-DIV is excluded; the K-1 path has no person filter (D-3b-1).
2. **Add box 1b and K-1 qualified dividends.** `qualifiedDividendsAmount` per kept 1099-DIV
   (`TRCS:19947`). K-1 qualified dividends are added at the return level, each K-1's amount capped at that K-1's
   ordinary dividends, and a K-1 with no ordinary dividends contributes nothing (`TRCS:36094-36102`, added at
   `TRCS:19732-19735`). PTEP is not asked (D-3b-5).
3. **Add qualified dividends with no Form 1099-DIV.** `manualQualifiedDividendsNotOnStatements` (`TRCS:19977`) and
   `qualifiedDividendsNoInformationReturn` (only inside the "no-form ordinary amount is positive" branch,
   `TRCS:19983-19985`). Modern mode only (`TRCS:19968`).
4. **Take out the exceptions.** `disallowedQualifiedTotal` = nominee qualified + the six exception fields
   (`TRCS:20011-20028`); `line3a = subtractNonNegative(candidate, disallowed)` (`TRCS:20060`). The app does not work
   out holding periods itself — the user types the amounts (comment `TRCS:19997-20010`). The PFIC excess-distribution
   subtraction reduces line 3b only (`TRCS:19979-19980`).
5. **Cap per person.** If the person's line 3a > the person's line 3b, it is set equal, silently
   (`TRCS:20061-20063`).
6. **Add Form 8814 line 9** of all built Forms 8814 when positive (`TRCS:19765-19769`), the same amount as line 3b.
7. **Cap for the return.** If line 3a > line 3b, line 3a = line 3b and the advisory
   `QUALIFIED_DIVIDENDS_LIMITED_TO_ORDINARY_DIVIDENDS` is raised (`TRCS:19784-19791`).
8. **Round** (`TRCS:19900`, again at `TRCS:18451`).
9. **Output.** `income.qualifiedDividends` when not null (`TRCS:18637-18639`). Nothing else writes it: a Form 4952
   line 4g election is applied inside the Schedule D Tax Worksheet (`TRCS:11700-11707`, which also forces that
   worksheet), not to line 3a — matching `f4952--2025.pdf p. 4`.

## 9. Limits and out of scope

- **Holding-period arithmetic** is not done by the app (no trade dates are collected); the user enters the amounts
  that fail. The IRS places that duty on the filer.
- **Form 1116 adjustments** to foreign-source qualified dividends are made on Form 1116, not on line 3a (see the
  Form 1116 docs).
- The child's own Form 1040 (dependent's own return) uses the same code with the child as "you"; the Form 8615
  kiddie tax reads that return's line 3a.
