# Form 1040 line 3b — how the app does it

IRS rules: `C:\us-tax\new_lines\3b.md`. Flowchart: `C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\3b.md`. Companions: `3a.md`, `3c.md`.
Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`.

---

## 1. Status

Line 3b is computed. The app adds 1099-DIV box 1a of the taxpayer's and (joint return) the spouse's statements —
told apart by the **recipient TIN** on each 1099-DIV — plus hand-entered dividends, less nominee dividends and a
PFIC excess distribution, then adds Schedule K-1 ordinary dividends (1065 box 6a, 1120-S box 5a, 1041 box 2a) and
Form 8814 line 9, rounds, and stores `form1040.income.ordinaryDividends`. Schedule B is built when the total is
over $1,500 or nominee dividends exist.

**The D-1a-1 root cause was checked.** For **1099-DIVs it does not repeat**: unlike W-2s, every 1099-DIV is matched
to the taxpayer or spouse by recipient TIN (`belongsToPerson`, `TRCS:36021-36040`), so a dependent's 1099-DIV
(saved on the dependent's tab, where the UI pre-fills the dependent's SSN) is left off the parent's line 3b on every
filing status. It goes instead to an automatically built Form 8814 (see `3c.md`). For **Schedule K-1 dividends it
does repeat** (D-3b-1): K-1s are filtered only to drop the other spouse's on a married-filing-separately return, so
a dependent's K-1 dividends land on the parent's lines 3a/3b, and on a dependent's own return every K-1 of the
household lands on the child's.

**5 differences from the IRS rules** (D-3b-1 … D-3b-5); the Form 8814 election problems that also change line 3b
are recorded under line 3c (D-3c-1 … D-3c-3). The most serious: D-3b-1 (K-1 dividends not attributed to a person)
and D-3b-2 (a person whose dividend form is not answered Yes loses his or her 1099-DIVs with no flag).

## 4. How it is computed

Methods:

- Load: `TRCS:3119-3125` — `listEntriesWithData(uid, "1099-div")` through `CommunityProperty.withCommunityPropertySplit`
  (MFS community split of boxes 1a, 1b, 2a…, `cpInterestDividendsCommunityPct`, default 0). K-1s: `TRCS:3244-3249`.
- K-1 dividends: `k1PortfolioDividends` `TRCS:36079-36106`, called `TRCS:3720-3723` after `filterK1EntriesForMfs`
  (`TRCS:31667-31679`).
- Orchestrator: `computeInterestIncome` `TRCS:18875-19160` (MFS guard `:18910-18911`, legacy mode `:18919-18923`,
  SSNs `:18944-18949`).
- Return level: `computeDividendIncome` `TRCS:19695-19908`. Per person: `computeDividendForPerson` `TRCS:19910-20081`.
- Attribution: `belongsToPerson` `TRCS:36021-36040`.
- Upload gate: `validateDividendStatementGating` `TRCS:19642-19693`.
- Output: `buildIncome` `TRCS:18452` (round), `:18571` (line 9), `:18640-18642` (store); Schedule B `buildScheduleB`
  `TRCS:36163-36179`.

Steps, mirroring `new_lines/3b.md` section 6:

1. **Collect the statements that belong on this return.** Every 1099-DIV in the account is loaded. For the taxpayer
   and then the spouse, `computeDividendForPerson` keeps a 1099-DIV only when `belongsToPerson` says so
   (`TRCS:19941-19944`): with a recipient TIN, it must equal the taxpayer's SSN (`you.ssn`) or the spouse's SSN
   (`spouse.ssn`); with **no** TIN, it goes to the taxpayer unless only the spouse answered Yes (`TRCS:36034-36039`).
   - A dependent's 1099-DIV (TIN = the dependent's SSN) matches neither person and is left off — on every filing
     status. It is picked up by the Form 8814 builder instead (`TRCS:24378-24382`; see `3c.md`).
   - On married filing separately the spouse form and spouse SSN are nulled (`TRCS:18911`, `:18949`), so only the
     filer's 1099-DIVs count. On a dependent's own return "you" is the dependent (`DependentOwnFormScoper.java:155-164`)
     and no dividend form exists, so the legacy mode below keeps only the dependent's TIN.
   - A 1099-DIV with a blank TIN counts as the taxpayer's on every return — including both MFS legs and a dependent's
     own return (D-3b-3).
   - **Per-person gate:** in "modern mode" (either dividend form saved or answered Yes) a person whose
     `hadDividendIncome` is not `true` gets nothing at all (`TRCS:19932-19934`), even if 1099-DIVs carry his or her
     SSN (D-3b-2). In "legacy mode" (no dividend form saved for anyone) the statements are still attributed by TIN,
     but no hand-entered amounts are read (`TRCS:19968`). (The code comment at `TRCS:19924` says legacy mode
     processes "all 1099-DIV entries … regardless of recipientTIN"; the code still calls `belongsToPerson`.)
2. **Add box 1a.** `totalOrdinaryDividendsAmount` per kept entry (`TRCS:19945-19946`); each adds a Schedule B item
   under the payer name (`TRCS:19958`). Box 1b, box 13 (Form 6251) and box 5 (199A) are summed alongside but not
   added to line 3b. Box 2a, 3, 9, 10 are not read here.
3. **Add K-1 ordinary dividends.** At the return level `k1PortfolioDividends.ordinary()` is added
   (`TRCS:19731-19735`), with a Schedule B item per K-1 (`TRCS:19814`). The K-1 list is filtered only by
   `filterK1EntriesForMfs`, which drops K-1s whose TIN equals `filing-status.mfsOtherSpouseSsn`; on single, joint,
   head-of-household and dependent's-own returns no filter runs (D-3b-1). The PTEP part is not asked (D-3b-5).
4. **Add dividends with no Form 1099-DIV.** `manualOrdinaryDividendsNotOnStatements` and
   `ordinaryDividendsNoInformationReturn` are added, each with a Schedule B item (`TRCS:19969-19989`).
   A PFIC excess distribution reported on a 1099-DIV (`pficExcessDistributionIncluded`, injected from Form 8621
   Part V at `TRCS:2881-2884`) is subtracted, floored at 0 (`TRCS:19979-19980`).
5. **Subtract nominee dividends.** `nomineeOrdinaryDividends` per person, floored at 0 (`TRCS:20047`), with a
   negative "nominee dividend distribution" Schedule B row (`TRCS:20059`).
6. **Add Form 8814 line 9.** `form8814QualifiedDividendsTotal` (sum of every built Form 8814's line 9,
   `TRCS:3675-3678`) is added to both line 3a and line 3b when positive (`TRCS:19765-19769`). Which children get a
   Form 8814 is decided by `computeChildInterestDividends` (`TRCS:24341-24523`) — see `3c.md` for D-3c-1 … D-3c-3.
7. **Schedule B.** Required from dividends when line 3b > $1,500 or any nominee dividends (`TRCS:19806-19808`);
   the interest side and Part III can also require it (`TRCS:19088-19101`). Schedule B line 6 = line 3b, line 5 =
   the collected items (`TRCS:36170,36174`). The Form 8814 line 9 amount and the PFIC subtraction have no line 5
   item, so line 5 does not add up to line 6 when they apply (D-3b-4).
8. **Round.** `roundMoney` on the return total (`TRCS:19901`, again at `:18452`); cents are kept while adding.
9. **Output.** `income.ordinaryDividends` is set when not null (`TRCS:18640-18642`). Null means no dividend input at
   all; 0 means an entered amount netting to zero (comment and code `TRCS:19891-19907`).

## 9. Limits and out of scope

- **Net investment income tax** uses line 3b through Form 8960 (computed elsewhere).
- **Box 3 nondividend distributions** are never read; recovering basis and reporting the excess as a capital gain
  is left to the capital-gain forms.
- **Holding-period and other qualified-dividend exceptions** are hand-entered (see `3a.md`); line 3b is unaffected.
- **Legacy mode** (no dividend form saved) exists for old data and test fixtures; it ignores every hand-entered
  amount (`TRCS:19968`).
- The Form 8814 election, eligibility and MFS rules are in `3c.md`.
