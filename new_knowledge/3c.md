## 1. Status

Both line 3c checkboxes are set to `true` together when the total of Form 8814 line 9 over all built Forms 8814 is
more than zero, and left null otherwise. That matches the IRS: Form 8814 line 9 is the only child amount that goes
on lines 3a and 3b, and it goes on both.

What decides whether a Form 8814 exists is where the app departs from the IRS. **3 differences** (D-3c-1 …
D-3c-3), all about the Form 8814 election, and each also changes the amounts on lines 3a and 3b (and Schedule 1
line 8z, Schedule D line 13 and line 16 box 1): the app builds a Form 8814 for **every** dependent whose SSN is on a
1099-INT or 1099-DIV, with no election saved (D-3c-1); it does not check several of the IRS eligibility conditions
from the data (D-3c-2); and on married-filing-separately returns a hand-entered child can be reported on both
spouses' returns (D-3c-3).

This is the answer to the D-1a-1 question for dividends: a dependent's 1099-DIV does not land on the parent's
lines 3a/3b as the parent's own dividends; it is forced through Form 8814 instead, unless the dependent has an
active dependent's-own return, in which case it stays on the child's return only.

## 4. How it is computed

1. **Which children get a Form 8814** — `computeChildInterestDividends` `TRCS:24341-24523`, called at `TRCS:3641-3643`
   (skipped entirely on a dependent's own return, `TRCS:3608`).
   - **Statement path** (`TRCS:24352-24473`): for each dependent on this return (on MFS, only the dependents
     assigned to this leg — `TRCS:874-894`): skip if the child has an active dependent's-own return
     (`FORM8814_SUPPRESSED_CHILD_FILES_OWN_RETURN`, `TRCS:24362-24371`); collect the 1099-INT and 1099-DIV whose
     recipient TIN is the child's SSN (`TRCS:24373-24384`); if there are none, skip. Otherwise run the qualifying
     test (relationship from the EIC list or foster child; age under 19, or under 24 and a full-time student, **or
     permanently and totally disabled at any age**, `TRCS:24309-24339`) — failing it raises the blocking
     `FORM8814_CHILD_NOT_QUALIFYING` and skips. Derive lines 1a, 1b, 2a (box 1a), 2b (box 1b), 3 (box 2a) from the
     statements (`TRCS:24406-24428`); a matching hand entry overrides any line it fills (`TRCS:24430-24450`); the
     four eligibility checkboxes block with `FORM8814_CHILD_FILED_JOINT_RETURN` or
     `FORM8814_CHILD_HAD_WITHHOLDING_OR_ESTIMATED_PAYMENTS` (`TRCS:24555-24590`); nominee portions are subtracted
     (`TRCS:24459-24462`). **No election answer is read.**
   - **Hand-entry path** (`TRCS:24475-24520`): every saved child entry whose SSN had no statements above — including
     a child not on this return's dependent list — is built the same way (without the qualifying test).
2. **Form 8814 line 9** — `buildForm8814` `TRCS:24610-24712`: line 4 = 1a + 2a + 3; nothing if 0; line 4 ≥ $13,500
   → non-overrideable blocker `FORM8814_CHILD_GROSS_INCOME_TOO_HIGH` (`TRCS:24619-24638`;
   `NonOverrideableFlags.java:379`); only when line 4 > $2,700: line 6 = line 4 − 2,700, line 7 = 2b ÷ 4 (10
   decimals), line 9 = round(line 6 × line 7), stored only if not zero (`TRCS:24673-24706`).
3. **Total** — `form8814QualifiedDividendsTotal` = Σ line 9 (`TRCS:3675-3678`).
4. **Checkboxes** — `hasChildQualifiedDividends = hasPositiveAmount(total)` (`TRCS:19765`); the total is added to
   lines 3a and 3b (`TRCS:19766-19769`); both checkbox values are `TRUE` when it holds, otherwise `null`
   (`TRCS:19905-19906`); `buildIncome` copies only `TRUE` (`TRCS:18653-18658`).

## 8. Compared with the IRS rules

| # | IRS rule (source) | What the app does | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Check box 1 / box 2 when the child's dividends are in line 3a / 3b (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 25–26`); only Form 8814 line 9 goes there, on both lines (`i8814.pdf p. 4`) | Both boxes `TRUE` when Σ line 9 > 0 (`TRCS:19765,19905-19906`) | Match |
| 2 | Form 8814 is an **election**: "Use this form if you elect to report your child's income on your return" (`i8814.pdf p. 1`); "To figure the most beneficial tax … compare the methods" (`i8814.pdf p. 2`) | A Form 8814 is built for every dependent whose SSN is on a 1099-INT/1099-DIV (`TRCS:24352-24384`). The UI question "Are you electing…?" is not saved and not read; a "No" changes nothing. The only way to decline is to start a dependent's own return (`TRCS:3630-3640`). A child at or above $13,500 blocks the parent's return with a non-overrideable flag instead of simply staying off it | **Deviation D-3c-1** (child's income put on the parent's lines 3a/3b, 8z, 7a and line 16 without an election; or the parent's return blocked) |
| 3 | Eligibility: child's only income interest and dividends; no federal income tax withheld; under 19 / full-time student under 24, January 1 birthdays count as older; required to file (`i8814.pdf p. 1`) | Not checked from data: a W-2 or other income of the child does not stop the election (and the W-2 itself lands on the parent's line 1a, D-1a-1); 1099-DIV / 1099-INT box 4 backup withholding is not read (only the hand checkbox); a permanently and totally disabled dependent of any age qualifies (`TRCS:24324-24326`) though Form 8814 has no such exception; age is the plain year-end age (`TRCS:42112-42123`), so a child born January 1, 2007 (non-student) or January 1, 2002 (student) is accepted | **Deviation D-3c-2** (election made where the IRS does not allow it; wrong amounts on lines 3a/3b; no flag) |
| 4 | Separate returns: only the parent with the greater taxable income may elect; one parent per child (`i8814.pdf p. 1–2`) | Statement path follows the MFS dependent assignment (`claimedByMfs`), not taxable income — advisory `FORM8814_MFS_ELECTION_REVIEW` only. The hand-entry path ignores the leg's dependent list (`TRCS:24475-24520`; entries are household-wide, `ChildInterestDividendsMapper.java:86`), so a hand-entered child is built on **both** MFS legs and line 9 lands on both spouses' lines 3a/3b | **Deviation D-3c-3** (double reporting on MFS; advisory only) |
| 5 | Child files own return → no Form 8814 (`i8814.pdf p. 1`) | Children with an active dependent's-own return are skipped (`TRCS:24362-24371`); no Form 8814 on the child's own return (`TRCS:3641-3642`) | Match |
| 6 | Joint return of the child, estimated payments, withholding, prior-year overpayment applied block the election (`i8814.pdf p. 1`) | Blocking flags from the hand checkboxes (`TRCS:24555-24590`) | Match (statement withholding: D-3c-2) |
| 7 | $13,500 gross income limit (`i8814.pdf p. 1`) | Non-overrideable blocker (`TRCS:24619-24638`) | Match for the limit (handling: D-3c-1) |
| 8 | Line 9 = (line 4 − $2,700) × line 2b ÷ line 4 (`i8814.pdf p. 3–4`) | Same; line 7 kept to 10 decimals (`TRCS:24673-24706`) | Match (Fred example gives $78) |
| 9 | "Enter "Form 8814" and this amount on the dotted line next to those lines" (`i8814.pdf p. 4`) | No write-in produced; the 2025 Form 1040 line 3c checkboxes carry the disclosure | Unverified (the 2025 Form 1040 is not in `docs\books`, so whether a dotted line still exists cannot be checked) |
