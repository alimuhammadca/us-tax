IRS rules: `C:\us-tax\new_lines\4b.md`. Flowchart: `C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\4b.md`. Gross amount: `new_knowledge\4a.md`;
checkboxes: `new_knowledge\4c.md`. Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` =

## 1. Status

Line 4b is computed per person (taxpayer, and spouse unless the return is married filing separately) from the IRA
Forms 1099-R (IRA/SEP/SIMPLE box checked, recipient TIN matched to the person) and the "IRA income" personal form.
Without a Form 8606 trigger it is box 2a less the typed rollover, the QCD (age-70½ tested, reduced by post-70½
deductible contributions, capped at $108,000) and the HFD, floored at 0. With a trigger (basis, Roth conversion, Roth
distribution other than all-code-Q, returned contribution / recharacterization, any code J or T) a Form 8606 is built
and its lines 15c + 18 + 25c **replace** that figure. Earnings on an excess contribution withdrawn by the due date and
Form 4852 IRA substitutes are added afterwards. Several blocking flags protect the Form 8606 inputs.

**12 differences from the IRS rules** (D-4b-1 … D-4b-12). The most serious: on the Form 8606 path an **inherited
traditional IRA distribution disappears from income** (D-4b-1), the **QCD cap and the post-70½ offset are lost**
(D-4b-2), a code-J Roth distribution is **taxed at 0 when the Form 8606 line 19 box is left blank** (D-4b-4), the
"disaster repayment" box reduces this year's taxable amount for a different quantity than Form 8606 line 15b
(D-4b-5), and a **code P** 1099-R (income of 2024) is taxed in 2025 (D-4b-7). All are silent. Person attribution of
1099-Rs with a blank TIN is D-4a-1 (same root cause as D-1a-1).

## 4. How it is computed

Methods:

- Load: `TRCS:3110-3111` (`CommunityProperty.split1099R` — IRAs are not split).
- Call: `TRCS:3746-3760` (`computeIraDistributions`, then `augmentIraWithReturnedExcessEarnings`); Form 4852
  `TRCS:3783-3787`.
- Orchestrator `computeIraDistributions` `TRCS:20083-20659`; per person `computeIraForPerson` `TRCS:20906-21318`;
  `buildForm8606` `TRCS:21344-21450`; attribution `belongsToPersonByTinAndFallback` `TRCS:36002-36019`;
  pre-filters `withoutQcdIfUnder70Half` `TRCS:20666-20745`, `withoutBarredIraRollover` `TRCS:20829-20853`.
- Augments: `augmentIraWithReturnedExcessEarnings` `TRCS:46884-46919`, `augmentIraWithForm4852` `TRCS:46922-46950`,
  `extractForm4852RetirementSubstitute` (line 8j routing).
- Output: `buildIncome` `TRCS:18454` (round), `:18662-18664` (set), line 9 `:18571`.

Steps, mirroring `new_lines\4b.md` section 6:

1. **Gather each person's IRA 1099-Rs.** All 1099-Rs of the account are loaded. Per person, an entry counts if
   `iraSepSimple == true` (`TRCS:20938`) and `belongsToPersonByTinAndFallback` says so (`TRCS:20941`): a filled
   recipient TIN must equal that person's SSN; a **blank TIN goes to the taxpayer** unless only the spouse answered Yes
   to "Did you receive money from an IRA" (`TRCS:36015-36018`) — see D-4a-1. On MFS the spouse form and SSN are nulled
   (`TRCS:20113`, `:20135`). A 1099-R with the IRA box neither checked nor unchecked blocks (non-overridable
   `PENSION_1099R_IRA_SEP_SIMPLE_FLAG_NOT_DETERMINED`, `TRCS:23200-23233`, called `:21727`). No code is filtered out —
   codes P, N, R, 8 all count (D-4b-7).
2. **No exception:** the person's taxable figure starts as Σ box 2a (`TRCS:20946`). A blank box 2a adds nothing (no
   box 1 fallback); box 2b counts only for an advisory.
3. **Rollovers:** `totalIraRolloverAmount` is subtracted (`TRCS:21118-21121`). Before that, a rollover received less
   than a year after the previous IRA-to-IRA rollover distribution is removed (`withoutBarredIraRollover`). A code G
   entry is not treated as a rollover on this path (box 2a is 0 there anyway).
4. **QCDs:** if the owner reaches 70½ after 2025, or the typed QCD date is before the 70½ date, the QCD fields are
   removed (`TRCS:20666-20745`). Then `effectiveQcd = max(0, totalQcd − inheritedIraQcd − post70½Deduction) +
   inheritedIraQcd` (`TRCS:21069-21075`) and **then** the $108,000 cap (`TRCS:21084-21090`) — the reverse of the Pub.
   590-B worksheet order, with the inherited part exempt from the offset and only this year's deduction counted
   (D-4b-3). The excess over the cap is taxable with the advisory `QCD_EXCEEDS_ANNUAL_CAP_*`.
5. **HFDs:** `totalHfdAmount` subtracted as typed, no cap (D-4b-9). Result floored at 0 (`subtractNonNegative`).
6. **Form 8606 (Exception 2).** `hasException2` = basis question Yes, conversion Yes, Roth question Yes unless every
   Roth-coded 1099-R is code Q, returned-contribution question Yes, or any code J / T 1099-R (`TRCS:21043-21047`). Then
   `buildForm8606` runs and, if lines 15c + 18 + 25c are not all null, their sum **replaces** the figure of steps 2–5
   (`TRCS:21140-21153`):
   - Line 7 = box 1 of the person's non-Roth IRA 1099-Rs (codes other than J/Q/T, `TRCS:20963-20970`) − max(rollover,
     code G box 1) − **total QCD as typed** − HFD − line 8 − returned contributions − inherited traditional
     distributions (`TRCS:21359-21369`). The QCD cap and offset of step 4 are not used here (D-4b-2), and the inherited
     amount is removed from line 7 and never taxed elsewhere (D-4b-1).
   - Lines 1–6, 8 typed; line 9 = 6 + 7 + 8; line 10 = line 5 ÷ line 9, at most 1.000 (`TRCS:21375-21386`); lines
     11–14; line 15a = 7 − 12; **line 15b = the typed "disaster repayments" amount** (`TRCS:21392`, D-4b-5); line 15c =
     15a − 15b.
   - Part II: line 16 = line 8; line 17 = line 11; line 18 = 16 − 17 (`TRCS:21395-21397`).
   - Part III: line 19 typed (`TRCS:21399` — not taken from the Roth 1099-Rs, D-4b-4); line 20 capped at $10,000;
     lines 21–25c in order, each floored at 0; line 25b typed "disaster repayments" (D-4b-5).
   - Each line is rounded to whole dollars when stored; the taxable sum uses the rounded values.
7. **Roth code Q / T shortcut:** all-code-Q → no Form 8606, Roth box 2a (blank) adds nothing — match. Code T always
   builds a Form 8606; with line 19 blank the Roth part is 0 — the right amount, but an unneeded Form 8606 (D-4b-10).
8. **Disaster distributions:** no Form 8915-F; nothing is spread over three years (D-4b-6).
9. **Add the persons:** each person's figure is rounded (`TRCS:21283`), the two are added and rounded again
   (`TRCS:20587`, `:20606`) (D-4b-11). If nothing meaningful exists (no non-zero 4a/4b, no box, no Form 8606) the whole
   record is null and line 4b is blank (`TRCS:20617-20626`).
10. **After the orchestrator:** earnings on an excess contribution withdrawn by the due date (Income adjustments) are
    added to 4a and 4b (`TRCS:46884-46919`; blocking `IRA_EXCESS_EARNINGS_MAY_REPEAT_1099R_CODE_8` if an IRA code-8
    1099-R is also on file). Form 4852 IRA substitutes add line 8b as typed (`TRCS:46922-46950`), on top of any 1099-R
    on file (D-4a-4).
11. **Output:** `roundMoney` → `Income.taxableIraDistributions` (`TRCS:18454`, `:18662-18664`).
