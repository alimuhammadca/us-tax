# Form 1040 line 6b — how the app does it

IRS rules: `C:\us-tax\new_lines\6b.md`. Flowchart: `C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\6b.md`.
Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`.

---

## 1. Status

Line 6b is computed by one routine that follows **Pub. 915 Worksheet 1** (the 19-line version with the exclusion
line), used for every return: line 6a, half of it, a rebuilt "line 3" income total, line 2a, the exclusion
add-backs (Form 2555 foreign earned income and housing exclusions, Form 8839 line 30 adoption exclusion, territory
exclusions, and the Form 8815 savings-bond exclusion folded into line 3), less the Schedule 1 subset (lines 11–20,
23, 25), then the $25,000 / $32,000 base, the $9,000 / $12,000 band, 50% / 85% tiers and the 85%-of-benefits cap.
Married filing separately and lived with the spouse at any time uses the $0-base 85% path. When a traditional IRA is
deducted and either spouse is covered by a work plan, the Pub. 590-A Appendix B coordination re-figures the IRA
deduction and line 6b together. The lump-sum election is compared afterwards (`6c.md`).

**4 differences from the IRS rules** (D-6b-1 … D-6b-4), plus three inherited from line 1z / 1a (D-1z-1, D-1z-2,
D-1a-9) because the worksheet rebuilds wages instead of reading line 1z. The most serious: the Form 8814 child amount
(Schedule 1 line 8z) is added to line 8 **after** the worksheet ran, so line 6b ignores it (D-6b-1); and the Pub. 590-A
coordination reads "lived with your spouse" without checking that the return is MFS, so a stale answer on a joint or
single return switches the coordinated line 6b to the 85% MFS path (D-6b-2).

## 4. How it is computed

Methods:

- Orchestrator `computeSocialSecurityBenefits` `TRCS:33715-34319` (worksheet inputs `TRCS:33960-34085`).
- Worksheet `computeTaxableSocialSecurityNormal` `TRCS:34824-34964`.
- Schedule 1 subset: `computeIncomeAdjustments` `TRCS:33229-33231`.
- Exclusion add-backs: `computeForm2555ExclusionForSsWorksheet` `TRCS:35854-35869`,
  `computePossessionExclusionForSsWorksheet` `TRCS:35615-35623`, `form8815ExclusionAppliedThisPass` `TRCS:8868-8870`.
- MFS residence answer: `prepare` `TRCS:2740-2815`.
- Pub. 590-A coordination: `TRCS:4484-4704`, `runPub590aCoordination` `TRCS:35513-35612`.
- Output: `buildIncome` `TRCS:18458`, `TRCS:18709-18711`; line 9 `TRCS:18571`.

Order: income lines 1z–8 (except the Form 8814 add-on), then `computeIncomeAdjustments` (`TRCS:4419`), then this
worksheet (`TRCS:4439`), then the Pub. 590-A coordination (`TRCS:4484`).

Steps, mirroring `new_lines/6b.md` section 6 (Pub. 915 numbering; the code's variable names are offset, see
`TRCS:34837-34844`):

1. **Pick the worksheet.** One routine for everyone: Pub. 915 Worksheet 1. Repayments > benefits → line 6a = 0 and
   the worksheet returns 0 (`TRCS:34863-34865`). IRA + work-plan coverage → step 12.
2. **Lines 1–2.** `w1` = line 6a; `w2` = 50% (`TRCS:34867-34868`).
3. **Line 3** (`TRCS:33964-34009`): **rebuilt**, not read from the return —
   - wages: `sumW2WagesAfterCarveOuts` (every W-2 in the account less statutory, box 11 and both spouses' inmate
     wages, **no SSN filter**, no Form 4852) + household wages + `line1c` … `line1h`, rounded once
     (`TRCS:33964-33969`) — D-1a-9, D-1z-1, D-1z-2;
   - plus rounded lines 2b, 3b, 4b, 5b (`TRCS:33971-33974`), line 7a or, if the computed 7a is null, the "override"
     field (`TRCS:33975-33978`), line 8 or, if null, the "override" field (`TRCS:33979-33982`) — D-6b-4;
   - Form 8815: when the taxpayer claims it and an exclusion was applied this pass, the exclusion is added back to
     line 2b (= Schedule B line 2) (`TRCS:33995-34004`);
   - `importedReturnWorksheetLine3IncomeTotal`, if present, replaces the whole line (`TRCS:34006-34009`).
   - Line 8 here is `otherIncome.line8FromSchedule1` **before** Form 8814 line 12 is added to Schedule 1 line 8z and
     Form 1040 line 8 (`TRCS:5088-5144`) — D-6b-1.
4. **Line 4** = rounded line 2a, unless the imported override is present (`TRCS:34010-34013`).
5. **Line 5** (`TRCS:34022-34041`) = Form 2555 foreign earned income exclusion + housing exclusion of the taxpayer
   and (not MFS) the spouse, recomputed from the foreign-income form (`TRCS:35854-35869`); Form 8839 line 30
   (excluded adoption benefits); the possession-residence exclusion of the taxpayer and (not MFS) the spouse
   (`TRCS:35615-35623`, any possession on that form). The Form 2555 **housing deduction** (Schedule 1 line 24j,
   typed on Income adjustments) is not added back — D-6b-3.
6. **Line 6** = lines 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 (`TRCS:34869`).
7. **Line 7** = Schedule 1 lines 11–20 + 23 + 25 (`line10FromSchedule1Pub915Subset`, `TRCS:33229-33231`), or the
   imported override (`TRCS:34058-34060`; advisory when they differ by more than $1, `TRCS:34068-34085`). Line 8 =
   line 6 − line 7, floored at 0; 0 → line 6b = 0 (`TRCS:34870-34873`).
8. **MFS lived with spouse.** `isMfs && livedWithSpouseAnyTime` (`TRCS:34094`) → `min(85% × line 1, 85% × line 8)`
   (`TRCS:34892-34894`). On MFS the filing-status answer, when given, overwrites both Social Security answers; when
   no answer exists anywhere the return is treated as "lived together" and the overrideable blocking flag
   `MFS_LIVED_WITH_SPOUSE_UNANSWERED` is raised (`TRCS:2740-2815`).
9. **Line 9** base: $32,000 if the filing status is "Married filing jointly", else $25,000 (QSS, HOH, single, MFS lived
   apart) (`TRCS:34896-34898`); not above → 0 (`TRCS:34900-34903`).
10. **Lines 10–17** in compact form: `min(50% × line 1, 50% × (line 8 − base))` below $34,000 / $44,000, and
    `min($4,500 / $6,000, 50% × line 1) + 85% × (line 8 − $34,000 / $44,000)` above (`TRCS:34951-34962`). The
    comment at `TRCS:34905-34950` shows this equals the IRS lines in every reachable case (checked: within the band
    line 13 = line 10; above it line 13 = line 11).
11. **Lines 18–19.** `min(85% × line 1, line 17)` (`TRCS:34963`). The orchestrator forces 0 when line 6a exists and
    the result is null, and clamps to `[0, 85% × line 6a]` again (`TRCS:34272-34279`). Intermediate values keep
    cents; the result is rounded half-up (`TRCS:34307-34308`).
12. **Pub. 590-A** (`TRCS:4484-4704`): runs when an IRA deduction is claimed, a work plan covers either spouse and
    taxable benefits exist (or, without benefits, for the phase-out alone). Worksheet 1's taxable figure for the
    MAGI is figured without the IRA deduction (`TRCS:35559-35562`); Worksheet 3's figure with the allowed deduction
    is iterated to a fixed point (`TRCS:35568-35610`). Line 6b and the Schedule 1 subset are replaced only when the
    allowed deduction is smaller than the claimed one (`TRCS:4605-4674`); the lump-sum election is re-run then
    (`TRCS:4638-4658`). The "lived with spouse" flag passed in is the raw Social Security form answer
    (`TRCS:4512`), not `isMfs && …` — D-6b-2.
13. **Lump-sum election** compared afterwards (`TRCS:34097-34186`, see `6c.md`).

## 9. Limits and out of scope

- **Worksheet layout**: one Pub. 915 Worksheet 1 routine for everyone; when no exclusions exist its line 5 is 0 and
  the result equals the Form 1040 instructions worksheet.
- **Imported overrides** for worksheet lines 3 / 4 / 7 have no UI; they exist for imported returns via the API.
- **Rounding**: intermediate worksheet values keep cents; only the result is rounded. The IRS gives no rule for the
  intermediate lines.
- **Base amounts** are hard-coded (not in `ReferenceData`); they are fixed by statute and the worksheet.
