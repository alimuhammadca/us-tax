# Form 1040 line 7a — how the app does it

IRS rules: `C:\us-tax\new_lines\7a.md`. Flowchart: `C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\7a.md`. Checkboxes: `7b.md`.
Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`.
Schedule D, Form 8949, Form 4797, 6252, 6781, 4684, 8824 and 8814 internals are documented with those forms; this
file follows only what reaches line 7a.

---

## 1. Status

Line 7a is computed in one method, `computeCapitalGainLoss` (`TRCS:23515-24288`). It builds Form 8949 rows from
1099-B, 1099-DA, 1099-S (with the home-sale exclusion), hand-entered rows, nonbusiness bad debt and bargain sales;
reads Schedule D lines 4 / 5 / 11 / 12 from Forms 2439, 6781, 6252, 4797, 4684, 8824 and the three K-1s; takes
1099-DIV box 2a for line 13; applies the carryover, Exception 1 (no Schedule D), the $3,000 / $1,500 limit and
the Form 8814 child amount. The carryover from the prior year's return is imported automatically.

**10 differences from the IRS rules** (D-7a-1 … D-7a-10). The most serious, all silent:

- **D-1a-1 repeat for sales (D-7a-1):** a dependent's 1099-B or 1099-DA (child's SSN) is put on the **parent's**
  Form 8949 / line 7a whenever the parent or spouse answered Yes to "capital gain or loss activity" — the 1099-B /
  1099-DA person match falls back to "the filer" for any SSN that is neither spouse's. (1099-DIV box 2a and K-1
  capital gains are matched by SSN and do **not** repeat D-1a-1 / D-2b-1 / D-3b-1; only a blank TIN leaks.)
- **D-7a-2:** capital gain distributions, Form 2439 and K-1 capital gains of a person whose Capital gain/loss form
  was never saved are dropped — always so on a dependent's own return, whose Capital gain/loss form is never read.
- **D-7a-3:** the prior-year carryover import runs on every return of the household, so each married-filing-
  separately return and each dependent's own return also deducts the household's carryover.
- **D-7a-4:** the "total loss deduction taken on last year's return" (prior Schedule D line 21) is used as this
  year's carryover.

The Form 8814 election problems (built without an election, D-3c-1; eligibility, D-3c-2) also change line 7a
through Form 8814 line 10.

## 4. How it is computed

Called from `prepare` at `TRCS:3790-3815` with every statement list of the account (not filtered by person on
Single / MFJ / HOH / QSS) and the Form 8814 line 10 total (`TRCS:3670-3674`).

1. **Prior-year carryover import** (`TRCS:2714-2726`, `:1139-1160`): the 2024 primary return's
   `nextYear…CapitalLossCarryover` is put on the taxpayer's capital form as `importedPriorYear…` with
   `hasCapitalLossCarryoverFromPriorYear = true`. No check of the return kind — it runs on MFS legs and on
   dependents' own returns too (**D-7a-3**).
2. **Gather the person's own capital items** (IRS step 1). `computeCapitalForPerson` (`TRCS:24734-24898`), once for
   the taxpayer and once for the spouse (the spouse side is nulled on MFS, `TRCS:23563-23564`):
   - returns **nothing** when the person did not answer Yes and their capital form is empty/absent, unless a Form
     4797 / 6252 / 4684 / 8824 gain belongs to them (`TRCS:24750-24768`) (**D-7a-2**);
   - 1099-DIV box 2a–2d for entries whose `recipientTIN` is the person's SSN (blank TIN → taxpayer)
     (`TRCS:24774-24782`, `belongsToPerson` `TRCS:36021-36040`); box 2a + hand-entered distributions;
   - Form 2439 (`shareholderTIN`), 6781, 6252, 4797, 4684, 8824 (`identifyingNumber`) and K-1 1041 / 1065 / 1120-S
     (`recipientTIN`) through `aggregateStatementCapitalAmountsForPerson` (`TRCS:25250-25505`): a filled TIN must be
     the person's SSN, a blank one falls to `ownerRole`, then to the taxpayer (`belongsToCapitalPersonByKey`
     `TRCS:25512-25535`). K-1 box 8 / 7 / 3 → line 5, 9a / 8a / 4a → line 12, collectibles and §1250 boxes → the
     worksheets; 6781 line 8 → line 4, line 9 → line 11; 6252 line 26 by holding period; 4797 line 9 (if > 0) →
     line 11; 4684 Section A line 15 → line 11 (always long-term, **D-7a-10**); 8824 by holding period. K-1 1041
     box 11 codes C / D are not read (**D-7a-9**);
   - carryovers: typed short / long amounts, else the imported ones, else (`TRCS:23788-23822`) the typed prior-year
     line 21 amount (**D-7a-4**);
   - §1033 involuntary-conversion gain → line 4 / 11 (`TRCS:24855-24865`).
3. **Exception 1 test** (IRS step 2) — `TRCS:23865-23893, 23956-23988`. Exception 1 holds only if there is no QOF
   indicator, no sale (screening answer, **any** 1099-B / 1099-DA in the account, line 1a / 8a values or any
   transaction), no loss, no carryover, no Form 2439, no other source (**any** 2439 / 6252 / 4797 / 4684 / 6781 /
   8824 / K-1 statement in the account, or a non-zero line 4 / 5 / 11 / 12), no 1099-DIV box 2b / 2c / 2d of this
   return's people, and no "other adjustment" amount. The account-wide tests (`TRCS:23738-23747`) force Schedule D
   even for a dependent's statements or a K-1 without capital items (**D-7a-8**). A child's Form 8814 box 2b–2d
   share is not tested (**D-7a-7**).
4. **Form 8949 rows** (IRS step 3) — `TRCS:23682-23714`:
   - 1099-B (`buildCapitalTransactionsFrom1099B` `TRCS:24900-24936`) and 1099-DA (`TRCS:25029-25065`): kept when
     `belongsToEitherCapitalPersonByKey` (`TRCS:26252-26278`) says so — the recipient TIN is the taxpayer's or the
     spouse's, **or** (any other or blank TIN) the taxpayer or spouse answered Yes (**D-7a-1**). On MFS the other
     spouse's SSN is excluded first. Box from the explicit Form 8949 box, else short / long + basis reported (A/B/D/E,
     G/H/J/K), else dates (`TRCS:26172-26231`); wash sale → code W. Rows of box A / D / G / J without a wash sale or
     QOF are line 1a / 8a totals (`isDirectScheduleDBox` `TRCS:26168`). The 1099-B "Ordinary" and "Collectibles"
     boxes, box 1f market discount and the 1099-DA "Ordinary" box / box 1h are not read (**D-7a-5**);
   - NUA employer-stock split (`TRCS:24951-25023`), hand-entered rows with code checks (`TRCS:25067-25160`),
     nonbusiness bad debt (box C), 1099-S property rows (`derive1099STransactionsAndEmitFlag` `TRCS:25619-25678`):
     principal residence with the §121 exclusion as code H (`TRCS:25815-25915`, cap $500,000 for any joint
     return, else $250,000 — `TRCS:25634-25636`, **D-7a-6**), second-home loss code L, investment property;
     business / rental property → advisory to use Form 4797. An unmatched 1099-S (any in the account) blocks.
5. **Part I / Part II / line 16** (IRS steps 4–6) — `TRCS:23716-23736, 23895-23915`: lines 1a / 8a (direct totals
   + typed), 1b / 2 / 3 / 8b / 9 / 10 (Form 8949 by box), line 4 / 5 / 11 / 12 sums, line 13 = distributions −
   nominee, line 6 / 14 carryovers. Line 7 = 1a…5 − 6; line 15 = 8a…13 − 14; line 16 = 7 + 15.
6. **Line 7a** — `TRCS:24025-24077`:
   - Exception 1: line 7a = distributions − nominee (`TRCS:24031`), plus Form 8814 line 10 (`TRCS:24076`);
   - otherwise Form 8814 line 10 is added to line 13 and lines 15 / 16 recomputed (`TRCS:24037-24044`); line 16 < 0 →
     line 21 = min(|line 16|, limit), line 7a = −line 21; else line 7a = line 16 (`TRCS:24045-24053`). Limit $1,500
     when the filing status is "Married filing separately", else $3,000 (`TRCS:24025-24027`;
     `ReferenceData.java:309-310`);
   - rounded to whole dollars (`roundMoney`, `TRCS:24077`).
7. **Schedule D / Form 8949 objects** (`TRCS:24098-24189`) whenever Exception 1 fails; lines 17–22; next-year
   carryover by the Capital Loss Carryover Worksheet (`computeCapitalLossCarryover` `TRCS:25977-26016`), refined
   with taxable income after line 15 is known (`TRCS:6095-6113`).
8. **Null vs zero** (`TRCS:24198-24205`): the whole result is null (line 7a blank) unless line 7a is non-zero, or
   zero with capital activity / a child amount, or Schedule D / Form 8949 / a child amount exists.
9. **Into the return** — `buildIncome` `TRCS:18459` (rounded), `:18719-18724`; line 9 `TRCS:18571`.
