# Form 1040 line 7b — how the app does it

IRS rules: `C:\us-tax\new_lines\7b.md`. Flowchart: `C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\7b.md`. Amount line: `7a.md`.
Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`.

---

## 1. Status

Both line 7b boxes and the entry space are set inside `computeCapitalGainLoss` from the same decisions that make
line 7a. "Schedule D not required" is `true` exactly when the app's Exception 1 test passes (and so no Schedule D is
produced); "Includes child's capital gain or (loss)" is `true` when the total of Form 8814 line 10 over all built
Forms 8814 is more than zero, and the entry space then holds that total. Both follow the IRS wording.

**No deviations of its own.** The boxes inherit the line 7a and Form 8814 differences: the child box can be checked
for a Form 8814 built without an election (D-3c-1) or for a child who is not eligible (D-3c-2); "Schedule D not
required" can be left blank because a statement that is not this return's capital item forces Schedule D
(D-7a-8, also D-7a-1), or checked although a child's 1099-DIV has box 2b / 2c / 2d amounts (D-7a-7).

## 2. Where the user enters it

Nothing is entered for line 7b. It is derived from the line 7a inputs (`7a.md` section 2) and from the Form 8814
inputs (`3c.md` section 2: dependent tab → "Child's int. & dividends", and the child's 1099-DIV box 2a).

## 4. How it is computed

1. **Form 8814 line 10 total** — `TRCS:3670-3674`: Σ `Form8814.line10CapGainDistributions` (set only when non-zero,
   `TRCS:24672-24706`: line 10 = round((line 4 − 2,700) × line 3 ÷ line 4)); `hasChildCapGainsFromForm8814` =
   total > 0. Passed into `computeCapitalGainLoss` (`TRCS:3810-3811`).
2. **Exception 1** — `TRCS:23981-23988` (see `7a.md` step 3).
3. **Values** — `TRCS:24255, 24277-24287`:
   - `line7bScheduleDNotRequired` = `exception1 ? TRUE : FALSE`;
   - `line7bIncludesChildCapitalGainLoss` = `includesChildCapitalGainLoss ? TRUE : FALSE`;
   - `line7bChildAmountFromForm8814Line10` = rounded line 10 total when the child box is true, else null.
   The child amount itself reaches line 7a directly under Exception 1 (`TRCS:24076`) or through Schedule D line 13
   (`TRCS:24037-24044`); the entry space is disclosure only.
4. **Whole result null** when there is no capital output (`TRCS:24198-24205`) — then all three are absent.
5. **Consistency guard** — `LINE7B_INTERNAL_INCONSISTENCY` (blocking, internal) if Exception 1 and the presence of a
   Schedule D disagree (`TRCS:24265-24276`); unreachable since B115 (`scheduleDRequired = !exception1`,
   `TRCS:24098`).
6. **Into the return** — `buildIncome` copies the three values (`TRCS:18719-18724`); they also make the income
   section non-empty (`TRCS:18413-18418`).
