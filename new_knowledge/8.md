# Form 1040 line 8 — how the app does it

IRS rules: `C:\us-tax\new_lines\8.md`. Flowchart: `C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\8.md`.
Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`.
`CP` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/CommunityProperty.java`.
`OI-UI` = `us-tax-ui/src/app/forms/form-other-incomes-taxpayer.component.ts` (the spouse copy is
`form-other-incomes-spouse.component.ts`).

Schedules C, E, F and Forms 4797, 4684, 2555, 461, 172, 982, 8889, 8853 get their own `forms\` files later; here
each appears only as "what produces its Schedule 1 line".

## 1. Status

Schedule 1 Part I is computed in one method, `computeOtherIncomes` (`TRCS:32015-32740`), and Form 1040 line 8 is
its line 10. Every Part I line exists and adds up as the IRS form says (line 9 = 8a…8v + 8z; line 10 = lines 1, 2a,
3–7 and 9). Many lines are fed by other computed forms — Schedule C (3), Form 4797 / 4684 (4), Schedule E and K-1s
(5), Schedule F (6), the NOL limit (8a), Form 982 (8c), Form 2555 (8d), Forms 8853 / 8889 (8e / 8f), Form 461 (8p),
the ABLE ratio (8q), Medicaid waiver (8s), W-2 box 11 (8t), the Employment form (8u) and several automatic 8z items —
and the state-refund worksheet is built in (line 1). The rest (8b, 8g–8o, 8r, 8v, most of 8z, alimony) are typed on
the "Other incomes" form.

**14 differences from the IRS rules** (D-8-1 … D-8-14). The most serious are **statements whose income is never
read**, even though the app promises it is: Form 1099-G box 1 unemployment (D-8-1), Form 1099-C box 2 canceled debt
(D-8-2) and Form W-2G box 1 gambling winnings (D-8-3) do not reach Schedule 1 unless the user types them again —
while the federal tax withheld on those same statements is credited. Form 1099-MISC (D-8-4) and 1099-G boxes 5–7
(D-8-5) are never read either. A typed taxable refund is added on top of the 1099-G refund (D-8-6). The D-1a-1 root
cause repeats for 1099-G box 2, K-1s and Form 1098 box 4 (D-8-8). Earlier findings that land on line 8 and are not
repeated here: D-1a-2 and D-1a-9 (8t), D-1a-5 (8u on MFS), D-1d-3 (8s), D-3c-1 … D-3c-3 (Form 8814 on 8z), D-1h-8
(negative 8z item for a corrective-distribution loss).

## 4. How it is computed

Order inside `prepare()`:

1. Forms that **inject** amounts into the Other incomes maps before the main method (put-if-absent: a typed value
   wins): Form 8889 lines 16 + 20 → `otherIncomeForm88898f` (`TRCS:2941-2945`, `:10459-10468`); Form 8853 lines 8 +
   12 + 26 → `otherIncomeForm88538e` (`TRCS:2974-2978`, `:10714-10723`); the state refund worksheet →
   `imported1099GLine1TaxableRefunds` (`TRCS:3000-3040`); the Form 1098 box 4 recovery → an 8z row
   (`TRCS:3044-3087`); Form 4797 recapture and the §1231 lookback → `otherGainsLossesLine4` (`TRCS:3203-3233`);
   the Form 8829 line 35 casualty → line 4 negative (`TRCS:4203-4215`); TCJA-modified alimony removed
   (`TRCS:4331-4334`); 1099-Q taxable earnings and the Form 8621 line 16b slice → 8z rows (`TRCS:4307-4320`);
   excess medical reimbursement → 8z row (`TRCS:8610-8637`).
2. `computeOtherIncomes(...)` (`TRCS:4379`, method `TRCS:32015`).
3. `buildIncome` copies line 10 to Form 1040 line 8 (`TRCS:18476-18479`, `:18725-18728`) and adds it into line 9
   (`TRCS:18571`).
4. `applySchedule1AdditionalIncome` (`TRCS:38113`) and then `applyForm8814Line12ToSchedule1` (`TRCS:24714-24731`)
   add Form 8814 line 12 to line 10; the difference is pushed back into Form 1040 lines 8, 9 and 11
   (`TRCS:5085-5125`).

**MFS:** at the top of `computeOtherIncomes` the spouse Other incomes form and the spouse inmate wages are set to
null on a married-filing-separately return (`TRCS:32054-32055`); every Part I line is then taxpayer-only.

Steps, mirroring `new_lines/8.md` section 6:

### 4.0 1099-K entry space
`form1099KPersonalItemsDisclosureAmount` (taxpayer + spouse) is stored on its own field and kept out of lines 9 / 10
(`TRCS:32251-32254`, `:32716`); it alone is enough to produce Schedule 1 (`TRCS:32603`). Match.

### 4.1 Line 1
- `line1 = taxableStateLocalRefundsLine1 (taxpayer) + taxableStateLocalRefundsLine1 (spouse) + effective import`
  (`TRCS:32166`). The **effective import** is `imported1099GLine1TaxableRefunds` if set (the worksheet writes it),
  otherwise the **full** sum of 1099-G box 2 over the household's 1099-Gs (`TRCS:32146-32155`).
- The worksheet (`computeStateLocalRefundTaxablePortion`, `TRCS:9778-9803`) runs only when "Did you itemize
  deductions on your 2024 return?" is answered (`TRCS:3025-3040`). Answer No → $0. Answer Yes → taxable =
  smallest of the refund, the SALT-cap benefit (cap $10,000 / $5,000 2024-MFS) and the 2024 itemized-over-standard
  excess, reduced by a negative 2024 taxable income. The 2024 standard deduction defaults from the 2024 status and
  dates of birth (`priorYearStandardDeduction2024`, `TRCS:9752-9768`); blindness is not known. Unanswered → the full
  box 2 is taxed with advisory `STATE_REFUND_PRIOR_YEAR_ITEMIZING_UNANSWERED` (`TRCS:3015-3022`, `:3331-3340`).
- The worksheet's arithmetic matches IRS lines 1–9 (SALT benefit = IRS lines 2–3; itemized excess = lines 4–8). It
  does not apply the 2024-MFS "spouse itemized → skip lines 5–7" note, and does not detect the nine Pub. 525
  exceptions (box 3 year is stored but not read) (D-8-7).
- A typed `taxableStateLocalRefundsLine1` is **added to** the box 2 amount, not instead of it (D-8-6).
- 1099-G list: on Single / MFJ / HOH all household 1099-Gs count; on MFS the other spouse's are dropped
  (`CP:639-646`, `:957-967`); with MFS community property the refund is split (`CP:609-627`) (D-8-8 for dependents).

### 4.2 Lines 2a / 2b
Sum of both spouses' `alimonyReceivedLine2a` (`TRCS:32167`). A date after 2018-12-31 raises the non-overrideable
blocker `OTHER_INCOME_ALIMONY_POST2018_AGREEMENT_NOT_TAXABLE_*` and drops that side's amount (`TRCS:32180-32191`,
`:33575-33612`); a blank date → advisory `OTHER_INCOME_ALIMONY_DATE_REQUIRED_*`. "Changed after 2018 to adopt the new
rule" = Yes removes the amount earlier (`TRCS:4331-4334`, `:53587`). Line 2b = the date of the larger side; when
both spouses received alimony, advisory `SCHEDULE1_LINE2B_OTHER_AGREEMENT_STATEMENT` asks for the statement
(`TRCS:32196-32210`). Several agreements for **one** person cannot be entered (one amount and one date per person)
— unverified whether that matters in practice; the total can be typed. Match.

### 4.3 Line 3
`scheduleC.netProfitLine3` from `computeScheduleC` (all businesses, both spouses), else the legacy typed field
`businessIncomeLossLine3OutOfScope` (`TRCS:32257-32259`), plus the signed Form 8990 §163(j) adjustment
(`TRCS:32264-32266`). Hobby (§183) activities go to 8j instead. Advisory `SCHEDULE_C_GROSS_RECEIPTS_1099_MISMATCH`
when gross receipts differ from 1099-NEC / 1099-K (`TRCS:32117-32124`). Detail: `forms\sch-c.md`.

### 4.4 Line 4
`otherGainsLossesLine4` of both spouses (`TRCS:32267`), which by then holds the typed amount **plus** the computed
Form 4797 Part II / III recapture and §1231 lookback (`TRCS:3203-3233`; the Business property sales form, Form 6252,
Form 8824 and Form 4684 Section B all feed the Form 4797 entries, `TRCS:3155-3158`), minus the Form 8829 line 35
casualty. A typed amount raises advisory `OTHER_INCOME_FORM_4797_REQUIRED_TO_BE_FILED_SEPARATELY`
(`TRCS:32454-32466`). Required-attachment stubs for Form 4797 and Form 4684 are produced whenever line 4 is non-zero
(`TRCS:38137-38170`). The "4797" / "4684" checkboxes are never set on the preview (D-8-14). Detail: `forms\4797.md`.

### 4.5 Line 5
Computed Schedule E net (`rentalScheduleE.netIncome`, which includes the §469 / §465 / §280A limits and passive
K-1s), else the typed `rentalRealEstateRoyaltiesLine5` (`TRCS:32271-32273`); plus nonpassive K-1 ordinary income
(1065 box 1 at-risk-limited, 1120-S box 1, 1041 box 6, `TRCS:31498-31525`) and K-1 portfolio royalties
(`TRCS:3724-3726`), passed in as one amount (`TRCS:4394`, added `TRCS:32275`); plus Form 4835 farm rental
(`TRCS:32277-32279`). K-1s are filtered only to drop the other spouse's on MFS (`TRCS:3832-3834`) (D-8-8).
1099-MISC rents / royalties are not read (D-8-4). Detail: `forms\sch-e.md`.

### 4.6 Line 6
`scheduleF.netFarmProfitLine6`, else the legacy typed `farmIncomeLossLine6OutOfScope` (`TRCS:32282-32284`). Detail:
`forms\sch-f.md`.

### 4.7 Line 7
`line7Gross = unemploymentCompensationLine7 (taxpayer) + (spouse) + imported1099GLine7UnemploymentCompensation`
(`TRCS:32316`), plus the MFS community-property shift (`TRCS:32318-32320`). **No code reads 1099-G box 1**; the
`imported…` key has no writer in the backend or the UI (it is only seeded by tests) (D-8-1). The same-year repayment
`unemploymentRepaymentAdjustment` is subtracted, floor 0 (`TRCS:32334-32343`) and stored for the "repaid" box
(`TRCS:32720`). Earlier-year repayments are not netted (claim-of-right path, `TRCS:15518`). Governmental-program
contributions are not asked (D-8-12).

### 4.8 Line 8a
Typed NOL (both spouses), as a magnitude; when the §172(a)(2)(B) 80% limit binds, `prepare()` re-runs with
`NOL_DEDUCTION_LIMIT_OVERRIDE` and 8a is the smaller amount (`TRCS:32350-32354`); negated. Prior-year carryforward
auto-imports (Form 172 bridge). Detail: `forms\172.md`.

### 4.9 Line 8b
Typed `otherIncomeGambling8b` only (`TRCS:32355`). W-2G box 1 is not read (D-8-3). 8b is passed to Schedule A as the
gambling-loss cap (`TRCS:5070-5071`).

### 4.10 Line 8c
Gross = typed 8c (both) + `imported1099CLine8cCancellationOfDebt` (no writer — D-8-2) (`TRCS:32360`); minus the §108
exclusion from `computeCodExclusionBreakdown` (`TRCS:31946`), which also builds Form 982 (`TRCS:32361-32366`);
floor 0; null when there is no canceled debt (`TRCS:32373-32375`). Detail: `forms\982.md`.

### 4.11 Line 8d
Computed Form 2555 exclusion (FEIE + housing exclusion) of the taxpayer and (not MFS) the spouse; else the typed /
imported 8d (`TRCS:32383-32388`, helper `TRCS:35854-35870`); negated. Detail: `forms\2555.md`.

### 4.12 Lines 8e / 8f
Typed + injected Form 8853 / Form 8889 amounts (`TRCS:32389-32390`). Whenever any of them is non-zero the advisory
`OTHER_INCOME_FORM_8853_…` / `OTHER_INCOME_FORM_8889_REQUIRED_TO_BE_FILED_SEPARATELY` says "This application does not
compute Form 8853 / 8889 … file it separately" — also when the app itself computed the form (D-8-13).

### 4.13 Lines 8g–8l, 8n, 8o, 8r, 8v
Typed, both spouses (`TRCS:32391-32402`, `:32477-32478`, `:32492`, `:32532`); 8j adds the Schedule C hobby income
(`TRCS:32397-32399`). 8l feeds the 24b cap (`TRCS:33045-33057`). §962: an election with 8n / 8o still filled raises
the non-overrideable blocker `OTHER_INCOME_SECTION_962_ELECTION_OUT_OF_SCOPE` (`TRCS:32108-32133`). Match.

### 4.14 Line 8m
Typed; advisory `OTHER_INCOME_OLYMPIC_EXCLUSION_VERIFY_LINE24C` (`TRCS:32467-32476`). Line 24c is **typed** on Income
adjustments; the app only removes it on a second pass when AGI is over the ceiling (`TRCS:33059-33075`). An empty
24c leaves the exempt medals taxed (D-8-10).

### 4.15 Line 8p
Computed: business aggregate = line 3 + line 4 + line 6 + nonpassive K-1 ordinary + nonpassive rental
(`TRCS:32294-32298`); if aggregate + $313,000 / $626,000 MFJ (`TRCS:43683-43688`; `ReferenceData.java:621-622`) is
negative, 8p = that shortfall, with advisory `SECTION_461L_EXCESS_BUSINESS_LOSS_CARRYFORWARD` (`TRCS:32299-32313`);
the typed field is used only when no business data exists (`TRCS:32482-32484`). Detail: `forms\461.md`.

### 4.16 Line 8q
Computed taxable ABLE earnings when 1099-QA statements and the qualified-disability-expenses answer exist
(`computeAbleTaxableEarnings`, `TRCS:8439-8478`); else the typed amount (`TRCS:32488-32491`). With a 1099-QA on file
and the expenses question blank, 8q is empty, no flag (D-8-11).

### 4.17 Lines 8s, 8t, 8u
8s from `MedicaidComputation.schedule1Line8s()` (`TRCS:32493`; see `new_knowledge\1d.md`, D-1d-3). 8t = typed +
`sumW2Box11Nqdc` of every W-2 (`TRCS:32498-32499`, `:41207`; D-1a-2, D-1a-9). 8u = typed + Employment-form inmate
wages (`TRCS:32506`; D-1a-5), with advisories `INMATE_WAGES_DUPLICATE_RISK_*` (`TRCS:32510-32531`).

### 4.18 Line 8z
`buildSchedule1OtherIncomeItems` adds each taxpayer / spouse row with a description and a non-zero amount, prefixed
"Taxpayer: " / "Spouse: " (`TRCS:33623-33656`); automatic rows arrive through step 1 (1098 box 4 recovery, 1099-Q,
Form 8621, excess medical reimbursement). Form 8814 line 12 is added afterwards (`TRCS:24714-24731`; D-3c-1 …
D-3c-3). Negative rows are allowed by the sum but the UI has no prompt for a corrective-distribution loss (D-1h-8).
1099-G box 5 RTAA is not added (D-8-5).

### 4.19 Lines 9, 10 and Form 1040 line 8
Line 9 = null-as-zero sum of 23 sublines (`TRCS:32558-32561`); line 10 = lines 1, 2a, 3–7, 9 (`TRCS:32580`). If no
Part I input is non-zero (and none of the carryforward-preserving cases apply), the whole Schedule 1 Part I is null
(`TRCS:32582-32654`). Each line is rounded half-up to whole dollars for output; line 9 and 10 are rounded from the
unrounded sum (`TRCS:32657-32722`) — the IRS "round only the total" rule. Form 1040 line 8 = line 10, **or**, when
there is no Schedule 1 at all, `manualOtherIncomeLine8` from the Social Security form (`TRCS:18476-18479`) (D-8-9).

## 9. Limits and out of scope

- **Form 1099-K payments** reach Schedule C (gross receipts) through the business form; the top entry space is typed.
- **Form 5471 / Form 8992** are not produced; 8n / 8o are typed totals.
- **Several alimony agreements for one person**: one amount and one date per person; the statement for other
  agreements is prompted only for the two-spouse case.
- **Spouse Other incomes form on a non-joint return** (Single / HOH after a switch from MFJ): only the MFS guard nulls
  it; whether stale spouse data is dropped elsewhere on Single / HOH is unverified.
- **Legacy fields** `businessIncomeLossLine3OutOfScope`, `farmIncomeLossLine6OutOfScope` are fallbacks used only when
  no Schedule C / F was computed; `hasScheduleCBusinessIncomeOutOfScope` / `hasScheduleFFarmIncomeOutOfScope` are
  inert.
- Design: Part I is a three-state value — null (no Schedule 1), 0 (activity netting to zero), signed amount
  (`TRCS:32638-32654`).
