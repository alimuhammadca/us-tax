# Form 1040 line 9 — how the app does it

IRS rules: `C:\us-tax\new_lines\9.md`. Flowchart: `C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\9.md`.
Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`.

---

## 1. Status

Line 9 is computed in one place as the signed sum of the app's lines 1z, 2b, 3b, 4b, 5b, 6b, 7a and 8, with no floor,
the null / 0 / amount three-state rule, and whole-dollar rounding. The base formula matches the form. Any error in
an operand comes from that operand's file (D-1a-* … D-5b-*, and the 6b / 7a / 8 files when written).

What goes wrong happens **after** the sum: line 9 is patched twice later in the pipeline, and a third later change
(line 1f) does not reach it at all:

- the Form 8814 child income is added to line 8 / line 9 / AGI only after every figure that reads line 8, line 9 or
  AGI before the deductions has run (D-9-3);
- the Form 4972 lump sum is taken back out of line 9 with a floor at zero (D-9-2), and the AGI-based figures are not
  redone (D-5b-11);
- the adoption phase-2 change of line 1f never reaches line 9 (D-1f-1).

In addition, an "Other income override" typed on the Social Security form becomes Form 1040 line 8 when Schedule 1
is empty (D-9-1).

**3 differences from the IRS rules** (D-9-1 … D-9-3), all silent.

## 4. How it is computed

Methods: `buildIncome` `TRCS:18226` (called through `buildForm1040` `TRCS:13578`, from `prepareReturn` at `TRCS:4839`);
later patches at `TRCS:5086-5145` (Form 8814) and `TRCS:6059-6094` (Form 4972).

Steps, mirroring `new_lines\9.md` section 6:

1. **Operands.** Each line is rounded half-up to whole dollars: `line1z` (`TRCS:18488`), `line2b`, `line3b`,
   `line4b`, `line5b`, `line6b`, `line7a` (`TRCS:18450-18458`). Line 8 is
   `roundMoney(firstNonNullAmount(otherIncome.line8FromSchedule1(), socialSecurityTaxpayer.manualOtherIncomeLine8))`
   (`TRCS:18476-18479`): Schedule 1 line 10 when Schedule 1 exists, otherwise the typed Social Security override
   (D-9-1). At this point Schedule 1 line 10 does **not** yet include Form 8814 line 12 (step 5).
2. **Add.** `line9 = roundMoney(sumAmounts(line1z, line2b, line3b, line4b, line5b, line6b, line7a, line8))`
   (`TRCS:18571`). `sumAmounts` / `addNonNull` (`TRCS:42432-42451`) skip nulls and keep signs; no floor, so line 9 can
   be negative. Companion lines 1i, 2a, 3a, 4a, 5a, 6a and the line 7b child amount are not operands.
3. **Round.** `roundMoney` (`TRCS:42389-42401`, `HALF_UP` to scale 0 under `WHOLE_DOLLAR`) — a no-op on whole-dollar
   operands. Matches "round off only the total".
4. **Store (null / 0 / amount).** `income.setTotalIncome(line9)` only when not null (`TRCS:18729-18731`). Line 9 is
   null when every operand is null; it is 0 when operands net to 0 or when line 1z was set to 0 because wages were
   answered Yes (`TRCS:18626-18630`, see `1z.md`).
5. **Patch 1 — Form 8814 line 12** (`TRCS:5086-5145`, after deductions and line 15 were first figured). Schedule 1
   line 10 is snapshotted, `applyForm8814Line12ToSchedule1` (`TRCS:24713-24732`) adds Σ Form 8814 line 12 to Schedule
   1 line 8z / 9 / 10, and the difference is added to Form 1040 line 8 (`setOtherIncomeSchedule1`), line 9
   (`addNonNull`, no floor, `TRCS:5116-5120`) and, if an `Adjustments` object exists, to AGI (`TRCS:5122-5131`), then
   line 15 is refigured as AGI − line 14 (`TRCS:5139-5142`). Line 14 itself, Schedule A and everything else computed
   earlier from line 8 / 9 / AGI keep the pre-8814 value (D-9-3).
6. **Patch 2 — Form 4972** (`TRCS:6059-6094`, after line 15 and Schedule 1-A / QBI). `adjustPensionLinesForForm4972`
   lowers line 5b; the same amount is taken off line 9 with `subtractNonNegative` (`TRCS:6071-6074`) — **floored at
   0** (D-9-2) — and off AGI and line 15. The code comment says the AGI-dependent figures "are not re-run"
   (`TRCS:6065-6066`) — D-5b-11.
7. **Not patched — adoption phase 2.** `applyAdoptionCredit` (called `TRCS:6906`) overwrites line 1f at `TRCS:44328`;
   line 1z, line 9 and AGI are not refigured (D-1f-1).

The IRS worksheets that start from line 9 (IRA deduction, student loan interest) and the Social Security worksheet
(same operands) do **not** read the real line 9; they use rebuilt sums (table below, and `new_knowledge\11a.md`
section 4.3).

## 9. Limits and out of scope

- Line 9 is a three-state value (null / 0 / amount) by design (`knowledge/canonical-null-zero-semantic.md`); a return
  with no taxable income line at all shows line 9 blank rather than 0. Not treated as a deviation (display only).
- `roundMoney` rounds −$0.50 to −$1 (half-up away from zero); the operands are already whole, so it never bites here.
