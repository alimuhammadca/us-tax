# Form 172 / NOL — how the app does it

IRS rules: `C:\us-tax\new_lines\forms\172.md`. Flowchart: `C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\forms\172.md`. The line it fills:
`C:\us-tax\new_knowledge\8.md` §4.8 (Schedule 1 line 8a); the excess business loss that joins the carryover:
`forms\461.md`.
Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`.

---

## 1. Status

Three parts are computed at the end of `prepare()`:

1. **The 2025 NOL deduction** (Schedule 1 line 8a): the typed or imported carryforward, limited to 80% of taxable
   income figured without the NOL and QBI deductions; when the limit binds the whole return is computed again with the
   capped amount.
2. **A current-year NOL** (Form 172 Part I): line 1, the nonbusiness add-back (lines 6–9), the capital-loss lines
   (2–5, 15–22), the section 1202 exclusion (17) and the NOL-deduction add-back (23), combined as line 24.
3. **The carryforward to 2026**: unused carried-in NOL + current-year NOL + section 461(l) excess business loss, less
   any Form 982 attribute reduction; persisted in `out_form_172` and **auto-imported** into the next year's line 8a
   (user entry wins).

Carrybacks (farming losses), NOL vintages, and the marital-status rules are not modelled.

**8 differences from the IRS rules** (D-172-1 … D-172-8). The most serious: Part I's nonbusiness lines take only the
standard / itemized deduction and five income items, so most current-year NOLs with IRA / HSA / SE-retirement
deductions or other nonbusiness income come out wrong (D-172-1); a farming loss is always carried forward although the
IRS default is a 2-year carryback unless waived by statement (D-172-4); and the Form 172 preview puts the computed
figures on the wrong printed lines (D-172-5).

## 4. How it is computed

Methods: import `importedPriorYearNolCarryforward` `TRCS:1111-1124`, seeded `TRCS:2327-2340`; line 8a
`TRCS:32346-32354`, output `TRCS:32692`; §172 block `TRCS:7388-7509`; Form 982 reduction `TRCS:55460-55464`.

Steps, mirroring `new_lines\forms\172.md` section 6:

**A. Carryforward import (next-year bridge).** On the primary return only (`SCOPED_FORMS_OVERRIDE` unset), the app
finds last year's primary `tax_return_v2` and reads its Form 172 `nolCarryforwardToNextYear` (`TRCS:1111-1124`). If the
taxpayer's 8a field is blank, the amount is put in it in memory; a typed amount wins (`TRCS:2330-2340`). The spouse's
form is never seeded, and MFS legs (scoped computes) never import (D-172-8).

**B. NOL deduction and the 80% limit (Schedule 1 line 8a).**

1. `nolAvailable` = |taxpayer 8a + spouse 8a| (spouse ignored on MFS) (`TRCS:7395-7397`).
2. `mti` = max(0, AGI + NOL applied − (standard / itemized deduction + line 13b)) — QBI (line 13a) not subtracted
   (`TRCS:7420`). The standard / itemized deduction is the one already figured with the NOL in AGI (D-172-7).
3. `limit` = round(0.80 × mti) (`TRCS:7421-7423`) — applied to the whole amount; there is no pre-2018 part (D-172-3).
4. If `limit < nolAvailable`, `NOL_DEDUCTION_LIMIT_OVERRIDE` = limit and `prepare()` runs again
   (`TRCS:7425-7434`); in that pass line 8a = −min(available, limit) (`TRCS:32350-32354`). Otherwise line 8a =
   −available.
5. No 80% statement is produced (D-172-6).

**C. Part I — current-year NOL** (`TRCS:7436-7480`), run when there is an NOL carried in, an excess business loss, or
line 1 < 0 (`TRCS:7410-7411`):

1. **Line 1** = AGI − standard / itemized deduction, may be negative (`TRCS:7409`). Match.
2. **Lines 2–5** — from the **net** Schedule D line 16 (before the $3,000 limit), else Form 1040 line 7a: a net loss
   is line 4, a net gain is line 5 (`TRCS:7450-7454`). Every capital item is treated as nonbusiness (D-172-2).
3. **Line 6** = standard / itemized deduction only (`TRCS:7455`) — no IRA, HSA, Archer MSA, SEP / SIMPLE / qualified
   plan or alimony-paid deduction; itemized casualty losses from a federally declared disaster and state tax on
   business income are not taken out (D-172-1).
4. **Line 7** = taxable interest + ordinary dividends + taxable IRA + taxable pensions + taxable social security
   (`TRCS:7456-7458`) — other nonbusiness income (for example gambling winnings, prizes, nonbusiness K-1 items not on
   lines 2b / 3b) is missed (D-172-1).
5. **Lines 8, 9** — line 5 + line 7; line 9 = max(0, line 6 − line 8) (`TRCS:7459-7460`).
6. **Lines 10–14** — not computed (business capital items are zero) (D-172-2).
7. **Line 15** = line 4 (`TRCS:7461`).
8. **Lines 16–22** — line 16 = line 4; if no loss and no §1202 exclusion, line 22 = line 15; otherwise line 18 =
   16 − 17, line 19 = min(16, $3,000 / $1,500 MFS), line 20, line 21, line 22 = 15 − 20 (`TRCS:7462-7474`). Match.
9. **Line 17** = §1202 exclusion from the capital-gain forms (taxpayer + spouse, spouse ignored on MFS)
   (`TRCS:7445-7447`).
10. **Line 23** = the NOL applied this pass (`TRCS:7475`).
11. **Line 24** = 1 + 9 + 17 + 21 + 22 + 23; a negative result is the NOL (`TRCS:7477-7479`). Match.

**D. Carryforward** — `remaining` = available − applied; `carryforward` = remaining + excess business loss + current-
year NOL, rounded (`TRCS:7488-7489`), stored with the other Form 172 fields (`TRCS:7490-7507`). The whole Part I NOL is
carried forward — no farming-loss carryback and no waiver statement (D-172-4). Later, a Form 982 §108(b) attribute
reduction lowers `nolCarryforwardToNextYear` first (`TRCS:55460-55464`).

## 5. Flags and advisories

No flag is raised by the §172 block. The related advisory `SECTION_461L_EXCESS_BUSINESS_LOSS_CARRYFORWARD`
(`TRCS:32307-32313`) mentions Form 172. There is no flag for a farming loss, for an NOL vintage, or for the 80%
statement.

| # | IRS rule (source) | What the app does | Result |

| 3 | The base is taxable income "computed without regard to" the NOL deduction — AGI-based deductions follow the higher AGI (`i172.pdf p. 1, 4`) | The standard / itemized deduction from the pass that already deducts the NOL is reused (`TRCS:7401-7402, 7420`); an itemizer's medical floor / charitable limit are not refigured on the higher AGI | **Deviation D-172-7** (80% base slightly off for itemizers with AGI-limited deductions; silent; low) |

## 9. Limits and out of scope

- Estates and trusts (Form 1041), section 965 years — out of scope.
- Form 172 Part II (carryback-year refiguring) is not computed; the preview fields there carry carryforward figures
  (see D-172-5).
- AMT: the ATNOL is a separate typed field and Form 6251 line 2e / 2f are documented with Form 6251.
