IRS rules: `C:\us-tax\new_lines\forms\2106.md`. Flowchart: `C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\forms\2106.md`.
Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`.
`IA-UI` = `us-tax-ui/src/app/forms/form-income-adjustments-taxpayer.component.ts` (spouse copy:

## 1. Status

Form 2106 **Part I is computed, Part II is not, and the form is not produced.** For each person the app takes the
typed Part I amounts (lines 1–5 and the two line 7 reimbursement columns), floors each column at zero, limits meals to
50% (80% with the DOT box), and adds the result to **Schedule 1 line 12** — but only after an eligibility gate: the
person must pick one of the three line 12 groups, a reservist must answer that the travel was more than 100 miles from
home, and a performing artist must pass the two-employer, 10%-of-wages, joint-return and $16,000-AGI tests (the last
on a second compute pass). The vehicle expense (line 1) is a typed figure: Part II (mileage at 70 cents, actual
expenses, depreciation) is not modelled. Impairment-related work expenses are a separate typed amount on the
Standard deductions form that goes straight to Schedule A line 16. A Form 2106 "required attachment" marker is created
when line 12 is not zero; the Form 2106 tax-return preview is always blank.

**6 differences from the IRS rules** (D-2106-1 … D-2106-6). The most serious: the form itself never appears on the
return (D-2106-5); a column A reimbursement larger than the expenses is not added to income (D-2106-1); and the
reservist per diem / mileage caps are not checked (D-2106-2). All are silent.

## 4. How it is computed

Methods:

- `withLine12Eligibility` `TRCS:10298-10353` — called in `prepare` for the taxpayer and (not on MFS) the spouse
  (`TRCS:3589-3594`).
- `form2106Line10` `TRCS:52379-52409` — Part I lines 6–10.
- Performing-artist AGI pass: `TRCS:7533-7548` (`PERFORMING_ARTIST_AGI_OVER_LIMIT`, declared `TRCS:242`).
- Schedule 1 line 12: `TRCS:32857`; output `TRCS:33261`.
- Attachment marker: `buildLine10AttachmentForms` `TRCS:38219-38226`.
- Impairment-related: `buildScheduleA` `TRCS:16708-16745`, total `TRCS:16871`, output `TRCS:16931`.

Steps, mirroring `new_lines/forms/2106.md` section 6:

1. **Who must file.** The app asks the group (`line12ExpenseCategory`). When the person's Form 2106 total is positive
   and no group is chosen, or a reservist / artist test is unanswered, the blocking flag
   `SCHEDULE1_LINE12_ELIGIBILITY_ANSWER_REQUIRED_<side>` is raised and every line 12 key is removed from the map
   (`TRCS:10306-10352`). A failed test removes the amounts with the advisory `SCHEDULE1_LINE12_NOT_ELIGIBLE_<side>`.
   One group per person (D-2106-6).
2. **Lines 1–5.** Read as typed (`TRCS:52383-52389`). Line 1 is not derived from Part II (D-2106-3).
3. **Line 6.** Column A = line 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 (`sumAmounts`); column B = line 5.
4. **Line 7.** The two typed reimbursement columns; no allocation worksheet (the user splits it).
5. **Line 8.** Column A = column A − non-meal reimbursement, column B = meals − meal reimbursement, each floored at 0
   by `subtractNonNegative` (`TRCS:52396`, `:52398`). A column A excess is dropped, not reported on line 1a
   (D-2106-1).
6. **Line 9.** Column B × 0.50, or × 0.80 when `form2106SubjectToDotHoursOfService` is true, rounded half-up to dollars
   (`TRCS:52400-52403`).
7. **Line 10.** Column A + line 9 column B, rounded (`TRCS:52404-52405`). If no Form 2106 field is filled, the legacy
   single amount is used as-is (`TRCS:52392-52394`).
8. **Line 10 → Schedule 1 line 12, eligibility.**
   - Reservist: `reservistTravelOver100Miles` must be true; otherwise the whole amount is refused (`TRCS:10308-10314`).
     The per diem and standard-mileage caps and the "part attributable to" travel more than 100 miles are not checked
     (D-2106-2).
   - Performing artist: `performingArtistTwoEmployers200` true and `performingArtsWagesAmount` entered; line 10 must
     be more than 10% of the arts wages; on MFS the spouses must have lived apart all year; and AGI + the allowed
     artist amount must not exceed $16,000 — tested after pass 1 and, if over, `prepare` is re-run with the deduction
     refused (`TRCS:10315-10331`, `:7533-7548`).
   - Fee-basis official: accepted with no further test.
9. **Schedule 1 line 12** = taxpayer line 10 + spouse line 10 (`TRCS:32857`); the spouse map is null on MFS
   (`TRCS:32782`). Output `Schedule1Adjustments.certainBusinessExpenses` (`TRCS:33261`).
10. **Impairment-related work expenses** (Schedule A line 16). A separate amount on the Standard deductions form: if
    `impairmentDisabilityLimitsWork` is blank → blocking `SCHEDULE_A_IMPAIRMENT_DISABILITY_ANSWER_REQUIRED`; if No →
    refused with `SCHEDULE_A_IMPAIRMENT_NOT_ELIGIBLE`; if Yes → amount less `impairmentExpensesEmployerReimbursed`,
    floored at 0, with the advisory `SCHEDULE_A_IMPAIRMENT_WORK_EXPENSES_ADVISORY` (`TRCS:16710-16745`). No Form 2106
    Part I rules are applied to it and no Form 2106 marker is created for it (D-2106-4).
11. **Part II (lines 11–38).** Not computed (D-2106-3). `ReferenceData.STANDARD_MILEAGE_RATE` ($0.70,
    `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/ReferenceData.java:114-115`) exists but is not used for Form 2106.
12. **Form 2106 itself.** A `RequiredAttachmentForm` "Schedule 1 line 12 is used; attach Form 2106." carrying the
    combined line 12 is created when line 12 is not zero (`TRCS:38219-38226`; persisted by
    `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/output/Form2106AttachmentMapper.java`, form code `form2106`). No
    per-person Form 2106 with its lines exists (D-2106-5).

## 8. Compared with the IRS rules

| # | IRS rule (source) | What the app does | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Only reservists, qualified performing artists, fee-basis officials (line 12) and impairment-related work expenses (Schedule A) (`i2106.pdf p. 1`) | Group required; other employees have no input (`TRCS:10298-10353`) | Match |
| 2 | Line 6 / 8 / 9 / 10 arithmetic; meals 50%, 80% under DOT hours of service (`f2106.pdf p. 1`; `i2106.pdf p. 3`; `p463.pdf p. 15`) | `form2106Line10` (`TRCS:52379-52409`) | Match |
| 3 | Line 8: if line 7 column A is greater than line 6 column A, "report the excess as income on Form 1040 … line 1a" (`f2106.pdf p. 1`) | Column A floored at 0 (`TRCS:52396`); the excess is not added to line 1a or anywhere | **Deviation D-2106-1** (income understated, silent) |
| 4 | Reservist: only the part for travel more than 100 miles from home, "limited to the regular federal per diem rate … and the standard mileage rate … plus any parking fees, ferry fees, and tolls" (`i2106.pdf p. 3`) | One Yes/No for the whole amount (`TRCS:10308-10314`); no per diem, mileage-rate or "part attributable" check; the help text states the cap (`IA-UI:1664`) but nothing enforces it | **Not supported D-2106-2** (silent; over-claim possible) |
| 5 | Part II: line 22 = business miles × $0.70; actual expenses × business-use %; Section D depreciation with §179 / special allowance / §280F limits (`f2106.pdf p. 2`; `i2106.pdf p. 4-8`) | No Part II input or computation; line 1 is a typed total | **Not supported D-2106-3** (trusted entry; no vehicle detail on the return) |
| 6 | Impairment-related work expenses: complete Form 2106 and enter the line 10 part on Schedule A line 16 (`i2106.pdf p. 4`; `p463.pdf p. 51`) | Separate typed amount less a typed employer-reimbursed part (`TRCS:16710-16745`); Form 2106 meals limit and line 7 rules not applied; no Form 2106 marker for this path | **Deviation D-2106-4** (low; meals in the amount deducted at 100%) |
| 7 | Attach Form 2106 — one per person (`i2106.pdf p. 3-4`; `f2106.pdf p. 1` name / SSN) | Only a combined "attach Form 2106" marker; the Form 2106 preview is always blank and listed on every return | **Deviation D-2106-5** (form missing from the return) |
| 8 | Performing artist: two employers, $200 each, expenses > 10% of arts gross income, AGI ≤ $16,000 before the deduction, joint return unless apart, tests per spouse and AGI combined (`i2106.pdf p. 4`) | `TRCS:10315-10331`, `:7533-7548`; AGI test on combined AGI + both spouses' artist amounts | Match. The 10% test compares line 10 (after reimbursements and the 50% meals cut) with the arts wages; the IRS says "allowable business expenses attributable to the performing arts" — whether reimbursed amounts count is **unverified** (the instructions do not say) |
| 9 | Line 10 split by activity: "include the part of the line 10 amount attributable to" the qualifying activity (`i2106.pdf p. 3-4`) | One group per person; the whole Form 2106 total goes to that group; a person in two groups (e.g. reservist and fee-basis official) cannot enter both, and a reservist cannot separate non-qualifying travel | **Not supported D-2106-6** (low; trusted entry) |
| 10 | Married filing separately: each spouse's own Form 2106 | Spouse form null on MFS (`TRCS:32782`); eligibility run for the spouse only when not MFS (`TRCS:3592-3594`) | Match |
| 11 | W-2 box 12 code L reimbursements belong on line 7 (`i2106.pdf p. 3`) | Not read from the W-2; the user types line 7 | Match (typed; no import) |
| 12 | Rounding: whole dollars on the total | Meals rounded, then the total (`TRCS:52403-52405`) | Match |

## 9. Limits and out of scope

- The vehicle (Part II) and depreciation (Section D) are left to the user; Form 4562 for a Form 2106 vehicle is not
  produced.
- Rural mail carriers: no input (they do not file Form 2106, which matches).
- Statutory employees use Schedule C (see `new_knowledge/forms/schedule-c.md`).
- The line 12 detail inside Schedule 1 Part II is documented in `new_knowledge/10.md` (row 19 there: "Match (reservist
  per-diem limit unchecked — Form 2106 file)"; that unchecked limit is D-2106-2 here). The stale "Self-employment
  adjustments — not supported" text on the same Income adjustments form is D-10-15.
