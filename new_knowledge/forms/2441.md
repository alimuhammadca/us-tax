# Form 2441 — how the app does it

IRS rules: `C:\us-tax\new_lines\forms\2441.md`. Flowchart: `C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\forms\2441.md`. Part III and
Form 1040 line 1e are covered in depth in `C:\us-tax\new_knowledge\1e.md` (D-1e-1 … D-1e-11); this file covers Parts
I and II (the credit) and the form as a whole, and refers to the 1e ids instead of repeating them.
Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`.
`CCE` = `us-tax-ui/src/app/forms/form-childcare-expenses.component` (`.html` / `.ts`).

---

## 1. Status

Form 2441 is computed in full: Part I (up to three providers), Part II lines 2–11 (the $3,000 / $6,000 limit, the
earned-income limit, the AGI decimal table, Worksheet A for line 9b, the line 10 Credit Limit Worksheet) and Part III
lines 12–31. The credit goes to Schedule 3 line 2. It is figured in two phases: lines 3–6 (and Part III) inside
`computeDependentCareBenefits` before AGI exists, lines 7–11 in `finalizeForm2441PartII` once AGI and Form 1040
line 18 are known. Married filing separately without the "considered unmarried" answer gets no credit (Part III
still runs). Three non-overrideable blocking flags police the qualifying-person list (family membership, age 13+
not disabled, disabled person resident six months or less).

**9 differences from the IRS rules** (D-2441-1 … D-2441-9), plus the Part III earned-income deviations that also
drive lines 4 / 5 (D-1e-3, D-1e-4, D-1e-5, D-1e-6, D-1e-10, D-1b-5, D-1h-11, D-1z-1). The most serious: a parent with
no W-2 benefits cannot enter self-employment or other non-W-2 earned income at all, because that field only appears
when "employer benefits" is answered Yes, so a self-employed parent's credit is zero (D-2441-1); when the filer has
no W-2 of their own the app uses every W-2 in the account — including a dependent child's — as the filer's earned
income (D-2441-2, the D-1a-1 root cause); and a child who turned 13 during 2025, or a custodial parent's child
released to the other parent on Form 8332, blocks the return with a non-overrideable flag (D-2441-3, D-2441-4).

## 4. How it is computed

Methods:

- Merge (joint return): `mergeChildcareForms` `TRCS:38424-38498`, called `TRCS:2824-2826`.
- Phase 1 (before AGI): `computeDependentCareBenefits` `TRCS:38771-39263`, called `TRCS:3599-3600`. Part I
  `TRCS:38852-38882`; line 2 `TRCS:38884-38901`; line 30 `TRCS:38918`; earned income `TRCS:38956-39073`; lines 27–31
  and 3–6 `TRCS:39123-39135`; line 9b `TRCS:39140-39213`; output `TRCS:39218-39251`; credit gate `TRCS:39259`.
- Phase 2 (after AGI and line 18): `finalizeForm2441PartII` `TRCS:44415-44493`, then `applyChildDependentCareCredit`
  `TRCS:44495-44513`; called `TRCS:6576-6577`, after the foreign tax credit and the excess-APTC repayment are on the
  return, before Form 8880, Schedule R, Form 8863 and Schedule 8812.
- Helpers: `dependentCareApplicablePercentage` `TRCS:42471-42488`; `sumQualifiedExpenses` `TRCS:41694-41707`;
  `countQualifyingPersons` `TRCS:41709-41730`; `validateQualifyingPersons` `TRCS:41776-41900`;
  `computeAgeAtYearEnd` `TRCS:42112-42123`; `sumW2WagesForSsn` `TRCS:41652-41671`.

Steps, mirroring `new_lines\forms\2441.md` section 6:

0. **Joint-return merge.** Providers of both forms are appended; qualifying persons are appended, but a spouse-form
   row whose SSN is already on the taxpayer form is **dropped whole**, with its expenses (`TRCS:38444-38455`,
   D-2441-9); `priorYearQualifiedExpensesPaidThisYear` is summed; the Worksheet A 2024 inputs, deemed-income answers
   and `additionalEarnedIncome*` come from the taxpayer form only (`TRCS:38430, 38462-38471, 38484-38487`; D-1e-10).
1. **Line A.** `marriedFilingSeparatelyException` is copied to the form (`TRCS:38849`). Only two of the three IRS
   tests are asked (D-2441-5). MFS without it → `creditDisallowed` (`TRCS:39259`) and phase 2 returns early, lines
   7–11 stay blank (`TRCS:44426-44429`).
2. **Line B.** Set when either deemed-income answer is Yes (`TRCS:38850`).
3. **Part I.** First three provider rows copied (name, address split from a combined field, TIN, household employee,
   amount) (`TRCS:38852-38882`). Display only: no check that a provider exists, has a TIN, or is not the spouse /
   the child's parent / a dependent / a child under 19 (D-2441-8, same gap as D-1e-9).
4. **Line 2.** First three qualifying-person rows copied (`TRCS:38884-38901`); line 30 sums **all** rows
   (`TRCS:38918`). `countQualifyingPersons` counts rows with an SSN or an amount (`TRCS:41709-41730`) — that count
   chooses $3,000 / $6,000 and $250 / $500. Validation (`TRCS:41776-41900`): an SSN not matching you, your spouse
   (not on MFS) or a dependent → blocking `…_NOT_IN_FAMILY`; age at 31 Dec 2025 of 13 or more and not marked
   disabled → blocking `…_OVER_12_NOT_DISABLED` (D-2441-3: a child who turned 13 in 2025 is blocked); marked
   disabled and `monthsLivedWithTaxpayer` ≤ 6 → blocking `…_NOT_RESIDENT_MORE_THAN_HALF_YEAR`. The residency test
   runs only for persons marked disabled, and there is no custodial-parent concept (D-2441-4). Rows with no SSN are
   not validated. "No" to care expenses empties the list (`TRCS:38811-38814`).
5. **Line 3.** If W-2 box 10 (filer / spouse) > 0: line 3 = line 31 = min(line 29, line 30), with line 27 =
   $3,000 / $6,000, line 28 = line 24 (always 0) + line 25, line 29 = max(0, 27 − 28) (`TRCS:39123-39132`).
   Otherwise line 3 = min(line 30, line 27) (`TRCS:39132`). The switch is box 10 only, so Part III benefits that
   come only from a grace-period carryover (line 13) do not reduce the limit (D-2441-6).
6. **Lines 4 / 5 — earned income.** Line 4 = Part III line 18; line 5 = line 19 on a joint return, else line 4
   (`TRCS:39133-39134`). Lines 18 / 19 (`TRCS:38956-39073`, detailed in `new_knowledge\1e.md` §4 step 6) are: W-2
   box 1 of that SSN less inmate wages, + `additionalEarnedIncome*` (only enterable with benefits = Yes — D-2441-1),
   + line 1c tips, + line 1g, + the earned part of line 1h, then floored by deemed income. Gaps carried over from
   Part III: NQDC box 11 kept and Form 2555 not subtracted (D-1e-3); no self-employment earnings, statutory-employee
   Schedule C, line 1b / 1d, combat-pay election (D-1e-4, D-1b-5); Form 4852 wages missing (D-1z-1); corrective
   distributions and strike pay (D-1h-11); single filer's deemed income (D-1e-5); annual instead of monthly deemed
   test (D-1e-6); spouse-tab answers dropped (D-1e-10). When **neither** the filer's nor the spouse's SSN matches any
   W-2, the filer gets the account's whole W-2 total after carve-outs (`TRCS:38961, 38966-38968`) — a dependent's
   W-2 included (D-2441-2).
7. **Line 6** = smallest of 3, 4, 5 (`TRCS:39135`). Match.
8. **Line 7** = Form 1040 line 11b (same value as line 11a) (`TRCS:44431-44439`). Match.
9. **Line 8** = `dependentCareApplicablePercentage(AGI)`: .35 at ≤ $15,000, minus .01 per started $2,000, floor .20
   (`TRCS:42471-42488`). Reproduces the printed table including the band edges ("not over"). Match.
10. **Line 9a** = line 6 × line 8 (`TRCS:44457`). Match.
11. **Line 9b.** With every Worksheet A input present (line 2 > 0 and 2024 lines 3, 6, AGI, earned income, count;
    spouse earned income on a joint return), the 13-line worksheet runs (`TRCS:39170-39187`; line 5 blank = 0).
    Otherwise the entered expenses capped at this year's $3,000 / $6,000 are multiplied by the **2025** decimal in
    phase 2 (`TRCS:39188-39201`, `:44451-44454`) with the non-blocking `DEPENDENT_CARE_WORKSHEET_A_PARTIAL` — no
    subtraction of the 2024 line 6 base or the 2024 benefits (D-2441-7). The worksheet is not produced as an
    attachment.
12. **Line 9c** = 9a + 9b (`TRCS:44460`). Match.
13. **Line 10** = Form 1040 line 18 (`totalTaxBeforeCredits`) − (Schedule 3 line 1 + line 6l), floor 0
    (`TRCS:44467-44479`). Match (both credits are on the return at that point).
14. **Line 11** = min(9c, 10) (`TRCS:44482`) → `Schedule3NonrefundableCredits.childDependentCareCredit`
    (`TRCS:44511`). Match.
15. **Part III** — see `new_knowledge\1e.md` (line 26 → line 1e). Lines 22 and 24 are hard-coded to No / 0
    (`TRCS:39240-39244`; D-1e-7).
16. **Rounding.** Each output line `roundMoney` (half-up to dollars); line 9a is the product of the rounded line 6 and
    the decimal. Match.

## 9. Limits and out of scope

- A qualifying person "Died" without an SSN, care-provider "LAFCP" / "Tax-Exempt" and the due-diligence statement are
  not modelled; the user types what the form needs.
- Expense apportionment for part-time work or mixed-purpose payments is the user's job (one amount per person).
- The credit is per return; MFS legs each compute their own Form 2441 from their own form.
- Part III details: `new_knowledge\1e.md`.
