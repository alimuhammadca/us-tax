# Form 2555 — how the app does it

IRS rules: `C:\us-tax\new_lines\forms\2555.md`. Flowchart: `C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\forms\2555.md`.
Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`.

---

## 1. Status

Form 2555 is computed per person (taxpayer; spouse on a joint return) from the "Foreign income" personal form, for
**wages only**: qualifying days in 2025 from the test dates, the housing exclusion (Part VI lines 28–33, with the
$39,000 or Notice 2025-16 locality limit), and the foreign earned income exclusion (Part VII lines 37–42, net of the
housing exclusion). The sum goes to Schedule 1 line 8d as a negative amount; Form 1040 line 16 uses the Foreign Earned
Income Tax Worksheet (FEITW); the exclusion is added back to the modified-AGI figures, blocks the ACTC, reduces the
Form 1116 foreign taxes, and drives the AMT FEITW. Foreign pay with no W-2 is put on line 1h first. Not computed:
Part IV beyond wages, the employer-provided ratio (lines 34–36), line 44, Part IX (the housing deduction is typed on
Schedule 1 line 24j). No qualification test is checked by the backend and no Form 2555 flag exists.

**14 differences from the IRS rules** (D-2555-1 … D-2555-14), plus ones already recorded and only referenced: the
MAGI add-backs that miss the housing deduction or the net-of-deduction rule (**D-10-7**, **D-6b-3**, **D-11a-4**,
**D-11a-8**, **D-8815-1**), the spouse's Form 2555 on a primary MFS return (**D-11a-6**), Part IX typed only
(**D-10-16**), Form 2441 earned income not reduced by the exclusion (**D-1e-3**), and foreign-employer pay reaching
line 1h only through this form (**D-1h-14**). The most serious, all silent: a blank wage amount gives the full
$130,000 exclusion (D-2555-1); a bona fide residence with "Continues" (blank end date) counts as 365 days even when it
began during 2025 (D-2555-2); the FEITW skips the "capital gain excess" step (D-2555-7); and the EIC block reads a
self-attested answer instead of the computed Form 2555 (D-2555-9).

## 4. How it is computed

Methods: `populateForm2555Taxpayer` `TRCS:9218`, `populateForm2555Spouse` `TRCS:9258`, `computeForm2555Exclusions`
`TRCS:9305-9398`, `computeQualifyingDaysIn2025` `TRCS:9400-9436`, called `TRCS:5233-5234`; a second copy of the same
arithmetic for the add-backs, `computeForm2555ExclusionForSsWorksheet` `TRCS:35854-35870` (returns FEIE + housing
exclusion); declined-election check `foreignEarnedIncomeExclusionDeclined` `TRCS:35840`; FEITW
`computeForeignEarnedIncomeTaxWorksheet` `TRCS:12432-12520`, chosen at `TRCS:11600-11603`.

Steps, mirroring `new_lines/forms/2555.md` §6:

1. **Gate.** No form unless `hadForeignEarnedIncome` is true and the exclusion was not declined
   (`claimsForeignEarnedIncomeExclusion` = false) (`TRCS:9222-9224`). `foreignEarnedIncomeType` is not read here.
2. **Parts I–III.** Countries, test, dates, employer are copied to the output for printing. Nothing checks the tax home
   / abode, the treaty-country limit for resident aliens, an uninterrupted full tax year, the 330 full days, Cuba or
   U.S. Government pay; lines 6, 8, 11–15, 17–18 are not asked; income for U.S. business days is not separated
   (D-2555-3).
3. **Qualifying days** (`TRCS:9400-9436`). The bona fide dates (else the physical-presence dates) are clipped to
   2025 and counted. **If either date is blank, 365** (`TRCS:9417-9419`) — so a bona fide residence with "Continues"
   counts the whole year whatever the start date (D-2555-2). A period with no day in 2025 gives 0, and 0 is then treated
   as a full year (`TRCS:9333-9335`, `:9359-9360`, `:9368-9369`) (D-2555-2). Unparseable dates → 365.
4. **Part IV / line 27.** Only `foreignWagesAmount` (line 19). Business share, noncash income, allowances and §119
   meals / lodging are not asked (D-2555-11).
5. **Part VI** (`TRCS:9351-9378`), when the housing exclusion is claimed and expenses are entered: line 32 = $20,800 ×
   days ÷ 365 (rounded) — the form prints $56.99 per day; line 29b = $39,000, or the locality's full-year limit,
   prorated × days ÷ 365 — the form prints $106.85 / the Notice's daily column (D-2555-5); line 30 = min(expenses,
   limit); line 33 = line 30 − line 32, floor 0, stored as the housing exclusion. Lines 34–36 (employer-provided ratio
   and cap) are skipped (D-2555-4).
6. **Part VII** (`TRCS:9339-9340`, `:9387-9392`). Line 40 = $130,000 × days ÷ 365 (rounded to dollars; 365+ → full).
   Line 42 = min(line 40, wages − housing exclusion, floor 0). **If `foreignWagesAmount` is blank, line 42 = line 40**
   — the full limit with no income behind it (D-2555-1).
7. **Part VIII.** No line 43 / 44 / 45 field; the add-back helper returns FEIE + housing exclusion, used as line 45.
   Line 44 is never asked or subtracted (D-2555-6).
8. **Schedule 1 line 8d** (`TRCS:32376-32388`). = −(taxpayer's + spouse's computed exclusion; spouse left out on MFS);
   if no computed exclusion, the typed Other-incomes 8d. Required-attachment note "attach Form 2555"
   (`TRCS:38183-38188`).
9. **Part IX.** Not computed; line 24j is the typed `otherAdjustmentHousingDeductionForm2555Line24j` (`TRCS:33147`)
   (**D-10-16**). No carryover.
10. **Line 1h** (`TRCS:39938-39953`, `:40195-40201`). If the wages are marked "not already in income" and the type is
    wages, they are added to Form 1040 line 1h (spouse not on MFS). Unanswered = already in income (**D-1h-14** for the
    "FEC" side).
11. **FEITW** (`TRCS:12432-12520`), whenever a taxpayer or (non-MFS) spouse Form 2555 exists and line 15 > 0:
    line 2a = sum of FEIE (`v45`); the code's `v50` is the **housing exclusion**, not line 50; 2c = max(0, FEIE −
    typed 2b) + housing exclusion; the typed line 24j housing deduction is not in 2a (D-2555-8). Line 3 = line 15 + 2c.
    Line 4 = Form 8615 line 18 if typed, else Schedule D Tax Worksheet, else QDCG Worksheet, else brackets, all on line
    3 — **without** the capital gain excess reduction (D-2555-7; the AMT copy has it, `TRCS:38047-38110`). Line 5 =
    brackets on 2c. Line 6 = max(0, 4 − 5).
12. **Other consumers.** ACTC blocked from the computed form (`computeSchedule8812`, `TRCS:6766-6770`); EIC blocked
    only by the EIC form's own `hasForm2555` answer (`TRCS:51119-51121`) (D-2555-9); Form 1116 foreign tax reduced by
    excluded ÷ wages (`section911ForeignTaxReduction`, `TRCS:56830-56852`); AMT FEITW (`TRCS:37022`, `:38047`); MAGI
    add-backs: IRA (`TRCS:4565-4567`), SALT (`TRCS:5005-5006`), Roth (`TRCS:5253-5257`), NIIT (`TRCS:43562-43575`),
    Schedule 1-A (`TRCS:5603-5646`), Form 8962 (`TRCS:6215-6218`), Form 8880 (`TRCS:6585-6588`), Form 8815
    (`TRCS:7242-7245`), Form 8863 (`TRCS:44234-44253`), social security (`TRCS:34039-34040`) — all use FEIE + housing
    exclusion and none adds a housing deduction (D-10-7, D-6b-3, D-8815-1, D-11a-4, D-11a-8).

## 9. Limits and out of scope

- Self-employed foreign income (Schedule C / F with Form 2555) — blocked on the screen (D-2555-10).
- The first-year special extension (Form 2350), the waiver of time requirements, income received in another year
  than earned, and revocation statements are not produced.
- Form 2555 filing address and the automatic June 15 extension statement are not handled.
- The Foreign Earned Income Tax Worksheet result is not printed as a worksheet; line 16 shows method
  `FOREIGN_EARNED_INCOME`.
