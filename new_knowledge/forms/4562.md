## 1. Status

Depreciation is computed per asset from the "Depreciable assets (Form 4562)" intake (one list per spouse): the
section 179 election (with the heavy-SUV $31,300 cap), the special allowance (100% / 40% by acquisition date and the
40% election), MACRS by the half-year, mid-quarter (applied automatically when the 40% test is met) and mid-month
conventions, ADS, the listed-property 50% rule and the §280F passenger-auto caps. The amounts feed Schedule C line 13
(and line 12 §179 through one aggregate Part I computation shared with Schedule F), Schedule F line 14 and Schedule E
line 18; the Form 6251 line 2l adjustment is figured alongside. The Part I limit (dollar cap, $4,000,000 phase-out,
business-income limit, line 10 carryover in, line 13 carryover out) is computed and the carryover is bridged to next
year. After everything is figured, `Form4562Builder` produces **one Form 4562 per activity** (business, farm, rental)
for the preview, with lines 10, 12–14, 17, 19, 20, 21, 22 and Part V Sections A–B.

Not supported: Part VI amortization, lines 15, 16, 18, 23a/23b, 24c, Section C, disposals during the year, the
unrecovered basis of an automobile after its recovery period, long-production-period / QPP / specified-plant rates,
and the Schedule K-1 section 179 pass-through.

**18 differences from the IRS rules** (D-4562-1 … D-4562-18). The most serious: a building (27.5 / 39-year) used in a
Schedule C business or farm gets the 100% special allowance when its bonus box is ticked (D-4562-1); section 179 is
accepted on assets used 50% or less for business and on buildings (D-4562-2); a passenger car or SUV not also ticked
"Listed property" escapes the 50%-or-less ADS rule (D-4562-3); a 150% DB election on half-year property is depreciated
at 200% DB (D-4562-4); an asset tagged "Other" (or naming no matching activity) is deducted nowhere, silently
(D-4562-10); and K-1 section 179 is never used (D-4562-11). All produce wrong amounts with no flag.

No dependent leak (the D-1a-1 root cause does not apply): depreciation assets are personal forms that exist only for
the taxpayer and the spouse; a dependent's tab has no depreciation form (`us-tax-ui/src/app/shell/shell.component.ts:1006-1012`
lists only capital gain/loss and kiddie income for dependents). The other-spouse case on a married-filing-separately
return is D-4562-17.

## 4. How it is computed

Methods:

- Asset list: `depreciationAssets` `TRCS:27846-27850`; mid-quarter auto-switch `withMidQuarterConventionApplied`
  `TRCS:763-813` (applied to every compute's form map, `TRCS:737-739`); advisories `checkMidQuarterConvention`
  `TRCS:26701-26754`.
- Per asset (business and farm): `assetDepreciationSection179AndBonus` `TRCS:28111-28214`; rental:
  `rentalAssetDepreciation` `TRCS:28029-28104`.
- MACRS: `computeMacrsDepreciation` `TRCS:28501-28568`; table generator `macrsPercentages` `TRCS:28329-28355`; tables
  `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/MacrsTables.java:16-56`; AMT `computeAmtMacrsDepreciation`
  `TRCS:28426-28494`; ADS period `adsRecoveryPeriod` `TRCS:28237-28257`; allowance date `placedInServiceAfterBonusCutover`
  `TRCS:28259-28272`; prior-year allowance rate `ReferenceData.bonusRateForPlacedInService`
  (`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/ReferenceData.java:104-113`); §280F cap `luxuryAutoCap`
  `TRCS:28276-28286`.
- Matching to schedules: `matchAssetsToBusiness` `TRCS:27882-27905`, `matchAssetsToFarm` `TRCS:27484-27503`,
  `rentalAssetsForProperty` `TRCS:27965-28002`.
- Part I aggregate: inside `computeScheduleC`, Phase 2 `TRCS:27076-27128`, allocation `TRCS:27129-27197`
  (businesses) and `TRCS:27346-27360` (farms); call with the line 11 wage figure and line 10 `TRCS:4051-4074`.
- Form output: `Form4562Builder.build` (`F4562B:61-242`), called `TRCS:7675`; Part V `PartV:51-276`.
- Carryover: out `TRCS:27396`, `TRCS:32675-32676`; in (next year) `importedPriorYearSection179Carryover`
  `TRCS:1499-1512`, seeded `TRCS:2491-2503`.

Steps, mirroring `new_lines\forms\4562.md` section 6:

**Part I — section 179.**

1. **Lines 1–5.** `dollarCap` = $2,500,000 (`ReferenceData.SECTION_179_MAX_DEDUCTION`), halved when married filing
   separately; reduced by `totalRequested − $4,000,000` (`TRCS:27100-27106`). `totalRequested` is the sum of the
   **elected** §179 amounts of this return's assets, not the total cost of §179 property, and excludes the other
   spouse's property on MFS; the MFS halving comes before the phase-out (D-4562-13). Lines 1–5 are not printed on the
   form (D-4562-14).
2. **Line 6 / line 26 (i).** Per asset, the election is capped at the asset's business-use basis (the year-placed basis
   for a prior-year asset, `TRCS:28143-28144`), and at $31,300 for a heavy SUV (`TRCS:28150-28152`). It is zeroed for
   listed property used 50% or less (`TRCS:28122-28134`) and for any rental asset (with advisory
   `RENTAL_ASSET_SECTION179_NOT_APPLIED`, `TRCS:28039-28049`). It is **not** refused for a non-listed asset used 50% or
   less, nor for a 27.5 / 39-year building (D-4562-2). A §179 entry on a prior-year asset only reduces its basis
   (`TRCS:28183`).
3. **Lines 8–9.** `line9 = min(totalRequested, dollarCap)` (`TRCS:27108`). Schedule F farms' requests are added to the
   same total (`TRCS:27087-27090`).
4. **Line 10.** Typed `priorYearSection179Carryover` of both spouses (taxpayer only on MFS), else last year's line 13
   seeded by the bridge (`TRCS:2491-2503`, `:4071-4072`).
5. **Line 11.** `bizIncomeCeiling = max(0, Σ Schedule C and F net profit before §179 + W-2 wages)`, then
   `line11 = min(that, dollarCap)` (`TRCS:27107-27109`). The wages are `sumW2WagesBySsn(w2Entries, null)` — raw box 1
   of every W-2 in the account (`TRCS:4063`, D-1z-5). No other actively-conducted trade or business income (for
   example nonpassive K-1 business income) is counted (D-4562-12). The Schedule C net used is after the home office
   deduction and before §179 (`TRCS:27033`).
6. **Line 12.** `allowed179 = min(line9 + carryIn, line11)` (`TRCS:27110`); advisory `SCHEDULE_C_SECTION_179_LIMITED`
   when anything is cut (`TRCS:27113-27127`). The allowed amount is shared among businesses and farms in proportion to
   their elections; the carryover-in is weighted on the first business (`TRCS:27129-27197`, `:27346-27360`) and
   subtracted from each business's net profit (`TRCS:27197`).
7. **Line 13.** `carryOut = line9 + carryIn − allowed` (`TRCS:27111`); stored on `ScheduleCResult.section179Carryover`
   (`TRCS:27396`; with no business or farm, the whole carryover-in carries out, `TRCS:27062-27066`) and persisted on
   Schedule 1 (`TRCS:32675-32676`). Match.

**Part II — special allowance.**

8. **Line 14.** Only when `bonusDepreciationElected` is ticked and the asset was placed in service in 2025
   (`TRCS:28156`): rate 100% when `dateAcquired` (else the placed-in-service date) is after 2025-01-19 and the 40%
   election is not ticked, otherwise 40% (`TRCS:28162-28165`, `:28259-28272`), on the basis after §179. Not ticked = no
   allowance; the IRS default is the reverse and election out is per class (D-4562-9). The business / farm path does
   **not** refuse real property (D-4562-1); the rental path does (`TRCS:28067-28075`). Long-production property (60%),
   specified plants, reuse / recycling property and qualified production property are not modelled (D-4562-16).
   For a prior-year asset whose box is ticked, that year's rate (100% to 2022, 80% 2023, 60% 2024; 50% before
   2017-09-28) is taken off its MACRS basis but is not a 2025 deduction (`TRCS:28166-28170`; `ReferenceData.java:104-113`).
9. **Lines 15 / 16.** Not supported (D-4562-16): a pre-1987 asset is run through the MACRS tables (and gets 0 once
   past the table length).

**Part III — MACRS.**

10. **Recovery year** = 2025 − year placed in service + 1 (`TRCS:28511`).
11. **Real property** (27.5 / 39, and ADS 30 / 40): straight line, year 1 × (12 − month + 0.5) / 12, every later year a
    full 1/period up to `ceil(period) + 1` years (`TRCS:28518-28530`) — no partial final year and no cap at the
    remaining basis (D-4562-7).
12. **Mid-quarter**: the 40% test runs per person over that person's 2025 non-real-property assets, basis after §179
    (`TRCS:818-847`); when met, every such asset is switched to mid-quarter (the entry is kept under
    `conventionEntered`, `TRCS:795-806`); when not met, a 2025 asset typed mid-quarter is switched back to half-year
    (`TRCS:775-793`). Advisories `DEPRECIATION_MID_QUARTER_REQUIRED_*` / `DEPRECIATION_HALF_YEAR_REQUIRED_*`
    (`TRCS:26733-26752`). Table: the published 200% DB 5- / 7-year tables (`MacrsTables.java:35-45`), otherwise
    `macrsPercentages(factor, period, fraction)` with 0.875 / 0.625 / 0.375 / 0.125 (`TRCS:28533-28548`). Match. Listed
    property 50% or less and rental listed property always use half-year (D-4562-18).
13. **Half-year**: straight line / ADS → `straightLineHalfYearRate` (`TRCS:28549-28557`); otherwise the **200% DB /
    15–20-year 150% DB half-year table** whatever the method (`TRCS:28558-28565`) — a `150db` election on 3/5/7/10-year
    property gets the 200% DB rates (D-4562-4). A 25-year asset not set to straight line has no table: advisory
    `DEPRECIATION_MACRS_PENDING` and 0.
14. **ADS**: period from the typed class life, else 30 / 40 for real property, 5 for a passenger auto, else the GDS
    period with advisory `ADS_PERIOD_ASSUMED_GDS` (`TRCS:28237-28257`).
15. **Year of disposal**: no disposal date exists on an asset (`PfDepreciationAsset.java`); a sold asset either gets a
    full year or, if removed, nothing (D-4562-8).

**Part V — listed property.**

16. **50% rule**: only when `isListedProperty` is ticked and business use ≤ 50% (or recaptured earlier): no §179, no
    allowance, ADS straight line half-year (`TRCS:28122-28134`). A passenger auto or heavy SUV without the listed box
    takes the normal path (D-4562-3). The test uses the business/investment percentage, not qualified business use.
17. **§280F cap** for a passenger automobile: cap × business-use % applied to §179 + allowance + MACRS, §179 kept first
    (`TRCS:28191-28207`). The cap is the 2025-vintage table indexed by recovery year only, for every vintage
    (`TRCS:28276-28286`; `ReferenceData.java:118-122`) (D-4562-5). After the table ends the deduction is 0 — the
    unrecovered basis is never deducted (`TRCS:28566`) (D-4562-6).
18. **Rental listed property**: always line 27 (investment use), ADS, no allowance, advisory `RENTAL_LISTED_PROPERTY_ADS`
    (`TRCS:28051-28063`).

**Form output (`Form4562Builder`).**

19. Assets of both spouses' lists are grouped by `activityType` + `activityDescription` (`F4562B:66-81`; blank type
    skipped). On a married-filing-separately primary return the spouse's list is included (D-4562-17).
20. Per group: rental assets → `rentalAssetDepreciation`; others → `assetDepreciationSection179AndBonus`
    (`F4562B:108-120`). Listed assets go to Part V (`F4562B:122-132`); other 2025 assets add the allowance to line 14 and
    the rest to line 19 (GDS row by class, or Section C 20a–20d for the ADS method, `F4562B:133-162`); prior-year assets
    add everything to line 17 (`F4562B:163-165`).
21. Lines 10 / 12 / 13: line 12 is the sum of the per-asset §179; when the aggregate limit cut it, line 12 = this form's
    share and line 13 the rest; line 10 is shown on the first non-rental form (`F4562B:206-228`).
22. Line 22 = 12 + 14 + 17 + 19 + 21 (line 19 there already includes the Section C amounts) (`F4562B:230-231`).
    Lines 1–9 and 11, the "Summary" Part I, columns (d)–(f) of line 19, line 24c, Section C and Part VI are not filled
    (D-4562-14, D-4562-15).
23. An asset whose activity matches no business, farm or property ("Other", or a name that matches nothing) is on a
    Form 4562 here but on no schedule (D-4562-10).

## 9. Limits and out of scope

- **Section C (lines 37–41)** — employer questions about vehicles provided to employees; a self-filed return's
  vehicles are the owner's, so line 35 is answered Yes (`PartV:146-150`, `:268`).
- **Who must file**: a form is printed for any activity with a non-zero line 12 / 14 / 17 / 19 / 21
  (`F4562B:169-171`), including an activity with only prior-year non-listed MACRS, which the IRS does not require
  (`i4562.pdf p. 2`). Harmless.
- **General asset accounts, like-kind exchange carryover basis, converted personal-use property (FMV basis)** —
  no inputs; the user enters a basis.
- **Rev. Proc. 2019-13 safe harbor for capped autos with 100% bonus** — not in the sources and not modelled.
- **Ordering of the §179 business-income limit with the Form 8829 gross-income limit** (Pub. 946) — Pub. 946 is not
  in `docs\books`; the app uses the net after the home office. Unverified.
- **Qualified business use vs business/investment use** for the 50% test — the app uses the business-use
  percentage entered.
