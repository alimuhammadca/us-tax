# Form 461 — how the app does it

IRS rules: `C:\us-tax\new_lines\forms\461.md`. Flowchart: `C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\forms\461.md`. The line it fills:
`C:\us-tax\new_knowledge\8.md` §4.15 (Schedule 1 line 8p); the carryforward: `forms\172.md`.
Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`.

---

## 1. Status

The section 461(l) limit is computed inside the Schedule 1 build: the app adds Schedule 1 lines 3, 4 and 6, the
nonpassive K-1 ordinary business income and the nonpassive rental net, adds the $313,000 / $626,000 threshold, and when
the result is below zero puts the shortfall on Schedule 1 line 8p (positive) with a non-blocking advisory. The amount
is handed to Form 172 and carried to next year as an NOL (that part is in `forms\172.md`). The threshold amounts and
the filing-status rule are correct, wages are left out, and the at-risk and passive rules run first.

**No Form 461 is produced** — there is no output model, preview or PDF; only the line 8p amount exists.

**4 differences from the IRS rules** (D-461-1 … D-461-4): business capital gains (notably a net section 1231 gain on
Form 4797 that flows to Schedule D) are left out, so the excess business loss is overstated (D-461-1); K-1 portfolio
royalties are counted as business income (D-461-2); passive trade-or-business activities (passive K-1s, passive
rentals that are a trade or business, released suspended losses) are left out entirely (D-461-3); and Form 461 itself
is never produced although the IRS requires it to be attached (D-461-4). The out-of-date 2024 thresholds in the 8p help
text are already recorded as **D-8-14**.

## 2. Where the user enters it

There is no Form 461 screen. The inputs are the business forms that produce Schedule 1 lines 3–6:

| Sidebar | Form | Feeds |
|---|---|---|
| Incomes → Business income (Schedule C) | `business-income` | Schedule 1 line 3 |
| Incomes → Farm income (Schedule F) | `farm-income` | Schedule 1 line 6 |
| Statements → Form 4797 / Form 4684 / Form 6252; Other incomes "other gains or losses" | `4797`, `otherGainsLossesLine4` | Schedule 1 line 4 |
| Statements → Schedule K-1 (1065 / 1120-S / 1041) with "materially participated" | K-1 box 1 (1041 box 6), box 7 / 6 royalties | Schedule 1 line 5 part |
| Incomes → Rental income | rental properties (real-estate professional / material participation) | Schedule 1 line 5 part |
| Incomes → Other incomes (taxpayer / spouse) | "Excess business loss add-back (when business losses exceed the annual cap)" — `otherIncomeSection461l8p` (`us-tax-ui/src/app/forms/form-other-incomes-taxpayer.component.ts:1188`) | line 8p **only when no business data exists** |

The help text of that field still gives "$305,000 ($610,000 if married filing jointly in 2025)"
(`form-other-incomes-taxpayer.component.ts:1353`) — D-8-14. No question asks which line 1–8 amounts are not from a
trade or business (Form 461 Part II).

## 4. How it is computed

Method: `computeOtherIncomes` (`TRCS:32020…`), §461(l) block `TRCS:32285-32313`; line 8p choice `TRCS:32479-32484`;
output `TRCS:32707`; threshold `section461lThreshold` `TRCS:43683-43688` with `ReferenceData.SECTION_461L_THRESHOLD_SINGLE`
= 313,000 and `_MFJ` = 626,000 (`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/ReferenceData.java:617-622`);
hand-off to Form 172 `TRCS:7398, 7498`.

Steps, mirroring `new_lines\forms\461.md` section 6:

0. **Ordering.** At-risk limits are applied inside Schedule C / K-1 (`atRiskLimitedK1PartnershipOrdinary`,
   `TRCS:31534-31544`) and the §469 passive engine runs before Schedule 1 (`TRCS:31130-31172`). Match.
1. **Lines 1, 7.** Not applicable.
2. **Line 2.** `line3` = Schedule C net profit or loss (with the Form 8990 §163(j) adjustment) (`TRCS:32257-32266`).
3. **Line 3 (Form 1040 line 7a).** **Not included at all** (`TRCS:32297` sums only lines 3, 4, 6, K-1, rental). Capital
   losses are correctly out, but trade-or-business capital gains — a net §1231 gain from Form 4797 Part I (which goes to
   Schedule D, not line 4), a K-1 §1231 gain, a business capital gain — are lost (D-461-1).
4. **Line 4.** `line4` = `otherGainsLossesLine4`, which by this point holds the typed amount plus the Form 4797 Part II /
   III recapture, the §1231 look-back delta and the Form 8829 casualty (`TRCS:3205-3233, 4206-4215`) (`TRCS:32267`).
5. **Line 5.** Only two parts of Schedule 1 line 5 are used: the **nonpassive** rental net
   (`rentalScheduleE.netIncome − passiveNetForNiit`, `TRCS:32293-32296`) and the parameter `k1OrdinaryBusinessIncome`,
   which is nonpassive K-1 ordinary business income (`sumK1OrdinaryBusinessIncome`, `TRCS:31498-31520`) **plus K-1
   portfolio royalties** (passed as `addNonNull(k1OrdinaryBusinessIncome, k1PortfolioRoyalties)`, `TRCS:4394`) — D-461-2.
   Passive K-1 business income or loss and passive rentals that are a trade or business are left out (D-461-3).
6. **Line 6.** `line6` = Schedule F net farm profit or loss (`TRCS:32282-32284`).
7. **Line 8.** Nothing else is added.
8. **Line 9 / Part II / line 14.** There is no separate Part II. `businessAggregate461l = sumAmounts(line3, line4,
   line6, k1OrdinaryBusinessIncome, nonpassiveRentalForBusinessLoss)` (`TRCS:32297-32298`) stands in for line 14: the
   items treated as non-business are simply never added. Null when none of them exists.
9. **Line 15.** $626,000 only for "Married filing jointly", $313,000 for every other status, including MFS and
   qualifying surviving spouse (`TRCS:43683-43688`). Match.
10. **Line 16.** `form461Line16 = aggregate + threshold`; if negative, `computedExcessBusinessLoss = −line 16` rounded,
    else 0 (`TRCS:32303-32306`). A positive amount raises the advisory (§5).
11. **Schedule 1 line 8p.** When the aggregate exists, line 8p = the computed amount (0 when no excess); otherwise the
    typed `otherIncomeSection461l8p` of taxpayer + spouse (`TRCS:32482-32484`). Stored on
    `Schedule1AdditionalIncome.otherIncomeSection461l` (`TRCS:32707`) and included in line 9 (`TRCS:32558-32560`).
12. **Carry to 2026.** `OtherIncomeComputation.excessBusinessLoss461l` (`TRCS:32737`) is read by the §172 block
    (`TRCS:7398`) and added to `nolCarryforwardToNextYear` (`TRCS:7488-7498`).

Person scope: on MFS the K-1s are filtered to the filer (`TRCS:3833`) and the spouse's Other incomes form is ignored;
on a joint return both spouses' businesses are pooled (one Form 461, as the IRS says).

| 4 | Line 2 Schedule C, line 4 Form 4797 ordinary, line 6 Schedule F (`i461.pdf p. 2`) | `line3`, `line4`, `line6` | Match |

## 9. Limits and out of scope

- Estates, trusts, Form 1041 / 990-T filers — out of scope (individual returns only).
- The AMT refigure of the excess business loss with AMT figures is not done (unverified whether Form 6251 needs it here).
- Whether a §461(l)-disallowed loss is kept out of the QBI computation (Form 8995) was not traced — unverified.
