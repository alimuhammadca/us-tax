## 1. Status

Form 4684 is **figured from two personal forms**, never from a typed copy of the form:

- **Section A** (personal-use property) from the casualty details on the **Standard deductions** form: one casualty
  with up to many items (event 1, always a federally declared disaster), plus any number of further casualties (one
  property each, disaster Yes/No). Lines 2–12 per casualty and lines 13–18 once are figured in `SA` (V379); the
  Schedule A deduction itself is figured by `buildScheduleA` (`TRCS:16552-16670`), which takes `SA`'s lines 13–18 only
  when a casualty **gain** exists. A qualified disaster is decided from three dates.
- **Section B** (business and income-producing property) from the **Business casualties and thefts** form
  (taxpayer / spouse, V381), plus the investment-theft question on Standard deductions and the Form 8829 line 35
  home-office casualty: Part I per property and Part II lines 29–39 in `SB`, routed to Form 4797 lines 3 / 14 (and
  Part III recapture) and Schedule A line 16.
- **Sections C and D** are not supported. The "4684" statement (a typed Form 4684) is dormant: its mapper serves no
  form id, so no entry can exist (§3, §9).

**12 differences from the IRS rules** (D-4684-1 … D-4684-12). The ones that change the tax silently:

- **D-4684-1 (high):** a net qualified disaster loss figured from the casualty details cannot be added to the
  standard deduction — the election is only offered beside a *typed* net qualified disaster loss, and typing one adds
  it a second time. A non-itemizer loses the deduction (or doubles it).
- **D-4684-2 (medium):** only casualty 1 can be a qualified disaster; every further casualty gets $100 and the 10%-of-AGI
  reduction even when it is a qualified disaster.
- **D-4684-3 (medium):** the qualified-disaster test does not ask whether the declaration is a **major disaster** (DR)
  rather than an emergency (EM), nor exclude COVID-19-only declarations, so an emergency declaration inside the date
  window gets the $500 / no-10% treatment.
- **D-4684-4 (medium):** on married filing separately the Section A casualty details and the investment theft are not
  divided between the spouses: the head's return always takes all of them, and the spouse's return takes all of them
  too when she has no Standard deductions form of her own.

The rest are low or cosmetic. Inherited and only referenced: D-7a-10, D-sch-d-1, D-sch-d-2.

## 4. How it is computed

Methods: `SA.events` / `SA.figure` (`SA:85-142`), `SA.withGain` (`SA:151-165`), `SA.outputs` (`SA:185-232`);
`SB.compute` / `SB.property` (`SB:233-308`), `SB.Result.partII` (`SB:143-160`), `withForm4797` (`SB:180-215`),
`withScheduleA` (`SB:218-226`), `merge` (`SB:351-383`); `buildScheduleA` casualty block (`TRCS:16552-16670`),
investment theft (`TRCS:16838-16864`); `isQualifiedDisaster` (`TRCS:53247-53257`); wiring `TRCS:3152-3154`,
`:3235-3237`, `:4203-4226`, `:5035-5036`, `:5074`, `:7717-7719`.

Steps, mirroring `new_lines\forms\4684.md` §6:

**Section A**

1. **Disaster box.** Casualty 1 is always a federally declared disaster (the UI saves `true` without asking,
   `form-standard-deductions.component.ts:1523`); further casualties carry their own Yes/No. The FEMA DR/EM number and
   ZIP are not asked; the preview ticks the box only (`SA:195`) (D-4684-12).
2. **Lines 1–3.** Description (items and events), basis and reimbursement from the fields above. No date acquired or
   location.
3. **Line 4.** Gain = reimbursement − basis when reimbursement > basis (`SA:37-39`). The app cannot tell coverage the
   filer chose not to claim from coverage received, nor defer a gain to the year received (D-4684-6).
4. **Lines 5–7.** Only the decline (line 7) is entered; lines 5–6 are left blank on the preview (D-4684-12).
5. **Lines 8–9.** `min(basis, decline)` (either alone if the other is blank), less reimbursement, not below 0
   (`SA:42-51`; guided path `TRCS:25200-25225`).
6. **Lines 10–12.** Sum of line 9 per casualty; line 11 = $500 when the casualty is qualified, else $100 (`SA:61-67`).
   Only casualty 1 can be qualified (`SA:102`, `:110`) (D-4684-2); "qualified" = `casualtyIsFederallyDeclaredDisaster`
   and `isQualifiedDisaster` — declared 2020-01-01 … 2025-09-02, incident began 2019-12-28 … 2025-07-04, ended by
   2025-08-03 (`TRCS:53247-53257`), with no major-disaster / COVID test (D-4684-3). The instruction's comparison of
   line 10 with the sum of all line 4 gains is not made (D-4684-5).
7. **Line 13.** Sum of the gains of all casualties (`SA:120`).
8. **Line 14 / Worksheet 1-1.** Disaster losses' line 12 + the smaller of the non-disaster losses' line 12 and line 13
   (`SA:121-126`) — Worksheet 1-1 exactly.
9. **Line 15.** Line 13 > line 14 → gain (`SA:128-129`), carried to Schedule D by a synthetic entry
   (`partaLine15SmallerOfDifferenceOrAmountPerInstructions`, `ownerRole` taxpayer, `SA:175-182`) that
   `aggregateStatementCapitalAmountsForPerson` adds to **Schedule D line 11** (`TRCS:25380-25414`) — always long-term
   (D-7a-10); its presence also lets Schedule D be built without a capital form (`TRCS:24761`). Advisory
   `FORM_4684_CASUALTY_GAIN`. Line 13 < line 14 with a qualified casualty → min(line 14 − line 13, the qualified line 12)
   (`SA:131`).
10. **Lines 16–18.** Line 16 = 14 − (13 + 15); line 17 = 10% of AGI; line 18 = max(0, 16 − 17) (`SA:132-134`).
11. **Schedule A (what is actually deducted).** `buildScheduleA` figures the deduction on its own when there is no gain
    (`TRCS:16572-16628`): casualty 1 qualified → `lesser − insurance + items − $500` to the net-qualified-disaster amount
    (Schedule A line 16) and not to line 15 (`TRCS:16575-16591`); otherwise each disaster casualty less its own $100,
    summed, less one 10% of Form 1040 line 11b (`TRCS:16593-16614`, AGI from `TRCS:14891`); non-disaster casualties give
    nothing. When any gain exists, `SA`'s line 18 and line 15 replace these (`TRCS:16632-16636`). The two paths give the
    same numbers without a gain. With no casualty details, a typed "net loss already computed" is used as the final
    line 15 amount with an advisory (`TRCS:16617-16628`).
12. **Net qualified disaster loss on Schedule A line 16 / Form 1040 line 12e.** Typed `netQualifiedDisasterLoss` +
    the figured qualified loss (`TRCS:16669-16670`). Itemizing: in the itemized total (`TRCS:16871`). Not itemizing: added
    to the standard deduction **only** when `electDisasterLossStandardDeductionIncrease` is true (`TRCS:16872`,
    `:14975-14999`), and the UI saves that answer only beside a typed `netQualifiedDisasterLoss`
    (`form-standard-deductions.component.ts:1366`; `.html:1874`). A loss figured from the casualty details therefore
    either never reaches the standard deduction or, if the filer also types it, is counted twice (D-4684-1). Form 6251
    line 2a subtracts the net qualified disaster loss from line 12e (`TRCS:36677-36687`) — matches.
13. **Married filing separately.** The casualty fields are not in the allocator's amount list
    (`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/multireturn/MfsScheduleAAllocator.java:100-131`; only the legacy
    `personalCasualtyAndTheftLoss` is split). The head's leg keeps the whole household form; the spouse's leg receives a
    copy of it when she has no `standard-deductions-spouse` row (`MfsFormScoper.java:804-828`, `:1160-1176`) (D-4684-4).

**Section B**

14. **Part I per property** (`SB:262-308`). Employee-use property is dropped with an advisory (`SB:266-271`). Blocking
    `FORM_4684_BUSINESS_CASUALTY_INCOMPLETE` when the casualty date is missing or not in 2025 (D-4684-9), the acquisition
    date or adjusted basis is missing, or (not destroyed, no gain) an FMV is missing. Line 22 = reimbursement − basis
    (`SB:60-62`); 25 = before − after; 26 = basis if destroyed / stolen, else min(20, 25) (`SB:68-70`); 27 = max(0, 26 − 21)
    (`SB:72-74`); line 28 per casualty (`SB:385-407`). Holding period: long-term when the casualty date is more than one
    year after acquisition (`SB:306`). Column (b)(ii) when "Used in" = investment (`SB:306`).
15. **Investment theft** (Standard deductions): when for profit and above the recovery prospect, added as one
    long-term income-producing, destroyed property with basis = amount and reimbursement = recovery prospect
    (`SB:311-321`); `scheduleADeductions` then removes `investmentTheftLossAmount` from the Schedule A form so it is
    counted once (`SB:129-137`). When not for profit, nothing is deducted (advisory); when unanswered, blocking
    (`TRCS:16842-16856`).
16. **Home-office casualty** (Form 8829 line 35) is folded into line 34 (b)(i) only when line 36 has no long-term gain;
    otherwise it stays an ordinary loss on Schedule 1 line 4 with advisory `FORM_8829_LINE35_CASUALTY_TO_FORM_4684`
    (`SB:173-177`; `TRCS:4203-4226`) (D-4684-7).
17. **Line 33 / Form 4797 Part III.** A long-term gain on a building or equipment with depreciation goes through
    Part III: equipment recapture = min(depreciation, gain) (section 1245); building recapture = 0, with
    min(depreciation, gain) recorded as unrecaptured section 1250 gain for Schedule D line 19 (`SB:77-95`,
    `SB:325-330`) — additional depreciation (bonus / accelerated) on section 1250 property is not recaptured
    (D-4684-8; the line 19 amount is subject to D-sch-d-1). Column (c) = gain − recapture.
18. **Part II** (`SB:143-160`): 30 / 31 = gains − (b)(i) losses held ≤ 1 year; 32 = (b)(ii); 36 = line 33 + other long-term
    gains; 37 = long-term losses; 37 > 36 → 38a = 36 − (b)(i), 38b = (b)(ii); else 39 = 36 − 37. Matches the form.
19. **Routing** (`SB:180-226`): one synthesized Form 4797 entry per owner and Schedule-C use: line 3 (39), line 14
    (31 + 38a), Part III rows and recapture; `Form4797Fields` prints them (`Form4797Fields.java:45-55`, `:125`, `:155`).
    Lines 32 + 38b → one entry with key `partbPartiiLine38bAmountFromLine35BiiIncomeProducingProperty`, which
    `computeForm4684SectionBLine16Loss` adds to Schedule A line 16 (`TRCS:15982-16004`, `:16830`). Form 4797 is always
    used, even where the instructions send line 31 / 38a straight to Schedule 1 line 4 with the "4684" box (D-4684-10).

**Preview**

20. `SA.outputs` builds one `Form4684` per casualty (lines 1–12, lines 13–18 on the first); `SB.merge` adds Section B
    Part I per casualty (four properties per copy) and Part II on the first copy; more than two rows on line 29 / 34
    raise `FORM_4684_SECTION_B_MORE_ROWS` (`SA:185-232`; `SB:351-466`; called at `TRCS:7717-7719` with AGI).
21. **Rounding.** Every printed amount is `roundMoney`; the Schedule A path rounds each input first
    (`TRCS:16562-16564`).

## 9. Limits and out of scope

- **Typed Form 4684 (statement "4684")**: mapper dormant (`Form4684Mapper.java:34-39`), so `computeForm4684SectionALine18Loss`
  (`TRCS:15959-15980`), the legacy Section B keys in `computeForm4684SectionBLine16Loss` and the
  `…SUPERSEDES_GUIDED_ENTRY` advisory only ever see the app's own synthetic entries.
- **Section 1033 postponement** and the **main-home exclusion** are advisory only: the filer is told to leave a
  postponed or excluded gain out (`SA:157-162`; `SB:249-255`); the capital form's separate section 1033 question
  (`TRCS` `computeInvoluntaryConversionRecognizedGain`) is not linked to Form 4684.
- Rev. Proc. 2018-08 safe harbors, corrosive drywall, lump-sum reimbursement allocation, partial MACRS dispositions,
  passive-activity casualties, partnership / S corporation section 179 property: the filer supplies the result.
- Section A on a dependent's own return: the dependent-own scoper seeds only `someoneCanClaimYou`
  (`DependentOwnFormScoper.java:186-192`), so a dependent's casualty cannot be entered.
