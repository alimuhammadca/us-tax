# Form 4835 — how the app does it

IRS rules: `C:\us-tax\new_lines\forms\4835.md`. Flowchart: `C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\forms\4835.md`.
Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`.
`FR-UI` = `us-tax-ui/src/app/forms/form-farm-rental.component.ts`.

---

## 1. Status

Form 4835 is **computed but not produced**. Each farm rental entered on the "Farm rental (Form 4835)" form becomes one
activity: the app adds the taxable Part I amounts (line 7), subtracts the Part II expenses with the §175 conservation
expense capped at 25% of that activity's line 7 (line 31 / 32), caps a loss at the amount at risk (§465, line 34b),
and pools the activity with the rental real estate on the shared §469 passive-loss engine (active-participation
$25,000 allowance, MFS $12,500 / $0, prior-year suspended loss). The farm-rental part of the allowed result is
reported on **Schedule E line 40**, line 7 on **Schedule E line 42**, and line 40 is added to **Schedule 1 line 5**.
No self-employment tax is charged (match). The Form 4835 tax-return preview is always blank: **there is no Form 4835
output** (D-4835-6).

**10 differences from the IRS rules** (D-4835-1 … D-4835-10). The most serious, all silent: a farm rental **without**
active participation still gets the $25,000 allowance when any other rental in the pool has active participation
(D-4835-2, over-deduction); a farm's own prior-year suspended loss is added on top of the imported prior-year total
from the app's 2024 return (D-4835-3, possible double deduction); the conservation-expense excess over 25% is dropped
instead of carried forward (D-4835-4); and a farm's prior-year suspended loss, when released, lands on Schedule E line
26 instead of line 40 (D-4835-1).

## 4. How it is computed

Methods:

- Load and MFS scoping: `TRCS:3874-3877` — `CommunityProperty.shareRentals` first, then
  `computeForm4835Activities(farm-rental-taxpayer, farm-rental-spouse)`; the spouse form is dropped on an MFS return
  unless the community-property split filled the spouse slot.
- Per activity: `collectForm4835Activities` `TRCS:31302-31342`.
- Limits and pooling: the farm loop inside `computeRentalScheduleE` `TRCS:31050-31099`; §469 `compute469`
  `TRCS:30436-30459`; farm slice `TRCS:31157-31167`.
- Output: Schedule 1 line 5 `TRCS:32277-32279`; Schedule E line 40 / 41 `TRCS:31449-31453`; line 42
  `Form2210FGrossIncome.scheduleELine42` (`us-tax-be/.../microservices/Form2210FGrossIncome.java:102-110`, called
  `TRCS:7628-7629`).

Steps, mirroring `new_lines/forms/4835.md` section 6:

1. **Line A.** `activeParticipation` is copied to the activity map (`TRCS:31336`). In the pool, any activity with it
   true sets one flag `anyActiveParticipation` for the whole pool (`TRCS:31078`) (D-4835-2).
2. **Lines 1–7.** `gross` = `incomeFromProduction` + `coopDistributionsTaxable` + `agProgramPaymentsTaxable` +
   `cccLoansReported` + `cccLoansForfeitedTaxable` + `cropInsuranceTaxable` + `cropInsurancePriorYearDeferred` +
   `otherIncome`, rounded (`TRCS:31306-31314`). The "a" columns (2a, 3a, 4b, 5a) and the 5c election are stored but
   not read — matching the form, whose line 7 adds only the right column. (The method's comment says line 5d is "not
   modeled"; the code does add it.) The UI does not check that a "b" column was filled when its "a" column was.
3. **Lines 8–31.** `expenses` = the 24 expense fields + every `otherExpenses[].amount` (`TRCS:31315-31326`). No
   line 30g (§263A) subtraction is possible (D-4835-9).
4. **Line 10 limit.** `conservationCap` = 25% (`ReferenceData.SECTION_175_CONSERVATION_RATE`) × this activity's
   `gross`; the excess over the cap is taken back out of expenses (`TRCS:31327-31332`). The excess is not stored or
   carried forward and no prior-year excess can be entered (D-4835-4); the cap base is this one activity's line 7,
   not all gross income from farming (D-4835-5).
5. **Line 32.** `net` = `gross` − (expenses − disallowed conservation) (`TRCS:31332`), kept with `gross`,
   `amountAtRisk`, `priorYearSuspendedPassiveLoss` and the description (`TRCS:31333-31340`).
6. **Line 34a / 34b — at risk.** In the pool loop a loss larger than `amountAtRisk` is capped at it; the excess is
   added to the shared at-risk suspended total and to the Form 6198 accumulators (`TRCS:31052-31063`). Blank amount
   at risk = 34a (no cap). The comment at `TRCS:31290-31292` ("no §465 at-risk input is captured") is stale. A
   prior-year §465 carryover reaches a farm rental only as the form-level rental amount (the bridge from the app's
   2024 return, `TRCS:2364-2380`), and only when the farm rental is the filer's **only** §465 activity
   (`TRCS:30591-30613`, released against the farm's headroom at `TRCS:31092-31096`); the farm form has no field for
   it and the Rental form shows only per-property fields (D-4835-7).
7. **Form 8582 — passive pooling.** The (capped) net joins the rental real estate pool: income adds to
   `passiveIncome` and `farmPassiveIncome`, a loss to `passiveLoss` and `farmPassiveLoss` (`TRCS:31069-31077`). The
   activity's `priorYearSuspendedPassiveLoss` is added to the **rental** prior-suspended total
   (`TRCS:31082-31084`). The farm is always passive — there is no real-estate-professional path for it (D-4835-8).
   `compute469` then applies passive income first to non-rental passive losses, then to the rental pool, then the
   allowance (`passiveRentalAllowance`, `TRCS:31686-31694`), or releases everything on a qualifying disposition of a
   rental property. The allowance cap is $25,000 / phase-out from $100,000; on MFS $12,500 / $50,000 when the
   filer lived apart all year, else $0 (`TRCS:3893-3906`). The MAGI used is the rebuilt `rentalModifiedAgi`
   (`TRCS:3821-3827`) — see D-11a-3 / D-1z-4 (already recorded; not repeated here).
   The import of last year's total suspended loss (`importedPriorYearRentalPassiveLoss`, `TRCS:1267-1281`) is
   skipped only when a **rental property** has its own prior-suspended entry (`TRCS:31116-31118`); a farm's entry
   does not set that switch (D-4835-3).
8. **Line 34c / Schedule E line 40.** The allowed rental-pool loss (`incomeToRental + allowanceUsed`) is shared
   between rental real estate and farm rental in proportion to `farmPassiveLoss` / `rentalLoss`; `rentalLoss`
   includes every prior-year suspended loss but `farmPassiveLoss` holds only this year's farm loss
   (`TRCS:31162-31167`). `farmRentalNetForLine40` = farm income − farm share of the allowed loss. The farm's own
   prior-year loss, when allowed, is therefore credited to Schedule E line 26 instead of line 40 (D-4835-1). The
   rest of the pooled result becomes `netIncome` (line 26) (`TRCS:31171-31172`).
9. **Schedule E and Schedule 1.** `computeScheduleEOutput` writes `line40_net_farm_rental_income_or_loss` and adds it
   to line 41 (`TRCS:31449-31453`); `computeOtherIncomes` adds `farmRentalNetForLine40` to Schedule 1 line 5
   (`TRCS:32277-32279`). Line 42 = Σ activity `gross` + K-1 farming codes (`Form2210FGrossIncome.java:102-110`).
10. **Other consumers.** Form 8960: `passiveNetForNiit + farmRentalNetForLine40` (`TRCS:5529-5530`). Form 2210-F:
    activity `gross` is gross farming income; the line-40 net is swapped for the gross in total gross income
    (`Form2210FGrossIncome.java:88-100`, called `TRCS:7144-7146`). Carryforwards leave on Schedule 1 as
    `rentalPassiveLossCarryforward` and `rentalAtRiskCarryforward` (shared with rentals, `TRCS:32663-32668`).
    Schedule SE, QBI (Form 8995) and Form 8990 never read the farm rental (D-4835-10 for QBI).

## 8. Compared with the IRS rules

| # | IRS rule (source) | What the app does | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Form 4835 for a non-materially-participating crop/livestock-share landlord; not subject to SE tax (`f4835.pdf p. 1–2`; `i1040sse.pdf p. 4`) | Separate intake; never read by Schedule SE | Match |
| 2 | Line 7 = right column of lines 1–6 (`f4835.pdf p. 1`) | Sum of 1, 2b, 3b, 4a, 4c, 5b, 5d, 6 (`TRCS:31306-31314`) | Match |
| 3 | Line 31 = lines 8–30f less 30g (§263A) (`f4835.pdf p. 3`) | Sum of 24 lines + other expenses (≥ 0); no 30g | **Not supported D-4835-9** (silent; workaround: reduce the expense lines) |
| 4 | Line 10 limited to 25% of gross income from farming; excess carried forward (`f4835.pdf p. 3`; `i1040sf.pdf p. 6`) | Cap applied (`TRCS:31327-31332`); excess discarded, no carry-in field | **Not supported D-4835-4** (deduction lost) |
| 5 | The 25% base is gross income **from farming** (all farming) (`i1040sf.pdf p. 6`) | Base = this activity's line 7 only | **Deviation D-4835-5** (cap too low when the filer also has Schedule F or other farm rentals) |
| 6 | Line 32 income → Schedule E line 40; loss → line 34 (`f4835.pdf p. 1`) | `farmRentalNetForLine40` → line 40 / 41 → Schedule 1 line 5 | Match |
| 7 | Box 34b: Form 6198 before Form 8582 (`f4835.pdf p. 1, 3`) | Loss capped at `amountAtRisk` before pooling (`TRCS:31052-31063`) | Match (Form 6198 detail in `forms\6198.md`) |

| 9 | Loss through Form 8582; active participation → $25,000 allowance; MFS $12,500 / $0 (`f4835.pdf p. 3`; `i8582.pdf p. 1, 4`) | Pooled with rental real estate; allowance and MFS figures applied (`TRCS:3893-3906`) | Match for a farm rental with active participation |
| 10 | Without active participation the loss is "All Other Passive" — no special allowance (`i8582.pdf p. 1, 4`) | The pool's allowance applies to all rental + farm losses as soon as **any** activity has active participation (`TRCS:31078`, `:30440-30441`). | 11 | Prior-year unallowed loss of this activity: allowed part reduces line 32 ("PAL") or goes on line 34c (`f4835.pdf p. 3`; `i8582.pdf p. 14`) | Added to the rental prior total; the allowed share is apportioned only by current-year farm loss, so it lands on Schedule E line 26. | 12 | One prior-year unallowed loss per activity, counted once | The farm's own entry is added **and** last year's imported total (which already contains it) unless a rental property has an entry (`TRCS:31082-31084`, `:31116-31118`). | 13 | Real estate professional who materially participated: no Form 8582 limit; Schedule E line 43 (`f4835.pdf p. 3`) | Farm rental always passive | **Not supported D-4835-8** (silent, loss suspended; rare) |
| 14 | Form 4835 is attached to the return (`f4835.pdf p. 1`) | No Form 4835 output; preview blank | **Not supported D-4835-6** (form missing from the return package; amounts correct elsewhere) |
| 15 | Line 7 → Schedule E line 42 (`f4835.pdf p. 1`; `i1040se.pdf p. 11`) | Line 42 = Σ gross + K-1 codes | Match |
| 16 | Crop insurance deferral (5c) and 5d (`f4835.pdf p. 2–3`) | 5b and 5d read as entered; 5c stored only, nothing carries the deferred amount to 2026 | Match (5d is typed by the user next year) |

## 9. Limits and out of scope

- **Schedule J** (farm income averaging) is out of scope app-wide.
- **Form 8990** (§163(j)) does not see farm-rental interest (lines 19a/19b); unverified whether any 4835 filer would be
  subject to it.
- **AMT**: the farm rental has no AMT depreciation adjustment (`TRCS:31072`); depreciation is a typed amount, and the
  depreciation-asset form has no Form 4835 activity type.
- Only the "b" (taxable) columns count; a user who fills only 2a / 3a / 5a gets no income from them and no warning.
- The UI label "Add other expense (line 32)" names the old line; on the 2025 form other expenses are lines 30a–30f
  (cosmetic).
- `hasFarmRental` is not read by compute; the UI sends an empty list on No (`FR-UI:298`), so this is harmless.
