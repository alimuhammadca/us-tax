# Form 4972 — how the app does it

IRS rules: `C:\us-tax\new_lines\forms\4972.md`. Flowchart: `C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\forms\4972.md`.
Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`.

---

## 1. Status

Form 4972 is computed per person (taxpayer, and the spouse on a joint return) from the "Lump-sum distributions"
personal form and one picked Form 1099-R: the Part I gate, Part II (20% of box 3), Part III (lines 8–29 with the
1986 Tax Rate Schedule, the minimum distribution allowance and the annuity lines 20–22 / 26–28), and line 30. Line 30
of both spouses is added to Form 1040 line 16 with box 2 (`TaxAndCredits.box2Form4972Tax`). After line 15 is final,
the distribution is taken back out of lines 5a / 5b and the same taxable amount is subtracted from line 9, AGI and
line 15. Federal estate tax (line 18) is not asked.

**9 differences from the IRS rules** (D-4972-1 … D-4972-9), plus three already recorded elsewhere and only referenced
here: line 5a keeps box 1 − box 3 under Part II alone (**D-5a-4**), AGI-based figures are made with the lump sum still
in AGI (**D-5b-11**), and the removal floors line 9 / AGI at zero (**D-9-2**). The most serious: a shared
(multiple-recipient) distribution is scaled the wrong way — the tax is figured on a fraction of the recipient's own
share and the rest of that share stays on line 5b and is taxed again (D-4972-2); and the Part I birth-date / 5-year
test is an OR in the backend while the screen requires both, so a participant born after January 1, 1936 who
answers "5 years: Yes" gets the special tax (D-4972-1). Both are silent.

## 4. How it is computed

Methods: `computeForm4972Taxpayer` `TRCS:13299`, `computeForm4972Spouse` `TRCS:13318`, `computeForm4972`
`TRCS:13346-13577`, tax table `us-tax-be/.../microservices/Form4972TaxTable.java`, constants
`ReferenceData.java:558-566`, removal `adjustPensionLinesForForm4972` `TRCS:52022-52082`, cascade `TRCS:6059-6094`,
line 16 box 2 `TRCS:11805-11814`, `:11926`; call site `TRCS:6180-6181` (spouse dropped on MFS).

Order: Form 4972 runs **after** line 15 is final (`TRCS:6059`); the pension lines were built earlier with the whole
distribution in them.

1. **Gate.** Nothing happens unless `electsForm4972` (spouse: `spouseElectsForm4972`) is true (`TRCS:13307`, `:13326`).
2. **Part I** (`TRCS:13362-13390`). Eligible = Q1 Yes **and** Q2 not Yes **and** (Q3 Yes **or** Q4 Yes **or** QDRO payee
   with participant born before 1936) **and** Q5 not Yes **and** Q6 not Yes. Q4 alone (5 years) satisfies the gate even
   when Q3 says the participant was born after January 1, 1936; no date of birth is compared
   (`ReferenceData.FORM4972_BIRTH_CUTOFF_DATE` is read nowhere) (D-4972-1). Ineligible → non-blocking
   `FORM4972_TAXPAYER_INELIGIBLE` / `_SPOUSE_INELIGIBLE`, no form; the distribution stays on lines 5a / 5b (correct).
3. **Source amounts** (`TRCS:13393-13421`). The picked 1099-R is looked up in the **whole household's** 1099-R list
   (`form1099REntries`, `TRCS:3110`); if not found, the saved `source*` copies are used. Box 8 = typed
   `currentActuarialValue`.
4. **Multiple recipients** (`TRCS:13425-13431`). If `multipleRecipientPct` is between 0 and 100, **box 2a and box 3 are
   multiplied** by the percentage. The IRS divides line 8 by the percentage, leaves box 3 alone and multiplies line 29
   by it; box 8 is not touched; no "MRD" step (D-4972-2).
5. **No taxable amount** (`TRCS:13433-13441`). Box 2a not positive → non-blocking `FORM4972_*_NO_STATEMENT`, no form
   (D-4972-8).
6. **Part II** (`TRCS:13467-13473`). If `usePartII` and box 3 > 0: line 6 = box 3; line 7 = 20% (rounded). No NUA
   Worksheet capital-gain share, no Death Benefit Worksheet, no estate-tax reduction (D-4972-3, D-4972-4, D-4972-5).
7. **Part III** (`TRCS:13476-13565`), if `usePartIII`:
   - line 8 = box 2a − box 3 (when Part II was applied) or box 2a; + full box 6 if the NUA election is on (`:13487`) —
     with Part II the IRS adds only the NUA Worksheet line F (D-4972-3);
   - line 9 = typed death benefit (no $5,000 cap, no date test, no Part II split — D-4972-4); line 10 = line 8 − line 9;
   - line 11 = box 8; line 12 = line 10 + line 11;
   - line 13 = min(50% × line 12, $10,000); line 14 = max(0, line 12 − $20,000); line 15 = 20%; line 16 (MDA) =
     max(0, line 13 − line 15) — the floor also gives the "$70,000 or more" skip; line 17 = line 12 − MDA;
   - line 18 not asked; line 19 = line 17 (D-4972-5);
   - line 23 = 10%; line 24 = schedule tax **rounded to whole dollars**; line 25 = × 10 (`:13520-13521`);
   - lines 20–22, 26–28 only when line 11 > 0: ratio kept to 10 places, line 21 = MDA × ratio, line 22, line 26, line 27
     (rounded), line 28 = × 10; line 29 = max(0, line 25 − line 28).
   - Tax Rate Schedule: `Form4972TaxTable.BRACKETS` matches `f4972.pdf p. 4` bracket for bracket; the bracket is
     picked on the amount rounded to dollars, the excess uses cents.
8. **Line 30** = line 7 + line 29 (`TRCS:13567-13571`).
9. **Remove from lines 5a / 5b** (`adjustPensionLinesForForm4972`, `TRCS:52022-52082`). Part III → subtract box 1 from
   line 5a and box 2a from line 5b (and box 6 if the pension form put NUA on 5b); Part II only → subtract box 3 from both
   lines (D-5a-4). Line 5a is blanked when it reaches 0 or equals 5b. The box 2a used is the **scaled** one of step 4
   (D-4972-2). The spouse's form is used here even on an MFS return (D-4972-7).
10. **Cascade** (`TRCS:6070-6094`). The actual reduction of line 5b is subtracted from line 9, AGI (11a / 11b) and line
    15 with a floor of 0 (D-9-2). Figures already made from AGI are not redone (D-5b-11).
11. **Line 16** (`TRCS:11805-11814`). Box 2 = taxpayer line 30 + spouse line 30 (spouse passed as null on MFS,
    `TRCS:6181`), rounded; box 2 checked when > 0; added to line 16 even when line 15 is 0 (`TRCS:11909`).
12. **AMT.** Form 6251 line 10 = line 16 + PTC repayment − box 2 Form 4972 tax (`TRCS:37052-37054`) — matches.

Only one 1099-R per person can be picked; a second lump sum for the same participant, or a beneficiary's second
participant, cannot get its own Form 4972 (D-4972-6).

## 8. Compared with the IRS rules

| # | IRS rule (source) | What the app does | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Part I line 4 requires participant **and** born before Jan 2, 1936 **and** 5 years; line 3 covers beneficiaries; "No" to both 3 and 4 → don't use (`f4972.pdf p. 1`) | Backend: Q3 **or** Q4 (`TRCS:13370-13372`); a participant born after Jan 1, 1936 with Q4 = Yes is eligible, and a participant born before 1936 with fewer than 5 years is eligible through Q3; no DOB check. The screen shows "Disqualified" for either No (`form-lump-sum-distribution.component.ts:740-745`) but the backend still applies the election | **Deviation D-4972-1** (silent; special tax for an ineligible person) |
| 2 | Multiple recipients: line 8 = box 2a ÷ box 9a %; Part II uses the recipient's box 3; line 11 = box 8 ÷ box 8 %; line 29 = (line 25 − line 28) × box 9a % (`f4972.pdf p. 3`); each 1099-R already shows only that recipient's share (`i1099r--2025.pdf p. 10`) | Multiplies box 2a and box 3 by the percentage (`TRCS:13425-13431`); line 29 not multiplied; box 8 unscaled; line 5b loses only the scaled box 2a (`TRCS:52040-52045`). Example: 50% share, box 2a $50,000 — IRS line 29 = 10 × tax on $10,000 = $14,471 × 50% = $7,235.50 and nothing on 5b; app: Part III on $25,000 → $1,800, and $25,000 stays on line 5b at ordinary rates | **Deviation D-4972-2** (wrong tax and wrong AGI; rare) |
| 3 | NUA Worksheet: with Part II, line 6 = box 3 + box 6 × box 3 ÷ box 2a; line 8 adds only line F (`f4972.pdf p. 3-4`) | Line 6 = box 3; line 8 adds the full box 6 (`TRCS:13486-13488`) | **Deviation D-4972-3** (low; the NUA capital-gain share is taxed at 10-year rates instead of 20%) |
| 4 | Death benefit: participant died before Aug 21, 1996, up to $5,000; with Part II split by the Death Benefit Worksheet (`f4972.pdf p. 3-4`) | Typed amount used as is on line 9; no cap, no date, no split; line 6 not reduced | **Deviation D-4972-4** (low; trusted entry) |
| 5 | Line 18 federal estate tax, and its capital-gain share off line 6 (`f4972.pdf p. 4`) | No input; line 19 = line 17 (`TRCS:13509-13510`) | **Not supported D-4972-5** (silent; overstates tax for a beneficiary whose estate paid estate tax) |
| 6 | Combine all lump sums of the same participant on one form; a separate form per participant (`f4972.pdf p. 2-3`) | One picked 1099-R per person; other lump sums stay on 5a / 5b at ordinary rates, no flag | **Not supported D-4972-6** (silent; taxpayer-unfavourable) |
| 7 | Separate returns: only the filer's own Form 4972 | On a married-filing-separately return without separate returns enabled the spouse's form is kept (`us-tax-be/.../multireturn/MfsFormScoper.java:780-800`), `computeForm4972Spouse` runs with the unscoped 1099-R list (`TRCS:6060`) and `adjustPensionLinesForForm4972` subtracts the spouse's box 1 / 2a from **this** return's 5a / 5b, line 9, AGI and line 15 (`TRCS:6067-6094`, no MFS guard); the spouse's form is also handed to the output (`TRCS:7707`). Line 16 is guarded (`TRCS:6181`). Traced, not run; whether the spouse's 1099-R is on this return's line 5b was not traced | **Deviation D-4972-7** (wrong AGI / taxable income on MFS) |
| 8 | Box 2a blank → figure the taxable amount (Pub. 575) (`f4972.pdf p. 2, p. 4`) | Advisory `FORM4972_*_NO_STATEMENT`, no Form 4972; distribution taxed as ordinary income | **Not supported D-4972-8** (flagged, non-blocking) |
| 9 | Tax Rate Schedule tops out at 50% (`f4972.pdf p. 4`) | Engine table correct; the screen's worked example (`form-lump-sum-distribution.component.ts:420`) says the 1986 single rates "cap out at 11%–28%" and puts the tax on a $120,000 lump sum at "$13,000–$16,000"; the schedule gives 10 × ($1,706.30 + 20% × $560) = $18,183 | **Deviation D-4972-9** (cosmetic, help text) |
| 10 | Part I lines 1, 2, 5a, 5b; QDRO alternate payee (`f4972.pdf p. 1-2`) | `TRCS:13362-13378` | Match |

## 9. Limits and out of scope

- Trusts and estates (Form 1041) — the app files individual returns only.
- Amended-return elections (Form 1040-X) are not produced.
- Box 8 / 9a are typed, not read from the 1099-R statement.
- The early-distribution additional tax base (Schedule 2 line 8) for a Form 4972 amount was not traced here (see
  `new_knowledge/5b.md`).
