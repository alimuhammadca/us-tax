# Form 6198 — how the app does it

IRS rules: `C:\us-tax\new_lines\forms\6198.md`. Flowchart: `C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\forms\6198.md`.
Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`.

---

## 1. Status

The at-risk limit (§465) is applied in **five separate places**, each with a single typed "amount at risk" instead of
the Form 6198 Part II / Part III computation: each Schedule E rental / royalty property, each Form 4835 farm rental,
each Schedule C business and Schedule F farm, the **net of all passive K-1s** (one amount on the "Suspended passive
losses (K-1)" form), and each **nonpassive partnership** K-1 box 1 loss (amount at risk read from the K-1 itself). In
every place a loss larger than the amount at risk is cut to it before the passive-loss rules. Carryovers are kept for
rentals / farm rentals (one Schedule 1 total, released per property), Schedule C and Schedule F (bridged to 2026);
**not** for either kind of K-1. One Form 6198 preview per return is produced, only when a loss was actually cut.

**7 differences from the IRS rules** (D-6198-1 … D-6198-7). The most serious: a nonpassive partnership loss is limited
to "capital contributed during the year + ending recourse + ending qualified nonrecourse liabilities" — the partner's
beginning capital (basis) is ignored — and the cut-off part is simply dropped, never carried forward (D-6198-1, silent,
high). The passive K-1 at-risk excess is not carried forward either, although the code comment says it is (D-6198-2).
Already recorded: the farm-rental at-risk carryover (D-4835-7); Schedule C / F at-risk details are in
`new_knowledge\forms\schedule-c.md` (row 21, Match).

## 4. How it is computed

Methods: rental properties `TRCS:30897-30937`; farm rentals `TRCS:31050-31068`, lone-farm carryover `TRCS:31087-31099`;
form-level carryover routing `TRCS:30584-30613`; passive K-1 `TRCS:3842-3871` (`passiveK1AtRiskAmount`
`TRCS:43824-43836`); nonpassive partnership `atRiskLimitedK1PartnershipOrdinary` `TRCS:31527-31544`; Schedule C / F
`TRCS:27146-27265`, `:27740-27752`; preview `buildAtRiskForm6198` `TRCS:31258-31280`, slot choice `TRCS:7605-7623`;
bridges `importedPriorYearRentalAtRisk` `TRCS:1243-1257` (seeded `TRCS:2370-2380`).

Steps, following `new_lines/forms/6198.md` section 6:

1. **Part I (lines 1–5).** For each activity the "line 5" is only that schedule's net: the Schedule E property's line 21
   (`rawNet`), the farm rental's line 32, the Schedule C / F net, the passive K-1 **net of all passive K-1s** (box 1 of
   K-1s with material participation No + boxes 2 and 3, `TRCS:3837-3841`) (D-6198-3), or the partnership K-1 box 1.
   Gains and losses on the activity's assets (lines 2a–2c) are never added (D-6198-5). For a rental or Schedule C
   business the prior-year at-risk loss is then subtracted (it is "included" in line 1 as the instructions say).
2. **Part II / III (lines 6–19b).** Not computed: the typed `amountAtRisk` (or the K-1 fields) is used as line 20. A
   blank amount means fully at risk (no cap) on every path except the nonpassive partnership, which caps as soon as
   any one of its three K-1 fields is filled.
3. **Line 21 and the carryover.**
   - **Rental property:** loss beyond `amountAtRisk` → `atRiskSuspended`; then its own prior-year carryover is
     released up to `atRisk + net` (an income year adds its income), the rest re-suspended (`TRCS:30932-30937`). A
     form-level carryover goes to the only activity, else it is carried with `RENTAL_AT_RISK_CARRYOVER_NOT_ASSIGNED_TO_A_PROPERTY`.
   - **Farm rental:** same cap; no own carryover field; a form-level carryover reaches it only when it is the sole
     §465 activity (D-4835-7).
   - **Schedule C / F:** cap per business, per-business or return-level carryover (`schedule-c.md`).
   - **Passive K-1s:** if `someK1InvestmentNotAtRisk` and the passive net is a loss larger than `k1AmountAtRisk`, the net
     is cut to it (`TRCS:3863-3870`); the excess `k1AtRiskSuspended` is used only for the Form 6198 preview
     (`TRCS:7612-7621`) and is **not** saved to any carryforward (D-6198-2).
   - **Nonpassive partnership K-1:** box 1 loss limited to max(0, capital contributed during the year + recourse ending
     + QNR ending) (`TRCS:31537-31543`); the rest disappears (D-6198-1). S corporation and 1041 K-1 losses: no limit
     (D-6198-7).
4. **Order.** Every capped amount then goes to Form 8582 (rental, farm rental, passive K-1) or straight to the
   schedule (nonpassive), then Form 461 — the IRS order.
5. **Carryover out.** Rentals + farm rentals: `atRiskSuspended` → `rentalAtRiskCarryforward` (`TRCS:31247`, `:32666`)
   → next year via the bridge (one total). Schedule C → `businessAtRiskCarryforward`; Schedule F →
   `farmAtRiskCarryforward` (`TRCS:32678-32689`).
6. **Form 6198 preview.** One per return. If the rental / farm-rental pool cut anything, its aggregate (Σ raw losses
   that were cut, Σ their amounts at risk) fills it; otherwise Schedule C + Schedule F + passive K-1 together
   (`TRCS:7605-7623`). Lines printed: 1 = 5 = Σ raw losses; 6 = 8 = 10a = 10b = 20 = Σ amounts at risk; 21 = −line 20
   (`TRCS:31264-31280`). No activity description, no lines 2–4, 7, 9, 11–19 (D-6198-6). The nonpassive partnership
   cut never appears.

## 8. Compared with the IRS rules

| # | IRS rule (source) | What the app does | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Amount at risk = adjusted basis in the interest at the start of the year + increases − decreases (Part II), or Part III; K-1 item K1 recourse / QNR amounts count; "Partner / shareholder: … plus any prior year ordinary loss that you could not deduct because of the at-risk rules"; the disallowed loss "is treated as a deduction allocable to the activity" next year (`i6198.pdf p. 2–4`; `i1065sk1.pdf p. 12`; `i1040sc.pdf p. 15`) | Nonpassive 1065 K-1 box 1 loss capped at **capital contributed during the year** + recourse ending + QNR ending (`TRCS:31537-31543`); beginning capital / basis and the year's other increases ignored; the cut-off amount is not stored or carried (`sumK1OrdinaryBusinessIncome` keeps no suspended total). Example: beginning capital $50,000, nothing contributed in 2025, recourse $0, QNR $0 (the K-1 shows 0 in item L) — box 1 loss $10,000 → app $0 deductible and $10,000 lost; IRS $10,000 deductible | **Deviation D-6198-1** (under-deduction, deduction lost, silent; high) |
| 2 | At-risk-disallowed loss carries to next year for the same activity (`i6198.pdf p. 2`; `i1040sc.pdf p. 15`) | Passive K-1 excess (`k1AtRiskSuspended`) only shown on the preview; not in any Schedule 1 carryforward; no field to enter it next year (the code comment at `TRCS:7611-7612` says it is carried) | **Deviation D-6198-2** (deduction lost, silent; medium) |
| 3 | One Form 6198 per activity; income of one activity does not raise another's amount at risk (`i6198.pdf p. 2`) | One cap on the **net** of all passive K-1s with one total amount at risk (`TRCS:3858-3871`). Example: K-1 A loss $20,000 (at risk $5,000), K-1 B income $15,000 → IRS: A allowed $5,000, net +$10,000; app: net −$5,000 not capped | **Deviation D-6198-3** (over-deduction, silent; medium-low) |
| 4 | Qualified nonrecourse financing secured by real property is at risk (`i6198.pdf p. 1–2`; `i1040se.pdf p. 3`) | Rental field label says "cash invested + recourse debt" (`form-rental-income.component.ts:392`); a filer following it leaves out QNR and understates the amount at risk | **Deviation D-6198-4** (under-deduction when the label is followed; low-medium) |
| 5 | Line 5 includes gains and losses on the activity's assets or interest (lines 2a–2c); deductions are allowed first to the extent of income and gains (`i6198.pdf p. 2–3`, example: $4,600 Schedule C loss, $3,100 Schedule D gain) | Rental and farm-rental caps use only the schedule's net; a Form 4797 / Schedule D gain of the same activity does not raise the room (`TRCS:30898-30907`, `:31052-31063`). Example: rental loss $10,000, Form 4797 gain $4,000 on its equipment, at risk $3,000 → IRS deductible $7,000; app $3,000. Schedule C / F not separately checked (unverified) | **Deviation D-6198-5** (under-deduction, silent; low) |
| 6 | Form 6198 attached for each at-risk activity with amounts not at risk and a loss — also when line 5 is a profit (`f6198.pdf p. 1`; `i6198.pdf p. 1–3`); activity description; Part II / III shown | One aggregate preview only when a cap bound; rental slot wins over Schedule C / F / K-1; partnership cut never shown; no description; only lines 1, 5, 6, 8, 10a, 10b, 20, 21 | **Not supported D-6198-6** (form incomplete / missing; amounts in the return unaffected; low) |
| 7 | At-risk (and basis) limits apply to S corporation and estate / trust losses too (`i1040se.pdf p. 2, 8`) | 1120-S and 1041 nonpassive K-1 losses flow in full (`TRCS:31514-31523`) | **Not supported D-6198-7** (over-deduction possible, silent; medium) |
| 8 | Rental real estate and farm rental: loss capped at the amount at risk before Form 8582 (`f4835.pdf p. 3`; `i1040se.pdf p. 3`) | Cap per property / farm rental before pooling | Match |
| 9 | Prior-year at-risk loss of a property deductible against that property only (`i6198.pdf p. 2`) | Per-property field; unassigned total carried with an advisory | Match (farm rental: D-4835-7) |
| 10 | Schedule C / F line 32 / 36 and carryover (`i1040sc.pdf p. 15`; `i1040sf.pdf p. 10`) | Per business; bridge | Match (`schedule-c.md` row 21) |
| 11 | Pre-1987 real property not subject to at-risk (`i6198.pdf p. 1`) | Blank amount = fully at risk | Match (user leaves it blank) |
| 12 | Amount at risk below zero → recapture (Pub. 925) (`i6198.pdf p. 3–4, 8`) | Negative amounts clamped to 0; no recapture | Not supported (Pub. 925 not in `docs\books`; unverified how often it applies) |

## 9. Limits and out of scope

- **Part II / Part III worksheets** (lines 6–19b, Line 11 / 12 / 16 Worksheets) are not modelled: the user types the
  result. This is a deliberate simplification; it makes the label (D-6198-4) the only guidance the user gets.
- **Allocation of line 21 over several loss items** (`i6198.pdf p. 8`) is not needed because each path has one item.
- **Investment interest moved from Form 4952 to line 4** is not modelled.
