## 1. Status

Form 6252 is fully figured from the **Installment sales** intake form (V376, history 2026-10-23): one Form 6252 per
sale, lines 1–26 and Part III lines 27–37, with the year-of-sale / later-year split, line 12 recapture from the sale
facts, line 19 to four decimals, the line 14 / contingent-price / date blockers, and the line 26 routing — capital
assets to Schedule D line 4 / 11 by holding period, business or rental property through a Form 4797 entry (Part III in
the year of sale, Part I line 4 or Part II line 10 later) with the unrecaptured §1250 share as received (Schedule D
worksheet line 4 Steps 1–3). One preview per sale.

The **statement path is dead**: `Form6252Mapper` (`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/statement/Form6252Mapper.java:28-34`)
returns an empty `formIds()` set, so `listEntriesWithData(uid, "6252")` always returns an empty list
(`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/StatementDataService.java:63-68`); the "transcribed" Form 6252 entries
that compute still merges (`TRCS:3148`) exist only in unit tests. `StatementFormCatalog` still lists "6252" under
"Property, debt & other" (`StatementFormCatalog.java:66`), but the UI does not treat it as a statement
(`us-tax-ui/src/app/service/statement-selection.service.spec.ts:31`).

**7 differences from the IRS rules** (D-6252-1 … D-6252-7). Two change income silently:

- **D-6252-1:** the line 25 / line 36 ordinary recapture the filer types is taken out of line 26 / 37 but never
  reported anywhere (Form 4797 line 15 is not filled) — that income disappears.
- **D-6252-2:** rental / business **real** property held one year or less: line 12 is 0 while the Form 4797 side
  recognizes the depreciation part now, so the depreciation part is counted twice.

The rest are unsupported edge cases that are silent but rare (publicly traded securities D-6252-3, pledge rule
D-6252-5), advisory-only handling (§453(g) D-6252-4), and two advisories that misdirect (D-6252-6, D-6252-7).
Person attribution: the input is a taxpayer / spouse personal form, not a statement, so the D-1a-1 root cause (a
dependent's statement counted on the parent's return) does **not** apply — there is no Installment sales form on a
dependent tab.

## 4. How it is computed

Entry: `Form6252Compute.sales` (`F6252:54-87`) is called once per compute (`TRCS:3145-3148`); each sale is figured by
`figure` (`F6252:90-218`) into an engine entry under the semantic Form 6252 keys. `withForm4797` (`F6252:333-341`)
adds a Form 4797 entry for every business sale (`TRCS:3155`); the Form 4797 Part III loop links it back
(`TRCS:2103-2160`, `:2211-2225`); capital sales are aggregated into Schedule D (`TRCS:25308-25333`).

Steps, mirroring `new_lines\forms\6252.md` §6:

1. **Gate.** Only when `hasInstallmentSale` is true on the taxpayer or spouse form (`F6252:58-60`). On MFS the
   spouse's leg gets her `spouseSales` moved to the taxpayer slot (`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/multireturn/MfsFormScoper.java:473-474`);
   the head leg drops the spouse form.
2. **Lines 1–4.** Description, code, dates (ISO or US format, `StatementDate.parse`), line 3 from
   `soldToRelatedParty`, line 4 from `sellingPriceDeterminable` (blank = Yes) (`F6252:91-109`, `:179-187`). Missing
   date sold → blocking `FORM_6252_DATE_SOLD_MISSING`; a date after 2025 → blocking `FORM_6252_SOLD_AFTER_TAX_YEAR`
   (`F6252:95-104`). Year of sale = date sold in 2025.
3. **Line 5.** `sellingPrice`; a contingent sale (line 4 No) with no price → blocking `FORM_6252_CONTINGENT_NO_MAXIMUM`
   and the sale is left out (`F6252:110-117`) — the "attach a schedule … enter the taxable part on line 24" path is not
   supported.
4. **Lines 6–11.** Straight from the inputs; line 7 = 5 − 6, line 10 = 8 − 9 (`F6252:118-123`).
5. **Line 12.** Figured: `business_other` (§1245) → min(line 9, line 5 − line 10 − line 11), floored at 0 — Form 4797
   lines 25a / 25b; `business_real` and `capital` → 0 ("straight-line … no additional depreciation")
   (`F6252:127-130`). A typed amount wins, advisory `FORM_6252_RECAPTURE_DIFFERS` when a business sale's typed figure
   differs (`F6252:129-135`). Figured even in later years (it only keeps line 13 stable). Real property held one year
   or less still gets 0 — see D-6252-2.
6. **Lines 13–14.** Line 14 ≤ 0 → blocking `FORM_6252_NO_GAIN`, sale left out, "enter it on the Capital gain/loss
   form" (`F6252:136-144`).
7. **Lines 15–18.** Line 16 = 14 − 15; line 17 = max(0, 6 − 13); line 18 = 7 + 17 (`F6252:145-148`).
8. **Line 19.** 16 ÷ 18 to 4 decimals, HALF_UP (0 when line 18 is 0). For a later-year sale a typed year-of-sale
   percentage wins (rounded to 4 places), advisory `FORM_6252_GROSS_PROFIT_PERCENTAGE_DIFFERS` when it differs by more
   than 0.00005 (`F6252:149-157`).
9. **Lines 20–23.** Line 20 = line 17 in the year of sale, else 0; line 22 = 20 + 21; line 23 = typed prior payments
   only in a later year (`F6252:158-161`).
10. **Line 24.** `roundMoney(line 22 × line 19)`, floored at 0 (`F6252:162`).
11. **Line 25.** Typed `recaptureOrdinaryPortion`, floored at 0 and capped at line 24; blank → not printed
    (`F6252:163-164`, `:208`). Nothing reads line 25 afterwards — D-6252-1.
12. **Line 26.** 24 − 25 (`F6252:165`).
13. **Part III.** Only when line 3 is Yes, the final payment is not in (line 22 + line 23 < line 18) and the sale year is
    2023 or later (`F6252:213-216`). Line 27 = name + TIN / address; line 28 from `relatedPartyResold`; a line 29
    exception checks its box and stops (29e adds advisory `FORM_6252_LINE_29E_EXPLANATION`); otherwise line 30 typed,
    31 = line 18, 32 = min(30, 31), 33 = 22 + 23, 34 = max(0, 32 − 33), 35 = `roundMoney(34 × 19)`, 36 typed and capped
    at 35, 37 = 35 − 36 (`F6252:220-267`).
14. **Related-party depreciable property (§453(g)).** A business sale with line 3 Yes raises advisory
    `FORM_6252_RELATED_PARTY_DEPRECIABLE`; the installment computation continues (`F6252:167-173`) — D-6252-4.
15. **§453A.** After all sales: if any year-of-sale sale has line 5 over $150,000 and the year-of-sale balances
    (line 18 − line 22) of those sales exceed $5,000,000, advisory `FORM_6252_SECTION_453A_INTEREST`
    (`F6252:56-85`). The interest is typed on the Line 16 tax form (Schedule 2 line 15).
16. **Income to report.** `InstallmentSaleLink.income` = line 26 (line 24 when blank) **plus** line 37 (`ISL:164-169`).
17. **Routing — capital asset.** In `aggregateStatementCapitalAmountsForPerson`, an entry not marked
    `routedToForm4797` goes to Schedule D **line 11** if held more than one year (`isLongTermHold`: sold − 1 year is
    after acquired), else **line 4** (`TRCS:25314-25333`). Owner: `ownerRole` of the entry (no `identifyingNumber` on
    an intake sale) (`belongsToCapitalPersonByKey`, `TRCS:25512-25534`). A capital sale with income also puts the
    person on Schedule D without the Capital gain/loss screening (`Form6252Compute.hasIncomeFor`, `F6252:361-369`;
    `TRCS:24760`).
18. **Routing — business property, year of sale.** `form4797For` builds a Part III property: line 19 description /
    dates, line 20 = line 5, line 21 = line 8 + line 11, line 22 = line 9, line 26a = "0" for `business_real`
    (`F6252:275-295`), unless a typed Form 4797 property with the same description and dates exists
    (`onTypedForm4797`, `F6252:298-311`). The Part III loop matches it to the Form 6252 (`ISL:104-117`), marks the
    Form 6252 `routedToForm4797` and (`TRCS:2109-2120`):
    - held more than one year: recapture (§1245 line 25b = min(gain, depreciation); §1250 line 26g = min(gain, line
      26a) = 0) is ordinary now; the §1231 residual is **not** added; the installment income joins the §1231 net and is
      printed on Form 4797 **line 4** (`TRCS:2122-2129`, `:2137-2173`, `:2228-2233`);
    - held one year or less: min(gain, depreciation) ordinary now, plus the installment income — Form 4797 line 10 /
      Schedule 1 line 4 (`TRCS:2127-2136`).
    - §1250 property: the unrecaptured §1250 gain = min(gain, depreciation) − line 26g, then Step 3:
      `thisYearsUnrecaptured` = min(this year's income, total − line 23 × line 19) (`TRCS:2147-2153`; `ISL:143-151`).
19. **Routing — business property, later year.** The Form 4797 entry only carries the Form 6252 (`INSTALLMENT_ONLY`);
    its income goes to Form 4797 line 4 (§1231) if long-term, else ordinary; for `business_real`,
    `earlierYearUnrecaptured` = Step 3 on min(line 9, line 14) (`TRCS:2211-2225`; `F6252:318-323`).
20. **Schedule D line 19.** The per-property unrecaptured amounts are summed into the §1250 worksheet
    (`TRCS:25356-25360`; `ISL:80-101` excludes a transcribed property that is on Form 6252); the Form 4797 line 7 cap is
    D-sch-d-1.
21. **Rounding.** Every printed amount is `roundMoney` (`F6252:492-498`); line 19 is a 4-decimal string.

## 9. Limits and out of scope

- Statement entry of a finished Form 6252 is retired (`Form6252Mapper` dormant); only the intake form works.
- Contingent sales without a stated maximum are blocked, not figured.
- §453B dispositions of the installment obligation (sale, gift, cancellation, repossession) have no field; they must be
  entered as an ordinary sale on another form.
- Section 1202 (QSB stock) installment sales: the proportional exclusion (`i1040sd.pdf p. 8`) has no field on this
  form; whether the Capital gain/loss section 1202 fields can be used for it is **unverified**.
- §453A / §453(l) interest is not figured (the IRS does not figure it on Form 6252 either); the amount is typed for
  Schedule 2 lines 14 / 15.
- Line 12 for real property assumes straight-line depreciation (no §1250 additional depreciation); a typed line 12
  overrides, but the synthesized Form 4797 still uses line 26a = 0 (`F6252:293`), so a typed real-property line 12 is
  not mirrored on Form 4797 (advisory `FORM_6252_RECAPTURE_DIFFERS` names the difference).
- Form 4797 "N/A" on line 32 for recapture-only use is not printed.
