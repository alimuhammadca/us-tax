## 1. Status

Form 6781 **Part I is figured from Form 1099-B boxes 8–11** — nothing else feeds it (a Schedule K-1 section 1256 amount is
stored but never read — D-6781-9). Each 1099-B's box 11 (or box 8 −
box 9 + box 10 when box 11 is blank) becomes a line 1 row; the rows are grouped by the recipient's TIN, and each person's
line 7 is split 40% → Schedule D line 4 and 60% → Schedule D line 11 (`F6781:35-57`; `TRCS:25291-25307`). One Form 6781
preview is built for the return (`F6781:75-123`). The typed Form 6781 statement ("6781") is **dormant** (its mapper serves
no form id), so no line 4 adjustment, no line 6 carryback, no election box and no Part II / Part III can be entered on the
form. Straddle results (Part II) can only be typed as a net amount on the Capital gain/loss form's "other forms" lines,
which go straight to Schedule D lines 4 / 11.

**9 differences from the IRS rules** (D-6781-1 … D-6781-9). The ones that change the tax silently:

- **D-6781-1 (medium):** the line 4 adjustments are not asked — a box 11 loss on the section 1256 leg of a straddle is not
  reduced by the unrecognized gain on the offsetting position, and a hedge's box 11 amount stays a 60/40 capital item
  instead of ordinary. A dealer's section 1256 result never reaches Schedule SE.
- **D-6781-7 (medium):** married filing separately in a community property state with the community share answered:
  each 1099-B is copied to the other return with box 11 **not halved**, so the filer's Form 6781 carries the whole box 11
  of both spouses.
- **D-6781-6 (medium, printed form):** the Form 6781 preview lists box 11 of **every** 1099-B in the account — the other
  spouse's on a separate return, a dependent's — while Schedule D takes only the filer's, so the attached form and
  Schedule D disagree.

- **D-6781-9 (medium):** a partner's or S corporation shareholder's net section 1256 gain or loss (Schedule K-1 (Form
  1065) box 11 code C, (Form 1120-S) box 10 code C) is saved with the K-1 but never read, so it is dropped unless the
  filer also types it on the Capital gain/loss form.

The rest are low or cosmetic. Statement-person check (the D-1a-1 pattern): **not repeated for the amount** — Schedule D
filters Form 6781 by TIN, so a dependent's box 11 stays off the parent's Schedule D (only a 1099-B with a blank recipient
TIN falls to the taxpayer); it shows up only on the preview (D-6781-6).

## 4. How it is computed

Methods: `Form6781Compute.withForm1099B` (`F6781:35-57`), `box11` (`F6781:60-68`), `output` (`F6781:75-123`),
`account` (`F6781:125-132`); called at `TRCS:3238-3240` (entries) and `TRCS:7716` (preview); Schedule D routing in
`aggregateStatementCapitalAmountsForPerson` (`TRCS:25291-25307`) with `belongsToCapitalPersonByKey` (`TRCS:25512-25534`).

Steps, mirroring `new_lines\forms\6781.md` §6:

1. **Boxes A–D.** Not asked; never checked on the preview (D-6781-4).
2. **Line 1.** For every Form 1099-B (after the community-property step, `TRCS:3131-3135`): box 11 when entered, else
   box 8 − box 9 + box 10 when any of them is entered (`F6781:60-68`) — matches the 1099-B instruction "Use boxes 8, 9,
   and 10 to figure the aggregate". A zero or blank result is skipped (`F6781:40`). Column (a) = the first line of the
   payer name/address, plus " - account <number>" (`F6781:125-132`) — not the literal "Form 1099-B" the instruction asks
   for (D-6781-8). Transactions without a 1099-B cannot be entered (D-6781-5).
3. **Grouping by person.** Rows are grouped by the normalized `recipientTIN`; each group becomes one entry with
   `identifyingNumber` = that TIN (none when the TIN is blank) (`F6781:41-46`).
4. **Lines 2–7 per person.** Line 7 = the sum of the group's box 11 amounts, rounded to whole dollars (`F6781:47-51`);
   line 4 and line 6 are always 0 (D-6781-1, D-6781-4).
5. **Lines 8 and 9.** `roundMoney(line 7 × 0.40)` and `roundMoney(line 7 × 0.60)` (`F6781:52-53`) — whole-dollar
   rounding of a whole-dollar line 7 always keeps line 8 + line 9 = line 7. Matches the form.
6. **To Schedule D.** For the taxpayer and (on a joint return) the spouse, each entry whose `identifyingNumber` equals
   that person's SSN adds line 8 to Schedule D line 4 and line 9 to Schedule D line 11; an entry without a TIN goes to
   the taxpayer unless only the spouse has the capital workflow (`TRCS:25295-25307`, `:25512-25534`). A TIN that is
   neither spouse's (a dependent's) is dropped — correct, the dependent's own return takes it. A Form 6781 entry also
   makes Schedule D required (`TRCS:23738-23747`). Schedule D then nets, limits the loss to $3,000 / $1,500 and carries
   the rest forward (see `new_knowledge\forms\schedule-d.md`).
7. **Wash sales / holding period.** Box 11 never makes a Form 8949 row: a futures-only 1099-B has no box 2 / box 12 /
   dates, so `derive1099BBox` returns no box and the row is skipped (`TRCS` `derive1099BBox`, used by
   `buildCapitalTransactionsFrom1099B` at `TRCS:24900-24935`). So the 60/40 split is not overridden by a holding period —
   matches.
8. **Part II.** Not figured or printed. A filer can type the net short-term / long-term straddle result on the Capital
   gain/loss form, which reaches Schedule D lines 4 / 11 directly (D-6781-2); a collectibles part of it is not routed
   to the 28% worksheet (D-sch-d-2).
9. **Part III.** Not asked (D-6781-3).
10. **Married filing separately, community property** (`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/CommunityProperty.java:58-97`).
    With the community-property question answered Yes and a community share above 0, the filer's own 1099-B keeps all
    boxes except proceeds, basis, wash sale and withholding, which are reduced by the other spouse's share; the other
    spouse's 1099-B is copied in full with the filer's TIN and only those four amounts set to the share. Box 11 and boxes
    8–10 are in neither list, so the filer's Form 6781 group holds 100% of the filer's box 11 plus 100% of the other
    spouse's (D-6781-7).
11. **Arithmetic advisory.** `FORM_6781_ARITHMETIC_DIFFERS` checks line 8 = 40% and line 9 = 60% of line 7 on every
    entry, ±$1 (`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/StatementArithmetic.java:90-99`; `TRCS:3476-3477`). The
    app's own entries always pass, and typed entries cannot exist, so it never fires in practice.
12. **Preview** (`F6781:75-123`). All entries' line 1 rows are put on one form — **whoever's TIN** (D-6781-6). Rows 1–2
    as entered; with more than three rows, rows 3 … n are netted into one row "Other accounts (see attached statement)"
    (the app attaches no such statement — D-6781-8). Each row is rounded; a negative row goes in column (b), else (c);
    line 2 = column totals; line 3 = 5 = 7 = (c) − (b); line 6 printed as 0 only when line 3 is a loss (as the form
    says); lines 8 and 9 = 40% / 60% of that line 3. Header = the taxpayer's name and SSN (`TRCS:7716`).

## 9. Limits and out of scope

- **Typed Form 6781 (statement "6781")**: mapper dormant (`Form6781Mapper.java:26-31`); the transcribed-entry code paths
  (`withForm1099B` keeps them first, the arithmetic advisory, `Hrb2025Sc00292Test`) only ever see the app's own entries.
  History (2026-10-23) records the decision: "Not figured: Part II straddles, Part III unrecognized gains, the
  mixed-straddle elections (A-C) and the box D loss carryback (line 6) - their typed totals stay on the Capital gain/loss
  form."
- **Partnership / S corporation section 1256 amounts** should come from the K-1 onto Form 6781 line 1; the app does not
  read them (D-6781-9). The K-1 capital-gain boxes (1065 box 9a / 1120-S box 8a etc.) are a separate path to Schedule D
  lines 5 / 12.
- Form 1045 / amended prior-year returns for a carryback are outside a 2025 original return.
- Form 8960 (net investment income tax) reads the Schedule D result; not traced separately here.
