Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`.

## 1. Status

The self-employed health insurance deduction (Schedule 1 line 17) is **computed per person, but as one pooled
limit, and Form 7206 is not produced.** For each person who answered Yes on the Self-employed deductions form and has
a positive combined Schedule C + Schedule F net profit, the app takes the medical / dental / vision premiums (none when
all twelve months are marked as employer-plan months), the qualified LTC premiums limited by each covered person's age,
and Medicare premiums, and limits the total to that person's combined net profit less the trades' share of the
deductible half of SE tax and less the person's whole SE retirement deduction (line 16). A Marketplace plan with a
Form 1095-A is coordinated with the premium tax credit by a fixed-point solve (Pub. 974). Premiums the limit did not
take are moved to Schedule A medical expenses. The Form 7206 tax-return preview is always blank and no attachment
marker exists.

**8 differences from the IRS rules** (D-7206-1 … D-7206-8), plus the S-corporation shareholder gap already recorded as
D-10-9 and the stale "not supported" text on the Income adjustments form recorded as D-10-15. The most serious: a
partner whose self-employment income comes from a K-1 (box 14 code A) gets no deduction at all (D-7206-2); the limit is
pooled across all of a person's businesses instead of figured for the business under which the plan is established
(D-7206-1); and Form 7206 itself never appears (D-7206-8). All are silent.

## 4. How it is computed

Methods:

- `withLtcCoveredPersonAges` `TRCS:29651-29681` — puts each covered person's age at the end of 2025 on the LTC rows.
- `computeSeDeductions` `TRCS:29722-29846` — called at `TRCS:4232-4238` (spouse form null on MFS).
- `solvePub974SeHealthPtc` `TRCS:29875` — Marketplace fixed point; started at `TRCS:6237-6264`, second pass via
  `SE_HEALTH_PTC_DEDUCTION_OVERRIDE`.
- Schedule A leftover: `TRCS:29831-29844` → `SE_PREMIUMS_FOR_SCHEDULE_A` (`TRCS:4239-4246`) → `buildScheduleA`
  `TRCS:16064-16082`.
- Schedule 1 line 17: `TRCS:32881-32883`; output `TRCS:33267`.
- `ReferenceData.ltcPremiumAgeLimit` (`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/ReferenceData.java:62-72`).

Steps, mirroring `new_lines/forms/7206.md` section 6 (per person, side 0 = taxpayer, 1 = spouse):

1. **Who qualifies.** Nothing is computed unless a Schedule C or Schedule F result exists (`scheduleC == null` →
   all null, `TRCS:29726-29728`; Schedule F is merged into the same result by `mergeFarmIntoSelfEmployment`,
   `TRCS:27815-27840`), `hasSeDeductions` is Yes, and the person's net profit `taxpayerNetForSe` / `spouseNetForSe` is
   positive (`TRCS:29746-29748`). That net profit is the sum over all the person's Schedule C businesses (statutory-
   employee businesses left out, `TRCS:27321-27323`) and materially-participated farms (`TRCS:27755-27757`), **losses
   netted**. It does not contain K-1 (1065) box 14 code A (`TRCS:28970-28972`), so a partner with no Schedule C / F
   gets nothing (D-7206-2); a more-than-2% S-corporation shareholder has no path (D-10-9). When the person used an
   optional method the net profit, not Schedule SE line 4b, is used, and a loss business with an optional-method
   election is skipped (D-7206-3).
2. **Line 1.** `seHealthInsurancePremiums`, replaced by 0 when all twelve months are in `seEmployerPlanEligibleMonths`
   (`TRCS:29801-29802`, parser `TRCS:53471-53481`); for fewer months the typed figure is trusted ("Enter only the
   premiums for the other months"). Plus `medicarePremiums` (`TRCS:29803-29805`), which is **not** removed for
   employer-plan months; the employer question covers only "you or your spouse's employer"; no retired-public-safety
   input (D-7206-5, D-7206-7).
3. **Line 2.** Owner's `seLongTermCarePremiums` capped at the owner's age limit (`TRCS:29783-29788`); each covered
   person's row capped at that person's age limit (`TRCS:29789-29799`). An unknown date of birth leaves the premiums
   uncapped — with the advisory `SE_LTC_COVERED_PERSON_AGE_UNKNOWN_<person>` for a covered person, with no flag for the
   owner (D-7206-6). LTC is not affected by the employer-plan months (the IRS tests LTC plans separately; no LTC
   employer-plan input — part of D-7206-5).
4. **Line 3.** medical + LTC + Medicare (`TRCS:29803-29805`).
5. **Lines 4–8.** `ceiling = net profit − trade share of ½ SE` (`TRCS:29749`); `tradeShareOfHalfSe` removes the church-
   employee part of ½ SE (`TRCS:29690-29704`). The **whole** person's net profit and ½ SE are used — no "business under
   which the plan is established", no line 5 total of profitable businesses, no line 6 ratio (D-7206-1). CRP payments
   exempt from SE tax stay inside the farm net profit (`crp` is tracked separately, `TRCS:27758`) (D-7206-4).
6. **Lines 9–10.** `healthCeiling = ceiling − line 16` of the person (all plans, `TRCS:29806`) (part of D-7206-1).
7. **Lines 11–13.** Not modelled: no S-corporation Medicare wages (D-10-9) and no Form 2555 line 45 subtraction — moot
   because foreign self-employment income cannot be entered (D-2555-10).
8. **Line 14.** `l17 = min(premiums, max(healthCeiling, 0))` (`TRCS:29807`); with the Pub. 974 override the solved
   amount bounded by the same ceiling (`TRCS:29810-29813`). Schedule 1 line 17 = taxpayer + spouse, rounded
   (`TRCS:29824-29828`, used at `TRCS:32881-32883`). If no SE result exists, the legacy hidden field
   `selfEmployedHealthInsuranceAdjustmentLine17OutOfScopeAmount` is used instead (`TRCS:32883`) — it is on no screen.
9. **Marketplace / premium tax credit.** When a side answered "Marketplace" (or a Form 1095-A claims a credit — B54),
   the return is computed twice: `solvePub974SeHealthPtc` bisects the deduction so that deduction + allocable PTC ≤
   premiums, MFS excluded (`TRCS:6237-6264`, `:29875-…`). Otherwise the advisory
   `SE_HEALTH_MARKETPLACE_COORDINATION_ADVISORY` says the plain cap was used (`TRCS:32835-32841`). Not checked against
   Pub. 974 here — the publication is not in `docs\books\` (unverified).
10. **Leftover premiums → Schedule A.** Per side, medical (all months) + age-limited LTC + Medicare − line 17 is added to
    Schedule A line 1 with `SE_HEALTH_PREMIUMS_ADDED_TO_SCHEDULE_A`; with a Marketplace plan only the advisory
    `SE_HEALTH_PREMIUMS_SCHEDULE_A_MARKETPLACE` (`TRCS:29831-29844`, `:16064-16082`).
11. **SE tax.** `computeSeDeductions` takes the finished `scheduleSE` as input (`TRCS:4232-4238`), so line 17 never
    reduces net earnings from self-employment — as the IRS requires.
12. **The form.** No Form 7206 output, no worksheet, no attachment marker (D-7206-8).

## 8. Compared with the IRS rules

| # | IRS rule (source) | What the app does | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Line 4 = net profit of the business under which the plan is established; lines 5–7 prorate Schedule 1 line 15; line 9 = line 16 of the same business; separate Form 7206 per plan's business (`f7206.pdf p. 1`; `i7206.pdf p. 2`) | Pools all of the person's Schedule C / F net profit (losses netted), subtracts the whole trade ½ SE and the whole line 16 (`TRCS:29731-29749`, `:29806`); no business link. E.g. plan business $50,000, other business −$30,000: IRS limit $48,587 (50,000 − ½ SE 1,413), app $18,587 (too low); a plan under a $5,000 business beside a $60,000 one: IRS $4,647 (5,000 − 4,592 × 5/65), app $60,408 (too high) | **Deviation D-7206-1** (wrong amount either way when more than one business, silent) |
