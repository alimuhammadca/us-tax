# Form 8582 — how the app does it

IRS rules: `C:\us-tax\new_lines\forms\8582.md`. Flowchart: `C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\forms\8582.md`.
Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`.
Inputs are described in `new_knowledge\forms\schedule-e.md` §2 (rental form, K-1s, the "Suspended passive losses (K-1)"
form) and `new_knowledge\forms\4835.md` §2 (farm rentals).

---

## 1. Status

The passive activity loss limit is **computed on the aggregate** by one engine inside `computeRentalScheduleE`, and a
Form 8582 preview (Part I lines 1a–3, Part II lines 4–9, Part III lines 10–11) is produced whenever there is a passive
loss. Pooled: Schedule E rental and royalty properties (after the at-risk cap), Form 4835 farm rentals, passive K-1
box 1 net, K-1 boxes 2 and 3, the typed "other passive income", and prior-year suspended losses (per property, per farm
rental, the K-1 total, or last year's carryforward imported from the app's 2024 return). The $25,000 special allowance
(MFS $12,500 / $0), its MAGI phase-out, the Pub. 925 rental-activity exceptions, real estate professionals and full
dispositions to unrelated buyers are handled. A second pass with AMT amounts gives Form 6251 line 2m and an AMT
carryover. Parts IV–IX (per-activity allocation) are **not** produced; the unallowed loss is carried as one total.

**15 differences from the IRS rules** (D-8582-1 … D-8582-15). The most serious, all silent over-deductions: rental
income is used against non-rental passive losses first, so the special allowance covers rental losses that rental
income should have absorbed (D-8582-1); selling **one** rental frees the suspended and current losses of **every**
rental (D-8582-2); one actively-participated property gives the allowance to every property and farm rental in the
pool (D-8582-3, same root as D-4835-2); and passive K-1 prior-year losses get the rental allowance (D-8582-4) and can be
counted twice (D-8582-5). Royalties are treated as passive (D-8582-6). Already recorded and not repeated: the §469 MAGI
is rebuilt from too few items (D-11a-3, D-1z-4); Schedule C passive businesses never reach Form 8582 (D-sch-c-1); the
farm-rental findings D-4835-1 / -2 / -3 / -8.

## 2. Where the user enters it

No form of its own. The inputs are on: the Rental & royalty income form (`activeParticipation`,
`materialParticipation`, `rentalActivityType`, `isRealEstateProfessional`, `otherPassiveIncome`,
`priorYearSuspendedPassiveLoss`, `priorYearAmtSuspendedPassiveLoss`, `fullyDisposedInCurrentYear`,
`soldToRelatedParty`); the Farm rental form (`activeParticipation`, `priorYearSuspendedPassiveLoss`); the K-1
statements ("Did you materially participate in this business activity?" → `materiallyParticipatedInActivity`; boxes 1,
2, 3); the "Suspended passive losses (K-1)" form (`passive-activity-carryforward-taxpayer` / `-spouse`:
"Do you have a suspended passive loss from a K-1 to carry forward?" `hasPassiveActivityCarryforward`, "Total prior-year
suspended passive loss from K-1s" `priorYearSuspendedPassiveLoss`); and, on MFS, the Filing status answer "Did you live
with your spouse at any time in 2025?" (`mfsLivedWithSpouseAnyTime`, copied into the Social Security form's
`livedApartFromSpouseEntireTaxYear`, `TRCS:2747-2753`). Details and labels: `new_knowledge\forms\schedule-e.md` §2.

Not asked: the 10% ownership test for active participation, limited vs general partner on a K-1, PTP status
(K-1 item D is stored as `part1DPubliclyTradedPartnership` but not read), grouping, self-rental / land
recharacterization, the real-estate-professional aggregation election, a per-activity breakdown of the K-1 prior loss.

## 4. How it is computed

Methods: pool building in `computeRentalScheduleE` `TRCS:30490-31122`; `compute469` `TRCS:30436-30459`; allowance
`passiveRentalAllowance` `TRCS:31686-31694`; MFS figures `TRCS:3893-3906`; Form 8582 preview `TRCS:31174-31234`; K-1
inputs `TRCS:3837-3871`, `:3885-3887`; bridge `importedPriorYearRentalPassiveLoss` `TRCS:1267-1281`.

Steps, following `new_lines/forms/8582.md` section 6:

1. **Parts IV / V — which pool.** Each Schedule E property, after its at-risk cap and its own released at-risk carryover
   (`TRCS:30897-30937`), is classified (`TRCS:30942-31042`):
   - **nonpassive** — royalty with material participation Yes; rental of the owner who is a real estate professional
     (unless material participation No); rental-activity exception with material participation Yes. Its net goes to
     `nonpassiveTotal`; its prior-year suspended loss is **ignored** (D-8582-9);
   - **exception, not materially participated** — "other passive" (line 2): income to `passiveIncome` and
     `exceptionPassiveIncome`, loss to `exceptionPassiveLoss`, prior loss to `exceptionPriorSuspended`; a qualifying
     disposition frees that property alone;
   - **everything else** — the **rental pool** (line 1): long-term rentals with or without active participation,
     **royalties** (D-8582-6) and **homes used as a home** (D-8582-7). Income to `passiveIncome`, loss to `passiveLoss`,
     prior loss to `priorSuspendedTotal`; any `activeParticipation` sets one switch `anyActiveParticipation` for the
     whole pool (D-8582-3); any qualifying disposition sets `anyDisposed` (D-8582-2).
   - Form 4835 farm rentals join the rental pool the same way (`TRCS:31050-31085`; `forms\4835.md`).
   - Passive K-1s: box 1 of K-1s with material participation **No** plus boxes 2 and 3 of every 1065 / 1120-S K-1
     (`TRCS:3837-3841`), after an aggregate at-risk cap (`TRCS:3858-3871`; `forms\6198.md`): net income joins
     `passiveIncome`, a net loss becomes `otherPassiveLoss` (line 2, no allowance) (`TRCS:30528-30529`). Boxes 2 / 3
     are always passive and never get the allowance (D-8582-12). PTP K-1s are not separated (D-8582-11).
   - The typed `otherPassiveIncome` joins `passiveIncome` (`TRCS:30524-30530`) (D-8582-8).
2. **Prior-year unallowed losses (columns (c)).** Per-property and per-farm entries are summed into
   `priorSuspendedTotal`; when **no property** has an entry, last year's whole Schedule 1 carryforward (rental, farm,
   K-1 and exception losses together) is imported into the same rental total (`TRCS:31116-31118`); the K-1 form's
   total is then added to it too (`TRCS:31119-31121`) (D-8582-4, D-8582-5; D-4835-3 for farms).
3. **Part I lines 1a–3.** Preview figures (`TRCS:31180-31212`): 1a = pooled income less K-1 / other / exception income;
   1b = rental-pool loss; 1c = `priorSuspendedTotal` (with the K-1 and imported amounts); 2a = K-1 / typed / exception
   income; 2b = passive K-1 net loss + exception losses; 2c = exception prior losses; 1d, 2d, 3 as on the form. When no
   property has active participation, line 1 is moved to line 2 (`TRCS:31189-31200`).
4. **The limit (`compute469`).** Not the form's order: passive income (rental, farm, K-1, typed) is first applied to
   the **other passive** losses, then to the rental pool loss (current + all prior), then the allowance covers the
   rest of the rental pool (`TRCS:30439-30457`) (D-8582-1). If `anyDisposed`, the whole remaining rental pool is
   allowed instead (`TRCS:30448-30450`) (D-8582-2).
5. **Part II lines 4–9.** Allowance = cap − 50% × (MAGI − phase-out start), not below 0 (`TRCS:31686-31694`); cap /
   start $25,000 / $100,000, MFS lived apart all year $12,500 / $50,000, MFS otherwise $0 (`TRCS:3893-3906`). MAGI =
   `rentalModifiedAgi` (`TRCS:3821-3827`, D-11a-3 / D-1z-4). The preview prints lines 4–9 when line 3 is a loss and any
   active participation exists: line 4 = min(loss 1d, loss 3), line 5 = start + cap / 50%, line 6 MAGI, line 7, line 8
   = min(50% × line 7, cap), line 9 = min(4, 8) (`TRCS:31213-31231`). On MFS-lived-together the cap is 0 but line 5
   still prints $100,000 (D-8582-14).
6. **Part III lines 10–11.** Line 10 = 1a + 2a; line 11 = `lossesAllowed` from `compute469` — which is not line 9 +
   line 10 when D-8582-1 applies (`TRCS:31232-31233`).
7. **Parts VI–IX.** Not computed. The rental-pool allowed loss is split between rental real estate and farm rental only
   by current-year loss share (`TRCS:31162-31167`); everything else lands in Schedule E line 26 as one figure; the
   unallowed loss leaves as one carryforward (`passiveCarryforward` = rental + other suspended, `TRCS:30456`,
   `:31246`) (D-8582-14).
8. **AMT twin.** `compute469` again with AMT nets and the AMT prior loss (manual → bridge → regular)
   (`TRCS:31145-31156`) → Form 6251 line 2m and `amtPassiveLossCarryforward`.
9. **Reporting.** Allowed result → Schedule E line 26 (and line 40 for the farm slice) → Schedule 1 line 5; carryforward
   → `rentalPassiveLossCarryforward` (`TRCS:32665`) → next year's bridge.

Not routed to Form 8582 at all: Schedule C businesses with "materially participate" No (D-sch-c-1) and Schedule F farms
with material participation No (`TRCS:27754-27758` uses the answer only for SE tax) (D-8582-10).

## 8. Compared with the IRS rules

| # | IRS rule (source) | What the app does | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rental real estate with active participation is netted on line 1d; the allowance applies to the smaller of the line 1d loss or the line 3 loss; line 11 = line 9 + line 10 (`f8582.pdf p. 1`; `i8582.pdf p. 11`) | Passive income is applied to other-passive losses before the rental pool (`TRCS:30442-30444`), so rental income is "spent" on a K-1 loss and the allowance then covers the whole rental loss. Example: rental A +$10,000, rental B −$20,000 (active), passive K-1 −$10,000, MAGI $80,000 → IRS allows $20,000 (line 9 $10,000 + line 10 $10,000), carries $10,000; app allows $30,000, carries $0; the preview shows line 9 $10,000, line 10 $10,000, line 11 $30,000 | **Deviation D-8582-1** (over-deduction, silent; high) |
| 2 | A full taxable disposition to an unrelated person frees the losses **of that activity** (`i8582.pdf p. 1, 9`) | Any qualifying disposition sets one switch that allows the entire remaining rental pool, every property's current and prior losses (`TRCS:31041`, `:30448-30450`). Example: rental A sold with $3,000 suspended; rental B loss $30,000 + $20,000 suspended, MAGI $160,000 → IRS frees A's $3,000 only; app allows all $53,000 | **Deviation D-8582-2** (over-deduction, silent; high) |
| 3 | Only activities with active participation go in Part IV (special allowance); a prior-year loss qualifies only with active participation in both years (`i8582.pdf p. 10`) | One `anyActiveParticipation` switch for the whole pool (`TRCS:31040`, `:31078`, `:30440-30441`); every pooled loss, including royalties and non-active properties, and every prior loss in `priorSuspendedTotal` gets the allowance. Same root as D-4835-2 (there for farm rentals). Example: house A active −$8,000; house B (no active participation, 5% owner) −$12,000; MAGI $90,000 → IRS allows $8,000; app $20,000 | **Deviation D-8582-3** (over-deduction, silent; medium-high) |
| 4 | Prior-year unallowed losses of non-rental-real-estate passive activities go in Part V column (c), no allowance (`i8582.pdf p. 10`) | The K-1 form's prior loss and the imported carryforward (which contains last year's K-1 and exception losses) are added to the rental pool's prior total (`TRCS:31116-31121`) and get the allowance; the preview shows them on line 1c. Example: K-1 prior loss $15,000, no current K-1 income, one actively participated rental with net $0, MAGI $90,000 → IRS $0 allowed (Part V, no passive income); app $15,000 | **Deviation D-8582-4** (over-deduction, silent; medium) |
| 5 | Each prior-year unallowed loss counted once | The import is skipped only when a **property** has its own prior entry; the K-1 form's entry does not stop it. Example: the app's 2024 return carried $5,000 of K-1 loss; the user also types $5,000 on the 2025 K-1 form → $10,000 in the pool | **Deviation D-8582-5** (double deduction possible, silent; medium; same pattern as D-4835-3) |
| 6 | Royalties not derived in the ordinary course of a trade or business are portfolio income, not passive (`i8582.pdf p. 7`; `i1040se.pdf p. 4`) | A royalty property without material participation Yes is pooled: its income offsets passive losses, its loss is suspended (`TRCS:30962-30963`, `:31020-31034`). Example: royalty income $6,000 and a non-active rental loss $6,000 → IRS: royalty taxable, rental loss suspended; app: they cancel. A royalty loss of $4,000 with MAGI $160,000 → IRS allowed (at-risk only); app suspended | **Deviation D-8582-6** (over- or under-deduction, silent; medium) |
| 7 | The rental of a dwelling unit used as a home (§280A(c)(5)) is not a passive activity (`i8582.pdf p. 3`; `i1040se.pdf p. 4`) | A home used as a home is pooled; its (non-negative) net counts as passive income and frees other passive losses (`TRCS:30857-30892`, `:31021-31022`) | **Deviation D-8582-7** (over-deduction, silent; low-medium) |
| 8 | Each amount is reported once, on its own form; passive income only shapes Form 8582 (`i8582.pdf p. 7`) | The typed "Passive income from other activities (e.g. limited partnership K-1s)" is added to passive income **and** to Schedule E line 26 / Schedule 1 line 5 as income (`TRCS:30524-30530`, `:31171-31172`). If the same income is also on a K-1 statement, Schedule C, Form 4797 … it is counted twice | **Deviation D-8582-8** (double count when the label is followed alongside a K-1 statement, silent; medium) |

| 10 | Schedule C / Schedule F businesses without material participation are passive (Part V) (`i8582.pdf p. 3`) | Schedule F loss of a farm with material participation No is deducted in full (`TRCS:27754-27758`); Schedule C: D-sch-c-1 | **Not supported D-8582-10** (over-deduction, silent; medium) |

## 9. Limits and out of scope

- **Form 8582-CR** (passive credits) is not produced.
- **Grouping** of activities, the NIIT regrouping, self-charged interest, significant-participation recharacterization:
  not modelled; each property is its own line in the pool (consistent with no grouping).
- **Estates / trusts (K-1 (1041)) passive rules**: the IRS says the beneficiary rules "haven't yet been issued"
  (`i1041sk1.pdf p. 2`); the app treats a 1041 K-1 like the others (box 6 by the material-participation answer).
- The aggregate engine is a deliberate design ("this app limits on the aggregate, not per activity", `TRCS:31175-31176`);
  it is exact only when no activity sale, mixed participation, or mixed income / loss types are present.
