IRS rules: `C:\us-tax\new_lines\forms\8814.md`. Flowchart: `C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\forms\8814.md`. The Form 1040
lines it feeds are covered at line level in `C:\us-tax\new_knowledge\3c.md` (the election and eligibility —
D-3c-1 … D-3c-3), `3a.md`, `3b.md` (D-3b-4), `7a.md` / `7b.md` (D-7a-7), `6b.md` (D-6b-1), `9.md` (D-9-3),
`11a.md` (D-11a-2) and `8.md` (Schedule 1 line 8z). Those findings are referenced here, not re-derived.
Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`.

## 1. Status

Form 8814 is computed line by line (lines 1a–15, one form per child) and its four results are routed to the parent's
return: line 9 to Form 1040 lines 3a and 3b, line 10 to Schedule D line 13 or line 7a, line 12 to Schedule 1 line 8z,
line 15 to Form 1040 line 16 box 1. The arithmetic of the form matches the IRS (the IRS "Fred" example reproduces),
the $13,500 ceiling blocks, and the $1,350 / $2,700 / $135 amounts are correct.

What decides whether a Form 8814 exists — the election itself, the child's eligibility and which parent may elect —
is already recorded as **D-3c-1** (built for every dependent with a 1099-INT / 1099-DIV, no election read),
**D-3c-2** (eligibility not checked from data) and **D-3c-3** (MFS double reporting). Downstream timing and
presentation issues are recorded as D-3b-4, D-7a-7, D-6b-1, D-9-3 and D-11a-2.

**5 new form-level differences** (D-8814-1 … D-8814-5): line 12 never reaches the parent's Form 8960 net investment
income (D-8814-1); the child's private activity bond interest never reaches the parent's Form 6251 (D-8814-2); the
statement path reads only the child's 1099-INT and 1099-DIV, so 1099-OID interest, Alaska Permanent Fund dividends and
K-1 dividends are missed (D-8814-3); exempt-interest dividends are left off line 1b (D-8814-4); and the "parents who
qualify" rule for unmarried parents is not checked (D-8814-5). All are silent.

## 4. How it is computed

Methods: call site `TRCS:3641-3643` (skipped on a dependent's own return, `TRCS:3608`); `computeChildInterestDividends`
`TRCS:24341-24523`; qualifying-child test `dependentMeetsForm8814QualifyingChildTests` `TRCS:24309-24339`; eligibility
`validateForm8814ElectionEligibility` `TRCS:24555-24590`; nominee `applyChildNomineeAdjustment` `TRCS:24602-24608`;
`buildForm8814` `TRCS:24610-24712`; line 12 → Schedule 1 `applyForm8814Line12ToSchedule1` `TRCS:24714-24731`
(called `TRCS:5095`); line 15 → line 16 `TRCS:11792-11803, 11925`.

Steps, mirroring `new_lines\forms\8814.md` section 6:

0. **Eligibility and election.** For each dependent on this return (on MFS, the dependents assigned to this leg) whose
   SSN is the recipient TIN of at least one 1099-INT or 1099-DIV: skip with an advisory if the child has an active
   dependent's own return (`TRCS:24362-24371`); otherwise run the relationship / age test (blocking
   `FORM8814_CHILD_NOT_QUALIFYING`, `TRCS:24391-24404`) and the four hand checkboxes (blocking, `TRCS:24555-24590`).
   No election answer is read (D-3c-1); several IRS conditions are not checked (D-3c-2). A saved child entry whose SSN
   has no statements is built too, without the qualifying test, even if the child is not on this return
   (`TRCS:24475-24520`; D-3c-3). The "parents who qualify" rule (custodial parent, higher taxable income) is not
   checked (D-8814-5).
1. **Heading, lines A, B.** The parent header is set from the return header (`TRCS:5468-5470`); child name / SSN
   from the dependent record or entry (`TRCS:24641-24643`).
2. **Line C.** `boxCMultipleFormsAttached = true` on every form when more than one is built (`TRCS:3649-3653`).
3. **Line 1a.** Σ over the child's 1099-INTs of (box 1 + box 3) − (box 11 + box 12), floored at 0 per statement
   (`TRCS:24408-24419`); a typed `line1aTaxableInterest` replaces it (`TRCS:24436, 24441`); the nominee amount is
   subtracted, floor 0 (`TRCS:24460`). The child's **1099-OID is never read** (D-8814-3).
4. **Line 1b.** Σ (box 8 − box 13), floor 0 per statement (`TRCS:24415-24419`); typed value replaces it. 1099-DIV box 12
   exempt-interest dividends are not read (D-8814-4). Line 1b is stored and also added to the parent's line 2a
   (`TRCS:3690-3694`, `:18998`; IRS basis unverified, see `2a.md`).
5. **Lines 2a, 2b, 3.** Σ 1099-DIV box 1a, 1b, 2a (`TRCS:24424-24428`); typed values replace them; nominee amounts
   subtracted from 2a and 3 (`TRCS:24461-24462`). Box 2b / 2c / 2d (§1250 / §1202 / 28% parts) are not read
   (D-7a-7). Alaska Permanent Fund dividends and K-1 dividends reach line 2a only if typed (D-8814-3).
6. **Line 4.** 1a + 2a + 3 (`TRCS:24614`); 0 or blank → no form. **≥ $13,500** → blocking, non-overrideable
   `FORM8814_CHILD_GROSS_INCOME_TOO_HIGH` and no form (`TRCS:24619-24638`; `NonOverrideableFlags.java:379`).
7. **Line 5.** Constant `ReferenceData.FORM8814_BASE_AMOUNT` = 2,700 (`us-tax-be/.../microservices/ReferenceData.java:548`);
   not stored, printed by the preview.
8. **Line 6.** Only when line 4 > 2,700: line 4 − 2,700 (`TRCS:24673-24675`); otherwise lines 6–12 stay blank (the
   IRS "skip lines 5 through 12").
9. **Lines 7, 8.** Only when line 2b or line 3 is positive: 2b ÷ 4 and 3 ÷ 4, ten decimals half-up
   (`TRCS:24679-24683`); otherwise lines 7–10 blank and line 11 = 0, as the form says.
10. **Lines 9, 10.** line 6 × ratio, rounded half-up to whole dollars (`TRCS:24684-24685`), floored at 0, stored only
    when not zero (`TRCS:24702-24707`).
11. **Line 11.** 9 + 10 (`TRCS:24691-24692`).
12. **Line 12.** line 6 − line 11, floor 0, rounded (`TRCS:24693, 24704, 24708`). Because lines 9 and 10 are rounded
    separately, line 11 can exceed line 6 by $1 in a tiny-amount edge case; line 12 is then 0 (no IRS rule covers it).
13. **Line 13.** Constant 1,350 (`ReferenceData.java:550`).
14. **Line 14.** max(0, line 4 − 1,350), stored when not zero (`TRCS:24652, 24663`).
15. **Line 15.** 0 if line 14 = 0; 10% × line 14 rounded if line 14 < 1,350 (branch flag "Yes"); else $135
    (`ReferenceData.java:552`; branch flag "No") (`TRCS:24652-24670`). Stored only when not zero.

Routing (after all forms are built):

- **Line 9** — Σ over forms (`TRCS:3675-3678`) added to lines 3a and 3b and sets both line 3c boxes
  (`TRCS:19765-19769, 19905-19906`; `3c.md`). No Schedule B line 5 item (D-3b-4).
- **Line 10** — Σ (`TRCS:3670-3674`) added to Schedule D line 13 when Schedule D is required, else to line 7a; line 7b
  box and amount (`TRCS:24037-24044, 24076, 24255, 24280-24281`; `7a.md`, `7b.md`).
- **Line 12** — Σ written to `Schedule1AdditionalIncome.otherIncomeForm8814` and added to Schedule 1 lines 9 and 10
  (`TRCS:24714-24731`), then pushed into Form 1040 lines 8, 9, 11 and 15 (`TRCS:5097-5145`). Figures computed before
  that patch (Social Security worksheet, IRA MAGI, Schedule A floors, first QBI pass) miss it — D-6b-1, D-9-3; with no
  other income AGI stays blank — D-11a-2. The EIC investment-income test adds it explicitly (`TRCS:51637-51641`, set
  `TRCS:3695-3698`).
- **Line 15** — Σ added to line 16, box 1 checked (`TRCS:11792-11803, 11909, 11925`).
- **Form 8960** — line 2 (3b) carries line 9 and line 5 (7a) carries line 10, but line 12 enters only if the user types
  `form8960Line7OtherModifications` (`TRCS:43477, 43514`) — D-8814-1.
- **Form 6251 line 2g** — built only from the filer's and spouse's own 1099-INT box 9 / 1099-DIV box 13
  (`TRCS:19203`, `:19952-19956`; person filter `belongsToPerson` `TRCS:36021-36040`); the child's private
  activity bond interest is excluded and has no field — D-8814-2.
- **Form 4952 line 4a** — typed by the user (`TRCS:44582`); no automatic child amount (the user's figure; see §8).

## 9. Limits and out of scope

- No side-by-side comparison of the election against a separate child's return ("Rate may be higher").
- Schedule B Part III for a child's foreign account is answered by the user on the parent's Schedule B questions; the
  "Form 8814" note next to line 7a / 8 is not produced (unverified whether any user path covers it).
- Estimated-tax penalty advice for the next year is not given.
