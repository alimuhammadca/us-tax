# Form 8815 — how the app does it

IRS rules: `C:\us-tax\new_lines\forms\8815.md`. Flowchart: `C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\forms\8815.md`. The line it
reduces: `C:\us-tax\new_knowledge\2b.md` (step 5 and D-2b-5); the schedule it feeds: `forms\schedule-b.md`.
Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`.

---

## 1. Status

Form 8815 is computed line by line (lines 1-14, the Line 6 Worksheet and the Line 9 Worksheet) from a three-step
section on the taxpayer's Interest form, or taken as a typed line 14 ("override"). The claim is refused on a
married-filing-separately return (advisory) and at or above the modified-AGI ceiling (advisory, no form). Because the
exclusion changes AGI, the app computes the return once without it, then re-runs the whole compute with line 14
subtracted from Form 1040 line 2b (Schedule B line 3).

**6 differences from the IRS rules** — one already recorded under line 2b, five here: a qualifying surviving spouse
gets the joint-return amounts (**D-2b-5**, not repeated); the default modified AGI leaves out three IRS add-backs
(D-8815-1); expenses already used for an education credit are not kept off line 2 (D-8815-2); educator expenses are
not reduced by the exclusion (D-8815-3); the override path files a Form 8815 with only line 14 (D-8815-4); and a
partly filled Line 9 or Line 6 worksheet treats every blank as zero — filling only the "add-backs" the screen invites
throws away AGI and gives a full exclusion at any income (D-8815-5, the most serious).

## 4. How it is computed

Methods:

- `computeForm8815` `TRCS:8911-9110`, called at `TRCS:7249-7250` with the foreign add-back sum built at
  `TRCS:7240-7248`.
- `resolveForm8815Line9` `TRCS:8872-8893`; `computeForm8815Line6Worksheet` `TRCS:9136-9162`;
  `computeForm8815Line9Worksheet` `TRCS:9181-9205`; ceiling advisory `form8815AboveCeilingFlag` `TRCS:8899-8909`.
- Second pass: `TRCS:7511-7529` (`FORM8815_LINE_BY_LINE_EXCLUSION`); applied amount `form8815ExclusionAppliedThisPass`
  `TRCS:8868-8870`, subtracted from line 2b at `TRCS:19040-19041`.
- Constants: `us-tax-be/.../microservices/ReferenceData.java:810-818`.

Steps, mirroring `new_lines/forms/8815.md` section 6:

1. **Eligibility.** No interest form or "claims" not Yes → no form (`TRCS:8918-8921`). MFS → advisory
   `FORM_8815_MFS_BLOCKED`, no form (`TRCS:8922-8930`). "Special circumstance" checked → no form, no message
   (`TRCS:8934-8935`). Filing status picks the amounts: `isMfj` is true for married filing jointly **and** qualifying
   surviving spouse (`TRCS:8941-8948`) — D-2b-5. Ceiling = line 10 + band (`TRCS:8949`).
2. **Override path.** If `savingsBondExclusionAmount` > 0: line 9 is resolved (step 10) and, at or above the ceiling,
   the advisory is raised and no form is produced (`TRCS:8962-8967`); otherwise a Form 8815 carrying **only** the
   header and line 14 = the override is returned (`TRCS:8968-8972`). The phase-out is not re-applied (D-8815-4). The
   override wins over any line-by-line entries (`form8815ManualOverrideWinsOverLineByLine`).
3. **Line 1.** Up to three person / school / address rows copied as typed (`TRCS:9067-9075`); not checked against the
   return's taxpayer, spouse or dependents.
4. **Lines 2-4.** Line 2 and line 3 as typed — no reduction for expenses used on Form 8863 or for a tax-free Coverdell
   / QTP distribution (D-8815-2). Line 4 = max(0, line 2 − line 3); 0 → no form (`TRCS:8996-8998`).
5. **Line 5** as typed.
6. **Line 6.** If any Line 6 Worksheet input is present, line 6 = worksheet line 8 when prior-year interest > 0, else
   worksheet line 6 (`TRCS:8983-8986`, worksheet `TRCS:9136-9162`: line 3 = paper EE face × 0.50 rounded, line 6 =
   max(0, line 5 − (line 3 + line 4)), line 8 = max(0, line 6 − line 7)). Blank worksheet inputs count as 0
   (D-8815-5). Otherwise line 6 as typed. Lines 2, 5 or 6 missing or ≤ 0 → no form (`TRCS:8990-8993`).
7. **Line 7** = 1.000 if line 4 ≥ line 5, else line 4 ÷ line 5 rounded half-up to 3 places (`TRCS:9002-9007`).
8. **Line 8** = line 6 × line 7, rounded to whole dollars (`TRCS:9010`).
9. **Line 9.** `resolveForm8815Line9` precedence (`TRCS:8872-8893`):
   (a) `form8815ManualMagiOverride`;
   (b) the Line 9 Worksheet if **any** of its five inputs is present: line 3 = line 1 + line 2, line 5 = max(0,
   line 3 − line 4 + Form 2555 add-back + other add-backs), blanks = 0 (`TRCS:9183-9202`) (D-8815-5);
   (c) otherwise Form 1040 line 11b, plus the exclusion already in that pass (so it is pre-exclusion), plus the sum of
   both spouses' Form 2555 `foreignEarnedIncomeExclusion` + `housingExclusionAmount`, `puertoRicoExcludedIncome` and the
   Form 4563 line 15 exclusion (`TRCS:7240-7248`, `:8886-8892`). This default does **not** add back the Schedule 1
   line 21 student loan interest deduction, excluded employer adoption benefits (Form 8839 line 30 — which the same
   compute adds back for the IRA MAGI at `TRCS:4573-4575`), or the Form 2555 line 50 housing deduction (Schedule 1
   line 24j), and has no royalty-interest special computation (D-8815-1). Line 11b missing → 0.
10. **Ceiling.** Line 9 ≥ ceiling → advisory `FORM_8815_MAGI_ABOVE_CEILING`, no form (`TRCS:9035-9038`).
11. **Lines 10-13.** Line 10 from `ReferenceData` (`TRCS:8944-8946`); line 11 = max(0, line 9 − line 10)
    (`TRCS:9042`); if 0: line 12 left blank, line 13 = 0, line 14 = line 8 (`TRCS:9047-9056`); else line 12 = line 11 ÷
    band rounded half-up to 3 places, line 13 = line 8 × line 12 rounded to whole dollars (`TRCS:9057-9060`).
12. **Line 14** = max(0, line 8 − line 13) (`TRCS:9061`).
13. **To Schedule B / line 2b.** On pass 1 nothing is excluded (line 11b is pre-exclusion). If line 14 > 0 and
    line 2b > 0, `prepare` runs once more with line 14 in `FORM8815_LINE_BY_LINE_EXCLUSION` (`TRCS:7511-7529`); then
    line 2b = max(0, Schedule B line 2 − line 14) and Schedule B line 3 = line 14 (`TRCS:19040-19041`, `:36172`).
    Line 14 is not capped at the savings-bond interest actually reported on Schedule B line 1.
14. **Downstream add-backs** read the same applied amount: Social Security worksheet line 3 uses line 2b + line 14
    (`TRCS:33984-34004`), IRA deduction MAGI (`TRCS:4570`), Roth / §4973 MAGI (`TRCS:5258`). Schedule 1 line 11
    educator expenses are taken as typed (`TRCS:32849-32853`) — not reduced by line 14 (D-8815-3).

## 5. Flags and advisories

| Code | Blocks? | When | Tells the user | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `FORM_8815_MFS_BLOCKED` | No | Claim = Yes on an MFS return | Exclusion not allowed on MFS; suppressed | `TRCS:8924-8929` |
| `FORM_8815_MAGI_ABOVE_CEILING` | No | Line 9 (either path) at or above $114,500 / $179,250 | No exclusion; Form 8815 not filed; clear the claim if in error | `TRCS:8899-8909`, raised `:8965`, `:9036` |

No flag for: a "special circumstance" refusal, line 4 ≤ 0, missing lines 2 / 5 / 6, a partly filled worksheet, an
expense overlap with Form 8863, or line 14 larger than the reported savings bond interest.

## 9. Limits and out of scope

- Three line 1 rows; no continuation statement and no check that a person is the taxpayer, spouse or a dependent.
- Line 14 is not capped at the series EE / I interest actually on Schedule B line 1 (line 2b is floored at 0 only).
- Bond-level records (serial number, issue date, face value — Form 8818) are not collected.
- Deliberate design: an exclusion that the law denies produces no form rather than a form with line 14 = 0
  (`TRCS:9022-9034` comment; git `d4acfc89`).
