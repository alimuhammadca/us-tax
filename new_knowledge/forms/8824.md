## 1. Status

Form 8824 Parts I–III are figured from the **Like-kind exchanges** intake form (V380, history 2026-10-23), one Form
8824 per exchange, following the 2025 instructions line by line (the instructions' Taylor / Finley examples are unit
tests): line 15 netting of liabilities each way with exchange expenses first, line 18, lines 19–24, section 1245
recapture on line 21, line 25 and its 25a / 25b split by FMV. Investment property goes to Schedule D line 4 / 11;
business, rental or depreciated property through a synthesized Form 4797 entry (line 16 recapture, line 5 section 1231
or line 16 ordinary), with its unrecaptured section 1250 gain to Schedule D line 19. Related-party exchanges are filed
again in the 2 following years and line 24 becomes taxable after a disposition without an exception. The qualifying
tests (real property, U.S. vs foreign, 45 / 180 days, dates) block. Since V384 (2026-10-23) a "Does any of these
apply?" question blocks a former main home (section 121), a note received (installment method) and a section 1043 sale,
and flags a multi-asset exchange.

The **statement path is dead**: `Form8824Mapper.formIds()` returns an empty set
(`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/statement/Form8824Mapper.java:27-33`, "DORMANT since
form-8824-plan.md"), exactly like `Form6252Mapper` (`statement/Form6252Mapper.java:28-34`). `listEntriesWithData(uid,
"8824")` therefore returns nothing; the "transcribed entry" branches in compute (`F8824:374-379`, `TRCS:3478`) are only
fed by unit tests. `StatementFormCatalog` still lists "8824" (`StatementFormCatalog.java:67`) but the UI picker omits it
(`us-tax-ui/src/app/service/statement-selection.service.spec.ts:64-66`). By contrast `Form4797Mapper` is **active**
(API only) — see `4797.md`.

**7 differences from the IRS rules** (D-8824-1 … D-8824-7), all low severity and rare:

- **D-8824-1:** a related-party deferred gain made taxable later is reported with **no depreciation recapture** — all
  section 1231 / capital, not "as if the exchange had been a sale".
- **D-8824-2:** line 21 recapture covers only **section 1245** parts; section 1250 additional depreciation (e.g. bonus
  on improvements — the instructions' Finley example) and sections 1252–1255 have no input.
- **D-8824-3:** the related-party question's hint lists fewer relatives than the IRS (no grandparents, trusts, estates,
  tax-exempt organizations), and the "related party sold the replacement into the exchange" case is not asked.
- **D-8824-4 / -5:** a multi-asset exchange and other property given up (line 14) are simplified with advisories.
- **D-8824-6:** the three V384 special cases are **not supported but blocked** when the box is ticked.
- **D-8824-7:** a stale required-attachment note.

Person attribution: personal forms per taxpayer / spouse, no dependent path — the D-1a-1 root cause does not apply.

## 4. How it is computed

Entry: `Form8824Compute.exchanges` (`F8824:58-72`) once per compute (`TRCS:3150-3151`); each exchange is figured by
`figure` (`F8824:75-232`). Business exchanges are appended to the Form 4797 entries by `withForm4797` (`F8824:401-408`,
called `TRCS:3155`); every exchange is appended to the Form 8824 entries by `withExchanges` (`F8824:394-398`,
`TRCS:3241-3242`).

Steps, mirroring `new_lines\forms\8824.md` §6:

1. **Gate.** Only when `hasLikeKindExchange` is Yes on the taxpayer or spouse form (`F8824:62`).
2. **V384 special cases** (`supported`, `F8824:239-274`): `main_home` → blocking `FORM_8824_MAIN_HOME_NOT_SUPPORTED`;
   `installment_note` → blocking `FORM_8824_INSTALLMENT_NOTE_NOT_SUPPORTED`; `section_1043` → blocking
   `FORM_8824_SECTION_1043_NOT_SUPPORTED`; the exchange is left out. `multi_asset` (only when not blocked) → advisory
   `FORM_8824_MULTI_ASSET_FIGURED_AS_ONE_GROUP`, figured as one group (D-8824-4, D-8824-6).
3. **Dates and year** (`F8824:79-103`): no date transferred → blocking `FORM_8824_DATE_TRANSFERRED_MISSING`; after 2025
   → blocking `FORM_8824_AFTER_TAX_YEAR`; an earlier year that is not a related-party exchange of 2023 / 2024 →
   advisory `FORM_8824_EARLIER_YEAR_NOTHING_TO_FILE` and left out; no date acquired → blocking
   `FORM_8824_DATE_ACQUIRED_MISSING`.
4. **Qualification in the year of the exchange** (`valid`, `F8824:277-316`): "No" to both-real-property → blocking
   `FORM_8824_NOT_REAL_PROPERTY`; one side outside the U.S. → blocking `FORM_8824_US_AND_FOREIGN_PROPERTY`; no date
   received → blocking; identified more than 45 days after (or, with no identification date, received more than 45
   days after) → blocking `FORM_8824_45_DAY_IDENTIFICATION`; received more than 180 days after → blocking
   `FORM_8824_180_DAY_RECEIPT`; received after April 15 of the next year → advisory `FORM_8824_RECEIVED_AFTER_DUE_DATE`
   (due date with extensions is not known).
5. **Business or investment** (`F8824:106-113`): business = "Used in a business or as a rental" **or** any
   depreciation; investment with depreciation → advisory `FORM_8824_INVESTMENT_DEPRECIATED` (reported through Form
   4797).
6. **Lines 12–14** (`F8824:116-124`): line 14 = 12 − 13; when non-zero in the exchange year → advisory
   `FORM_8824_LINE_14_REPORTED_LIKE_THE_EXCHANGE` (D-8824-5).
7. **Line 15** (`F8824:127-133`): net relief = max(0, debt relieved − (debt assumed + cash paid + line 12)); gross =
   cash + other property + net relief; expenses used first, line 15 = gross − min(expenses, gross).
8. **Lines 16–19** (`F8824:134-138`): 17 = 15 + 16; 18 = basis + unused expenses + max(0, paid − relieved); 19 = 17 − 18.
9. **Line 20** = max(0, min(15, 19)) (`F8824:139`).
10. **Line 21** (`F8824:143-145`): business only: min(section 1245 depreciation, max(0, line 19), line 20 + (line 16 −
    FMV of section 1245 parts received)), not below 0. No section 1250 / 1252–1255 input (D-8824-2).
11. **Lines 22–24** (`F8824:146-148`): 22 = max(0, 20 − 21); 23 = 21 + 22; 24 = line 19 when a loss, else 19 − 23.
12. **Line 25 / 25a / 25b** (`F8824:149-152`): 25 = 18 + 23 − 15; 25b = 25 × FMV of section 1245 parts ÷ line 16
    (when given); 25a = the rest for business property; 25c (intangible) never split.
13. **Part II** (`F8824:155-170`, `:200-208`): related → line 8 fields and lines 9 / 10; a disposition with an exception
    prints line 11 (11c → advisory `FORM_8824_LINE_11C_EXPLANATION`); without one → advisory
    `FORM_8824_RELATED_PARTY_DISPOSITION` and line 24 becomes taxable this year. Part III is printed in the exchange
    year, or later only when line 24 is recognized.
14. **What is reported this year** (`F8824:172-181`): exchange year → sale gain = line 22 + line 14, recapture = line
    21; recognized later → + line 24 (no recapture added — D-8824-1). Unrecaptured section 1250 gain (business) =
    max(0, min(sale gain, depreciation − section 1245 depreciation)). Holding period: date transferred − 1 year after
    date acquired (`F8824:187`).
15. **Investment → Schedule D** (`scheduleD`, `F8824:367-373`; read `TRCS:25428-25438`): the sale gain on line 11
    (long-term) or line 4 (short-term), per owner; it also opens Schedule D without the Capital gain/loss screening
    (`hasIncomeFor`, `F8824:383-391`; `TRCS:24760-24762`).
16. **Business → Form 4797** (`form4797For`, `F8824:332-341`; `form4797Amounts`, `F8824:348-360`): a synthesized entry
    (marked "not Schedule C", so its recapture is not Schedule C QBI) when there is a sale gain or recapture. Long-term:
    the sale gain → section 1231 net and preview line 5; line 21 → ordinary, preview line 16; unrecaptured → Schedule D
    line 19 key. Short-term: gain + recapture → ordinary, line 16. Then the Form 4797 Part III loop (`TRCS:2225-2230`)
    and the netting / lookback described in `4797.md` (D-4797-2 / -3 / -4 apply).
17. **Output** (`outputs`, `F8824:411-435`, `TRCS:7720`): one `Form8824` per exchange, header = the owner (spouse header
    for a spouse exchange), semantic fields (`F8824:438-491`; dates MM/DD/YYYY, descriptions split at ~60 characters).
18. **Rounding** — `roundMoney` on every printed amount (`F8824:542-544`); 25b ratio to 10 places before rounding.
