# Form 8829 — how the app does it

IRS rules: `C:\us-tax\new_lines\forms\8829.md`. Flowchart: `C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\forms\8829.md`.
Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`.
`HO-UI` = `us-tax-ui/src/app/forms/form-home-office-actual.component.ts`.

---

## 1. Status

Form 8829 is **computed but not produced**. For each Schedule C business whose home-office method is "Form 8829
(actual)", the app matches one home-office entry, figures the business percentage from square feet, applies the
three-tier §280A order (lines 9–11 unlimited, operating expenses then excess casualty losses and depreciation limited
by the business income), takes the line 35 casualty part to Form 4684 / Schedule 1 line 4, puts the rest on
Schedule C line 30, carries lines 43 and 44 to next year (with an automatic year-to-year bridge), moves lines 10/11 to
lines 16/17 for a standard-deduction filer (with an itemize-or-not comparison), and removes the business part of the
interest and taxes from Schedule A. There is **no Form 8829 output**: the Form 8829 tax-return preview is always blank.

**10 differences from the IRS rules** (D-8829-1 … D-8829-10). The most serious: line 8 is figured **before** the
§179 deduction, so the gross-income limit is too high when §179 is elected (D-8829-1); home depreciation is always a
full year at 1/39 — the 2025 first-use month table, stopped-use and improvements are not supported (D-8829-5); a
non-exclusive daycare's hours share (lines 4–6) is ignored although the days are asked (D-8829-3); and Form 8829
itself never appears on the return (D-8829-9). All four are silent.

## 4. How it is computed

Methods:

- Call: `computeScheduleC(...)` `TRCS:4051-4061` — the spouse's home-office form is passed as `null` on an MFS return
  (`TRCS:4061`).
- Per business, inside Phase 1 of `computeScheduleC`: tentative profit `TRCS:26991-26996`, carryover routing
  `TRCS:26997-27018`, `computeHomeOfficeDeduction` `TRCS:28763-28871`, casualty carve-out `TRCS:27026-27033`.
- Helpers: `homeOfficeEntries` `TRCS:28716-28719`, tier constants `TRCS:28724-28747`, `sumHomeOfficePairs`
  `TRCS:28750-28757`, `form8829Line9Entered` `TRCS:28703-28707`, `matchHomeOfficeToBusiness` `TRCS:28900-28919`,
  `carryHomeOfficeForward` `TRCS:28874-28877`, `sumAppliedPerOfficeCarryover` `TRCS:28885-28898`.
- Carryover in: aggregate fields `TRCS:26884-26890`, per-office de-duplication `TRCS:26898-26905`, bridge
  `importedPriorYearHomeOfficeCarryover` `TRCS:1390-1407` used at `TRCS:2395-2430`.
- Carryover out: `TRCS:27386-27395`, persisted `TRCS:32670-32674`.
- Standard deduction re-run: `TRCS:7333-7390`, ThreadLocals `TRCS:310-320`,
  `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/HomeOfficeDeductionChoice.java:21-60`.
- Schedule A parts: `withHomeOfficeScheduleAParts` `TRCS:53491-53550`, called `TRCS:4340-4341`.
- Line 35 → Schedule 1 line 4: `TRCS:4203-4228`.

Steps, mirroring `new_lines/forms/8829.md` section 6:

1. **Which office, which business.** Entries are read only when `hasActualHomeOffice` is Yes (`TRCS:28717`). Only a
   business with `homeOfficeMethod = form8829` uses Form 8829 (`TRCS:28782`). The office is matched by
   `activityDescription` equal (case-insensitive) to the business name or profession, to the first such business; an
   office with a blank description goes to the first for-profit business (`TRCS:28900-28919`). An unmatched office,
   or a matched business with another method, is silently unused (D-8829-7). Each business entry is its own Form 8829
   with its own line 8 (D-8829-7).
2. **Lines 1–3, 7.** `pct = min(businessSqft, totalSqft) / totalSqft`, 6 decimals (`TRCS:28795-28801`). Blank or zero
   areas → no deduction, carryovers pass through (`TRCS:28797-28800`).
3. **Lines 4–6 (daycare).** Not computed: `daycareDays` is saved (`HomeOfficeMapper.java:69`) but no compute code reads
   it, and hours are not asked (D-8829-3).
4. **Line 8.** The per-business "tentative profit" = gross income (line 7, including Form 4797 Part IV recapture)
   − Part II expenses − standard mileage − vehicle loan interest − line 13 **bonus + MACRS only** (`TRCS:26992-26996`),
   floored at 0 (`TRCS:28774`). The §179 deduction is applied later, in Phase 2 (`TRCS:27076-27197`), so it does not
   reduce line 8 (D-8829-1). Home-use gains, unrelated Form 4797 / 8949 losses and the other-location allocation are
   not modelled (D-8829-2).
5. **Lines 9–14 (tier 1).** `tier1 = Σ direct + indirect × pct` for mortgage interest and real estate taxes
   (`TRCS:28803`). Line 9: the typed disaster amount, only when "federally declared disaster" is Yes
   (`TRCS:28703-28707`), capped at the indirect casualty loss with an advisory (`TRCS:28817-28825`), dropped with an
   advisory for a standard-deduction filer without a net qualified disaster loss (`TRCS:28826-28832`); its business
   part (× pct) joins tier 1 (`TRCS:28834`, `:28843`). No Line 11 Worksheet: all real estate taxes stay on line 11,
   unlimited (D-8829-4). Column (a) is accepted for mortgage interest although the IRS forbids it (D-8829-10).
6. **Line 15.** `afterTier1 = max(0, ceiling − tier1)` (`TRCS:28849`).
7. **Lines 16–26 (tier 2).** `tier2 = Σ direct + indirect × pct` for excess mortgage interest, insurance, rent,
   repairs, utilities, other, **plus the line 25 carryover** (`TRCS:28805`). There is no line 17 field. Standard
   deduction (second pass only, see step 13): tier 1 is moved into tier 2 (`TRCS:28806-28811`) — lines 10/11 become
   16/17. Match.
8. **Line 27.** `allowedTier2 = min(tier2, afterTier1)` (`TRCS:28850`).
9. **Lines 28–32 (tier 3).** `tier3 = max(0, homeAdjustedBasis − landValue) × pct ÷ 39` (2 decimals) + line 31
   carryover + casualty (direct + indirect × pct) − line 9 business part (`TRCS:28837-28842`). The ÷ 39 is the
   full-year 2.564% for every home; no first-use month, stopped-use, pre-1993 rate or improvements (D-8829-5). The
   1/39 vs 2.564% rounding difference is about $1 per $1,000,000 of business basis.
10. **Line 33.** `allowedTier3 = min(tier3, afterTier1 − allowedTier2)` (`TRCS:28851`).
11. **Lines 34–36.** `allowable = tier1 + allowedTier2 + allowedTier3`, rounded (`TRCS:28852`, `:28870`). Line 35 =
    line 9 business part + `min(line 29, allowedTier3)` — casualty taken before depreciation inside line 33, a stated
    convention (`TRCS:28856-28862`). Back in `computeScheduleC` line 35 is subtracted from the Schedule C line 30
    amount and recorded per person (`TRCS:27026-27032`) — **except for a qualified joint venture**, where it stays on
    Schedule C (D-8829-6). Line 36 = the rest → `netProfitBefore179` (`TRCS:27033`).
12. **Lines 43–44.** `disallowed = (tier2 − allowedTier2) + (tier3 − allowedTier3)`; line 44 part kept apart
    (`TRCS:28853-28855`); advisory `HOME_OFFICE_LIMITED_BY_GROSS_INCOME`. A carryover not consumed this year (simplified
    method, no Form 8829 business, unmatched office) passes through whole (`TRCS:28779`, `:28782`, `:28785`,
    `:27386-27392`). Totals: `homeOfficeCarryforward` (43 + 44) and `homeOfficeDepreciationCarryforward` (44)
    (`TRCS:27393-27394`), persisted on Schedule 1 (`TRCS:32670-32674`).
13. **Lines 25 / 31 in.** Order of precedence: a per-office value (`priorYearOperatingCarryover` /
    `priorYearDepreciationCarryover`, `TRCS:28790-28794`), else the person's aggregate field, routed to that person's
    first Form 8829 office without its own value (`TRCS:27004-27018`), after subtracting per-office values that are
    applied (`TRCS:26898-26905`). If nobody typed any carryover, last year's persisted amount is imported onto the
    **taxpayer's** form, split into line 25 / line 31 by the stored line 44 (`TRCS:2395-2430`); only on the primary
    return (`SCOPED_FORMS_OVERRIDE` null), so an MFS spouse leg imports nothing (D-8829-8).
14. **Standard vs itemized.** Pass 1 treats lines 10/11 as unlimited. If pass 1 took the standard deduction and any
    office has line 9/10/11 amounts, `prepare` re-runs with `HOME_OFFICE_STANDARD_DEDUCTION = true`; with election AUTO
    it also computes a forced-itemized return and keeps the one with the better refund/owed position, adding
    `ITEMIZED_FOR_HOME_OFFICE_INTEREST_AND_TAXES`; on MFS it keeps the standard return and adds
    `MFS_ITEMIZING_WOULD_LOWER_HOME_OFFICE_TAX` (`TRCS:7333-7390`; `HomeOfficeDeductionChoice.java:31-60`). Match
    (`i8829.pdf p. 3`: "You may prefer to itemize").
15. **Schedule A.** Per office with interest or taxes: answer Yes ("also entered in full on the Deductions form") →
    the business part (direct + indirect × pct) is taken off Schedule A mortgage interest and real estate taxes; No →
    the personal part (indirect × (1 − pct)) is added; unanswered with Schedule A amounts → advisory
    (`TRCS:53491-53550`). Spouse offices left out on MFS (`TRCS:4341`). Match.
16. **Line 35 onward.** Each person's line 35 is subtracted from that person's Other incomes `otherGainsLossesLine4`
    (Schedule 1 line 4) with advisory `FORM_8829_LINE35_CASUALTY_TO_FORM_4684`; Form 4684 Section B folds it in
    unless a long-term casualty gain is present (`TRCS:4203-4228`). It stays a QBI item of the business
    (`TRCS:27048`, `:27330-27331`) and is not self-employment income.

**Person scoping (D-1a-1 check).** Form 8829 reads no statements; its inputs are the taxpayer's and spouse's own
personal forms, the spouse's dropped on MFS (`TRCS:4061`, `:4341`), and a dependent tab has no Deductions items. The
household-statement root cause of D-1a-1 does not reach this form. The only cross-person path is the carryover bridge
(D-8829-8).

## 9. Limits and out of scope

- The **simplified method** is a Schedule C computation (`scheduleCSimplifiedHomeOffice` `TRCS:28682-28687`); see
  `schedule-c.md`.
- Partners and Schedule F filers use the Pub. 587 worksheet — not offered (Schedule F has no home-office path).
- Pub. 936 interest limits and the Form 4684 worksheet for line 9 are figured by the user; the app trusts the typed
  "deductible" interest and line 9 amount.
- Tax-exempt parsonage / military housing allowance restriction (`i8829.pdf p. 2`) is not modelled (unverified
  whether any clergy path reduces Form 8829 expenses — no code reference found).
- Part-year business use is handled only by what the user types (the instructions allow that).
