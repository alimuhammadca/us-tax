## 1. Status

Form 8839 is computed for up to six children on one model: Part I, Part II lines 2–18 including the 2025 refundable
portion (line 11b ≤ $5,000 per child → line 13 → Form 1040 line 30) and the nonrefundable credit (line 18 → Schedule
3 line 6c) with the Credit Limit Worksheet (including the Schedule 8812 Credit Limit Worksheet B substitution), and
Part III lines 19–31. The special-needs deemed credit (only in the finality year), the shared-adoption split, the
"no double dip" floor between employer benefits and credit expenses, and the phase-out are modelled. MAGI is derived
after AGI in a second phase. The line 15 carryforward is a typed number; the carryforward to 2026 is not computed.

**10 differences from the IRS rules** (D-8839-1 … D-8839-10). The most serious: the line 5 expense-timing rules are
not modelled — the user types "gross qualified adoption expenses paid … in 2025", so a U.S. adoption not yet final
gets a 2025 credit for 2025 expenses (IRS: 2026), and the 2024 expenses of an adoption that became final in 2025 are
left out (D-8839-1); the adoption of a **spouse's child** and the under-18-or-disabled test are not checked
(D-8839-8); on a joint return a carryforward typed on the spouse's form is dropped (D-8839-5); and the credit MAGI
is built from an AGI that holds the un-phased-out line 1f (D-8839-2, D-1f-1 root cause).

## 4. How it is computed

Methods:

- Merge (joint return; also the primary MFS return, `MfsFormScoper.java:772-790`): `mergeAdoptionForms`
  `TRCS:38664-38710`, called `TRCS:2830-2832`.
- Phase 1 (before AGI): `computeAdoptionBenefits` `TRCS:39265-39929`, called `TRCS:3601`. MFS gate
  `TRCS:39383-39409`; per-child Part II `TRCS:39570-39674`; return totals `TRCS:39796-39831`.
- Phase 2 (after Form 1040 line 18 and the earlier credits): `applyAdoptionCredit` `TRCS:44154-44408`, called
  `TRCS:6906` — after Schedule 3 lines 1–6b and Schedule 8812, **before** Form 8396 (`TRCS:6909`), Form 8859 and the
  final Form 5695 Part I pass.
- Line 30 wiring: `computeLine31ThroughLine38` `TRCS:49900-49918` (called `TRCS:7063`).
- Phase-out fraction: `computeAdoptionPhaseoutFraction` `TRCS:42496-42506`.

Steps, mirroring `new_lines\forms\8839.md` section 6:

0. **Who may claim.** No code T and no adoption inputs → nothing (`TRCS:39379-39382`). MFS without
   `mfsOrHohLivedApartOrLegallySeparated` → advisory, line 1f = full code T, **no Form 8839** — so no credit and no
   carryforward (`TRCS:39393-39409`; D-8839-6). MFS with the answer → full form; the child-in-home and half-cost
   tests are not asked (D-8839-7). Joint return: children of both forms are appended, per-child Part II maps
   re-indexed, but `line15CreditCarryforward` is taken from the taxpayer form only (`TRCS:38686`; D-8839-5); a child
   listed on both forms is two children (D-8839-10).
1. **Part I.** `buildAdoptionChildren` copies names, year of birth, identifying number and the four checks. No
   eligibility test on age / disability or on whether the child is the spouse's child (D-8839-8).
2. **Per child — foreign / special-needs gates** (`TRCS:39573-39610`). Foreign and not final → blocking (overrideable)
   `ADOPTION_BENEFITS_FOREIGN_CHILD_NOT_FINAL` and the child is skipped for both parts (D-8839-9). Special needs with
   no finality answer → blocking `ADOPTION_BENEFITS_FINALIZATION_REQUIRED`.
3. **Line 2** = $17,280 or the shared-adoption share, the same figure as line 19 (`TRCS:39616-39622`). Match.
4. **Line 3** = `child{n}PriorYearCredit`; **line 4** = max(0, 2 − 3) (`TRCS:39623-39624`). Match.
5. **Line 5** = max(0, gross − max(typed reimbursement, this child's code T allocation)) (`TRCS:39634-39644`), with
   advisory `ADOPTION_CREDIT_EXPENSES_REDUCED_BY_EMPLOYER_BENEFITS` when the allocation is larger. Which year's
   expenses belong in "gross" is never asked (D-8839-1).
6. **Line 6** = line 4 for a special-needs child final in 2025 (finality year 2025 or blank); otherwise
   min(line 4, line 5) (`TRCS:39655-39657`). A special-needs adoption final before 2025 → expense-based, advisory
   `ADOPTION_CREDIT_SPECIAL_NEEDS_DEEMED_PRIOR_YEAR` (`TRCS:39645-39654`). Match.
7. **Line 7 MAGI.** Phase 1 uses only the API-only manual MAGI (`TRCS:39496`); normally null, so lines 7–13 are
   left for phase 2. Phase 2 (`TRCS:44230-44292`): line 7 = AGI (Form 1040 line 11b) + Puerto Rico exclusion +
   Form 2555 lines 45 and 50 (both spouses) + Form 4563 line 15 (`TRCS:44251`) — the right add-backs, but AGI
   already holds the phase-1 line 1f, which had no phase-out applied, and phase 2 then raises line 1f without
   recomputing AGI (D-1f-1) → line 7 too low when the exclusion phases out (D-8839-2).
8. **Lines 8–9.** Fraction = (MAGI − 259,190) / 40,000, 4 decimals half-up, floor 0, cap 1 (`TRCS:42496-42506`).
   Match ("at least three places").
9. **Lines 10–11b** per child: line 10 = round(line 6 × fraction); 11a = max(0, 6 − 10); 11b = min(11a, $5,000)
   (`TRCS:44262-44272`; phase 1 `TRCS:39664-39674`). Match.
10. **Lines 11c, 12, 13** = Σ 11b, Σ 11a, line 11c (`TRCS:44286-44289`). Line 13 → `Payments.refundableAdoptionCredit`
    when > 0 (`TRCS:49903-49918`) → line 32. Match.
11. **Lines 14–16** = max(0, 12 − 13); line 15 as typed; 16 = 14 + 15 (`TRCS:44290-44292`). Match.
12. **Line 17 — Credit Limit Worksheet** (`TRCS:44343-44396`): Form 1040 line 18 − (Schedule 3 lines 1, 2, 3, 4,
    5b, 6d, 6f, 6g, 6l, 6m + Form 1040 line 19, or Schedule 8812 Credit Limit Worksheet B line 14 when set), floor 0;
    line 17 = min(line 16, limit). The list matches the IRS, but line 6g (Form 8396) is computed later
    (`TRCS:6909`) and reads null here — the code comment says so (`TRCS:44377-44378`) (D-8839-3).
13. **Line 18** = min(16, limit) → `Schedule3NonrefundableCredits.adoptionCredit` (line 6c) (`TRCS:44401-44407`);
    0 when the limit is 0 (not wired). Match.
14. **Carryforward to 2026.** Not computed; no Carryforward Worksheet, no per-year aging, no next-year import
    (`line15CreditCarryforward` is read only from the form; grep finds no writer) (D-8839-4).
15. **Part III** — see `new_knowledge\1f.md` (line 31 → line 1f). Part III runs before Part II in the same loop; its
    per-child code T allocation also feeds the line 5 floor.
16. **Afterwards.** Form 5695 Part I and Schedule 8812 are re-finalized with line 6c known (`TRCS:6938-6956`).

## 9. Limits and out of scope

- More than six children: the user adds the extra children's per-child amounts by hand (advisory).
- Rev. Proc. 2005-31 / 2010-31 finality-year choice for a foreign adoption is the user's answer.
- Several attempts for one child must be combined by the user on one child row.
- The special-needs determination is trusted from the checkbox.
- Part III: `new_knowledge\1f.md`.
