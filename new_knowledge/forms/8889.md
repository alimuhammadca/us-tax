# Form 8889 — how the app does it

IRS rules: `C:\us-tax\new_lines\forms\8889.md`. Flowchart: `C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\forms\8889.md`.
Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`.
`HSA-UI` = `us-tax-ui/src/app/forms/form-hsa.component.ts`.

---

## 1. Status

Form 8889 is computed per person (taxpayer, spouse) from the HSA form, the person's Forms 1099-SA (matched by
recipient TIN, box 5 "HSA" checked) and the person's W-2 box 12 code W. Part I gives the deduction, which
**overwrites** any typed Schedule 1 line 13; Part II and Part III give the taxable amount for Schedule 1 line 8f and
the 20% / 10% taxes for Schedule 2 lines 17c / 17d (typed values win there). The 6% excise on excess contributions is
computed for Form 5329 Part VII. On a joint return with family coverage the $8,550 family limit is split equally by
default.

**10 differences from the IRS rules** (D-8889-1 … D-8889-10), plus the already-recorded D-8-13 (the line 8f advisory
says the app does not compute Form 8889) and D-4b-9 (an HFD on Form 1040 line 4 is not linked to Form 8889 line 10).
The most serious: on separate returns each spouse gets the full family limit (D-8889-1); excess employer
contributions are never added to income (D-8889-2); on a married-filing-separately return the other spouse's 6%
excise lands on this return (D-8889-3); and the Form 8889 preview prints blank, so the form the IRS requires is not
produced (D-8889-8).

## 4. How it is computed

Methods:

- Wiring: `TRCS:2901-2956` (family split `:2909-2932`; per-person compute `:2933-2938`; injections `:2939-2956`).
- Wrapper: `computeForm8889` `TRCS:9937-10019`. Pure lines: `computeForm8889FromInputs` `TRCS:9613-9715`.
- 1099-SA readers: `sum1099SaHsaDistributionsForSsn` `TRCS:10178-10191`, `sum1099SaCode2ExcessWithdrawal`
  `TRCS:10200-10219`, `has1099SaDisabilityOrDeathCode` `TRCS:10228-10242`.
- Injections: `injectHsaDeduction` `TRCS:10249-10255`, `injectHsaOtherIncomeLine8f` `TRCS:10459-10468`,
  `injectHsaSchedule2Taxes` `TRCS:10471-10489`.
- Advisories and excise: `TRCS:3374-3412`, `computeExcessHsaExcise` `TRCS:10121-10175`, Form 5329 `TRCS:5370-5376`.

Steps, mirroring `new_lines\forms\8889.md` section 6:

1. **Gate.** Nothing unless `hasHsaActivity` is Yes (`TRCS:9942`).
2. **Line 1 / 3.** Annual limit $4,300 or $8,550 by `coverageType` (`TRCS:9623-9625`, constants
   `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/ReferenceData.java:631-634`). Last-month rule or "all 12 months" →
   full limit; otherwise limit × months ÷ 12, months defaulting to 12 when blank (advisory
   `FORM_8889_MONTHS_ELIGIBLE_NEEDED_*`) (`TRCS:9628-9635`, `:9947`, `:10069-10089`). One coverage type for the whole
   year: a mid-year switch between self-only and family is not modelled (D-8889-5). The $1,000 catch-up never goes on
   line 3 (step 6).
3. **Line 4 / 5.** Typed Archer MSA amount (`TRCS:9637-9638`); not taken from the app's own Form 8853 lines 1 + 2
   (D-8889-10).
4. **Line 6.** Typed allocation, else the automatic split, capped at line 5 (`TRCS:9644-9646`). The automatic split
   ($8,550 ÷ 2 = $4,275) runs **only** on a joint return when both spouses' forms have HSA activity and at least one is
   family (`TRCS:2909-2931`); on a married-filing-separately return each spouse keeps the full line 5 (D-8889-1).
5. **Line 7.** $1,000 if age ≥ 55 at year end from the date of birth (`TRCS:9945-9946`), prorated by months unless
   full-year / last-month rule (`TRCS:9651-9660`) — for **every** filer, including unmarried and self-only filers whose
   $1,000 belongs on line 3 (D-8889-7, printed-line placement only).
6. **Line 8–12.** Line 9 = typed, else the person's W-2 box 12 code W (SSN-matched; null SSN → none)
   (`TRCS:9971-9974`, helper `:41568-41600`). Line 10 typed. Lines 8, 11, 12 as on the form (`TRCS:9661-9665`).
7. **Line 13.** min(line 2, line 12) (`TRCS:9666`); plus a prior-year excess absorbed by unused room (Form 5329 line 43
   rule) (`TRCS:10002-10012`). Injected into the person's income adjustments, **overwriting** a typed line 13
   (`TRCS:10249-10255`). Nothing removes the deduction for a filer who can be claimed as a dependent (D-8889-4).
8. **Excess contributions.** Excise base = own + line 11 − line 8, less a withdrawal by the due date, plus the prior
   excess not absorbed; 6% of the smaller of that and the year-end value (`TRCS:10121-10175`) → Form 5329 Part VII →
   Schedule 2 line 8. The **employer** part above the limit is included in the excise base but never added to income
   (D-8889-2). The taxpayer + spouse excise is added without a married-filing-separately guard (`TRCS:3391-3399`,
   `:5370-5376`) (D-8889-3).
9. **Line 14a.** Override, else box 1 of the person's 1099-SAs with box 5 "HSA" checked and recipient TIN = the
   person's SSN (`TRCS:9950-9953`, `:10178-10191`). A 1099-SA with no box-5 check or no TIN reaches no form
   (D-8889-9). Box 4 (FMV at death) is not used (D-8889-6).
10. **Line 14b.** Typed + the principal (box 1 − box 2) of code-2 1099-SAs (`TRCS:9957-9958`, `:10200-10219`).
11. **Line 15 / 16.** Typed medical expenses; line 16 = max(0, 14c − 15) (`TRCS:9669-9673`).
12. **Line 17a / 17b.** Exception = checkbox, or age ≥ 66 at year end, or any code 3 / 4 1099-SA (`TRCS:9978-9980`);
    full exception → 17b = 0; otherwise 20% × (line 16 − partial exempt amount − code-2 earnings) (`TRCS:9679-9681`).
    Code 6 (death, non-spouse beneficiary after the year of death) and code 5 (prohibited transaction) are not
    recognised (D-8889-6).
13. **Part III.** Lines 18 / 19 typed; 20 = sum; 21 = 10% (`TRCS:9684-9687`). Advisory when the prior-year last-month
    rule failed and line 18 is blank (`TRCS:10040-10059`).
14. **Routing.** 16 + 20 → `otherIncomeForm88898f` unless a typed / imported 8f exists (`TRCS:10459-10468`); 17b and
    21 summed over taxpayer + spouse (spouse dropped on MFS) → Schedule 2 lines 17c / 17d unless typed
    (`TRCS:2953-2956`, `:10471-10489`), read at `TRCS:43248-43249`. On MFS the spouse's line 13 and line 8f are dropped
    because the spouse forms are nulled in the adjustments and other-income steps (`TRCS:32782`, `:32054`).
15. **Rounding.** `roundMoney` (half-up, whole dollars) on each line.

## 8. Compared with the IRS rules

| # | IRS rule (source) | What the app does | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Limit $4,300 / $8,550; last-month rule → full year (`i8889.pdf p. 2, p. 4`) | As stated (`TRCS:9628-9635`) | Match |
| 2 | Part-year: monthly worksheet with each month's coverage, $0 for Medicare months (`i8889.pdf p. 6`) | Months × one coverage type ÷ 12; Medicare only via the months the user counts | **Not supported D-8889-5** (coverage switch mid-year: silent, can over- or under-state) |
| 3 | Line 4 = Form 8853 lines 1 + 2 (`f8889.pdf p. 1`) | Typed separately (`TRCS:9984`) although Form 8853 is computed in the same run | **Deviation D-8889-10** (low; a blank line 4 overstates the limit) |
| 4 | Family coverage on separate HSAs: family limit divided equally (or as agreed) "regardless of whether you file jointly or separately" (`i8889.pdf p. 4, p. 7`) | Automatic split only on MFJ (`TRCS:2909-2931`); on MFS each spouse gets the full $8,550 unless line 6 is typed | **Deviation D-8889-1** (over-deduction up to $4,275 per return; silent; medium) |
| 5 | Catch-up: unmarried or self-only → line 3; married with family → line 7 (`i8889.pdf p. 4-5, p. 7`) | Always line 7 (`TRCS:9651-9660`); line 8 total equal unless line 4 exceeds line 3 | **Deviation D-8889-7** (printed lines 3 / 5 / 7 differ; low) |
| 6 | Line 9 = W-2 box 12 code W, Employer Contribution Worksheet when timing differs (`i8889.pdf p. 7`) | Typed, else code W of the person's W-2s | Match (worksheet = typed) |
| 7 | Line 13 = min(line 2, line 12); "you cannot deduct contributions if you are someone else's dependent" (`i8889.pdf p. 4, p. 8`) | min as stated; no dependent check (the app has `someoneCanClaimYou`, `TRCS:14423`, but the HSA path never reads it) | **Not supported D-8889-4** (silent over-deduction; low–medium) |
| 8 | Excess employer contributions not in W-2 box 1 → "Other income" (`i8889.pdf p. 8`) | Counted in the 6% excise base (`TRCS:10136`), never added to income; the advisory does not mention it | **Deviation D-8889-2** (income under-reported; silent; medium) |
| 9 | Excess contributions → Form 5329 Part VII (`i8889.pdf p. 8`) | 6% of the smaller of excess and year-end value | Match |
| 10 | Separate returns: each spouse's own Form 5329 tax | Taxpayer + spouse excise added with no MFS guard (`TRCS:3391-3399`, `:5370-5376`); on a primary MFS return the spouse's HSA form is kept (`multireturn/MfsFormScoper.java:780-791`), so the spouse's excise lands on this return (the Archer MSA twin has the guard, `TRCS:5358-5360`) | **Deviation D-8889-3** (tax overstated on MFS; medium) |
| 11 | Line 14a = Form 1099-SA box 1, all HSAs (`i8889.pdf p. 8`) | Box 1 of 1099-SAs with box 5 "HSA" checked and TIN = SSN; others dropped | Match when flagged; **Not supported D-8889-9** for an unflagged or TIN-less 1099-SA (silent; low; override exists) |
| 12 | Death, non-spouse beneficiary: line 14a = FMV at death (1099-SA box 4), skip Part I, no 20% tax; prohibited transaction: FMV on January 1 (`i8889.pdf p. 3`; `i1099sa--2025.pdf p. 2-3`) | Box 1 used, box 4 ignored; only codes 3 / 4 waive the 20% (code 6 not); Part I still offered | **Not supported D-8889-6** (rare; silent; low) |
| 13 | Line 14b: rollovers + excess withdrawn by due date (`i8889.pdf p. 8`) | Typed + code-2 principal | Match |
| 14 | Line 16 = 14c − 15 → Schedule 1 line 8f (`f8889.pdf p. 1`) | As stated; typed 8f wins | Match |
| 15 | 20% unless death, disability, age 65; 20% only of the part not excepted (`i8889.pdf p. 9`) | Checkbox (full), partial amount, age ≥ 66 automatic, codes 3 / 4 | Match |
| 16 | Part III lines 18–21, 10% (`i8889.pdf p. 9`; `f8889.pdf p. 1`) | Typed 18 / 19, computed 20 / 21; advisory for 18 | Match |
| 17 | HFD on line 10 must equal the IRA HFD excluded on Form 1040 line 4 (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 27`; `i8889.pdf p. 7`) | Two unrelated typed fields | D-4b-9 (already recorded) |
| 18 | File Form 8889 with the return; SSN of the beneficiary in the heading (`i8889.pdf p. 1, p. 4`) | Computed and stored, but the preview is blank and the header is empty; the line 8f advisory tells the user to complete the form externally (D-8-13) | **Not supported D-8889-8** (the filed form is not produced; medium) |
| 19 | MFJ: separate Form 8889 per spouse, lines 13 added (`i8889.pdf p. 4`) | Two forms, two rows, both lines 13 on Schedule 1 | Match |

## 9. Limits and out of scope

- **Death of the account beneficiary, "statement" + controlling Forms 8889, deemed distributions (prohibited
  transaction, loan security)** — not modelled beyond what the user types (see D-8889-6).
- **Mistaken distributions (Notice 2004-50)** — the user leaves them out of line 14a by override.
- **Medicare months** — folded into "months eligible".
- **Form 5498-SA** — stored only; line 2 is typed.
- **Excess withdrawn after filing (section 301.9100-2 amended return)** — no amended-return workflow.
