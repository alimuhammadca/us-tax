IRS rules: `C:\us-tax\new_lines\forms\982.md`. Flowchart: `C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\forms\982.md`.
Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`.
`OI-UI` = `us-tax-ui/src/app/forms/form-other-incomes-taxpayer.component.ts` (the spouse copy,
`form-other-incomes-spouse.component.ts`, has the same fields).

---

## 1. Status

Form 982 Parts I and II are computed. The user types the canceled debt on the Other incomes form and answers the
section 108 questions there (insolvency balance sheet, main-home mortgage, title 11, farm, QRPBI, basis figures and the
two elections). One method (`computeCodExclusionBreakdown`) figures the exclusion in the statutory precedence
title 11 → QPRI → insolvency → farm → QRPBI; the same result reduces Schedule 1 line 8c and builds Form 982 Part I, so
the two cannot disagree. Part II reduces the carryovers the app persists (NOL, general business credit, capital loss,
passive loss, foreign tax credit) and the basis lines 4, 5, 10a, 10b, 11a–c, after the 2025 tax is figured, so next
year's carryforward bridges import the reduced values. Part III (corporations) is not applicable.

**5 differences from the IRS rules** (D-982-1 … D-982-5), plus the already-recorded **D-8-2**: Form 1099-C box 2 is
never read, so canceled debt reaches line 8c (and Form 982) only if typed. The most serious of the new ones: on a joint
return a title 11 answer on either spouse's form excludes **all** canceled debt of both spouses (D-982-1), and the
farm and QRPBI exclusions are never limited, so an excess the IRS adds back to income stays excluded (D-982-2,
D-982-3).

## 4. How it is computed

Methods:

- Gross canceled debt and line 8c: `computeOtherIncomes`, `TRCS:32356-32375` (spouse form set to null on MFS,
  `TRCS:32054`).
- Exclusion (Part I): `computeCodExclusionBreakdown` `TRCS:31946-32013`; QPRI ordering rule `qpriAfterOrderingRule`
  `TRCS:53457-53469`.
- Form 982 model: `buildForm982` `TRCS:55620-55644`.
- Part II: called from the end of `prepare` at `TRCS:7680-7701`; joint-return input merge `mergeForm982Inputs`
  `TRCS:55320-55359`; cascade `applySection108AttributeReduction` `TRCS:55370-55608`; one step
  `applySection108Reduction` `TRCS:55259-55275`.
- Printed map: `populateForm982Fields` `TRCS:55655-55722`.

Steps, mirroring `new_lines\forms\982.md` section 6:

1. **Gross canceled debt.** Typed line 8c of the taxpayer + spouse + `imported1099CLine8cCancellationOfDebt`
   (`TRCS:32360`). Nothing writes the imported key, so a 1099-C reaches line 8c only if its box 2 is typed (D-8-2).
2. **Line 1a — title 11.** If either form says Yes, the **whole** gross canceled debt (both spouses on a joint return)
   is excluded as title 11 and every other exclusion is skipped (`TRCS:31967-31968`, `:31990-31994`). There is no
   per-debt or per-spouse amount (D-982-1).
3. **Line 1e — QPRI.** Per form with the box Yes: the typed canceled amount; when the loan balance and its qualified
   part are both entered, the canceled amount less the non-qualified part, with the qualified part capped at $750,000
   ($375,000 when the filing status contains "separately") (`TRCS:53457-53469`). Without those two fields the typed
   amount is used **uncapped** (D-982-5). QPRI exclusion = min(QPRI, gross) (`TRCS:31995`).
4. **Line 1b — insolvency.** Per form with the box Yes: max(0, liabilities − FMV of assets), summed over both forms
   (`TRCS:31976-31982`); exclusion = min(insolvency, what QPRI left) (`TRCS:31997`). The combined insolvency of both
   spouses can shelter either spouse's debt (see §8 row 6, unverified).
5. **Line 1c — farm.** Typed `codFarmExclusionAmount`, min(what is left) (`TRCS:31999`). The 50% gross-receipts and
   qualified-lender tests are not asked; the "tax attributes + business basis" limit is not applied (D-982-2).
6. **Line 1d — QRPBI.** Typed `codQrpbiExclusionAmount`, min(what is left) (`TRCS:32001`). Neither QRPBI limit
   (principal over net FMV; depreciable real property basis) is applied to the exclusion (D-982-3).
7. **Line 2 and line 8c.** Total = QPRI + insolvency + farm + QRPBI (or title 11), rounded (`TRCS:32002-32012`).
   Line 8c = max(0, gross − total); null when there is no canceled debt (`TRCS:32373-32375`). Form 982 is built only
   when the total is positive (`TRCS:55621`); box 1x is checked when that exclusion is positive (`TRCS:55625-55629`).
8. **Line 3.** `codElectSection1221Depreciable`, tri-state (unanswered = both boxes blank) (`TRCS:7698`,
   `:55685-55689`). It is printed only; the line 5 basis is whatever the user types.
9. **Part II inputs.** On MFJ the two forms are merged: bases and money added, liabilities after discharge resolved
   per spouse then added, main-home answers taken from whichever spouse gave them (advisory on conflict), elections Yes
   if either made them (`TRCS:55320-55359`). Otherwise the taxpayer's form is used (`TRCS:7691-7693`).
10. **Line 4 (QRPBI).** min(QRPBI exclusion, `codDepreciableRealPropertyBasis`) (`TRCS:55388-55394`). The part the
    basis cannot absorb is not added back to income (D-982-3).
11. **Line 10b (QPRI).** Blocking flag if "still own the home" is unanswered; if Yes, min(QPRI exclusion, home basis)
    (`TRCS:55403-55429`).
12. **Cascade pool.** Only title 11 + insolvency + farm exclusions (`TRCS:55438-55440`) — QPRI and QRPBI stay on their
    own tracks, as section 108(b)(1) requires.
13. **Line 5** (election): min(pool, `codDepreciablePropertyBasis`) (`TRCS:55448-55456`).
14. **Line 6 NOL:** reduces Form 172 `nolCarryforwardToNextYear` dollar for dollar (`TRCS:55459-55467`).
15. **Line 7 GBC:** reduces `generalBusinessCreditCarryforward`; a $1 credit absorbs $3 of exclusion
    (`TRCS:55470-55478`, rate logic `:55259-55275`).
16. **Line 8 MTC: skipped** (`TRCS:55480`). The comment says no minimum tax credit carryforward is persisted, but Form
    8801 is computed earlier in the same run (`TRCS:6867`) with line 26 (the carryforward to 2026) set at
    `TRCS:45229` and stored in `out_form_8801.line26` (`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/entity/OutForm8801.java:71`)
    (D-982-4).
17. **Line 9:** next-year short-term, then long-term, capital loss carryover (`TRCS:55483-55506`).
18. **Line 10a:** only when `codPropertyBasisAfterDischarge` is entered and the debt is not farm debt; limit = basis;
    for title 11 / insolvency also basis + money − liabilities after (typed, else liabilities before − own 8c)
    (`TRCS:55513-55531`). Not entered with exclusion left → advisory (`TRCS:55599-55607`).
19. **Lines 11a–c (farm):** depreciable, land, other, in that order (`TRCS:55540-55553`).
20. **Line 12:** `rentalPassiveLossCarryforward`, and its AMT twin by the same amount (`TRCS:55556-55576`). Passive
    credit carryovers are not tracked by the app, so nothing is reduced for them.
21. **Line 13:** each Form 1116 `carryforwardToNextYears`, 33⅓ cents (`TRCS:55579-55593`).
22. **Leftover** under title 11 / insolvency / farm stays excluded (comment `TRCS:55302-55303`) — correct for title 11
    and insolvency, wrong for farm (D-982-2).
23. **Rounding.** `roundMoney` (half-up, whole dollars) on each exclusion and each reduction.

## 8. Compared with the IRS rules

| # | IRS rule (source) | What the app does | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Canceled debt on Schedule 1 line 8c, "may be shown in box 2 of Form 1099-C" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 91`) | Typed only; 1099-C box 2 never read (`TRCS:32360`) | **Deviation D-8-2** (already recorded) |
| 2 | Title 11: discharge "granted by the court or … under a plan approved by the court" while "you are under the jurisdiction of the court" is excluded; other exclusions do not apply (`i982.pdf p. 1–3`) | A Yes on either spouse's form excludes the whole gross canceled debt of both spouses, including debts canceled outside the case or owed by the spouse who is not in bankruptcy (`TRCS:31967-31968`, `:31990-31994`) | **Deviation D-982-1** (under-reported income on a joint return; silent) |
| 3 | QPRI: ordering rule, $750,000 / $375,000 limit (`i982.pdf p. 1, p. 3`) | Applied when loan balance and qualified part are entered (`TRCS:53457-53469`); otherwise the typed amount, uncapped | Match with inputs; **Deviation D-982-5** without them (low) |
| 4 | QPRI before insolvency unless insolvency elected; non-qualified part may use insolvency (`i982.pdf p. 2–3`) | QPRI first, insolvency on the remainder; electing insolvency = leaving the QPRI box No (`TRCS:31995-31998`) | Match |
| 5 | Insolvency limited to liabilities − FMV of assets immediately before (`i982.pdf p. 2`) | Exactly that per form, floored at 0 (`TRCS:31978-31981`) | Match |
| 6 | Measuring insolvency on a joint return | Both spouses' insolvency amounts are added and applied to the joint canceled debt (`TRCS:31962-31998`) | Unverified — Pub. 4681 (which explains it) is not in the sources |
| 7 | Farm and QRPBI do not apply in title 11 or to the extent insolvent (`i982.pdf p. 2–3`) | Cascade order title 11 → QPRI → insolvency → farm → QRPBI | Match |
| 8 | Farm: "can't exclude more than the total of your (a) tax attributes … and (b) basis of property used … in a trade or business …. Any excess is included in income" (`i982.pdf p. 2`); 50% gross-receipts and qualified-lender tests | Typed amount excluded in full (limited only by the remaining debt); leftover after lines 6–13 treated as forgiven (`TRCS:31999`, `:55302`); tests not asked | **Deviation D-982-2** (income under-reported; silent) |
| 9 | QRPBI: limited to principal over net FMV and to depreciable real property basis; "Any excess is included in income" (`i982.pdf p. 3`) | Typed amount excluded in full on line 8c; line 4 reduces basis only up to the basis entered, the rest is not added back (`TRCS:32001`, `:55388-55394`) | **Deviation D-982-3** (income under-reported; silent) |
| 10 | Line 2 = total excluded within the limits (`i982.pdf p. 3`) | Sum of the exclusions (`TRCS:32010`) | Match (subject to D-982-2/-3/-5) |
| 11 | Attribute order lines 5–13; QPRI → 10b only; QRPBI → line 4 only (`i982.pdf p. 2–4`) | As listed in §4 steps 10–21 | Match except line 8 |
| 12 | Line 8: minimum tax credit "as of the beginning of the tax year immediately after the tax year of the discharge", 33⅓ cents (`i982.pdf p. 2`; `f982.pdf p. 1`) | Never reduced (`TRCS:55480`) although Form 8801 line 26 is computed and stored (`TRCS:6867`, `:45229`); the exclusion meant for it falls through to capital loss, basis, passive loss and FTC | **Deviation D-982-4** (MTC carryforward overstated, later attributes over-reduced; low–medium) |
| 13 | Line 10a limit for title 11 / insolvency: basis − liabilities after (`i982.pdf p. 4`); nonbusiness smallest-of rule (`p. 2`) | min(basis, basis + money − liabilities after) (`TRCS:55516-55525`) | Match |
| 14 | Line 10b: smaller of the QPRI part of line 2 and home basis, only if you keep the home (`i982.pdf p. 4`) | As stated; unanswered blocks (`TRCS:55403-55429`) | Match |
| 15 | Credits reduced 33⅓ cents per dollar (`i982.pdf p. 2, p. 4`) | $1 of credit absorbs $3 (`TRCS:55259-55275`) | Match |
| 16 | Line 12 includes passive **credit** carryovers (`i982.pdf p. 2`) | Only passive loss carryovers; the app keeps no passive credit carryover | Match for what the app tracks (see §9) |

## 9. Limits and out of scope

- **Part III / section 1082** — corporations only.
- **Late elections / amended returns** (section 301.9100-2) — no Form 1040-X workflow.
- **Passive activity credit carryovers (Form 8582-CR)** — not tracked anywhere, so line 12 holds losses only.
- **Section 108(i)** (2009–2010 deferral) and **section 382(l)(5)** (corporations) — not applicable.
- **Basis-reduction statement** — the filer must attach it.
- **Joint-return insolvency** — see §8 row 6.
