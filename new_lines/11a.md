# Form 1040 line 11a (and 11b) — Adjusted gross income

Tax year 2025. What the IRS requires. How the app does it: `C:\us-tax\new_knowledge\11a.md`. Picture:
`C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\11a.md`.

---

## 1. At a glance

Adjusted gross income (AGI) is total income (Form 1040 line 9) minus the adjustments to income (line 10, which is
Schedule 1 line 26). It is entered on line 11a at the bottom of page 1 and repeated, unchanged, on line 11b at the top
of page 2, where the deduction and tax lines start. AGI can be zero or negative; only taxable income (line 15) is
stopped at zero.

```text
Form 1040 line 11a = line 9 − line 10          (no floor; may be negative)
Form 1040 line 11b = line 11a                  (a copy)
```

Terms used below:

- **AGI** — adjusted gross income, the figure on line 11a / 11b.
- **MAGI** — modified adjusted gross income: AGI changed (usually increased) by amounts that a particular rule lists.
  There is **no single MAGI**: each credit, deduction or limit has its own definition (section 10 below).
- **Add-back** — an amount that was excluded from income or deducted in figuring AGI and is added back for a MAGI.

## 2. The IRS wording

**Form 1040 (2025)** (form face, `docs\IRS-Forms\f1040.pdf`):

- p. 1, line 9: "Add lines 1z, 2b, 3b, 4b, 5b, 6b, 7a, and 8. This is your total income"
- p. 1, line 10: "Adjustments to income from Schedule 1, line 26"
- p. 1, line 11a: "Subtract line 10 from line 9. This is your adjusted gross income"
- p. 2, line 11b: "Amount from line 11a (adjusted gross income)"
- p. 2, line 15: "Subtract line 14 from line 11b. If zero or less, enter -0-. This is your taxable income"

**2025 Instructions for Form 1040** (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 33`), under "Total Income and Adjusted Gross Income", the
only entry is: "Line 10 — Enter any adjustments to income from Schedule 1, line 26, on line 10." There are no
separate instructions for lines 11a and 11b; the form face defines them.

**Rounding** (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 23`): "If you have to add two or more amounts to figure the amount to enter on
a line, include cents when adding the amounts and round off only the total." Lines 9 and 10 are already entered line
amounts, so on a rounded return line 11a is their whole-dollar difference.

**Negative AGI.** The form puts the "-0-" floor on line 15 only. Line 9 can be negative (Schedule 1 line 8a NOL "as a
negative number", `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 91`; business, rental, farm and capital losses) and line 10 can exceed line
9, so AGI can be negative.

## 3. What goes on this line

- Line 9 (see `new_lines\9.md`), every operand with its sign.
- Minus line 10 = Schedule 1 line 26 (Part II adjustments: educator expenses, certain business expenses, HSA, moving
  expenses for Armed Forces, ½ self-employment tax, SE retirement and health insurance, early-withdrawal penalty,
  alimony paid, IRA deduction, student loan interest, Archer MSA, line 24 other adjustments).

## 4. What does not go here

| Look-alike | Where it goes | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Standard / itemized deduction, QBI deduction, Schedule 1-A deductions | Lines 12e, 13a, 13b — after AGI ("below the line") | form face p. 2 |
| Any MAGI (IRA, Roth, education, PTC, …) | Figured on the worksheet or form that needs it; never written on line 11a | each form's instructions (section 10) |
| Excluded income (Form 2555, Form 4563, Puerto Rico, Form 8815, employer adoption benefits) | Not in AGI; added back only inside particular MAGI figures | section 10 |

## 6. How to figure it, step by step

1. Finish line 9 (all eight operands final, including Form 8814 line 12 on Schedule 1 line 8z and the Form 4972
   reduction of line 5b).
2. Finish Schedule 1 Part II. Two of its lines depend on a MAGI built from line 9: the IRA deduction (line 20) and the
   student loan interest deduction (line 21) (worksheets on `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 97–99`). Their worksheets start
   from line 9 and subtract the other Part II lines, so no circularity arises; when social security benefits are
   taxable as well, Pub. 590-A Appendix B worksheets resolve the IRA ↔ social security loop (`p590a.pdf` App. B).
3. Line 10 = Schedule 1 line 26.
4. Line 11a = line 9 − line 10. Keep a negative result as a negative number.
5. Copy line 11a to line 11b.
6. Every later figure that the instructions tie to "line 11a" or "line 11b" — or a MAGI built from it — uses this
   final amount. If any operand of line 9 or line 10 changes, AGI and everything built from it is refigured.

## 8. Married filing jointly / separately

AGI has no person-level rule of its own. On a joint return it is the couple's combined AGI; on separate returns each
spouse's own. Several MAGI rules are joint figures on a joint return (for example the Schedule 1-A and Form 8863
phase-outs) and per-filer on separate returns; some MAGI-based benefits are barred on MFS (student loan interest
`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 98`; education credits `i8863_2025.pdf`; Schedule 1-A tips / overtime / car loan / senior
require a joint return when married, `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 6–7`).

## 11. Worked example

Casey and Drew Sample (000-00-0020 / 000-00-0021), married filing jointly:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Line 9 total income | $142,600 |
| Schedule 1 line 13 HSA deduction | $4,300 |
| Schedule 1 line 15 deductible part of SE tax | $2,119 |
| Schedule 1 line 21 student loan interest | $2,500 |
| Line 10 (Schedule 1 line 26) | $8,919 |

Line 11a = 142,600 − 8,919 = **$133,681**; line 11b = **$133,681**.

Their student-loan MAGI is line 9 − (4,300 + 2,119) = $136,181 (not AGI); their Schedule 8812 MAGI is $133,681 (no
exclusions). Two different MAGIs from the same return.

Negative case: Jamie Sample (000-00-0030), single, wages $18,000, Schedule C loss ($30,000), HSA deduction $1,000.
Line 9 = −$12,000; line 11a = −$13,000; line 15 = $0.
