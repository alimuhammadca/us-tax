# Form 1040 line 1a — Total amount from Form(s) W-2, box 1

`C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\1a.md`.

## 1. At a glance

Form 1040 line 1a is the sum of box 1 ("Wages, tips, other compensation") of every Form W-2 issued to **you** — and,
on a joint return, to **your spouse**. Two kinds of amount that sit inside W-2 box 1 are taken out and reported on
Schedule 1 instead: wages earned as a prison inmate (Schedule 1 line 8u) and a pension or annuity from a
nonqualified deferred compensation plan or a nongovernmental section 457 plan (Schedule 1 line 8t). A W-2 with the
"Statutory employee" box checked does not go on line 1a at all — it goes on Schedule C. When a W-2 is missing or
wrong and no corrected one arrives, Form 4852 (Substitute for Form W-2) takes its place, and its line 7a is used
like box 1.

```text
Form 1040 line 1a =
    Σ W-2 box 1            (each W-2 of you, + your spouse if married filing jointly;
                            statutory-employee W-2s left out;
                            a Form 4852 line 7a stands in for a missing / incorrect W-2)
  − wages earned while an inmate in a penal institution        (→ Schedule 1 line 8u)
  − pension / annuity from an NQDC or nongovernmental §457 plan included in box 1 (→ Schedule 1 line 8t)
```

Terms used below:

- **NQDC** — nonqualified deferred compensation: an employer plan that lets an employee defer pay but does not meet
  the rules for a tax-qualified plan (such as a 401(k)).
- **Nongovernmental section 457 plan** — a deferred-compensation plan of a tax-exempt (non-government) employer.
- **Statutory employee** — a worker who is an independent contractor under the common-law rules but is treated by
  law as an employee for social security and Medicare (certain drivers, full-time life insurance agents, traveling
  salespeople, homeworkers). Their W-2 has the box 13 "Statutory employee" box checked.

## 2. The IRS wording

**Form 1040 (2025), line 1a:** "Total amount from Form(s) W-2, box 1 (see instructions)".

**2025 Instructions for Form 1040 — Line 1a** (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 23`):

> "Enter the total amount from Form(s) W-2, box 1. If a joint return, also include your spouse's income from
> Form(s) W-2, box 1."
>
> "If you earned wages while you were an inmate in a penal institution, report these amounts on Schedule 1,
> line 8u. Do not report these wages on line 1a. See the instructions for Schedule 1, line 8u."

(`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 24`, continuing line 1a):

> "If you received a pension or annuity from a nonqualified deferred compensation plan or a nongovernmental section
> 457 plan and it was reported in box 1 of Form W-2, do not include this amount on Form 1040, line 1a. This amount
> is reported on Schedule 1, line 8t."

**Were You a Statutory Employee?** (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 25`):

> "If you were a statutory employee, the "Statutory employee" box in box 13 of your Form W-2 should be checked. …
> Statutory employees report the amount shown in box 1 of Form W-2 on a Schedule C along with any related business
> expenses."

**Missing or Incorrect Form W-2?** (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 25`):

> "Your employer is required to provide or send Form W-2 to you no later than February 2, 2026. … Even if you don't
> get a Form W-2, you must still report your earnings. If you lose your Form W-2 or it is incorrect, ask your
> employer for a new one."

**Schedule 1 line 8t / 8u** (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 92`):

> "Line 8t … Enter the amount that you received as a pension or annuity from a nonqualified deferred compensation
> plan or a nongovernmental 457 plan. This may be shown in box 11 of Form W-2. If you received such an amount but
> box 11 is blank, contact your employer or the payer for the amount received."
>
> "Line 8u Wages earned while incarcerated. Enter the amount that you received for services performed while an
> inmate in a penal institution. You may receive Form(s) W-2 or Form(s) 1099."

**What W-2 box 1 holds** (General Instructions for Forms W-2 and W-3 (2025), `iw2w3--2025.pdf p. 17`):

> "Box 1—Wages, tips, other compensation. Show the total taxable wages, tips, and other compensation that you paid
> to your employee during the year. However, do not include elective deferrals (such as employee contributions to a
> section 401(k) or 403(b) plan) except section 501(c)(18) contributions."

The list that follows includes "3. Total tips reported by the employee to the employer (not allocated tips)",
"14. Distributions to an employee or former employee from an NQDC plan (including a rabbi trust) or a
nongovernmental section 457(b) plan", "15. Amounts includible in income under section 457(f) because the amounts
are no longer subject to a substantial risk of forfeiture" and "16. Payments to statutory employees …".

**What W-2 box 11 holds** (`iw2w3--2025.pdf p. 19`):

> "Report distributions to an employee from a nonqualified plan or nongovernmental section 457(b) plan in box 11.
> Also report these distributions in box 1. … Under nonqualified plans or nongovernmental 457(b) plans, deferred
> amounts that are no longer subject to a substantial risk of forfeiture are taxable even if not distributed.
> Report these amounts in boxes 3 (up to the social security wage base) and 5. … If you made distributions and are
> also reporting any deferrals in boxes 3 and/or 5, do not complete box 11."

The NQDC Reporting Example Chart (`iw2w3--2025.pdf p. 32`) shows that box 11 is **not always** a part of box 1:
Example 3 (prior-year deferrals vesting, no distribution) gives "Box 1 = $180", "Box 11 = $115"; Examples 5, 7 and 8
(a distribution in the same year as reportable deferrals) put the $50 distribution in box 1 but give "Box 11 = $0".

## 3. What goes on this line

- W-2 box 1 of every W-2 issued to you (and to your spouse on a joint return): wages, salaries, bonuses, prizes and
  awards from the employer, reported tips, taxable fringe benefits, taxable group-term life insurance over $50,000,
  designated Roth contributions, section 457(f) amounts that vested, and the other items in the box 1 list
  (`iw2w3--2025.pdf p. 17`).
- Form 4852 line 7a ("Wages, tips, and other compensation") for each W-2 that was never received or was incorrect
  and not corrected in time (`f4852.pdf p. 1–2`).
- Wages earned abroad shown in W-2 box 1. They stay on line 1a; the foreign earned income exclusion, if claimed, is
  taken on Form 2555 and Schedule 1 (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 23`: "You must also report earned income, such as wages
  and tips, from sources outside the United States. If you worked abroad, you may be able to exclude part or all of
  your foreign earned income. For details, see Pub. 54 and Form 2555.").
- Nontaxable Medicaid waiver payments that the employer included in box 1. They stay in line 1a and are taken back
  out as a negative amount on Schedule 1 line 8s (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 92`: "on line 8s, enter the total amount
  of the nontaxable payments reported on Form 1040 or 1040-SR, line 1a or 1d … (as a negative number)").
- Wages shown on a Guam, U.S. Virgin Islands or American Samoa W-2 (W-2GU, W-2VI, W-2AS) of a U.S. citizen or
  resident who is **not** a bona fide resident of that territory: Pub. 570 tells such a person to "file your income
  tax return with the United States. • Include income from worldwide sources on your U.S. return" (`p570.pdf
  p. 24`, Guam; the other territories have parallel rules in the same chapter).

## 5. Where the numbers come from

| Source document / form | Box or line | How it enters line 1a |
|---|---|---|
| Form W-2 | Box a (employee's SSN) | Decides whose W-2 it is: yours, your spouse's (joint return only), or someone else's (not on this return) |
| Form W-2 | Box 1 | Added |
| Form W-2 | Box 13 "Statutory employee" | If checked, the whole box 1 is left out (goes to Schedule C) |
| Form W-2 | Box 11 "Nonqualified plans" | Identifies the NQDC / §457 distribution inside box 1 that moves to Schedule 1 line 8t. Box 11 is only a guide: it can be blank when such a distribution exists, and can hold vested deferrals that are not in box 1 (`iw2w3--2025.pdf p. 19, p. 32`) |
| Taxpayer's own records | Inmate wages | Subtracted (moved to Schedule 1 line 8u) |
| Form 4852 | Line 7a | Added, in place of the missing / incorrect W-2 |
| Form 8958 (community property, married filing separately) | Wages allocation | On separate returns in a community property state each spouse generally reports half of the community wages (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 23`; Pub. 555) |
| Form W-2GU / W-2VI / W-2AS | Box 1 | Added when the person is not a bona fide resident of that territory (`p570.pdf p. 24–25`) |

## 6. How to figure it, step by step

1. **Collect the W-2s that belong on this return.** Use box a (the employee's SSN). On a joint return take your W-2s
   and your spouse's; on any other return take only your own. A child's W-2 never goes on the parent's return
   (`p17.pdf p. 10`: "Amounts a child earns by performing services are included in the child's gross income and not
   the gross income of the parent.").
2. **Set aside statutory-employee W-2s.** For each W-2 with box 13 "Statutory employee" checked, carry its box 1 to
   Schedule C line 1 instead (`i1040sc.pdf p. 5`).
3. **Replace missing or incorrect W-2s with Form 4852.** If a W-2 never came, or is wrong and the employer has not
   corrected it in time, complete Form 4852 and use its line 7a in place of that W-2's box 1 (`f4852.pdf p. 1`:
   "Form 4852 serves as a substitute for Forms W-2, W-2c, and 1099-R … when (a) your employer or payer doesn't issue
   you a Form W-2 …, or (b) an employer or payer has issued an incorrect Form W-2"). Do not also count the incorrect
   W-2. If a Form W-2c (corrected W-2) arrived, use the corrected figure instead of the original.
4. **Add the box 1 amounts** (and the Form 4852 line 7a amounts). Keep cents while adding.
5. **Subtract inmate wages** included in that total (they go to Schedule 1 line 8u).
6. **Subtract any NQDC / nongovernmental §457 pension or annuity** included in box 1 (it goes to Schedule 1 line 8t).
   Box 11 usually shows it; if box 11 is blank but such a payment was received, get the amount from the employer
   (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 92`). Do not subtract box 11 amounts that were never in box 1 (for example vested
   prior-year deferrals, `iw2w3--2025.pdf p. 32`, Example 3).
7. **Round** the result to whole dollars if you round the return: drop amounts under 50 cents, raise 50–99 cents to
   the next dollar, and "include cents when adding the amounts and round off only the total"
   (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 23`).
8. Enter the result on **Form 1040 line 1a**.

There is no IRS worksheet for line 1a.

| Statutory employee | Box 1 to Schedule C line 1 (box checked); no self-employment tax; a separate Schedule C from other self-employment | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 25`; `i1040sc.pdf p. 5` |
| Missing or incorrect W-2 | Ask the employer; if not received in time use Form 4852; amend with Form 1040-X if the W-2 later shows different figures | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 25`; `f4852.pdf p. 1–2` |
| Community property state, married filing separately | Follow state law; generally half of community wages on each return; Form 8958 | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 23` |
| Registered domestic partners in Nevada, Washington, California | "must generally report half the combined community income of the individual and their domestic partner" | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 23` |
| Chapter 11 bankruptcy | Post-petition wages are taxable to the estate; allocate the W-2 wages and withholding between you and the estate and attach a statement | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 23` |
| Foreign wages | Stay on line 1a; any exclusion is figured on Form 2555 | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 23` |
| Nontaxable Medicaid waiver payments in box 1 | Stay on line 1a; subtracted on Schedule 1 line 8s | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 92` |
| Territory W-2 (W-2GU / W-2VI / W-2AS), not a bona fide resident | Include on the U.S. return (worldwide income); Form 5074 for Guam when its thresholds are met | `p570.pdf p. 24–25` |

## 8. Married filing jointly / separately

- **Joint return:** line 1a includes both spouses' W-2 box 1 (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 23`). Each spouse's inmate
  wages and NQDC pension amounts are taken out the same way.
- **Separate returns:** each spouse reports only the W-2s issued to them (box a), plus their own Form 4852. In a
  community property state each spouse generally reports half of the community wages of both spouses (Form 8958;
  `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 23`).
- There is no per-person limit or cap on line 1a.

## 10. Where the amount goes next

- **Form 1040 line 1z** = lines 1a through 1h (line 1i is not added).
- Through line 1z: **line 9** (total income) → **line 11** (AGI) → taxable income.
- Earned-income figures that start from wages: EIC (line 27), Schedule 8812, Form 2441, the dependent's standard
  deduction ("earned income"), IRA compensation, the Social Security Benefits Worksheet (line 1z is part of its
  income figure).
- The carve-outs land on **Schedule 1 line 8t / 8u** (and so in Form 1040 line 8).

## 11. Worked example

Alex Sample (000-00-0001) and Jordan Sample (000-00-0002) file jointly. Their records:

| Document | Box 1 | Box 11 | Box 13 statutory | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Alex — W-2 from Acme Co. | $62,000.40 | — | No | |
| Alex — W-2 from Former Employer Inc. | $8,000.00 | $8,000.00 | No | NQDC plan distribution |
| Jordan — W-2 from Route Foods | $15,000.00 | — | **Yes** | Statutory employee (commission driver) |
| Jordan — no W-2 from Corner Café | — | — | — | Form 4852 line 7a: $4,500.00 |
| Jordan — W-2 from State Correctional Industries | $1,200.00 | — | No | Earned while an inmate |
| Taylor Sample (000-00-0003, their dependent son) — W-2 | $3,000.00 | — | No | Belongs on Taylor's own return |

Steps:

1. W-2s for this return: Alex's two and Jordan's two. Taylor's W-2 is left out (his own return).
2. Statutory-employee W-2 (Route Foods, $15,000) is set aside for Jordan's Schedule C line 1.
3. Add: $62,000.40 + $8,000.00 + $1,200.00 + Form 4852 $4,500.00 = **$75,700.40**.
4. Subtract inmate wages $1,200.00 → $74,500.40 (Schedule 1 line 8u: $1,200).
5. Subtract the NQDC distribution $8,000.00 → $66,500.40 (Schedule 1 line 8t: $8,000).
6. Round the total: **Form 1040 line 1a = $66,500**.
