# Form 1040 line 1c — Tip income not reported on line 1a

Tax year 2025. What the IRS requires. How the app does it: `C:\us-tax\new_knowledge\1c.md`. Picture:
`C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\1c.md`.

---

## 1. At a glance

Form 1040 line 1c holds the tips an **employee** received in 2025 that are **not already inside W-2 box 1**. Tips
that the employee reported to the employer are part of W-2 box 1 and so are already on line 1a. Line 1c picks up
everything else:

- cash and charge tips the employee did not report to the employer (including months under $20, which did not
  have to be reported);
- **allocated tips** shown in W-2 box 8, unless the employee has adequate records showing the unreported tips were
  smaller than box 8;
- the value of **noncash tips** (tickets, passes, other items of value), which are never reported to the employer.

The cash and charge part is figured on **Form 4137** (Social Security and Medicare Tax on Unreported Tip Income):
its line 4 is "Include as income on Form 1040 … line 1c". Noncash tips are added to line 1c but are not on
Form 4137. Form 4137 also figures the social security and Medicare tax on the unreported tips, which goes to
Schedule 2 line 5 — that tax is **not** part of line 1c.

```text
Form 1040 line 1c =
    Σ Form 4137 line 4 (each person: line 2 − line 3, i.e. cash/charge tips received − tips reported to the employer;
                        column (c) includes allocated tips from W-2 box 8 unless records show less,
                        and tips under $20 in a month)
  + Σ value of noncash tips (tickets, passes, other items of value)
  (+ spouse's amounts on a joint return)
```

Terms used below:

- **Reported tips** — tips the employee told the employer about in a written (or electronic) monthly report. The
  employer includes them in W-2 box 1 and, for social security, box 7.
- **Allocated tips** — an amount a large food or beverage employer assigns to an employee when the tips reported
  by all employees are below 8% (or an approved lower rate) of sales. Shown in W-2 box 8; **not** in box 1.
- **Cash tips / charge tips** — tips paid in cash, by check, or by debit or credit card.
- **Noncash tips** — tips given as property or services (tickets, passes, other items of value).
- **Adequate records** — a daily tip record (tip diary or copies of bills and charge slips) kept as the tips are
  received.

## 2. The IRS wording

**Form 1040 (2025), line 1c:** "Tip income not reported on line 1a (see instructions)".

**2025 Instructions for Form 1040 — Line 1c** (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 24`):

> "Enter the total of your tip income that was not reported on Form 1040, line 1a. This should include any tip
> income you didn't report to your employer and any allocated tips shown in box 8 on your Form(s) W-2 unless you
> can prove that your unreported tips are less than the amount in box 8. Allocated tips aren't included as income in
> box 1. See Pub. 531 for more details. Also, include the value of any noncash tips you received, such as tickets,
> passes, or other items of value. Although you don't report these noncash tips to your employer, you must report
> them on line 1c."
>
> "You may owe social security and Medicare or railroad retirement (RRTA) tax on unreported tips. See the
> instructions for Schedule 2, line 5."

**Form 4137 (2025), line 4** (`f4137--2025.pdf p. 1`): "Subtract line 3 from line 2. Include as income on Form
1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR, line 1c. (See Allocated tips in the instructions.)"

**Form 4137 — Purpose of form** (`f4137--2025.pdf p. 1`):

> "Use Form 4137 only to figure the social security and Medicare tax owed on tips you didn't report to your
> employer, including any allocated tips shown on your Form(s) W-2 that you must report as income. You must also
> report the income on Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR, line 1c."

**Form 4137 — Who must file** (`f4137--2025.pdf p. 1`):

> "You must file Form 4137 if you received cash and charge tips of $20 or more in a calendar month and didn't
> report all of those tips to your employer. You must also file Form 4137 if your Form(s) W-2, box 8, shows
> allocated tips that you must report as income."

**Form 4137 — Allocated tips** (`f4137--2025.pdf p. 2`):

> "You must report all your tips from 2025, including both cash tips and noncash tips, as income on Form 1040,
> 1040-SR, or 1040-NR. Any tips you reported to your employer in 2025 are included in the wages shown on your Form
> W-2, box 1. Enter on Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR, line 1c, only the tips you received in 2025 and didn't
> report to your employer. This should include any allocated tips shown on your Form(s) W-2, box 8, unless you have
> adequate records to show that your unreported tips are less than the amount in box 8. Although allocated tips are
> shown on your Form W-2, box 8, they aren't included in Form W-2, box 1, and no tax is withheld from these tips."

**Form 4137 — Columns (c) and (d)** (`f4137--2025.pdf p. 2`):

> "Include all cash and charge tips you received. All of the following tips must be included.
> • Total tips you reported to your employer timely. Tips reported timely are included in gross income of the
> employee for the month and year in which the report is made. …
> • Total tips you reported to your employer late. Tips reported late are included in gross income of the employee
> for the month and year earned and received. …
> • Tips you didn't report at all (include any allocated tips … shown on your Form(s) W-2, box 8, unless you can
> prove that your unreported tips are less than the amount in box 8). Include these tips in column (c). …
> • Tips you received that you weren't required to report to your employer because they totaled less than $20
> during the month. Include these tips in column (c)."

**Pub. 531, What tips to report** (`p531.pdf p. 4`):

> "Generally, you must report all tips you received in the tax year on your tax return including both cash tips
> and noncash tips. Any tips you reported to your employer as required in the tax year are included in the wages
> shown in box 1 of your Form W-2. Add to the amount in box 1 only the tips you didn't report to your employer."
>
> "If you participate in a tip-splitting or tip-pooling arrangement, report only the tips you receive and retain.
> Don't report on your income tax return any portion of the tips you receive that you pass on to other employees.
> However, you must report tips you receive from other employees."

**Pub. 531, Allocated tips** (`p531.pdf p. 5`):

> "If your employer allocated tips to you, they're shown separately in box 8 of your Form W-2. They're not
> included in box 1 with your wages and reported tips. If box 8 is blank, this discussion doesn't apply to you."
>
> "Add to the amount in box 1 only the tips you didn't report to your employer as required. This should include
> any allocated tips shown in box 8 on your Form(s) W-2, unless you have adequate records to show that you received
> less tips in the year than the allocated amount."

**W-2 box 8** (`iw2w3--2025.pdf p. 18`): "Box 8—Allocated tips (not applicable to Forms W-2AS, W-2CM, W-2GU, or
W-2VI). … Do not include this amount in box 1, 3, 5, or 7." **W-2 box 7**: "Show the tips that the employee
reported to you … Report all tips in box 1 along with wages and other compensation."

## 3. What goes on this line

- **Unreported cash and charge tips** — tips received from customers (directly, by card, or from other employees
  through tip pools) that the employee did not report to the employer, or reported **late** (after the 10th of the
  following month) (`f4137--2025.pdf p. 2`).
- **Tips under $20 in a calendar month** from one job — not required to be reported to the employer, but still
  income (`f4137--2025.pdf p. 2`; `p531.pdf p. 4–5`, the "$18 unreported tips" example).
- **Allocated tips (W-2 box 8)** — unless the employee has adequate records showing that the unreported tips were
  less than box 8; in that case the smaller, proven amount (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 24`; `p531.pdf p. 5`).
- **Noncash tips** — the value of tickets, passes or other items of value (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 24`;
  `p531.pdf p. 2`).
- On a joint return, the spouse's amounts too (each spouse completes their own Form 4137, `f4137--2025.pdf p. 1`:
  "If married, complete a separate Form 4137 for each spouse with unreported tips").

## 4. What does not go here

| Look-alike amount | Where it goes instead | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Tips reported to the employer on time (in W-2 box 1, box 7) | Already on Form 1040 line 1a | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 24`; `iw2w3--2025.pdf p. 18` |
| December 2025 tips reported to the employer timely in January 2026 | The 2026 return (they are income "for the month and year in which the report is made") | `f4137--2025.pdf p. 2` |
| Tips passed on to other employees through a tip pool | Not income of the person who passed them on | `p531.pdf p. 4` |
| Service charges the employer adds to the bill and pays as wages (e.g. an automatic 18% for large parties) | Wages, already in W-2 box 1 (line 1a) | `p531.pdf p. 2` |
| Tips received as a self-employed person | Schedule C | `p531.pdf p. 5` |
| Social security and Medicare tax on unreported tips (Form 4137 line 13) | Schedule 2 line 5 (a tax, not income) | `f4137--2025.pdf p. 1`; `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 112` |
| Uncollected social security / Medicare tax on reported tips (W-2 box 12 codes A, B) | Schedule 2 line 13 | `f4137--2025.pdf p. 2`; `p531.pdf p. 5` |
| Wages of a worker misclassified as a contractor (Form 1099-NEC instead of W-2) | Form 1040 line 1g via Form 8919 — "don't use this form" (Form 4137) | `f4137--2025.pdf p. 1` |
| The deduction for qualified tips ("No tax on tips") | Schedule 1-A Part II → Form 1040 line 13b; it is a deduction, the tips stay in income on line 1a / 1c | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 101` |

## 5. Where the numbers come from

| Source document / form | Box or line | How it enters line 1c |
|---|---|---|
| Employee's daily tip record (tip diary, charge slips) | Cash and charge tips received, per employer | Form 4137 line 1 column (c) |
| Employee's monthly reports to the employer (or W-2 box 7 as a guide) | Tips reported to the employer | Form 4137 line 1 column (d); subtracted |
| Form W-2 | Box 8 (allocated tips) | Included in column (c) as unreported tips, unless records show less |
| Form W-2 | Box a (employee's SSN) | Decides whose Form 4137 the box 8 amount belongs to |
| Form W-2 | Box b (employer's EIN) and employer name | Form 4137 line 1 columns (a), (b) (identification only) |
| Employee's records | Value of noncash tips | Added to line 1c directly (not on Form 4137) |
| Form 4137 | Line 4 | Added to line 1c |

## 6. How to figure it, step by step

Do this for each person (you, and your spouse on a joint return), one Form 4137 per person:

1. **List each employer** where tips were received (Form 4137 line 1, one row per employer; attach more rows if
   more than five, `f4137--2025.pdf p. 2`).
2. **Column (c) — total cash and charge tips received** from that employer in 2025: tips reported timely, tips
   reported late, tips not reported at all, and tips under $20 in a month. For unreported tips, include W-2 box 8
   **allocated tips** unless records prove the unreported tips were less than box 8 (`f4137--2025.pdf p. 2`). Count
   only the tips kept after any tip pool (`p531.pdf p. 4`). Leave out December 2025 tips reported timely in January
   2026 (they belong to 2026) and include December 2024 tips reported timely in January 2025.
3. **Column (d) — cash and charge tips reported to the employer.**
4. **Form 4137 line 2** = total of column (c); **line 3** = total of column (d).
5. **Form 4137 line 4** = line 2 − line 3. This is the cash and charge part of line 1c.
6. **Add the value of noncash tips** (not on Form 4137 — no social security or Medicare tax applies to them,
   `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 112`).
7. **Add the spouse's** steps 1–6 on a joint return.
8. **Round** to whole dollars if you round the return: "include cents when adding the amounts and round off only the
   total" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 23`).
9. Enter the result on **Form 1040 line 1c**.

The rest of Form 4137 (lines 5–13: tips under $20 are taken out of the tax base, the social security wage base of
$176,100, the 6.2% and 1.45% taxes) figures the tax for Schedule 2 line 5 and does not change line 1c.

## 7. Special cases and elections

| Case | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Allocated tips with adequate records | Report the smaller, proven unreported amount instead of box 8 | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 24`; `f4137--2025.pdf p. 2`; `p531.pdf p. 5` |
| Actual unreported tips larger than box 8 | Report all tips actually not reported (box 8 is a floor only in the sense that it is used unless records show less) | `p531.pdf p. 5` ("Add to the amount in box 1 only the tips you didn't report …") |
| Tips under $20 in a month | Income on line 1c; on Form 4137 they are in column (c) and taken out of the tax base on line 5 | `f4137--2025.pdf p. 1–2` |
| Noncash tips | Income on line 1c; not on Form 4137; no social security or Medicare tax | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 24, p. 112` |
| Tips reported late to the employer | Income in the year earned and received (so in 2025 column (c) and (d)) | `f4137--2025.pdf p. 2` |
| December tips reported timely in January | Income of the next year | `f4137--2025.pdf p. 2`; `p531.pdf p. 4` |
| Tip pooling / splitting | Only the tips kept; tips received from other employees count | `p531.pdf p. 4` |
| Railroad (RRTA) employees | Do not use Form 4137 for tips from railroad work; the tips are still income | `f4137--2025.pdf p. 2` |
| Government employees whose pay is Medicare-only | Same line 1c; only the Form 4137 tax changes (line 10) | `f4137--2025.pdf p. 2` |
| Self-employed tips | Schedule C, not line 1c | `p531.pdf p. 5` |
| Community property state, married filing separately | Earned income such as tips follows state law; generally half of community income on each return (Form 8958) | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 23` |
| Filing requirement | Owing social security and Medicare tax on unreported tips is itself a reason to file | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 11` (Chart C, 1d) |

## 8. Married filing jointly / separately

- **Joint return:** line 1c includes both spouses' amounts; each spouse with unreported tips completes a separate
  Form 4137 (`f4137--2025.pdf p. 1`).
- **Separate returns:** each spouse reports only their own tips and their own W-2 box 8. In a community property
  state, follow state law (Form 8958) (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 23`).
- No cap or limit applies to line 1c.

## 9. Forms to attach or keep

| Form | Filed with the return? |
|---|---|
| Form 4137 | Attached ("Attach to your tax return", `f4137--2025.pdf p. 1`) whenever there are unreported cash/charge tips of $20 or more in a month or W-2 box 8 allocated tips that must be reported. Not needed when the only line 1c amount is noncash tips or tips under $20 in a month |
| Statement of reasonable cause for not reporting tips to the employer | Attached if you want to avoid the 50% penalty (`f4137--2025.pdf p. 2`; `p531.pdf p. 3`) |
| Daily tip record | Kept, not filed ("Prove your tip income if your return is ever questioned", `p531.pdf p. 2`) |
| Form W-2 | As for line 1a |

## 10. Where the amount goes next

- **Form 1040 line 1z** = lines 1a through 1h → line 9 → line 11 (AGI).
- Earned income: "Earned income includes wages, salaries, tips …" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 35`), so line 1c is part
  of line 1z for the dependent's standard deduction, the EIC, Form 2441 and Schedule 8812.
- The Form 4137 that produced it feeds **Schedule 2 line 5** (its tax), **Form 8959 line 2** (its line 6), the
  **Schedule SE** social security base (its line 10), and **Schedule 1-A line 4b** (qualified tips "included on
  Form 4137, line 1, row A, column (c)", `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 104`).

## 11. Worked example

Robin Sample (000-00-0011) and Casey Sample (000-00-0012) file jointly.

| Person | Employer | Column (c) tips received | Column (d) reported | W-2 box 8 | Noncash tips | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Robin | Harbor Grill (EIN 00-0000001) | $9,000.00 | $8,200.00 | — | $150.00 (concert tickets) | $40 of the unreported were months under $20 |
| Casey | Main St. Diner (EIN 00-0000002) | $6,000.00 (all reported) | $6,000.00 | $1,100.00 | — | No daily tip record |
| Casey | Corner Café (EIN 00-0000003) | $2,000.00 | $2,000.00 | $500.00 | — | Diary shows $200.00 of unreported tips |

Steps:

1. Robin, Form 4137: line 2 $9,000.00; line 3 $8,200.00; **line 4 $800.00** (the $40 under-$20 amount stays in;
   it only reduces Form 4137 line 6).
2. Casey, Main St. Diner: no records, so the box 8 $1,100.00 is an unreported tip; column (c) = $6,000 + $1,100 =
   $7,100.00, column (d) = $6,000.00.
3. Casey, Corner Café: records prove $200.00 < box 8 $500.00, so $200.00 is the unreported amount; column (c) =
   $2,200.00, column (d) = $2,000.00.
4. Casey, Form 4137: line 2 $9,300.00; line 3 $8,000.00; **line 4 $1,300.00**.
5. Noncash tips: Robin $150.00.
6. Line 1c = $800.00 + $1,300.00 + $150.00 = $2,250.00 → **Form 1040 line 1c = $2,250**.
