# Form 1040 line 1e — Taxable dependent care benefits from Form 2441, line 26

Tax year 2025. What the IRS requires. How the app does it: `C:\us-tax\new_knowledge\1e.md`. Picture:
`C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\1e.md`.

This file covers Form 2441 **Part III (Dependent Care Benefits)** in full, because its line 26 is Form 1040
line 1e. Form 2441 Parts I and II (the credit) are covered only where they affect line 26; the full credit
will have its own `forms\` file.

---

## 1. At a glance

Employers can pay for child or dependent care tax-free, up to a limit — usually through a dependent care
flexible spending account (FSA) or by paying a daycare directly. The total appears in **Form W-2 box 10**. Box 10
is not income by itself: you run it through **Form 2441 Part III**, which works out how much you may exclude
(keep tax-free) and how much is taxable. The taxable part, **Form 2441 line 26**, goes on **Form 1040 line 1e**.

The excluded amount is the **smallest** of: the benefits, the qualified care expenses you incurred, your earned
income, your spouse's earned income (joint return, and most separate returns), and $5,000 ($2,500 married filing
separately) or your plan's lower limit.

```text
Form 2441 line 15 = line 12 (benefits received, W-2 box 10 less any part already in box 1)
                  + line 13 (2024 benefits used in the 2025 grace period)
                  − line 14 (2025 benefits forfeited or carried to 2026)
line 17 = min(line 15, line 16 qualified expenses incurred in 2025)
line 20 = min(line 17, line 18 your earned income, line 19 spouse's earned income*)      (0 if negative)
line 21 = $5,000 ($2,500 if MFS and you had to enter your spouse's earned income), capped at the plan's maximum
line 23 = line 15 − line 22 (benefits from your own sole proprietorship / partnership)
line 24 = deductible benefits = min(line 20, line 21, line 22)                            (self-employed only)
line 25 = excluded benefits  = min(line 20, line 21)            if line 22 is "No"
                             = min(line 20, line 21) − line 24  if line 22 is "Yes"    (0 if negative)
line 26 = taxable benefits   = max(0, line 23 − line 25)

Form 1040 line 1e = Form 2441 line 26
```

\* line 19: joint return — spouse's earned income; married filing separately — your spouse's earned income unless
you are "considered unmarried"; everyone else — line 18 again.

Terms used below:

- **Dependent care benefits** — employer payments for care of a qualifying person, the fair market value of
  employer-provided daycare, and pre-tax contributions to a dependent care FSA (`i2441.pdf p. 1`).
- **Dependent care assistance program** — the employer plan (IRC section 129) under which the benefits are paid.
- **Qualifying person** — a dependent child under 13, or a spouse or dependent who cannot care for themselves and
  lived with you more than half the year (`i2441.pdf p. 1`).
- **Qualified expenses** — amounts for care of a qualifying person (and household services) so that you could
  work or look for work (`i2441.pdf p. 1–2`).
- **Earned income** — wages, tips, other taxable employee pay and net self-employment earnings (defined in
  section 6, step 6).
- **Grace period / carryforward** — plan features that let unused benefits of one year be used early the next year
  (`i2441.pdf p. 5`, Notice 2005-42).

## 2. The IRS wording

**Form 1040 (2025), line 1e:** "Taxable dependent care benefits from Form 2441, line 26".

**2025 Instructions for Form 1040 — Line 1e** (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 24`):

> "Enter the total of your taxable dependent care benefits from Form 2441, line 26. Dependent care benefits should
> be shown in box 10 of your Form(s) W-2. But first complete Form 2441 to see if you can exclude part or all of the
> benefits."

**Form 2441 (2025), line 26** (`f2441.pdf p. 2`):

> "Taxable benefits. Subtract line 25 from line 23. If zero or less, enter -0-. Also, enter this amount on
> Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR, line 1e."

**Instructions for Form 2441 (2025) — Purpose of Form** (`i2441.pdf p. 1`):

> "In addition, if you (or your spouse if filing jointly) received any dependent care benefits for 2025, you must
> use Form 2441 to figure the amount, if any, of the benefits you can exclude from your income. You must complete
> Part III of Form 2441 before you can figure the credit, if any, in Part II."
>
> "You (and your spouse if filing jointly) must have earned income to take the credit or exclude dependent care
> benefits from your income."

**Line 26** (`i2441.pdf p. 6`):

> "If line 26 is more than zero, you have taxable dependent care benefits. Enter this amount on Form 1040,
> 1040-SR, or 1040-NR, line 1e."

**What W-2 box 10 holds** (General Instructions for Forms W-2 and W-3 (2025), `iw2w3--2025.pdf p. 18–19`):

> "Box 10—Dependent care benefits … Show the total dependent care benefits under a dependent care assistance
> program (section 129) paid or incurred by you for your employee. … Report all amounts paid or incurred
> (regardless of any employee forfeitures), including those in excess of the $5,000 exclusion. … Include any
> amounts over your plan's exclusion in boxes 1, 3, and 5."

The remaining Part III line instructions are quoted in section 6 at the step that uses them.

## 3. What goes on this line

- The part of your (and, on a joint return, your spouse's) dependent care benefits that Form 2441 Part III does
  not let you exclude or deduct — Form 2441 line 26, only when it is more than zero (`i2441.pdf p. 6`).
- Typical causes of a taxable amount:
  - benefits larger than the qualified expenses incurred in 2025 (`i2441.pdf p. 5`, line 16 example: $2,000
    received, $900 of expenses);
  - benefits larger than the lower-earning spouse's earned income (for example a spouse with no job who was not a
    student or disabled);
  - benefits above $5,000 ($2,500 married filing separately) that the employer did **not** already put in box 1;
  - benefits above a plan limit lower than $5,000 (`i2441.pdf p. 5`, line 21).

## 4. What does not go here

| Look-alike amount | Where it goes instead | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Box 10 benefits the employer already included in W-2 box 1 (excess over the plan exclusion) | Already on Form 1040 line 1a; leave them out of Form 2441 line 12 | `f2441.pdf p. 2` line 12; `i2441.pdf p. 5` |
| Excluded benefits (Form 2441 line 25) | Not income; they reduce the credit's dollar limit (Form 2441 lines 27–31) | `p503.pdf p. 11` "Effect of exclusion on credit" |
| Deductible benefits of a self-employed person or partner (Form 2441 line 24) | Schedule C line 14, Schedule E line 19 or 28, or Schedule F line 15 | `i2441.pdf p. 5` line 24 |
| Child and dependent care **credit** | Schedule 3 line 2 (Form 2441 line 11) | `f2441.pdf p. 1` |
| Employer-provided adoption benefits (W-2 box 12 code T) | Form 1040 line 1f | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 24` |
| A dependent's own W-2 box 10 | The dependent's own return | `p17.pdf p. 10` (a child's earnings are the child's) |
| Benefits forfeited or carried to 2026 | Form 2441 line 14 (subtracted); not taxable for 2025 | `i2441.pdf p. 5` |

## 6. How to figure it, step by step

1. **Decide whether Part III is needed.** Complete Part III if you (or your spouse on a joint return) received
   any dependent care benefits — normally W-2 box 10 is more than zero (`i2441.pdf p. 1`). On Form 2441 page 1 the
   question "Did you receive dependent care benefits?" is answered Yes (`f2441.pdf p. 1`). If you had no
   qualifying person and no care provider and file only to report taxable benefits, write "none" on line 1,
   column (a) (`i2441.pdf p. 3`).
2. **Line 12 — benefits received.** Add box 10 of your W-2s (and your spouse's on a joint return). "Amounts you
   received as an employee should be shown in box 10 of your Form(s) W-2; however, don't include amounts
   reported in box 10 that exceed your plan's exclusion and are therefore reported as wages in box 1 of
   Form(s) W-2. If you were self-employed or a partner, include amounts you received under a dependent care
   assistance program from your sole proprietorship or partnership." (`i2441.pdf p. 5`)
3. **Lines 13–15 — grace period and forfeitures.** Line 13: "the amount you carried forward and used in 2025
   during the grace period." Line 14: "Any amount you forfeited … Any amount you didn't receive but are permitted
   by your employer to carry forward and use in the following year." Line 15: "Add the amounts on lines 12 and 13
   and subtract from that total the amount on line 14." (`i2441.pdf p. 5`)
4. **Line 16 — qualified expenses incurred in 2025.** "Enter the total of all qualified expenses incurred in 2025
   for the care of your qualifying person(s). It doesn't matter when the expenses were paid." (`i2441.pdf p. 5`).
   Expenses paid with the benefits **count** here (they are qualified expenses); this differs from Part II
   line 2(d), which leaves out benefits shown on line 28 (`i2441.pdf p. 3`). Only expenses for a qualifying
   person, paid to an eligible provider (not your spouse, the child's parent, your dependent, or your child under
   19) count (`i2441.pdf p. 2–3`).
5. **Line 17** = the smaller of line 15 or line 16 (`f2441.pdf p. 2`).
6. **Lines 18 and 19 — earned income.** "Earned income for figuring the amount of dependent care benefits you are
   able to exclude or deduct from your income is determined in the same way as earned income for figuring the
   credit … however, for purposes of lines 18 and 19, earned income doesn't include any dependent care benefits
   shown on line 12." (`i2441.pdf p. 5`). The credit definition (`i2441.pdf p. 4`) is:
   1. Form 1040 line 1z, minus any foreign earned income / housing exclusion on Form 2555 line 43, and minus
      amounts also reported on Schedule SE as clergy or church-employee income;
   2. plus Schedule SE line 3 minus the Schedule 1 line 15 deduction (optional-method and church-employee
      variants add lines 4b / 5a);
   3. plus Schedule C line 1 if you file Schedule C as a statutory employee;
   4. plus nontaxable combat pay **if you elect** — a separate election for the exclusion
      (`i2441.pdf p. 5`: "You can elect to include your nontaxable combat pay in earned income when figuring your
      exclusion or deduction, even if you elect not to include it in earned income for the EIC or the credit");
   5. "You must reduce your earned income by any loss from self-employment." (`i2441.pdf p. 4`)

   Not earned income: pensions and annuities, social security, workers' compensation, interest, dividends,
   unemployment, nontaxable workfare, child support, and "Any amount received for work while an inmate in a penal
   institution" (`p503.pdf p. 4`). Strike benefits and disability pay reported as wages **are** earned income
   (`p503.pdf p. 4`).

   - **Joint return:** "figure your and your spouse's earned income separately. Enter your earned income on line
     18 and your spouse's earned income on line 19." Community property laws are disregarded (`i2441.pdf p. 5`).
   - **Student or disabled (joint return):** for each month or part of a month a spouse was a full-time student
     (some part of 5 months) or not able to care for themselves, that spouse's earned income for the month is at
     least $250 ($500 if two or more qualifying persons); "If your spouse also worked during that month, use the
     higher of $250 (or $500) or their actual earned income for that month." Only one spouse may use the rule in
     any month. Check box B (`i2441.pdf p. 2, p. 4`). Pub. 503 limits the rule for **you** to a joint return:
     "These rules also apply if you are a student or not able to care for yourself and are filing a joint return"
     (`p503.pdf p. 11`; also `p503.pdf p. 4`).
   - **Married filing separately (line 19):** "Are you considered unmarried under that rule? Yes. Enter your earned
     income (from line 18) on line 19. No. Enter your spouse's earned income on line 19." (`i2441.pdf p. 5`)
   - **Everyone else:** line 19 = line 18 (`f2441.pdf p. 2`).
7. **Line 20** = the smallest of lines 17, 18 and 19; zero if zero or less (`f2441.pdf p. 2`).
8. **Line 21 — the dollar limit.** "For 2025, the maximum amount that can be excluded from your income through a
   dependent care assistance program is $5,000 ($2,500 if married filing separately). Don't enter more than the
   maximum amount allowed under your dependent care plan." (`i2441.pdf p. 5`). The form applies $2,500 only "if
   married filing separately and you were required to enter your spouse's earned income on line 19"
   (`f2441.pdf p. 2`). The limit is per return, not per spouse.
9. **Line 22 — own business benefits.** "Is any amount on line 12 or 13 from your sole proprietorship or
   partnership?" No → 0; Yes → that amount (`f2441.pdf p. 2`).
10. **Line 23** = line 15 − line 22.
11. **Line 24 — deductible benefits** (self-employed / partner only) = the smallest of lines 20, 21 and 22; deduct
    it on Schedule C line 14, Schedule E line 19 or 28, or Schedule F line 15 (`i2441.pdf p. 5`; `p503.pdf p. 10`:
    "If you are self-employed and receive benefits from a qualified dependent care benefit plan, you are treated as
    both employer and employee. Therefore, you wouldn't get an exclusion from wages. Instead, you would get a
    deduction").
12. **Line 25 — excluded benefits.** "If you checked 'No' on line 22, enter the smaller of line 20 or line 21.
    Otherwise, subtract line 24 from the smaller of line 20 or line 21. If zero or less, enter -0-"
    (`f2441.pdf p. 2`).
13. **Line 26 — taxable benefits** = line 23 − line 25; zero if zero or less. Enter on **Form 1040 line 1e**.
14. **Round** to whole dollars if you round the return (add with cents, round the total;
    `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 23`).

**How Part III changes Part II (the credit).** Line 27 is $3,000 ($6,000 for two or more qualifying persons);
line 28 = lines 24 + 25; line 29 = line 27 − line 28 (if zero or less, no credit); line 31 = the smaller of
line 29 or line 30 (expenses not paid with excluded benefits) and replaces line 3 (`f2441.pdf p. 2`). Part II never
changes line 26.

## 8. Married filing jointly / separately

- **Joint return:** line 12 includes both spouses' box 10; one $5,000 limit for the couple; line 18 is your earned
  income and line 19 your spouse's, figured separately; the lower one limits the exclusion (`i2441.pdf p. 5`).
- **Separate return:** only your own benefits (box a of your W-2s). If you are not considered unmarried, line 19
  is your spouse's earned income and line 21 is $2,500; if you are considered unmarried, line 19 = line 18 and
  line 21 is $5,000 (`f2441.pdf p. 2`; `i2441.pdf p. 2, p. 5`).
- **Other statuses:** line 19 = line 18.

## 10. Where the amount goes next

- **Form 1040 line 1z** (line 1a through 1h) → line 9 → line 11 (AGI) → taxable income.
- Form 2441 line 25 (and 24) → line 28 → reduces the credit limit on line 29 (Part II, Schedule 3 line 2).
- Taxable dependent care benefits are wages, so they also enter earned-income figures built from line 1z (for
  example the earned income credit).

## 11. Worked example

Dana Example (000-00-0011) and Robin Example (000-00-0012) file jointly. Their son Sam, age 4, attended a daycare
center (an eligible provider) all year.

| Document | Data |
|---|---|
| Dana — W-2 from Bright Co. | Box 1 $60,000; box 10 $5,000 (dependent care FSA; nothing over the plan limit in box 1) |
| Robin — W-2 from Corner Shop | Box 1 $3,200; box 10 — |
| Daycare receipts | $4,000 of care incurred and paid in 2025 |
| Plan | No grace period, nothing forfeited, plan limit $5,000 |

Part III:

1. Line 12 = $5,000. Lines 13 and 14 = 0. Line 15 = $5,000.
2. Line 16 = $4,000. Line 17 = min($5,000, $4,000) = $4,000.
3. Line 18 (Dana) = $60,000. Line 19 (Robin) = $3,200.
4. Line 20 = min($4,000, $60,000, $3,200) = $3,200.
5. Line 21 = $5,000. Line 22 = 0 (No). Line 23 = $5,000. Line 24 = 0.
6. Line 25 = min($3,200, $5,000) = $3,200.
7. Line 26 = $5,000 − $3,200 = **$1,800 → Form 1040 line 1e = $1,800**.

Part II effect: line 27 = $3,000, line 28 = $3,200, line 29 = 0 → no credit.

Second example (from `i2441.pdf p. 5`): $5,000 set aside, $4,950 of expenses incurred and reimbursed, $50
forfeited. Line 12 $5,000, line 14 $50, line 15 $4,950, line 16 $4,950; with enough earned income line 25 = $4,950
and line 26 = 0, so line 1e is blank.
