# Form 1040 line 1f — Employer-provided adoption benefits from Form 8839, line 31

Tax year 2025. What the IRS requires. How the app does it: `C:\us-tax\new_knowledge\1f.md`. Picture:
`C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\1f.md`.

This file covers Form 8839 **Part III** (employer-provided adoption benefits) in full, and Parts I–II only where they
change line 31. The adoption **credit** (Part II, Form 1040 line 30 and Schedule 3 line 6c) will get its own forms file.

---

## 1. At a glance

An employer can pay or reimburse an employee's adoption expenses under a written adoption assistance program. Those
payments ("employer-provided adoption benefits") are **not** in W-2 box 1; the employer reports them in W-2 box 12 with
code **T**. Up to **$17,280 per eligible child** can be left out of income (the "exclusion"), less what was already
excluded for that child in earlier years, and the exclusion shrinks when modified adjusted gross income (MAGI) is
between **$259,190 and $299,190**. Form 8839 Part III does this arithmetic child by child. Its line 31 is the part
that stays taxable, and that is Form 1040 line 1f. Line 31 — and so line 1f — can be **negative**: in the year the
adoption of a U.S. child with special needs becomes final, the full $17,280 can be excluded even when the employer paid
less (or nothing) this year, and the excess over this year's benefits reduces wages.

```text
Per child (Form 8839 lines 19–29):
    line 21 = $17,280 (or your agreed share)  −  benefits excluded for this child in earlier years (line 20)
    line 22 = employer benefits received in 2025 for this child (W-2 box 12 code T)
    line 24 = smaller of line 21 or line 22
              (special-needs U.S. child, adoption final in 2025, employer has a qualified program: line 21)
    line 29 = line 24 × (1 − phase-out fraction)
Return level:
    line 23 = Σ line 22            line 30 = Σ line 29
    phase-out fraction (line 27) = (MAGI − 259,190) / 40,000, at least 0, at most 1, rounded to ≥ 3 decimals
    line 31 = line 23 − line 30    (negative when line 30 > line 23)

Form 1040 line 1f = Form 8839 line 31
```

Terms used below:

- **Employer-provided adoption benefits** — amounts an employer paid directly, or reimbursed, for qualified adoption
  expenses under a qualified adoption assistance program (`i8839.pdf p. 2`).
- **Qualified adoption assistance program (QAAP)** — "a separate written plan set up by an employer to provide adoption
  assistance to its employees" (`i8839.pdf p. 2`).
- **Qualified adoption expenses** — reasonable and necessary adoption fees, attorney fees, court costs, travel (with
  meals and lodging) and re-adoption expenses of a foreign child (`i8839.pdf p. 2`).
- **Eligible child** — a child under age 18 (for the part of the year they were under 18), or a person of any age
  physically or mentally unable to care for themselves (`i8839.pdf p. 2`).
- **U.S. child / foreign child** — a child who was / was not a citizen or resident of the United States (including
  U.S. territories) when the adoption effort began (`i8839.pdf p. 2–3`).
- **Child with special needs** — a U.S. child for whom a state or Indian tribal government has determined that the child
  can't or shouldn't be returned to the parents' home and won't be adopted unless assistance is provided
  (`i8839.pdf p. 4`).
- **MAGI** — modified adjusted gross income, figured on the Form 8839 Line 25 worksheet (section 6, step 4).

> as a negative number. Also, enter the result on line 1f of Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR."

**Purpose** (`i8839.pdf p. 1`):

> "Use Form 8839, Part III, to figure the employer-provided adoption benefits you can exclude from your income on Form
> 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR, line 1f. You may be able to exclude these benefits from income if your employer had a
> written qualified adoption assistance program … and any of the following statements are true. 1. You received
> employer-provided adoption benefits in 2025. … 2. You adopted an eligible U.S. child with special needs and the
> adoption became final in 2025. 3. You received employer-provided adoption benefits in connection with the adoption of
> an eligible foreign child in: a. 2025 or prior years and the adoption became final in 2025, or b. 2025 and the
> adoption became final before 2025."
>
> "You can't exclude employer-provided adoption benefits if your employer is an S corporation in which you own more
> than 2% of the stock or stock with more than 2% of the voting power."

**What the W-2 shows** (`iw2w3--2025.pdf p. 7`, Adoption benefits; `p. 21`, Code T):

> "Amounts paid or expenses incurred by an employer for qualified adoption expenses under an adoption assistance
> program are not subject to federal income tax withholding and are not reportable in box 1. However, these amounts …
> are subject to social security, Medicare, and railroad retirement taxes and must be reported in boxes 3 and 5. …
> Also, the total amount, including any amount in excess of the $17,280 exclusion, must be reported in box 12 with
> code T."
>
> "Code T—Adoption benefits. Show the total that you paid or reimbursed for qualified adoption expenses furnished to
> your employee under an adoption assistance program. Also include adoption benefits paid or reimbursed from the
> pre-tax contributions made by the employee under a section 125 (cafeteria) plan. However, do not include adoption
> benefits forfeited from a section 125 (cafeteria) plan."

Pub. 17 (`p17.pdf p. 51`): "Adoption benefits are reported by your employer in box 12 of Form W-2, with code T. … they
aren't included as wages in box 1. To determine the taxable and nontaxable amounts, you must complete Part III of Form
8839. File the form with your return."

## 3. What goes on this line

- Form 8839 line 31, which is:
  - the **taxable part** of the 2025 code T benefits: benefits above the per-child limit (line 21), and the part lost to
    the MAGI phase-out (line 28);
  - benefits that can't be excluded at all because no exclusion line 24 exists for them — for example 2025 benefits
    for a **foreign child whose adoption was not final** by the end of 2025: "you must include the benefits in the
    total entered on line 1f" (`i8839.pdf p. 6`);
  - a **negative amount** when line 30 exceeds line 23 — the special-needs rule (line 24 = line 21 although less was
    paid in 2025) and the prior-year benefits of a foreign adoption that became final in 2025 (Exclusion of Prior Year
    Benefits Worksheet, `i8839.pdf p. 6`).
- Benefits paid from employee pre-tax cafeteria-plan (section 125) contributions, because the employer includes them in
  code T (`iw2w3--2025.pdf p. 21`).

## 6. How to figure it, step by step

1. **Check who may exclude** (`i8839.pdf p. 2`). Filing status single, head of household, qualifying surviving
   spouse or married filing jointly; a married person filing separately only under the Married Persons Not Filing
   Jointly rule (section 8). MAGI under $299,190 (or a credit carryforward — relevant to the credit only). Part I
   completed for each child. The employer had a written QAAP. Not a more-than-2% owner of an S corporation employer
   (`i8839.pdf p. 1`).
2. **Decide which benefits are this year's** (`i8839.pdf p. 3`).
   - U.S. child: employer payments are excluded "the year of the payment", for "any year", final or not — even for an
     adoption that never becomes final.
   - Foreign child: no exclusion unless the adoption becomes final. Payments before the final year are taxed when paid
     and excluded later in the final year; payments in or after the final year are excluded the year paid.
     "If your employer makes adoption assistance payments in a year before the adoption of a foreign child is final,
     you must include the payments in your income in the year of the payment" (`i8839.pdf p. 3`).
3. **Per child, lines 19–24.**
   - Line 19: $17,280; if you and another person (not your spouse on a joint return) each received benefits for the
     same child, your agreed share (`i8839.pdf p. 8`).
   - Line 20: if you received benefits for this child in a prior year, the total of lines 20 and 24 of the last Form
     8839 filed for the child (`i8839.pdf p. 8`). Foreign child final in 2025: enter 0 and use the prior-year worksheet
     (step 7).
   - Line 21 = line 19 − line 20.
   - Line 22: 2025 code T benefits for this child.
   - Line 24: the smaller of line 21 or line 22. **Special-needs U.S. child, adoption final in 2025:** line 21 — "only
     if your employer has a qualified adoption assistance program … whether or not you received any employer-provided
     adoption benefits under this plan. If your employer has no qualified adoption assistance program, you must enter
     the smaller of line 21 or line 22" (`i8839.pdf p. 8`).
4. **MAGI, line 25** — Modified Adjusted Gross Income Worksheet—Line 25 (`i8839.pdf p. 9`):
   1. Form 1040 line 1z figured as if you could exclude **all** of Form 8839 line 23 (that is, line 1z without any
      line 1f amount);
   2. plus Form 8839 line 23;
   3. plus Form 1040 lines 2b, 3b, 4b, 5b, 6b, 7a and Schedule 1 line 10;
   4. = add lines 1–3;
   5. minus Schedule 1 lines 11 through 20, 23 and 25 (the adjustments **except** line 21, the student loan interest
      deduction, which is therefore not subtracted);
   6. = line 4 − line 5;
   7. plus Form 2555 lines 45 and 50, Form 4563 line 15, and any Puerto Rico exclusion. The result is line 25.

   The instructions warn: "Your MAGI for the adoption credit may not be the same as the MAGI figured in the above
   worksheet" (`i8839.pdf p. 9`). The credit's line 7 MAGI is Form 1040 line 11b plus the Puerto Rico, Form 2555
   lines 45/50 and Form 4563 line 15 amounts (`i8839.pdf p. 7`).
5. **Phase-out, lines 26–29.** If line 25 ≤ $259,190, line 28 is 0. Otherwise line 26 = line 25 − $259,190 and
   line 27 = line 26 ÷ $40,000, "rounded to at least three places", at most 1.000. Per child, line 28 = line 24 ×
   line 27 and line 29 = line 24 − line 28.
6. **Lines 30–31.** Line 30 = Σ line 29. If line 30 ≤ line 23, line 31 = line 23 − line 30 (≥ 0); otherwise
   line 31 = −(line 30 − line 23).
7. **Foreign child final in 2025 with benefits received before 2025** — Exclusion of Prior Year Benefits Worksheet
   (`i8839.pdf p. 6`):
   - Line 19 = smaller of (total benefits received in 2025 and all prior years for that child) or $17,280 (your share
     if shared). Line 20 = 0. Line 21 = worksheet line 3. Line 22 = **total** benefits of 2025 **and all prior years**.
   - Complete Form 8839 through line 30. If line 30 < line 23: worksheet line 6 = (line 23 − line 30) − benefits
     received before 2025, "If zero or less, enter -0-" — that is line 31. Otherwise: worksheet line 9 = line 30 −
     2025 benefits = "Prior year excluded benefits"; "If zero or less, stop; you can't exclude any of your prior year
     benefits".
   - With several foreign children final in 2025, worksheet lines 1–3 per child, lines 4–9 combined.
8. **More than three children**: attach more Forms 8839; per-child lines 19–22, 24, 28, 29 on each; lines 23, 25–27,
   30 and 31 on one form only, with line 23 and line 30 the combined totals (`i8839.pdf p. 4`).
9. **Round** line 1f to whole dollars if you round the return (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 23`).
10. Enter line 31 on **Form 1040 line 1f**.

## 7. Special cases and elections

| Case | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Benefits above $17,280 for a child | Excess is taxable (stays in line 31); the employer still reports all of it in code T | `i8839.pdf p. 8`; `iw2w3--2025.pdf p. 7` |
| Special-needs U.S. child, final in 2025 | Line 24 = line 21 even with little or no 2025 benefits — if the employer has a QAAP; can make line 31 negative | `i8839.pdf p. 2, 3, 8` |
| Foreign child, not final at end of 2025 | 2025 benefits are taxable — include them on line 1f | `i8839.pdf p. 3, 6` |
| Foreign child, final in 2025, benefits in earlier years | Exclusion of Prior Year Benefits Worksheet; prior-year benefits may be excluded now | `i8839.pdf p. 6` |
| Year a foreign adoption is final | Rev. Proc. 2005-31 (non-Hague) / 2010-31 (Hague): you may choose, e.g., the foreign decree year or the U.S. re-adoption year | `i8839.pdf p. 5–6` |
| U.S. child, adoption not final / unsuccessful | Exclusion still allowed in the year of payment | `i8839.pdf p. 3` |
| Child shared with a non-spouse co-adopter | Split $17,280 (line 19) any way you agree | `i8839.pdf p. 8` |
| Several attempts for one U.S. child | Combine on the "Child 1" line | `i8839.pdf p. 4` |
| More-than-2% S corporation owner-employee | No exclusion from that employer | `i8839.pdf p. 1` |
| Same expenses for credit and exclusion | Not allowed; reimbursed expenses aren't qualified expenses for the credit, so Part III comes first | `i8839.pdf p. 1–2` (Examples 1–4) |
| Adopting a spouse's child | Not qualified adoption expenses | `i8839.pdf p. 2` |
| Eligible child turned 18 during the year | Eligible for the part of the year under 18 | `i8839.pdf p. 2` |

Where the IRS documents in `docs\books` do not say where a more-than-2% S corporation owner reports non-excludable
code T benefits (line 22 and no exclusion, or elsewhere), this file does not decide it.

## 8. Married filing jointly / separately

- **Joint return:** one Form 8839 for both spouses; each child appears once, and the $17,280 per child is not doubled
  (line 19 instruction: the split applies to "another person (other than your spouse if filing jointly)",
  `i8839.pdf p. 8`). Both spouses' code T benefits go on line 22.
- **Married filing separately:** "Generally, if you are married, you must file a joint return to take the credit or
  exclusion" (`i8839.pdf p. 2`). Exception — all must apply (`i8839.pdf p. 2`): statements (2) and (3) (MAGI limit,
  Part I) are true; "You lived apart from your spouse during the last 6 months of 2025"; "The eligible child lived in
  your home more than half of 2025"; "You provided over half the cost of keeping up your home." Without the exception
  none of the code T benefits can be excluded, so all of them are taxable on line 1f.
- **Per-person limit:** none — the limit is per child.

## 10. Where the amount goes next

- **Form 1040 line 1z** (sum of lines 1a–1h), then line 9 and line 11 (AGI). A negative line 1f reduces line 1z.
- **Earned income** — line 1f is part of line 1z, which the EIC Step 5 worksheet starts from ("1. Enter the amount
  from Form 1040 or 1040-SR, line 1z", `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 42`; see also `p. 35`).
- **Line 30 (excluded benefits)** is added back in:
  - the Social Security benefits figure — taxpayers excluding adoption benefits must use Pub. 915 Worksheet 1
    (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 31`; `p17.pdf p. 66`, "Adoption benefits (Form 8839, line 30)");
  - traditional IRA deduction MAGI — Pub. 17 Worksheet 9-1 line 6 "excluded employer-provided adoption benefits from
    Form 8839, line 30" (`p17.pdf p. 84`; `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 96` points to Pub. 590-A);
  - Roth IRA MAGI — Worksheet 9-2 line 9 (`p17.pdf p. 91`).
- **Form 8839 Part II** — benefits excluded in Part III reduce qualified adoption expenses for the credit
  (`i8839.pdf p. 2`, Examples 1–4; `p. 7`, line 5).

## 11. Worked example

**A — the common case.** Dana Example (000-00-0021), single, MAGI $90,000. W-2 box 12 code T $6,000 for adopting a
U.S. child, adoption not yet final. Line 19 $17,280, line 20 $0, line 21 $17,280, line 22 $6,000, line 24 $6,000,
line 25 $90,000 → line 28 $0, line 29 $6,000. Line 23 $6,000, line 30 $6,000, line 31 $0. **Line 1f = $0.**

**B — joint return, phase-out, special needs.** Pat Example (000-00-0011) and Sam Example (000-00-0012) file jointly.

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Pat — W-2 box 1 | $150,000 |
| Pat — W-2 box 12 code T, for Riley (U.S. child, final 2025, not special needs) | $20,000 |
| Sam — W-2 box 1 | $90,000 |
| Sam — W-2 box 12 code T, for Jamie (U.S. child, special needs, final 2025; employer has a written QAAP) | $3,000 |
| Benefits excluded for Jamie on the 2024 Form 8839 (lines 20 + 24) | $2,000 |
| Taxable interest (line 2b) | $1,000 |
| No adjustments, no foreign or territory exclusions | |

| Line | Riley | Jamie |
|---|---|---|
| 19 | 17,280 | 17,280 |
| 20 | 0 | 2,000 |
| 21 | 17,280 | 15,280 |
| 22 | 20,000 | 3,000 |
| 24 | 17,280 (smaller of 21, 22) | 15,280 (special needs: line 21) |

Line 23 = 20,000 + 3,000 = **23,000**.

MAGI worksheet: line 1 = line 1z with all of line 23 excluded = 150,000 + 90,000 = 240,000; line 2 = 23,000; line 3 =
1,000; line 4 = 264,000; line 5 = 0; line 25 = **264,000**.

Line 26 = 264,000 − 259,190 = 4,810. Line 27 = 4,810 ÷ 40,000 = 0.12025 → **0.120** (three places).

| Line | Riley | Jamie |
|---|---|---|
| 28 = 24 × 0.120 | 2,073.60 | 1,833.60 |
| 29 = 24 − 28 | 15,206.40 | 13,446.40 |

Line 30 = 28,652.80. Line 30 > line 23, so line 31 = −(28,652.80 − 23,000) = **−5,652.80**.
**Form 1040 line 1f = −$5,653.** Line 1z = 150,000 + 90,000 − 5,653 = $234,347. (Carrying line 27 to four places,
0.1203, gives −$5,640; both are allowed — "at least three places".)
