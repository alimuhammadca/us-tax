# Form 1040 line 1z — Add lines 1a through 1h

Tax year 2025. What the IRS requires. How the app does it: `C:\us-tax\new_knowledge\1z.md`. Picture:
`C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\1z.md`.

---

## 1. At a glance

Form 1040 line 1z is the total of the wage-type lines 1a through 1h. It is the first figure of total income
(line 9), and it is also the starting point of almost every "earned income" calculation on the return — the EIC,
the additional child tax credit (Schedule 8812), the child and dependent care credit (Form 2441), the standard
deduction of a dependent, and the Social Security Benefits Worksheet. Line 1i (nontaxable combat pay election) sits
just above it but is **not** added.

```text
Form 1040 line 1z = line 1a  (W-2 box 1 wages, after carve-outs)
                  + line 1b  (household employee wages not on a W-2)
                  + line 1c  (tip income not reported on line 1a)
                  + line 1d  (Medicaid waiver payments not on a W-2)
                  + line 1e  (taxable dependent care benefits, Form 2441 line 26)
                  + line 1f  (employer-provided adoption benefits, Form 8839 line 31 — can be negative)
                  + line 1g  (wages from Form 8919 line 6)
                  + line 1h  (other earned income)
        (line 1i is NOT added)
```

Terms used below:

- **Earned income** — pay for personal services (wages, salaries, tips, net self-employment earnings). Each credit
  defines it slightly differently, but the wage part normally starts from line 1z.
- **Sub-line** — one of lines 1a … 1h.

## 2. The IRS wording

**Form 1040 (2025), page 1:** "z Add lines 1a through 1h . . . 1z" (form face, `docs\IRS-Forms\f1040.pdf` p. 1).
Line 1i, "Nontaxable combat pay election (see instructions)", is printed in the inner column directly above it and is
not one of the amounts added.

The 2025 instructions have no separate "Line 1z" paragraph. The sub-lines are explained on
`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 23–25`, and the rounding rule applies (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 23`):

> "You can round off cents to whole dollars on your return and schedules. If you do round to whole dollars, you must
> round all amounts. To round, drop amounts under 50 cents and increase amounts from 50 to 99 cents to the next
> dollar. … If you have to add two or more amounts to figure the amount to enter on a line, include cents when adding
> the amounts and round off only the total."

**Line 1f can be negative** (Form 8839 (2025) line 31, form face `docs\IRS-Forms\f8839.pdf`): "Is line 30 more than
line 23? … Yes. Subtract line 23 from line 30. Enter the result as a negative number. Also, enter the result on line
1f of Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR."

**Line 1z as the start of earned income:**

- EIC Step 5 (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 42`): "1. Enter the amount from Form 1040 or 1040-SR, line 1z" — then subtract
  Medicaid waiver payments not elected (line 2) and add elected combat pay (line 4).
- Social Security Benefits Worksheet (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 32`): "3. Combine the amounts from Form 1040 or 1040-SR,
  lines 1z, 2b, 3b, 4b, 5b, 7a, and 8".
- Standard Deduction Worksheet for Dependents (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 35`): "Generally, your earned income is the
  total of the amount(s) you reported on Form 1040 or 1040-SR, line 1z, and Schedule 1, lines 3, 6, 8r, 8t, and 8u
  minus the amount, if any, on Schedule 1, line 15."
- Schedule 8812 Earned Income Worksheet (`i1040s8.pdf p. 8`): "1. a. Enter the amount from line 1z of Form 1040,
  1040-SR, or 1040-NR … b. Enter the amount of any nontaxable combat pay received."
- Form 2441 lines 4 and 5 (`i2441.pdf p. 4`): "Earned income for figuring the credit generally includes the following
  amounts. 1. The amount shown on Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR, line 1z, minus any amount: a. Excluded as foreign
  earned income … on Form 2555, line 43; or b. Also reported on Schedule SE (Form 1040) because you were a member of
  the clergy or you received $108.28 or more of church employee income."
- Clergy and the EIC (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 43`): "Determine how much of the amount on Form 1040 or 1040-SR, line
  1z, was also reported on Schedule SE, Part I, line 2 … Subtract that amount from the amount on Form 1040 or
  1040-SR, line 1z."

## 3. What goes on this line

The eight amounts already figured on lines 1a through 1h, each as entered (rounded, if the return is rounded). A
negative line 1f reduces the total, so line 1z itself can be negative.

## 6. How to figure it, step by step

1. Figure lines 1a through 1h (each with its own rules).
2. Add them, with their signs.
3. If the return is rounded, the sub-lines are already whole dollars, so the sum is too; if not rounded, add with
   cents (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 23`).
4. Enter the result on line 1z. Do not add line 1i.
5. Use this same line 1z figure wherever an instruction says "line 1z" (section 10).

## 7. Special cases and elections

| Case | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Special-needs adoption, Form 8839 line 30 > line 23 | Line 1f negative; line 1z reduced | `f8839` line 31 |
| Clergy / church employees and the EIC | Use line 1z less the part also on Schedule SE | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 43` |
| Form 2555 filer and Form 2441 | Earned income = line 1z less the Form 2555 line 43 exclusion | `i2441.pdf p. 4` |
| Medicaid waiver payments and the EIC / ACTC | Line 1z less non-elected excluded payments | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 42`; `i1040s8.pdf p. 8` |

## 8. Married filing jointly / separately

- **Joint return:** every sub-line includes both spouses, so line 1z does too.
- **Separate returns:** each return's sub-lines hold only that spouse's amounts (community-property splits per
  line 1a).
- Some consumers need per-person figures (Form 2441 lines 4 and 5 "figure your and your spouse's earned income
  separately", `i2441.pdf p. 4`); line 1z itself is one combined number.

## 9. Forms to attach or keep

None for line 1z itself; each sub-line's forms (W-2, Form 4852, 4137, 2441, 8839, 8919) are covered on that line.

## 11. Worked example

Dana Example (000-00-0001) and Lee Example (000-00-0002) file jointly.

| Line | Amount figured | Entered (rounded) |
|---|---|---|
| 1a W-2 wages (both) | $58,000.40 | $58,000 |
| 1b household wages without a W-2 (Lee) | $1,200.00 | $1,200 |
| 1c unreported tips (Dana) | $300.50 | $301 |
| 1d–1e | — | — |
| 1f adoption benefits, Form 8839 line 31 (special-needs adoption) | −$2,000.00 | −$2,000 |
| 1g–1h | — | — |
| 1i combat pay election (Lee) | $5,000.00 | $5,000 (memo) |

Line 1z = 58,000 + 1,200 + 301 − 2,000 = **$57,501**. Line 1i's $5,000 is not added. The Social Security worksheet,
the EIC, Schedule 8812 and Form 2441 all start from this $57,501.
