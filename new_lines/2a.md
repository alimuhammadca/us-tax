# Form 1040 line 2a — Tax-exempt interest

## 1. At a glance

Form 1040 line 2a reports the interest you received that is **not taxed** by the federal government — mainly
interest on bonds of a state, a city or another local government ("municipal bonds"), tax-exempt original issue
discount, and exempt-interest dividends paid by a mutual fund. Line 2a is an **information line**: it is not added
into total income (Form 1040 line 9). It matters because several other computations add it back — the Social
Security Benefits Worksheet, the premium tax credit's modified AGI, the Form 8815 and other modified-AGI tests.

```text
Form 1040 line 2a =
    Σ Form 1099-INT box 8      (tax-exempt interest; box 9 is a part of box 8, never added again)
  + Σ Form 1099-OID box 2      (only for a tax-exempt OID bond: its tax-exempt stated interest)
  + Σ Form 1099-OID box 11     (tax-exempt OID)
  + Σ Form 1099-DIV box 12     (exempt-interest dividends from a mutual fund / RIC)
  + tax-exempt interest from Schedules K-1 (Form 1065 box 18 code A, Form 1120-S box 16 code A)
  + tax-exempt interest received with no information return
  − amortized bond premium on tax-exempt bonds (Form 1099-INT box 13, or figured by you)
  − amortized acquisition premium on tax-exempt OID bonds
  (you, + your spouse on a joint return)
```

Terms used below:

- **Tax-exempt interest** — interest that federal law excludes from gross income, chiefly interest on obligations of
  a state, the District of Columbia, a U.S. territory, an Indian tribal government or their political subdivisions.
- **OID (original issue discount)** — the amount by which a bond's stated redemption price at maturity exceeds its
  issue price; it is a form of interest that accrues over the bond's life (`p17.pdf p. 62`).
- **Bond premium / acquisition premium** — what you paid above the bond's face amount (or, for an OID bond, above its
  adjusted issue price). On a tax-exempt bond it is amortized and reduces the tax-exempt interest you report; it is
  never deductible.
- **Exempt-interest dividends** — dividends a regulated investment company (RIC, such as a municipal-bond fund) pays
  out of its tax-exempt interest.
- **Specified private activity bond interest** — tax-exempt interest from certain private activity bonds; still
  tax-exempt for the regular tax but a preference item for the alternative minimum tax (AMT, Form 6251).

## 2. The IRS wording

**Form 1040 (2025), line 2a:** "Tax-exempt interest".

**2025 Instructions for Form 1040 — Line 2a** (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 25`):

> "If you received any tax-exempt interest (including any tax-exempt original issue discount (OID)), such as from
> municipal bonds, each payer should send you a Form 1099-INT or a Form 1099-OID. In general, your tax-exempt stated
> interest should be shown in box 8 of Form 1099-INT or, for a tax-exempt OID bond, in box 2 of Form 1099-OID, and
> your tax-exempt OID should be shown in box 11 of Form 1099-OID. Enter the total on line 2a. However, if you
> acquired a tax-exempt bond at a premium, only report the net amount of tax-exempt interest on line 2a (that is,
> the excess of the tax-exempt interest received during the year over the amortized bond premium for the year).
> Also, if you acquired a tax-exempt OID bond at an acquisition premium, only report the net amount of tax-exempt
> OID on line 2a (that is, the excess of tax-exempt OID for the year over the amortized acquisition premium for the
> year)."
>
> "Also include on line 2a any exempt-interest dividends from a mutual fund or other regulated investment company.
> This amount should be shown in box 12 of Form 1099-DIV."
>
> "Don't include interest earned on your IRA, health savings account, Archer or Medicare Advantage MSA, or Coverdell
> education savings account."
>
> "Don't include any amounts related to the forgiveness of PPP Loans on this line."

The Schedule B instructions repeat the same text and add (`i1040sb.pdf p. 2`):

> "If an amount is shown in box 9 of Form 1099-INT, you must generally report it on line 2g of Form 6251."

**Box 9 is already inside box 8** (`p17.pdf p. 63`):

> "Form 1099-INT, box 9, and Form 1099-DIV, box 13, show the tax-exempt interest subject to the AMT on Form 6251.
> These amounts are already included in the amounts on Form 1099-INT, box 8, and Form 1099-DIV, box 12. Don't add
> the amounts in Form 1099-INT, box 9 and Form 1099-DIV, box 13 to or subtract them from the amounts on Form
> 1099-INT, box 8 and Form 1099-DIV, box 12."
>
> "Don't report interest from an IRA as tax-exempt interest."

**It is information only** (`p17.pdf p. 62`): "If you file a tax return, you are required to show any tax-exempt
interest you received on your return. … This is an information reporting requirement only. It doesn't change
tax-exempt interest to taxable interest." For exempt-interest dividends (`p17.pdf p. 58`): "Although
exempt-interest dividends aren't taxable, you must show them on your tax return if you have to file."

**Schedules K-1** — Partner's Instructions for Schedule K-1 (Form 1065), box 18 code A (`i1065sk1.pdf p. 25`):
"Tax-exempt interest income. Report on your return, as an item of information, your share of the tax-exempt interest
received or accrued by the partnership during the year. Individual partners include this amount on Form 1040 or
1040-SR, line 2a." The Shareholder's Instructions for Schedule K-1 (Form 1120-S), box 16 code A, say the same for an
S corporation (`i1120ssk.pdf p. 15`).

## 3. What goes on this line

- Form 1099-INT box 8 — tax-exempt interest that is not OID (`i1099int.pdf p. 3`: "Enter tax-exempt interest that
  is not OID … on obligations issued by a state, the District of Columbia, a U.S. territory, an Indian tribal
  government, or their political subdivisions …"). It includes box 9.
- Form 1099-OID box 2 — for a **tax-exempt** OID bond only, the tax-exempt stated (periodic) interest
  (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 25`). For a taxable OID bond box 2 is taxable interest (line 2b).
- Form 1099-OID box 11 — tax-exempt OID (`i1099int.pdf p. 7`).
- Form 1099-DIV box 12 — exempt-interest dividends (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 25`).
- Your share of a partnership's or S corporation's tax-exempt interest (K-1 Form 1065 box 18 code A, Form 1120-S
  box 16 code A).
- Tax-exempt interest you received with no Form 1099 (for example under $10, which payers need not report —
  `i1099int.pdf p. 3`), because line 2a asks for all tax-exempt interest.

All of these are **net** of amortized premium on the tax-exempt bond (box 13 of Form 1099-INT, box 10 or box 6 of
Form 1099-OID for a tax-exempt covered security, or your own amortization when the payer did not report it).

## 4. What does not go here

| Look-alike amount | Where it goes instead | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Form 1099-INT box 9 / Form 1099-DIV box 13 (private activity bond interest) — never **added** | Already in box 8 / box 12; also Form 6251 line 2g | `p17.pdf p. 63`; `i1040sb.pdf p. 2` |
| Market discount on a tax-exempt bond | Taxable interest — Form 1040 line 2b ("The market discount on a tax-exempt bond is taxable interest income and not tax-exempt interest") | `i1040sb.pdf p. 1` |
| Interest earned inside an IRA, HSA, Archer / Medicare Advantage MSA or Coverdell ESA | Not reported as interest; distributions go to lines 4a/4b, Form 8889, Form 8853, Form 1099-Q rules | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 25`; `p17.pdf p. 58, p. 63` |
| PPP loan forgiveness | Not on line 2a | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 25` |
| Interest on U.S. savings bonds and Treasury obligations (exempt only from **state** tax) | Taxable — Form 1040 line 2b | `p17.pdf p. 61` ("subject to federal income tax but is exempt from all state and local income taxes") |
| Federally taxable interest on a Build America / other tax credit bond | Form 1040 line 2b | `i1099int.pdf p. 3` |
| A dependent child's tax-exempt interest | The child's own return; if the parent elects Form 8814, it goes on Form 8814 line 1b (see section 7) | `i8814.pdf p. 2–3`; `p17.pdf p. 58` |
| Excess of tax-exempt bond premium over the bond's interest | Lost — never deductible, never negative on line 2a | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 25` ("the excess of the tax-exempt interest … over the amortized bond premium") |

## 6. How to figure it, step by step

1. **Collect the statements that belong on this return** using the recipient's TIN: yours, and your spouse's on a
   joint return. A custodial account's interest belongs to the child whose TIN the payer holds (`p17.pdf p. 57`:
   "you must give your child's SSN to the payer of interest on an account owned by your child"); it is not on the
   parent's return.
2. **Add Form 1099-INT box 8.** Do not add box 9 on top (`p17.pdf p. 63`).
3. **Subtract tax-exempt bond premium.** Box 13 of each Form 1099-INT; if the payer reported neither a net box 8 nor
   box 13 and you amortize the premium yourself, subtract your amortized amount. Never go below zero for a bond.
4. **Add tax-exempt OID**: Form 1099-OID box 11, and box 2 when the OID bond is tax-exempt. Subtract box 10 and
   box 6 amounts that belong to those tax-exempt bonds (net amount only — `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 25`).
5. **Add exempt-interest dividends** — Form 1099-DIV box 12 (not box 13 on top).
6. **Add K-1 tax-exempt interest** (Form 1065 box 18 code A, Form 1120-S box 16 code A) and any tax-exempt interest
   received without a 1099.
7. **Round** the total to whole dollars if you round (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 23`: round off only the total).
8. Enter it on **Form 1040 line 2a**. It is **not** carried to line 9.

There is no IRS worksheet for line 2a.

## 7. Special cases and elections

| Case | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Private activity bond interest | Stays inside box 8 / box 12 for line 2a; the same amount is a Form 6251 line 2g preference | `i1040sb.pdf p. 2`; `p17.pdf p. 63` |
| Bond bought at a premium | Report only the net of interest over amortized premium; payer may report gross + box 13, or net box 8 with box 13 blank | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 25`; `i1099int.pdf p. 4` |
| Tax-exempt OID bond with acquisition premium | Report the net tax-exempt OID | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 25` |
| Form 8814 (parent reports child's interest) | The child's tax-exempt interest is entered on Form 8814 line 1b ("If your child received any tax-exempt interest income … report it on line 1b"); Form 8814 then works only with the child's taxable items. Whether line 1b is also entered on the parent's Form 1040 line 2a is **not stated** in the 2025 IRS documents available here (`i8814.pdf p. 2–3`) | `i8814.pdf p. 2–3` |
| Child's private activity bond interest under Form 8814 | Taken into account for the parent's AMT | `i8814.pdf p. 2` |
| Bonds held jointly / community property | Each owner's share under local law; on separate returns in a community property state generally half each | `p17.pdf p. 58, p. 60` |
| Nominee interest | Follows the Schedule B nominee rules for taxable interest; for tax-exempt interest the IRS gives no separate rule — report only your own | `i1040sb.pdf p. 1` |

## 8. Married filing jointly / separately

- **Joint return:** both spouses' tax-exempt interest (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 25`, line 2a reports "the total").
- **Separate returns:** each spouse reports only the interest on statements issued to them (recipient TIN), plus
  half of community interest in a community property state (`p17.pdf p. 60`).
- No per-person limit or cap.

## 9. Forms to attach or keep

| Form | Filed with the return? |
|---|---|
| Forms 1099-INT / 1099-OID / 1099-DIV | Kept with your records ("You don't have to attach it to your tax return", `p17.pdf p. 58`) |
| Schedule B | Not required by tax-exempt interest alone; tax-exempt interest is never listed on Schedule B line 1 (`i1040sb.pdf p. 1`: "Don't report on line 1 any tax-exempt interest") |
| Form 6251 | Filed when the AMT rules require it; box 9 / box 13 amounts go on its line 2g |

## 10. Where the amount goes next

Line 2a itself is not part of line 9. Other worksheets read it:

- **Social Security Benefits Worksheet** — tax-exempt interest is part of the income tested against the base amount
  (`p17.pdf p. 64`: "All your other income, including tax-exempt interest"; worksheet line "Enter the amount, if
  any, from Form 1040 or 1040-SR, line 2a", `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 32`, Social Security Benefits Worksheet).
- **Modified AGI** for the premium tax credit (Form 8962), Form 8828 recapture, and other tests that add back
  tax-exempt interest.
- **Form 6251 line 2g** receives the private-activity subset (box 9 / box 13), not line 2a itself.

## 11. Worked example

Robin Example (000-00-0011) and Casey Example (000-00-0012) file jointly.

| Document | Amounts |
|---|---|
| Robin — Form 1099-INT, City of Springfield bonds | box 8 $2,400.00; box 9 $300.00; box 13 $150.00 |
| Casey — Form 1099-DIV, Muni Bond Fund | box 12 $900.00; box 13 $100.00 |
| Robin — Form 1099-OID, County zero-coupon bond (tax-exempt) | box 11 $500.00 |
| Casey — Schedule K-1 (Form 1065), Partnership XYZ | box 18 code A $250.00 |
| Riley Example (000-00-0013, their dependent) — Form 1099-INT | box 8 $80.00 — Riley's own return |

Steps:

1. Statements for this return: Robin's and Casey's. Riley's is left out.
2. Form 1099-INT box 8 $2,400.00 (box 9 $300 is inside it, not added).
3. Less box 13 premium $150.00 → $2,250.00.
4. Plus tax-exempt OID $500.00 → $2,750.00.
5. Plus exempt-interest dividends $900.00 (box 13 $100 not added) → $3,650.00.
6. Plus K-1 tax-exempt interest $250.00 → $3,900.00.
7. **Form 1040 line 2a = $3,900.** Form 6251 line 2g receives $300 + $100 = $400. Line 9 is unchanged.
