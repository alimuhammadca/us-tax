# Form 1040 line 2b — Taxable interest

Tax year 2025. What the IRS requires. How the app does it: `C:\us-tax\new_knowledge\2b.md`. Picture:
`C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\2b.md`. Tax-exempt interest is line 2a: `C:\us-tax\new_lines\2a.md`.

---

## 1. At a glance

Form 1040 line 2b is all the interest you (and your spouse, on a joint return) must include in income: bank and
credit-union interest, corporate-bond interest, U.S. Treasury and savings-bond interest, taxable original issue
discount, interest from a mortgage you carry for a buyer, interest passed through to you on a Schedule K-1, and
interest you received with no form at all. Amounts on the Forms 1099 that are not yours or not taxable this year
(nominee interest, accrued interest you paid when you bought a bond, amortized bond premium, savings-bond interest you
already reported, frozen deposits) are taken back out, and the Series EE / I savings-bond education exclusion
(Form 8815) is subtracted. When the total is over $1,500 — or one of the other Schedule B conditions applies — the
figure is built on **Schedule B, Part I** and line 2b equals Schedule B line 4.

```text
Schedule B line 1  = every taxable-interest item, payer by payer (gross, as reported)
                     − "Nominee Distribution", "Accrued Interest", "OID Adjustment", "ABP Adjustment",
                       "U.S. Savings Bond Interest Previously Reported", frozen-deposit exclusion
Schedule B line 2  = total of line 1
Schedule B line 3  = Form 8815 line 14 (excludable savings-bond interest)
Schedule B line 4  = line 2 − line 3        →  Form 1040 line 2b
```

Terms used below:

- **Taxable interest** — interest that is part of gross income (everything except tax-exempt interest, line 2a).
- **OID (original issue discount)** — the excess of a debt instrument's stated redemption price at maturity over its
  issue price; it accrues as interest each year whether or not paid (`p17.pdf p. 62`).
- **Amortizable bond premium (ABP)** — the amount paid above a taxable bond's face value; if you elect to amortize it
  (IRC §171), the year's amortization reduces that bond's interest.
- **Acquisition premium** — the amount paid for an OID bond above its adjusted issue price; its amortization reduces
  the OID.
- **Market discount** — the amount by which a bond bought after issue costs less than its stated redemption price (or
  adjusted issue price). It is taxed as interest when the bond is sold, or each year if you elect under §1278(b).
- **Nominee** — someone who receives interest in their own name that belongs to another person.
- **Seller-financed mortgage** — a loan you (the seller) made to the buyer of your property; the buyer's payments
  include interest you must report.

## 2. The IRS wording

**Form 1040 (2025), line 2b:** "Taxable interest".

**2025 Instructions for Form 1040 — Line 2b** (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 25`):

> "Each payer should send you a Form 1099-INT or Form 1099-OID. Enter your total taxable interest income on line 2b.
> But you must fill in and attach Schedule B if the total is over $1,500 or any of the other conditions listed at
> the beginning of the Schedule B instructions applies to you."
>
> "Interest credited in 2025 on deposits that you couldn't withdraw because of the bankruptcy or insolvency of the
> financial institution may not have to be included in your 2025 income."
>
> "If you get a 2025 Form 1099-INT for U.S. savings bond interest that includes amounts you reported before 2025,
> see Pub. 550."

**Schedule B — when it is required** (`i1040sb.pdf p. 1`):

> "• You had over $1,500 of taxable interest or ordinary dividends. • You received interest from a seller-financed
> mortgage and the buyer used the property as a personal residence. • You have accrued interest from a bond. • You
> are reporting original issue discount (OID) of less than the amount shown on Form 1099-OID. • You are reporting
> interest income of less than the amount shown on a Form 1099 due to amortizable bond premium. • You are claiming
> the exclusion of interest from series EE or I U.S. savings bonds issued after 1989. • You received interest or
> ordinary dividends as a nominee. • You had a financial interest in, or signature authority over, a financial account
> in a foreign country or you received a distribution from, or were a grantor of, or transferor to, a foreign trust."

Pub. 17 adds two more interest triggers (`p17.pdf p. 63`): "4. You received a Form 1099-INT for U.S. savings bond
interest that includes amounts you reported in a previous tax year." and "6. You received a Form 1099-INT for
interest on frozen deposits."

**Schedule B line 1** (`i1040sb.pdf p. 1`):

> "Report on line 1 all of your taxable interest. Taxable interest generally should be shown on your Forms 1099-INT,
> Forms 1099-OID, or substitute statements. Include interest from series EE, H, HH, and I U.S. savings bonds. Also
> include any accrued market discount that is includible in income and any gain on a contingent payment debt
> instrument that is includible in income as interest income. List each payer's name and the amount. Don't report on
> line 1 any tax-exempt interest."
>
> "The market discount on a tax-exempt bond is taxable interest income and not tax-exempt interest."
>
> "Seller-financed mortgages. If you sold your home or other property and the buyer used the property as a personal
> residence, list first any interest the buyer paid you on a mortgage or other form of seller financing. Be sure to
> show the buyer's name, address, and SSN."
>
> "Nominees. … report the total on line 1. … Under your last entry on line 1, put a subtotal of all interest listed
> on line 1. Below this subtotal, enter "Nominee Distribution" and show the total interest you received as a nominee.
> Subtract this amount from the subtotal and enter the result on line 2."

(`i1040sb.pdf p. 2`):

> "Accrued interest. When you buy bonds between interest payment dates and pay accrued interest to the seller, this
> interest is taxable to the seller. … follow the rules earlier under Nominees … But identify the amount to be
> subtracted as "Accrued Interest.""
>
> "Original issue discount (OID). If you are reporting OID in an amount less than the amount shown in box 1 or box 8
> of Form 1099-OID, follow the rules earlier under Nominees … But identify the amount to be subtracted as "OID
> Adjustment." However, if the payer reported to you a net amount of OID … no reduction of the amount of OID income
> reported to you by the payer is permitted on Schedule B for the bond."
>
> "Amortizable bond premium. If you elect to reduce your interest income on a taxable bond by the amount of taxable
> amortizable bond premium, follow the rules earlier under Nominees … But identify the amount to be subtracted as
> "ABP Adjustment." However, if the payer reported to you a net amount of interest income on the bond reflecting the
> offset of the gross amount of interest income by the amortizable bond premium, no reduction … is permitted on
> Schedule B for the bond."
>
> "Line 3. If, during 2025, you cashed series EE or I U.S. savings bonds issued after 1989 and you paid qualified
> higher education expenses for yourself, your spouse, or your dependents, you may be able to exclude part or all of
> the interest on those bonds. See Form 8815."

**Form 8815 line 14** (`f8815.pdf p. 1`): "Excludable savings bond interest. Subtract line 13 from line 8. Enter the
result here and on Schedule B (Form 1040), line 3."

**How to report** (`p17.pdf p. 63`): "Generally, you report all your taxable interest income on Form 1040 or
1040-SR, line 2b." "Form 1099-INT. Your taxable interest income, except for interest from U.S. savings bonds and
Treasury obligations, is shown in Form 1099-INT, box 1. … You must report all of your taxable interest income even if
you don't receive a Form 1099-INT." "Form 1099-INT, box 3 shows the interest income you received from U.S. savings
bonds, Treasury bills, Treasury notes, and Treasury bonds. Generally, add the amount shown in Form 1099-INT, box 3 to
any other taxable interest income you received."

**Form 1099-OID** (`p17.pdf p. 62`): "In most cases, you must report the entire amount in Form 1099-OID, boxes 1, 2,
and 8 as interest income."

## 3. What goes on this line

- **Form 1099-INT box 1** (interest other than U.S. savings bonds / Treasury) and **box 3** (U.S. savings bonds and
  Treasury obligations) — `p17.pdf p. 63`.
- **Form 1099-INT box 10** (market discount) when you made the §1278(b) election to include market discount
  currently — the payer fills it only "if the recipient notified you that a section 1278(b) election was made"
  (`i1099int.pdf p. 3`); Schedule B line 1 "include any accrued market discount that is includible in income".
- **Form 1099-OID boxes 1, 2 and 8** (taxable OID, other periodic interest on a taxable bond, OID on Treasury
  obligations; box 8 may be negative for a TIPS deflation adjustment, `i1099int.pdf p. 7`), and **box 5** (market
  discount) under a §1278(b) election.
- **Schedule K-1 interest**: Form 1065 box 5 (`i1065sk1.pdf p. 15`: "Report interest income on Form 1040 or 1040-SR,
  line 2b"), Form 1120-S box 4 (`i1120ssk.pdf p. 7`), Form 1041 box 1 (`i1041sk1.pdf p. 2`: "This amount is
  reported on line 2b of Form 1040 or 1040-SR and Schedule B (Form 1040), Part I, line 1, if applicable").
- **Seller-financed mortgage interest** you received (`i1040sb.pdf p. 1`).
- **Interest with no Form 1099**: loans to others, interest on tax refunds, condemnation awards, installment-sale
  interest, usurious interest, gifts for opening an account (over $10 / $20), interest on insurance dividends
  (`p17.pdf p. 58–59`).
- **Imputed interest on a below-market loan** you made (IRC §7872; `p17.pdf p. 59`).
- **Tax credit bond credits** treated as interest (clean renewable energy bonds, qualified zone academy bonds, etc.;
  `i1099int.pdf p. 3`).
- **Accrued interest received when you sell a bond between interest dates** (`p17.pdf p. 62`).
- **U.S. savings bond interest** on redemption (Series EE / I under method 1) or each year (method 2)
  (`p17.pdf p. 59–60`).

## 6. How to figure it, step by step

1. **Collect the statements that belong on this return.** Use the recipient's TIN. On a joint return take both
   spouses' statements; on any other return only your own. Interest on an account the child owns is the child's even
   if paid to you as custodian (`p17.pdf p. 57`: "you must give your child's SSN to the payer of interest on an
   account owned by your child, even though the interest is paid to you as custodian"; `p17.pdf p. 58`: income from
   property given to a child "is taxable to the child"). Joint-account interest is split by local law
   (`p17.pdf p. 58`).
2. **List every taxable interest item on Schedule B line 1, seller-financed mortgages first**, at the gross amount
   reported: Form 1099-INT box 1 + box 3 (and box 10 under §1278(b)); Form 1099-OID box 1 + box 8 + box 2 (taxable
   bond; and box 5 under §1278(b)); K-1 interest; seller-financed interest; interest received without a 1099;
   §7872 imputed interest.
3. **Subtotal, then subtract** (each labelled): "Nominee Distribution"; "Accrued Interest"; "ABP Adjustment"
   (1099-INT box 11 / box 12, or your own amortization when the payer reported neither net nor premium); "OID
   Adjustment" (1099-OID box 6, box 10, or your own acquisition-premium amortization); "U.S. Savings Bond Interest
   Previously Reported" (`p17.pdf p. 63`); frozen-deposit interest you could not withdraw (`p17.pdf p. 59`). Do not
   subtract premium when the payer already reported a net amount (`i1040sb.pdf p. 2`).
4. **Schedule B line 2** = the result.
5. **Schedule B line 3** = Form 8815 line 14, if you claim the Series EE / I education exclusion (not allowed when
   married filing separately; modified AGI limits in section 12).
6. **Schedule B line 4** = line 2 − line 3 → **Form 1040 line 2b**.
7. If Schedule B is **not** required (total ≤ $1,500 and none of the other conditions), simply add the taxable
   interest and enter the total on line 2b.
8. **Round** the total to whole dollars if you round (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 23`).

## 7. Special cases and elections

| Case | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Bond premium | Payer may report gross + box 11/12, or net with box 11/12 blank; reduce only once. (What happens to premium larger than the bond's interest is a Pub. 550 rule; Pub. 550 is not among the 2025 documents used here) | `i1099int.pdf p. 2, p. 4`; `i1040sb.pdf p. 2` |
| OID acquisition premium | Same choice for the payer (box 6); reduce only once | `i1099int.pdf p. 6`; `i1040sb.pdf p. 2` |
| Market discount, §1278(b) election | Accrued market discount reported each year as interest (1099-INT box 10 / 1099-OID box 5) | `i1099int.pdf p. 3, p. 6`; `i1040sb.pdf p. 1` |
| Market discount on a tax-exempt bond | Taxable interest (line 2b), not line 2a | `i1040sb.pdf p. 1` |
| Frozen deposits | Exclude interest credited on a deposit you could not withdraw at year end (minus amounts withdrawn / withdrawable); include it the year it becomes available | `p17.pdf p. 59`; `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 25` |
| Savings bonds — method 1 / method 2 | Cash-method owners report EE / I interest at redemption (method 1) or yearly (method 2); interest already reported is subtracted on Schedule B | `p17.pdf p. 60, p. 63` |
| Savings bonds — co-owners, reissue, community property | Interest taxed to the co-owner who paid; community property bonds half each on separate returns | `p17.pdf p. 60` (Table 6-1) |
| Education Savings Bond Program (Form 8815) | Exclusion on Schedule B line 3; Series EE after 1989 or I bonds, owner at least 24 before issue, qualified expenses for you, spouse or dependents; not on MFS | `p17.pdf p. 61`; `f8815.pdf p. 1, p. 3` |
| Seller-financed mortgage | List first with buyer's name, address and SSN; $50 penalty if omitted | `i1040sb.pdf p. 1` |
| Nominee | Report the total, subtract "Nominee Distribution"; issue Forms 1099-INT to the owner unless it is your spouse | `i1040sb.pdf p. 1–2` |
| Below-market (gift / demand) loans | Lender may have imputed interest income under §7872 | `p17.pdf p. 59` |
| Bonds sold between interest dates | Seller reports the accrued interest; buyer treats it as return of capital | `p17.pdf p. 62` |
| Child's interest, Form 8814 election | Child's taxable interest on Form 8814 line 1a (net of nominee, accrued interest, ABP, OID adjustments shown on the dotted line); child must be under 19 (24 student), only interest and dividends, gross income under $13,500, no withholding; never on the parent's line 2b | `i8814.pdf p. 1–2` |
| Tax credit bonds | The credit is reported (and included) as interest on the credit allowance dates | `i1099int.pdf p. 3` |

## 8. Married filing jointly / separately

- **Joint return:** both spouses' taxable interest; one Schedule B for the couple.
- **Separate returns:** each spouse reports interest on their own statements (recipient TIN). Joint accounts follow
  local law; community property states generally split community interest half each (`p17.pdf p. 58, p. 60`).
- **Form 8815 is not available** to a married-filing-separately filer (`f8815.pdf p. 3`; `p17.pdf p. 61`).
- No per-person cap on line 2b.

## 10. Where the amount goes next

- **Form 1040 line 9** (total income) → line 11 (AGI) → taxable income.
- **Form 8960 line 1** (net investment income tax).
- EIC investment-income limit, Form 4952 investment income, the Social Security Benefits Worksheet, and every
  modified-AGI test built from AGI.
- **Schedule B line 4** equals line 2b whenever Schedule B is filed.

## 11. Worked example

Morgan Sample (000-00-0021) and Drew Sample (000-00-0022) file jointly.

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Morgan — Form 1099-INT, First Bank: box 1 | $900.00 |
| Morgan — Form 1099-INT, Broker A: box 1 $1,200.00, box 11 $200.00 (corporate bond premium) | |
| Drew — Form 1099-INT, Treasury redemption: box 3 (Series EE bonds) | $3,000.00 |
| Drew — Form 1099-OID, Broker B: box 1 $400.00, box 6 $50.00 | |
| Morgan — seller-financed note on the old house, buyer Pat Buyer (000-00-0031) | $2,000.00 |
| Drew — Schedule K-1 (Form 1065), box 5 | $150.00 |
| Morgan holds $500 at First Bank as nominee for Morgan's brother (included in the $900) | |
| Form 8815 line 14 (Drew paid tuition; MAGI below the phase-out) | $1,800.00 |
| Kim Sample (000-00-0023, dependent), custodial account 1099-INT box 1 under Kim's SSN | $600.00 — Kim's return, or Form 8814 line 1a if elected |

Steps:

1. Statements for this return: Morgan's and Drew's. Kim's 1099-INT is left out.
2. Schedule B line 1 (seller-financed first): Pat Buyer $2,000; First Bank $900; Broker A $1,200; Treasury $3,000;
   Broker B $400; Partnership $150. Subtotal **$7,650.00**.
3. Subtract: Nominee Distribution $500; ABP Adjustment $200; OID Adjustment $50 → **Schedule B line 2 = $6,900.00**.
4. Schedule B line 3 (Form 8815) = $1,800.
5. **Schedule B line 4 = $5,100 → Form 1040 line 2b = $5,100.** Schedule B is required (over $1,500, plus the
   seller-financed, nominee, ABP, OID and Form 8815 conditions).
