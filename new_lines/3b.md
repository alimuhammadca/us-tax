# Form 1040 line 3b — Ordinary dividends

Tax year 2025. What the IRS requires. How the app does it: `C:\us-tax\new_knowledge\3b.md`. Picture:
`C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\3b.md`. Companion lines: `3a.md` (qualified dividends, a part of this line) and `3c.md`
(the child-dividends checkbox).

---

## 1. At a glance

Form 1040 line 3b is the total of the **ordinary dividends** that belong to you — and, on a joint return, to your
spouse. "Ordinary dividends" are the dividends taxed as income on this return: the amount in box 1a of each Form
1099-DIV, the ordinary dividends passed through to you on a Schedule K-1 (partnership box 6a, S corporation box 5a,
estate or trust box 2a), and dividends you received with no form at all. Dividends you received only as a
**nominee** (in your name, but belonging to someone else) are taken back out. If you elect on Form 8814 to report
your child's interest and dividends on your return, the part of the child's income that Form 8814 line 9 calls
qualified dividends is added here too (and the box on line 3c is checked). Line 3b is part of total income
(line 9). Line 3a, qualified dividends, is a **part of** line 3b, not an addition to it.

```text
Form 1040 line 3b =
    Σ Form 1099-DIV box 1a             (your forms; + your spouse's on a joint return)
  + Σ Schedule K-1 ordinary dividends  (Form 1065 box 6a, Form 1120-S box 5a, Form 1041 box 2a),
                                       less any part attributable to previously taxed earnings (PTEP)
  + ordinary dividends with no Form 1099-DIV
  − ordinary dividends received as a nominee for someone else
  + Form 8814 line 9 for each child whose income you elect to report     (→ also line 3a; check line 3c box 2)
```

Terms used below:

- **Ordinary dividend** — a distribution a corporation pays out of its earnings and profits, plus the items the
  payer is told to include in Form 1099-DIV box 1a (money-market dividends, a fund's net short-term capital gains,
  reinvested dividends, section 404(k) dividends, a nonpublic fund's investment expenses) (`i1099div.pdf p. 4`).
- **Qualified dividend** — the part of the ordinary dividends that is taxed at the lower capital-gain rates
  (Form 1040 line 3a; see `3a.md`).
- **Nominee** — a person who receives income in their own name that legally belongs to someone else
  (`i1040sb.pdf p. 2`).
- **Capital gain distribution** — a long-term gain a mutual fund or REIT passes to its shareholders (1099-DIV
  box 2a). It is not an ordinary dividend.
- **Nondividend distribution** — a return of your own investment (1099-DIV box 3).
- **PTEP** — previously taxed earnings and profits of a foreign corporation; distributions out of them are not
  income again.

## 2. The IRS wording

**Form 1040 (2025), line 3b:** "Ordinary dividends". (The 2025 Form 1040 itself is not among the documents in
`C:\us-tax\docs\books\`; the label is taken from the instructions and the app's semantic field map
`pdfs\f1040_field_mapping_semantic.csv`, field `line3b_ordinary_dividends`.)

**2025 Instructions for Form 1040 — Line 3b** (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 26`):

> "Each payer should send you a Form 1099-DIV. Enter your total ordinary dividends on line 3b. This amount should
> be shown in box 1a of Form(s) 1099-DIV. If you are including your child's ordinary dividends in the total on
> line 3b, check box 2 on line 3c. For more information, see the Instructions for Form 8814."
>
> "You must fill in and attach Schedule B if the total is over $1,500 or you received, as a nominee, ordinary
> dividends that actually belong to someone else."

**Nondividend Distributions** (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 26`):

> "Some distributions are a return of your cost (or other basis). They won't be taxed until you recover your cost
> (or other basis). You must reduce your cost (or other basis) by these distributions. After you get back all of
> your cost (or other basis), you must report these distributions as capital gains on Form 8949."

> "Dividends on insurance policies are a partial return of the premiums you paid. Don't report them as dividends.
> Include them in income on Schedule 1, line 8z, only if they exceed the total of all net premiums you paid for the
> contract."

**Line 3a sentence that ties the two lines** (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 25`):

> "Qualified dividends are also included in the ordinary dividend total required to be shown on line 3b."

**Schedule B, Part II** (`i1040sb.pdf p. 2`):

> "Line 5. Report on line 5 all of your ordinary dividends. This amount should be shown in box 1a of your Forms
> 1099-DIV or substitute statements."
>
> "Nominees. If you received a Form 1099-DIV that includes ordinary dividends you received as a nominee (that is,
> in your name, but the ordinary dividends actually belong to someone else), report the total on line 5. Do this
> even if you later distributed some or all of this income to others. Under your last entry on line 5, put a
> subtotal of all ordinary dividends listed on line 5. Below this subtotal, enter "Nominee Distribution" and show
> the total ordinary dividends you received as a nominee. Subtract this amount from the subtotal and enter the
> result on line 6."

Schedule B is also required when "You had over $1,500 of taxable interest or ordinary dividends" or "You received
interest or ordinary dividends as a nominee", or when you had a foreign account or foreign trust
(`i1040sb.pdf p. 1`).

**What box 1a holds** (Instructions for Form 1099-DIV, Rev. January 2024 — the edition in `docs\books`,
`i1099div.pdf p. 4`):

> "Box 1a. Total Ordinary Dividends. Enter dividends, including dividends from money market funds, net short-term
> capital gains from mutual funds, and other distributions on stock. Include reinvested dividends and section
> 404(k) dividends paid directly from the corporation. Box 1a includes amounts entered in boxes 1b and 2e and it
> also includes the amount of the recipient's share of investment expenses that you report in box 6."

Box 5 (`i1099div.pdf p. 5`): "Enter the qualified REIT dividends paid by a REIT or section 199A dividends paid by a
RIC to the recipient. This amount is included in the amount reported in box 1a." Boxes 9 and 10: "Boxes 9 and 10
apply only to corporations in partial or complete liquidation. Do not include these amounts in box 1a or 1b."

**Schedule K-1 dividends:**

- Partnership, box 6a (`i1065sk1.pdf p. 15`): "Report ordinary dividends on Form 1040 or 1040-SR, line 3b. … Don't
  include the amount attributable to PTEP in your annual PTEP accounts on Form 1040 or 1040-SR, line 3b."
- S corporation, box 5a (`i1120ssk.pdf p. 7`): "Report ordinary dividends on Form 1040 or 1040-SR, line 3b. The
  amount in box 5a may be attributable to previously taxed earnings and profits (PTEP) … You will need to determine
  the amount of the ordinary dividends that are attributable to PTEP in your annual PTEP accounts."
- Estate or trust, box 2a (`i1041sk1.pdf p. 2`): "This amount is reported on line 3b of Form 1040 or 1040-SR and
  Schedule B (Form 1040), Part II, line 5, if applicable."

**Child's dividends (Form 8814)** — line 9 (`i8814.pdf p. 4`):

> "Line 9. Include this amount on Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR, lines 3a and 3b. Enter "Form 8814" and this
> amount on the dotted line next to those lines unless you file Schedule B (Form 1040). If you file Schedule B,
> include this amount on line 5 and identify it as from "Form 8814." … You must file Schedule B if this amount plus
> the parents' dividends is more than $1,500."

## 3. What goes on this line

- Box 1a of every Form 1099-DIV issued to you (and to your spouse on a joint return), including money-market fund
  dividends, a fund's net short-term capital gains, reinvested dividends, section 404(k) dividends, section 897
  ordinary dividends (box 2e) and a nonpublic fund's investment expenses (box 6) (`i1099div.pdf p. 4–5`).
- Section 199A dividends (box 5) — they are already inside box 1a; they are not added a second time
  (`i1099div.pdf p. 5`). Their only extra role is the qualified business income deduction (Form 8995 / 8995-A,
  Form 1040 line 13a).
- Ordinary dividends from a partnership, S corporation, estate or trust on Schedule K-1 (`i1065sk1.pdf p. 15`,
  `i1120ssk.pdf p. 7`, `i1041sk1.pdf p. 2`), less any part attributable to PTEP.
- Dividend income distributed to you by a trust or estate: "if a trust's dividend income is distributed to you, you
  report the distribution as dividend income on your return" (`p17.pdf p. 77`).
- Ordinary dividends for which no Form 1099-DIV arrived (substitute statements, small payers, foreign payers) —
  Schedule B line 5 takes "box 1a of your Forms 1099-DIV or substitute statements" (`i1040sb.pdf p. 2`).
- Dividends on restricted stock that you elected to include in income when it was transferred (section 83(b)):
  "treated the same as any other dividends. Report them on your return as dividends" (`p17.pdf p. 53`).
- Form 8814 line 9 of each child whose interest and dividends you elect to report (`i8814.pdf p. 4`).

## 6. How to figure it, step by step

1. **Collect the dividend statements that belong on this return.** Use the recipient's TIN on each Form 1099-DIV
   and Schedule K-1. Take yours, and your spouse's on a joint return. A child's dividends are the child's income
   (`p17.pdf p. 10`, `p. 57`); they come onto your return only through a Form 8814 election (step 6).
2. **Add box 1a** of each Form 1099-DIV. Do not add boxes 1b, 2e, 5 or 6 — they are already inside box 1a
   (`i1099div.pdf p. 4–5`). Do not add boxes 2a, 3, 9, 10, 12.
3. **Add K-1 ordinary dividends** (Form 1065 box 6a, Form 1120-S box 5a, Form 1041 box 2a), leaving out any part
   attributable to PTEP.
4. **Add ordinary dividends that had no Form 1099-DIV.**
5. **Subtract nominee dividends.** If any of the above were received as a nominee, list the full amount on
   Schedule B line 5, subtotal, write "Nominee Distribution" with the nominee total, and subtract it
   (`i1040sb.pdf p. 2`). Give the actual owner a Form 1099-DIV (unless the owner is your spouse) and file Forms 1096
   and 1099-DIV.
6. **Add Form 8814 line 9** for each child whose income you elect to report (`i8814.pdf p. 4`). Form 8814 line 9 is
   figured as: line 4 = child's taxable interest (1a) + ordinary dividends (2a) + capital gain distributions (3);
   if line 4 is more than $2,700, line 6 = line 4 − $2,700; line 7 = line 2b ÷ line 4; line 9 = line 6 × line 7
   (`i8814.pdf p. 3–4`, Fred example). Check line 3c box 2 (and box 1, because the same amount goes on line 3a).
7. **Decide whether Schedule B is required:** total over $1,500 (including Form 8814 line 9 — "this amount plus
   the parents' dividends", `i8814.pdf p. 4`), or any nominee dividends, or a Schedule B Part III condition
   (`i1040sb.pdf p. 1`). If filed, Schedule B line 6 is the line 3b amount.
8. **Round** to whole dollars if you round the return; add with cents and round only the total
   (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 23`).
9. Enter the result on **Form 1040 line 3b**.

There is no IRS worksheet for line 3b; Schedule B Part II is its itemized list.

| Child's interest and dividends (Form 8814) | An **election**: "Use this form if you elect to report your child's income on your return." Only line 9 reaches line 3b | `i8814.pdf p. 1, p. 4` |

## 8. Married filing jointly / separately

- **Joint return:** both spouses' dividends are added. Each spouse's nominee amounts are subtracted. No per-person
  limit.
- **Separate returns:** each spouse reports only dividends on statements issued to them (recipient TIN), plus their
  share of community dividends in a community property state.
- **Form 8814 on separate returns:** only the parent with the greater taxable income can elect, and only one parent
  per child (`i8814.pdf p. 1–2`).

## 10. Where the amount goes next

- **Form 1040 line 9** (total income) → line 11 (AGI) → taxable income.
- **Schedule B line 6** equals line 3b when Schedule B is filed.
- Line 3a (the qualified part) drives the Qualified Dividends and Capital Gain Tax Worksheet / Schedule D Tax
  Worksheet for line 16 (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 36`).
- Net investment income tax (Form 8960 line 2), the investment income limit of the Earned Income Credit, the
  investment interest expense limit (Form 4952), and every modified AGI built on line 9.

## 11. Worked example

Pat Sample (000-00-0001) and Lee Sample (000-00-0002) file jointly. Their son Fred (000-00-0003, age 6) had only
dividend income, and they elect to report it on Form 8814.

| Document | Box 1a / K-1 ordinary | Box 1b / K-1 qualified | Box 2a | Box 5 | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pat — 1099-DIV, Fund A | $1,200 | $900 | $300 | $100 | $100 of box 1b fails the 61-day holding period |
| Lee — 1099-DIV, Stock B | $600 | $600 | — | — | |
| Lee — 1099-DIV, Bank C money-market | $400 | $0 | — | — | $150 belongs to Lee's sister (nominee) |
| Pat — Schedule K-1 (Form 1065) | box 6a $500 | box 6b $400 | — | — | no PTEP |
| Fred — 1099-DIV | $2,190 | $2,190 | $610 | — | Form 8814 elected |

Steps:

1. Statements for this return: Pat's and Lee's. Fred's 1099-DIV goes onto Form 8814, not directly onto line 3b.
2. Box 1a: $1,200 + $600 + $400 = $2,200. (Box 5 $100 is already inside $1,200; box 2a $300 goes to Schedule D
   line 13.)
3. K-1 box 6a: + $500 → $2,700.
4. No dividends without a form.
5. Nominee: − $150 → $2,550.
6. Form 8814 for Fred: line 4 = $2,190 + $610 = $2,800; line 6 = $100; line 7 = 2,190 ÷ 2,800 = 0.78; line 9 =
   $78; line 10 = $22 (to Schedule D line 13) (`i8814.pdf p. 3–4`). Line 3b: + $78 → **$2,628**. Line 3c box 2 (and
   box 1) checked.
7. Schedule B required ($2,628 > $1,500, and nominee dividends). Part II line 5 lists Fund A $1,200, Stock B $600,
   Bank C $400, the partnership $500, "Form 8814" $78; subtotal $2,778; "Nominee Distribution" ($150); line 6
   $2,628.
8. **Form 1040 line 3b = $2,628.**
