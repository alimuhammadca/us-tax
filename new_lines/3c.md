# Form 1040 line 3c — Child's dividends included (checkboxes)

Tax year 2025. What the IRS requires. How the app does it: `C:\us-tax\new_knowledge\3c.md`. Picture:
`C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\3c.md`. The amounts themselves are on line 3a (`3a.md`) and line 3b (`3b.md`).

---

## 1. At a glance

Line 3c holds no amount. It is two checkboxes that tell the IRS that a child's dividends — reported by the parent
through the **Form 8814 election** ("Parents' Election To Report Child's Interest and Dividends") — are inside the
totals on line 3a (box 1) and/or line 3b (box 2). The only child amount Form 8814 sends to lines 3a and 3b is
**Form 8814 line 9**, and it goes to **both** lines, so in practice both boxes are checked together whenever any
Form 8814 line 9 is more than zero.

```text
Line 3c box 1 (line 3a) = checked  if  Σ Form 8814 line 9 (all elected children) > 0
Line 3c box 2 (line 3b) = checked  if  Σ Form 8814 line 9 (all elected children) > 0
otherwise both blank
```

## 2. The IRS wording

**Form 1040 (2025), line 3c:** "Check if your child's dividends are included in 1 ☐ Line 3a 2 ☐ Line 3b" (wording
from the assignment and the semantic field map `pdfs\f1040_field_mapping_semantic.csv`, fields
`line3c_child_dividends_included_in_line3a` / `…_in_line3b`; the 2025 form is not in `docs\books`).

**2025 Instructions for Form 1040:**

- Line 3a (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 25`): "If you are including your child's qualified dividends in the total on
  line 3a, check box 1 on line 3c. For more information, see the Instructions for Form 8814."
- Line 3b (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 26`): "If you are including your child's ordinary dividends in the total on
  line 3b, check box 2 on line 3c. For more information, see the Instructions for Form 8814."
- Tip (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 26`): "If you are including your child's dividends on either line 3a or 3b, check the
  applicable box on line 3c."

**Instructions for Form 8814 (2025)** — the election (`i8814.pdf p. 1`): "Use this form if you elect to report your
child's income on your return. If you do, your child will not have to file a return." Line 9 (`i8814.pdf p. 4`):
"Include this amount on Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR, lines 3a and 3b. Enter "Form 8814" and this amount on the
dotted line next to those lines unless you file Schedule B (Form 1040)."

## 3. When the boxes are checked

Both boxes are checked when **all** of these hold (`i8814.pdf p. 1–4`):

1. The parent elects Form 8814 for at least one child. The child must have been under 19 at the end of 2025 (or a
   full-time student under 24), have had only interest and dividend income (including capital gain distributions
   and Alaska Permanent Fund dividends), gross income under $13,500, be required to file, not file a joint return,
   have had no estimated tax payments and no federal income tax withheld. A child born on January 1, 2007 counts as
   19, and one born on January 1, 2002 as 24 (`i8814.pdf p. 1`).
2. The parent is the one allowed to elect: a joint return of the parents; on separate returns, the parent with the
   greater taxable income; otherwise the custodial parent (`i8814.pdf p. 1–2`).
3. The child's interest + ordinary dividends + capital gain distributions (Form 8814 line 4) are more than $2,700,
   and the child had qualified dividends (line 2b), so line 9 = (line 4 − $2,700) × line 2b ÷ line 4 is more than
   zero (`i8814.pdf p. 3–4`).

## 4. When the boxes stay blank

| Situation | Why | Source |
|---|---|---|
| No Form 8814 election (child files own return, or no child income) | No child dividends on the parent's lines 3a/3b | `i8814.pdf p. 1` |
| Form 8814 line 4 is $2,700 or less | Lines 5–12 do not produce a line 9; the child's income is covered by the $1,350 untaxed amount plus the 10% extra tax on line 15 | `i8814.pdf p. 1, p. 4` |
| Child had interest and nonqualified dividends only (line 2b = 0) | Line 9 is 0; the amount above $2,700 goes to line 12 → Schedule 1 line 8z | `i8814.pdf p. 4` |
| Child had only capital gain distributions | Line 10 → Schedule D line 13 / line 7a (and the line 7b box), not line 3 | `i8814.pdf p. 4` |

## 5. Where the numbers come from

| Source | Line | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Form 8814 (each elected child) | Line 2b (child's qualified dividends, from 1099-DIV box 1b) | Ratio numerator |
| Form 8814 | Line 4 (1a + 2a + 3) | Ratio denominator and $2,700 test |
| Form 8814 | Line 9 | The amount on lines 3a and 3b that makes the boxes apply |

## 6. Married filing jointly / separately

- Joint return of the child's parents: the election is made on that return; one Form 8814 per child
  (`i8814.pdf p. 1`).
- Separate returns: only the parent with the greater taxable income can elect; the other parent's line 3c stays
  blank for that child (`i8814.pdf p. 1`).

## 7. Forms to attach

| Form | Filed? |
|---|---|
| Form 8814 | Attached, one per child; box C checked when more than one is attached (see the Form 8814 docs) |
| Schedule B | When line 3b (including line 9) is over $1,500; the line 9 amount is listed on line 5 as "Form 8814" (`i8814.pdf p. 4`) |

## 8. Where it goes next

Line 3c is a disclosure only; it changes no arithmetic. Lines 3a and 3b carry the amounts.

## 9. Worked example

Fred Sample (age 6) received $2,190 of ordinary dividends, all qualified, and a $610 capital gain distribution; his
parents elect Form 8814 (the IRS example, `i8814.pdf p. 3–4`): line 4 = $2,800, line 6 = $100, line 7 = 0.78,
line 9 = $78, line 10 = $22. The parents include $78 on lines 3a and 3b, **check line 3c box 1 and box 2**, and put
$22 on Schedule D line 13. If Fred's dividends had been only $2,000 (line 4 = $2,610, not over $2,700), there would
be no line 9 and line 3c would stay blank.
