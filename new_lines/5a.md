# Form 1040 line 5a — Pensions and annuities

Tax year 2025. What the IRS requires. How the app does it: `C:\us-tax\new_knowledge\5a.md`. Picture:
`C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\5a.md`. The taxable part (line 5b) is in `C:\us-tax\new_lines\5b.md`; the checkboxes
(line 5c) in `C:\us-tax\new_lines\5c.md`.

## 1. At a glance

Form 1040 line 5a is the **gross** (before-tax, before-withholding) amount of pension and annuity payments received
in 2025 — normally the total of box 1 of every Form 1099-R that is **not** an IRA distribution, plus the railroad
annuity on Form RRB-1099-R — for you and, on a joint return, your spouse. Line 5a is a disclosure line: it is **not**
added into total income (line 9 adds line 5b, the taxable amount). It is left **blank** when every pension or annuity
is fully taxable — then only line 5b is filled.

```text
Form 1040 line 5a =
    blank, if every pension / annuity is fully taxable (enter the total on line 5b only)
    otherwise
    Σ Form 1099-R box 1   (IRA/SEP/SIMPLE box NOT checked; you, + spouse if joint)
  + Σ Form RRB-1099-R gross (tier 2, NSSEB part of tier 1, vested dual benefits)
  + Form 4852 line 8a for a missing / incorrect non-IRA Form 1099-R
  − disability pensions received before the plan's minimum retirement age      (→ line 1h)
  − corrective distributions of excess deferrals / contributions (codes 8, P)   (→ line 1h, or the earlier year)
  − a lump sum taxed under Form 4972 Part III (all of it); Part II only: report the ordinary-income part
```

Terms used below:

- **Pension / annuity** — a series of payments (or a single payment) from an employer retirement plan or an annuity
  contract: a qualified plan such as a 401(k), a 403(b) plan, a governmental 457(b) plan, or a commercial annuity.
- **Fully taxable** — you contributed nothing after tax to the plan ("cost"), or you already recovered all of your
  cost tax free before 2025 (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 28`).
- **Partially taxable** — part of each payment is a tax-free return of your cost (line 5b).
- **Form 1099-R** — the payer's statement of a retirement distribution. Box 1 = gross distribution; box 2a = taxable
  amount; box 2b = "taxable amount not determined" / "total distribution"; box 5 = employee contributions; box 7 =
  distribution code(s) and the IRA/SEP/SIMPLE checkbox.

## 2. The IRS wording

**Form 1040 (2025), line 5a:** "Pensions and annuities . . 5a" (`docs/IRS-Forms/f1040.pdf p. 1`).

**2025 Instructions for Form 1040 — Lines 5a and 5b** (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 28`):

> "You should receive a Form 1099-R showing the total amount of your pension and annuity payments before income tax
> or other deductions were withheld. This amount should be shown in box 1 of Form 1099-R. Pension and annuity
> payments include distributions from 401(k), 403(b), and governmental 457(b) plans. Rollovers and lump-sum
> distributions are explained later. Don't include the following payments on lines 5a and 5b. Instead, report them on
> line 1h.
> • Disability pensions received before you reach the minimum retirement age set by your employer.
> • Corrective distributions (including any earnings) of excess elective deferrals or other excess contributions to
> retirement plans. The plan must advise you of the year(s) the distributions are includible in income."

> "Fully Taxable Pensions and Annuities. Your payments are fully taxable if (a) you didn't contribute to the cost
> … of your pension or annuity, or (b) you got your entire cost back tax free before 2025. … If your pension or
> annuity is fully taxable, enter the total pension or annuity payments (from Form(s) 1099-R, box 1) on line 5b;
> don't make an entry on line 5a. Fully taxable pensions and annuities also include military retirement pay shown on
> Form 1099-R. … If you received a Form RRB-1099-R, see Pub. 575 to find out how to report your benefits."

> "Partially Taxable Pensions and Annuities. Enter the total pension or annuity payments (from Form 1099-R, box 1)
> on line 5a."

**Simplified Method Worksheet, "More than one pension or annuity"** (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 29`):

> "If you had more than one partially taxable pension or annuity, figure the taxable part of each separately. Enter
> the total of the taxable parts on Form 1040 or 1040-SR, line 5b. Enter the total pension or annuity payments
> received in 2025 on Form 1040 or 1040-SR, line 5a."

**Rollovers** (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 30`): "Use lines 5a and 5b to report a rollover, including a direct rollover,
from one qualified employer's plan to another or to an IRA. Enter on line 5a the distribution from Form 1099-R,
box 1."

**Lump-Sum Distributions** (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 30`): "Enter the total distribution on line 5a and the taxable
part on line 5b. … If you or the plan participant was born before January 2, 1936, you could pay less tax on the
distribution. See Form 4972."

**Insurance Premiums for Retired Public Safety Officers** (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 28`): "Report your total
distributions on line 5a and the taxable amount on line 5b. Also check box 2 on line 5c."

**Line 1h, disability pensions** (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 25`): "Disability pensions received after you reach minimum
retirement age and other payments shown on Form 1099-R (other than payments from an IRA) are reported on lines 5a and
5b. Payments from an IRA are reported on lines 4a and 4b."

**Form 4972, Where to report** (`f4972.pdf p. 2`):

> "1. If you don't use Form 4972, and you file: a. Form 1040 … Report the entire amount from box 1 (Gross
> distribution) of Form 1099-R on line 5a, and the taxable amount on line 5b. If your pension or annuity is fully
> taxable, enter the amount from box 2a (Taxable amount) of Form 1099-R on line 5b; don't make an entry on line 5a.
> 2. If you don't use Part III of Form 4972, but use Part II, report only the ordinary income portion of the
> distribution on Form 1040 … lines 5a and 5b … The ordinary income portion is the amount from box 2a of Form
> 1099-R, minus the amount from box 3 of that form.
> 3. If you use Part III of Form 4972, don't include any part of the distribution on Form 1040 … lines 5a and 5b."

## 3. What goes on this line

Line 5a is filled only when at least one pension or annuity is not fully taxable (or a rollover, PSO exclusion or
other exception applies). Then it holds the total of:

- Box 1 of every Form 1099-R whose IRA/SEP/SIMPLE box is **not** checked: pensions and annuities from qualified
  plans, 401(k), 403(b) and governmental 457(b) plans (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 28`), military retirement pay,
  designated Roth account distributions (code B), commercial (nonqualified) annuities (code D), death benefits to a
  beneficiary (code 4), plan loans treated as distributions (code L), plan loan offsets (code M).
- Rollovers, including direct rollovers (code G, box 2a = 0): the full box 1 (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 30`).
- Lump-sum distributions not taxed on Form 4972 (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 30`).
- Payments elected under the retired public safety officer (PSO) exclusion: the total distribution
  (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 28`).
- Disability pensions after the plan's minimum retirement age (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 25`; `p525.pdf p. 18`:
  "Beginning on the day after you reach minimum retirement age, payments you receive are taxable as a pension or
  annuity. Report the payments on lines 5a and 5b").
- Railroad retirement annuities on Form RRB-1099-R: "Tier 1 railroad retirement benefits that are more than the
  social security equivalent benefit. • Tier 2 benefits. • Vested dual benefits" are "treated as pension or annuity
  income" (`p525.pdf p. 35`).
- Form 4852 line 8a, when it substitutes for a missing or incorrect non-IRA Form 1099-R (`f4852.pdf p. 1`: "Form
  4852 serves as a substitute for Forms W-2, W-2c, and 1099-R").

## 4. What does not go here

| Look-alike amount | Where it goes instead | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Form 1099-R with the IRA/SEP/SIMPLE box checked (traditional, Roth, SEP, SIMPLE IRA) | Form 1040 lines 4a / 4b | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 25, p. 26` |
| Disability pension before the employer's minimum retirement age (often code 3) | Form 1040 line 1h | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 25, p. 28`; `p525.pdf p. 18` |
| Corrective distribution of excess deferrals / contributions (code 8 = taxable in 2025) | Form 1040 line 1h | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 28`; `i1099r--2025.pdf p. 19` |
| Code P (excess taxable in 2024 or earlier) | The earlier year's return, not 2025 | `i1099r--2025.pdf p. 20` |
| Lump sum taxed with the Form 4972 10-year option (Part III) | Form 4972 only — no part on line 5a | `f4972.pdf p. 2` |
| Capital-gain part (box 3) of a lump sum taxed with the Form 4972 20% election (Part II) | Form 4972 Part II | `f4972.pdf p. 2–3` |
| Pension from an NQDC or nongovernmental §457 plan reported in W-2 box 1 / box 11 | Schedule 1 line 8t | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 92` |

## 5. Where the numbers come from

| Source document / form | Box or line | How it enters line 5a |
|---|---|---|
| Form 1099-R | Recipient's TIN | Decides whose distribution it is (you; spouse on a joint return; anyone else — not this return) |
| Form 1099-R | Box 7 IRA/SEP/SIMPLE checkbox | Checked → lines 4a/4b instead |
| Form 1099-R | Box 7 distribution code | 3 (disability, before minimum retirement age) → line 1h; 8 / P → line 1h / earlier year; A → may qualify for Form 4972 |
| Form 1099-R | Box 1 | Added |
| Form 1099-R | Box 2a / box 5 | Decide whether the payment is fully taxable (line 5a blank) — see line 5b |
| Form RRB-1099-R | Box 7 total gross paid (= boxes 4 + 5 + 6) | Added (`p525.pdf p. 35`; reporting details are in Pub. 575, which is not in the 2025 source set) |
| Form 4852 | Line 8a (non-IRA) | Added for a missing / incorrect Form 1099-R |
| Form 4972 | Part II / Part III election | Removes the ordinary part (Part III) or adjusts to the ordinary-income portion (Part II only) |

## 6. How to figure it, step by step

1. **Collect the non-IRA Forms 1099-R and RRB-1099-R that belong on this return**, by the recipient's TIN. On a joint
   return take yours and your spouse's; on any other return only yours. A child's distribution is the child's (no
   parent election covers pension income: `i8814.pdf p. 1`).
2. **Remove what belongs elsewhere:** IRA distributions (lines 4a/4b); disability pensions before the minimum
   retirement age and code 8 corrective distributions (line 1h); code P (earlier year).
3. **Replace a missing or incorrect Form 1099-R with Form 4852** and use its line 8a (do not also count the incorrect
   Form 1099-R).
4. **Lump sums under Form 4972:** take a Part III distribution out entirely; with Part II only, keep only the
   ordinary-income portion (box 2a − box 3) (`f4972.pdf p. 2`).
5. **Decide whether line 5a is used:** if every remaining pension or annuity is fully taxable and no rollover / PSO
   exclusion applies, leave line 5a blank and put the total on line 5b (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 28`).
6. **Otherwise add the box 1 amounts** (and RRB gross, Form 4852 line 8a) with cents, round the total to whole dollars
   if you round (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 23`), and enter it on **Form 1040 line 5a**.

## 8. Married filing jointly / separately

- **Joint return:** both spouses' distributions are added; line 5a is one figure for the return.
- **Separate returns:** each spouse reports only the Forms 1099-R / RRB-1099-R issued to them. In a community
  property state the community part of a pension is split under state law (Form 8958; Pub. 555 — referenced, not
  quoted here).
- No per-person limit on line 5a.

## 10. Where the amount goes next

- **Nowhere in the arithmetic.** Line 9 adds line 5b, not line 5a ("Form 1040 line 9 = 1z + 2b + 3b + 4b + 5b + 6b +
  7a + 8"). Line 5a is shown on the return and compared by the IRS with the payers' Forms 1099-R.
- Its partner, line 5b, feeds line 9, AGI and everything after.

## 11. Worked example

Pat Example (000-00-0001) and Robin Example (000-00-0002) file jointly. Their son Sam (000-00-0003) is their dependent.

| Document | Box 1 | Box 2a | Code | IRA box | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pat — City pension | $24,000.00 | $21,600.00 | 7 | No | Pat paid after-tax contributions (partially taxable) |
| Pat — old 401(k) | $50,000.00 | $0.00 | G | No | Direct rollover to an IRA |
| Pat — insurance annuity | $6,000.00 | $6,000.00 | 7 | No | Fully taxable |
| Robin — employer disability plan | $12,000.00 | $12,000.00 | 3 | No | Robin is 50; plan's minimum retirement age is 55 |
| Robin — 401(k) corrective | $800.00 | $800.00 | 8 | No | Excess deferral returned |
| Robin — traditional IRA | $5,000.00 | $5,000.00 | 7 | Yes | IRA |
| Sam — survivor annuity | $3,000.00 | $3,000.00 | 4 | No | Sam's own income |

Steps:

1. Sam's Form 1099-R is left out (his own return). Robin's IRA goes to lines 4a/4b.
2. Robin's disability pension ($12,000) and corrective distribution ($800) go to line 1h.
3. Remaining: $24,000 + $50,000 + $6,000. Not all are fully taxable (the city pension is partly tax free, and the
   rollover has box 2a = 0), so line 5a is used.
4. **Form 1040 line 5a = $80,000.** Line 5b = $21,600 + $0 + $6,000 = $27,600 (see `5b.md`); line 5c box 1 is checked.
