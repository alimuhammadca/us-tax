# Form 1040 line 5c — Check if: 1 Rollover, 2 PSO, 3 (other)

Tax year 2025. What the IRS requires. How the app does it: `C:\us-tax\new_knowledge\5c.md`. Picture:
`C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\5c.md`. The amounts are in `C:\us-tax\new_lines\5a.md` and `5b.md`.

---

## 1. At a glance

Line 5c has three checkboxes that tell the IRS **why line 5b is smaller than line 5a**. Box 1: a rollover. Box 2: the
retired public safety officer (PSO) premium exclusion. Box 3: a word or code that another publication or instruction
says to write next to line 5b, entered in the space after box 3. Check every box that applies. Line 5c has no amount
and changes no computation.

```text
line 5c box 1 = any rollover (incl. direct) from a qualified employer plan to another plan or an IRA reported on 5a/5b
line 5c box 2 = PSO exclusion elected
line 5c box 3 = another publication / instruction says to write a word or code next to line 5b (+ that text)
```

## 2. The IRS wording

**Form 1040 (2025), line 5c:** "c Check if (see instructions) . . . . . 1 Rollover   2 PSO   3 [entry space]"
(`docs/IRS-Forms/f1040.pdf p. 1`).

**2025 Instructions for Form 1040 — Line 5c** (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 30–31`):

> "If you have a rollover, including a direct rollover, from one qualified employer's plan to another or to an IRA,
> check box 1 on line 5c. See Rollovers, earlier, for more information.
> If you are making the election to exclude from your income distributions made from your eligible retirement plan to
> pay premiums for coverage by an accident or health plan or a long-term care contract, check box 2 on line 5c. See
> Insurance Premiums for Retired Public Safety Officers, earlier, for more information.
> If another publication or instruction tells you to write a word or a code next to line 5b, check box 3 on line 5c and
> enter that word or code on the entry space next to box 3.
> Check each box that applies to you."

**Rollovers** (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 30`): "Enter the remaining amount on line 5b. If the remaining amount is zero and
you have no other distribution to report on line 5b, enter -0- on line 5b. Also check box 1 on line 5c."

**PSO** (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 28`): "Report your total distributions on line 5a and the taxable amount on line 5b.
Also check box 2 on line 5c." For a PSO taking the exclusion on a **line 1h** disability pension: "include only the
taxable amount on that line and enter "PSO" and the amount excluded on the line next to line 1h"
(`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 28, p. 30`) — that case does not use line 5c.

## 3. When each box is checked

| Box | Check when | Not when | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Rollover | Any part of a line-5a distribution was rolled over, directly (code G / H, box 2a = 0) or within 60 days, to another plan or an IRA — including a rollover to a Roth IRA (it is still a rollover, though generally taxable) | Only IRA-to-IRA rollovers (those are line 4c box 1) | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 27, p. 30` |
| 2 PSO | The retired public safety officer elects the premium exclusion against a line-5b pension | The exclusion is taken on a line-1h disability pension (write "PSO" next to line 1h instead) | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 28, p. 30` |
| 3 (other) | Another publication or instruction tells you to write a word or code next to line 5b; enter it in the space | — | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 31` |

No 2025 source in the set names a specific line-5b write-in code; line 4c's equivalent requires a statement when more
than one exception applies ("Line 4b – $1,000 Rollover and $500 HFD", `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 28`), but the line 5c
instructions contain no such statement requirement.

## 4. Married filing jointly / separately

One set of boxes per return: on a joint return a box is checked if it applies to either spouse. On separate returns
each spouse checks only for his or her own pensions.

## 5. Where it goes next

Nowhere in the arithmetic. It explains line 5a − line 5b to the IRS matching program (Forms 1099-R and 5498).
