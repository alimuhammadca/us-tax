# Form 1040 line 6b — Social security benefits: taxable amount

Tax year 2025. What the IRS requires. How the app does it: `C:\us-tax\new_knowledge\6b.md`. Picture:
`C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\6b.md`. Line 6a (the net benefits that start the computation) is in `6a.md`; the
lump-sum election (line 6c) in `6c.md`; the married-filing-separately box (line 6d) in `6d.md`.

---

## 1. At a glance

Line 6b is the part of the net benefits on line 6a that is taxable. It comes from the **Social Security Benefits
Worksheet** (in the Form 1040 instructions) or, when certain exclusions are claimed, the identical **Pub. 915
Worksheet 1**, which adds one line for those exclusions. The worksheet compares "provisional income" — half the
benefits plus all other income, including tax-exempt interest and income the filer excluded, less certain Schedule 1
adjustments — with a **base amount** ($25,000; $32,000 joint; $0 married filing separately and lived with the spouse
at any time). Up to 50% of the benefits become taxable above the base amount and up to 85% above a second amount
($34,000; $44,000 joint). Line 6b is never more than 85% of line 6a.

```text
L1  = line 6a (net benefits)                 L2 = 50% × L1
L3  = Form 1040 lines 1z + 2b + 3b + 4b + 5b + 7a + 8
L4  = Form 1040 line 2a (tax-exempt interest)
L5  = exclusions added back (Pub. 915 Worksheet 1 only: Form 8839 line 30, Form 2555 lines 45 and 50,
      Form 4563 line 15, Puerto Rico income of a bona fide resident; with Form 8815 use Schedule B line 2 on L3)
L6  = L2 + L3 + L4 + L5                       L7 = Schedule 1 lines 11–20 + 23 + 25
L8  = L6 − L7   (if L7 ≥ L6 → line 6b = 0)
MFS, lived with spouse at any time:   line 6b = min(85% × L8, 85% × L1)
otherwise:
L9  = base: $32,000 MFJ; $25,000 single / HOH / QSS / MFS lived apart all year   (L8 ≤ L9 → line 6b = 0)
L10 = L8 − L9                                 L11 = $12,000 MFJ; $9,000 others
L12 = max(0, L10 − L11)                       L13 = min(L10, L11)          L14 = 50% × L13
L15 = min(L2, L14)                            L16 = 85% × L12              L17 = L15 + L16
L18 = 85% × L1                                line 6b = L19 = min(L17, L18)
```

(Numbering of Pub. 915 Worksheet 1 as printed in `p17.pdf p. 66–68`. The worksheet in the Form 1040 instructions has
no exclusion line, so its lines 5–18 are Pub. 915 lines 6–19.)

Terms used below:

- **Provisional income** — the figure on worksheet line 8: one-half of the benefits plus all other income
  (tax-exempt interest and excluded income included) less the listed adjustments. The term is used here for
  readability; the IRS text calls it "the total of one-half of your benefits and all your other income".
- **Base amount** — the first threshold (`p17.pdf p. 65`).
- **Schedule 1 subset** — Schedule 1 (Form 1040) Part II lines 11 through 20, 23 and 25: every adjustment except the
  student loan interest deduction (line 21) and the reserved line 22.

> earned income or foreign housing, or • Income earned by bona fide residents of American Samoa or Puerto Rico."
>

(`p17.pdf p. 65`) "Base amount. Your base amount is: • $25,000 if you are single, head of household, or qualifying

married filing separately and lived with your spouse at any time during 2025."

## 3. What goes on this line

The result of worksheet line 18 (Form 1040 instructions) or line 19 (Pub. 915 Worksheet 1 / Pub. 590-A Worksheet 3):
the taxable part of the net benefits — 0 when provisional income is not above the base amount, otherwise up to 50%
and then up to 85% of line 6a. It is **0, not blank,** when line 6a has an amount but nothing is taxable
(`p17.pdf p. 65`: "Enter -0- on Form 1040 or 1040-SR, line 6b").

## 5. Where the numbers come from

| Worksheet line (Pub. 915 numbering) | Source | How it enters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Form 1040 line 6a | Net benefits |
| 3 | Form 1040 lines 1z, 2b, 3b, 4b, 5b, 7a, 8 (with Form 8815: Schedule B line 2 instead of line 2b) | Added |
| 4 | Form 1040 line 2a | Tax-exempt interest, added |
| 5 | Form 8839 line 30; Form 2555 lines 45 and 50; Form 4563 line 15; Puerto Rico excluded income | Added back |
| 7 | Schedule 1 lines 11–20, 23 and 25 (not line 21 student loan interest) | Subtracted |
| 9 / 11 | Filing status and the "lived with your spouse" fact | Base amount and $9,000 / $12,000 band |

What is inside the line 3 lines matters: line 1z includes wages from Form 4852 and every 1z sub-line; line 8 is
Schedule 1 line 10, which includes the negative foreign earned income exclusion (Schedule 1 line 8d), the Form 8814
child amount on line 8z, and every other Schedule 1 Part I line.

## 6. How to figure it, step by step

1. **Pick the worksheet.** IRA contribution with plan coverage (you or spouse) → Pub. 590-A Appendix B worksheets
   (section 7). Box 4 total more than box 3 total → stop, line 6b = 0. Form 2555 / 4563 / 8815, excluded adoption
   benefits or Puerto Rico income → Pub. 915 Worksheet 1. Otherwise the Form 1040 instructions worksheet. Figure any
   Schedule 1 line 24z write-in first (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 32`).
2. **Line 1** = line 6a. **Line 2** = half of line 1.
3. **Line 3** = Form 1040 lines 1z + 2b + 3b + 4b + 5b + 7a + 8, the amounts as they appear on the return. With
   Form 8815, use Schedule B line 2 (interest before the savings-bond exclusion) instead of line 2b.
4. **Line 4** = tax-exempt interest (line 2a).
5. **Line 5** (Pub. 915 only) = the exclusions listed above, added back.
6. **Line 6** = lines 2 + 3 + 4 + 5.
7. **Line 7** = Schedule 1 lines 11–20, 23 and 25. If line 7 is not less than line 6, stop: line 6b = 0.
8. **Line 8** = line 6 − line 7.
9. **Married filing separately and lived with the spouse at any time in 2025:** line 17 = 85% × line 8; skip to
   line 19 (= the smaller of line 17 and 85% × line 1).
10. **Line 9** = base amount ($32,000 / $25,000). If line 8 is not more than line 9, stop: line 6b = 0.
11. **Lines 10–17:** line 10 = line 8 − line 9; line 11 = $12,000 / $9,000; line 12 = line 10 − line 11 (not below
    0); line 13 = smaller of lines 10 and 11; line 14 = half of line 13; line 15 = smaller of line 2 and line 14;
    line 16 = 85% of line 12; line 17 = line 15 + line 16.
12. **Line 18** = 85% of line 1. **Line 19** = smaller of lines 17 and 18 → **Form 1040 line 6b**.
13. If the benefits include a lump-sum payment for an earlier year, compare with the lump-sum election (`6c.md`).

The worksheets do not prescribe rounding of intermediate lines; whole-dollar returns round the result
(`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 23`).

## 8. Married filing jointly / separately

- **Joint return:** one worksheet with both spouses' benefits and both spouses' income, base $32,000, band $12,000
  (`p17.pdf p. 64–65`).
- **Separate returns:** each spouse uses their own benefits and income. Base $25,000 only if they lived apart for
  all of 2025 (check line 6d); otherwise $0 and 85% of provisional income (`p17.pdf p. 65`).
- **Qualifying surviving spouse and head of household** use $25,000 / $9,000 (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 32`).

## 10. Where the amount goes next

- **Form 1040 line 9** (total income) → line 11 (AGI) → taxable income. Line 6b is the only part of line 6 in line 9.
- AGI-based figures downstream (IRA deduction phase-out through Pub. 590-A, student loan interest MAGI, credits'
  MAGI). Nontaxable benefits (line 6a − 6b) feed Schedule R line 13a and premium-tax-credit household income.

## 11. Worked example

Robin Sample (000-00-0004) and Casey Sample (000-00-0005) file jointly. Benefits (line 6a) $30,000; pension (line 5b)
$40,000; taxable interest (line 2b) $3,000; municipal bond interest (line 2a) $2,000; Casey's deductible IRA
contribution (Schedule 1 line 20) $1,000, neither covered by a work plan (so the Pub. 590-A worksheets are not
needed); student loan interest (Schedule 1 line 21) $2,000.

| Line | Figure |
|---|---|
| 1 | 30,000 |
| 2 | 15,000 |
| 3 | 40,000 + 3,000 = 43,000 |
| 4 | 2,000 |
| 5 | 0 |
| 6 | 15,000 + 43,000 + 2,000 = 60,000 |
| 7 | 1,000 (line 20; line 21 is left out) |
| 8 | 59,000 |
| 9 | 32,000 |
| 10 | 27,000 |
| 11 | 12,000 |
| 12 | 15,000 |
| 13 | 12,000 |
| 14 | 6,000 |
| 15 | smaller of 15,000 and 6,000 = 6,000 |
| 16 | 85% × 15,000 = 12,750 |
| 17 | 18,750 |
| 18 | 85% × 30,000 = 25,500 |
| 19 | **18,750 → Form 1040 line 6b** |

Had they filed separately while living together, Robin (benefits $30,000, other income $43,000, no adjustments)
would have line 8 = $15,000 + $43,000 + $2,000 = $60,000 and line 6b = smaller of 85% × $60,000 and 85% × $30,000 =
$25,500.
