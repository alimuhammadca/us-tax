# Form 1040 line 7a — Capital gain or (loss)

Tax year 2025. What the IRS requires. How the app does it: `C:\us-tax\new_knowledge\7a.md`. Picture:
`C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\7a.md`. The two checkboxes beside the line are on line 7b (`7b.md`). Schedule D,
Form 8949, Form 4797, Form 6252, Form 6781, Form 8824, Form 4684 and Form 8814 get their own `forms\` files; this
file explains how they combine into line 7a.

---

## 1. At a glance

Form 1040 line 7a is the taxpayer's (and, on a joint return, the spouse's) **net capital gain or capital loss** for
the year. In almost every case it is the bottom line of **Schedule D** (Capital Gains and Losses): Schedule D
line 16 when it is a gain or zero, or — when it is a loss — Schedule D line 21, which limits the loss to
**$3,000 ($1,500 if married filing separately)**. The part of a loss above that limit is not lost: it is a
**capital loss carryover** to 2026.

There is one short cut. When the only capital items are **capital gain distributions** from Form 1099-DIV box 2a
(no sales, no losses, no carryover, no box 2b / 2c / 2d amounts, no qualified opportunity fund), Schedule D is not
needed: the box 2a total goes straight on line 7a and the "Schedule D not required" box on line 7b is checked
("Exception 1").

When a parent elects to report a child's interest and dividends on **Form 8814**, the child's share of capital gain
distributions (Form 8814 line 10) is part of line 7a — on Schedule D line 13, or directly on line 7a when no
Schedule D is needed — and the "Includes child's capital gain or (loss)" box on line 7b is checked.

```text
Exception 1 applies (only 1099-DIV box 2a capital gain distributions, see section 6):
    Form 1040 line 7a = Σ 1099-DIV box 2a (your own share, nominee amounts removed)
                      + Σ Form 8814 line 10 (each elected child)

Otherwise (Schedule D):
    Schedule D line 7  = 1a + 1b + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 − 6               (net short-term)
    Schedule D line 15 = 8a + 8b + 9 + 10 + 11 + 12 + 13 − 14      (net long-term)
    Schedule D line 16 = line 7 + line 15
    Form 1040 line 7a  = line 16                        if line 16 ≥ 0
                       = − min(|line 16|, 3,000)        if line 16 < 0   (1,500 if married filing separately)
```

Terms used below:

- **Capital asset** — "Most property you own and use for personal purposes or investment", such as a house,
  furniture, car, stocks and bonds; not inventory, business receivables, depreciable business property or business
  real estate (`i1040sd.pdf p. 1`).
- **Short-term / long-term** — held one year or less / more than one year (`i1040sd.pdf p. 2`; three years for
  certain "applicable partnership interests").
- **Capital gain distribution** — a distribution from a mutual fund or REIT out of its net realized long-term
  capital gains, shown in 1099-DIV box 2a. It is long-term "regardless of how long you held your investment"
  (`i1040sd.pdf p. 2`).
- **Basis reported to the IRS** — the broker checked box 12 (Form 1099-B) or box 2 (Form 1099-DA).
- **Capital loss carryover** — the part of a net capital loss not deductible this year, carried to later years
  with its short-term or long-term character.

## 2. The IRS wording

**Form 1040 (2025), page 1** (`docs\IRS-Forms\f1040.pdf p. 1`; the 2025 form itself is not in `docs\books`):
"7a Capital gain or (loss). Attach Schedule D if required" and "b Check if: ☐ Schedule D not required ☐ Includes
child's capital gain or (loss)".

**2025 Instructions for Form 1040, line 7a** (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 31`):

- "If you sold a capital asset, such as a stock, bond, or digital asset, you must complete and attach Form 8949 and
  Schedule D."
- "Exception 1. You don't have to file Form 8949 or Schedule D if you aren't deferring any capital gain by
  investing in a qualified opportunity fund and both of the following apply. 1. You have no capital losses, and
  your only capital gains are capital gain distributions from Form(s) 1099-DIV, box 2a (or substitute statements);
  and 2. None of the Form(s) 1099-DIV (or substitute statements) have an amount in box 2b (unrecaptured section 1250
  gain), box 2c (section 1202 gain), or box 2d (collectibles (28%) gain)."
- "Exception 2. You must file Schedule D but generally don't have to file Form 8949 if Exception 1 doesn't apply,
  you aren't deferring any capital gain by investing in a qualified opportunity fund or terminating deferral from
  an investment in a qualified opportunity fund, and your only capital gains and losses are: • Capital gain
  distributions; • A capital loss carryover from 2024; • A gain from Form 2439 or 6252 or Part I of Form 4797;
  • A gain or loss from Form 4684, 6781, or 8824; • A gain or loss from a partnership, S corporation, estate, or
  trust; or • Gains and losses from transactions for which you received a Form 1099-B or 1099-DA … that shows basis
  was reported to the IRS, the QOF box … isn't checked, and you don't need to make any adjustments in column (g) of
  Form 8949 or enter any codes in column (f) of Form 8949." (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 31, p. 33`)
- "If Exception 1 applies, enter your total capital gain distributions (from box 2a of Form(s) 1099-DIV) on line 7a
  and check the box "Schedule D not required" on line 7b. If you received capital gain distributions as a nominee
  … report on line 7a only the amount that belongs to you. Include a statement showing the full amount you
  received and the amount you received as a nominee." (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 33`)
- Tip: "If you don't have to file Schedule D, use the Qualified Dividends and Capital Gain Tax Worksheet in the
  line 16 instructions to figure your tax." (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 33`)

**Schedule D (Form 1040) 2025, Part III** (`docs\IRS-Forms\f1040sd.pdf p. 2`):

- Line 16: "Combine lines 7 and 15 … • If line 16 is a gain, enter the amount from line 16 on Form 1040 … line 7a
  … • If line 16 is a loss, skip lines 17 through 20 below. Then, go to line 21 … • If line 16 is zero … enter -0-
  on Form 1040 … line 7a."
- Line 21: "If line 16 is a loss, enter here and on Form 1040 … line 7a, the smaller of: • The loss on line 16; or
  • ($3,000), or if married filing separately, ($1,500)."

**2025 Instructions for Schedule D:**

- Capital losses (`i1040sd.pdf p. 3`): "You can deduct capital losses up to the amount of your capital gains plus
  $3,000 ($1,500 if married filing separately). You may be able to use capital losses that exceed this limit in
  future years. … Be sure to report all of your capital gains and losses even if you can't use all of your losses
  in 2025."
- Line 21 (`i1040sd.pdf p. 14`): "You have a capital loss carryover from 2025 to 2026 if you have a loss on
  line 16 and either: • That loss is more than the loss on line 21; or • The amount on Form 1040 … line 15, would
  be less than zero if you could enter a negative amount on that line."

**Instructions for Form 8814, line 10** (`i8814.pdf p. 4`): "Include this amount on Schedule D (Form 1040),
line 13; or Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR, line 7a. Enter "Form 8814" and this amount on the dotted line next to
line 13 of Schedule D or in the space to the left of line 7a … Check the box on line 7b, "Schedule D not
required", if applicable."

## 6. How to figure it, step by step

1. **Gather your own capital items.** Only items reported to you (and your spouse on a joint return) — a
   dependent's 1099-B or 1099-DIV is the dependent's, unless the parent elects Form 8814 for the child's interest
   and dividends (Form 8814 covers interest and dividends only — a child with a sale cannot be included,
   `i8814.pdf p. 1`).
2. **Test Exception 1** (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 31`): no QOF deferral; no capital losses; the only capital gains are
   1099-DIV box 2a distributions; no amounts in boxes 2b, 2c, 2d. If it applies: line 7a = your box 2a total (less
   any nominee share) + any Form 8814 line 10; check "Schedule D not required" on line 7b; stop.
3. **Otherwise list each sale.** Use Form 8949 unless the sale qualifies for the line 1a / 8a totals
   (`i1040sd.pdf p. 10`: basis reported, no box 1f / 1g adjustments, "The Ordinary box in box 2 on Form 1099-B isn't
   checked", no QOF, not collectibles, no code needed). Each row: column (h) = proceeds (d) − basis (e) +
   adjustment (g) (`i1040sd.pdf p. 11`). Home sale exclusion: code H, exclusion as a negative column (g)
   (`i1040sd.pdf p. 3`).
4. **Fill Part I (short-term):** lines 1a, 1b, 2, 3 from step 3; line 4 short-term gains/losses from Forms 6252,
   4684, 6781 (40%), 8824; line 5 short-term K-1 amounts; line 6 short-term carryover (worksheet line 8). Line 7 =
   lines 1a–6.
5. **Fill Part II (long-term):** lines 8a, 8b, 9, 10; line 11 Form 4797 Part I gain, Form 2439 box 1a, long-term
   Form 6252 / 4684 / 6781 (60%) / 8824; line 12 long-term K-1 amounts; line 13 capital gain distributions
   (including Form 8814 line 10); line 14 long-term carryover (worksheet line 13). Line 15 = lines 8a–14.
6. **Line 16 = line 7 + line 15.** Gain → line 7a. Zero → 0 on line 7a. Loss → line 21 = the smaller of the loss
   and $3,000 ($1,500 MFS), entered as a negative number on line 7a.
7. **Carryover to 2026** when the line 16 loss is more than line 21, or taxable income would be negative
   (`i1040sd.pdf p. 14`); figured with the 2026 Capital Loss Carryover Worksheet.
8. **Round** to whole dollars if you round the return (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 23`).

**Capital Loss Carryover Worksheet (2024 → 2025)** (`i1040sd.pdf p. 10`) — needed when the 2024 Schedule D line 21
was a loss smaller than the line 16 loss, or 2024 taxable income would have been negative:

| Line | Instruction |
|---|---|
| 1 | 2024 Form 1040 line 15 (negative allowed) |
| 2 | 2024 Schedule D line 21 loss, as a positive amount |
| 3 | line 1 + line 2; if zero or less, 0 |
| 4 | smaller of line 2 or line 3 |
| 5 | 2024 Schedule D line 7 loss (positive), else 0 and go to line 9 |
| 6 | 2024 Schedule D line 15 gain (0 if a loss) |
| 7 | line 4 + line 6 |
| 8 | **Short-term carryover** = line 5 − line 7 (not below 0) → 2025 Schedule D line 6 |
| 9 | 2024 Schedule D line 15 loss (positive); skip 9–13 if not a loss |
| 10 | 2024 Schedule D line 7 gain (0 if a loss) |
| 11 | line 4 − line 5 (not below 0) |
| 12 | line 10 + line 11 |
| 13 | **Long-term carryover** = line 9 − line 12 (not below 0) → 2025 Schedule D line 14 |

The carryover is **not** the 2024 line 21 amount: that amount was already deducted in 2024.

## 8. Married filing jointly / separately

- **Joint return:** one Schedule D for both spouses' transactions; one $3,000 limit (`f1040sd.pdf p. 2`, line 21).
- **Separate returns:** each spouse reports only their own transactions and has a **$1,500** limit
  (`f1040sd.pdf p. 2`; `i1040sd.pdf p. 3`). "If you and your spouse once filed a joint return and are filing
  separate returns for 2025, any capital loss carryover from the joint return can be deducted only on the return of
  the spouse who actually had the loss." (`i1040sd.pdf p. 10`)
- **Main home:** the $500,000 exclusion needs a joint return and both spouses meeting the use test and Test 2
  (`i1040sd.pdf p. 2`).
- **Form 8814:** only one parent elects per child (joint return of the parents; otherwise the parent with the
  greater taxable income) (`i8814.pdf p. 1–2`).
- **A dependent's own sales** belong on the dependent's own return.

## 10. Where the amount goes next

- **Form 1040 line 9** (total income), so AGI (line 11) and taxable income.
- **Social Security Benefits Worksheet line 3** includes line 7a (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 32`).
- **Line 16 tax:** Qualified Dividends and Capital Gain Tax Worksheet line 3 uses line 7a when no Schedule D is
  filed, else Schedule D lines 15 / 16; the Schedule D Tax Worksheet when lines 18 / 19 are not zero
  (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 36, 38`; `f1040sd.pdf p. 2`).
- **EIC investment income** includes line 7a if positive; a Form 4797 amount in line 7a sends the filer to Pub. 596
  Worksheet 1 (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 40, 43`).
- Also used by Form 8960 (net investment income tax), Form 6251 (AMT), Form 4952 (investment interest), Form 8615
  (a child's return) — documented with those forms.
- The 2026 Capital Loss Carryover Worksheet.

## 11. Worked example

**A. Schedule D path.** Pat Sample (000-00-0001) and Robin Sample (000-00-0002) file jointly.

| Document | Detail | Schedule D |
|---|---|---|
| Pat — 1099-B, long-term, basis reported, no adjustment | Proceeds $10,000, basis $4,000 | line 8a: +$6,000 |
| Robin — 1099-B, short-term, basis reported, box 1g wash sale $500 | Proceeds $3,000, basis $5,000 | Form 8949 box A code W: −2,000 + 500 = −$1,500 → line 1b |
| Pat — 1099-DIV box 2a | $800 | line 13: $800 |
| Robin — Schedule K-1 (1065) box 8 | −$9,000 | line 5 |
| 2024 Capital Loss Carryover Worksheet line 8 | $2,000 short-term | line 6: ($2,000) |

1. Line 7 = −1,500 − 9,000 − 2,000 = **−$12,500**.
2. Line 15 = 6,000 + 800 = **$6,800**.
3. Line 16 = −12,500 + 6,800 = **−$5,700** (a loss).
4. Line 21 = smaller of $5,700 and $3,000 → **($3,000)**. **Form 1040 line 7a = −$3,000.** Line 7b: neither box.
5. Carryover to 2026 (worksheet, assuming 2025 taxable income is positive): short-term = 12,500 − (3,000 + 6,800)
   = **$2,700**; long-term = 0.
6. Had they filed separately with the same items on one return, line 21 would be ($1,500).

**B. Exception 1.** Lee Sample (single) has only a 1099-DIV with box 2a $1,200 (no 2b–2d), and elects Form 8814
for son Fred, whose line 10 is $22 (`i8814.pdf p. 3–4` example). Line 7a = 1,200 + 22 = **$1,222**; line 7b: both
"Schedule D not required" and "Includes child's capital gain or (loss)" checked, $22 in the entry space.
