# Form 1040 line 7b — Schedule D not required / Includes child's capital gain or (loss) (checkboxes)

Tax year 2025. What the IRS requires. How the app does it: `C:\us-tax\new_knowledge\7b.md`. Picture:
`C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\7b.md`. The amount itself is on line 7a (`7a.md`).

---

## 1. At a glance

Line 7b holds no amount of its own. It has two checkboxes and one entry space:

- **"Schedule D not required"** — checked when Exception 1 of the line 7a instructions applies: the only capital
  items are 1099-DIV box 2a capital gain distributions (no sales, losses, carryover, box 2b / 2c / 2d amounts or
  qualified opportunity fund deferral), so line 7a is the box 2a total and no Schedule D is attached.
- **"Includes child's capital gain or (loss)"** — checked when line 7a includes a child's capital gain
  distributions reported through the **Form 8814** election; the **Form 8814 line 10** amount is written in the
  entry space next to the box.

```text
Box "Schedule D not required"           = checked  if Exception 1 applies (no Schedule D filed)
Box "Includes child's capital gain"     = checked  if Σ Form 8814 line 10 (all elected children) > 0
Entry space                             = Σ Form 8814 line 10       (when that box is checked)
```

The two boxes are independent: either, both or neither may be checked.

## 2. The IRS wording

**Form 1040 (2025)** (`docs\IRS-Forms\f1040.pdf p. 1`; not in `docs\books`): "b Check if: ☐ Schedule D not required
☐ Includes child's capital gain or (loss)", with an entry space between the boxes and line 7a.

**2025 Instructions for Form 1040:**

- Line 7b (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 33`): "If Exception 1 applies, check the "Schedule D not required" box on line 7b.
  If you are including your child's capital gain or (loss) in the total on line 7a, check the "includes child's
  capital gain or (loss)" box on line 7b and enter the amount from Form 8814, line 10, in the entry space. For more
  information, see the Instructions for Form 8814."
- Line 7a (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 33`): "If Exception 1 applies, enter your total capital gain distributions (from
  box 2a of Form(s) 1099-DIV) on line 7a and check the box "Schedule D not required" on line 7b."
- Exception 1 (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 31`): no QOF deferral, "You have no capital losses, and your only capital
  gains are capital gain distributions from Form(s) 1099-DIV, box 2a", and no 1099-DIV with an amount in box 2b,
  2c or 2d.
- QDCG worksheet (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 38`): "If you don't have to file Schedule D and you received capital gain
  distributions, be sure you checked the box on Form 1040 or 1040-SR, line 7b."
- Final checklist (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 81`): "If you received capital gain distributions but weren't required to
  file Schedule D, make sure you checked the box on line 7b."

**Instructions for Form 8814, line 10** (`i8814.pdf p. 4`): "Include this amount on Schedule D (Form 1040),
line 13; or Form 1040 … line 7a. Enter "Form 8814" and this amount on the dotted line next to line 13 of Schedule D
or in the space to the left of line 7a … Check the box on line 7b, "Schedule D not required", if applicable."

## 3. When the boxes are checked

**"Schedule D not required"** — all of these hold (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 31`):

1. No capital gain is being deferred by investing in a qualified opportunity fund.
2. No capital losses (current year or carried over).
3. The only capital gains are capital gain distributions (1099-DIV box 2a or substitute statement; Form 8814
   line 10 counts as such, `i8814.pdf p. 3`).
4. No 1099-DIV shows an amount in box 2b, 2c or 2d.

**"Includes child's capital gain or (loss)"** — the parent made the Form 8814 election for at least one child and
that child's Form 8814 line 10 is more than zero, i.e. the child's interest + dividends + capital gain
distributions (line 4) are over $2,700 and the child had capital gain distributions (line 3)
(`i8814.pdf p. 3–4`). This holds whether line 10 reaches line 7a directly (Exception 1) or through Schedule D
line 13.

## 4. When the boxes stay blank

| Situation | Box | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Any sale, loss, carryover, Form 2439 / 4797 / 6252 / 4684 / 6781 / 8824, K-1 capital item, QOF | "Schedule D not required" blank (Schedule D filed) | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 31, 33` |
| A 1099-DIV with box 2b, 2c or 2d (including a child's included under Form 8814, `i8814.pdf p. 3`) | "Schedule D not required" blank | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 31` |
| No capital items at all (line 7a blank) | Both blank | — |
| No Form 8814 election, or Form 8814 line 4 $2,700 or less, or no child capital gain distributions | "Includes child's…" blank | `i8814.pdf p. 1, 4` |

## 5. Married filing jointly / separately

- The test covers everything on the return — on a joint return, both spouses' capital items.
- Form 8814: only one parent elects per child (`i8814.pdf p. 1–2`); on the other parent's separate return the
  child box stays blank.

## 6. Forms to attach

| Form | Filed? |
|---|---|
| Schedule D | Not filed when "Schedule D not required" is checked; filed otherwise |
| Form 8814 | Filed, one per child, when the child box is checked |

## 7. Where it goes next

Line 7b changes no arithmetic. It tells the IRS (and the line 16 tax worksheets) that line 7a holds only capital
gain distributions when no Schedule D is filed — then the QDCG worksheet line 3 uses line 7a
(`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 38`).

## 8. Worked example

Lee Sample (single) received a 1099-DIV with box 2a $1,200 and nothing in boxes 2b–2d, sold nothing, and has no
carryover. Lee elects Form 8814 for son Fred (`i8814.pdf p. 3–4` example: line 10 = $22). Line 7a = $1,222; check
**"Schedule D not required"** and **"Includes child's capital gain or (loss)"**, and enter **22** in the entry
space. If Lee had also sold one share of stock, Schedule D would be filed, the $22 would go on Schedule D line 13,
the first box would be blank and the child box would still be checked with $22.
