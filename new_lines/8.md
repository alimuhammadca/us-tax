# Form 1040 line 8 — Additional income from Schedule 1, line 10

Tax year 2025. What the IRS requires. How the app does it: `C:\us-tax\new_knowledge\8.md`. Picture:
`C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\8.md`.

Schedules C, E and F and Forms 4797, 4684, 2555, 461, 172, 982, 8889 and 8853 each get their own file under
`forms\`. This file covers **Schedule 1, Part I as a whole**: what each Part I line holds, which form or statement
produces it, and the line-level rules. The detailed computations of those feeding forms are left to their own files.

---

## 1. At a glance

Form 1040 line 8 is a straight copy of **Schedule 1 (Form 1040), line 10** — the "additional income" that has no
line of its own on Form 1040. Schedule 1 Part I collects it in nine places: taxable state and local tax refunds
(line 1), alimony under an old agreement (line 2a), business, farm and rental results carried from Schedules C, E and
F (lines 3, 5, 6), business-property gains and losses from Form 4797 / Form 4684 (line 4), unemployment
compensation (line 7), and a list of 23 lettered "other income" items, 8a through 8z, totalled on line 9. Three of
the lettered items are subtractions (8a net operating loss, 8d foreign earned income exclusion, 8s nontaxable
Medicaid waiver payments), so line 8 can be negative.

```text
Form 1040 line 8  = Schedule 1 line 10
Schedule 1 line 10 = line 1 + line 2a + line 3 + line 4 + line 5 + line 6 + line 7 + line 9
                     (line 2b is a date, not an amount)
Schedule 1 line 9  = 8a + 8b + 8c + 8d + 8e + 8f + 8g + 8h + 8i + 8j + 8k + 8l + 8m + 8n + 8o
                     + 8p + 8q + 8r + 8s + 8t + 8u + 8v + 8z
                     (8a, 8d, 8s are entered as negative numbers; there is no 8w, 8x or 8y)
The 1099-K entry space at the top of Schedule 1 is a disclosure only — it is in neither line 9 nor line 10.
```

Terms used below:

- **Schedule 1** — "Additional Income and Adjustments to Income". Part I (lines 1–10) is additional income and goes
  to Form 1040 line 8; Part II (lines 11–26) is adjustments to income and goes to Form 1040 line 10.
- **Tax benefit rule** — an amount you get back (a refund or reimbursement) is income only to the extent that
  deducting it in an earlier year lowered your tax that year.
- **NOL** — net operating loss: a business loss larger than the year's income, carried to later years.
- **Excess business loss** — under section 461(l), the part of a net business loss above $313,000 ($626,000 joint)
  that cannot be deducted this year; it is added back on line 8p and becomes an NOL carryforward.
- **Write-in** — an item the filer describes in words (line 8z: "List type and amount").

## 3. What goes on this line

Everything below goes on Schedule 1 Part I and so, through line 10, on Form 1040 line 8:

- Taxable part of a state or local **income tax refund**, credit or offset (line 1).
- **Alimony** received under a divorce or separation agreement made on or before December 31, 2018 (line 2a).
- Net profit or loss from **Schedule C** (line 3), **Schedule E** (line 5) and **Schedule F** (line 6).
- Ordinary gain or loss from **Form 4797** line 18b, or from **Form 4684** line 31 when no Form 4797 is required
  (line 4).
- **Unemployment compensation** actually paid in 2025, less any 2025 overpayment repaid in 2025 (line 7).
- The lettered items 8a–8v and 8z: NOL deduction (negative), gambling winnings, canceled debt, the Form 2555
  exclusion (negative), Form 8853 and Form 8889 income, Alaska Permanent Fund dividends, jury duty pay, prizes and
  awards, hobby income, stock-option income not on a W-2, personal-property rental income, Olympic / Paralympic
  medals and USOC prize money, section 951(a) and 951A(a) inclusions, the section 461(l) excess business loss
  adjustment, taxable ABLE distributions, scholarships not on a W-2, nontaxable Medicaid waiver payments (negative),
  NQDC / nongovernmental 457 pensions, inmate wages, digital-asset ordinary income, and "other income" (8z), such
  as recoveries of earlier-year deductions, RTAA payments, taxable Coverdell / 529 distributions, taxable disaster
  relief, insurance dividends above net premiums and charitable-deduction recaptures (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 92`).
- The child's income that a parent elects to report on **Form 8814** — the line 12 amount is entered on Schedule 1
  line 8z ("Form 8814") (`i8814.pdf`; see `new_lines\3c.md`).

## 6. How to figure it, step by step

Work through Part I in order, then add. Each line below has its rule, its source and where its amount is produced.

### 6.0 The 1099-K entry space (top of Schedule 1)

- Enter the part of Form(s) 1099-K box 1(a) that was reported in error (gifts, reimbursements) or is for personal
  items sold at a loss. Add the incorrect amounts of several 1099-Ks together. The rest of the 1099-K is reported
  "elsewhere on your return depending on the nature of the transactions"; a personal item sold at a gain goes to
  Form 8949 / Schedule D (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 88`).
- This space is **not** a numbered line: it is not added into line 9 or line 10.
- The IRS example: a couch bought for $1,000 and sold for $700 → $700 in the entry space; a handbag bought for $800
  and sold for $1,200 on the same 1099-K → $400 capital gain on Form 8949 (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 88`).

### 6.1 Line 1 — Taxable refunds, credits, or offsets of state and local income taxes

Rule (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 88`):

> "None of your refund is taxable if, in the year you paid the tax, you either (a) didn't itemize deductions, or (b)
> elected to deduct state and local general sales taxes instead of state and local income taxes."
>
> "If you chose to apply part or all of the refund to your 2025 estimated state or local income tax, the amount
> applied is treated as received in 2025. If the refund was for a tax you paid in 2024 and you deducted state and
> local income taxes on your 2024 Schedule A, use the State and Local Income Tax Refund Worksheet … Report any
> taxable refund you received even if you didn't receive Form 1099-G."

**State and Local Income Tax Refund Worksheet** (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 90`):

1. Income tax refund from Form(s) 1099-G (or similar), but not more than the state and local income taxes on the
   2024 Schedule A, line 5d.
2. If 2024 Schedule A line 5d (state and local taxes paid) is more than line 5e (the capped deduction): line 5d −
   line 5e. Otherwise enter line 1 on line 3 and go to line 4.
3. Line 1 − line 2. If line 1 is not more than line 2: **STOP, none of your refund is taxable**.
4. 2024 total itemized deductions (Schedule A line 17).
   *Note: if the 2024 status was married filing separately and the spouse itemized, skip lines 5–7 and enter line 4
   on line 8.*
5. 2024 standard deduction for the 2024 filing status: $14,600 single / MFS; $29,200 MFJ / QSS; $21,900 HOH.
6. $1,550 for each box (born before January 2, 1960; blind; the same for the spouse) — $1,950 if the 2024 status was
   single or head of household.
7. Line 5 + line 6.
8. If line 7 is less than line 4: line 4 − line 7; otherwise **STOP, none is taxable**.
9. **Taxable part = smaller of line 3 or line 8** → Schedule 1 line 1.

**Use Pub. 525 instead** ("Itemized Deduction Recoveries", `p525.pdf p. 24`) when any of nine exceptions applies
(`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 88–89`): the refund is for a year other than 2024; it is a refund of a non-income tax (sales,
real property) deducted earlier; 2024 had taxable income but no tax because of the 0% capital-gain rate; the 2024
refund is more than the 2024 income-tax deduction minus the sales tax you could have deducted; the last 2024
estimated state payment was made in 2025; you owed AMT in 2024; 2024 credits were limited by tax; you could be
claimed as a dependent in 2024; or the refund came from a joint state return and you are not filing a joint 2025
return with the same person.

Recoveries of other earlier-year itemized deductions (real estate taxes, sales taxes, mortgage interest, medical)
are **not** line 1; they go on line 8z (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 92`).

### 6.2 Lines 2a and 2b — Alimony received

Rule (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 89`):

> "Enter amounts received as alimony or separate maintenance pursuant to a divorce or separation agreement entered
> into on or before December 31, 2018, unless that agreement was changed after December 31, 2018, to expressly
> provide that alimony received isn't included in your income. Alimony received is not included in your income if
> you entered into a divorce or separation agreement after December 31, 2018."
>
> "If you are including alimony payments from more than one divorce or separation agreement in your income, enter
> the total of all alimony received on line 2a."
>
> "Line 2b … enter the month and year of your original divorce or separation agreement … If you have alimony
> payments from more than one divorce or separation agreement, on line 2b enter the month and year of the divorce or
> separation agreement for which you received the most income. Attach a statement listing the month and year of the
> other agreements."

Line 2b is a date, not part of the line 10 sum.

### 6.3 Line 3 — Business income or (loss) (Schedule C)

Rule (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 89`): "If you operated a business or practiced your profession as a sole proprietor,
report your income and expenses on Schedule C." The Schedule C form face sends line 31 here: "If a profit, enter on
both Schedule 1 (Form 1040), line 3, and on Schedule SE, line 2 … If you checked 32a, enter the loss on both
Schedule 1 (Form 1040), line 3, and on Schedule SE" (`docs\IRS-Forms\f1040sc.pdf p. 1`). A loss is entered only
after the at-risk and passive-activity rules (`i1040sc.pdf p. 14`, Line 31). Each business (and each spouse) has its
own Schedule C; line 3 is the total of all of them. A statutory employee's W-2 box 1 is reported on a Schedule C
(`new_lines\1a.md`). Detail: `new_lines\forms\sch-c.md` (later).

### 6.4 Line 4 — Other gains or (losses) (Form 4797 / Form 4684)

Rule (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 89`):

> "If you sold or exchanged assets used in a trade or business, and are filing Form 4797, include the amount from
> Form 4797, line 18b, on line 4 and check the "4797" box. … If Form 4797 isn't otherwise required, include the
> amount from Form 4684, line 31, on line 4 and check the "4684" box."

Form 4797 line 18b: "Redetermine the gain or (loss) on line 17 excluding the loss, if any, on line 18a. Enter here
and on Schedule 1 (Form 1040), Part I, line 4" (`docs\IRS-Forms\f4797.pdf p. 1`). Form 4684 line 31 / 38a: "If Form
4797 … isn't otherwise required, enter the amount from this line on your Schedule 1 (Form 1040), line 4 and check
the "4684" box" (`i4684.pdf p. 12`). Line 4 is an ordinary amount (section 1231 gains that are capital go to
Schedule D instead). Detail: `forms\4797.md`, `forms\4684.md` (later).

### 6.5 Line 5 — Rental real estate, royalties, partnerships, S corporations, trusts (Schedule E)

Schedule E line 41: "Total income or (loss). Combine lines 26, 32, 37, 39, and 40. Enter the result here and on
Schedule 1 (Form 1040), line 5" (`docs\IRS-Forms\f1040se.pdf p. 2`). Line 26 is rentals / royalties (Part I),
line 32 partnerships and S corporations (Part II), line 37 estates and trusts (Part III), line 39 REMICs (Part IV),
line 40 farm rental from Form 4835. Passive-loss (Form 8582) and at-risk (Form 6198) limits are applied inside
Schedule E; a loss on lines 26, 32, 37 or 39 may also be limited by Form 461 (`i1040se.pdf p. 1`). Royalties
reported on Form 1099-MISC box 2 and rents on box 1 (not a business) are Schedule E items. Detail:
`forms\sch-e.md` (later).

### 6.6 Line 6 — Farm income or (loss) (Schedule F)

Schedule F instructions (`i1040sf.pdf p. 9`, Line 34): "Individuals. Enter your net profit or loss on line 34 and on
Schedule 1 (Form 1040), line 6 and; Schedule SE (Form 1040), line 1a." Losses again only after the at-risk, passive
and Form 461 rules. Detail: `forms\sch-f.md` (later).

### 6.7 Line 7 — Unemployment compensation

Rule (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 89`):

> "You should receive a Form 1099-G showing in box 1 the total unemployment compensation paid to you in 2025. Report
> this amount on line 7."
>
> "If the amount reported in box 1 of your Form(s) 1099-G is incorrect, report on line 7 only the actual amount of
> unemployment compensation paid to you in 2025."
>
> "If you made contributions to a governmental unemployment compensation program and you aren't itemizing
> deductions, reduce the amount you report on line 7 by those contributions."
>
> "If you received an overpayment of unemployment compensation in 2025, subtract the amount you repaid from the
> total amount you received. Enter the result on line 7. Also, check the box on line 7 and enter the amount you
> repaid in the entry space. If, in 2025, you repaid more than $3,000 of unemployment compensation that you included
> in gross income in an earlier year, see Repayments in Pub. 525."

Pub. 525 (`p525.pdf p. 29`) confirms the split: "If you repaid in 2025 unemployment compensation you received in
2025, subtract the amount you repaid … If you repaid unemployment compensation in 2025 that you included in your
income in an earlier year and the amount is more than $3,000, you can deduct the amount repaid on Schedule A (Form
1040), line 16, if you itemize deductions, or you can take a credit against your tax on Schedule 3 (Form 1040),
line 13b." Unemployment compensation includes state benefits, railroad unemployment, government disability paid in
place of unemployment, trade readjustment allowances and disaster unemployment assistance (`p525.pdf p. 28–29`).

### 6.8 Line 8a — Net operating loss

"Enter any deduction for an NOL from an earlier year. Enter the amount in the preprinted parentheses (as a negative
number) … See the Instructions for Form 172 for details" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 91`). An NOL from a tax year after
2017 can offset no more than 80% of taxable income figured without it (`i172.pdf p. 1–2`). Detail: `forms\172.md`.

### 6.9 Line 8b — Gambling

"Enter gambling winnings not attributable to a trade or business. Gambling winnings include lotteries, raffles, a
lump-sum payment from the sale of a right to receive future lottery payments, etc. … Attach Form(s) W-2G to Form
1040 or 1040-SR if any federal income tax was withheld" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 91`). All winnings count, whether or
not a W-2G was issued; noncash prizes at fair market value; fantasy-sports winnings are gambling winnings
(`p525.pdf p. 33`). Losses are never netted here: they are a Schedule A line 16 deduction "only up to the amount of
your winnings" (`p525.pdf p. 33`).

### 6.10 Line 8c — Cancellation of debt

"Enter any canceled debt. Canceled debt may be shown in box 2 of Form 1099-C. However, part or all of your income
from cancellation of debt may be nontaxable. See Pub. 4681" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 91`). The nontaxable part
(bankruptcy, insolvency, qualified farm debt, qualified real property business debt, qualified principal residence
debt) is figured and reported on Form 982, which also reduces tax attributes (`i982.pdf`). Line 8c is the canceled
debt **less** the Form 982 exclusion. Detail: `forms\982.md` (later).

### 6.11 Line 8d — Foreign earned income exclusion (Form 2555)

"Enter the amount of your foreign earned income and housing exclusion from Form 2555, line 45. Enter the amount in
the preprinted parentheses (as a negative number) … Complete the Foreign Earned Income Tax Worksheet if you enter an
amount on Form 2555, line 45" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 91`; `i2555.pdf p. 7`, Line 45). The housing **deduction**
(self-employed) is not here — it is Schedule 1 line 24j. Detail: `forms\2555.md`.

### 6.12 Lines 8e and 8f — Income from Form 8853 and Form 8889

- 8e: "Enter the total of the amounts from Form 8853, lines 8, 12, and 26" (Archer MSA / Medicare Advantage MSA
  taxable distributions and taxable long-term-care payments) (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 91`).
- 8f: "Enter the total of the amounts from Form 8889, lines 16 and 20" (HSA taxable distributions, and income from
  failing the testing period) (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 91`). An HSA or Archer MSA pledged as security for a loan is a
  deemed distribution included on 8f / 8e (`i8889.pdf p. 3`; `i8853.pdf p. 2`).
- The 20% / 10% / 50% additional taxes are on Schedule 2, not here. Detail: `forms\8889.md`, `forms\8853.md`.

### 6.13 Line 8g — Alaska Permanent Fund dividends

The 2025 Schedule 1 keeps line 8g "Alaska Permanent Fund dividends" (`docs\IRS-Forms\f1040s1.pdf p. 1`). The 2025
Form 1040 instructions have no separate paragraph for 8g; Pub. 525 says "report it as income on Schedule 1 (Form
1040), line 8g" and "the entire … Alaska Permanent Fund dividend payment … is taxable" (`p525.pdf p. 31`).

### 6.14 Line 8h — Jury duty pay

"Enter any jury duty pay and see the instructions for line 24a" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 91`). Pay you must give to
your employer (because the employer kept paying your salary) is entered in full here and deducted on line 24a
(`p525.pdf p. 34`).

### 6.15 Line 8i — Prizes and awards

"Enter prizes and awards but see the instructions for line 8m" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 91`). Prizes in goods or
services are included at fair market value (`p525.pdf p. 35`). Often reported on Form 1099-MISC box 3.

### 6.16 Line 8j — Activity not engaged in for profit income

"See Pub. 525" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 91`). Pub. 525: "You must include on your return income from an activity,
even if you don't expect to make a profit from that activity. An example … is a hobby or a farm you operate mostly
for recreation and pleasure. Enter this income on Schedule 1 (Form 1040), line 8j" (`p525.pdf p. 31`). Hobby losses
are not deductible from other income (`p525.pdf p. 34`).

### 6.17 Line 8k — Stock options

"Enter any income from the exercise of stock options not otherwise reported on Form 1040 or 1040-SR, line 1h"
(`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 91`). Nonstatutory options are income at grant (when readily valued) or exercise
(`p525.pdf p. 11`); an employer normally includes the spread in W-2 box 1, in which case nothing goes here.

### 6.18 Line 8l — Rental of personal property (not a business)

"Income from the rental of personal property if you engaged in the rental for profit but were not in the business of
renting such property. Also see the instructions for line 24b" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 91`). Gross income here; the
related expenses on line 24b (deductible only up to this income).

### 6.19 Line 8m — Olympic and Paralympic medals and USOC prize money

"These amounts should be reported to you in box 3 of Form 1099-MISC. To see if these amounts are nontaxable, first
figure your adjusted gross income, including the amount of your medals and prize money. If your adjusted gross
income is not more than $1,000,000 ($500,000 if married filing separately), these amounts are nontaxable and you
should include the amount in box 3 of Form 1099-MISC on line 8m, then subtract it by including it on line 24c"
(`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 91`).

### 6.20 Lines 8n and 8o — Section 951(a) and 951A(a) inclusions

- 8n: "Enter on line 8n the sum of any amounts reported on lines 1a through 1h and line 2 of your Forms 5471,
  Schedule(s) I."
- 8o: "Enter on line 8o from your Forms 8992 the sum of any amounts reported on Part II, line 5. Remember to attach
  copies of your Forms 8992."
- "If you made a section 962 election and have an income inclusion under section 951 or 951A, do not report that
  income on line 8n or 8o … Instead, report the tax … on Form 1040 or 1040-SR, line 16, and attach a statement"
  (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 91`). See `new_lines` / `lines\section962.md` for the line 16 side.

### 6.21 Line 8p — Section 461(l) excess business loss adjustment

"Enter the amount of your excess business loss from Form 461, line 16" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 92`). Form 461:
"If the resulting figure on this line is a negative amount, then it's your excess business loss. Although it's a
loss, you will report the excess business loss adjustment as a positive number … For Schedule 1 (Form 1040), enter
any excess business loss adjustment on line 8p … it's treated as an NOL carryover for subsequent taxable years"
(`i461.pdf p. 3`). The 2025 threshold is $313,000 ($626,000 joint) (`i461.pdf p. 1`). Schedule C, E and F losses are
entered in full on their own lines; the disallowed part comes back here (`i1040sc.pdf p. 1`; `i1040se.pdf p. 1`).
Detail: `forms\461.md`.

### 6.22 Line 8q — Taxable distributions from an ABLE account

"Distributions from this type of account may be taxable if (a) they are more than the designated beneficiary's
qualified disability expenses, and (b) they were not included in a qualified rollover. See Pub. 907" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf
p. 92`). Only the earnings part of the excess is taxable; the 10% additional tax is on Form 5329.

### 6.23 Line 8r — Scholarship and fellowship grants not reported on Form W-2

"However, if you were a degree candidate, include on line 8r only the amounts you used for expenses other than
tuition and course-related expenses. For example, amounts used for room, board, and travel must be reported on line
8r" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 92`).

### 6.24 Line 8s — Nontaxable Medicaid waiver payments (negative)

"On line 8s, enter the total amount of the nontaxable payments reported on Form 1040 or 1040-SR, line 1a or 1d, in
the entry space in the preprinted parentheses (as a negative number)" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 92`). Full rule:
`new_lines\1d.md`.

### 6.25 Line 8t — Pension or annuity from an NQDC plan or a nongovernmental section 457 plan

"Enter the amount that you received as a pension or annuity from a nonqualified deferred compensation plan or a
nongovernmental 457 plan. This may be shown in box 11 of Form W-2. If you received such an amount but box 11 is
blank, contact your employer" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 92`). The same amount is left out of line 1a. Full rule:
`new_lines\1a.md`.

### 6.26 Line 8u — Wages earned while incarcerated

"Enter the amount that you received for services performed while an inmate in a penal institution. You may receive
Form(s) W-2 or Form(s) 1099" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 92`). Left out of line 1a. Full rule: `new_lines\1a.md`.

### 6.27 Line 8v — Digital assets not reported elsewhere

"If, in 2025, you received ordinary income in connection with digital assets that isn't reported elsewhere on your
return (for example, digital assets, such as income from forks, staking, or mining, which aren't wages reported on
line 1a or capital gain or loss reported on Form 8949 and Schedule D), report this income on line 8v. Don't report
a gift or inheritance of digital assets on line 8v" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 92`). Mining or staking as a business
belongs on Schedule C.

### 6.28 Line 8z — Other income

"Use line 8z to report any taxable income not reported elsewhere on your return or other schedules. List the type
and amount of income. If necessary, include a statement showing the required information" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf
p. 92`). The IRS examples:

- Reimbursements or other amounts received for items deducted in an earlier year (medical, real estate taxes,
  general sales taxes, home mortgage interest) — figured under "Recoveries" in Pub. 525 (`p525.pdf p. 24`);
- Reemployment trade adjustment assistance (RTAA) payments — Form 1099-G box 5;
- Loss on certain corrective distributions of excess deferrals (a negative item);
- Dividends on insurance policies above total net premiums paid;
- Recapture of a charitable deduction (fractional interest; property disposed of within 3 years);
- Taxable part of disaster relief payments (attach a statement);
- Taxable distributions from a Coverdell ESA or a qualified tuition program (Form 1099-Q).

"Nontaxable income. Don't report any nontaxable income on line 8z", such as child support, life insurance death
proceeds and gifts and bequests (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 93`). Form 8814 line 12 (the parent's election for a
child's interest and dividends) is also written in on line 8z (see `new_lines\3c.md`). Pub. 525 lists more 8z
items (kickbacks, `p525.pdf p. 34`, and others).

### 6.29 Lines 9 and 10, and Form 1040 line 8

1. **Line 9** = 8a + 8b + … + 8v + 8z, with 8a, 8d and 8s as negative numbers. "The amount of your deduction [8a]
   will be subtracted from the other amounts of income listed on lines 8b through 8z" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 91`).
2. **Line 10** = lines 1, 2a, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 9 combined ("Combine" — lines 3–6 and 9 can be negative).
3. **Form 1040 line 8** = line 10.
4. Rounding: "include cents when adding the amounts and round off only the total" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 23`); if
   you round, round every amount on the schedule.

## 8. Married filing jointly / separately

- **Joint return:** every Part I line is the total of both spouses' amounts — two Schedule Cs on line 3, both
  spouses' 1099-G box 1 on line 7, both spouses' write-ins on 8z, and so on. Line 2b takes the date of the
  agreement under which the most was received.
- **Separate returns:** each spouse reports only their own income (in community property states, generally half of
  community income; Form 8958). The 8m ceiling is $500,000 (MFS) instead of $1,000,000. The section 461(l)
  threshold is $313,000 for a separate return (`i461.pdf p. 1`).
- **A dependent's income** (a child's 1099-G, K-1, W-2G, 1099-MISC) belongs on the dependent's own return, not the
  parent's, unless the parent elects Form 8814 for the child's interest and dividends only (`p17.pdf p. 10`;
  `new_lines\3c.md`).
- Per-person limits on this line: none at the Schedule 1 level (the per-person rules live in the feeding forms — for
  example each person's Form 8889).

## 10. Where the amount goes next

- **Form 1040 line 9** (total income) = 1z + 2b + 3b + 4b + 5b + 6b + 7a + **8** → line 11 (AGI) → taxable income.
- Pieces of Schedule 1 Part I are reused elsewhere:
  - Social Security Benefits Worksheet line 3 includes Form 1040 line 8 (through lines 1z…8);
  - Schedule 1 Part II: 24a needs 8h, 24b is capped by 8l, 24c mirrors 8m;
  - Schedule A line 16 gambling losses are capped by 8b;
  - the Foreign Earned Income Tax Worksheet needs 8d;
  - Schedule SE takes Schedule C line 31 / Schedule F line 34 (not line 3 / 6 themselves);
  - Form 461 starts from lines 3, 4, 5 (business part) and 6;
  - Form 172 and the next year's 8a take the NOL and the 8p excess business loss;
  - Form 8960 (net investment income) takes rental / passive parts of line 5 and some line 4 gains;
  - Form 6251 adds back or adjusts several items (refund on line 1 via 2b of Form 6251, 8a via ATNOLD).

## 11. Worked example

Sam Example (000-00-0011) and Riley Example (000-00-0012) file jointly. Their 2024 return itemized $32,000
(Schedule A line 5d $9,000 of state income and property tax, under the $10,000 cap, so 5e = $9,000); both were under
65 and not blind. Their 2025 records:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Riley — Form 1099-G box 2 (2024 state income tax refund), box 3 "2024" | $1,200 |
| Riley — alimony from an agreement signed June 2017 | $12,000 |
| Sam — Schedule C line 31 (consulting) | $8,000 profit |
| Sam — Schedule E line 41 (one rental house) | $3,000 |
| Sam — Form 1099-G box 1 unemployment | $6,000; Sam repaid $500 of a 2025 overpayment in 2025 |
| NOL carried from 2023 (80% limit not reached) | $1,000 |
| Sam — Form W-2G box 1 (slot jackpot) | $2,500 |
| Riley — Form 1099-C box 2 (credit card); insolvent by $1,500 just before | $4,000 |
| Riley — jury duty pay, all handed to employer | $300 |
| Sam — Form 1099-MISC box 3, prize from a radio contest | $700 |
| Riley — Form 1099-K box 1(a) $700 for a couch bought for $1,000 | $700 (loss) |

Steps:

1. **1099-K space:** $700 (couch sold at a loss). Not added below.
2. **Line 1 — worksheet:** line 1 = $1,200; line 2: 5d ($9,000) is not more than 5e ($9,000) → line 3 = $1,200;
   line 4 = $32,000; line 5 = $29,200 (2024 MFJ); line 6 = $0; line 7 = $29,200; line 8 = $32,000 − $29,200 =
   $2,800; line 9 = smaller of $1,200 and $2,800 = **$1,200**.
3. **Line 2a** = $12,000; **line 2b** = 06/2017.
4. **Line 3** = $8,000. **Line 4** = blank. **Line 5** = $3,000. **Line 6** = blank.
5. **Line 7** = $6,000 − $500 = **$5,500**; repayment box checked, "$500" entered.
6. **Line 8a** = ($1,000). **8b** = $2,500. **8c** = $4,000 − $1,500 Form 982 exclusion = **$2,500**.
   **8h** = $300 (line 24a = $300). **8i** = $700.
7. **Line 9** = −1,000 + 2,500 + 2,500 + 300 + 700 = **$5,000**.
8. **Line 10** = 1,200 + 12,000 + 8,000 + 3,000 + 5,500 + 5,000 = **$34,700** → **Form 1040 line 8 = $34,700**.
