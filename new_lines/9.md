# Form 1040 line 9 — Total income

Tax year 2025. What the IRS requires. How the app does it: `C:\us-tax\new_knowledge\9.md`. Picture:
`C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\9.md`.

---

## 1. At a glance

Form 1040 line 9 is a plain sum of eight lines that are already filled in on page 1: wages (line 1z), taxable
interest (2b), ordinary dividends (3b), taxable IRA distributions (4b), taxable pensions and annuities (5b), taxable
social security benefits (6b), capital gain or loss (7a) and additional income from Schedule 1 (line 8). It is the
starting point for adjusted gross income (line 11a) and is also read directly by several IRS worksheets (the IRA
deduction and student loan interest worksheets start from it).

```text
Form 1040 line 9 = line 1z + line 2b + line 3b + line 4b + line 5b + line 6b + line 7a + line 8
                   (each operand keeps its sign; line 9 itself has no floor and can be negative)
```

Terms used below:

- **Entered line amount** — the figure written on a line of the form (after rounding, if the return rounds to whole
  dollars).
- **Companion line** — an "a" line that is shown for information but not added: 2a (tax-exempt interest), 3a
  (qualified dividends), 4a, 5a, 6a (gross amounts), and line 1i (nontaxable combat pay election).

## 2. The IRS wording

**Form 1040 (2025), page 1, line 9:** "Add lines 1z, 2b, 3b, 4b, 5b, 6b, 7a, and 8. This is your total income"
(form face, `docs\IRS-Forms\f1040.pdf` p. 1).

The 2025 Instructions for Form 1040 have a heading "Total Income and Adjusted Gross Income" but give no separate
instruction for line 9; the first entry under the heading is line 10 (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 33`). The line is
therefore defined entirely by the form face and by the instructions for its eight operands.

**Rounding** (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 23`):

> "You can round off cents to whole dollars on your return and schedules. If you do round to whole dollars, you must
> round all amounts. … If you have to add two or more amounts to figure the amount to enter on a line, include cents
> when adding the amounts and round off only the total."

Line 9 adds amounts that are themselves "amounts to enter on a line", so on a rounded return it is the sum of the
eight rounded line amounts.

**Operands can be negative.** Line 7a can be a loss (Schedule D line 21 allows up to $3,000 / $1,500 MFS), line 1f
can be negative (Form 8839 line 31, see `new_lines\1z.md`), and Schedule 1 line 8 carries losses:

> "Line 8a Net operating loss (NOL) deduction. … Enter the amount in the preprinted parentheses (as a negative
> number). The amount of your deduction will be subtracted from the other amounts of income listed on lines 8b
> through 8z." (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 91`)

Schedule 1 lines 3 (business), 4 (other gains), 5 (rental) and 6 (farm) can also be losses. Nothing on the form tells
the filer to stop line 9 at zero — contrast line 15, "If zero or less, enter -0-" (`f1040.pdf` p. 2).

## 5. Where the numbers come from

| Source | Line | How it enters line 9 |
|---|---|---|
| Form 1040 | 1z, 2b, 3b, 4b, 5b, 6b, 7a | Added with their signs |
| Schedule 1 | line 10 (via Form 1040 line 8) | Added with its sign |
| Form 8814 | line 12 (all Forms 8814 combined) | Through Schedule 1 line 8z: "add the amounts from line 12 of all your Forms 8814. Include the result on Schedule 1 (Form 1040), line 8z" (`i8814.pdf p. 4`) |

## 6. How to figure it, step by step

1. Finish lines 1z, 2b, 3b, 4b, 5b, 6b, 7a and 8. Line 6b needs a worksheet that itself reads lines 1z, 2b, 3b, 4b,
   5b, 7a and 8 (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 32`), so every other operand — including any Form 8814 amount on line 8z and
   the Form 4972 reduction of line 5b — must be final first.
2. Add the eight entered line amounts, keeping each sign (a loss on 7a or line 8 reduces the total).
3. If the return is rounded, the operands are already whole dollars; the total needs no further rounding.
4. Enter the result on line 9, even if it is zero or negative.

There is no IRS worksheet for line 9.

## 7. Special cases and elections

| Case | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Net operating loss deduction | Negative on Schedule 1 line 8a, so it lowers line 8 and line 9 | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 91` |
| Capital loss | Line 7a negative, up to the $3,000 / $1,500 limit | Schedule D instructions |
| Form 4972 lump sum | Taken out of line 5b before line 9 is added | `f4972.pdf p. 2` |
| Parent's election to report child's interest and dividends | Form 8814 line 12 goes on Schedule 1 line 8z and so into line 9; lines 9 and 10 of Form 8814 go to lines 3a/3b and 7a | `i8814.pdf p. 4` |
| Optional rounding | Round each line, then add | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 23` |

## 8. Married filing jointly / separately

Line 9 has no person-level rule of its own: it adds whatever the eight operands hold. On a joint return those
include both spouses' income; on separate returns each spouse's operands hold only that spouse's income (see each
operand's spec).

## 9. Forms to attach or keep

None for line 9 itself. Schedule 1 is attached when line 8 is used.

## 10. Where the amount goes next

| Consumer | Use | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Form 1040 line 11a | Line 9 − line 10 = adjusted gross income | form face |
| IRA Deduction Worksheet line 3 | "Enter the amount from Form 1040 or 1040-SR, line 9" — line 9 less Schedule 1 lines 11–19a, 23 and 25 is the modified AGI for the deduction phase-out | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 97` |
| Student Loan Interest Deduction Worksheet line 2 | "Enter the amount from Form 1040 or 1040-SR, line 9" — less Schedule 1 lines 11–20, 23, 25 | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 99` |
| Social Security Benefits Worksheet line 3 | Not line 9 itself but the same operands without 6b ("Combine the amounts from Form 1040 or 1040-SR, lines 1z, 2b, 3b, 4b, 5b, 7a, and 8") | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 32` |
| Form 8615 / Form 8814 (child's return) | Child's unearned income often equals line 9 less earned income | Form 8615 instructions |

The IRA and student-loan worksheets are only for filers without Form 2555, Form 4563 or Puerto Rico excluded
income (and, for the IRA worksheet, without Form 8815 or excluded adoption benefits); others must use Pub. 590-A /
Pub. 970 (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 96, p. 98`).

## 11. Worked example

Robin Sample (000-00-0010), single:

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| 1z wages | $52,000 |
| 2b taxable interest | $310 |
| 3b ordinary dividends | $1,240 |
| 4b taxable IRA distributions | $0 (blank) |
| 5b taxable pensions | $0 (blank) |
| 6b taxable social security | $0 (blank) |
| 7a capital loss (limited) | ($3,000) |
| 8 Schedule 1 line 10: business loss ($6,500) + unemployment $2,000 | ($4,500) |

Line 9 = 52,000 + 310 + 1,240 − 3,000 − 4,500 = **$46,050**.

If instead Robin's business loss were $60,000, line 8 would be ($58,000) and line 9 = 52,000 + 310 + 1,240 − 3,000 −
58,000 = **($7,450)** — entered as a negative number, not zero.
