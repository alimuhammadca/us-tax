# Form 172 — Net Operating Losses (NOLs) for Individuals, and the NOL deduction

Tax year 2025. What the IRS requires. How the app does it: `C:\us-tax\new_knowledge\forms\172.md`. Picture:
`C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\forms\172.md`. The line it feeds is documented in `C:\us-tax\new_lines\8.md` (Schedule 1
line 8a); the excess business loss that joins the carryover in `forms\461.md`.

Form 172 (Rev. December 2024) is the current revision ("For use with Form 172 (Rev. December 2024) or later revision",
`i172.pdf p. 1`). The form itself (`f172.pdf`) is in `C:\us-tax\docs\IRS-Forms\`, not in `docs\books\`; it is cited for
the printed line wording only.

---

## 1. At a glance

Form 172 does two jobs:

1. **Figure this year's NOL** (Part I). A net operating loss is a loss caused mainly by business deductions. Start
   from AGI minus the standard or itemized deduction; add back what may not create an NOL (nonbusiness deductions
   beyond nonbusiness income, capital losses beyond capital gains, the section 1202 exclusion, NOLs from other years).
   A negative result is the NOL.
2. **Track an NOL through the years it is used** (Part II — for carryback years; the same "modified taxable income"
   idea applies to carryforwards).

For 2025, an NOL arising after 2020 is generally **carried forward only**, indefinitely; only the farming-loss part
may be carried back 2 years. A carryforward is deducted on **Schedule 1 line 8a** (as a negative number) and, for NOLs
from tax years after 2017, may not exceed **80%** of taxable income figured without the NOL, QBI (section 199A) and
section 250 deductions. A section 461(l) excess business loss (Form 461) is added to the NOL carried to the next year.

```text
Part I line 24 = line 1 + line 9 + line 17 + line 21 + line 22 + line 23      (< 0 -> NOL)
   line 1  = AGI − standard / itemized deduction
   line 9  = nonbusiness deductions in excess of nonbusiness income (incl. nonbusiness capital gain)
   line 17 = section 1202 exclusion;  lines 2–22 = capital loss limits;  line 23 = NOL deduction from other years

2025 NOL deduction (Schedule 1 line 8a) =
    pre-2018 NOLs carried in
  + min( post-2017 NOLs carried in,
         80% × max(0, taxable income without NOL / QBI / §250 deductions − pre-2018 NOLs) )

NOL carried to 2026 = unused NOLs carried in + 2025 Part I NOL (not carried back) + 2025 excess business loss
```

Terms used below:

- **NOL year** — "the year in which an NOL occurs" (`i172.pdf p. 1`).
- **Nonbusiness deductions / income** — deductions and income not connected with a trade or business (lists in
  section 6, lines 6 and 7).
- **Modified taxable income** — taxable income refigured without the NOL being measured (and later NOLs), without a
  capital loss in excess of capital gains, with the section 1202 exclusion added back, and with AGI-based items
  refigured (`i172.pdf p. 4`).
- **Farming loss** — the smaller of the NOL figured from farming businesses only, or the NOL (`i172.pdf p. 2`).

## 2. The IRS wording

**Carryback eliminated** (`i172.pdf p. 1`): "Generally, you can only carry NOLs arising in tax years after 2020 to a
later year. An exception applies to certain farming losses, which may be carried back 2 years."

**NOL deduction limitation** (`i172.pdf p. 1`): "your NOL deduction for tax years beginning after December 31, 2020,
cannot exceed the sum of: (1) the NOLs carried to the year from tax years beginning before January 1, 2018; plus, (2)
the lesser of: (a) the NOLs carried to the year from tax years beginning after December 31, 2017, or (b) 80% of the
excess (if any) of taxable income computed without regard to deductions for NOLs, or Qualified Business Income (QBI),
or section 250 deductions, over the NOLs carried to the year from tax years beginning before January 1, 2018."

**What may not create an NOL** (`i172.pdf p. 2`): "• Capital losses in excess of capital gains. • The section 1202
exclusion … • Nonbusiness deductions in excess of nonbusiness income. • The NOL deduction. • The section 199A deduction
for qualified business income."

**Farming losses** (`i172.pdf p. 2`): "Farming losses … qualify for a 2-year carryback period. Only the farming loss
portion of an NOL can be carried back 2 years." **Waiving the carryback** (`i172.pdf p. 2`): "attach a statement to
your original return filed by the due date (including extensions) for the NOL year. This statement must show that you
are choosing to waive the carryback period under section 172(b). … If you do not file this statement on time, you
cannot waive the carryback period."

**Deducting a carryforward** (`i172.pdf p. 3`): "list your NOL deduction as a negative figure on Schedule 1 (Form 1040)
for the year to which the NOL is carried. … Attach a Form 172 for each NOL to your Form 1040 or Form 1041 if it
applies." **Schedule 1 line 8a** (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 91`): "Enter any deduction for an NOL from an earlier year.
Enter the amount in the preprinted parentheses (as a negative number). … See the Instructions for Form 172 for
details."

**80% statement** (`i172.pdf p. 6`): "Attach a statement to your tax return showing how you figured the 80%
limitation, if applicable."

**Excess business loss** (`i172.pdf p. 4`): "The excess is treated as an NOL to be carried forward. Further, when
carryforwards can be used, they can only offset 80% of taxable income in future years. … Business gains and losses
reported on Schedule D and Form 4797 are included in the excess business loss calculation."

## 3. What goes on this form

- Part I: the 2025 return's AGI and deduction, the nonbusiness / business split of deductions, income and capital
  items, the section 1202 exclusion and any NOL deduction taken in 2025.
- Part II: for each carryback year (farming losses only), that year's taxable income and the refigured AGI-based items.
- The NOL Carryover with an Excess Business Loss Worksheet (kept, not filed): this year's NOL, the part carried back
  and used, and the Form 461 excess business loss.

| QBI deduction (line 13a) | Not in Part I line 1; ignored for the 80% base | `i172.pdf p. 1–2`; `f172.pdf` line 1 |

## 6. How to figure it, step by step (every line of the form)

**NOL Steps** (`i172.pdf p. 1`): "Step 1. Complete your tax return for the year. … Step 2. Determine whether you have an
NOL and its amounts. … Step 3. If applicable, decide whether to carry the NOL back … or to waive the carryback period …
Step 4. Deduct the NOL in the carryback or carryforward year. … Step 5. Determine the amount of your unused NOL."

**Part I — NOL** (printed wording from `f172.pdf p. 1`; line notes from `i172.pdf p. 4–5`)

1. **Line 1** — "For individuals, subtract your standard deduction or itemized deductions from your adjusted gross
   income (AGI) and enter it here." May be negative.
2. **Line 2** — "Nonbusiness capital losses before limitation", positive (no §1202 amounts).
3. **Line 3** — "Nonbusiness capital gains (without regard to any section 1202 exclusion)".
4. **Line 4** — line 2 − line 3 if positive, else 0.
5. **Line 5** — line 3 − line 2 if positive, else 0.
6. **Line 6** — "Nonbusiness deductions", positive: HSA deduction, Archer MSA deduction, payments to a SEP / SIMPLE /
   qualified plan for a self-employed person, IRA deductions, alimony paid, most itemized deductions (not casualty
   losses from a federally declared disaster or state income tax on business income), the standard deduction. Not:
   state income tax on business income (including wages), Armed Forces moving expenses, educator expenses, deductible
   part of SE health insurance and SE tax, rental losses, business property losses, partnership / S corporation business
   losses, §1244 losses, disaster casualty losses when itemizing, and others listed (`i172.pdf p. 4–5`).
7. **Line 7** — "Nonbusiness income other than capital gains": taxable IRA distributions, pension benefits, social
   security benefits, annuity income, dividends, interest on investments, nonbusiness K-1 income. Not wages, salaries,
   self-employment income, unemployment compensation, rental income, business property gains, business K-1 income
   (`i172.pdf p. 5`).
8. **Line 8** — line 5 + line 7.
9. **Line 9** — line 6 − line 8 if positive, else 0 (nonbusiness deductions in excess of nonbusiness income).
10. **Line 10** — line 8 − line 6 if positive, else 0, "But don't enter more than line 5" (nonbusiness capital gain
    left to absorb business capital losses).
11. **Line 11** — "Business capital losses before limitation", positive.
12. **Line 12** — "Business capital gains (without regard to any section 1202 exclusion)".
13. **Line 13** — line 10 + line 12.
14. **Line 14** — line 11 − line 13, or 0.
15. **Line 15** — line 4 + line 14 (capital losses that cannot be used).
16. **Line 16** — "the combined net short-term and long-term capital loss from your Schedule D", positive. "If you don't
    have a loss on that line (and don't have a section 1202 exclusion), skip lines 16 through 21 and enter on line 22
    the amount from line 15."
17. **Line 17** — "Section 1202 exclusion", positive.
18. **Line 18** — line 16 − line 17, or 0.
19. **Line 19** — the smaller of the loss on line 16 or $3,000 ($1,500 married filing separately).
20. **Line 20** — line 18 − line 19 if positive, else 0.
21. **Line 21** — line 19 − line 18 if positive, else 0.
22. **Line 22** — line 15 − line 20, or 0.
23. **Line 23** — "NOL deduction for losses from other years", positive (`i172.pdf p. 5`: "You cannot deduct any NOL
    carryovers or carrybacks from other years").
24. **Line 24** — "NOL. Combine lines 1, 9, 17, and 21 through 23. If the result is less than zero, enter it here. If
    the result is zero or more, you don't have an NOL."

**Using the NOL** (`i172.pdf p. 2`): a non-farming NOL is carried forward "indefinitely until used up"; a farming
loss is carried back to the earliest of the 2 prior years unless the carryback is waived by a statement on the
original, timely return. When "your NOL deduction includes more than one NOL amount, apply Step 5 separately to each
NOL amount, starting with the amount from the earliest year" (`i172.pdf p. 1`).

**Part II — NOL Carryover** (per carryback year, earliest first; `f172.pdf p. 2–3`; `i172.pdf p. 5–7`)

1. **Line 1** — NOL deduction (second preceding year: the farming loss carried back; first preceding year: line 10 of
   the earlier column).
2. **Line 2** — taxable income before the current-year NOL carryback (refigured without the zero floor).
3. **Line 3** — net capital loss deduction.
4. **Line 4** — section 1202 exclusion.
5. **Line 5** — QBI deduction (and §199A(g)).
6. **Line 6** — adjustment to AGI (refigure the rental $25,000 allowance, taxable social security, IRA deduction,
   savings bond exclusion, adoption exclusion, student loan interest, tuition and fees with AGI increased by lines 3
   and 4; attach a computation).
7. **Line 7** — adjustment to itemized deductions from line 33.
8. **Line 8** — estates and trusts only.
9. **Line 9** — modified taxable income = lines 2 through 8, not below 0.
10. **Line 10** — NOL carryover to the subsequent year = line 1 − line 9, not below 0. "NOLs arising after 2017 and
    carried forward to a year after 2020 are subject to the 80%-of-taxable-income limit."
11. **Lines 11–13** — AGI before the carryback, lines 3–6, modified AGI.
12. **Lines 14–18** — medical expenses refigured with the 7.5% floor on modified AGI.
13. **Lines 19–21** — mortgage insurance premiums (pre-2022 years) refigured (Mortgage Insurance Premiums Deduction
    Worksheet, `i172.pdf p. 7`).
14. **Lines 22–27** — charitable contributions refigured on modified AGI plus prior carrybacks.
15. **Lines 28–32** — casualty and theft losses refigured with the 10% floor.
16. **Line 33** — lines 18 + 21 + 27 + 32 → Part II line 7.

**Carryforward deduction in 2025 (Schedule 1 line 8a)** — apply the 80% rule quoted in section 2, deduct NOLs oldest
first, and carry the unused part forward (`i172.pdf p. 2, 4`). "Your carryover is the excess of your NOL deduction over
your modified taxable income for the carryback or carryforward year" (`i172.pdf p. 4`).

**NOL Carryover with an Excess Business Loss Worksheet** (`i172.pdf p. 4`): "1. Enter the amount from Form 172, line
33, if less than zero. … 2. Portion of line 1 above that is a loss that was carried back and used. … 3. Enter the total
excess business loss from Form 461. … 4. Combine lines 1 through 3. This is your NOL to carry over to the next tax
year." (The printed reference to "line 33" is to the NOL line; on the December 2024 form the NOL is Part I line 24 —
line 33 is the itemized-deduction adjustment in Part II.)

## 7. Special cases and elections

| Case | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-2018 NOLs | Not subject to the 80% limit ("Only NOLs arising after 2017 and carried forward to a year after 2020 are subject to the 80%-of-taxable-income limit") | `i172.pdf p. 1–2` |
| Farming loss | 2-year carryback of the farming part; the 80% limit does not apply to a carryback year before 2021 | `i172.pdf p. 2` |
| Waiving the carryback | Statement attached to the timely original return; 6-month relief under §301.9100-2; irrevocable | `i172.pdf p. 2` |
| Carryback claim | Form 1045 (within 1 year) or Form 1040-X (within 3 years), Form 172 attached; may create AMT in that year | `i172.pdf p. 2–3` |
| Change in marital status | Only the spouse who had the loss deducts it; on a joint return limited to that spouse's income | `i172.pdf p. 3` |
| Joint to separate returns | Split the joint NOL by each spouse's separately figured NOL | `i172.pdf p. 3–4` |
| Separate to joint | Treat separate carryovers as joint | `i172.pdf p. 3` |
| Several NOLs in one year | Apply in the order incurred, earliest first | `i172.pdf p. 4–5` |
| Excess business loss | Joins the NOL carryover; subject to the 80% limit when used | `i172.pdf p. 4` |
| Records | Keep for 3 years after the carryover is used or expires | `i172.pdf p. 1` |

## 8. Married filing jointly / separately

- **Joint return:** one joint NOL; a carryover is deducted on a joint return like an individual's (`i172.pdf p. 3`).
- **Separate returns:** each spouse deducts only his or her own NOL; a joint-year NOL carried to separate-return years
  must be split (`i172.pdf p. 3–4`). Line 19 uses $1,500 instead of $3,000.

## 10. Where the amounts go next

- **2025 NOL deduction** → Schedule 1 line 8a (negative) → line 9 → line 10 → Form 1040 line 8 → AGI.
- **Part I line 24 / carryover worksheet line 4** → next year's Schedule 1 line 8a (and Form 172 of that year).
- **Farming loss carryback** → Form 1045 or Form 1040-X for 2023 / 2024.
- The NOL deduction is an AMT adjustment (Form 6251 line 2f, with the separate ATNOLD) — outside this file.

## 11. Worked example

**(a) A 2025 NOL.** Lee Example (000-00-0005), single. Schedule C loss −$60,000; wages $10,000; bank interest $2,000;
standard deduction $15,750; no capital items, no NOL carried in.

| Line | Figure | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| AGI | −60,000 + 10,000 + 2,000 | −48,000 |
| 1 | −48,000 − 15,750 | −63,750 |
| 2–5 | no capital items | 0 |
| 6 | standard deduction | 15,750 |
| 7 | interest (wages are business income) | 2,000 |
| 8 | 0 + 2,000 | 2,000 |
| 9 | 15,750 − 2,000 | 13,750 |
| 15, 22 | no capital loss; skip 16–21 | 0 |
| 23 | no NOL deduction | 0 |
| 24 | −63,750 + 13,750 | **−50,000 → NOL $50,000** |

The NOL equals the business loss net of wages (−60,000 + 10,000). Lee is not a farmer, so it carries forward.

**(b) Using a carryforward in 2025.** Kim Example (000-00-0006), single, carries in a $50,000 NOL from 2024. 2025 AGI
before the NOL deduction is $70,000; standard deduction $15,750; QBI deduction $1,000; no Schedule 1-A deductions.

- Taxable income without the NOL and QBI deductions: 70,000 − 15,750 = 54,250.
- 80% limit: 0.80 × 54,250 = **43,400**.
- Schedule 1 line 8a = **(43,400)**; AGI = 26,600; carryover to 2026 = 50,000 − 43,400 = **6,600**.
- Attach the statement showing the 80% figure (`i172.pdf p. 6`).
