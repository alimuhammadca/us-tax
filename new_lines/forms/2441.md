# Form 2441 — Child and Dependent Care Expenses

Tax year 2025. What the IRS requires. How the app does it: `C:\us-tax\new_knowledge\forms\2441.md`. Picture:
`C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\forms\2441.md`. Part III (dependent care benefits → Form 1040 line 1e) is documented in
depth in `C:\us-tax\new_lines\1e.md`; this file covers the whole form with the weight on Parts I and II (the credit)
and summarizes Part III.

---

## 1. At a glance

Form 2441 does two jobs. **Part II** figures the **credit for child and dependent care expenses**: a percentage
(35% down to 20%, by AGI) of the care expenses you paid so that you (and your spouse) could work or look for work,
limited to $3,000 for one qualifying person or $6,000 for two or more, limited again to the smaller earned income of
you and your spouse, and finally limited to your tax. The credit is nonrefundable and goes on **Schedule 3 line 2**.
**Part III** figures how much of the **dependent care benefits** your employer gave you (W-2 box 10) is tax-free; the
taxable rest goes on **Form 1040 line 1e**, and the tax-free part reduces the $3,000 / $6,000 limit for the credit.
**Part I** lists who provided the care. Part III must be done before Part II.

```text
Part III (only if you received benefits)                          see new_lines\1e.md
  line 26 = taxable benefits                                     → Form 1040 line 1e
  line 28 = deductible (24) + excluded (25) benefits
  line 31 = min( $3,000 / $6,000 − line 28 , Part II expenses )  → Part II line 3

Part II
  line 3  = Σ qualified expenses (line 2 col. d), at most $3,000 (1 person) / $6,000 (2+) — or line 31
  line 6  = smallest of line 3, your earned income (line 4), spouse's earned income (line 5)
  line 8  = decimal from the AGI table (.35 at AGI ≤ $15,000 … .20 over $43,000)
  line 9c = line 6 × line 8  +  line 9b (credit for 2024 expenses paid in 2025, Worksheet A)
  line 11 = smaller of line 9c and line 10 (tax liability limit)  → Schedule 3 line 2
```

Terms used below:

- **Qualifying person** — a dependent child under 13, or a spouse or dependent who cannot care for themselves and
  lived with you more than half the year (section 2).
- **Earned income** — wages, tips, other employee pay and net self-employment earnings (line 4 rules); a student or
  disabled spouse is "deemed" to earn $250 / $500 a month.
- **Dependent care benefits** — employer payments for care, employer-provided daycare, and pre-tax dependent care
  FSA contributions (W-2 box 10; Schedule K-1 (Form 1065) box 13 code O for a partner).
- **Applicable percentage** — the line 8 decimal.
- **Nonrefundable** — the credit can reduce tax to zero but is not paid out beyond that.

## 2. The IRS wording

**Purpose** (`i2441.pdf p. 1`):

> "If you paid someone to care for your child or other qualifying person so you (or your spouse if filing jointly)
> could work or look for work in 2025, you may be able to take the credit for child and dependent care expenses."
>
> "You must complete Part III of Form 2441 before you can figure the credit, if any, in Part II."
>
> "You (and your spouse if filing jointly) must have earned income to take the credit or exclude dependent care
> benefits from your income."

**Qualifying person** (`i2441.pdf p. 1`):

> "1. A qualifying child under age 13 whom you can claim as a dependent. If the child turned 13 during the year, the
> child is a qualifying person for the part of the year they were under age 13.
> 2. Your disabled spouse who wasn't physically or mentally able to care for themselves and lived with you for more
> than half the year.
> 3. Any disabled person who wasn't physically or mentally able to care for themselves who lived with you for more
> than half the year and whom you can claim as a dependent or could claim as a dependent except: a. The disabled
> person had gross income of $5,200 or more, b. The disabled person filed a joint return, or c. You (or your spouse
> if filing jointly) could be claimed as a dependent on another taxpayer's 2025 tax return."

**Divorced or separated parents** (`i2441.pdf p. 1`): "Even if you can't claim your child as a dependent, they are
treated as your qualifying person if: … You were the child's custodial parent." and "Generally, the noncustodial
parent can't treat the child as a qualifying person even if that parent is entitled to claim the child as a
dependent under the special rules for a child of divorced or separated parents".

**Who can take the credit** (`i2441.pdf p. 2`) — all five: filing status single, HOH, QSS or MFJ (MFS see below);
care so you (and your spouse) could work or look for work; one or more qualifying persons; "The person who provided
the care wasn't your spouse, the parent of your qualifying child, or a person whom you can claim as a dependent. If
your child (including stepchild or foster child) provided the care, they must have been age 19 or older by the end of
2025 and they can't be your dependent."; and the provider and qualifying-person information is reported.

**Married filing separately** (`i2441.pdf p. 2`):

> "If your filing status is married filing separately and all of the following apply, you are considered unmarried
> for purposes of claiming the credit on Form 2441. • You lived apart from your spouse during the last 6 months of
> 2025. • Your home was the qualifying person's main home for more than half of 2025. • You paid more than half of
> the cost of keeping up that home for 2025. … If you don't meet all of the requirements to be treated as unmarried,
> you can't generally take the credit. However, you can generally take the exclusion if you meet items 2 through 5."

**Form 2441 (2025) Part II captions** (`f2441.pdf p. 1`): line 3 "Add the amounts in column (d) of line 2. Don't
enter more than $3,000 if you had one qualifying person or $6,000 if you had two or more persons. If you completed
Part III, enter the amount from line 31"; line 5 "If married filing jointly, enter your spouse's earned income …;
all others, enter the amount from line 4"; line 6 "Enter the smallest of line 3, 4, or 5. If zero or less, enter
-0-"; line 7 "Enter the amount from Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR, line 11a"; line 11 "Credit for child and
dependent care expenses. Enter the smaller of line 9c or line 10 here and on Schedule 3 (Form 1040), line 2".

**How the credit is claimed** (`p503.pdf p. 13`): "Enter the credit on your Schedule 3 (Form 1040), line 2. The
amount of credit you can claim is limited to your tax. You can't get a refund for any part of the credit that is
more than this limit."

## 3. What goes on this form

- Part I: every person or organization that provided the care, with TIN and amount paid (including amounts your
  employer paid a third party for you).
- Part II line 2: each qualifying person and the qualified expenses **incurred and paid in 2025** for that person,
  less the benefits shown on line 28 if Part III is completed (`i2441.pdf p. 3`).
- Qualified expenses: household services and care while you worked or looked for work, including your share of
  employment taxes on care wages; day camp; care outside the home for a dependent under 13 (or a qualifying person
  who spends 8 hours a day in your home); a dependent care center that meets state and local rules
  (`i2441.pdf p. 1-2`).
- Line 9b: the credit for 2024 expenses you paid in 2025 (Worksheet A).
- Part III: dependent care benefits received in 2025 (box 10; partner's K-1 code O; sole-proprietorship plan) —
  see `new_lines\1e.md`.

## 6. How to fill it in, line by line

**Line A (MFS checkbox).** If you file separately and meet all three "considered unmarried" tests, check line A
(`i2441.pdf p. 2`). Otherwise an MFS filer cannot take the credit (Part III may still be used).

**Line B (deemed income checkbox).** Check if you entered $250 / $500 a month deemed income on line 4, 5, 18 or 19
(`i2441.pdf p. 2, p. 4`).

**Part I, line 1 — providers.** For each provider: (a) name, (b) address, (c) SSN / ITIN or EIN ("Tax-Exempt" for a
tax-exempt organization; "LAFCP" for a foreign provider abroad with no U.S. number), (d) household employee yes / no,
(e) total actually paid in 2025, including amounts your employer paid a third party, not reduced by reimbursements
(`i2441.pdf p. 3`). Employer-run care: employer's name, "See W-2", column (c) blank, "No", -0-. More than three
providers: check the box and attach a statement; list the three highest paid on the form (`i2441.pdf p. 2-3`).
Missing provider data: show due diligence (Form W-10), enter what you have and "See Attached Statement"
(`i2441.pdf p. 3`). "Don't list an ineligible related individual as a care provider" (`i2441.pdf p. 3`). The
"Did you receive dependent care benefits?" box below line 1 sends you to Part III first when Yes (`f2441.pdf p. 1`).

**Part II, line 2 — qualifying persons.** For each: (a) name, (b) SSN ("Died" + documents for a child born and died
in 2025 with no SSN), (c) check if over 12 and disabled, (d) qualified expenses incurred and paid in 2025, not
including benefits shown on line 28 (`i2441.pdf p. 3`). More than three: check the box and attach a statement.
"The maximum amount of work-related expenses you can take into account for purposes of the credit is $6,000 if you
have two or more qualifying persons even if you only incurred expenses for just one of them" (`i2441.pdf p. 4`).

**Line 3.** Without Part III: total of column (d), at most $3,000 (one person) or $6,000 (two or more). With Part
III: the amount from line 31 (`f2441.pdf p. 1`).

**Line 4 — your earned income** (`i2441.pdf p. 4`): Form 1040 line 1z, less the Form 2555 line 43 exclusion and less
clergy / church-employee amounts also on Schedule SE; plus Schedule SE line 3 less the Schedule 1 line 15 deduction
(lines 3 + 4b, or 3 + 4b + 5a, under the optional method / church employee income); plus statutory-employee
Schedule C line 1; plus nontaxable combat pay if you elect it. Reduce by any self-employment loss. Child support is
not earned income. A student or disabled person: at least $250 a month ($500 with two or more qualifying persons),
or the actual earnings of that month if higher; only one spouse per month (`i2441.pdf p. 4`). (Pub. 503 states the
"you" version of the deemed-income rule for a person "filing a joint return" — `p503.pdf p. 11`.)

**Line 5 — spouse's earned income.** Joint return: the spouse's earned income figured the same way. All others,
including a separated MFS filer who checks line A: the line 4 amount (`f2441.pdf p. 1`).

**Line 6.** Smallest of lines 3, 4 and 5; if zero or less, -0- (`f2441.pdf p. 1`).

**Line 7.** Form 1040 line 11a (AGI).

**Line 8 — decimal** (`f2441.pdf p. 1`): AGI over $0–15,000 → .35; 15,000–17,000 → .34; 17,000–19,000 → .33;
19,000–21,000 → .32; 21,000–23,000 → .31; 23,000–25,000 → .30; 25,000–27,000 → .29; 27,000–29,000 → .28;
29,000–31,000 → .27; 31,000–33,000 → .26; 33,000–35,000 → .25; 35,000–37,000 → .24; 37,000–39,000 → .23;
39,000–41,000 → .22; 41,000–43,000 → .21; over 43,000 → .20 ("Over … But not over").

**Line 9a.** Line 6 × line 8.

**Line 9b — 2024 expenses paid in 2025 (Worksheet A, `i2441.pdf p. 7`).** Only if you did not claim the credit on
the maximum 2024 expenses. Worksheet: (1) 2024 Form 2441 line 3; (2) 2024 expenses paid in 2025; (3) = 1 + 2;
(4) $3,000 / $6,000 by the 2024 count; (5) 2024 Form 2441 lines 24 + 25; (6) = 4 − 5; (7) smaller of your and your
spouse's 2024 earned income; (8) smallest of 3, 6, 7; (9) 2024 Form 2441 line 6; (10) = 8 − 9 ("If zero or less,
stop"); (11) 2024 AGI; (12) the same decimal table applied to 2024 AGI; (13) = 10 × 12 → line 9b. Attach the
worksheet or an equivalent statement with provider and qualifying-person details.

**Line 9c.** Line 9a + line 9b.

**Line 10 — tax liability limit** (Credit Limit Worksheet, `i2441.pdf p. 5`): Form 1040 line 18 minus Schedule 3
line 1 (foreign tax credit) and line 6l (Form 8978 line 14); "if zero or less, stop; you can't take the credit".

**Line 11.** Smaller of line 9c and line 10 → Schedule 3 line 2.

**Part III, lines 12–31 (summary — full rules in `new_lines\1e.md`).** Line 12 benefits received (box 10, not the
part already in box 1; plus sole-proprietorship / partnership benefits); 13 grace-period carryover used; 14 forfeited
or carried to 2026; 15 = 12 + 13 − 14; 16 qualified expenses incurred in 2025; 17 = min(15, 16); 18 / 19 earned
income (as lines 4 / 5 but without the line 12 benefits; MFS not considered unmarried enters the spouse's earned
income); 20 = smallest of 17, 18, 19; 21 = $5,000 ($2,500 MFS when line 19 is the spouse's) or the plan's lower
maximum; 22 self-employment / partnership part; 23 = 15 − 22; 24 deductible benefits = smallest of 20, 21, 22;
25 excluded benefits; 26 taxable = 23 − 25 → Form 1040 line 1e; **27** = $3,000 / $6,000; **28** = 24 + 25;
**29** = 27 − 28 ("If zero or less, stop. You can't take the credit. Exception. If you paid 2024 expenses in 2025,
see the instructions for line 9b"); **30** = line 2 column (d) total, without the line 28 benefits; **31** = smaller
of 29 and 30 → line 3 (`f2441.pdf p. 2`).

## 8. Married filing jointly / separately

- **Joint return:** one Form 2441; you and your spouse must both have earned income (or deemed income); line 4 is
  yours, line 5 your spouse's, figured separately (`i2441.pdf p. 4`).
- **Separate return:** credit only when considered unmarried (all three tests, line A); then line 5 = line 4. If not
  considered unmarried there is no credit, but Part III may still exclude up to $2,500 using the spouse's earned
  income on line 19 (`i2441.pdf p. 2, p. 5`).
- The $3,000 / $6,000 limit is per return, not per person.

## 10. Where the amount goes next

- **Line 11 → Schedule 3 line 2** → Schedule 3 line 8 → Form 1040 line 20 → line 21 → reduces tax (line 22).
- Schedule 3 line 2 is subtracted in the credit limit worksheets of the credits figured after it (Form 8863,
  Form 8880, Schedule R, Form 8839 Credit Limit Worksheet line 3, Schedule 8812 Credit Limit Worksheet A, etc.).
- **Line 26 → Form 1040 line 1e** (see `new_lines\1e.md`) → line 1z → line 9 → AGI.
- Line 24 → Schedule C line 14 / Schedule E line 19 or 28 / Schedule F line 15 (`i2441.pdf p. 5`).
