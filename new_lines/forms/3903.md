# Form 3903 — Moving Expenses

Tax year 2025. What the IRS requires. How the app does it: `C:\us-tax\new_knowledge\forms\3903.md`. Picture:
`C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\forms\3903.md`.

The form wording is quoted from the 2025 form itself, `f3903.pdf` (kept in `C:\us-tax\docs\IRS-Forms\`, one page,
"Form 3903 (2025) Created 4/29/25"); the rules are from the 2025 Instructions for Form 3903 (`i3903.pdf`, dated

## 1. At a glance

For 2018–2025 only a **member of the Armed Forces on active duty** who moves because of a military order and a
**permanent change of station** (PCS) can deduct moving expenses. Form 3903 adds the cost of moving household goods
and personal effects (line 1) and the travel and lodging from the old home to the new one (line 2), subtracts what the
government paid for those costs that was not in W-2 box 1 — the amount shown in W-2 box 12 with code P (line 4) — and
puts a positive difference on **Schedule 1 line 14**. If the government paid more than the costs, the excess is
income on **Form 1040 line 1h**.

```text
line 3 = line 1 (transport + storage of household goods) + line 2 (travel incl. lodging, no meals)
line 4 = government payments for lines 1-2 not in W-2 box 1 (W-2 box 12 code P)
line 3 > line 4:  line 5 = line 3 − line 4  → Schedule 1 line 14 (moving expense deduction)
line 3 < line 4:  line 4 − line 3            → Form 1040 line 1h (income)
```

Terms used below:

- **Permanent change of station** — "A move from your home to your first post of duty when appointed, reappointed,
  reinstated, or inducted, • A move from one permanent post of duty to another permanent post of duty, and • A move
  from your last post of duty to your home or to a nearer point in the United States" within 1 year of ending active
  duty or the Joint Travel Regulations period (`i3903.pdf p. 1`).
- **Code P** — W-2 box 12 "Code P—Excludable moving expense reimbursements paid directly to a member of the U.S. Armed
  Forces" (`iw2w3--2025.pdf p. 21`); Form 3903 line 4 says the government's payments "should be shown in box 12 of
  your Form W-2 with code P" (`f3903.pdf p. 1`).
- **Member of your household** — "anyone who has both your former home and your new home as their main home"; not a
  tenant or employee unless a dependent (`i3903.pdf p. 1`).
- **Foreign move** — a move from the United States or its territories to a foreign country, or between foreign
  countries; a U.S. military base abroad is not a U.S. territory (`i3903.pdf p. 2`).

## 2. The IRS wording

**Form 3903 (2025)** (`f3903.pdf p. 1`):

> "Before you begin: You can deduct moving expenses only if you are a Member of the Armed Forces on active duty and,
> due to a military order, you, your spouse, or your dependents move because of a permanent change of station. Check
> here to certify that you meet these requirements."
>
> "1 Transportation and storage of household goods and personal effects … 2 Travel (including lodging) from your old
> home to your new home … Do not include the cost of meals … 3 Add lines 1 and 2 … 4 Enter the total amount the
> government paid you for the expenses listed on lines 1 and 2 that is not included in box 1 of your Form W-2 (wages).
> This amount should be shown in box 12 of your Form W-2 with code P … 5 Is line 3 more than line 4? No. You cannot
> deduct your moving expenses. If line 3 is less than line 4, subtract line 3 from line 4 and include the result on
> Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR, line 1h. Yes. Subtract line 4 from line 3. Enter the result here and on Schedule 1
> (Form 1040), line 14. This is your moving expense deduction."

**Reminders** (`i3903.pdf p. 1`): "For tax years beginning after 2017, you can no longer deduct moving expenses unless
you are a member of the Armed Forces on active duty and, due to a military order, you move because of a permanent
change of station." "If you qualify to deduct expenses for more than one move, use a separate Form 3903 for each
move."

**Government services and allowances** (`i3903.pdf p. 1`):

> "Don't include in income the value of moving and storage services provided by the government because of a permanent
> change of station. Similarly, don't include in income amounts received as a dislocation allowance, temporary lodging
> expense, temporary lodging allowance, or move-in housing allowance."
>
> "However, if any reimbursements or allowances (other than dislocation allowances, temporary lodging expenses,
> temporary lodging allowances, or move-in housing allowances) exceed the cost of moving and the excess isn't included
> in your wages on Form W-2, the excess still must be included in gross income on Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR,
> line 1h."

**Schedule 1 line 14** (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 93`): "You can deduct moving expenses if you are a member of the Armed
Forces on active duty and due to a military order you move because of a permanent change of station … If you are
claiming only storage fees during your absence from the United States, check the box on line 14."

## 3. What goes on this form

- Line 1: amounts **you paid** to pack, crate and move household goods and personal effects, and to store and insure
  them within any 30 consecutive days after they left the old home and before delivery; for a move outside the United
  States, also moving them to and from storage and storing them while the new post remains the main job location
  (`i3903.pdf p. 2`).
- Line 2: travel from the old home to the new home — transportation and lodging on the way, including the day of
  arrival, one trip per person; your own car at actual gas and oil or 21 cents a mile, plus parking and tolls
  (`i3903.pdf p. 1–2`).
- For you, your spouse and dependent household members; when the military moves family members to or from separate
  locations, all of it is one move (`i3903.pdf p. 1–2`).

## 4. What does not go here

| Look-alike amount | Where it goes instead | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Moving or storage services provided by the government | Nowhere (not income, not deductible) | `i3903.pdf p. 1–2` |
| Dislocation, temporary lodging, move-in housing allowances | Not income; expenses they paid are not deducted | `i3903.pdf p. 1–2` |
| Meals during the move | Not deductible | `i3903.pdf p. 1–2` |
| House-hunting trips, costs of buying / selling a home, lease costs, home improvements, loss on sale, club memberships, mortgage penalties, real estate taxes, carpet / drapery refitting, return trips, security deposits, car tags, driver's license, storage other than in transit or foreign moves | Not deductible | `i3903.pdf p. 2` |
| Car repairs, maintenance, insurance, depreciation | Not deductible | `i3903.pdf p. 1–2` |
| Expenses of a maid, nanny or nurse | Not deductible | `i3903.pdf p. 2` |
| Storage fees only, earlier-year move, government payment in W-2 box 1 | Schedule 1 line 14 directly with the storage-fees box — **no Form 3903** | `i3903.pdf p. 2` |
| Excess reimbursement already in W-2 box 1 | Already in wages (line 1a) | `i3903.pdf p. 1` |
| A civilian's job-related move | Not deductible for 2025 | `i3903.pdf p. 1` |

## 5. Where the numbers come from

| Source | Box or line | How it enters Form 3903 |
|---|---|---|
| Member's receipts | Moving, storage, travel, lodging | Lines 1 and 2 (actual expenses) |
| Mileage | Miles × $0.21 | Line 2 (or actual gas and oil) |
| Form W-2 | Box 12 code P | Line 4 |
| Form W-2 | Box 1 | Government payments already in wages are **not** on line 4 |
| Military orders | PCS | The certification checkbox |

## 6. How to figure it, step by step (every line of the 2025 form)

1. **Certification checkbox.** Check only if you are an active-duty Armed Forces member and you, your spouse or your
   dependents moved because of a military order and PCS (`f3903.pdf p. 1`). Otherwise stop — nothing is deductible.
2. **Storage-only shortcut.** If you moved in an earlier year, claim only storage fees during your absence from the
   United States, and the government's payment for them is in W-2 box 1, do not file Form 3903: enter the fees on
   Schedule 1 line 14 and check its storage-fees box (`i3903.pdf p. 2`).
3. **Line 1 — Transportation and storage of household goods and personal effects**: actual amounts paid (30-day
   storage window for moves within or to the United States; storage while abroad for foreign moves). Exclude services
   the government provided and anything reimbursed by an allowance you don't include in income.
4. **Line 2 — Travel (including lodging)** from the old home to the new home, no meals, no house hunting; car at
   actual gas and oil or 21 cents a mile, plus parking and tolls. Same exclusions as line 1.
5. **Line 3** = line 1 + line 2.
6. **Line 4** = total government payments for the line 1 and line 2 expenses **not** in W-2 box 1, which "should be
   shown in box 12 of your Form W-2 with code P". Do not include the value of government-provided services or any part
   of a dislocation, temporary lodging (allowance or expense) or move-in housing allowance (`i3903.pdf p. 2`).
7. **Line 5.** If line 3 is more than line 4: line 5 = line 3 − line 4, enter on Schedule 1 line 14. If line 3 is
   equal to or less than line 4: no deduction; line 4 − line 3, if more than zero, goes on Form 1040 line 1h
   (`i3903.pdf p. 2`).

## 7. Special cases and elections

| Case | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| More than one qualifying move | A separate Form 3903 for each move | `i3903.pdf p. 1` |
| Member deserts, is imprisoned or dies | Spouse / dependents' move to the place of enlistment, home of record or a nearer U.S. point is a PCS | `i3903.pdf p. 1` |
| Family moved to / from separate locations | One move; reimbursements for both moves not income | `i3903.pdf p. 1–2` |
| Foreign move | Storage while the foreign post remains the main job location is deductible | `i3903.pdf p. 2` |
| Form 2555 filer | Full deduction on Form 3903 and Schedule 1 line 14; the part allocable to excluded income is reported on Form 2555 (Pub. 54 — not in `docs\books\`) | `i3903.pdf p. 2` |
| Storage fees only, earlier move | No Form 3903; Schedule 1 line 14 with the box checked | `i3903.pdf p. 2`; `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 93` |
| Estimated tax | Include taxable reimbursements and the deduction (Pub. 505) | `i3903.pdf p. 1` |

## 8. Married filing jointly / separately

- The deduction covers moving "you, your spouse, or your dependents" (`f3903.pdf p. 1`). On a joint return one Form
  3903 per qualifying move; when both spouses are active-duty members moved by separate orders, each move has its own
  form.
- On separate returns the member who paid the expenses claims them; line 4 uses that member's W-2 code P.

## 10. Where the amount goes next

- **Line 5** → **Schedule 1 line 14** → Schedule 1 line 26 → Form 1040 line 10 → line 11 (AGI).
- **Line 4 − line 3** (excess) → **Form 1040 line 1h** → line 1z → line 9; it is earned income for the EIC and other
  earned-income figures.
- Storage-fees-only case: Schedule 1 line 14 with its box checked.

## 11. Worked example

Staff Sergeant Alex Sample (000-00-0001), active duty, is ordered from a post in Texas to one in Virginia (PCS) in 2025
and moves with spouse Jordan (000-00-0002). Records:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Truck rental and packing materials | $2,400 |
| Storage for 20 days in transit | $300 |
| Mileage, two cars, 1,300 miles each: 2,600 × $0.21 | $546 |
| Tolls | $54 |
| Motel nights on the way | $500 |
| Meals on the way | $250 (not deductible) |
| Dislocation allowance | $3,000 (not income, not on line 4) |
| W-2 box 12 code P (government reimbursement of the truck rental) | $1,200 |

1. Certification box checked.
2. Line 1 = 2,400 + 300 = **$2,700**.
3. Line 2 = 546 + 54 + 500 = **$1,100** (meals left out).
4. Line 3 = **$3,800**.
5. Line 4 = **$1,200** (code P).
6. Line 3 > line 4: line 5 = 3,800 − 1,200 = **$2,600** → Schedule 1 line 14.

Variation: if code P had been $4,000, line 5 would be blank and $4,000 − $3,800 = **$200** would go on Form 1040 line 1h.
