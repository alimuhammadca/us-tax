## 1. At a glance

Form 4562 is the worksheet-and-report on which a business or rental owner claims the year's **depreciation**
(recovering the cost of property over several years), makes the **section 179 election** (deducting all or part of
the cost of qualifying business property in the year it is placed in service), claims the **special depreciation
allowance** ("bonus" depreciation), gives the required details for **listed property** (cars, other vehicles,
certain aircraft, entertainment / recreation property) and claims **amortization** that begins this year. One form
is filed for each business or activity; the section 179 limits are figured once for the taxpayer. Its line 22 total
goes to the depreciation line of the schedule the activity belongs to (Schedule C line 13, Schedule E line 18,
Schedule F line 14 …); line 44 (amortization) goes to that schedule's "other expenses" line.

```text
Part I   (section 179, one computation per taxpayer):
  line 5  = max(0, $2,500,000 − max(0, line 2 − $4,000,000))      [× your share if married filing separately]
  line 9  = min(line 5, line 8)          line 8 = Σ elected cost (line 6 col (c) + line 7 listed property)
  line 11 = min(line 5, business income ≥ 0)     (active trades or businesses + wages; no §179, no ½ SE tax, no NOL)
  line 12 = min(line 9 + line 10, line 11)       line 10 = 2024 Form 4562 line 13
  line 13 = line 9 + line 10 − line 12           → 2026 Form 4562 line 10
Part II  line 14 = special allowance on qualified non-listed property placed in service in 2025
         line 15 = property under a §168(f)(1) election (unit-of-production etc.);  line 16 = other (ACRS, pre-1981 …)
Part III line 17 = MACRS on property placed in service before 2025
         line 19 (GDS) / line 20 (ADS), column (g) = 2025 MACRS on property placed in service in 2025
Part IV  line 21 = line 28 (listed property)
         line 22 = line 12 + lines 14–17 + line 19 col (g) + line 20 col (g) + line 21   → the activity's schedule
Part V   line 28 = Σ column (h) lines 25–27 → line 21;   line 29 = Σ column (i) line 26 → line 7
Part VI  line 44 = line 42 col (f) + line 43           → "Other deductions / Other expenses" of the return
```

Terms used below:

- **Placed in service** — the date the property is ready and available for its business or income-producing use.
  Depreciation "starts when you first use the property in your business or for the production of income"
  (`i4562.pdf p. 2`).
- **Basis for depreciation** — cost (or other basis) × business/investment-use percentage, less the section 179
  deduction, the special allowance and certain credits (`i4562.pdf p. 11`, column (c)).
- **MACRS** — Modified Accelerated Cost Recovery System: GDS (General Depreciation System, the normal accelerated
  schedules) and ADS (Alternative Depreciation System, straight line over longer periods) (`i4562.pdf p. 9`).
- **Convention** — the rule that sets how much of the first (and last) year counts: half-year (HY), mid-quarter (MQ)
  or mid-month (MM) (`i4562.pdf p. 11`).
- **Listed property** — "Passenger automobiles weighing 6,000 pounds or less", "Any other property used for
  transportation if the nature of the property lends itself to personal use, such as motorcycles, pickup trucks,
  SUVs, aircraft, etc." and "Any property used for entertainment or recreational purposes" (`i4562.pdf p. 3`).
- **Qualified business use** — business use, not counting investment use and certain use by 5% owners / related
  persons (`i4562.pdf p. 14`). Listed property used 50% or less in a qualified business use gets no section 179 and
  no special allowance and must use ADS straight line.
- **Amortization** — recovering certain intangible or start-up costs ratably over a fixed period (`i4562.pdf p. 3`).

## 2. The IRS wording

**Form 4562 (2025)** (`f4562.pdf p. 1–3`): header "Business or activity to which this form relates"; Part I
"Election To Expense Certain Property Under Section 179" with "Note: If you have any listed property, complete Part V
before you complete Part I"; Part II "Special Depreciation Allowance and Other Depreciation (Don't include listed
property. See instructions.)"; Part III "MACRS Depreciation (Don't include listed property. See instructions.)";
Part IV "Summary"; Part V "Listed Property (Include automobiles, certain other vehicles, certain aircraft, and
property used for entertainment, recreation, or amusement.)"; Part VI "Amortization". Line 22: "Total. Add amounts
from line 12, lines 14 through 17, lines 19 and 20 in column (g), and line 21. Enter here and on the appropriate
lines of your return."

**Who must file** (`i4562.pdf p. 2`):

> "Except as otherwise noted, complete and file Form 4562 if you are claiming any of the following.
> • Depreciation for property placed in service during the 2025 tax year.
> • A section 179 expense deduction (which may include a carryover from a previous year).
> • Depreciation on any vehicle or other listed property (regardless of when it was placed in service).
> • A deduction for any vehicle reported on a form other than Schedule C (Form 1040), Profit or Loss From Business.
> • Any depreciation on a corporate income tax return (other than Form 1120-S).
> • Amortization of costs that begins during the 2025 tax year."
>
> "File a separate Form 4562 for each business or activity on your return for which Form 4562 is required. … However,
> complete only one Part I in its entirety when computing your section 179 expense deduction."

**What's New (2025)** (`i4562.pdf p. 1`):

> "For tax years beginning in 2025, the maximum section 179 expense deduction is $2,500,000. This limit is reduced by
> the amount by which the cost of section 179 property placed in service during the tax year exceeds $4,000,000.
> Also, the maximum section 179 expense deduction for sport utility vehicles (SUVs) placed in service in tax years
> beginning in 2025 is $31,300."
>
> "P.L. 119-21, commonly known as the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, reinstated the 100% special depreciation allowance
> for certain qualified property acquired and placed in service after January 19, 2025 … However, you can elect to
> take a 40% special depreciation allowance (60% for long production period property and certain aircraft) for this
> property during first tax year ending after January 19, 2025, instead of taking the 100% special depreciation
> allowance."
>
> "Certain qualified property (other than property with a long production period and certain aircraft) placed in
> service after December 31, 2024, and before January 1, 2026, is limited to a special allowance of 40% of the
> depreciable basis of the property." (the phase-down for property acquired before January 20, 2025)

Also new for 2025: lines 19h and 20e (50-year property), lines 23a / 23b, line 24c (aircraft), the section 168(n)
allowance for qualified production property, solar / wind property no longer 5-year, and section 174A domestic
research expenditures (`i4562.pdf p. 1`).

**Section 179 election — Part I** (`i4562.pdf p. 3`):

> "You can elect to expense part or all of the cost of section 179 property (defined earlier) that you placed in
> service during the tax year and used predominantly (more than 50%) in your trade or business."
>
> "Caution: If you elect to expense section 179 property, you must reduce the amount on which you figure your
> depreciation or amortization deduction (including any special depreciation allowance) by the section 179 expense
> deduction." (`p. 4`)

**Line 11 — business income limit** (`i4562.pdf p. 5`):

> "Individuals. Enter the smaller of line 5 or the total taxable income from any trade or business you actively
> conducted, computed without regard to any section 179 expense deduction, the deduction for one-half of
> self-employment taxes under section 164(f), or any net operating loss deduction. Also, include all wages, salaries,
> tips, and other compensation you earned as an employee (from Form 1040, line 1a). Do not reduce this amount by
> unreimbursed employee business expenses. If you are married filing a joint return, combine the total taxable
> incomes for you and your spouse."

**Line 14 — special allowance** (`i4562.pdf p. 5–7`):

> "The special depreciation allowance applies only for the first year the property is placed in service. The
> allowance is an additional deduction you can take after any section 179 expense deduction and before you figure
> regular depreciation under MACRS."
>
> "Qualified property is: • Tangible property depreciated under MACRS with a recovery period of 20 years or less;
> • Computer software defined in and depreciated under section 167(f)(1); • Water utility property … ; and
> • Qualified film, television, and live theatrical productions …" (`p. 6`)
>
> "Election out. You can elect, for any class of property, to not deduct any special depreciation allowance for all
> such property in such class placed in service during the tax year. To make an election, attach a statement to
> your timely filed return …" (`p. 7`)

**Part V — listed property** (`i4562.pdf p. 13`):

> "Listed property used 50% or less in a qualified business use … does not qualify for the section 179 expense
> deduction or special depreciation allowance."
>
> "if you file Schedule C (Form 1040) and are claiming the standard mileage rate or actual vehicle expenses (except
> depreciation), and you are not required to file Form 4562 for any other reason, report vehicle information in
> Part IV of Schedule C and not on Form 4562."

**Limits for passenger automobiles** (`i4562.pdf p. 15`):

> "The depreciation deduction, including the section 179 expense deduction and special depreciation allowance, for
> passenger automobiles is limited. … If the business/investment use percentage in column (c) for the automobile is
> less than 100%, you must reduce the applicable limit to an amount equal to the limit multiplied by that percentage.
> For example, for an automobile (including a truck or van) placed in service in 2025 (for which you elect not to
> claim any special depreciation allowance) that is used 60% for business/investment, the limit is $7,320 ($12,200 x
> 60% (0.60))."

**Line 44** (`i4562.pdf p. 19`): "Report the total amortization … on the applicable "Other Deductions" or "Other
Expenses" line of your return."

## 3. What goes on this form

- The **section 179 election** for 2025 property (Part I), including listed property used more than 50% for business
  (Part V line 26 column (i) → line 7), a share of a partnership's or S corporation's section 179 deduction ("enter
  "from Schedule K-1 (Form 1065)" or "from Schedule K-1 (Form 1120-S)" across columns (a) and (b)", `i4562.pdf p. 5`)
  and last year's disallowed carryover (line 10).
- The **special allowance** on qualified non-listed property placed in service in 2025 (line 14) and on qualified
  listed property used more than 50% (line 25).
- **MACRS depreciation**: prior-year property (line 17), 2025 property under GDS (line 19) or ADS (line 20), listed
  property (lines 26–27).
- Depreciation under non-MACRS methods: §168(f)(1) unit-of-production and similar (line 15); ACRS, pre-1981 property,
  income-forecast property, certain intangibles such as off-the-shelf software over 36 months (line 16)
  (`i4562.pdf p. 8–9`).
- The **listed-property information** (Part V Sections A–C), even for an old vehicle, unless the Schedule C Part IV
  exception applies.
- **Amortization**: costs whose amortization begins in 2025 (line 42), and earlier amortization when Form 4562 is
  filed anyway (line 43) (`i4562.pdf p. 17–19`).
- A home used for business for the first time in 2025: "enter the amounts from Form 8829, lines 40 and 42, in
  columns (c) and (g) of line 19j on Form 4562" (`i8829.pdf p. 6`).

## 6. How to figure it, step by step (every line of the 2025 form)

**Header.** Name, identifying number, and "Business or activity to which this form relates" — one form per business
or activity (`i4562.pdf p. 2`).

### Part I — Election To Expense Certain Property Under Section 179 (one computation for the taxpayer)

Complete Part V first if there is listed property (`f4562.pdf p. 1`). Section 179 property is property acquired by
purchase "for use in the active conduct of your trade or business" — tangible personal property, off-the-shelf
software, certain other tangible property, and (by election) qualified section 179 real property: qualified
improvement property and roofs, HVAC, fire-protection / alarm and security systems added to nonresidential real
property; it excludes buildings and their structural components, property held for investment and property used
mainly outside the U.S. (`i4562.pdf p. 2–3`). The asset must be used more than 50% in the business (`p. 3`).

1. **Line 1** — maximum amount: $2,500,000 (`i4562.pdf p. 4`). Worksheet 1 enters the smaller of total §179 cost and
   $2,500,000 (`p. 4`).
2. **Line 2** — total cost of all section 179 property placed in service in 2025 (not just the elected part),
   including listed property and "Any property placed in service by your spouse, even if you are filing a separate
   return" (`p. 4`).
3. **Line 3** — threshold $4,000,000 (`p. 4`).
4. **Line 4** — line 2 − line 3, not below zero.
5. **Line 5** — line 1 − line 4, not below zero. Married filing separately: "multiply the total limitation that you
   would otherwise enter on line 5 by 50% (0.50), unless you both elect a different allocation" (`p. 5`). If line 5
   is zero, skip lines 6–11, enter 0 on line 12 and the 2024 carryover on line 13.
6. **Line 6** — for each non-listed asset: (a) description, (b) cost (business use only; on a trade-in only the cost
   above the value traded in), (c) elected cost (`p. 5`). A heavy SUV (over 6,000 and not over 14,000 lb GVW) can
   have at most $31,300 elected (`p. 16`).
7. **Line 7** — listed property: line 29 (`p. 5`).
8. **Line 8** — add column (c) of lines 6 and 7. Worksheet 1 line 7 caps the total elected cost at the smaller of the
   total cost and line 5 (`p. 4`).
9. **Line 9** — tentative deduction: smaller of line 5 or line 8.
10. **Line 10** — carryover from line 13 of the 2024 Form 4562 (`p. 5`).
11. **Line 11** — business income limitation: smaller of line 5 or the taxable income from every trade or business
    actively conducted (meaningful participation; "A mere passive investor is not considered to actively conduct a
    trade or business"), figured without the §179 deduction, the ½ self-employment-tax deduction and any NOL
    deduction, plus wages from Form 1040 line 1a; both spouses combined on a joint return (`p. 5`). Not less than
    zero (`f4562.pdf p. 1`).
12. **Line 12** — section 179 expense deduction: lines 9 + 10, not more than line 11. "The limitations on lines 5 and
    11 apply to the taxpayer, and not to each separate business or activity" — with several activities, prepare one
    "Summary" Part I and put on line 12 of each activity's form only the amount allocated to it (`p. 5`).
13. **Line 13** — carryover to 2026: lines 9 + 10 − line 12. Only the business-income limit creates a carryover; an
    election above the dollar limit is not deductible.

### Part II — Special Depreciation Allowance and Other Depreciation (no listed property)

14. **Line 14** — special allowance on qualified property placed in service in 2025: depreciable basis (business part
    of cost less §179 and other basis reductions) × rate (`i4562.pdf p. 7`):
    - acquired after January 19, 2025: **100%**, or 40% (60% long-production / certain aircraft) by election for the
      first tax year ending after that date (`p. 6`);
    - acquired after September 27, 2017 and before January 20, 2025, placed in service in 2025: **40%** (60%
      long-production / certain aircraft) (`p. 6`);
    - specified plants (fruit / nut trees and vines): 100% / 40% by election, in the year planted or grafted (`p. 6`);
    - qualified reuse and recycling property: 50% (`p. 6–7`); qualified production property (nonresidential real
      property used in manufacturing, placed in service after July 4, 2025): up to 100% by election and designation
      (`p. 7–8`).
    Not qualified: listed property used 50% or less, property required to use ADS, property placed in service and
    disposed of in the same year, property converted to personal use in the year acquired, property for which the
    class was elected out (`p. 7`). Real property (27.5 / 39 years) is not qualified property (recovery period over 20
    years) unless it is qualified production property.
15. **Line 15** — property under a §168(f)(1) election (unit-of-production or another method not based on years);
    attach a statement (`p. 8`).
16. **Line 16** — other depreciation: ACRS (pre-1987), property placed in service before 1981, certain related-party
    / nonrecognition acquisitions, income-forecast property, and non-§197 intangibles such as off-the-shelf software
    (straight line over 36 months) (`p. 8–9`). "Prior years' depreciation, plus current year's depreciation, can never
    exceed the depreciable basis of the property." (`p. 9`)

### Part III — MACRS Depreciation (no listed property)

17. **Line 17** — MACRS for property placed in service in tax years beginning before 2025: figure each asset as for
    line 19 column (g), using its original method, convention and recovery period (`p. 9`).
18. **Line 18** — check the box to elect general asset accounts (irrevocable) (`p. 9`).
19. **Lines 19a–19j (Section B, GDS)** — property placed in service in 2025 (`p. 9–12`):
    - **(a) Classification**: 3-, 5- (autos, light trucks, computers, office machines, appliances / carpet / furniture
      in a rental, new farm machinery), 7- (office furniture, property with no class life), 10-, 15- (qualified
      improvement property), 20-, 25-year (water utility), residential rental 27.5, nonresidential real 39, 50-year
      (railroad grading / tunnel bores). Other property: by class life (4 or less → 3-year; >4 and <10 → 5-year; 10 to
      <16 → 7-year; 16 to <20 → 10-year; 20 to <25 → 15-year; 25 or more → 20-year) (`p. 10–11`).
    - **(b) Month and year placed in service** — required on lines 19i and 19j (`p. 11`).
    - **(c) Basis for depreciation** — cost × business/investment use %, less §179, the line 14 allowance and listed
      credits (`p. 11`).
    - **(d) Recovery period** — 3, 5, 7, 10, 15, 20, 25, 27.5, 39 or 50 years (`p. 11`).
    - **(e) Convention** — **HY** for lines 19a–19h unless mid-quarter applies; **MQ** when "the total depreciable bases
      (before any special depreciation allowance) of MACRS property placed in service during the last 3 months of
      your tax year exceed 40% of the total depreciable bases of MACRS property placed in service during the entire
      tax year" (real property and property placed in service and disposed of in the same year are left out of the
      test); **MM** for 27.5-, 39- and 50-year property (`p. 11`).
    - **(f) Method** — 200% DB switching to straight line for 3/5/7/10-year property; 150% DB for 15/20-year property
      and for farm property not covered by the post-2017 rule; straight line for 25-year, 27.5, 39 and 50-year
      property and for trees / vines. An irrevocable per-class election allows 150% DB (3/5/7/10-year) or straight line
      instead (`p. 11–12`).
    - **(g) Depreciation deduction** — column (c) × the table rate (optional Tables A–E, `p. 21–22`: Table A 200% DB HY,
      Table B 150% DB HY, Tables C–E straight line MM for 27.5 / 39 / 50-year). Or figure it: rate = 2.00 or 1.50 ÷
      recovery years (switching to straight line when that is larger; straight line = 1 ÷ remaining years); × the
      unrecovered basis; × the first-year fraction — HY 0.5; MQ 0.875 / 0.625 / 0.375 / 0.125 for quarters 1–4; MM
      0.9583 … 0.0417 for months 1–12 (`p. 12`). In the year of disposal use the "Disposed of" fractions (HY 0.5; MQ
      0.125 / 0.375 / 0.625 / 0.875; MM 0.0417 … 0.9583) (`p. 12`).
20. **Lines 20a–20e (Section C, ADS)** — 2025 property depreciated under ADS: required for property used mainly
    outside the U.S., tax-exempt use / bond-financed property, farm property after a §263A(d)(3) election, real
    property / QIP of an electing real property trade or business and 10-year-plus property of an electing farming
    business; elective otherwise (per class; per property for real property) (`p. 12`). 20a class life, 20b 12-year
    (no class life), 20c 30-year residential rental, 20d 40-year nonresidential real, 20e 50-year; straight line over
    the ADS period with the GDS conventions (`p. 13`).

### Part IV — Summary

21. **Line 21** — listed property: amount from line 28.
22. **Line 22** — total of line 12, lines 14 through 17, lines 19 and 20 column (g), and line 21; "Enter here and on
    the appropriate lines of your return" (`f4562.pdf p. 2`).
23. **Lines 23a / 23b** — for 2025 Part III assets with costs capitalized under §263A: basis attributable to interest
    capitalized under §263A(f) (23a) and other §263A costs (23b) (`i4562.pdf p. 13`).

### Part V — Listed Property

Use Part V for every listed asset, whatever year placed in service, if depreciation or actual vehicle expenses are
claimed (`i4562.pdf p. 13`). Compute the §179 deduction first; listed property used 50% or less gets neither §179 nor
the special allowance (`p. 13`).

24. **Line 24a / 24b** — evidence to support the business / investment use claimed? Is it written? **Line 24c** — own,
    lease or charter an aircraft (new for 2025) (`p. 13`).
25. **Line 25** — special allowance on qualified listed property placed in service in 2025 and used more than 50% in
    a qualified business use; included in the passenger-auto limit (`p. 13–14`).
26. **Line 26** — property used more than 50% in a qualified business use: (a) type (vehicles first), (b) date placed
    in service, (c) business/investment use % — miles for business ÷ total miles, commuting excluded, (d) cost or other
    basis, (e) basis for depreciation = (d) × (c) less §179, special allowance and certain credits, (f) recovery
    period (GDS table), (g) method / convention, (h) depreciation (as line 19 column (g)), (i) elected §179 cost
    (`p. 14–16`).
27. **Line 27** — property used 50% or less: straight line over the ADS period ("The ADS recovery period is 5 years
    for automobiles and computers"), column (h) = column (e) ÷ column (f) with the same conventions, never more than
    the unrecovered basis (`p. 15`).
    - **Passenger-automobile limits**: for each passenger automobile (≤ 6,000 lb; trucks / vans by gross vehicle
      weight) the total of columns (h) and (i) on lines 26 / 27 and column (h) on line 25 cannot exceed the limit for
      its year, × the business-use percentage (`p. 15`). For a car placed in service in 2025: $20,200 in year 1 with
      the special allowance ($12,200 without), $19,600 year 2, $11,800 year 3, $7,060 each later year; each earlier
      vintage keeps its own table (e.g. placed in 2024: $19,800 for 2025 as its year 2; placed 2018–2020: $5,760 for
      year 4 on) (`i4562.pdf p. 15–16` Tables 1–4; `p463.pdf p. 1`, `p. 28`). If the recovery period ended before
      2025, enter the unrecovered basis, still subject to the limit (`i4562.pdf p. 15`; `p463.pdf p. 30`).
    - **SUVs**: at most $31,300 of the cost of an SUV over 6,000 and not over 14,000 lb GVW can be elected under §179
      (exceptions: more than nine seats behind the driver, an open cargo area at least 6 feet long, certain cab-forward
      vans) (`p. 16`).
28. **Line 28** — add column (h), lines 25–27; also on line 21.
29. **Line 29** — add column (i), line 26; also on line 7.
30. **Section B, lines 30–36** — for each vehicle used by a sole proprietor, partner, more-than-5% owner or related
    person: (30) business / investment miles (no commuting), (31) commuting miles, (32) other personal miles, (33)
    total, (34) available for personal use off duty?, (35) used primarily by a more-than-5% owner or related person?,
    (36) another vehicle available for personal use? (`f4562.pdf p. 2`; `i4562.pdf p. 16`).
31. **Section C, lines 37–41** — employers providing vehicles to employees: written policy prohibiting all personal use
    (37), all but commuting (38), all use treated as personal (39), more than five vehicles with information retained
    (40), qualified demonstration use (41); a Yes means Section B is not completed for those vehicles (`f4562.pdf
    p. 3`; `i4562.pdf p. 16–17`).

### Part VI — Amortization

32. **Line 42** — costs whose amortization begins in 2025: (a) description, (b) date amortization begins, (c)
    amortizable amount, (d) Code section, (e) period or percentage, (f) this year's amortization = (c) ÷ months ×
    months in 2025, or (c) × (e) (`i4562.pdf p. 17–19`). Examples: §195 start-up and §248 / §709 organizational costs
    over 180 months (after the limited first-year deduction), §197 intangibles over 15 years (goodwill, franchise,
    covenant not to compete …), §178 lease acquisition costs over the lease term, §194 reforestation over 84 months,
    §169 pollution control facilities over 60 months, §167(h) geological and geophysical costs over 24 months, §174 /
    §174A research expenditures, §59(e) optional write-offs (`p. 17–19`).
33. **Line 43** — amortization of costs begun before 2025 (only when Form 4562 is filed anyway; research expenditures
    always go here, with a statement) (`p. 19`).
34. **Line 44** — total of column (f); report it on the "Other Deductions" / "Other Expenses" line of the return
    (`p. 19`).

## 8. Married filing jointly / separately

- **Joint return:** one section 179 computation for the couple — one $2,500,000 limit, one $4,000,000 threshold, and
  line 11 combines both spouses' business income and wages (`i4562.pdf p. 5`). Each business or activity still gets
  its own Form 4562.
- **Separate returns:** the dollar limit is split (50% each unless both elect otherwise), and line 2 counts the
  spouse's §179 property too (`i4562.pdf p. 4–5`). Each spouse reports only the depreciation of property used in
  their own businesses or activities.

## 11. Worked example

Sam Example (000-00-0001), single, runs one sole proprietorship, "Example Design Studio" (Schedule C). Tentative
profit before any 2025 depreciation or §179: $50,000. No W-2 wages. 2025 and earlier assets (all 100% business use
unless stated):

| Asset | Placed in service | Acquired | Cost | Choices |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Computer workstations (5-year, used only at the studio — not listed property) | 2025-03-10 | 2025-03-10 | $12,000 | §179 $12,000 |
| Office furniture (7-year) | 2025-06-15 | 2025-06-01 | $7,000 | Special allowance (not elected out) |
| Passenger car, 75% business use (5-year, listed) | 2025-08-01 | 2025-07-20 | $40,000 | Special allowance |
| Shelving bought in 2023 (7-year, 200% DB, HY, no §179 / bonus) | 2023-05-01 | 2023 | $10,000 | — |
| Franchise right (§197 intangible) | 2025-07-01 | 2025-07-01 | $15,000 | Amortized over 180 months |

Steps:

1. **Mid-quarter test** (2025 MACRS property, basis after §179): workstations $0, furniture $7,000 (June), car $30,000
   (August) — nothing in October–December, so the half-year convention applies.
2. **Part V first (the car).** Business use 75% > 50% → line 26. Column (d) $40,000; business part $30,000; special
   allowance 100% (acquired after January 19, 2025) = $30,000, so column (e) = $0. Passenger-auto limit for 2025 with
   the allowance: $20,200 × 75% = **$15,150**. Line 25 = $15,150; line 26 column (h) = $0; column (i) = $0. The
   $14,850 not allowed stays as unrecovered basis, recovered after the recovery period (`p463.pdf p. 30`).
   Line 28 = $15,150 → line 21; line 29 = $0 → line 7.
3. **Part I.** Line 1 $2,500,000; line 2 (total cost of §179 property: $12,000 + $7,000 + $40,000) $59,000; line 3
   $4,000,000; line 4 $0; line 5 $2,500,000; line 6 "Computer workstations", $12,000, $12,000; line 7 $0; line 8
   $12,000; line 9 $12,000; line 10 $0; line 11 = smaller of $2,500,000 or business income $50,000 = $50,000; line 12
   **$12,000**; line 13 $0.
4. **Line 14:** furniture $7,000 × 100% = **$7,000**.
5. **Line 17:** shelving, 2025 is recovery year 3: Table A 7-year year 3 = 17.49% × $10,000 = **$1,749**.
6. **Line 19:** nothing left to depreciate on 2025 property (workstations fully expensed, furniture fully allowed).
7. **Line 22** = $12,000 + $7,000 + $1,749 + $15,150 = **$35,899** → Schedule C line 13.
8. **Part VI.** Line 42: "Franchise", 07/2025, $15,000, §197, 15 years, $15,000 ÷ 180 × 6 = **$500**; line 44 $500 →
   Schedule C Part V "Other expenses" (line 27b).
9. **Business-income check after the fact:** $50,000 − $35,899 − $500 = $13,601 Schedule C net profit (before other
   expenses). AMT: all 2025 property is bonus-eligible and placed in service after 2015, and the shelving is 7-year
   property placed in service after 2015 → no Form 6251 line 2l adjustment (`i6251.pdf p. 5`).
