# Form 461 — Limitation on Business Losses (section 461(l) excess business loss)

Tax year 2025. What the IRS requires. How the app does it: `C:\us-tax\new_knowledge\forms\461.md`. Picture:
`C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\forms\461.md`. The line it feeds is documented in `C:\us-tax\new_lines\8.md` (Schedule 1
line 8p); the carryforward it creates in `forms\172.md`.

The form itself (`f461.pdf`, 2025, "Created 2/26/25") is in `C:\us-tax\docs\IRS-Forms\`, not in `docs\books\`; it is
cited for the printed line wording only. The rules come from the 2025 instructions (`i461.pdf`), the Form 172
instructions (`i172.pdf`) and the 2025 Form 1040 instructions.

## 1. At a glance

An individual cannot deduct an **excess business loss** in 2025: the amount by which the combined net loss of all
trades or businesses is more than $313,000 ($626,000 on a joint return). Form 461 adds up the business income and
losses already on the return, takes out amounts that are not from a trade or business, and compares the net with the
threshold. A shortfall (line 16 below zero) is added back as a **positive** amount on Schedule 1 line 8p, and the same
amount becomes a net operating loss (NOL) carried to 2026.

```text
line 9  = lines 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 6 + 8         (business items as reported on the return)
line 12 = line 10 (non-business income / gain) − line 11 (non-business loss / deduction, positive)
line 13 = − line 12
line 14 = line 9 + line 13                     (net of all trades or businesses)
line 16 = line 14 + 313,000 (626,000 MFJ)
excess business loss = − line 16 if line 16 < 0  -> Schedule 1 line 8p (positive), and an NOL carryover
```

Terms used below:

- **Trade or business** — an activity carried on "for income or profit" with "continuity and regularity"
  (`i461.pdf p. 1`). For a partnership or S corporation interest, the determination "is made at that entity's level".
- **Excess business loss** — "the amount by which the total deductions (computed without regard to any deduction
  allowed under section 172 or 199A) from your trades or businesses are more than your total gross income or gains
  from your trades or businesses, plus the threshold amount" (`i461.pdf p. 1`).
- **NOL (net operating loss)** — see `forms\172.md`.

## 2. The IRS wording

**Purpose of form** (`i461.pdf p. 1`): "Code section 461(l) limits the amount of losses from the trades or businesses
of noncorporate taxpayers that the taxpayers can claim each year. Taxpayers can't deduct an excess business loss …
in the current year. However, the excess business loss is treated as a net operating loss (NOL) carryover for
subsequent years." What's New: the One Big Beautiful Bill Act "permanently extended the disallowance".

**Who must file** (`i461.pdf p. 1`): "File Form 461 if you're a noncorporate taxpayer … and either (i) your net losses
from all of your trades or businesses are more than $313,000 ($626,000 for taxpayers filing a joint return), or (ii)
you would report a loss of more than $156,500 on any one of Form 461, lines 1 through 8. … Attach Form 461 to the
applicable tax return you file."

**Employee services excluded** (`i461.pdf p. 1`): excess losses "should be determined without regard to any
deductions, gross income, or gains attributable to any trade or business of performing services of an employee."

**Capital gains and losses** (`i461.pdf p. 1`): "Losses from sales or exchanges of capital assets are not included in
the calculation of the total deductions from your trades or businesses. Gains from the sales or exchanges of capital
assets should not exceed the lesser of: 1. Capital gain net income limited to only gains and losses attributable to a
trade or business, or 2. Capital gain net income."

**Ordering** (`i461.pdf p. 1`): "First, apply the at-risk rules; next, apply the passive activity loss rules; and then
apply the excess business loss rules."

**Line 16** (`i461.pdf p. 3`): "If the resulting figure on this line is a negative amount, then it's your excess
business loss. Although it's a loss, you will report the excess business loss adjustment as a positive number on the
"Other income" line on your tax return and enter "ELA" on the dotted line. For Schedule 1 (Form 1040), enter any
excess business loss adjustment on line 8p." "You'll need to keep a record of your excess business loss from each tax
year because it's treated as an NOL carryover for subsequent taxable years."

**Schedule 1 line 8p** (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 92`): "461(l) excess business loss adjustment. Enter the amount of
your excess business loss from Form 461, line 16."

## 3. What goes on this form

Every business item already reported on the return (Part I), the non-business part of those items (Part II), and
the threshold (Part III):

- Schedule C net profit or loss (Schedule 1 line 3).
- Capital gains and losses on Form 1040 line 7a — then the non-business gains and **all** capital losses are taken
  back out in Part II, so only trade-or-business capital gain (limited) remains (`i461.pdf p. 2`).
- Form 4797 other gains or losses (Schedule 1 line 4).
- Schedule E: rental real estate, royalties, partnerships, S corporations, estates, trusts (Schedule 1 line 5) — the
  non-business part (for example portfolio royalties, a rental that is not a trade or business) taken out in Part II.
- Schedule F farm income or loss (Schedule 1 line 6).
- Any other trade-or-business income, gain or loss on the return (line 8).
- Losses or deductions disallowed in earlier years under other Code sections and allowed this year (for example
  released suspended passive losses) — "included in figuring the amount, if any, of your excess business loss in the
  current year" (`i461.pdf p. 2`, Transition Rules).
- Business gains and losses on Schedule D and Form 4797 (`i172.pdf p. 4`: "Business gains and losses reported on
  Schedule D and Form 4797 are included in the excess business loss calculation").

## 4. What does not go here

| Look-alike amount | Treatment | Source |
|---|---|---|
| W-2 wages and employee business expenses | Never on Form 461 (services as an employee) | `i461.pdf p. 1` |
| Capital losses (business or not) | Removed on line 11 | `i461.pdf p. 1–2` |
| Investment capital gains, interest, dividends, portfolio royalties | Not business; investment capital gains included on line 3 are removed on line 10 | `i461.pdf p. 2` |
| The NOL deduction (Schedule 1 line 8a) and the QBI deduction | Ignored ("computed without regard to any deduction allowed under section 172 or 199A") | `i461.pdf p. 1` |
| Losses still suspended by the at-risk or passive rules | Not counted this year (those rules apply first) | `i461.pdf p. 1` |
| Adjustments to income (½ SE tax, SE health insurance, SE retirement) | Not Form 461 lines (not on Schedule 1 lines 3–8) | `f461.pdf` Part I |

## 6. How to figure it, step by step (every line of the 2025 form)

Apply the at-risk rules (Form 6198) and passive activity rules (Form 8582) first (`i461.pdf p. 1`). On a joint return
"Complete one Form 461 containing all the information for both spouses" (`i461.pdf p. 2`).

**Part I — Total Income/Loss Items**

1. **Line 1** — "Reserved for future use"; leave blank.
2. **Line 2** — "Enter amount from Schedule 1 (Form 1040), line 3" (business income or loss).
3. **Line 3** — "Enter amount from Form 1040 or 1040-SR, line 7a." Capital losses included here are added back on
   line 11; capital gains not from a trade or business are added back on line 10 (`i461.pdf p. 2`).
4. **Line 4** — Schedule 1 line 4 (other gains or losses, Form 4797).
5. **Line 5** — Schedule 1 line 5 (Schedule E supplemental income or loss).
6. **Line 6** — Schedule 1 line 6 (farm income or loss).
7. **Line 7** — "Reserved for future use"; leave blank.
8. **Line 8** — "other income, gain, or losses from a trade or business not reported on lines 1 through 7".
9. **Line 9** — combine lines 1 through 8; may be positive or negative.

**Part II — Adjustment for Amounts Not Attributable to Trade or Business**

10. **Line 10** — income or gain on lines 1–8 that is **not** from a trade or business (for example investment capital
    gains on line 3, portfolio royalties on line 5).
11. **Line 11** — losses or deductions on lines 1–8 that are not from a trade or business, **as a positive number**,
    plus every capital loss included on line 3 (`i461.pdf p. 2`).
12. **Line 12** — line 10 minus line 11 ("your gain or loss that's not from a trade or business").

**Part III — Limitation on Losses**

13. **Line 13** — line 12 with its sign reversed ("If line 12 is a negative number, enter it here as a positive
    number. If line 12 is a positive number, enter it here as a negative number").
14. **Line 14** — line 9 + line 13 (net of all trades or businesses).
15. **Line 15** — "$313,000 (or $626,000 if married filing jointly)".
16. **Line 16** — line 14 + line 15. "If less than zero, enter the amount from line 16 as a positive number on
    Schedule 1 (Form 1040), line 8p", with "ELA" on the dotted line (`i461.pdf p. 3`).

Keep a record of the line 16 amount: it is an NOL carryover to 2026 (`i461.pdf p. 3`; `i172.pdf p. 4`).

## 7. Special cases and elections

| Case | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Partnership / S corporation interests | Limitation at the partner / shareholder level; trade-or-business determination at the entity level (not by the partner's participation) | `i461.pdf p. 1`; `i172.pdf p. 4` |
| Farming losses | Apply the excess business loss limitation before carrying any NOL back 2 years; with farming and nonfarming losses over the threshold, allocate the threshold to farming losses first | `i461.pdf p. 1` |
| Prior-year limited losses (transition rule) | Included in this year's computation when allowed | `i461.pdf p. 2` |
| Amended return | Attach Form 461 to it | `i461.pdf p. 2` |
| Excess business loss carried forward | Treated as an NOL; next year it can offset only 80% of taxable income | `i172.pdf p. 4` |

## 8. Married filing jointly / separately

- **Joint return:** one Form 461 for both spouses; threshold $626,000 (`i461.pdf p. 1–2`; `f461.pdf` line 15).
- **Separate returns (and single, head of household, qualifying surviving spouse):** threshold $313,000 — the form
  doubles it only for "married filing jointly" (`f461.pdf` line 15).

## 10. Where the amount goes next

- **Schedule 1 line 8p** (positive) → Schedule 1 line 9 → line 10 → Form 1040 line 8 → line 9 → AGI.
- **NOL carryover to 2026** — added to any Form 172 NOL on the "NOL Carryover with an Excess Business Loss Worksheet"
  (line 3 "Enter the total excess business loss from Form 461"), then deducted in 2026 on Schedule 1 line 8a subject to
  the 80% limit (`i172.pdf p. 4`; `forms\172.md`).

## 11. Worked example

Robin Example (000-00-0004), single. 2025 return: Schedule C loss −$450,000; a materially participating partnership
K-1 ordinary income $20,000 (on Schedule E); a $30,000 gain on stock held as an investment (line 7a); wages $90,000.

| Line | Figure | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Schedule 1 line 3 | −450,000 |
| 3 | Form 1040 line 7a | 30,000 |
| 4, 6, 8 | — | 0 |
| 5 | Schedule 1 line 5 (K-1) | 20,000 |
| 9 | total | −400,000 |
| 10 | stock gain is not business | 30,000 |
| 11 | — | 0 |
| 12 | 30,000 − 0 | 30,000 |
| 13 | sign reversed | −30,000 |
| 14 | −400,000 − 30,000 | −430,000 |
| 15 | single | 313,000 |
| 16 | −430,000 + 313,000 | **−117,000** |

Schedule 1 line 8p = **$117,000** ("ELA"); the wages never enter Form 461. Robin's 2025 AGI includes the add-back, and
$117,000 (plus any Form 172 NOL) carries to 2026 as an NOL.
