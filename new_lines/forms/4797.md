## 1. At a glance

Form 4797 reports the sale, exchange or other disposition of **property used in a trade or business** (and of
business-type rental property): buildings, equipment, vehicles, land used in the business, oil and gas property,
business livestock. It does three jobs:

1. **Depreciation recapture (Part III).** Gain on depreciated property is ordinary income up to the depreciation
   taken (section 1245 property — equipment, vehicles) or up to the depreciation above straight line (section 1250
   property — buildings). The recapture goes to Part II line 13.
2. **Section 1231 netting (Part I).** The rest of the gain on business property held more than one year, and the
   losses on such property, are netted on line 7. A **net gain** is a long-term capital gain (Schedule D line 11),
   except that it is ordinary income to the extent of **nonrecaptured section 1231 losses** of the 5 preceding years
   (lines 8–9, the "5-year lookback"). A **net loss** is an ordinary loss.
3. **Ordinary gains and losses (Part II).** Property held one year or less, the recapture, the lookback amount and a
   net section 1231 loss are combined on line 17 and reported on **Schedule 1 line 4** (line 18b).

Part IV figures the recapture of a section 179 deduction or of listed-property depreciation when business use drops
to 50% or less; that amount goes back to the schedule where the deduction was taken.

```text
Part III (per property, held > 1 year, sold at a gain)
    23 = 21 − 22                      (adjusted basis)
    24 = 20 − 23                      (total gain)
    25b = min(24, 25a)                (section 1245: 25a = depreciation from line 22)
    26g = 26b + 26e + 26f             (section 1250: 26b = % × min(24, 26a additional depreciation))
    27c, 28b, 29b                     (sections 1252, 1254, 1255)
    30 = Σ line 24;  31 = Σ (25b, 26g, 27c, 28b, 29b) → line 13;  32 = 30 − 31 → line 6 (casualty part → Form 4684 line 33)
Part I (section 1231)
    7 = 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 6
    7 ≤ 0              → line 11 (ordinary loss)
    7 > 0, no prior losses → Schedule D line 11 (long-term capital gain)
    8 = nonrecaptured net section 1231 losses of 2020–2024
    9 = max(0, 7 − 8)  → Schedule D line 11;   12 = 9 > 0 ? 8 : 7
Part II (ordinary)
    17 = 10 + 11 + 12 + 13 + 14 + 15 + 16
    18b = 17 − 18a     → Schedule 1 line 4
Part IV
    35 = 33 − 34       → "other income" on the schedule that took the deduction
```

Terms used below:

- **Section 1231 property** — real or depreciable property used in a trade or business and held more than one year
  (plus timber, coal, iron ore, certain livestock, unharvested crops, and involuntary conversions of business or
  investment property) (`i4797.pdf p. 6`).
- **Section 1245 property** — depreciable (or amortizable) personal property and some special real property: equipment,
  vehicles, furniture, single-purpose agricultural structures, storage facilities, real property to the extent of a
  section 179 deduction (`i4797.pdf p. 10`).
- **Section 1250 property** — "depreciable real property (other than section 1245 property)" — buildings and their
  structural components (`i4797.pdf p. 10`).
- **Additional depreciation** — "the excess of actual depreciation (including any special depreciation allowance, or
  commercial revitalization deduction) over depreciation figured using the straight line method" (`i4797.pdf p. 11`).
- **Unrecaptured section 1250 gain** — the part of a building's long-term gain that equals straight-line depreciation;
  not ordinary, but taxed at up to 25% through the Schedule D worksheet (`i1040sd.pdf p. 11–13`).
- **Nonrecaptured section 1231 losses** — "your net section 1231 losses deducted during the 5 preceding tax years that
  have not yet been applied against any net section 1231 gain" (`i4797.pdf p. 6`).

## 2. The IRS wording

Form header (`f4797.pdf p. 1`): "Sales of Business Property (Also Involuntary Conversions and Recapture Amounts Under
Sections 179 and 280F(b)(2)). Attach to your tax return."

**Purpose** (`i4797.pdf p. 1–2`): "Use Form 4797 to report the following. • The sale or exchange of: 1. Real property
used in your trade or business; 2. Depreciable and amortizable tangible property used in your trade or business …;
3. Oil, gas, geothermal, or other mineral properties; and 4. Certain cost-sharing payment property under section 126.
• The involuntary conversion (from other than casualty or theft) of property used in your trade or business and
capital assets held for more than 1 year in connection with a trade or business or a transaction entered into for
profit … • The disposition of noncapital assets (other than inventory or property held primarily for sale to customers
…). • The disposition of capital assets not reported on Schedule D. • The gain or loss (including any related
recapture) for partners and S corporation shareholders from certain section 179 property dispositions by partnerships
and S corporations. • The computation of recapture amounts under sections 179 and 280F(b)(2) when the business use of
section 179 or listed property decreases to 50% or less. • Gains or losses treated as ordinary gains or losses, if you
are a trader in securities or commodities and made a mark-to-market election under section 475(f). • Election to defer
a qualified section 1231 gain … invested in a qualified opportunity fund (QOF)."

**Where to make the first entry** (chart, `i4797.pdf p. 1`): depreciable tangible business property and depreciable
real business property — held one year or less: Part II; held more than one year: sold at a gain → **Part III
(1245 / 1250)**, sold at a loss → **Part I**. Farmland with soil / water expenses held under 10 years at a gain → Part
III (1252). De minimis safe-harbor property → Part II. Other farmland → Part I (more than one year). Section 126
property at a gain → Part III (1255). Cattle and horses: "Held less than 24 months" → Part II, "Held 24 months or more"
→ Part III (gain) / Part I (loss); raised cattle and horses at a gain → Part I. Other livestock: 12 months.

**Line 7** (`f4797.pdf p. 1`): "Combine lines 2 through 6 … Individuals … If line 7 is zero or a loss, enter the amount
from line 7 on line 11 below and skip lines 8 and 9. If line 7 is a gain and you didn't have any prior year section 1231
losses, or they were recaptured in an earlier year, enter the gain from line 7 as a long-term capital gain on the
Schedule D filed with your return and skip lines 8, 9, 11, and 12 below."

**Line 9** (`f4797.pdf p. 1`): "Subtract line 8 from line 7. If zero or less, enter -0-. If line 9 is zero, enter the
gain from line 7 on line 12 below. If line 9 is more than zero, enter the amount from line 8 on line 12 below and
enter the gain from line 9 as a long-term capital gain on the Schedule D filed with your return."

**Line 18b** (`f4797.pdf p. 1`): "Redetermine the gain or (loss) on line 17 excluding the loss, if any, on line 18a.
Enter here and on Schedule 1 (Form 1040), Part I, line 4."

**Schedule 1 line 4** (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 89`): "If you sold or exchanged assets used in a trade or business, and
are filing Form 4797, include the amount from Form 4797, line 18b, on line 4 and check the '4797' box."

**Schedule D** (`i1040sd.pdf p. 1`): "Use Schedule D: … To report a gain from Form 2439 or 6252 or Part I of Form 4797".

## 6. How to figure it, line by line

### Top of the form (`f4797.pdf p. 1`; `i4797.pdf p. 5–6`)

1. **Line 1a** — "Enter the gross proceeds from sales or exchanges reported to you for 2025 on Form(s) 1099-B or 1099-S
   (or substitute statement) that you are including on line 2, 10, or 20." Only 1099-S real estate and a trader's
   1099-B (`i4797.pdf p. 5–6`).
2. **Line 1b / 1c** — the gain / loss included on lines 2, 10 and 24 (1b) or 2 and 10 (1c) "due to the partial
   dispositions of MACRS assets".

### Part I — Sales or Exchanges of Property Used in a Trade or Business … Most Property Held More Than 1 Year

3. **Line 2** — one row per property: (a) description, (b) date acquired ("INHERITED" for property acquired from a
   decedent, `i4797.pdf p. 5`), (c) date sold, (d) gross sales price, (e) "Depreciation allowed or allowable since
   acquisition", (f) "Cost or other basis, plus improvements and expense of sale", (g) "Gain or (loss) Subtract (f) from
   the sum of (d) and (e)". Here go section 1231 transactions "that are not required to be reported in Part III"
   (`i4797.pdf p. 6`): losses on depreciable property, land, and the special entries (QOF deferral, DC Zone exclusion,
   section 121 exclusion as a loss).
   Holding period: "begin counting on the day after you received the property and include the day you disposed of it"
   (`i4797.pdf p. 6`).
4. **Line 3** — "Gain, if any, from Form 4684, line 39".
5. **Line 4** — "Section 1231 gain from installment sales from Form 6252, line 26 or 37".
6. **Line 5** — "Section 1231 gain or (loss) from like-kind exchanges from Form 8824".
7. **Line 6** — "Gain, if any, from line 32, from other than casualty or theft".
8. **Line 7** — combine lines 2 through 6. Partners' and S shareholders' K-1 amounts also enter Part I: "enter any
   amounts from your Schedule K-1 (Form 1120-S), box 9, or Schedule K-1 (Form 1065), box 10, in Part I of Form 4797"
   (`i4797.pdf p. 6`). Zero or a loss → line 11; a gain with no unrecaptured prior losses → Schedule D (long-term).
9. **Line 8** — "Nonrecaptured net section 1231 losses from prior years". "You had a net section 1231 loss if section
   1231 losses exceeded section 1231 gains. Gains are included only to the extent taken into account in figuring gross
   income. Losses are included only to the extent taken into account in figuring taxable income except that the
   limitation on capital losses does not apply. Your net section 1231 gain on line 7 is treated as ordinary income to
   the extent of your nonrecaptured section 1231 losses." The IRS example (`i4797.pdf p. 6–7`): losses of $4,000
   (2020) and $6,000 (2021), gains of $3,000 (2024) and $2,000 (2025): line 8 = $10,000 − $3,000 = **$7,000**, and "The
   entire $2,000 net section 1231 gain on line 7 is treated as ordinary income and is entered on line 12"; the losses
   are used oldest first ("the $4,000 loss from 2020 is all recaptured ($3,000 in 2024 and $1,000 in 2025)").
10. **Line 9** — line 7 − line 8, not below zero. "If line 9 is zero, enter the gain from line 7 on line 12 below. If
    line 9 is more than zero, enter the amount from line 8 on line 12 below and enter the gain from line 9 as a
    long-term capital gain on the Schedule D". "If line 9 is more than zero, you have recaptured all of your net section
    1231 losses from prior years" (`i4797.pdf p. 7`).

### Part II — Ordinary Gains and Losses

11. **Line 10** — "Ordinary gains and losses not included on lines 11 through 16 (include property held 1 year or
    less)": business property held one year or less, de minimis safe-harbor property, abandonments, section 1244 and
    1242 stock losses, a trader's mark-to-market gains and losses, section 451(k) deferred gain (`i4797.pdf p. 8–9`).
12. **Line 11** — "Loss, if any, from line 7".
13. **Line 12** — "Gain, if any, from line 7 or amount from line 8, if applicable".
14. **Line 13** — "Gain, if any, from line 31".
15. **Line 14** — "Net gain or (loss) from Form 4684, lines 31 and 38a".
16. **Line 15** — "Ordinary gain from installment sales from Form 6252, line 25 or 36".
17. **Line 16** — "Ordinary gain or (loss) from like-kind exchanges from Form 8824".
18. **Line 17** — combine lines 10 through 16.
19. **Line 18a** — the part of a line 11 loss that comes from Form 4684 line 35 column (b)(ii) (income-producing
    property): "Enter the loss from income-producing property on Schedule A (Form 1040), line 16." Required "if there
    is a gain on Form 4797, line 3; a loss on Form 4797, line 11; and a loss on Form 4684, line 35, column (b)(ii)"
    (`i4797.pdf p. 9`).
20. **Line 18b** — line 17 without the line 18a loss → **Schedule 1 line 4**.

### Part III — Gain From Disposition of Property Under Sections 1245, 1250, 1252, 1254, and 1255

"Generally, for property held 1 year or less, do not complete Part III; instead, use Part II." "Use Part III to figure
recapture of depreciation and other items that must be reported as ordinary income on the disposition of certain
property. Complete lines 19 through 24 to determine the gain … If you have more than four properties to report, use
additional forms." "If the property was sold on the installment sale basis, see the instructions for Form 6252 before
completing Part III." (`i4797.pdf p. 9`). Building and land sold together: allocate by fair market value; "report the
sale of a building in Part III and the land in Part I" (`i4797.pdf p. 2`).

21. **Line 19** — (a) description, (b) date acquired, (c) date sold, for properties A–D.
22. **Line 20** — gross sales price: "money, the FMV of other property received, and any existing mortgage or other debt
    the buyer assumes or takes the property subject to" (`i4797.pdf p. 9`).
23. **Line 21** — "Cost or other basis plus expense of sale", reduced by any enhanced oil recovery or disabled access
    credit, not by the line 22 items (`i4797.pdf p. 9`).
24. **Line 22** — "Depreciation (or depletion) allowed or allowable": Step 1 add depreciation (including any special
    allowance), amortization, depletion, preproductive expenses, section 179, commercial revitalization, section 50(c)
    basis reduction, clean-fuel, section 190 / 193 / 1253(d), vehicle and childcare credit basis reductions, section
    179D, advanced manufacturing credit adjustments; Step 2 subtract investment-credit recapture, "Any section 179 or
    280F(b)(2) recapture amount included in gross income in a prior tax year", and the other listed recaptures
    (`i4797.pdf p. 9–10`).
25. **Line 23** — line 21 − line 22.
26. **Line 24** — "Total gain. Subtract line 23 from line 20".
27. **Line 25 (section 1245 property)** — 25a "Depreciation allowed or allowable from line 22"; 25b "Enter the smaller
    of line 24 or 25a". Exceptions: gifts, transfers at death, certain tax-free transactions, like-kind exchanges,
    partnership distributions, timber, section 197 intangibles (`i4797.pdf p. 10`).
28. **Line 26 (section 1250 property)** — "If straight line depreciation was used, enter -0- on line 26g, except for a
    corporation subject to section 291." (`f4797.pdf p. 2`). "Generally, section 1250 recapture applies if you used an
    accelerated depreciation method or you claimed any special depreciation allowance, or the commercial
    revitalization deduction." MACRS residential rental (27.5-year) and nonresidential (39-year) real property placed
    in service after 1986: "You are not required to calculate additional depreciation" (`i4797.pdf p. 10`).
    - 26a additional depreciation after 1975 (`i4797.pdf p. 11`);
    - 26b "Applicable percentage multiplied by the smaller of line 24 or line 26a" — "Generally, use 100%" (low-income
      housing: section 1250(a)(1)(B));
    - 26c line 24 − 26a ("If residential rental property or line 24 isn't more than line 26a, skip lines 26d and 26e");
    - 26d additional depreciation 1970–1975; 26e smaller of 26c or 26d;
    - 26f section 291 amount (corporations only);
    - 26g = 26b + 26e + 26f.
29. **Line 27 (section 1252 farmland, held more than 1 year but less than 10 years)** — 27a soil, water and
    land-clearing expenses; 27b × 100% (80% in the 6th year, 60% 7th, 40% 8th, 20% 9th; skip from the 10th year);
    27c smaller of 24 or 27b (`i4797.pdf p. 11`).
30. **Line 28 (section 1254 oil, gas, geothermal, mineral property)** — 28a intangible drilling and development costs,
    mine development / exploration costs, depletion; 28b smaller of 24 or 28a (`i4797.pdf p. 11–12`).
31. **Line 29 (section 1255 cost-sharing property)** — 29a applicable percentage of excluded payments (100% under 10
    years, less 10% per year after, 0 at 20 years); 29b smaller of line 24 (less other recapture) or 29a
    (`i4797.pdf p. 12`).
32. **Line 30** — total gains (line 24) of all properties.
33. **Line 31** — "Add property columns A through D, lines 25b, 26g, 27c, 28b, and 29b. Enter here and on line 13".
34. **Line 32** — "Subtract line 31 from line 30. Enter the portion from casualty or theft on Form 4684, line 33. Enter
    the portion from other than casualty or theft on Form 4797, line 6." For an installment sale, "Don't enter any gain
    for this property on line 32" and, if Form 4797 was used only for line 12 of Form 6252, "enter 'N/A' on line 32"
    (`f6252--2025.pdf p. 3`).

### Part IV — Recapture Amounts Under Sections 179 and 280F(b)(2) When Business Use Drops to 50% or Less

35. **Column (a)** — section 179 property placed in service after 1986 (not listed property) whose business use fell to
    50% or less this year; **column (b)** — listed property placed in service in a prior year whose business use fell
    to 50% or less (`i4797.pdf p. 12`). Separate statement when more than one property.
36. **Line 33** — (a) the section 179 deduction claimed; (b) the depreciation allowable in prior years plus any section
    179.
37. **Line 34** — (a) the depreciation that would have been allowable on the section 179 amount from the year placed in
    service through the current year; (b) the depreciation that would have been allowable had the property not been
    used more than 50% in a qualified business, up to (not including) the current year (Pub. 463, Pub. 946).
38. **Line 35** — line 33 − line 34: "enter the recapture amount as 'other income' on the same form or schedule on
    which you took the deduction. For example, if you took the deduction on Schedule C … report the recapture amount
    as other income on Schedule C". A Schedule C / F recapture "is subject to self-employment tax". "Be sure to
    increase your basis in the property by the recapture amount." (`i4797.pdf p. 12`).

### Unrecaptured section 1250 gain (Schedule D line 19 worksheet, `i1040sd.pdf p. 13`)

"If you aren't reporting a gain on Form 4797, line 7, skip lines 1 through 9 and go to line 10." Line 1: for each section
1250 property in Part III with an entry in Part I (not on Form 6252), the smaller of Form 4797 line 22 or line 24; line
2: its line 26g; line 3: line 1 − line 2 (summed over properties, `i1040sd.pdf p. 12`); line 4: Form 6252 amounts; line
5: K-1 amounts; line 7: "the smaller of line 6 or the gain from Form 4797, line 7"; line 8: Form 4797 line 8; line 9:
line 7 − line 8. Details in `schedule-d.md`.

| Section 197(f)(9)(B)(ii) election | Extra tax on Form 1040 line 16 box 3 "197" | `i4797.pdf p. 4` |
| QOF deferral of section 1231 gain | Report the sale normally; on line 2 "QOF investment to Form 8949" as a negative column (g); only the gain above section 1245 / 1250 recapture can be deferred; Form 8997 | `i4797.pdf p. 4` |

## 8. Married filing jointly / separately

Form 4797 is one form per return ("Name(s) shown on return", `f4797.pdf p. 1`). On a joint return the dispositions of
both spouses are reported together and **netted together**: Part I line 7 combines all of the return's section 1231
gains and losses, and line 8 is the return's own nonrecaptured losses. The net section 1231 rules are applied to the
taxpayer's return as a whole ("Your net section 1231 gain on line 7 …", `i4797.pdf p. 6`). The instructions do not say
how to split prior joint losses between spouses who later file separately; that is outside the instructions
(unverified — not covered by any document in `docs\books`).

Limits per return: section 1244 ordinary loss $50,000, or "$100,000 if married filing jointly" (`i4797.pdf p. 8`); a
capital loss from investment depreciable property: $3,000, "$1,500 if you are a married individual filing a separate
return" (`i4797.pdf p. 2`).

## 11. Worked example

**Dana Sample** (000-00-0031), single, Schedule C contractor with a rental building. 2025 dispositions:

| Property | Acquired | Sold | Price | Cost + expense of sale | Depreciation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Excavator (section 1245) | 03/01/2021 | 06/30/2025 | 20,000 | 50,000 | 38,000 |
| B. Rental building (section 1250, MACRS straight line) | 05/01/2010 | 09/15/2025 | 400,000 | 320,000 | 100,000 |
| Business lot (land) | 04/01/2018 | 09/15/2025 | 30,000 | 40,000 | 0 |

Dana's 2022 return had a **net section 1231 loss of 15,000**; no section 1231 gain since.

1. **Part III, A:** line 23 = 50,000 − 38,000 = 12,000; line 24 = 20,000 − 12,000 = 8,000; 25a = 38,000; **25b = 8,000**.
2. **Part III, B:** line 23 = 320,000 − 100,000 = 220,000; line 24 = 180,000; straight line → 26a = 0, **26g = 0**.
3. **Line 30** = 188,000; **line 31** = 8,000 → line 13; **line 32** = 180,000 → line 6.
4. **Part I:** line 2 (lot) = 30,000 + 0 − 40,000 = (10,000); line 6 = 180,000; **line 7 = 170,000**.
5. **Line 8** = 15,000 (2022 loss, none recaptured yet); **line 9** = 155,000 → Schedule D line 11 (long-term).
6. **Part II:** line 12 = 15,000 (line 8, since line 9 > 0); line 13 = 8,000; **line 17 = 23,000 = line 18b** →
   Schedule 1 line 4.
7. **Unrecaptured section 1250 gain:** worksheet line 1 = min(100,000, 180,000) = 100,000; line 2 = 0; line 3 =
   100,000; line 6 = 100,000; line 7 = min(100,000, 170,000) = 100,000; line 8 = 15,000; **line 9 = 85,000** → Schedule
   D line 19 (taxed at up to 25%).

Income effect: 23,000 ordinary; 155,000 long-term capital gain, of which 85,000 is unrecaptured section 1250 gain.

**Married filing jointly — netting together.** Sam and Alex Sample (000-00-0032 / 000-00-0033) file jointly. Sam sells
business equipment held 3 years at a section 1231 gain (after recapture) of 10,000; Alex sells a business trailer held 2
years at a 10,000 loss. One Form 4797: line 6 = 10,000, line 2 = (10,000), **line 7 = 0** → line 11 = 0; nothing on
Schedule D and no ordinary loss.
