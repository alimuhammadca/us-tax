# Form 4835 — Farm Rental Income and Expenses

Tax year 2025. What the IRS requires. How the app does it: `C:\us-tax\new_knowledge\forms\4835.md`. Picture:
`C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\forms\4835.md`.

The form and its instructions are one document: `f4835.pdf` (3 pages, "Form 4835 (2025) Created 1/7/26"; page 1 is
the form, pages 2–3 the instructions). Form 4835 sends the reader to the Schedule F instructions for most lines
(`i1040sf.pdf`), to the Form 8582 and Form 6198 instructions for losses, and to Pub. 225 (Farmer's Tax Guide). Pub. 225
is **not** in `docs\books\`; where the instructions send the reader there, this file says so and stops.

---

## 1. At a glance

Form 4835 is filed by a **landowner (or sub-lessor) who rents farmland for a share of the tenant's crops or livestock
and does not materially participate** in running the farm. It reports the landlord's share of production income
(Part I) less the landlord's farm expenses (Part II). The result is **not subject to self-employment tax** (the form's
own subtitle: "Income Not Subject to Self-Employment Tax"). It is a **rental activity** under the passive activity
loss rules, so a loss goes first through the at-risk rules (Form 6198) and then the passive-loss rules (Form 8582)
before it reaches Schedule E.

```text
line 7  = 1 + 2b + 3b + 4a + 4c + 5b + 5d + 6                  (gross farm rental income → Schedule E line 42)
line 31 = lines 8 … 30f − line 30g (§263A capitalized)          (line 10 limited to 25% of gross farming income)
line 32 = line 7 − line 31                                      (if income, and no prior-year PAL → Schedule E line 40)
if line 32 would be a loss (or prior-year passive losses exist):
    box 34a (all at risk) or 34b (some not at risk → Form 6198 first)
    Form 8582 (unless an exception applies) → deductible loss
    line 34c = deductible loss                                  (→ Schedule E line 40)
Schedule E line 40 = Σ Form 4835 line 32 / 34c    → Schedule E line 41 → Schedule 1 line 5 → Form 1040 line 8
```

Terms used below:

- **Crop share / livestock share rental** — rent paid with a share of what the tenant produces, not a flat cash rent.
- **Material participation (for self-employment tax)** — the landlord's own involvement in producing the crop; the
  definition for landlords is in Pub. 225, chapter 12 (`f4835.pdf p. 2`), not available here.
- **Active participation** — a lighter test (making management decisions in a significant and bona fide sense) that
  makes a rental real estate loss eligible for the $25,000 special allowance of Form 8582 (`f4835.pdf p. 2`,
  `i8582.pdf p. 4`).
- **At risk** — the amount the landlord can actually lose (cash and property put in, and debt the landlord is
  personally liable for); a loss above it is not deductible this year (Form 6198).
- **PAL** — passive activity loss; a loss from a passive activity that Form 8582 does not allow this year is carried
  to next year ("prior-year unallowed loss").
- **CCC** — Commodity Credit Corporation, the U.S. Department of Agriculture lender for crop loans.

## 2. The IRS wording

**Form 4835 (2025), heading** (`f4835.pdf p. 1`): "Farm Rental Income and Expenses (Crop and Livestock Shares (Not
Cash) Received by Landowner (or Sub-Lessor)) (Income Not Subject to Self-Employment Tax)". "Attach to Form 1040,
1040-SR, or 1040-NR."

**Purpose of Form** (`f4835.pdf p. 2`):

> "If you were the landowner (or sub-lessor) and did not materially participate (for self-employment tax purposes) in
> the operation or management of the farm, use Form 4835 to report farm rental income based on crops or livestock
> produced by the tenant."
>
> "Use this form only if the activity was a rental activity for purposes of the passive activity loss limitations."
>
> "If you have net income on line 32, your tax may be less if you figure it using Schedule J (Form 1040)."

**Do not use Form 4835** (`f4835.pdf p. 2`) if you were a tenant (Schedule F); a landowner who materially participated
(Schedule F); a landowner who "received cash rent for pasture or farmland based on a flat charge—instead report as
income on Schedule E (Form 1040), Part I"; an estate or trust (Schedule E Part I); or a partnership or S corporation
(Form 8825).

**Schedule E instructions** (`i1040se.pdf p. 6`): "Report farm rental income and expenses on Form 4835 if: • You are
an individual, • You received rental income based on crops or livestock produced by the tenant, and • You did not
materially participate in the management or operation of the farm."

**Line A** (`f4835.pdf p. 2`):

> "Generally, you are considered to actively participate if you participated in making management decisions or
> arranging for others to provide services (such as repairs) in a significant and bona fide sense. … You do not,
> however, actively participate if at any time during the year your interest (including your spouse's interest) in
> the activity was less than 10% (by value) of all interests in the activity."

**Line 1** (`f4835.pdf p. 2`): "Under both the cash and the accrual methods of accounting, you must report livestock
or crop share rentals received in the year you convert them into money or its equivalent."

**Line 10** (`f4835.pdf p. 3`): conservation expenses "can be deducted only if they are consistent with a conservation
plan approved by the Natural Resources Conservation Service … Your deduction, however, cannot exceed 25% of your gross
income from farming." Schedule F line 12 (`i1040sf.pdf p. 6`): "If your conservation expenses are more than the limit,
the excess can be carried forward and deducted in later tax years. However, the amount deductible for any 1 year can't
exceed the 25% gross income limit for that year."

**Line 32** (`f4835.pdf p. 1`): "Net farm rental income or (loss). Subtract line 31 from line 7. If the result is
income, enter it here and on Schedule E (Form 1040), line 40. If the result is a loss, you must go to line 34."

**Line 34c** (`f4835.pdf p. 1`): "You may have to complete Form 8582 to determine your deductible loss, regardless of
which box you checked. If you checked box 34b, you must complete Form 6198 before going to Form 8582. In either case,
enter the deductible loss here and on Schedule E (Form 1040), line 40."

**Line 32 instructions** (`f4835.pdf p. 3`):

> "If line 7 is more than line 31 and you have prior year unallowed passive activity losses from this activity, don't
> enter your net farm rental income on line 32 until you have figured the amount of prior year unallowed passive
> activity losses you may claim this year. … If there is a net loss after applying the prior year unallowed loss
> against the net farm rental income, enter zero on line 32 and enter the net loss on line 34c. Indicate that you are
> including prior year passive activity losses by entering "PAL" on the dotted line to the left of the entry space
> for line 32."

**Line 34 — when Form 8582 is not needed** (`f4835.pdf p. 3`): when you meet the "Exception for Certain Rental Real
Estate Activities", or "You were a real estate professional … and you materially participated in the operation of
this activity (under the passive activity loss rules) … Your loss is not subject to the passive activity loss
limitations. Instead, enter your deductible loss on line 34c and on Schedule E (Form 1040), line 40. Also, include the
loss in the total for Schedule E (Form 1040), line 43."

**Passive loss rules reach line 40** (`i1040se.pdf p. 3`): "These rules apply to losses in Parts I, II, and III, and
line 40 of Schedule E. Losses from passive activities may be subject first to the at-risk rules."

**Form 8582, reporting the allowed loss** (`i8582.pdf p. 14`): "Schedules C and F, and Form 4835. Enter on the net
profit or loss line of your Schedule C or F, or line 34c of Form 4835, the allowed passive loss from the part. To the
left of the entry space, enter "PAL.""

**Self-employment tax** (Schedule SE instructions, `i1040sse.pdf p. 4`): not included in net earnings from
self-employment: "Income from farm rentals (including rentals paid in crop shares) if, as landlord, you didn't
materially participate in the production or management of the production of farm products on the land. … Report
this income on Form 4835. Use two Forms 4835 if you and your spouse made an election to be taxed as a QJV."

## 3. What goes on this form

- The landlord's share of crops or livestock **when converted to cash or its equivalent** (line 1).
- The farm-rental part of cooperative distributions (Form 1099-PATR), agricultural program payments (Form 1099-G /
  CCC-1099-G), CCC loans reported under the election and forfeited CCC loans, crop insurance and federal crop disaster
  payments, and other farm-rental income such as fuel-tax credits or refunds (lines 2–6).
- The landlord's ordinary expenses of the farm rental property (lines 8–30), including depreciation and §179 not
  claimed elsewhere (line 12) and conservation expenses within the 25% limit (line 10).
- One Form 4835 per farm rental activity; with a qualified joint venture (QJV), one per spouse (`f4835.pdf p. 2`).

| Look-alike amount | Where it goes instead | Source |

| Flat **cash** rent for pasture or farmland | Schedule E Part I (rental real estate) | `f4835.pdf p. 2` |
| Crop-share rent received by an estate or trust | Schedule E Part I | `f4835.pdf p. 2`; `i1040se.pdf p. 4` |
| Crop-share rent of a partnership or S corporation | Form 8825 (the partner reports the K-1) | `f4835.pdf p. 2` |
| Personal or living expenses | Not deductible ("Do not include personal or living expenses") | `f4835.pdf p. 1` |
| Expenses that must be capitalized under §263A | Not deducted; shown in parentheses on line 30g | `f4835.pdf p. 3` |
| Conservation expenses over 25% of gross farming income | Carried forward to later years | `i1040sf.pdf p. 6` |
| Crop insurance received in 2025 and elected to be deferred | 2026 return (line 5d next year) | `f4835.pdf p. 2`; `i1040sf.pdf p. 5` |

| Gain or loss on the sale of the farmland or equipment | Form 4797 / Schedule D | (not a Form 4835 line) |

## 6. How to figure it, step by step (every line of the 2025 form)

Header: name, SSN, EIN only if required (`f4835.pdf p. 2`, "You need an EIN only if you had a qualified retirement plan
or were required to file an employment, excise, estate, trust, partnership, or alcohol, tobacco, and firearms tax
return").

**Line A — Active participation.** Answer Yes if you made management decisions (approving tenants, setting rental
terms, approving capital or repair spending) in a significant and bona fide sense; No if your and your spouse's
interest was under 10% by value at any time in 2025 (`f4835.pdf p. 2`). Yes makes a loss eligible for the $25,000
special allowance on Form 8582 (`i8582.pdf p. 4`).

**Part I — Gross Farm Rental Income — Based on Production** ("Include amounts converted to cash or the equivalent",
`f4835.pdf p. 1`). Only the right-hand column (lines 1, 2b, 3b, 4a, 4c, 5b, 5d, 6) is added on line 7.

1. **Line 1** — income from production of livestock, produce, grains and other crops, in the year converted to money
   or its equivalent (`f4835.pdf p. 2`).
2. **Line 2a** — total cooperative distributions from Form 1099-PATR. **Line 2b** — the taxable amount; per Schedule F
   lines 3a/3b, patronage dividends from buying personal or family items, capital assets or depreciable assets go on
   line 2a only (`f4835.pdf p. 2`; `i1040sf.pdf p. 4`).
3. **Line 3a** — total agricultural program payments (price-loss and agriculture-risk coverage, market facilitation,
   CCC market gain, diversion, cost-share, payments in materials or services). **Line 3b** — taxable amount; do not
   include CCC market gain if the CCC loan proceeds were reported as income under the election (`i1040sf.pdf p. 4`).
4. **Line 4a** — CCC loans reported as income under the election (attach a statement). **Line 4b** — the full amount
   of CCC loans forfeited, "even if you reported the loan proceeds as income". **Line 4c** — taxable amount: the
   forfeited amount when no election was made; generally nothing when the election was made, unless the forfeited
   amount differs from your basis in the commodity (`f4835.pdf p. 2`; `i1040sf.pdf p. 4–5`).
5. **Line 5a** — crop insurance proceeds and federal crop disaster payments received in 2025, even if deferred.
   **Line 5b** — the taxable amount, leaving out proceeds elected to be included in 2026. **Line 5c** — check the box
   and attach a statement to elect deferral (cash method; damage year 2025); deferring any eligible proceeds means
   deferring all of them from that trade or business. **Line 5d** — proceeds received in 2024 and elected to be
   included in 2025 (`f4835.pdf p. 2–3`; `i1040sf.pdf p. 5`). What qualifies for the election is in Pub. 225
   chapter 3 (not available).
6. **Line 6** — other income, including federal and state gasoline or fuel-tax credits or refunds (see Schedule F
   line 8) (`f4835.pdf p. 3`).
7. **Line 7** — gross farm rental income: add the right column of lines 1–6. Enter it here **and on Schedule E line
   42** (`f4835.pdf p. 1`).

**Part II — Expenses — Farm Rental Property** ("Do not include personal or living expenses", `f4835.pdf p. 1`).
Do not reduce lines 8–30f for amounts capitalized under §263A; show the total capitalized in parentheses on line 30g
with "263A" (`f4835.pdf p. 3`).

8. **Line 8** — car and truck expenses (Schedule F rules; attach Form 4562).
9. **Line 9** — chemicals.
10. **Line 10** — conservation expenses (soil and water conservation, erosion prevention, endangered-species recovery)
    consistent with an approved plan, **limited to 25% of gross income from farming**; the excess carries forward and
    each later year is again limited to that year's 25% (`f4835.pdf p. 3`; `i1040sf.pdf p. 6`). Gross income from
    farming excludes "certain gains from selling assets such as farm machinery and land" (`i1040sf.pdf p. 6`).
11. **Line 11** — custom hire (machine work).
12. **Line 12** — depreciation and §179 deduction not claimed elsewhere (Form 4562; Pub. 225 chapter 7)
    (`f4835.pdf p. 3`).
13. **Line 13** — employee benefit programs other than line 21.
14. **Line 14** — feed. **Line 15** — fertilizers and lime. **Line 16** — freight and trucking. **Line 17** —
    gasoline, fuel and oil. **Line 18** — insurance (other than health).
15. **Line 19a** — mortgage interest paid to banks, etc.; **line 19b** — other interest. Interest may be limited by
    Form 8990 (§163(j)) and must be allocated by use (`f4835.pdf p. 3`).
16. **Line 20** — labor hired, less employment credits. **Line 21** — pension and profit-sharing plans.
17. **Line 22a** — rent or lease of vehicles, machinery and equipment (business portion; a vehicle lease of 30 days
    or more may need an inclusion amount, Pub. 463); **line 22b** — other rent (land, animals) (`f4835.pdf p. 3`).
18. **Line 23** — repairs and maintenance. **Line 24** — seeds and plants. **Line 25** — storage and warehousing.
    **Line 26** — supplies. **Line 27** — taxes. **Line 28** — utilities. **Line 29** — veterinary, breeding and
    medicine.
19. **Lines 30a–30f** — other expenses (specify). **Line 30g** — §263A capitalized costs, in parentheses
    (`f4835.pdf p. 3`).
20. **Line 31** — total expenses: lines 8 through 30f, reduced by line 30g (`f4835.pdf p. 3`).
21. **Line 32** — line 7 minus line 31:
    - income and no prior-year unallowed passive loss from this activity → enter it here and on Schedule E line 40;
    - income and a prior-year unallowed loss from this activity → figure on Form 8582 how much of the prior loss is
      allowed, enter line 7 − line 31 − allowed prior loss with "PAL" on the dotted line; if that is a loss, enter 0
      on line 32 and the loss on line 34c;
    - a loss → do not enter it; go to line 34 (`f4835.pdf p. 3`).
22. **Line 33** — "Reserved for future use" (`f4835.pdf p. 1`).
23. **Line 34a / 34b** — check 34a if all of your investment is at risk, 34b if some is not. With 34b, complete
    Form 6198 first; its deductible loss is what goes on to Form 8582 (`f4835.pdf p. 1, 3`).
24. **Line 34c** — the deductible loss after Form 6198 (if 34b) and Form 8582. Form 8582 is skipped when the "Exception
    for Certain Rental Real Estate Activities" applies (active participation, no other passive activities, no prior
    unallowed losses, total loss ≤ $25,000 ($12,500 MFS living apart), MAGI ≤ $100,000 ($50,000), no limited-partner
    interest — `i8582.pdf p. 1`), or when you were a real estate professional who materially participated (then also
    include the loss on Schedule E line 43). Enter the deductible loss on line 34c and on Schedule E line 40
    (`f4835.pdf p. 1, 3`; `i8582.pdf p. 2, 14`). A loss not allowed by Form 6198 or Form 8582 is carried to 2026.

## 8. Married filing jointly / separately

- **Joint return:** one Form 4835 per activity, in the name of the owner; with a QJV each spouse files one for their
  share (`f4835.pdf p. 2`). Both spouses' passive activities are combined on one Form 8582. For line A, the 10% test
  counts "your interest (including your spouse's interest)" (`f4835.pdf p. 2`).
- **Separate returns:** each spouse reports their own activities. The $25,000 special allowance becomes $12,500 (MAGI
  phase-out $50,000–$75,000) if the spouses lived apart all year, and is not available if they lived together at any
  time (`i8582.pdf p. 4`). Community property rules may divide the income (Pub. 555, not cited here).
- No per-person dollar limit on Form 4835 itself.

## 10. Where the amount goes next

- **Line 32 / 34c → Schedule E line 40** ("Net farm rental income or (loss) from Form 4835") → **Schedule E line 41**
  → **Schedule 1 line 5** → Schedule 1 line 10 → **Form 1040 line 8** → line 9 → line 11 (AGI).
- **Line 7 → Schedule E line 42** (reconciliation of farming and fishing income; used for the farmer estimated-tax
  rules and Form 2210-F) (`f4835.pdf p. 1`; `i1040se.pdf p. 11`).
- **Form 8582** (a loss, or income with prior-year unallowed losses, or other passive activities) and **Form 6198**
  (box 34b).
- **Form 8960** (net investment income tax): passive rental income is net investment income (Form 8960 line 4a; not
  re-derived here).
- **Not** Schedule SE (`i1040sse.pdf p. 4`).
- Possibly **Form 8995 / 8995-A** (QBI) if the activity is a §162 trade or business (`i8995.pdf p. 1–2`).
- Schedule J (optional) for net income on line 32 (`f4835.pdf p. 2`).

## 11. Worked example

Pat Landowner (000-00-0004), single, rents 160 acres to a tenant for one-third of the crop. Pat approves the tenant and
the crop plan (active participation: Yes), has no other passive activities and no prior-year unallowed losses, and
all of Pat's investment is at risk. Modified AGI for Form 8582 is $80,000.

| Line | Item | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pat's crop share sold in 2025 | $20,000 |
| 2a / 2b | Form 1099-PATR distributions, all taxable | $1,000 / $1,000 |
| 3a / 3b | Agricultural program payments, all taxable | $2,000 / $2,000 |
| 5a / 5b | Crop insurance received, no deferral | $4,000 / $4,000 |
| **7** | **Gross farm rental income** | **$27,000** |
| 10 | Conservation expenses paid $12,000; limit 25% × $27,000 = $6,750 | $6,750 (carry $5,250 to 2026) |
| 12 | Depreciation | $8,000 |
| 15 | Fertilizers and lime | $4,000 |
| 18 | Insurance | $1,500 |
| 19a | Mortgage interest | $5,000 |
| 23 | Repairs and maintenance | $2,500 |
| 27 | Taxes | $3,000 |
| **31** | **Total expenses** | **$30,750** |
| 32 | $27,000 − $30,750 is a loss → go to line 34 | — |
| 34a | All investment at risk | ✓ |
| 34c | The Form 8582 exception applies (active participation, only passive activity, no prior losses, loss $3,750 ≤ $25,000, MAGI ≤ $100,000) → deductible loss | **($3,750)** |

Pat's Schedule E line 40 = ($3,750); line 42 = $27,000; Schedule 1 line 5 includes ($3,750). No self-employment tax.
