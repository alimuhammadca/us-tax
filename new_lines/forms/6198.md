# Form 6198 — At-Risk Limitations

Tax year 2025. What the IRS requires. How the app does it: `C:\us-tax\new_knowledge\forms\6198.md`. Picture:
`C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\forms\6198.md`.

Form 6198 is not an annual form. The edition used for 2025 is **Form 6198 (Rev. November 2025)** — its wording is
quoted from `f6198.pdf` (kept in `C:\us-tax\docs\IRS-Forms\`, one page, "Form 6198 (Rev. 11-2025) Created 9/9/25") —
with the **Instructions for Form 6198 (Rev. November 2025)**, `i6198.pdf` in `C:\us-tax\docs\books\` ("For use with

## 1. At a glance

The **at-risk rules** (Internal Revenue Code section 465) stop you from deducting a loss from a business or
investment activity that is larger than the amount you could actually lose in it — your **amount at risk**: the cash
and the adjusted basis of property you put in, plus money you borrowed for the activity and are personally liable to
repay (and, for holding real estate, qualified nonrecourse financing). Money you cannot lose — a nonrecourse loan, an
investment protected by a guarantee or stop-loss, a loan from someone with an interest in the activity — does not
count. Form 6198 figures, **for one activity at a time**:

1. **Part I** — the activity's profit or loss for the year, including last year's at-risk-disallowed loss (line 5);
2. **Part II** (simplified) **or Part III** (detailed) — the amount at risk (line 10b or line 19b);
3. **Part IV** — the deductible loss: the smaller of the loss and the amount at risk (line 21).

The rest of the loss is not lost: it "is treated as a deduction allocable to the business in 2026" (`i1040sc.pdf
p. 15`). The at-risk limit is applied **before** the passive activity loss rules (Form 8582) and before the excess
business loss limit (Form 461).

```text
line 5  = line 1 + line 2a + line 2b + line 2c + line 3 − line 4         (profit or loss, incl. prior-year disallowed)
line 10b = max(0, line 6 + line 7 − line 9)                              (Part II, simplified)
line 19b = max(0, line 15 + line 16 − line 18)                           (Part III, detailed)
line 20 = max(line 10b, line 19b)
line 21 = −min(|line 5 loss|, line 20)                                    (deductible loss; 0 if line 20 = 0)
carryover to 2026 = |line 5 loss| − |line 21|  (allocated over the loss items)
```

Terms used below:

- **Activity** — the unit the limit is applied to. Each film, leased section 1245 property, farm, real-property
  holding, oil and gas or geothermal property is a separate activity unless the aggregation rules join them
  (`i6198.pdf p. 2`).
- **Nonrecourse loan** — a loan nobody is personally liable to repay; the lender can only take the property securing it.
- **Qualified nonrecourse financing (QNR)** — a nonrecourse loan for holding real property, secured by that real
  property, not convertible, and from a government or a "qualified person" (a commercial lender that is not the seller,
  a related person or a fee-earner) (`i6198.pdf p. 1–2`). It **is** treated as at risk.
- **Effective date** — the date the at-risk rules first applied to the activity (Part III).

## 2. The IRS wording

**Form 6198 (Rev. November 2025)** (`f6198.pdf p. 1`): heading "At-Risk Limitations … Attach to your tax return.
… Attachment Sequence No. 31"; "Description of activity (see instructions)".

> "Part I Current Year Profit (Loss) From the Activity, Including Prior Year Nondeductible Amounts."
> "Part II Simplified Computation of Amount at Risk."
> "Part III Detailed Computation of Amount at Risk. If you completed Part III of Form 6198 for the prior year, see the
> instructions."
> "Part IV Deductible Loss … 21 Deductible loss. Enter the smaller of the line 5 loss (treated as a positive number) or
> line 20."
> "Note: If the loss is from a passive activity, see the Instructions for Form 8582 … If only part of the loss is
> subject to the passive activity loss rules, report only that part on Form 8582".

**Purpose and who must file** (`i6198.pdf p. 1`):

> "The at-risk rules of section 465 limit the amount of the loss you can deduct to the amount at risk."
>
> "Form 6198 is filed by individuals (including filers of Schedules C, E, and F (Form 1040 or 1040-SR)), estates,
> trusts, and certain closely held C corporations … File Form 6198 if during the tax year you, a partnership in which
> you were a partner, or an S corporation in which you were a shareholder had any amounts not at risk … invested in an
> at-risk activity … that incurred a loss."

**Amounts not at risk** (`i6198.pdf p. 1`):

> "You are not considered at risk for any of the following. 1. Nonrecourse loans used to finance the activity … (unless
> the nonrecourse loan is secured by your own property that is not used in the activity). However, you are considered
> at risk for qualified nonrecourse financing secured by real property used in the activity of holding real property
> (other than mineral property). 2. Cash, property, or borrowed amounts used in the activity that are protected against
> loss by a guarantee, stop-loss agreement, or other similar arrangement … 3. Amounts borrowed for use in the activity
> from a person who has an interest in the activity other than as a creditor or who is related under section
> 465(b)(3)(C) … 4. Any cash or property contributed to the activity … that is: a. Financed through nonrecourse
> indebtedness or protected against loss …; or b. Borrowed from a person who has an interest in the activity …"

**Exception** (`i6198.pdf p. 1`): "Holding real property placed in service before 1987 and holding an interest
acquired before 1987 in a partnership, an S corporation, or other pass-through entity already engaged in an activity of
holding real property before 1987 are not affected by the at-risk rules. This exception does not apply to holding
mineral property."

**One form per activity** (`i6198.pdf p. 2`): "File one form if your activities are listed under the aggregation
rules. File a separate form for each activity if your activities are listed under the separation rules." … "If you
are engaged in more than one at-risk activity or in both at-risk activities and not-at-risk activities, you must
allocate income, gains, losses, and deductions to each activity."

**Order of limits** (`i8582.pdf p. 2`): "Generally, PALs are subject to other limitations (for example, basis and
at-risk limitations) before they're subject to the passive loss limitations." (`i8582.pdf p. 1`): "allowable business
losses after taking into account first the at-risk limitations and then the passive loss limitations (this form) …
may be subject to the excess business loss limitation."

## 3. What goes on this form

- Every item of income, gain, deduction and loss of **one** at-risk activity for 2025 — ordinary (line 1), gains and
  losses on its assets or on the interest in it (lines 2a–2c), other K-1 items (lines 3–4), including the activity's
  share of allowable investment interest (line 4) (`i6198.pdf p. 2–3`).
- Losses from an earlier year that the at-risk rules disallowed (on the schedule for a sole proprietor; directly on
  lines 1–2c for a partner or S corporation shareholder) (`i6198.pdf p. 2`).
- The amount at risk for that activity, figured by Part II or Part III.

Activities covered (`i6198.pdf p. 1`): "1. Holding, producing, or distributing motion picture films or videotapes.
2. Farming … 3. Leasing any section 1245 property … 4. Exploring for or exploiting oil and gas resources.
5. Exploring for or exploiting geothermal deposits … 6. Any other activity that is not included in (1) through (5)
above" — in practice: a Schedule C business, a Schedule F farm, a Schedule E rental or royalty, a Form 4835 farm
rental, and each partnership / S corporation activity on a K-1.

## 6. How to figure it, step by step (every line of the form)

**Heading and description.** Name, identifying number, and "Description of activity". For a partnership or
S corporation activity, "enter the name and identifying number of the partnership or S corporation" (`i6198.pdf p. 2`).
Enter only amounts of this activity: "When filling in Parts I, II, and III, enter only amounts that relate to the
activity included on this form" (`i6198.pdf p. 2`).

**Part I — Current year profit (loss), including prior-year nondeductible amounts.**

Before starting, a sole proprietor puts last year's disallowed loss back on this year's schedule: "if … your 2024
Schedule C … had a $1,500 loss on line 31, but because of the at-risk rules your loss was limited to $500, include the
$1,000 on your 2025 Schedule C … in Part V, Other Expenses, and identify it as a prior year loss" (`i6198.pdf p. 2`).
A partner or shareholder includes it directly on Form 6198 ("include both the $1,000 loss from your prior year and the
amount from your current year Schedule K-1 on line 1", `i6198.pdf p. 2`).

1. **Line 1 — Ordinary income (loss).** Sole proprietor / landlord: "your ordinary income or loss from the at-risk
   activity without regard to the at-risk limitations … including prior year deductions that were not allowed because
   of the at-risk rules". Do **not** include gains and losses on the disposition of the activity's assets, casualty
   items or investment interest (they go on lines 2a–2c and 4). Partner / shareholder: "the amount from box 1 of your
   current year Schedule K-1 … (plus any prior year ordinary loss that you could not deduct because of the at-risk
   rules)" (`i6198.pdf p. 2`).
2. **Lines 2a / 2b / 2c — Gain (loss)** from the sale or other disposition of assets used in the activity or of your
   interest, reported on Schedule D (2a), Form 4797 (2b) or another form (2c; write the form number next to it, e.g.
   "Form 4684"). "Enter gains and losses without regard to the at-risk limitations, the limitation on capital losses,
   or the passive activity loss limitations" (`i6198.pdf p. 2–3`). Prior-year disallowed losses of this kind are
   included the same way (`i6198.pdf p. 3`).
3. **Line 3 — Other income and gains** from Schedule K-1 (Form 1065 or 1120-S) not on lines 1–2c (`i6198.pdf p. 3`).
4. **Line 4 — Other deductions and losses** from the K-1 not on lines 1–2c, and the investment interest of this
   activity allowed on Form 4952 line 8 ("You must reduce the allowable investment interest deduction on Form 4952 by
   the amount you carry to Form 6198"); include last year's at-risk-limited investment interest (`i6198.pdf p. 3`).
   Entered in parentheses.
5. **Line 5 — Current year profit (loss).** Combine lines 1 through 4 (`f6198.pdf p. 1`).
   - **Profit:** report every item on its normal form; "you may not have to complete the rest of this form", but
     attach Form 6198. Even with a profit you may have **recapture income** if a distribution or transaction reduced
     the amount at risk below zero at year end — "See Pub. 925 for information on the recapture rules" (`i6198.pdf
     p. 3`).
   - **Loss:** the income and gains on lines 1–3 are fully reportable, and the deductions and losses are allowable "to
     the extent of the income and gains"; only the **excess** loss (line 5) is tested against the amount at risk
     (`i6198.pdf p. 3`, Example: Schedule C loss $4,600, Schedule D gain $3,100, line 5 loss $1,500, $600 deductible →
     Schedule C shows $3,700).

**Part II — Simplified computation of amount at risk.** Usable "only if you know your adjusted basis in the activity
or in your interest"; Part III "may allow a larger amount at risk" (`i6198.pdf p. 3`).

6. **Line 6 — Adjusted basis** (section 1011) in the activity or in your interest on the first day of the year; not
   less than zero. Schedule C and F filers "must not reduce the amount on this line by any liabilities"; partners see
   Pub. 541; S corporation shareholders the Form 1120-S instructions; allocate basis among a partnership's activities
   (`i6198.pdf p. 3`).
7. **Line 7 — Increases for the tax year** (not the current income on lines 1–3): net FMV of your own property (not
   used in the activity) pledged for nonrecourse loans of the activity; cash and adjusted basis of property contributed
   in the year; loans you are personally liable for and QNR; percentage depletion in excess of basis
   (`i6198.pdf p. 3–4`).
8. **Line 8** — add lines 6 and 7.
9. **Line 9 — Decreases for the tax year** (not the current deductions on lines 1–4): nonrecourse loans other than QNR
   included on line 6; protected amounts included on line 6; amounts borrowed from an interested or related person
   (activities 1–5); "Withdrawals and distributions during the tax year — both cash and the adjusted basis of noncash
   items"; nonrecourse liabilities on property you contributed (`i6198.pdf p. 4`).
10. **Line 10a** — line 8 minus line 9.
11. **Line 10b — Amount at risk.** "If line 10a is more than zero, enter that amount here and go to line 20 (or
    complete Part III). Otherwise, enter -0- and see Pub. 925 for information on the recapture rules"
    (`f6198.pdf p. 1`). If smaller than the line 5 loss, Part III may give more (`i6198.pdf p. 4`).

**Part III — Detailed computation of amount at risk.** If Part III was completed last year, skip lines 11–14 and start
from last year's line 19b; if the activity began on or after its effective date and Part III was not completed last
year, enter 0 on line 15 (`i6198.pdf p. 4`). Effective dates: generally the first tax year beginning after 1975
(activities 1–4); October 1, 1978 for geothermal wells; real property placed in service after 1986; generally the first
tax year beginning after 1978 for others (`i6198.pdf p. 4`).

12. **Line 11 — Investment at the effective date** (not below zero): Line 11 Worksheet for others (cash, inventories,
    adjusted basis of depreciable and depletable assets and land, other assets; accrual filers add net receivables and
    subtract payables); a partner or shareholder enters the basis of the interest at the effective date, allocated to
    the at-risk activity (`i6198.pdf p. 4–5`).
13. **Line 12 — Increases at effective date:** net FMV of own property securing the activity's nonrecourse loans
    included on line 14; and pre-effective-date losses for which there were equal or greater amounts not at risk (Line 12
    Worksheet, year by year) (`i6198.pdf p. 6`).
14. **Line 13** — add lines 11 and 12.
15. **Line 14 — Decreases at effective date:** nonrecourse loans outstanding; protected amounts; amounts borrowed from
    interested or related persons; liens on contributed property (not for S corporation shareholders), each only if
    included on line 11 (`i6198.pdf p. 6`).
16. **Line 15 — Amount at risk:** box a — line 13 minus line 14 (not below zero); box b — "If you completed Part III of
    Form 6198 for the prior tax year, check box b and enter the amount from line 19b of the prior year form … Do not
    enter the amount from line 10b of the prior year tax form" (`i6198.pdf p. 7`).
17. **Line 16 — Increases since** the effective date (box a) or the end of the prior year (box b): net FMV of own
    property pledged; cash and adjusted basis of property contributed; personally liable loans and QNR; "Total net
    income from this activity since the effective date … Do not include the current year income or gains"; gain on
    transfer of the activity or interest; amounts included in income because the amount at risk was below zero;
    outside money used to repay loans on lines 14 and 18; excess percentage depletion (Line 16 Worksheet); for an
    S corporation shareholder, loans made to the corporation (`i6198.pdf p. 7–8`).
18. **Line 17** — add lines 15 and 16.
19. **Line 18 — Decreases since:** protected amounts; borrowings from interested or related persons; cash and adjusted
    basis of property withdrawn or distributed; recourse loans and QNR changed to nonrecourse; "Total losses from this
    activity deducted since the effective date … Your prior tax year line 21 deductible loss reduces your at-risk
    investment as of the beginning of your current tax year"; nonrecourse liabilities of contributed property; other
    at-risk amounts that became not at risk; S corporation adjustments. "For loans, enter the amount of the loan you
    incurred, not the current balance of the loan" (`i6198.pdf p. 8`).
20. **Line 19a** — line 17 minus line 18. **Line 19b** — if more than zero, enter it; otherwise 0 and see Pub. 925 for
    recapture (`f6198.pdf p. 1`; `i6198.pdf p. 8`).

**Part IV — Deductible loss.**

21. **Line 20 — Amount at risk:** "Enter the larger of line 10b or line 19b" (`f6198.pdf p. 1`).
22. **Line 21 — Deductible loss:** "the smaller of the line 5 loss (treated as a positive number) or line 20". The
    instructions' examples: line 5 loss $400 and line 20 $1,000 → ($400); loss $1,600 and line 20 $1,200 → ($1,200);
    loss $800 and line 20 zero → -0- (`i6198.pdf p. 8`).
23. **Report it.** If line 21 is one loss item, report it on the return; if it is made up of several (for example a
    Schedule C loss and a Schedule D loss), each item is allowed in proportion: "multiplying the loss on line 21 by a
    fraction … dividing each item of deduction or loss from the activity by the total loss from the activity on
    line 5. The remaining portion of each deduction or loss item from the activity is disallowed and must be carried
    over to next year" (`i6198.pdf p. 8`).

## 7. Special cases and elections

| Case | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-1987 real property | Holding real property placed in service before 1987 (and pre-1987 interests in a pass-through holding it) is outside the at-risk rules; not mineral property | `i6198.pdf p. 1`; `i1040se.pdf p. 3` |
| Qualified nonrecourse financing | At risk when secured by real property used in holding real property; both the partnership and the partner must meet the rules | `i6198.pdf p. 1–2`; `i1065sk1.pdf p. 12` |
| Borrowings before May 4, 2004 (activity 6) | No Form 6198 needed if these are the only amounts not at risk | `i6198.pdf p. 1` |
| Amount at risk below zero | Recapture income — see Pub. 925 (not in `docs\books`) | `i6198.pdf p. 3, 4, 8` |
| Partner / S corporation shareholder | Prior-year disallowed loss goes directly on lines 1–2c; separate statements per activity from the entity | `i6198.pdf p. 2` |
| Several loss items | Line 21 allocated pro rata; the rest carries over item by item | `i6198.pdf p. 8` |
| Carryover | "Any loss from this business not allowed for 2025 only because of the at-risk rules is treated as a deduction allocable to the business in 2026" | `i1040sc.pdf p. 15`; `i1040sf.pdf p. 10` |
| Basis limits first | For a partnership or S corporation loss, the basis limit applies before the at-risk rules ("after applying the basis and at-risk limitations") | `i1040se.pdf p. 8`; `i1065sk1.pdf p. 14` |

## 8. Married filing jointly / separately

Form 6198 is per **activity**, not per person; the instructions give no separate joint-return rule. Each spouse's own
activities (or interests) each get their own form. On separate returns each spouse figures the at-risk amount of their
own interest. (The joint-ownership detail is in Pub. 925, which is not in `docs\books`: unverified.)

## 10. Where the amount goes next

| Activity | Where the at-risk-allowed loss goes | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Schedule C business | Schedule C line 31 (box 32b checked); passive (line G "No") → Form 8582 first | `i1040sc.pdf p. 15` |
| Schedule F farm | Schedule F line 34 (box 36b checked); passive → Form 8582 | `i1040sf.pdf p. 10` |
| Schedule E rental / royalty | Schedule E line 21, with "Form 6198" written to the left; then line 22 via Form 8582 for a passive rental loss | `i1040se.pdf p. 7–8` |
| Partnership / S corporation (Schedule E Part II) | Box in column (f) checked; nonpassive deductible loss in column (i); passive → Form 8582 → column (g) | `i1040se.pdf p. 8` |
| Form 4835 farm rental | Box 34b; "you must complete Form 6198 … before you complete Form 8582"; deductible loss on line 34c and Schedule E line 40 | `f4835.pdf p. 1, 3` |
| Gains / losses on lines 2a–2c | Schedule D, Form 4797 or the other form, as limited | `i6198.pdf p. 2–3, 8` |

Then: Form 8582 (passive) → Form 461 (excess business loss) → Schedule 1 lines 3 / 5 / 6 → Form 1040 line 8 → AGI.
The disallowed part becomes next year's deduction for the same activity.

## 11. Worked example

Robin Sample (000-00-0001), single, runs a sole-proprietor equipment-leasing business (Schedule C). Records for 2025:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Schedule C loss before the at-risk limit (includes $2,000 disallowed in 2024, entered in Part V) | $(30,000) |
| Gain on sale of a business trailer (Form 4797) | $4,000 |
| Adjusted basis in the business on 1/1/2025 (cash, equipment; liabilities not subtracted) | $40,000 |
| — of which bought with a nonrecourse seller loan | $25,000 |
| Cash put in during 2025 | $3,000 |
| Cash withdrawn during 2025 | $6,000 |

1. Line 1 = (30,000). Line 2b = 4,000. Lines 2a, 2c, 3, 4 = 0. **Line 5 = (26,000).**
2. Line 6 = 40,000. Line 7 = 3,000. Line 8 = 43,000. Line 9 = 25,000 (nonrecourse loan included on line 6) + 6,000
   (withdrawals) = 31,000. Line 10a = 12,000. **Line 10b = 12,000.** (Part III not used.)
3. Line 20 = 12,000. **Line 21 = (12,000)** (smaller of 26,000 and 12,000).
4. Reporting: the $4,000 Form 4797 gain is reported in full; the Schedule C loss allowed = gain $4,000 + line 21
   $12,000 = **$16,000**, so Schedule C line 31 shows $(16,000) with box 32b checked.
5. Carryover: $30,000 − $16,000 = **$14,000**, a deduction allocable to the business in 2026.
6. If the business were passive (line G "No"), the $16,000 would next go to Form 8582.
