# Form 6781 — Gains and Losses From Section 1256 Contracts and Straddles

Tax year 2025. What the IRS requires. How the app does it: `C:\us-tax\new_knowledge\forms\6781.md`. Picture:
`C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\forms\6781.md`. How the two results enter Schedule D is in
`C:\us-tax\new_lines\forms\schedule-d.md` (lines 4 and 11; 28% Rate Gain Worksheet line 3). This file covers every line
of the 2025 Form 6781: the election boxes A–D, Part I (section 1256 contracts marked to market, lines 1–9), Part II
(straddles, lines 10–13) and Part III (unrecognized gains, line 14).

The 2025 Form 6781 prints its own instructions on pages 2–4; they are cited as `f6781.pdf p. N`. The Form 1099-B boxes
that feed line 1 are in `i1099b--2025.pdf` (the 2025 edition).

---

## 1. At a glance

Form 6781 reports gains and losses on **section 1256 contracts** (regulated futures, foreign currency contracts,
nonequity options such as options on futures or broad-based index options, dealer equity options and dealer securities
futures) and on **straddles**. Every section 1256 contract still open at year end is treated as sold at its fair market
value on the last business day of the year (**mark to market**), and the net capital gain or loss is split **40%
short-term / 60% long-term whatever the holding period** (`f6781.pdf p. 2`). Straddle losses are deferred to the extent
of unrecognized gain on the offsetting positions (Part II). A net section 1256 loss may be carried back three years
(box D).

```text
Part I
  line 1  = one row per account: Form 1099-B box 11, or each transaction without a 1099-B   (gain in (c), loss in (b))
  line 2  = totals of column (b) and column (c)
  line 3  = line 2 (c) − line 2 (b)                         net gain or (loss)
  line 4  = Form 1099-B adjustments (straddles, hedges)       attach a statement
  line 5  = line 3 + line 4
  line 6  = loss carried back (box D checked), as a positive number; else 0; skipped when line 5 is a gain
  line 7  = line 5 + line 6
  line 8  = 40% × line 7  → Schedule D line 4   (short-term)
  line 9  = 60% × line 7  → Schedule D line 11  (long-term)
Part II
  line 10 (per loss position): (f) = max(0, (e) − (d)); (h) = max(0, (f) − (g))
  line 11a / 11b = short-term / long-term part of line 10 (h) → Schedule D line 4 / line 11
  line 12 (per gain position): (f) = max(0, (d) − (e))
  line 13a / 13b = short-term / long-term part of line 12 (f) → Schedule D line 4 / line 11
Part III (memo only)
  line 14 (per position held at year end with value above basis): (e) = max(0, (c) − (d))
```

Terms used below:

- **Section 1256 contract** — "any: • Regulated futures contract, • Foreign currency contract, • Nonequity option,
  • Dealer equity option, or • Dealer securities futures contract" (`f6781.pdf p. 2`). It does **not** include "any
  securities future contract, option on a securities future contract, interest rate swap, currency swap, basis swap,
  commodity swap, equity swap, equity index swap, credit default swap, interest rate cap, interest rate floor, or similar
  agreement" (`f6781.pdf p. 2`).
- **Mark-to-market rules** — "each section 1256 contract held at year end is treated as if it were sold at fair market
  value (FMV) on the last business day of the tax year. The wash sale rules don't apply" (`f6781.pdf p. 2`).
- **Straddle** — "offsetting positions with respect to personal property of a type that is actively traded"; positions
  are **offsetting** when holding one substantially decreases the risk of loss on another (`f6781.pdf p. 2`).
- **Mixed straddle** — "any straddle in which at least one but not all of the positions is a section 1256 contract"
  (`f6781.pdf p. 2`).
- **Unrecognized gain** — the gain on an offsetting position still held at year end: its settlement price at the close
  of the last business day of 2025 minus its cost or other basis (`f6781.pdf p. 4`, line 10 column (g)).
- **Hedging transaction** — a position identified as a hedge (section 1256(e)(2)); its gain or loss is ordinary, and the
  mark-to-market rules do not apply (`f6781.pdf p. 2–3`).
- **Net section 1256 contracts loss** — the amount that may be carried back under box D (defined in §6, line 6).

## 2. The IRS wording

**Form 6781 (2025)** (`f6781.pdf p. 1`): heading "Attach to your tax return." Boxes "A Mixed straddle election, B
Straddle-by-straddle identification election, C Mixed straddle account election, D Net section 1256 contracts loss
election". Part I line 8: "Short-term capital gain or (loss). Multiply line 7 by 40% (0.40). Enter here and include on
line 4 of Schedule D or on Form 8949." Line 9: "Long-term capital gain or (loss). Multiply line 7 by 60% (0.60). Enter
here and include on line 11 of Schedule D or on Form 8949." Part II heading: "Gains and Losses From Straddles. Attach a
separate statement listing each straddle and its components." Part III heading: "Unrecognized Gains From Positions Held
on Last Day of Tax Year. Memo entry only".

**Purpose** (`f6781.pdf p. 2`): "Use Form 6781 to report: • Any capital gain or loss on section 1256 contracts under the
mark-to-market rules, and • Gains and losses under section 1092 from straddle positions."

**The 60/40 rule** (`f6781.pdf p. 2`): "If your section 1256 contracts produce capital gain or loss, gains or losses on
section 1256 contracts open at the end of the year, or terminated during the year, are treated as 60% long term and 40%
short term, regardless of how long the contracts were held. The mark-to-market rules don't apply if you properly and
timely identified a section 1256 contract as a hedge."

**General rule for straddles** (`f6781.pdf p. 2`): "If you don't make any of the elections described in box A, B, or C,
and you have a loss on the section 1256 contract component, use Part II to reduce the loss by any unrecognized gain on the
non-section 1256 contract component before making an entry in Part I."

**Line 1** (`f6781.pdf p. 3`): "Include on line 1 all capital gains and losses from section 1256 contracts open at the
end of your tax year or closed out during the year. If you received a Form 1099-B … include on line 1 the amount from
box 11 of each form. In column (a), enter 'Form 1099-B' and the broker's name. List separately each transaction for
which you didn't receive a Form 1099-B or substitute statement, or received a Form 1099-B that isn't for your tax year."

**Schedule D** (`i1040sd.pdf p. 1`): "Use Form 6781 to report gains and losses from section 1256 contracts and
straddles"; Schedule D is used "To report a gain or loss from Form 4684, 6781, or 8824".

## 6. How to figure it, line by line

### Election boxes A–D (top of page 1)

1. **Box A — mixed straddle election** (section 1256(d)): the mark-to-market rules do not apply to the section 1256
   contracts in an identified mixed straddle; each position must be identified on the day the first section 1256
   contract is acquired; the election applies to later years and cannot be revoked without IRS consent. "check box A and
   report the section 1256 contract component in Part II instead of in Part I" (`f6781.pdf p. 2`).
2. **Box B — straddle-by-straddle identification election** (Temp. Regs. 1.1092(b)-3T(d)): each position identified by
   the earlier of the close of the day the identified mixed straddle is established or its disposal; positions held the
   day before the election are deemed sold at FMV. Each year: mark the section 1256 contracts to market and determine the
   net; a net attributable to a non-section 1256 position is short-term and goes directly on Form 8949; a net
   attributable to a section 1256 position goes on Part I (`f6781.pdf p. 2`).
3. **Box C — mixed straddle account election** (Temp. Regs. 1.1092(b)-4T(f)): to establish accounts for **2026**, by the
   due date (without extensions) of the 2025 return, with the required statement; the account's annual net gain or loss
   is reported in Part II (`f6781.pdf p. 2`).
4. **Box D — net section 1256 contracts loss election**: see line 6.

### Part I — Section 1256 contracts marked to market

5. **Line 1** — one row per Form 1099-B with box 11 ("Form 1099-B" and the broker's name in column (a)), plus one row per
   transaction without a 1099-B; a gain in column (c), a loss in column (b) (`f6781.pdf p. 3`). A Schedule K-1 section
   1256 amount (Form 1065 box 11 code C, Form 1120-S box 10 code C) is also reported here: "The partnership will report
   any net gain or loss from section 1256 contracts. Report this amount on Form 6781" (`i1065sk1.pdf p. 16`; same
   wording for S corporations, `i1120ssk.pdf p. 8`). (An amended 2025
   form that receives a carryback from 2026 or later shows "Net section 1256 contracts loss carried back from" and the
   year in column (a), the amount in column (b).)
6. **Line 2** — "Add the amounts on line 1 in columns (b) and (c)" (`f6781.pdf p. 1`).
7. **Line 3** — "Net gain or (loss). Combine line 2, columns (b) and (c)".
8. **Line 4** — "Form 1099-B adjustments. See instructions and attach statement". When a Form 1099-B includes a straddle
   or hedging transaction, list each adjustment and enter the total (`f6781.pdf p. 3`):
   - the section 1256 contract part of a mixed straddle, if a mixed straddle election (A, B or C) was made (it is
     reported in Part II instead);
   - without such an election (or if the straddle was not identified as mixed), a loss on the section 1256 part that is
     less than the unrecognized gain on the non-section 1256 part — the amount of the loss; if the unrecognized gain is
     less than the loss, the unrecognized gain;
   - the section 1256 contract part of a hedging transaction ("treated as ordinary income or loss").
   "Don't use line 4 to show the adjustment for a QOF-related deferral."
9. **Line 5** — "Combine lines 3 and 4". "Note: If line 5 shows a net gain, skip line 6 and enter the gain on line 7"
   (`f6781.pdf p. 1`).
10. **Line 6** — "If you have a net section 1256 contracts loss and checked box D above, enter the amount of loss to be
    carried back. Enter the loss as a positive number. If you didn't check box D, enter -0-" (`f6781.pdf p. 1`). The
    box D rules (`f6781.pdf p. 3`):
    - Only individuals: "Corporations, estates, and trusts are not eligible to make this election."
    - "Your net section 1256 contracts loss is the smaller of: • The excess of your losses from section 1256 contracts
      over the total of (a) your gains from section 1256 contracts plus (b) $3,000 ($1,500 if married filing
      separately), or • The total you would figure as your short-term and long-term capital loss carryovers to 2026 if
      line 6 of Form 6781 were zero."
    - The amount carried to any prior year is limited to the smaller of the Schedule D line 16 gain of that year counting
      only section 1256 contracts, or that year's actual Schedule D line 16 gain, both figured before the carryback; it
      may not create or increase a net operating loss; "The loss is carried to the earliest year first."
    - The carryback is claimed on Form 1045 or an amended return with amended Forms 6781 and Schedule D for the
      carryback years.
11. **Line 7** — "Combine lines 5 and 6" (line 6 is positive, so it reduces the loss carried into this year).
12. **Line 8** — line 7 × 40%. Without a QOF deferral, "include this amount on Schedule D (Form 1040), line 4". With a
    QOF deferral, enter it in Form 8949 Part I with box B checked (all transactions on line 1 had a Form 1099-B) or box
    C, "Form 6781, Part I" in column (a) and the amount in column (h) (`f6781.pdf p. 3`).
13. **Line 9** — line 7 × 60% → **Schedule D line 11**; with a QOF deferral, Form 8949 Part II with box E or F
    (`f6781.pdf p. 3`).

### Part II — Gains and losses from straddles (`f6781.pdf p. 3–4`)

Section A (losses) is for positions that are part of a straddle: "Generally, a loss is allowed to the extent it exceeds
the unrecognized gain on offsetting positions. The part of the loss not allowed is treated as if incurred in the
following year and is allowed to the same extent." A loss from a position in an **identified** straddle established
after October 21, 2004 is not allowed at all; instead the basis of each offsetting position with unrecognized gain is
increased by the unallowed loss × (that position's unrecognized gain ÷ total unrecognized gain of all offsetting
positions).

14. **Line 10** — one row per loss position: (a) the property and delivery date, long or short position; (b) date
    entered into or acquired; (c) date closed out or sold; (d) closing or sales price; (e) cost or other basis plus
    commissions, nondeductible interest and carrying charges, and any basis increase from an unallowed identified-
    straddle loss; (f) loss = (e) − (d) if (e) is more, else 0, including "any loss not allowed in the prior year to the
    extent of the unrecognized gain"; (g) unrecognized gain on the offsetting positions (including those held by a
    related party) = settlement price at the close of the last business day of 2025 − basis; (h) recognized loss =
    (f) − (g) if (f) is more, else 0.
15. **Line 11a** — the short-term part of line 10 column (h) → **Schedule D line 4** (or Form 8949 Part I, box C,
    "Form 6781, Part II"). **Line 11b** — the long-term part → **Schedule D line 11** (or Form 8949 Part II, box F).
    Holding periods for straddle positions follow Pub. 550 and Temp. Regs. 1.1092(b)-2T; attach a statement for each.
16. **Line 12** — one row per gain position: columns (a)–(e) as line 10; (f) gain = (d) − (e) if (d) is more, else 0.
17. **Line 13a** — short-term part of line 12 (f) → **Schedule D line 4** (Form 8949 box C with a QOF deferral);
    **line 13b** — long-term part → **Schedule D line 11** (Form 8949 box F).
18. **Collectibles** — "If any of the gain or loss you reported in Part II is a collectibles gain or (loss) … If you
    checked 'Yes' on line 17 of Schedule D (Form 1040), include the collectibles gain or (loss) from Part II on line 3 of
    the 28% Rate Gain Worksheet" (`f6781.pdf p. 4`; `i1040sd.pdf p. 11`, worksheet line 3: "Form 6781, Part II").
19. **Statements** — separate statements for section 988 contracts in a mixed straddle and for gain on a conversion
    transaction; "Identify the net gain or loss and report it on Form 4797, line 10" (`f6781.pdf p. 4`).

### Part III — Unrecognized gains from positions held on the last day of the tax year (`f6781.pdf p. 4`)

20. **Line 14** — "listing each position (whether or not part of a straddle) that you held at the end of the tax year
    (including any position you are treated as holding because it is held by a related party) if the FMV of the position
    at such time exceeds your cost or other basis as adjusted": (a) description, (b) date acquired, (c) FMV on the last
    business day, (d) adjusted basis, (e) unrecognized gain = (c) − (d) if more, else 0. Excluded: identified straddle or
    hedging positions, inventory, depreciable business property. "Don't complete Part III if you don't have a recognized
    loss on any position (including section 1256 contracts)." It is a memo entry: nothing carries from it.

## 8. Married filing jointly / separately

Form 6781 has no special joint-return rule: one form for the return ("Name(s) shown on tax return", `f6781.pdf p. 1`),
listing every Form 1099-B of both spouses. On separate returns each spouse reports their own contracts, and the box D
threshold is **$1,500 instead of $3,000** (`f6781.pdf p. 3`). In a community property state each spouse filing
separately must "report half of all community income and all of your separate income" (`f8958.pdf p. 3`), so a gain on
contracts that are community property is divided in half between the two returns.

## 11. Worked example

**Pat Sample** (000-00-0031), single, trades futures at two brokers. No straddles, hedges or QOF investments.

| Broker | Box 8 | Box 9 (12/31/2024) | Box 10 (12/31/2025) | Box 11 = 8 − 9 + 10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Alpha Futures | 12,000 | (1,500) | 500 | 12,000 + 1,500 + 500 = **14,000** |
| Beta Clearing | (3,000) | 1,000 | 0 | −3,000 − 1,000 + 0 = **(4,000)** |

1. Line 1: "Form 1099-B Alpha Futures" 14,000 in column (c); "Form 1099-B Beta Clearing" 4,000 in column (b).
2. Line 2: (b) 4,000; (c) 14,000. Line 3 = 14,000 − 4,000 = **10,000**.
3. Line 4 = 0; line 5 = **10,000** (a gain, so line 6 is skipped); line 7 = **10,000**.
4. Line 8 = 40% × 10,000 = **4,000** → Schedule D line 4.
5. Line 9 = 60% × 10,000 = **6,000** → Schedule D line 11.
6. Parts II and III: none (no straddle and no recognized loss on any position).

*Loss variant with box D.* Pat's line 3 is instead **(15,000)** (losses 20,000, gains 5,000) and Pat has no other capital
items. Net section 1256 contracts loss = smaller of (20,000 − (5,000 + 3,000)) = 12,000 and the carryover to 2026 if
line 6 were 0 (15,000 − 3,000 allowed = 12,000) = **12,000**. If Pat checks box D and enters 12,000 on line 6:
line 7 = −15,000 + 12,000 = **(3,000)**; line 8 = (1,200); line 9 = (1,800). The 12,000 goes back to the earliest of the
three prior years, limited each year to that year's section-1256-only Schedule D line 16 gain, on Form 1045 or amended
returns. Without box D: line 6 = 0, line 7 = (15,000), line 8 = (6,000), line 9 = (9,000); Schedule D allows 3,000 in
2025 and carries 12,000 forward.
