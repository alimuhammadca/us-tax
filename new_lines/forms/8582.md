`C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\forms\8582.md`.

The 2025 form is quoted from `f8582.pdf` (Form 8582 (2025), "Created 5/8/25", in `C:\us-tax\docs\IRS-Forms\`); the
rules from the **2025 Instructions for Form 8582** (`i8582.pdf`, in `C:\us-tax\docs\books\`), with the Schedule E

## 1. At a glance

The **passive activity loss (PAL)** rules (Internal Revenue Code §469) stop losses from passive activities — rentals,
and businesses you do not materially participate in — from offsetting wages, interest, dividends and other nonpassive
income. Form 8582 combines all passive income and losses for the year, including **prior-year unallowed losses**:

- if the total is income or zero, every passive loss is allowed;
- if it is a loss, the loss is allowed only up to passive income, **plus** a special allowance of up to **$25,000** for
  rental real estate in which you **actively participated**, phased out between $100,000 and $150,000 of modified AGI;
- the rest is **unallowed**, allocated back to each loss activity, and carried to 2026.

```text
line 1d = 1a (rental RE w/ active participation, net income) − 1b (net loss) − 1c (prior-year unallowed)
line 2d = 2a (all other passive, net income)          − 2b (net loss) − 2c (prior-year unallowed)
line 3  = line 1d + line 2d (− prior-year unallowed CRD)            ≥ 0 → all losses allowed, stop
line 4  = smaller of the loss on line 1d or line 3                     (only if 1d is a loss)
line 7  = line 5 ($150,000; $75,000 MFS apart) − line 6 (MAGI)
line 8  = min(50% × line 7, $25,000; $12,500 MFS apart)
line 9  = min(line 4, line 8)                                          special allowance
line 10 = line 1a + line 2a
line 11 = line 9 + line 10                                             total losses allowed
unallowed = |line 3| − line 9, allocated by Parts VI–IX, carried forward
```

Terms used below:

- **Passive activity** — "Trade or business activities in which you did not materially participate for the tax year"
  and "Rental activities, regardless of your participation" (`i8582.pdf p. 1`).
- **Net income / net loss** of an activity — current-year income less deductions, *including* gains and losses on
  dispositions of its assets; **overall gain / overall loss** — net income or loss after the prior-year unallowed
  losses (`i8582.pdf p. 2`).
- **Prior-year unallowed losses** — losses disallowed by the PAL rules in an earlier year, carried to 2025 under
  §469(b) (`i8582.pdf p. 2`).
- **Active participation**, **material participation**, **real estate professional** — see section 7.
- **MAGI** (line 6) — modified adjusted gross income as defined for this form (section 6, line 6).
- **CRD** — commercial revitalization deduction; only prior-year unallowed CRDs remain (`i8582.pdf p. 1, 10`).
- **PTP** — publicly traded partnership; its passive items stay off Form 8582 (`i8582.pdf p. 15`).

## 2. The IRS wording

**Purpose** (`i8582.pdf p. 1`): "Form 8582 is used by noncorporate taxpayers to figure the amount of any passive
activity loss (PAL) for the current tax year and to report the application of prior-year unallowed PALs. A PAL occurs
when total losses (including prior-year unallowed losses) from all your passive activities exceed the total income
from all your passive activities. … PALs can't be used to offset income from nonpassive activities. However, a special
allowance for rental real estate activities may allow some losses even if the losses exceed passive income. PALs not
allowed in the current year are carried forward until they're allowed either against passive activity income; against
the special allowance, if applicable; or when you sell or exchange your entire interest in the activity in a fully
taxable transaction to an unrelated party."

**Who must file** (`i8582.pdf p. 1`): "Form 8582 is filed by individuals, estates, and trusts who have passive activity
deductions (including prior year unallowed losses)." Reminder: "Form 8582 must generally be filed by taxpayers who have
an overall gain (including any prior-year unallowed losses) from business or rental passive activities."

**Order of limits** (`i8582.pdf p. 2`): "Generally, PALs are subject to other limitations (for example, basis and at-risk
limitations) before they're subject to the passive loss limitations." Afterwards the excess business loss limit
(Form 461) applies (`i8582.pdf p. 1–2`).

**Rental activity** (`i8582.pdf p. 3`): "A rental activity is a passive activity even if you materially participated in
the activity (unless it's a rental real estate activity in which you materially participated and you were a real
estate professional)."

**Line 3** (`f8582.pdf p. 1`): "If this line is zero or more, stop here and include this form with your return; all
losses are allowed, including any prior year unallowed losses entered on line 1c or 2c. … If line 3 is a loss and:
• Line 1d is a loss, go to Part II. • Line 2d is a loss (and line 1d is zero or more), skip Part II and go to line 10.
Caution: If your filing status is married filing separately and you lived with your spouse at any time during the
year, do not complete Part II. Instead, go to line 10."

**Line 11** (`f8582.pdf p. 1`): "Total losses allowed from all passive activities for 2025. Add lines 9 and 10."

## 3. What goes on this form

- Every passive activity's current-year net income or net loss, **including** gains and losses from dispositions of its
  assets and of less than an entire interest (`i8582.pdf p. 2, 9`).
- Every passive activity's prior-year unallowed losses (from 2024 Form 8582 Part VII column (c) / Part VIII, IX)
  (`i8582.pdf p. 10`). "The partnership or S corporation doesn't have a record of your prior-year unallowed losses"
  (`i8582.pdf p. 7`).
- Activity types: Schedule E Part I rentals, Schedule E Part II / III passive K-1 activities, Schedule C and Schedule F
  businesses you did not materially participate in, Form 4835 farm rentals, Form 4797 / 8949 / 4684 Section B passive
  gains and losses (`i8582.pdf p. 3, 14–15`).
- A **former passive activity** (passive in an earlier year, not now): its prior-year unallowed loss is entered, with
  current net income only up to that loss (`i8582.pdf p. 8–9`).

## 6. How to figure it, line by line (2025 form, in order)

Complete **Parts IV and V first** ("Caution: Complete Parts IV and V before completing Part I", `f8582.pdf p. 1`).

### Part IV — rental real estate activities with active participation (for lines 1a–1c)

1. One line per activity. "Don't enter a prior-year unallowed loss in column (c) of Part IV unless you actively
   participated in the activity in both the year the loss arose and the current tax year. If you didn't actively
   participate in both years, enter the prior-year unallowed loss in column (c) of Part V." MFS filers who lived with
   their spouse at any time "don't qualify under the active participation rule and must use Part V instead of
   Part IV" (`i8582.pdf p. 10`).
2. **Column (a)** current-year net income (example: Schedule E profit $5,000 + Form 4797 gain $2,000 → $7,000);
   **(b)** current-year net loss (no prior-year losses; income on one form and a loss on another are entered
   separately in (a) and (b)); **(c)** prior-year unallowed losses; **(d) / (e)** overall gain / overall loss of each
   activity — used only for the allocation in Parts VI–IX (`i8582.pdf p. 10`).

### Part V — all other passive activities (for lines 2a–2c)

3. Passive trade or business activities, passive rental real estate without the special allowance (no active
   participation, limited partners, MFS lived together), and rental activities other than rental real estate; same
   columns (a)–(e) (`i8582.pdf p. 10`).

### Part I — 2025 passive activity loss

4. **Line 1a** = Part IV column (a) total. **1b** = column (b) total (a loss). **1c** = column (c) total (a loss).
   **1d** = 1a + 1b + 1c.
5. **Line 2a / 2b / 2c / 2d** — the same from Part V.
6. **Line 3** = 1d + 2d, minus any prior-year unallowed CRD (enter "CRD" and the amount on the dotted line)
   (`i8582.pdf p. 10`). Zero or more → stop; all losses allowed. Loss and 1d a loss → Part II. Loss and 1d zero or more
   → line 10. MFS lived together → line 10 (`f8582.pdf p. 1`).

### Part II — special allowance for rental real estate with active participation

All Part II amounts are positive numbers (`i8582.pdf p. 11`).

7. **Line 4** = smaller of the loss on line 1d or the loss on line 3 (example: 1d (3,000), 2d 100, line 3 (2,900) →
   2,900) (`i8582.pdf p. 11`).
8. **Line 5** = $150,000 ($75,000 if MFS and lived apart at all times) (`f8582.pdf p. 1`; `i8582.pdf p. 11`).
9. **Line 6 — MAGI**, not less than zero: "combine all the amounts used to figure adjusted gross income, except don't
   take into account: • Any passive activity loss as defined in section 469(d)(1), • Any rental real estate loss allowed
   to real estate professionals …, • The taxable amount of social security and tier 1 railroad retirement benefits,
   • Deductible contributions to traditional individual retirement accounts (IRAs) and section 501(c)(18) pension
   plans, • The deduction allowed for the deductible part of self-employment taxes, • The exclusion from income of
   interest from series EE and I U.S. savings bonds used to pay higher education expenses, • The exclusion of amounts
   received under an employer's adoption assistance program, • The student loan interest deduction, or • The deduction
   allowed for foreign-derived intangible income and global intangible low-taxed income." Include portfolio income and
   expenses, nonpassive income such as an overall PTP gain and recharacterized income, and "any overall loss from the
   entire disposition of a passive activity". Example: AGI $92,000 with taxable social security $5,500 → MAGI $86,500
   (`i8582.pdf p. 11`). If line 6 ≥ line 5, skip lines 7–8 and enter 0 on line 9 (`f8582.pdf p. 1`).
10. **Line 7** = line 5 − line 6.
11. **Line 8** = 50% of line 7, not more than $25,000 ($12,500 MFS lived apart) (`f8582.pdf p. 1`; `i8582.pdf p. 11`).
12. **Line 9** = smaller of line 4 or line 8 (example: line 4 $42,000, line 8 $25,000 → $25,000). With prior-year
    unallowed CRD, the CRD worksheet adds the unused allowance for the CRD (`i8582.pdf p. 11`).

### Part III — total losses allowed

13. **Line 10** = income on lines 1a + 2a.
14. **Line 11** = line 9 + line 10 — total losses allowed from all passive activities; "Use Parts IV through IX … to
    figure the unallowed loss to be carried forward and the allowed loss to report on your forms and schedules"
    (`i8582.pdf p. 11`).

### Parts VI–IX — allocating the allowed and unallowed loss to each activity

15. **Part VI** (only if line 9 is not zero): list every Part IV activity with an overall loss in column (e); column (a)
    its overall loss; (b) its ratio to the total; (c) ratio × line 9 (the special allowance); (d) = (a) − (c), carried to
    Part VII column (a). If (c) equals (a) in total, all Part IV losses are allowed in full (`i8582.pdf p. 12`).
16. **Part VII — allocation of unallowed losses**: column (a) = Part VI column (d) amounts (or Part IV column (e) when
    Part VI was skipped) plus Part V column (e) overall losses; (b) ratios; (c) = ratio × (|line 3| − line 9) — each
    activity's unallowed loss (`i8582.pdf p. 12–13`).
17. **Part VIII — allowed losses** (activity reported on one form): (a) net loss + prior-year unallowed loss; (b) the
    Part VII unallowed loss ("Keep a record of these amounts so the losses can be used to figure your PAL next year");
    (c) = (a) − (b), the allowed loss, reported on the normal form (`i8582.pdf p. 13`).
18. **Part IX** (activity with losses on two or more forms, or separately identified items such as 28% rate and other
    capital losses): line 1a loss per form, 1b income on the same form, 1c = 1a − 1b (not below zero); ratio; (d) =
    ratio × the activity's unallowed loss; (e) = 1a − (d), the allowed loss per form. Example: 28% rate loss $1,000 and
    other loss $3,000, unallowed $3,130 → unallowed $782.50 / $2,347.50, allowed $217.50 / $652.50 (`i8582.pdf p. 13–14`).

### Reporting the allowed loss (`i8582.pdf p. 14–15`)

- Line 3 income or zero, or line 11 = all losses (lines 1b + 1c + 2b + 2c), or Part VI (a) = (c): report every amount
  on its normal form.
- Schedule C / F / Form 4835: allowed loss on the net profit or loss line (Form 4835 line 34c), "PAL" to its left; a
  profitable activity is reduced by its allowed prior-year loss (example: Schedule C profit $5,000, Form 4797 gain
  $2,500, prior loss $6,000 → line 31 $(1,000) "PAL").
- **Schedule E Part I: allowed loss on line 22**; "An activity that has net profit for the year and prior-year unallowed
  losses will have net profit on line 21 and the allowed loss on line 22." Line 25 shows total losses allowed (passive
  and nonpassive); line 26 the net.
- **Schedule E Parts II / III:** passive income in its passive-income column; the allowed loss from Part VIII / IX in the
  passive-loss column.
- Form 4797 / 8949 / 4684: gains as "FPA"; allowed losses with "PAL"; entire dispositions "EDPA".

## 7. Special cases and elections

| Case | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Exception — no Form 8582 | Only actively participated rental real estate; no prior-year unallowed losses; total loss ≤ $25,000 ($12,500 MFS); MFS lived apart all year; no passive credits; MAGI ≤ $100,000 ($50,000 MFS); no limited-partner / beneficiary interest. Then Schedule E line 21 loss goes to line 22; K-1 loss to column (g); Form 4835 line 32 to 34c | `i8582.pdf p. 1–2` |
| Active participation | Significant and bona fide management decisions (approving tenants, rental terms, repairs); not if your interest (with your spouse's) was below 10% by value at any time; limited partners never; only individuals, qualifying estates and electing revocable trusts | `i8582.pdf p. 4` |
| Special allowance amounts | $25,000 single / MFJ; $12,500 MFS lived apart all year; none MFS lived together at any time; qualifying estate $25,000 less the surviving spouse's | `i8582.pdf p. 4` |
| MAGI phase-out | ≤ $100,000 full; $100,000–$150,000: 50% of (150,000 − MAGI); ≥ $150,000 none ($50,000 / $75,000 MFS) | `i8582.pdf p. 4` |
| Material participation tests | > 500 hours; substantially all participation; > 100 hours and not less than anyone else; significant participation activities > 500 hours in total; 5 of the last 10 years; personal service activity 3 prior years; facts and circumstances (> 100 hours) | `i8582.pdf p. 5` |
| Spouse's participation | Counts as yours, whether or not you file jointly | `i8582.pdf p. 5` |
| Limited partners | Generally not material participation except tests 1, 5 or 6 | `i8582.pdf p. 5` |
| Real estate professional | > half of personal services and > 750 hours in real property trades or businesses with material participation; MFJ: one spouse must meet both alone; each rental interest a separate activity unless the aggregation election is made | `i8582.pdf p. 2` |
| Former passive activity | Prior-year unallowed loss allowed to the extent of current net income from the activity; the rest stays suspended | `i8582.pdf p. 8–9` |
| Rental exceptions (not a rental activity) | Average use ≤ 7 days; ≤ 30 days with significant personal services; extraordinary services; incidental; business hours; provided to own partnership / S corporation → trade or business test | `i8582.pdf p. 3–4` |
| Recharacterization | Net income from significant participation activities, rentals with < 30% depreciable basis, equity-financed lending, rentals incidental to development, **rental to a nonpassive activity (self-rental)**, licensing entities → not passive income (Pub. 925; not in `docs\books`) | `i8582.pdf p. 8` |
| Self-charged interest | May be passive income / deduction (Reg. 1.469-7) | `i8582.pdf p. 8` |
| Entire disposition to an unrelated person, fully taxable | Losses of that activity not limited; overall loss → nonpassive (include in MAGI); overall gain → enter in Part IV / V; installment sale: allowed fraction = gain recognized / unrecognized gain at start of year; partial disposition does not free prior losses | `i8582.pdf p. 9` |
| PTP | Each PTP separately; its losses offset only its own income; no special allowance; overall gain is nonpassive income | `i8582.pdf p. 15–16` |
| Grouping | An appropriate economic unit; consistent; disclosure statements; NIIT regrouping | `i8582.pdf p. 5–7` |
| Estates / trusts as K-1 sources | Rules for beneficiaries "haven't yet been issued" | `i1041sk1.pdf p. 2` |

## 8. Married filing jointly / separately

- **Joint return:** one Form 8582 for both spouses; a single $25,000 allowance and one MAGI; real estate professional
  status if either spouse alone meets both tests; spouses' participation is combined for material participation
  (`i8582.pdf p. 2, 4, 5`).
- **Separate returns, lived apart all year:** $12,500 allowance, line 5 $75,000, phase-out from $50,000 (`i8582.pdf
  p. 4, 11`).
- **Separate returns, lived together at any time:** no special allowance; Part IV not used (losses go to Part V); Part
  II skipped (`f8582.pdf p. 1`; `i8582.pdf p. 10`).

## 10. Where the amount goes next

- Allowed losses (Part VIII column (c), Part IX column (e)) → **Schedule E line 22** (Part I), Schedule E Part II column
  (g), Part III column (c), Schedule C line 31, Schedule F line 34, **Form 4835 line 34c → Schedule E line 40**,
  Form 4797, Form 8949, Form 4684 (`i8582.pdf p. 14–15`).
- Through those: Schedule 1 lines 3, 5, 6 (and line 4) → Form 1040 line 8 → AGI.
- After Form 8582: Form 461 (excess business loss) (`i8582.pdf p. 2`).
- Unallowed losses → 2026 Form 8582 column (c).
- The passive income and allowed losses also shape Form 8960 (NIIT) and Form 6251 (a separate AMT Form 8582
  computation where AMT amounts differ).

## 11. Worked example

Lee Sample (000-00-0001), single, MAGI $110,000 (wages; no social security).

| Activity | 2025 | Prior-year unallowed |
|---|---|---|
| Rental house A (active participation) | net loss $(18,000) | $(4,000) |
| Rental condo B (active participation) | net income $3,000 | — |
| Limited partnership K-1 box 1 (passive) | net loss $(6,000) | — |
| S corporation K-1, did not materially participate | net income $2,000 | — |

1. Part IV: A (b) 18,000, (c) 4,000 → (e) 22,000; B (a) 3,000 → (d) 3,000. **1a 3,000; 1b (18,000); 1c (4,000);
   1d (19,000).**
2. Part V: LP (b) 6,000 → (e) 6,000; S corp (a) 2,000 → (d) 2,000. **2a 2,000; 2b (6,000); 2d (4,000).**
3. **Line 3 = (23,000).** Line 1d is a loss → Part II.
4. Line 4 = smaller of 19,000 and 23,000 = 19,000. Line 5 150,000. Line 6 110,000. Line 7 40,000. Line 8 20,000.
   **Line 9 = 19,000.**
5. **Line 10 = 5,000. Line 11 = 24,000** allowed.
6. Part VI: only A has an overall loss in Part IV (22,000); ratio 1.00; special allowance 19,000; (d) 3,000.
7. Part VII: A 3,000, LP 6,000; total 9,000; line C = 23,000 − 19,000 = 4,000. Unallowed: A 4,000 × 3/9 = 1,333;
   LP 4,000 × 6/9 = 2,667.
8. Part VIII: A (a) 22,000 − 1,333 = **20,667 allowed** (Schedule E line 22); LP (a) 6,000 − 2,667 = **3,333 allowed**
   (Schedule E line 28 column (g)). Condo B and the S corporation report their income in full.
9. Check: allowed losses 20,667 + 3,333 = 24,000 = line 11. Carried to 2026: 1,333 (A) + 2,667 (LP) = 4,000.
