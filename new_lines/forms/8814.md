## 1. At a glance

A parent can choose (elect) to report a child's interest and dividends on the parent's own return instead of filing a
return for the child. One Form 8814 is filed per child. The first $1,350 of the child's interest and dividends is not
taxed; the next $1,350 is taxed at 10% (at most $135, added to the parent's Form 1040 line 16); everything above
$2,700 is added to the parent's income — split between qualified dividends (Form 1040 lines 3a and 3b), capital gain
distributions (Schedule D line 13 or Form 1040 line 7a) and the rest (Schedule 1 line 8z). The election is available
only when the child's gross income is under $13,500 and comes only from interest and dividends.

```text
line 4  = 1a (taxable interest) + 2a (ordinary dividends) + 3 (capital gain distributions)
          line 4 >= 13,500 -> the form cannot be used; the child files a return
          line 4 <=  2,700 -> skip lines 5-12
line 6  = line 4 - 2,700
line 7  = 2b / line 4          line 9  = line 6 x line 7   -> Form 1040 lines 3a and 3b
line 8  = 3  / line 4          line 10 = line 6 x line 8   -> Schedule D line 13 (or Form 1040 line 7a)
line 11 = line 9 + line 10     line 12 = line 6 - line 11  -> Schedule 1 line 8z
line 14 = max(0, line 4 - 1,350)
line 15 = line 14 < 1,350 ? 10% x line 14 : 135            -> Form 1040 line 16 (box 1 "8814")
```

Terms used below:

- **Election** — a choice the taxpayer makes; here, by attaching Form 8814 to a timely return. Without the
  election the child files his or her own return (and the child's tax may be figured on Form 8615).
- **Qualified dividends** — ordinary dividends taxed at the lower capital-gain rates (Form 1099-DIV box 1b).
- **Capital gain distributions** — mutual-fund distributions of long-term gain (Form 1099-DIV box 2a).
- **Nominee** — someone who receives income that belongs to another person; the nominee part is left out.
- **Base amount** — the $2,700 on line 5; the part of the child's income kept off the parent's income lines.
- **Full-time student** — enrolled full time "for some part of each of 5 calendar months during the year"
  (`i8814.pdf p. 1`).

## 2. The IRS wording

**Form 8814 (2025)** (`f8814.pdf p. 1`, `docs\IRS-Forms\`): title "Parents' Election To Report Child's Interest and
Dividends"; "Attach to parents' Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR." Caution box: "The federal income tax on your child's
income, including qualified dividends and capital gain distributions, may be less if you file a separate tax return
for the child instead of making this election."

**Purpose of form** (`i8814.pdf p. 1`):

> "Use this form if you elect to report your child's income on your return. If you do, your child will not have to
> file a return. You can make this election if your child meets all of the following conditions.
> • The child was under age 19 (or under age 24 if a full-time student) at the end of 2025. …
> • The child's only income was from interest and dividends, including capital gain distributions and Alaska
>   Permanent Fund dividends.
> • The child's gross income for 2025 was less than $13,500.
> • The child is required to file a 2025 return.
> • The child does not file a joint return for 2025.
> • There were no estimated tax payments for the child for 2025 (including any overpayment of tax from his or her
>   2024 return applied to 2025 estimated tax).
> • There was no federal income tax withheld from the child's income."

> "Note: The amounts at $1,350 and below are not taxed when using this election."

**Certain January 1 birthdays** (`i8814.pdf p. 1`): "A child born on January 1, 2007, is considered to be age 19 at
the end of 2025. You cannot make this election for such a child unless the child was a full-time student. A child
born on January 1, 2002, is considered to be age 24 at the end of 2025. You cannot make this election for such a
child." (Repeated in `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 8`.)

**How to make the election** (`i8814.pdf p. 1`): "complete and attach Form(s) 8814 to your tax return and file your
return by the due date (including extensions). A separate Form 8814 must be filed for each child whose income you
choose to report on your return. You can make the election for one or more children and not for others."

**Rate may be higher** (`i8814.pdf p. 2`): "you may pay up to $135 more tax if you make this election instead of
filing a separate tax return for the child. … To figure the most beneficial tax on your child's income, first figure
the tax on your child's income as if they are filing a return. Next, figure the tax as if you are electing to report
your child's income on your return. Then, compare the methods to determine which results in the lower tax."

**Line 12 / line 15 with several forms** (`i8814.pdf p. 4`): "Line 12. If you checked the box on line C, add the
amounts from line 12 of all your Forms 8814. Include the result on Schedule 1 (Form 1040), line 8z. Enter "Form 8814"
and the total of the line 12 amounts in the space on that line." "Line 15. … add the amounts from line 15 of all your
Forms 8814. Include the total on Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR, line 16. Be sure to check box 1 on Form 1040,
1040-SR, or 1040-NR, line 16."

## 3. What goes on this form

- Taxable interest of the child (Forms 1099-INT and 1099-OID), less nominee interest and nontaxable adjustments
  (accrued interest paid to a seller, amortizable bond premium, OID adjustment) — line 1a (`i8814.pdf p. 2`).
- Tax-exempt interest, including exempt-interest dividends from a mutual fund — line 1b only (`i8814.pdf p. 2–3`).
- Ordinary dividends (Form 1099-DIV box 1a), **including Alaska Permanent Fund dividends** (form line 2a caption) and
  dividends received "through a partnership, an S corporation, or an estate or trust" — line 2a (`i8814.pdf p. 3`).
- The qualified part of line 2a (Form 1099-DIV box 1b) — line 2b (`i8814.pdf p. 3`).
- Capital gain distributions (Form 1099-DIV box 2a), including their 28% / §1250 / §1202 parts — line 3
  (`i8814.pdf p. 3`).

## 4. What does not go here

| Look-alike amount | Where it goes instead | Source |
|---|---|---|
| The child's wages or any other non-investment income | The election is unavailable; the child files a return | `i8814.pdf p. 1` |
| A child with gross income of $13,500 or more | The child's own return ("Your child must file his or her own return"), usually with Form 8615 | `f8814.pdf` line 4; `i8814.pdf p. 1` |
| Nominee interest / dividends / capital gain distributions | Left out of lines 1a, 2a, 3 ("ND" on the dotted line) | `i8814.pdf p. 2–3` |
| The child's qualified dividends (line 2b) themselves | Not on the parent's line 3a; only line 9 goes there | `i8814.pdf p. 3` |
| Line 9 / line 10 amounts | Not in line 12 or Schedule 1 line 8z ("Don't include these dividends on Form 8814, line 12; or Schedule 1 (Form 1040), line 8z") | `i8814.pdf p. 3` |
| The child's capital gains from sales (Form 1099-B) | Not interest or dividends — the election is unavailable | `i8814.pdf p. 1` ("only income was from interest and dividends, including capital gain distributions") |
| Deductions the child could take (blind child's $2,000 additional standard deduction, early-withdrawal penalty, the child's itemized deductions) | Lost under the election | `i8814.pdf p. 2` |
| A child who files his or her own return (Form 8615) | No Form 8814 for that child | `i8814.pdf p. 1`; `p17.pdf p. 57` |

## 6. How to figure it, step by step (every line of the 2025 form)

**Before the form — eligibility.** Check every condition in section 2 (`i8814.pdf p. 1`) and that **you** are the
parent who may elect (section 7). If any fails, stop: the child files a return.

**Heading.** "Name(s) shown on your return" and "Your social security number" — on a joint return "enter both names
but enter the SSN of the person whose name is shown first on the return" (`i8814.pdf p. 2`).

- **Line A** — child's name (first, initial, last).
- **Line B** — child's social security number.
- **Line C** — "If more than one Form 8814 is attached, check here" (`f8814.pdf`). Checking it means lines 12 and 15
  are totalled across all the forms (`i8814.pdf p. 4`).

**Part I — Child's Interest and Dividends To Report on Your Return**

1. **Line 1a** — the child's taxable interest. Leave out nominee interest (write the amount and "ND" on the dotted
   line) and nontaxable amounts ("Accrued interest", "ABP adjustment", "OID adjustment") (`i8814.pdf p. 2`).
2. **Line 1b** — the child's tax-exempt interest, including exempt-interest dividends. "Do not include this amount on
   line 1a" (`f8814.pdf`). Line 1b is not used in any later line of the form. If it includes private activity bond
   interest, the parent takes it into account for the AMT (`i8814.pdf p. 2–3`; `i6251.pdf p. 4`).
3. **Line 2a** — ordinary dividends, "including any Alaska Permanent Fund dividends", and dividends received through
   a partnership, S corporation, estate or trust; less nominee dividends (`f8814.pdf`; `i8814.pdf p. 3`).
4. **Line 2b** — the qualified dividends included on line 2a (`i8814.pdf p. 3`).
5. **Line 3** — capital gain distributions, less nominee distributions (`i8814.pdf p. 3`).
6. **Line 4** — add lines 1a, 2a and 3. "If the total is $2,700 or less, skip lines 5 through 12 and go to line 13.
   If the total is $13,500 or more, do not file this form. Your child must file his or her own return to report the
   income." (`f8814.pdf`).
7. **Line 5** — base amount, printed **$2,700**.
8. **Line 6** — line 4 minus line 5. Instruction printed after line 6: "If both lines 2b and 3 are zero or blank, skip
   lines 7 through 10, enter -0- on line 11, and go to line 12. Otherwise, go to line 7."
9. **Line 7** — line 2b ÷ line 4, "as a decimal (rounded to at least three places)".
10. **Line 8** — line 3 ÷ line 4, same rounding.
11. **Line 9** — line 6 × line 7. Goes on Form 1040 lines 3a **and** 3b (and Schedule B line 5 as "Form 8814" if
    Schedule B is filed) (`i8814.pdf p. 4`).
12. **Line 10** — line 6 × line 8. Goes on Schedule D line 13 or, if Schedule D is not required, Form 1040 line 7a
    (`i8814.pdf p. 3–4`).
13. **Line 11** — line 9 + line 10.
14. **Line 12** — line 6 minus line 11. "Include this amount in the total on Schedule 1 (Form 1040), line 8z. In the
    space next to that line, enter "Form 8814" and show the amount." With line C checked, total line 12 of all forms
    (`f8814.pdf`; `i8814.pdf p. 4`).

**Part II — Tax on the First $2,700 of Child's Interest and Dividends**

15. **Line 13** — amount not taxed, printed **$1,350**.
16. **Line 14** — line 4 minus line 13; "If the result is zero or less, enter -0-".
17. **Line 15** — "Tax. Is the amount on line 14 less than $1,350? No. Enter $135 here … Yes. Multiply line 14 by 10%
    (0.10)." Then: "include the amount from line 15 in the tax you enter on Form 1040 … line 16. Be sure to check box 1"
    (`f8814.pdf`). With line C checked, total line 15 of all forms (`i8814.pdf p. 4`).

Rounding: the 2025 Form 1040 rounding rule applies to the dollar lines (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 23`); lines 7 and 8
are decimals of at least three places (the IRS example rounds to two: 0.78 / 0.22, `i8814.pdf p. 3`).

## 7. Special cases and elections

| Case | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Which parent may elect — married filing jointly | The joint return | `i8814.pdf p. 1` |
| Married, separate returns | Only the parent with the higher taxable income | `i8814.pdf p. 1` |
| Unmarried / separated / divorced | The custodial parent (child lived with you most of the year); if remarried and filing separately from the new spouse, only if your taxable income is higher | `i8814.pdf p. 1–2` |
| Unmarried parents living together all year | The parent with the higher taxable income | `i8814.pdf p. 1–2` |
| Custodial or surviving parent remarried | The stepparent is the "other parent"; a joint return with the stepparent is used; never the noncustodial parent | `i8814.pdf p. 2` |
| Child's 28% / §1250 / §1202 capital gain distributions | Line 10 × (that part ÷ total distributions) goes to the 28% Rate Gain Worksheet line 4 / Unrecaptured §1250 Gain Worksheet line 11 / §1202 exclusion | `i8814.pdf p. 3` |
| AMT | Child's private activity bond interest is a parent's AMT preference (Form 6251 line 2g) | `i8814.pdf p. 2`; `i6251.pdf p. 4` |
| Net investment income tax | "All income reported on line 12 is included in the parents' modified adjusted gross income"; all net investment income on line 12 (except Alaska Permanent Fund dividends) is the parents' NII — Form 8960 line 7 | `i8814.pdf p. 2`; `i8960.pdf p. 13` |
| Investment interest expense | The child's income other than qualified dividends, Alaska Permanent Fund dividends and capital gain distributions is the parent's investment income (Form 4952 line 4a) | `i8814.pdf p. 2`; `f4952--2025.pdf p. 3` |
| Earned income credit | Form 8814 filers use Pub. 596 Worksheet 1 for the investment-income test | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 40` |
| Foreign accounts or a foreign trust of the child | Parent completes Schedule B Part III, "Form 8814" next to line 7a or 8 | `i8814.pdf p. 2` |
| Reduced deductions and credits | The higher AGI can reduce the IRA deduction, student loan interest, Schedule 1-A deductions, medical / SALT / casualty, child care, child tax, education credits and the EIC | `i8814.pdf p. 2` |
| Estimated tax | The parent may owe an underpayment penalty; plan withholding for 2026 | `i8814.pdf p. 2` |

## 8. Married filing jointly / separately

- **Joint return:** one joint return makes the election; Form 8814 shows both names and the SSN of the first-named
  spouse (`i8814.pdf p. 1–2`).
- **Separate returns:** only the parent with the greater taxable income may elect (`i8814.pdf p. 1`); the child's
  income must not be on both returns.
- The $1,350 / $2,700 / $13,500 amounts are per child, not per return.

The 2025 Form 1040 has no dotted line next to lines 3a / 3b; the line 3c checkboxes ("Check if your child's dividends
are included in 1 Line 3a 2 Line 3b") take the place of the "Form 8814 — $" write-in the instructions describe
(`f1040.pdf` 2025, `docs\IRS-Forms\`). Whether line 1b is also entered on the parent's Form 1040 line 2a is **not
stated** in any 2025 IRS document available here (see `new_lines\2a.md`).

## 11. Worked example

Pat and Sam Example (000-00-0001 / 000-00-0002) file jointly. Their daughter Mia (000-00-0003, age 10) received only:
a Form 1099-INT with $1,000 interest; a Form 1099-DIV with $3,000 ordinary dividends of which $2,000 qualified, and
$1,000 capital gain distributions (no box 2b–2d amounts). No withholding, no estimated payments. Mia's gross income is
$5,000 (under $13,500); her parents elect.

| Line | Figure | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| 1a | interest | 1,000 |
| 1b | tax-exempt | 0 |
| 2a / 2b | ordinary / qualified | 3,000 / 2,000 |
| 3 | capital gain distributions | 1,000 |
| 4 | 1,000 + 3,000 + 1,000 | 5,000 |
| 5 | base | 2,700 |
| 6 | 5,000 − 2,700 | 2,300 |
| 7 | 2,000 ÷ 5,000 | 0.400 |
| 8 | 1,000 ÷ 5,000 | 0.200 |
| 9 | 2,300 × 0.400 | 920 → Form 1040 lines 3a and 3b |
| 10 | 2,300 × 0.200 | 460 → Schedule D line 13 (or 7a) |
| 11 | 920 + 460 | 1,380 |
| 12 | 2,300 − 1,380 | 920 → Schedule 1 line 8z "Form 8814" |
| 13 | not taxed | 1,350 |
| 14 | 5,000 − 1,350 | 3,650 |
| 15 | 3,650 is not less than 1,350 | 135 → Form 1040 line 16, box 1 |

The parents' total income rises by $920 + $460 + $920 = $2,300 (= line 6), and their tax by $135 plus the tax on the
$2,300 at their own rates. The IRS's own example (Fred, $2,190 qualified dividends and $610 capital gain
distributions) gives line 9 = $78 and line 10 = $22 (`i8814.pdf p. 3–4`).
