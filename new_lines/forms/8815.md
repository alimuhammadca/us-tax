# Form 8815 — Exclusion of Interest From Series EE and I U.S. Savings Bonds Issued After 1989

Tax year 2025. What the IRS requires. How the app does it: `C:\us-tax\new_knowledge\forms\8815.md`. Picture:
`C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\forms\8815.md`. The interest it reduces is documented in `C:\us-tax\new_lines\2b.md`; the
schedule it feeds in `forms\schedule-b.md`.

---

## 1. At a glance

When you cash series EE or I U.S. savings bonds issued after 1989 and, in the same year, pay qualified higher
education expenses for yourself, your spouse or your dependents, part or all of the bonds' interest can be left out
of income. Form 8815 figures that part. The exclusion is the bond interest times the share of the bond proceeds
covered by the expenses, reduced proportionally across a modified-AGI phase-out band, and zero at or above the top
of the band. The result (line 14) goes on Schedule B line 3 and so reduces Form 1040 line 2b. Not available when
married filing separately.

```text
line 4  = qualified expenses (line 2) − nontaxable educational benefits (line 3)     (≤ 0 → stop)
line 7  = 1.000 if line 4 ≥ proceeds (line 5), else line 4 ÷ line 5                   (≥ 3 decimals)
line 8  = bond interest (line 6) × line 7
line 9  = modified AGI (Line 9 Worksheet)               (≥ $114,500 S/HOH/QSS, ≥ $179,250 MFJ → stop)
line 11 = max(0, line 9 − line 10)        line 10 = $99,500 S/HOH/QSS, $149,250 MFJ
line 12 = line 11 ÷ $15,000 (S/HOH/QSS) or $30,000 (MFJ)                              (≥ 3 decimals)
line 13 = line 8 × line 12                (0 when line 11 is 0; line 12 skipped)
line 14 = line 8 − line 13                →  Schedule B line 3  →  reduces Form 1040 line 2b
```

Terms used below:

- **Series EE / I bond** — U.S. savings bonds sold by the Treasury; interest accrues and is normally reported when
  the bond is cashed (redeemed).
- **Proceeds** — what you received on cashing the bonds: principal (what was paid) plus interest.
- **Qualified higher education expenses** — tuition and required fees at an eligible educational institution, plus
  contributions to a Coverdell education savings account (ESA) or a qualified tuition program (QTP, a "529 plan").
- **Modified AGI (MAGI)** — adjusted gross income figured before this exclusion, with certain deductions and foreign
  / possession / adoption exclusions added back (line 9).

## 2. The IRS wording

**Purpose** (`f8815.pdf p. 3`): "If you cashed series EE or I U.S. savings bonds in 2025 that were issued after 1989,
you may be able to exclude from your income part or all of the interest on those bonds. Use this form to figure the
amount of any interest you may exclude."

**Who can take the exclusion** (`f8815.pdf p. 3`): "You can take the exclusion if all four of the following apply.
1. You cashed qualified U.S. savings bonds in 2025 that were issued after 1989. 2. You paid qualified higher education
expenses in 2025 for yourself, your spouse, or your dependents. 3. Your filing status is any status except married
filing separately. 4. Your modified adjusted gross income (AGI) is less than $114,500 if single, head of household,
or qualifying surviving spouse; or $179,250 if married filing jointly."

**Bonds that qualify** (`f8815.pdf p. 3`): "the bonds must be series EE or I U.S. savings bonds issued after 1989 in
your name, or, if you are married, they may be issued in your name and your spouse's name. Also, you must have been
age 24 or older before the bonds were issued. A bond bought by a parent and issued in the name of his or her child
under age 24 does not qualify for the exclusion by the parent or child." Pub. 17 adds: "The issue date of a bond may
be earlier than the date the bond is purchased because the issue date assigned to a bond is the first day of the
month in which it is purchased" and "If you claim the exclusion, the IRS will check it by using bond redemption
information from the Department of the Treasury" (`p17.pdf p. 61`).

**Line 2** (`f8815.pdf p. 3`): "Qualified higher education expenses include tuition and fees required for the
enrollment or attendance of the person(s) listed on line 1, column (a), at the institution(s) listed in column (b).
They also include contributions to a Coverdell ESA or a QTP for the person(s) listed on line 1. Qualified expenses do
not include expenses for: • Room and board; or • Courses involving sports, games, or hobbies that are not part of a
degree- or certificate-granting program. Do not include on line 2 expenses that were: • Covered by nontaxable
educational benefits paid directly to, or by, the educational institution; • Used to figure an education credit on
Form 8863; or • Used to figure the nontaxable amount of a distribution from a Coverdell ESA or QTP."

**Line 3** (`f8815.pdf p. 3`): "Enter on this line the total qualified higher education expenses included on line 2
that were covered by nontaxable educational benefits." — scholarships and fellowships excludable under section 117,
veterans' educational assistance, employer-provided educational assistance not in W-2 box 1, QTP payments / waivers /
reimbursements, and other tax-exempt payments (not gifts, bequests or inheritances). "Do not include on line 3
nontaxable educational benefits paid directly to, or by, the educational institution."

**Line 9** (`f8815.pdf p. 4`): five preparatory steps, then the Line 9 Worksheet. Step 5: "If you claim any of the
exclusion or deduction items listed below, you must add them to your AGI … 1. Foreign earned income exclusion.
2. Foreign housing exclusion and deduction. 3. Exclusion of income for bona fide residents of American Samoa.
4. Exclusion for income from Puerto Rico. 5. Exclusion for adoption benefits received under an employer's adoption
assistance program." Footnote: "For purposes of figuring the amount to put on Form 8815, line 9, do not reduce your
educator expenses, if any, by the amount on Form 8815, line 14."

**Line 14** (`f8815.pdf p. 1`): "Excludable savings bond interest. Subtract line 13 from line 8. Enter the result here
and on Schedule B (Form 1040), line 3."

## 3. What goes on this form

- Each person (you, your spouse, a dependent you claim) enrolled at or attending an eligible institution, or for whom
  you contributed to a Coverdell ESA or QTP, with the institution's (or account's) name and address (line 1).
- Tuition and required fees and Coverdell / QTP contributions paid in 2025 for those people (line 2).
- Tax-free benefits covering those expenses (line 3).
- Total proceeds and the interest part of every post-1989 series EE and I bond cashed in 2025 (lines 5 and 6).
- Your modified AGI (line 9).

## 4. What does not go here

| Item | Where it goes instead / why | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Series EE bonds issued before 1990, series E / H / HH bonds | Not qualified; interest stays taxable on Schedule B line 1 | `f8815.pdf p. 3` |
| Bonds in a child's name (or owner under 24 at issue) | Not qualified for parent or child | `f8815.pdf p. 3`; `p17.pdf p. 61` |
| Room and board, hobby / sports courses | Not qualified expenses | `f8815.pdf p. 3` |
| Expenses used for the American opportunity / lifetime learning credit | Form 8863 — may not be counted again on line 2 | `f8815.pdf p. 3`; `p17.pdf p. 61` |
| Expenses used to make a Coverdell ESA / QTP distribution tax-free | Form 5329 / Pub. 970 — may not be counted again | `f8815.pdf p. 3` |
| Benefits paid directly to or by the institution | Left out of both line 2 and line 3 | `f8815.pdf p. 3` |
| Treasury bill / note / bond interest | Taxable on Schedule B line 1; not savings bonds | `p17.pdf p. 61` |

## 6. How to fill it in, line by line

1. **Check eligibility first.** Filing status not MFS; bonds are post-1989 EE or I, in your (or your and your
   spouse's) name, bought when you were at least 24; expenses for you, your spouse or a dependent you claim
   (`f8815.pdf p. 3`).
2. **Line 1.** Column (a): name of each person who was enrolled at or attended an eligible educational institution
   (or for whom you contributed to a Coverdell ESA / QTP) — "This person must be you, your spouse, or your
   dependent(s) claimed on Form 1040". Column (b): institution name and address; list all institutions; for a
   Coverdell ESA write "Coverdell ESA" and the financial institution, for a QTP "QTP" and the program
   (`f8815.pdf p. 3`). Attach a statement if more space is needed.
3. **Line 2.** Total qualified higher education expenses paid in 2025 for the line 1 people, after leaving out the
   three excluded kinds (section 2).
4. **Line 3.** Total of line 2 expenses covered by nontaxable educational benefits.
5. **Line 4.** Line 2 − line 3. "If zero or less, stop. You cannot take the exclusion" (`f8815.pdf p. 1`).
6. **Line 5.** Total proceeds (principal and interest) of all post-1989 series EE and I bonds cashed in 2025.
7. **Line 6.** The interest included on line 5 (`f8815.pdf p. 3`):
   - No interest reported in earlier years for these bonds: Form 8818 line 8, or the Line 6 Worksheet through
     worksheet line 6.
   - Some interest reported in earlier years: the whole Line 6 Worksheet, result from worksheet line 8.
   - **Line 6 Worksheet:** (1) Form 8815 line 5; (2) face value of post-1989 **paper** series EE bonds cashed;
     (3) line 2 × 50%; (4) face value of all **electronic** EE bonds (including paper converted to electronic) and
     all series I bonds cashed; (5) lines 3 + 4; (6) line 1 − line 5 — stop here if no interest was reported earlier;
     (7) interest reported as income in earlier years; (8) line 6 − line 7.
8. **Line 7.** If line 4 ≥ line 5 enter "1.000"; otherwise line 4 ÷ line 5 "as a decimal (rounded to at least three
   places)".
9. **Line 8.** Line 6 × line 7.
10. **Line 9.** Modified AGI. First complete the steps on `f8815.pdf p. 4`: taxable Social Security (Pub. 915),
    the PTC / self-employed health insurance coordination (Pub. 974), the IRA deduction (Pub. 590-A), and Form 1040
    lines 2a, 3b-6b, 7a and Schedule 1 lines 1-9, 11-20 and 23-25. Then the **Line 9 Worksheet**:
    1. Schedule B line 2 (interest **before** this exclusion);
    2. Form 1040 lines 1z, 3b, 4b, 5b, 6b, 7a + Schedule 1 lines 1 through 7 and 9;
    3. lines 1 + 2;
    4. Schedule 1 lines 11 through 20, 23 and 25 — note that **line 21 (student loan interest deduction) is not
       subtracted**;
    5. line 3 − line 4; then **increase** it by Form 2555 lines 45 and 50, Form 4563 line 15, any excluded
       employer-provided adoption benefits and any excluded Puerto Rico income. The total is line 9.
    If you had interest expense on royalty income (Schedule E), figure the deductible interest on a "dummy" Form 4952
    that includes all EE / I bond interest in net investment income, use it only for this MAGI, and do not attach
    it (`f8815.pdf p. 4`, step 6). Do not reduce educator expenses by line 14 for this purpose (footnote).
    **Note:** if line 9 is $114,500 or more (single, head of household, qualifying surviving spouse) or $179,250 or
    more (married filing jointly), stop — no exclusion.
11. **Line 10.** $99,500 (single, head of household, qualifying surviving spouse) or $149,250 (married filing
    jointly).
12. **Line 11.** Line 9 − line 10. "If zero or less, skip line 12, enter -0- on line 13, and go to line 14."
13. **Line 12.** Line 11 ÷ $15,000 (single, HOH, QSS) or $30,000 (MFJ), "rounded to at least three places".
14. **Line 13.** Line 8 × line 12.
15. **Line 14.** Line 8 − line 13. Enter here and on **Schedule B line 3**.

## 7. Special cases and elections

| Case | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Married filing separately | No exclusion | `f8815.pdf p. 3`; `p17.pdf p. 61` |
| Bond in a child's name / owner under 24 at issue | Not qualified for parent or child | `f8815.pdf p. 3` |
| Interest reported yearly (method 2) before cashing | Line 6 Worksheet lines 7-8 remove the already-reported interest | `f8815.pdf p. 3` |
| Mix of paper EE and electronic EE / I bonds | Line 6 Worksheet: paper EE face × 50% + electronic / I face = principal | `f8815.pdf p. 3` |
| Coordination with education credits and Coverdell / QTP | The same expenses cannot be used twice | `f8815.pdf p. 3`; `p17.pdf p. 61` |
| Foreign earned income / housing, American Samoa, Puerto Rico, employer adoption exclusions | Added back to MAGI | `f8815.pdf p. 4` |
| Royalty interest expense | Special MAGI computation with a dummy Form 4952 | `f8815.pdf p. 4` |
| Educator expenses | Reduced by the line 14 exclusion on Schedule 1 line 11 — but not for figuring line 9 | `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 93`; `f8815.pdf p. 4` |
| Other MAGI worksheets | The Social Security worksheet and the IRA-deduction worksheet use Schedule B line 2 / Pub. 915 or Pub. 590-A when Form 8815 is filed | `p17.pdf p. 67`; `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 31, 96` |
| Records | Keep expense receipts and, per bond, serial number, issue date, face value and total proceeds (Form 8818 may serve) | `f8815.pdf p. 3` |

## 8. Married filing jointly / separately

- **Joint return:** one Form 8815; bonds may be in either spouse's name or both; expenses of either spouse or a
  dependent count; MFJ amounts ($149,250 start, $30,000 band, $179,250 ceiling).
- **Separate returns:** not allowed at all.
- **Qualifying surviving spouse** uses the **single** amounts ($99,500 / $15,000 / $114,500), not the joint ones
  (`f8815.pdf p. 1, 3`).

## 10. Where the amount goes next

- **Line 14 → Schedule B line 3** → reduces Schedule B line 4 → **Form 1040 line 2b** → line 9 → AGI.
- Schedule 1 line 11: qualified educator expenses are reduced by the excludable interest (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 93`).
- MAGI worksheets that must add the exclusion back or start from Schedule B line 2: Social Security Benefits Worksheet
  (`p17.pdf p. 67`), IRA deduction (Pub. 590-A, `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 96`), and the other MAGI tests that list
  Form 8815.

## 11. Worked example

Robin Sample (000-00-0041), single, cashed post-1989 series EE bonds in 2025 and paid tuition for Robin's dependent
son Casey (000-00-0042) at State College. No interest on the bonds was reported in earlier years. Casey received a
$1,000 tax-free scholarship paid to him (not to the college). No education credit was claimed on these expenses.

| Line | Figure | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Casey Sample — State College, 1 College Way, Springfield | — |
| 2 | Tuition and required fees | $8,000 |
| 3 | Nontaxable scholarship | $1,000 |
| 4 | $8,000 − $1,000 | $7,000 |
| 5 | Bond proceeds (principal $6,000 + interest $4,000) | $10,000 |
| 6 | Interest included on line 5 | $4,000 |
| 7 | $7,000 ÷ $10,000 | 0.700 |
| 8 | $4,000 × 0.700 | $2,800 |
| 9 | Line 9 Worksheet: Schedule B line 2 $5,000 + wages $100,000 + dividends $500 − Schedule 1 adjustments $0 | $105,500 |
| 10 | Single | $99,500 |
| 11 | $105,500 − $99,500 | $6,000 |
| 12 | $6,000 ÷ $15,000 | 0.400 |
| 13 | $2,800 × 0.400 | $1,120 |
| 14 | $2,800 − $1,120 | **$1,680** |

Schedule B line 2 $5,000, **line 3 $1,680**, line 4 = Form 1040 line 2b = **$3,320**.
