## 1. At a glance

A **like-kind exchange** (section 1031) is a trade of real property held for business or investment for other real
property of a like kind. "Generally, if you exchange business or investment real property solely for business or
investment real property of a like kind, section 1031 provides that no gain or loss is recognized. If, as part of the
exchange, you also receive other (non-like-kind) property or money, gain is recognized to the extent of the other
property and money received, but a loss isn't recognized." (`i8824.pdf p. 2`). Form 8824 figures the **realized
gain**, the part that must be **recognized** now (the "boot"), the **deferred gain**, and the **basis** of the property
received. Since 2018 only real property qualifies.

```text
Part III
  14 = 12 − 13                       (other property given up: gain / loss reported as a sale)
  15 = cash + FMV other property received + net liabilities assumed by other party − exchange expenses  (≥ 0)
  17 = 15 + 16                       (16 = FMV of like-kind property received)
  18 = adjusted basis given up + exchange expenses not used on 15 + net amount paid
  19 = 17 − 18                       (realized gain or loss)
  20 = max(0, min(15, 19))
  21 = ordinary income under the recapture rules        → Form 4797 line 16
  22 = max(0, 20 − 21)               → Schedule D (capital asset) or Form 4797 line 5 / 16 (business property)
  23 = 21 + 22                       (recognized gain)
  24 = 19 < 0 ? 19 : 19 − 23         (deferred gain or loss)
  25 = 18 + 23 − 15                  (basis of like-kind property received; 25a / 25b / 25c split by FMV)
Part IV (section 1043): 32 = 30 − 31; 34 = max(0, 30 − 33); 36 = max(0, 34 − 35); 37 = 32 − (35 + 36); 38 = 33 − 37
```

Terms used below:

- **Like-kind property** — "Properties are of like kind if they are of the same nature or character, even if they
  differ in grade or quality. Generally, real properties are like-kind properties, regardless of whether they are
  improved or unimproved properties." "However, real property in the United States and real property outside the
  United States aren't like-kind properties." (`i8824.pdf p. 2`).
- **Boot** — the cash, other (not like-kind) property and net debt relief received; it is what line 15 measures.
- **Deferred exchange** — "the property received in the exchange is received after the transfer of the property given
  up" (`i8824.pdf p. 2`).
- **Qualified intermediary (QI)** — a third party who holds the proceeds in a deferred exchange; "Related parties and
  agents of the taxpayer aren't eligible to be QIs, and are referred to as 'disqualified persons.'" (`i8824.pdf p. 2`).
- **Related party** — "your spouse, child, grandchild, parent, grandparent, brother, sister, or a related corporation,
  S corporation, partnership, trust, estate, or tax-exempt organization. See section 1031(f)." (`i8824.pdf p. 4`).
- **Multi-asset exchange** — "the transfer and receipt of more than one group of like-kind properties. The transfer or
  receipt of multiple properties within one like-kind group is also a multi-asset exchange." (`i8824.pdf p. 3`).

## 2. The IRS wording

Form header (`f8824.pdf p. 1`): "Like-Kind Exchanges (and section 1043 conflict-of-interest sales). Attach to your tax
return." Part I note: "Only real property should be described on lines 1 and 2. If the property described on line 1 or
line 2 is real property located outside the United States, indicate the country."

**Purpose** (`i8824.pdf p. 1`): "Use Parts I, II, and III of Form 8824 to report each exchange of business or investment
real property for real property of a like kind. Form 8824 figures the amount of gain deferred as a result of a like-kind
exchange. Use Part III to figure the amount of gain required to be reported on the tax return in the current year if
cash or property that isn't of a like kind is involved in the exchange. Also, use Part III to figure the basis of the
like-kind property received."

**Real property only** (`i8824.pdf p. 1`): "For 2018 and later years, section 1031 like-kind exchange treatment applies
only to exchanges of real property held for use in a trade or business or for investment, other than real property
held primarily for sale."

**When to file** (`i8824.pdf p. 1`): "If during the current tax year you transferred property to another party in a
like-kind exchange, you must file Form 8824 with your tax return for that year. Also file Form 8824 for the 2 years
following the year of a related party exchange."

**Timing** (`i8824.pdf p. 2`): "The replacement property for the exchange must be identified within 45 days after the
property being given up is transferred. The replacement property must be received within 180 days, or by the due date
of your tax return (including extensions), whichever is earlier."

**Line 22** (`i8824.pdf p. 5`): "Report a gain from the exchange of property used in a trade or business (and other
noncapital assets) on Form 4797, line 5 or line 16. Report a gain from the exchange of capital assets according to the
Schedule D (Form 1040) instructions for your tax return. Be sure to use the date of the exchange as the date for
reporting the gain. If the installment method applies to this exchange, see section 453(f)(6) …"

## 6. How to figure it, line by line

### Part I — Information on the Like-Kind Exchange (`f8824.pdf p. 1`; `i8824.pdf p. 3–4`)

1. **Line 1** — "Description of like-kind property given up". "Enter the address and type of property. For property
   that is treated as real property for like-kind exchange purposes, but doesn't have an address, enter a short
   description. If … real property located outside the United States, indicate the country."
2. **Line 2** — "Description of like-kind property received" (same rules).
3. **Line 3** — "Date like-kind property given up was originally acquired". Decides the holding period of a recognized
   gain.
4. **Line 4** — "Date you actually transferred your property to the other party". It is "the date of the exchange" used
   for reporting the gain (`i8824.pdf p. 5`) and starts the 45- and 180-day periods.
5. **Line 5** — date the replacement was "identified by written notice to another party". The 45-day rule requires a
   written designation signed by you (or a written agreement signed by all parties), describing the property clearly
   (legal description, street address or distinguishable name), sent or signed "No later than 45 days after the date
   you transferred the property you gave up". "If you received the replacement property before the end of the 45-day
   period, you are automatically treated as having met the 45-day written identification requirement. In this case,
   enter on line 5 the date you received the replacement property." (`i8824.pdf p. 3–4`).
6. **Line 6** — date received; "The property must be received by the earlier of … The 180th day after the date you
   transferred the property given up … The due date (including extensions) of your tax return for the year in which
   you transferred the property given up." (`i8824.pdf p. 4`).
7. **Line 7** — "Was the exchange of the property given up or received made with a related party, either directly or
   indirectly (such as through an intermediary)? … If 'Yes,' complete Part II. If 'No,' go to Part III." An indirect
   exchange includes one "with a related party through an intermediary (such as a QI or an EAT …)" or "made by a
   disregarded entity … if you or a related party owned that entity" (`i8824.pdf p. 4`). Note on the form: "Do not file
   this form if a related party sold property into the exchange, directly or indirectly …; that property became your
   replacement property; and none of the exceptions on line 11 applies to the exchange. Instead, report the
   disposition of the property as if the exchange had been a sale." (`f8824.pdf p. 1`).

### Part II — Related Party Exchange Information (`f8824.pdf p. 1`; `i8824.pdf p. 4`)

8. **Line 8** — related party's name, relationship, identifying number and address.
9. **Line 9** — "During this tax year (and before the date that is 2 years after the last transfer of property that was
   part of the exchange), did the related party sell or dispose of any part of the like-kind property received from
   you (or an intermediary) in the exchange?"
10. **Line 10** — the same question for "you".
    "If both lines 9 and 10 are 'No' and this is the year of the exchange, go to Part III. If both lines 9 and 10 are
    'No' and this is not the year of the exchange, stop here. If either line 9 or line 10 is 'Yes,' complete Part III and
    report on this year's tax return the deferred gain or (loss) from line 24 unless one of the exceptions on line 11
    applies." "Tolling of holding period. The running of the 2-year holding period will be tolled for any period during
    which your risk of loss is substantially reduced." (`i8824.pdf p. 4`).
11. **Line 11** — exceptions: (a) "The disposition was after the death of either of the related parties"; (b) "an
    involuntary conversion, and the threat of conversion occurred after the exchange"; (c) "You can establish to the
    satisfaction of the IRS that neither the exchange nor the disposition had tax avoidance as one of its principal
    purposes. If this box is checked, attach an explanation." Tax avoidance is generally not a principal purpose for a
    disposition in a nonrecognition transaction, an exchange with no tax advantage from shifting basis, or an exchange
    of undivided interests (`i8824.pdf p. 4`). With an exception: "check the applicable box on line 11, attach any
    required explanation, and stop."

### Part III — Realized Gain or (Loss), Recognized Gain, and Basis of Like-Kind Property Received (`f8824.pdf p. 2`)

Caution on the form: "If you transferred and received (a) more than one group of like-kind properties, or (b) cash or
other (not like-kind) property, see Reporting of multi-asset exchanges". Instructions: in that case "don't complete
lines 12 through 18 of Form 8824. Instead, attach your own statement showing how you figured the realized and
recognized gain, and enter the correct amount on lines 19 through 25." (`i8824.pdf p. 3`). (An exchange of a single
piece of land, a vehicle and cash for land and a vehicle is not a multi-asset exchange.)

12. **Lines 12 / 12a / 13 / 14** — "Complete lines 12 through 14 only if you gave up property that was not like-kind."
    Line 12 FMV of the other property given up; 12a its description; 13 its adjusted basis; 14 = 12 − 13, "Report the
    gain or (loss) in the same manner as if the exchange had been a sale" (`f8824.pdf p. 2`; `i8824.pdf p. 4`).
13. **Line 15 / 15a** — the sum of "Any cash paid to you by the other party; The FMV of other (non-like-kind) property you
    received, if any; and Net liabilities assumed by the other party—the excess, if any, of liabilities (including
    mortgages) assumed by the other party over the total of (a) any liabilities you assumed, (b) cash you paid to the
    other party, and (c) the FMV of the other (non-like-kind) property you gave up." "Reduce the sum of the above
    amounts (but not below zero) by any exchange expenses you incurred." Recourse debt is assumed by the party that
    agreed to and is expected to pay it; nonrecourse debt by the party receiving the property subject to it
    (`i8824.pdf p. 4`). 15a describes the other property received.
14. **Line 16** — "FMV of like-kind property you received".
15. **Line 17** — line 15 + line 16.
16. **Line 18** — "the sum of: The adjusted basis of the like-kind real property you gave up; Exchange expenses, if any
    (except for expenses used to reduce the amount reported on line 15); and The net amount paid to the other party—the
    excess, if any, of the total of (a) any liabilities you assumed, (b) cash you paid to the other party, and (c) the
    FMV of the other (non-like-kind) property you gave up over any liabilities assumed by the other party."
    (`i8824.pdf p. 4`).
17. **Line 19** — realized gain or (loss) = 17 − 18. A section 121 exclusion is written beside it, not subtracted
    (`i8824.pdf p. 1, 5`).
18. **Line 20** — "Generally, enter the smaller of line 15 or line 19, but not less than zero" (`i8824.pdf p. 5`).
19. **Line 21** — "Ordinary income under recapture rules. Enter here and on Form 4797, line 16." (`f8824.pdf p. 2`):
    - "Section 1245 real property. Enter the smaller of: 1. The total adjustments for deductions … allowed or allowable …
      for depreciation or amortization (up to the amount of gain shown on line 19); or 2. The gain shown on line 20, if
      any, plus the FMV of non-section 1245 like-kind property received."
    - "Section 1250 property. Enter the smaller of: 1. The gain you would have had to report as ordinary income because
      of additional depreciation if you had sold the property (see the Form 4797 instructions for line 26); or 2. The
      larger of: a. The gain shown on line 20, if any; or b. The excess, if any, of the gain in (1) above over the FMV of
      the section 1250 property received."
    - Sections 1252, 1254, 1255: "similar to those for section 1245 property" (`i8824.pdf p. 5`).
    - With the installment method: Form 6252 lines 25 / 36 and Form 4797 line 15 instead (`i8824.pdf p. 5`).
20. **Line 22** — line 20 − line 21, not below zero; "If more than zero, enter here and on Schedule D or Form 4797,
    unless the installment method applies." (`f8824.pdf p. 2`). Business property → **Form 4797 line 5** (section 1231,
    held more than 1 year) or **line 16** (ordinary); capital asset → **Schedule D line 4 / 11**; "use the date of the
    exchange as the date for reporting the gain" (`i8824.pdf p. 5`).
21. **Line 23** — recognized gain = 21 + 22.
22. **Line 24** — "If line 19 is a loss, enter it on line 24. Otherwise, subtract the amount on line 23 from the amount on
    line 19 and enter the result. For exchanges with related parties, see Line 7" (`i8824.pdf p. 5`).
23. **Line 25** — basis of like-kind property received = 18 + 23 − 15. "Your basis in other property (non-like-kind)
    received in the exchange, if any, is its FMV." **25a** section 1250 property, **25b** sections 1245 / 1252 / 1254 /
    1255 property, **25c** like-kind intangible property: "Amounts entered on lines 25a, 25b, and 25c must be
    proportionate to their FMVs." (`i8824.pdf p. 6`).

### Part IV — Deferral of Gain From Section 1043 Conflict-of-Interest Sales (`f8824.pdf p. 2`; `i8824.pdf p. 6–7`)

Only for an eligible federal executive-branch officer or employee or federal judicial officer (and spouse, minor or
dependent children, trustees) selling under a certificate of divestiture, and only "if the cost of the replacement
property is more than the basis of the divested property and you elect to defer the gain".

24. **Line 26** — certificate number (keep the certificate; do not attach). **27 / 28** — descriptions. **29** — date
    sold.
25. **Line 30** — "the amount you received from the sale of the divested property, minus any selling expenses".
    **31** — basis. **32** — realized gain = 30 − 31.
26. **Line 33** — cost of replacement property (U.S. obligations or OGE-approved diversified funds) bought within 60 days.
    **34** = max(0, 30 − 33).
27. **Line 35** — ordinary recapture, figured on a Form 4797 Part III worksheet (line 31), "Report the amount from line
    35 on Form 4797, line 10, in column (g). In column (a), enter 'From Form 8824, line 35.'"
28. **Line 36** — max(0, 34 − 35): capital asset → Schedule D; business property → Form 4797 line 2 or 10 ("From Form
    8824, line 36").
29. **Line 37** — deferred gain = 32 − (35 + 36). **Line 38** — basis of replacement property = 33 − 37.

## 8. Married filing jointly / separately

Form 8824 is one form per exchange (or one summary form), with the "Name(s) shown on tax return" (`f8824.pdf p. 1`).
On a joint return, each spouse's exchanges are reported on the joint return. On separate returns, each spouse reports
the exchanges of property they owned. The related-party list includes "your spouse" (`i8824.pdf p. 4`). Part IV
"Eligible persons" includes "any spouse, minor, or dependent child whose ownership of any property is attributable to
such an officer or employee" (`i8824.pdf p. 6`). The instructions set no per-person dollar limits.

## 11. Worked example

**Jordan Sample** (000-00-0041), single, exchanges a rental duplex (held since 2014, a section 1231 property) for an
apartment building on 03/10/2025; the replacement was identified on 04/01/2025 and received on 06/20/2025. Straight-line
depreciation of 100,000 (cost 250,000, adjusted basis 150,000); no section 1245 parts. The other party takes over
Jordan's 100,000 mortgage; Jordan takes over the 120,000 mortgage on the apartment building (FMV 380,000) and receives
40,000 cash. Exchange expenses 5,000. Not a related party.

| Line | Figure |
|---|---|
| 1 / 2 | Duplex, 12 Example St / Apartment building, 40 Sample Ave |
| 3 / 4 / 5 / 6 | 05/01/2014 / 03/10/2025 / 04/01/2025 (22 days) / 06/20/2025 (102 days) |
| 7 | No |
| 15 | cash 40,000 + net liabilities assumed by other party max(0, 100,000 − 120,000) = 0 → 40,000 − expenses 5,000 = **35,000** |
| 16 / 17 | 380,000 / 415,000 |
| 18 | 150,000 + 0 (expenses all used on 15) + net paid (120,000 − 100,000 = 20,000) = **170,000** |
| 19 | 415,000 − 170,000 = **245,000** realized |
| 20 | min(35,000, 245,000) = **35,000** |
| 21 | 0 (no section 1245 parts; straight-line section 1250 property has no additional depreciation) |
| 22 / 23 | **35,000** / 35,000 → Form 4797 line 5 (business property held more than 1 year) |
| 24 | 245,000 − 35,000 = **210,000** deferred |
| 25 / 25a | 170,000 + 35,000 − 35,000 = **170,000**, all section 1250 property |

On Form 4797 the 35,000 joins the section 1231 netting; with no other section 1231 items and no prior losses it is a
long-term capital gain on Schedule D line 11, and all of it is unrecaptured section 1250 gain (it is less than the
100,000 of straight-line depreciation).

**Related party, later year:** if Jordan had exchanged with a sibling in 2024 and the sibling sold the duplex in 2025
(within 2 years, no line 11 exception), Jordan files Form 8824 for 2025 with Parts I–III and reports the 2024 line 24
deferred gain on the 2025 return "as if the exchange had been a sale" (`i8824.pdf p. 4`).
