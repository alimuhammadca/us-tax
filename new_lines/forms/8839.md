# Form 8839 — Qualified Adoption Expenses

Tax year 2025. What the IRS requires. How the app does it: `C:\us-tax\new_knowledge\forms\8839.md`. Picture:
`C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\forms\8839.md`. Part III (employer-provided adoption benefits → Form 1040 line 1f) is
documented in depth in `C:\us-tax\new_lines\1f.md`; this file covers the whole form with the weight on Parts I and
II (the credit) and summarizes Part III.

The 2025 form is not in `C:\us-tax\docs\books\`; its captions are quoted from `C:\us-tax\docs\IRS-Forms\f8839.pdf`
(Form 8839 (2025), "Created 9/2/25"), cited as `f8839.pdf`.

---

## 1. At a glance

Form 8839 does two jobs for each **eligible child** you adopted or tried to adopt. **Part II** figures the
**adoption credit**: up to $17,280 per child of qualified adoption expenses (a child with special needs whose
adoption became final in 2025 gets the full amount even without expenses), reduced proportionally when modified AGI
is between $259,190 and $299,190. **New for 2025, up to $5,000 per child of that credit is refundable** (Form 1040
line 30); the rest is nonrefundable (Schedule 3 line 6c), limited by your tax, with any unused part carried forward
up to five years. **Part III** figures how much of your employer's adoption benefits (W-2 box 12 code T) is tax-free;
the taxable rest (or a negative adjustment) goes on Form 1040 line 1f. Part III must be done before Part II, because
expenses the employer paid are not credit expenses. **Part I** lists the children.

```text
Per child (Part II):
  line 4   = $17,280 (or your agreed share) − line 3 (expenses used for this child on an earlier Form 8839)
  line 5   = qualified expenses for 2025 (timing rules; special needs final in 2025: $17,280 − line 3)
  line 6   = min(line 4, line 5)
  line 10  = line 6 × line 9            line 9 = (MAGI − $259,190) / $40,000, ≥ 3 decimals, ≤ 1.000
  line 11a = line 6 − line 10           line 11b = min(line 11a, $5,000)
Return totals:
  line 11c = Σ 11b → line 13 refundable credit                    → Form 1040 line 30
  line 12  = Σ 11a;  line 14 = max(0, 12 − 13);  line 16 = 14 + line 15 (carryforward from prior years)
  line 18  = min(line 16, line 17 Credit Limit Worksheet)          → Schedule 3 line 6c
  carryforward to 2026 = line 16 − line 18 (Carryforward Worksheet, 5-year life, stays nonrefundable)
Part III (lines 19–31)                                            → Form 1040 line 1f (see new_lines\1f.md)
```

Terms used below:

- **Eligible child** — a child under 18 (for the part of the year under 18), or a person of any age physically or
  mentally unable to care for themselves.
- **U.S. child / foreign child** — a citizen or resident of the United States or its territories when the adoption
  effort began / a child who was not.
- **Child with special needs** — a U.S. child for whom a state or (from 2025) Indian tribal government determined
  the child cannot return to the parents' home and will not be adopted without assistance.
- **Qualified adoption expenses** — reasonable and necessary adoption fees, attorney fees, court costs, travel
  (meals, lodging) and foreign re-adoption costs, minus anything reimbursed or otherwise excluded.
- **QAAP** — a qualified adoption assistance program: a separate written employer plan.
- **MAGI** — modified adjusted gross income (line 7 for the credit; line 25 for the exclusion — they differ).

## 2. The IRS wording

**What's New** (`i8839.pdf p. 1`):

> "Up to $5,000 of adoption credit is refundable. Up to $5,000 of your adoption credit may be refundable. The amount
> of the refundable credit portion is determined separately for each eligible child."
>
> "2025 maximum credit. The maximum credit (the total of the nonrefundable and refundable portion) and the exclusion
> for employer-provided benefits are both $17,280 per eligible child in 2025. This amount begins to phase out if you
> have modified adjusted gross income in excess of $259,190 and is completely phased out for modified adjusted gross
> income of $299,190 or more."

**Adoption credit** (`i8839.pdf p. 1`): "Use Form 8839, Part II, to figure the nonrefundable adoption credit you can
take on Schedule 3 (Form 1040), line 6c, and the refundable adoption credit you can take on Form 1040, line 30. You
may be able to take this credit in 2025 if any of the following statements are true." — (1) expenses for an eligible
U.S. child paid in 2024 with the adoption not final at the end of 2024, or paid in 2025 with the adoption final in
or before 2025; (2) a U.S. child with special needs whose adoption became final in 2025 ("even if you didn't pay any
qualified adoption expenses"); (3) expenses for a foreign child paid in 2025 or earlier with the adoption final in
2025, or paid in 2025 with the adoption final before 2025; (4) "You have a carryforward of an adoption credit from
2024. Any amount of the carryforward credit from 2024 remains a nonrefundable credit."

**Order of the parts** (`i8839.pdf p. 1`): "Qualified Adoption Expenses don't include expenses reimbursed by an
employer under a written qualified adoption assistance program … For this reason, you must complete Form 8839,
Part III, before you can figure the credit, if any, in Part II."

**Who can take the credit** (`i8839.pdf p. 2`): filing status single, HOH, QSS or MFJ; MAGI less than $299,190 or a
carryforward; Part I completed. **Married persons not filing jointly** may take it only if "You lived apart from your
spouse during the last 6 months of 2025. • The eligible child lived in your home more than half of 2025. • You
provided over half the cost of keeping up your home." and "a person who is filing separately may claim an adoption
credit carryforward from a prior year or years, provided that, if the person was married in the year in which the
qualified adoption expenses first became allowable for the credit, the person filed a joint return for that year."

**Qualified adoption expenses exclude** (`i8839.pdf p. 2`): expenses "For which you received funds under any state,
local, or federal program; That violate state or federal law; For carrying out a surrogate parenting arrangement;
For the adoption of your spouse's child; Reimbursed by your employer or otherwise; or Allowed as a credit or
deduction under any other provision".

**Form 8839 (2025) Part II captions** (`f8839.pdf p. 1`): line 2 "Maximum adoption credit per child. Enter
$17,280."; line 3 "Did you file Form 8839 for a prior year for the same child?"; line 5 "Qualified adoption expenses.
See instructions … Caution: Your qualified adoption expenses may not be equal to the adoption expenses you paid in
2025."; line 9 "Divide line 8 by $40,000. Enter the result as a decimal (rounded to at least three places). Do not
enter more than 1.000"; line 11b "Enter the smaller of the amount on line 11a or $5,000"; line 13 "Refundable
adoption credit. Enter the amount from line 11(c) here and on Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR, line 30."; line 15
"Credit carryforward, if any, from prior years"; line 18 "Nonrefundable adoption credit. Enter the smaller of line 16
or line 17 here and on Schedule 3 (Form 1040), line 6c. If line 17 is smaller than line 16, you may have a credit
carryforward."

**Form 1040 line 30** (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 61`): "If you are eligible to claim the refundable portion of the
adoption credit, enter on line 30 the amount from Form 8839, line 13. You may also be eligible to claim a
nonrefundable adoption credit on Schedule 3, line 6c."

## 3. What goes on this form

- Part I: every eligible child you adopted or tried to adopt (several attempts for one child are combined on one
  line, `i8839.pdf p. 4`), with year of birth, disabled / special-needs / foreign checks, identifying number (SSN,
  ATIN or ITIN; blank if none yet) and whether the adoption became final in 2025 or earlier.
- Part II: qualified adoption expenses not reimbursed by an employer or a government program, in the year the timing
  rules assign them; the special-needs deemed amount; your prior-year credit carryforward.
- Part III: W-2 box 12 code T and other employer-provided adoption benefits (see `new_lines\1f.md`).

## 4. What does not go here

| Look-alike amount | Where it goes instead | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Expenses your employer paid or reimbursed under a QAAP | Part III (exclusion), never also Part II | `i8839.pdf p. 1-2`, examples 1–3 |
| Expenses for adopting your spouse's child; surrogacy; amounts paid with government funds | Nowhere (not qualified) | `i8839.pdf p. 2` |
| 2025 expenses for a U.S. adoption not final at end of 2025 | 2026 Form 8839 | `i8839.pdf p. 3`, p. 7 example |
| Expenses for a foreign adoption not final at end of 2025 | Year the adoption becomes final | `i8839.pdf p. 3, p. 5` |
| Expenses above the $17,280 per-child limit | Never claimable | `i8839.pdf p. 2` example 4; p. 8 example |
| Carryforward from prior years | Line 15 (nonrefundable only) | `i8839.pdf p. 1, p. 8` |

## 6. How to fill it in, line by line

**Part I, line 1 (`i8839.pdf p. 3-7`).** One row per eligible child; more than three children → more Forms 8839,
"See Attached" by the caution, per-child lines on each and return-level lines (7–9, 11c, 12–18; 23, 25–27, 30, 31)
on only one form with the combined totals (`i8839.pdf p. 4`). Column (c) born before 2008 and disabled; (d) child
with special needs (keep the determination); (e) foreign child — if checked, also check (g); (f) SSN / ATIN / ITIN;
(g) adoption final in 2025 or earlier. Use the same Child 1 / 2 / 3 line as in the prior year (`i8839.pdf p. 4`).
Foreign finality year: Rev. Proc. 2005-31 (non-Hague) / 2010-31 (Hague) choices (`i8839.pdf p. 5`).

**Line 2.** $17,280 per child; if you and someone other than your joint-return spouse adopted the same child, your
agreed share (`i8839.pdf p. 7`).

**Line 3.** "If you filed Form 8839 for a prior year for the same child, enter on line 3 the total of the amounts
shown on lines 3 and 6 (or corresponding line) of the last form you filed for the child." (`i8839.pdf p. 7`).

**Line 4.** Line 2 − line 3.

**Line 5 (`i8839.pdf p. 7`).** Qualified adoption expenses paid in: "2024 if the adoption wasn't final by the end of
2025, • 2024 and 2025 if the adoption became final in 2025, or • 2025 if the adoption became final before 2025."
Foreign child final in 2025: the total paid in 2025 and all prior years (`i8839.pdf p. 5`). Employer-reimbursed
expenses never enter. Special needs final in 2025: "$17,280, minus … the amount you entered on line 3 … even if you
didn't have any qualified adoption expenses". Unsuccessful U.S. attempt: treat as not final (the 2025 part "may
qualify in 2026").

**Line 6.** Smaller of line 4 and line 5.

**Line 7 — MAGI for the credit (`i8839.pdf p. 7`).** Form 1040 line 11b plus the Puerto Rico exclusion, Form 2555
lines 45 and 50, and Form 4563 line 15. (Not the line 25 worksheet: "Your MAGI for the adoption credit may not be the
same as the MAGI figured in the above worksheet", `i8839.pdf p. 9`.)

**Lines 8–9.** If line 7 ≤ $259,190: line 8 and 9 skipped, line 10 = 0. Otherwise line 8 = line 7 − $259,190;
line 9 = line 8 ÷ $40,000, at least three decimals, at most 1.000.

**Line 10.** Each child's line 6 × line 9.

**Line 11a.** Line 6 − line 10 (per child).

**Line 11b.** Smaller of line 11a and $5,000 (per child).

**Line 11c.** Total of line 11b.

**Line 12.** Total of line 11a.

**Line 13 — refundable credit.** Line 11c → Form 1040 line 30 (`f8839.pdf p. 1`; `i8839.pdf p. 7`: "You will figure
the refundable credit amount for each eligible child on lines 11a and 11b, add the credit amounts for a total on
line 11c, then report the total refundable credit amount on line 13").

**Line 14.** Line 12 − line 13; if zero or less, -0-.

**Line 15.** Carryforward from prior years (2024 Form 8839 instructions' carryforward worksheet). Always
nonrefundable.

**Line 16.** Line 14 + line 15.

**Line 17 — Credit Limit Worksheet (`i8839.pdf p. 7`).** (1) line 16; (2) Form 1040 line 18; (3) Form 1040 line 19
(or Schedule 8812 Credit Limit Worksheet B line 14 when directed there) plus Schedule 3 lines 1 through 4, 5b, 6d,
6f, 6g, 6l and 6m; (4) = 2 − 3; (5) smaller of 1 and 4 → line 17.

**Line 18.** Smaller of line 16 and line 17 → Schedule 3 line 6c.

**Carryforward to 2026 (`i8839.pdf p. 7-8`).** "If Form 8839, line 17, is smaller than line 16, you may have an
unused nonrefundable credit to carry forward to the next 5 years or until used". The Nonrefundable Adoption Credit
Carryforward Worksheet ages the unused credit by year of origin (2021–2025 rows; oldest used first; the 2020 row
drops off) to give line 23, the total carryforward to 2026. "Unused nonrefundable credit can't be turned into a
refundable credit in a future year."

**Part III, lines 19–31 (summary — full rules in `new_lines\1f.md`).** Per child: 19 $17,280 or share; 20 prior-year
benefits (lines 20 + 24 of the last form); 21 = 19 − 20; 22 benefits received in 2025; 24 = smaller of 21 and 22
(special needs final in 2025 with a QAAP: line 21). Return: 23 = Σ 22; 25 MAGI from the line 25 worksheet (line 1z
as if line 23 were excluded, + line 23 + other income − most adjustments, + foreign / territory exclusions); 26–27
phase-out fraction; 28 = 24 × 27; 29 = 24 − 28; 30 = Σ 29; 31 = 23 − 30 (negative allowed) → Form 1040 line 1f.
Foreign child final in 2025 with prior-year benefits: Exclusion of Prior Year Benefits Worksheet (`i8839.pdf p. 6`).

## 7. Special cases and elections

| Case | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| U.S. child, adoption not final | Credit for an expense the year after it is paid; exclusion in the year paid | `i8839.pdf p. 3` |

| Unsuccessful U.S. attempt | Treated as not final; all attempts for one child combined | `i8839.pdf p. 4, p. 7` |
| Foreign child | No credit or exclusion until final; expenses of all years in the finality year | `i8839.pdf p. 3, p. 5` |
| Child with special needs final in 2025 | Line 5 = $17,280 − line 3, even with no expenses | `i8839.pdf p. 3-4, p. 7` |
| Special-needs determination by an Indian tribal government | Recognized from 2025 | `i8839.pdf p. 1, p. 3` |
| Shared adoption with a non-spouse | Split $17,280 any way agreed (line 2; separately line 19) | `i8839.pdf p. 7-8` |
| More than three children | Extra forms, totals on one | `i8839.pdf p. 4` |
| Credit above the tax limit | Carry forward 5 years, nonrefundable | `i8839.pdf p. 7-8` |
| Refundable portion | $5,000 per child, figured per child after the phase-out | `i8839.pdf p. 7` |
| MFS | Only if lived apart, child in your home > ½ year, you paid > ½ the home's cost; carryforward allowed if a joint return was filed for the origin year | `i8839.pdf p. 2` |
| More-than-2% S corporation owner | Cannot exclude employer benefits (Part III); the expenses may then be credit expenses | `i8839.pdf p. 1` |

## 8. Married filing jointly / separately

- **Joint return:** one Form 8839; each child once; one $17,280 limit per child (not per spouse); expenses of both
  spouses combined (`i8839.pdf p. 2, p. 7`).
- **Separate return:** no credit unless the three tests are met; a carryforward may still be claimed under the
  joint-return-in-origin-year rule (`i8839.pdf p. 2`).

## 10. Where the amount goes next

- **Line 13 → Form 1040 line 30** → line 32 (total other payments and refundable credits) → line 33 → refund or
  amount owed.
- **Line 18 → Schedule 3 line 6c** → Schedule 3 line 7 → line 8 → Form 1040 line 20 → line 21 → line 22.
- Schedule 3 line 6c is one of the credits that trigger Schedule 8812 Credit Limit Worksheet B, and later credit
  worksheets (for example Form 5695 Part I) subtract it.
- **Line 31 → Form 1040 line 1f** (see `new_lines\1f.md`); line 30 is an add-back in the Social Security benefits
  worksheet and the IRA deduction MAGI.
- The unused nonrefundable credit (carryforward worksheet line 23) → 2026 Form 8839 line 15.

## 11. Worked example

**A. The IRS example (`i8839.pdf p. 8`).** Morgan Example (000-00-0021) and Casey Example (000-00-0022), MFJ, adopt
a U.S. child, Jamie (ATIN 900-00-0023), not special needs; the adoption became final in 2025. They paid $20,000 of
qualified adoption expenses in 2025, nothing earlier, no employer benefits, no carryforward. AGI (= MAGI) $150,000.
Form 1040 line 18 tax $10,000; no other credits, no line 19.

| Line | Amount | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 2 / 3 / 4 | 17,280 / 0 / 17,280 | first year for Jamie |
| 5 | 20,000 | paid 2025, final 2025 |
| 6 | 17,280 | min(4, 5) |
| 7 | 150,000 | ≤ 259,190 → line 10 = 0 |
| 11a / 11b | 17,280 / 5,000 | |
| 11c / 12 | 5,000 / 17,280 | |
| **13** | **5,000** | → Form 1040 line 30 |
| 14 / 15 / 16 | 12,280 / 0 / 12,280 | |
| 17 | 10,000 | worksheet: min(12,280, 10,000 − 0) |
| **18** | **10,000** | → Schedule 3 line 6c |
| carryforward | 2,280 | 12,280 − 10,000, nonrefundable for up to 5 years; $2,720 (20,000 − 17,280) is never claimable |

**B. Phase-out, two children.** Same couple, but MAGI $279,190 and two children each with line 6 = $17,280: line 8
= 20,000; line 9 = 0.500; per child line 10 = 8,640, line 11a = 8,640, line 11b = 5,000; line 11c = 10,000 → line 13
= **$10,000** (Form 1040 line 30); line 12 = 17,280; line 14 = 7,280; line 18 = smaller of 7,280 and the limit.
