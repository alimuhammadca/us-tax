## 1. At a glance

Form 8853 has three independent sections, each per person:

- **Section A — Archer MSAs** (medical savings accounts for employees of small employers and the self-employed with an
  HDHP; new contributions only for those already in the program before 2008 or joining through a participating
  employer, `i8853.pdf p. 1`). Part I: the deduction for your own contributions — the smallest of what you contributed,
  65% (self-only) / 75% (family) of the HDHP deductible prorated by eligible months, and your compensation; nothing if
  an employer contributed. Line 5 → Schedule 1 line 23. Part II: distributions not used for qualified medical expenses
  are taxable (line 8 → Schedule 1 line 8e) with a 20% additional tax (line 9b → Schedule 2 line 17e) unless the
  holder died, became disabled or was 65+.
- **Section B — Medicare Advantage MSA distributions**: taxable part (line 12 → Schedule 1 line 8e) and a 50%
  additional tax (line 13b → Schedule 2 line 17f) unless the holder died or became disabled.
- **Section C — long-term care (LTC) insurance and accelerated death benefits paid per diem**: the part above the
  per-diem limitation ($420 a day for 2025, or actual LTC costs if larger, less reimbursements) is taxable (line 26 →
  Schedule 1 line 8e).

```text
Section A  line 3 = Σ months (65% / 75% × annual deductible) ÷ 12      line 4 = compensation
           line 5 = smallest of lines 2, 3, 4 (0 if an employer contributed)        → Schedule 1 line 23
           line 6c = 6a 1099-SA box 1 − 6b rollovers / excess withdrawn
           line 8 = max(0, 6c − 7 qualified medical expenses)                       → Schedule 1 line 8e
           line 9b = 20% × (line 8 not meeting an exception)                        → Schedule 2 line 17e
Section B  line 12 = max(0, 10 − 11)                                                → Schedule 1 line 8e
           line 13b = 50% × (line 12 not excepted), worksheet if MA MSA held 12/31/2024 → Schedule 2 line 17f
Section C  line 20 = 18 qualified-contract per diem + 19 chronically-ill accelerated death benefits
           line 23 = max(21 = $420 × days, 22 = actual LTC costs)    line 25 = max(0, 23 − 24 reimbursements)
           line 26 = max(0, 20 − 25)                                                → Schedule 1 line 8e
Schedule 1 line 8e = line 8 + line 12 + line 26   (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 91`)
```

Terms used below:

- **HDHP (Archer)** — for 2025, deductible $2,850–$4,300 self-only / $5,700–$8,550 family; out-of-pocket at most
  $5,700 / $10,500 (`i8853.pdf p. 2`).
- **Medicare Advantage MSA** — an Archer MSA designated as such, funded only by Medicare, for a person enrolled in
  Medicare (`i8853.pdf p. 6`).
- **Per diem payment** — "payments of a fixed amount made on a periodic basis without regard to actual expenses
  incurred"; Form 1099-LTC box 3 says whether payments were per diem or reimbursements (`i8853.pdf p. 8`).
- **Qualified LTC insurance contract** — issued after 1996 meeting section 7702B (insured chronically ill), or issued
  before 1997 meeting state law then and not materially changed (`i8853.pdf p. 8`).
- **Chronically ill / terminally ill** — certified unable to perform 2 of 6 daily activities for 90 days or needing
  supervision for severe cognitive impairment / certified as expected to die within 24 months (`i8853.pdf p. 8`).
- **LTC period** — the period the per-diem limit is measured over (contract period or equal-payment-rate method)
  (`i8853.pdf p. 8-9`).

## 2. The IRS wording

**Who must file** (`i8853.pdf p. 1`): when you (or your employer) contributed to your Archer MSA for 2025; on a joint
return, when your spouse's MSA received contributions; when you acquired an MSA through a death; "You (or your spouse,
if filing jointly) were a policyholder who received payments under an LTC insurance contract or received any
accelerated death benefits from a life insurance policy on a per diem or other periodic basis in 2025"; and when you
(or your spouse) received Archer MSA or Medicare Advantage MSA distributions — then "you must file Form 8853 … even if
you have no taxable income".

**Line 5** (`f8853.pdf p. 1`): "Archer MSA deduction. Enter the smallest of line 2, 3, or 4 here. Also include this
amount on Schedule 1 (Form 1040), line 23".

**Employer contributions** (`i8853.pdf p. 3`): "If an employer made contributions to your Archer MSA, you aren't
entitled to a deduction. If you and your spouse are covered under an HDHP with family coverage and an employer made
contributions to either of your Archer MSAs, neither you nor your spouse is allowed to make deductible contributions".

**Line 9b** (`i8853.pdf p. 6`): "The additional 20% tax doesn't apply to distributions made after the date that the
account holder: • Dies, • Becomes disabled …, or • Turns age 65. If any of the exceptions applies to any of the
distributions included on line 8, check the box on line 9a. Enter on line 9b only 20% (0.20) of any amount included on
line 8 that doesn't meet any of the exceptions."

**Line 26** (`f8853.pdf p. 2`): "Taxable payments. Subtract line 25 from line 20. If zero or less, enter -0-. Also
include this amount in the total on Schedule 1 (Form 1040), line 8e".

**Schedule 1 / Schedule 2** (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 91, p. 99, p. 113`): line 8e "Enter the total of the amounts from
Form 8853, lines 8, 12, and 26"; line 23 "Archer MSA Deduction. See Form 8853"; Schedule 2 line 17e "additional tax on
Archer MSA distributions from Form 8853, line 9b"; line 17f "additional tax on Medicare Advantage MSA distributions from
Form 8853, line 13b".

## 3. What goes on this form

- Archer MSA contributions (employer and own), the limitation, compensation, and the deduction (lines 1–5).
- Archer MSA distributions, rollovers / returned excess, qualified medical expenses, taxable part and 20% tax
  (lines 6a–9b).
- Medicare Advantage MSA distributions, expenses, taxable part and 50% tax (lines 10–13b).
- Per insured person: per-diem LTC payments and chronically-ill accelerated death benefits, the per-diem limitation,
  and the taxable excess (lines 14a–26).

## 6. How to fill it in, line by line

### Section A — Archer MSAs

**Part I — contributions and deduction** (`i8853.pdf p. 2-5`). Eligible: employee of a small employer (≤ 50 employees)
or self-employed, HDHP with no other coverage except permitted coverage, not in Medicare, not someone's dependent, on
the first day of the month (`p. 1`). If an employer contribution blocks the deduction: complete lines 1–2, skip 3–4,
enter 0 on line 5 (`p. 3`).

1. **Line 1** — employer contributions for you or your spouse for 2025, "shown in box 12 of Form W-2 with code R"
   (`i8853.pdf p. 3`).
2. **Line 2** — your own contributions for 2025, including January 1 – April 15, 2026; not rollovers (`p. 3`).
3. **Line 3** — Line 3 Limitation Chart and Worksheet, month by month: Medicare or not eligible on the 1st → $0;
   self-only → 65% of the annual deductible ($2,850–$4,300); family → 75% ($5,700–$8,550); total ÷ 12 (`p. 3-4`). More
   than one HDHP: family rules, and with two family plans the lowest deductible (`p. 3`). Married filing separately
   with family coverage: 37.5% (one-half of 75%) unless the spouses agree otherwise (`p. 5`).
4. **Line 4** — compensation from the employer maintaining the HDHP (wages, commissions, tips, bonuses), or for the
   self-employed the net earnings from the business under which the HDHP was set up (after the deductible part of SE
   tax); not pensions or deferred compensation (`p. 5`).
5. **Line 5** — smallest of lines 2, 3 and 4 → Schedule 1 line 23. Excess own contributions = line 2 − line 5; excess
   employer contributions = employer contributions over the smaller of line 3 and line 4, "Other income" if not in W-2
   box 1; either may be withdrawn with earnings by the due date; a prior-year excess may be deducted against unused
   room; any excess left is taxed on Form 5329 (`p. 5`). Spouses each with self-only HDHPs: separate "statement"
   Part I for each, lines 1, 2 and 5 added on the controlling form (`p. 3`).

**Part II — distributions.**

6. **Line 6a** — total distributions you **and your spouse** received from all Archer MSAs, Form 1099-SA box 1
   (`i8853.pdf p. 5`). **Line 6b** — rollovers and excess (with earnings) withdrawn by the due date. **Line 6c** = 6a −
   6b.
7. **Line 7** — distributions used for qualified medical expenses of you, your spouse and dependents (and certain
   would-be dependents) — but not for a person who was not covered by an HDHP when you (or your employer) contributed
   for 2025 (`p. 6`).
8. **Line 8** = 6c − 7, not below 0 → Schedule 1 line 8e.
9. **Lines 9a / 9b** — 20% of the part of line 8 not distributed after death, disability or age 65 (`p. 6`). Examples:
   a spouse aged 63 gets no exception from the other spouse's age; a holder who turned 65 during 2025 pays 20% only on
   distributions made on or before the 65th birthday (Examples 1–3). Line 9b → Schedule 2 line 17e.

### Section B — Medicare Advantage MSA distributions (separate Section B per spouse on a joint return)

10. **Line 10** — total distributions from all Medicare Advantage MSAs (Form 1099-SA box 1), excluding Medicare's
    erroneous contributions and trustee-to-trustee transfers (`i8853.pdf p. 7`).
11. **Line 11** — distributions used only for the account holder's qualified medical expenses (`p. 7`).
12. **Line 12** = 10 − 11, not below 0 → Schedule 1 line 8e.
13. **Lines 13a / 13b** — no 50% tax on distributions made on or after death or disability. If the exception covers
    all, 13b = 0; otherwise the **Additional 50% Tax Worksheet**: line 1 = non-excepted part of line 12; if you had a
    Medicare Advantage MSA on December 31, 2024: line 2 = its value then, line 4 = 60% of the January 1, 2025 HDHP
    deductible, line 5 = max(0, 2 − 4), line 6 = max(0, 1 − 5), line 7 = ½ × line 6; if not, ½ × line 1 (`p. 7`).
    Line 13b → Schedule 2 line 17f.

### Section C — Long-term care insurance contracts (one Section C per insured; per LTC period)

Filing chart (`i8853.pdf p. 9`): per-diem LTC payments under a qualified contract, or per-diem accelerated death
benefits for a chronically ill insured → complete all of Section C; non-qualified-contract payments only → lines 14a,
14b and 17; terminally-ill accelerated death benefits only → lines 14a, 14b, 15, 16, 17 (if any) and 26.

14. **Lines 14a / 14b** — name and SSN of the insured.
15. **Line 15** — did anyone else receive per-diem payments or accelerated death benefits for the same insured? Yes →
    **Multiple Payees** (`p. 9-10`): an aggregate statement for all recipients using one LTC period; the per-diem
    limitation goes first to the insured (and the insured's spouse on a joint return), the rest pro rata; enter your
    share on lines 25 and 26 and leave lines 21–24 blank.
16. **Line 16** — was the insured terminally ill? Yes and only terminally-ill accelerated death benefits → skip 17–25,
    line 26 = 0 (`f8853.pdf p. 2`).
17. **Line 17** — "the total of the amounts from box 1 of all Forms 1099-LTC you received with respect to the insured on
    which the 'Per diem' box in box 3 is checked" (`f8853.pdf p. 2`).
18. **Line 18** — the part of line 17 from qualified LTC contracts; more than one LTC period → a separate Section C per
    period, line 26 totals added (`i8853.pdf p. 8`).
19. **Line 19** — per-diem accelerated death benefits received while the insured was chronically (not terminally) ill,
    "generally shown in box 2 of Form 1099-LTC" (`p. 8`).
20. **Line 20** = 18 + 19.
21. **Line 21** — $420 × days in the LTC period (contract-period or equal-payment-rate method) (`p. 8-10`).
22. **Line 22** — costs of qualified LTC services for the insured in the period (`p. 9`).
23. **Line 23** = larger of 21 and 22. **Line 24** — reimbursements received or expected for those services (1099-LTC
    box 3 "Reimbursed amount"); reimbursements under pre-August-1996 contracts generally excluded (`p. 9`).
24. **Line 25** = 23 − 24 (per-diem limitation). **Line 26** = 20 − 25, not below 0 → Schedule 1 line 8e.

## 8. Married filing jointly / separately

- Section A Part I: either spouse family → both treated as family; both family → the lower deductible; both self-only →
  separate statement Part I each; an employer contribution to either spouse's MSA under family coverage blocks both
  spouses' deductions (`i8853.pdf p. 3`). MFS family: 37.5% each unless agreed otherwise (`p. 5`).
- Line 6a includes both spouses' Archer distributions; the 20% exception is tested per account holder (`p. 5-6`).
- Section B: a separate statement Section B per spouse, totals on the controlling form (`p. 6`).
- Section C: the per-diem limitation goes first to the insured and an insured's spouse on a joint return (`p. 10`).

## 9. Forms to attach or keep

- **Attach** Form 8853 whenever a filing condition applies (`i8853.pdf p. 1`), plus statement forms (Sections A / B),
  and the multiple-payee aggregate statement for Section C (`p. 9-10`).
- **Keep** the Line 3 Limitation Chart and Worksheet (`p. 4`), the Additional 50% Tax Worksheet (`p. 7`), receipts.
- Form 5329 Part VI for excess contributions (`p. 5`).

## 11. Worked example

Lee Park (fictional, SSN 000-00-0003), single, age 50, self-employed, self-only Archer HDHP with a $3,000 deductible
all year (not in Medicare). Net self-employment earned income $40,000. Own contributions $2,200; no employer. One
Form 1099-SA (box 5 Archer MSA): box 1 $1,000, code 1; $700 of qualified medical expenses paid with it. Lee is also
policyholder of a qualified LTC contract on his father (000-00-0004, chronically ill all year, Lee the only payee):
one Form 1099-LTC, box 1 $160,000, box 3 "Per diem"; LTC costs $40,000; no reimbursements; one LTC period of 365 days.

1. Line 1 = 0; line 2 = $2,200; line 3 = 65% × $3,000 = **$1,950**; line 4 = $40,000.
2. Line 5 = smallest of $2,200, $1,950, $40,000 = **$1,950** → Schedule 1 line 23. Excess $250 → Form 5329 Part VI
   unless withdrawn by the due date.
3. Line 6a = $1,000; 6b = 0; 6c = $1,000; line 7 = $700; line 8 = **$300**.
4. Line 9b: age 50, no exception → 20% × $300 = **$60** → Schedule 2 line 17e.
5. Section C: line 17 = $160,000; line 18 = $160,000; line 19 = 0; line 20 = $160,000; line 21 = $420 × 365 =
   $153,300; line 22 = $40,000; line 23 = $153,300; line 24 = 0; line 25 = $153,300; line 26 = **$6,700**.
6. Schedule 1 line 8e = $300 + $0 + $6,700 = **$7,000**.
