# Form 8949 — Sales and Other Dispositions of Capital Assets

Tax year 2025. What the IRS requires. How the app does it: `C:\us-tax\new_knowledge\forms\8949.md`. Picture:
`C:\us-tax\new_flowcharts\forms\8949.md`. Form 8949 feeds Schedule D lines 1b, 2, 3, 8b, 9 and 10
(`C:\us-tax\new_lines\forms\schedule-d.md`), which in turn makes Form 1040 line 7a (`C:\us-tax\new_lines\7a.md`).

The 2025 form is in `docs\IRS-Forms\f8949.pdf` (not in `docs\books`); its instructions are `i8949.pdf` in
`docs\books`. Broker reporting rules come from `i1099b--2025.pdf` and `i1099da--2025.pdf` (the 2025 editions).

---

## 1. At a glance

Form 8949 lists, one row per transaction, every sale or exchange of a capital asset that is not summarised directly
on Schedule D line 1a / 8a and is not reported on another form (4684, 4797, 6252, 6781, 8824). It reconciles the
figures brokers reported to the IRS (Forms 1099-B, 1099-DA, 1099-S) with what the filer reports. **Part I** is
short-term (held 1 year or less), **Part II** long-term. Each Part carries exactly **one** of six boxes, so a filer
uses a separate Part for each box that applies — three boxes for non-digital assets and, new for 2025, three for
digital assets:

| | Basis reported to the IRS | Basis not reported to the IRS | No 1099-B / 1099-DA received |
|---|---|---|---|
| Part I, 1099-B (non-digital) | **A** | **B** | **C** |
| Part I, 1099-DA (digital) | **G** | **H** | **I** |
| Part II, 1099-B (non-digital) | **D** | **E** | **F** |
| Part II, 1099-DA (digital) | **J** | **K** | **L** |

```text
each row:   (h) = (d) proceeds − (e) cost or other basis + (g) adjustment     (code(s) in (f) explain (g))
each Part:  line 2 totals of (d), (e), (g), (h) → Schedule D
            A or G → line 1b    B or H → line 2    C or I → line 3
            D or J → line 8b    E or K → line 9    F or L → line 10
```

Terms used below:

- **Covered security** — an asset whose broker must report basis (for example stock acquired after 2010, most debt
  bought after 2013, and "A digital asset acquired after 2025") (`i8949.pdf p. 7`). Basis of a noncovered asset may
  be missing or not reported to the IRS.
- **Basis reported to the IRS** — Form 1099-B box 12 or Form 1099-DA box 2 is checked (`i8949.pdf p. 3`).
- **Digital asset** — "any digital representation of value that is recorded on a cryptographically secured
  distributed ledger" — cryptocurrency, NFTs and similar (`i8949.pdf p. 3`).
- **Wash sale** — a loss sale of stock or securities with a purchase of substantially identical stock or securities
  within 30 days before or after (`i1040sd.pdf p. 5`).
- **Adjustment code** — a letter in column (f) that explains column (g) (`i8949.pdf p. 7–11`).

## 2. The IRS wording

**Form 8949 (2025)** (`docs\IRS-Forms\f8949.pdf p. 1–2`): "File with your Schedule D to list your transactions for
lines 1b, 2, 3, 8b, 9, and 10 of Schedule D." "You must check Box A, B, C, G, H, or I below. Check only one box. If
more than one box applies for your short-term transactions, complete a separate Form 8949, page 1, for each applicable
box." Column (g): "If you enter an amount in column (g), enter a code in column (f)." Line 2: "Totals. Add the amounts
in columns (d), (e), (g), and (h) (subtract negative amounts)." Note below line 2: "If you checked Box A or Box G above
but the basis reported to the IRS was incorrect, enter in column (e) the basis as reported to the IRS, and enter an
adjustment in column (g) to correct the basis."

**What's New** (`i8949.pdf p. 1`): "New boxes G, H, and I for short-term digital asset transactions. … Do not use
box C to report short-term digital asset transactions." "New boxes J, K, and L for long-term digital asset
transactions. … Do not use box F to report long-term digital asset transactions."

**Purpose** (`i8949.pdf p. 1`): "If you receive Form 1099-B, Form 1099-DA, or Form 1099-S (or substitute statements),
always report the proceeds (sales price) shown on the form (or statement) in column (d) of Form 8949. If Form 1099-B
or Form 1099-DA (or substitute statement) shows that the cost or other basis was reported to the IRS, always report
the basis shown on that form (or statement) in column (e). If any correction or adjustment to these amounts is
needed, make it in column (g)."

**Joint returns** (`i8949.pdf p. 1`): "If you are filing a joint return, complete as many copies of Form 8949 as you
need to report all of your and your spouse's transactions. You and your spouse may list your transactions on separate
forms or you may combine them."

## 3. What goes on this form

Individuals use Form 8949 for (`i8949.pdf p. 1`):

- "The sale or exchange of a capital asset reported on a Form 1099-K, Form 1099-B, or Form 1099-DA."
- "The sale or exchange of a capital asset not reported on another form or schedule."
- "Gains from involuntary conversions (other than from casualty or theft) of capital assets not used in your trade or
  business."
- "Nonbusiness bad debts" (always short-term, `i8949.pdf p. 2`) and "Worthlessness of a security."
- "The election to defer capital gain invested in a qualified opportunity fund (QOF)" and "The disposition of
  interests in QOFs."
- "To elect out of the installment method."

Also on Form 8949 (`i8949.pdf p. 3, 5`; `i1040sd.pdf p. 2–8`): a main-home sale that is not fully excluded or that
was reported on a 1099-S; personal-use property sold at a gain, or at a loss when a 1099-S / 1099-K arrived (code L);
losses on depreciable property held for investment; charitable gift annuity 1099-R box 3 amounts (box F / L); Form
2438 amounts; nondividend distributions beyond basis (box C / F or I / L); money-market NAV-method gains (Part I,
box C / I); other gains where price or basis is unknown (for example a Form 8621 long-term gain); the capital part of
a section 1244 loss (code S); the section 1202 exclusion row for a 1099-DIV box 2c or Form 2439 box 1c gain (code Q);
short sales; options that closed or expired.

## 6. How to figure it, line by line

### Header

Name(s) shown on the return and the SSN or TIN. Before checking a box, look at the 1099-B / 1099-DA "They will show
whether your basis … was reported to the IRS by your broker and may even tell you which box to check" (`f8949.pdf
p. 1`).

### Part I — Short-term (page 1)

1. **Note** — "You may aggregate all short-term transactions reported on Form(s) 1099-B and Form(s) 1099-DA showing
   basis was reported to the IRS and for which no adjustments or codes are required. Enter the totals directly on
   Schedule D, line 1a" (the Exception 1 summary rule, `i8949.pdf p. 4`).
2. **Check one box** (`i8949.pdf p. 3–4`):
   - **Box A / G** — "all short-term transactions reported to you on Form 1099-B or Form 1099-DA … with an amount
     shown for cost or other basis unless the statement indicates that amount wasn't reported to the IRS".
   - **Box B / H** — reported "without an amount shown for cost or other basis or showing that cost or other basis
     wasn't reported to the IRS".
   - **Box C / I** — "all short-term transactions for which you can't check box A, B, G or H because you didn't
     receive a Form 1099-B or Form 1099-DA … Do not use box C to report digital asset transactions. Use box I."
   - "Complete as many copies of Part I as you need to report all transactions of each type." Attach the Form(s) 8949
     that list code Z first.
3. **Line 1, one row per transaction** (the 2025 form has 11 rows per Part):
   - **(a) Description** — "For stock, include the number of shares"; ticker symbols allowed; "For a digital asset,
     include the full name or an abbreviated symbol of the digital asset and the exact units sold … and include the
     sale transaction ID number, if available" (`i8949.pdf p. 6`).
   - **(b) Date acquired** — trade date; "INHERITED" for inherited property (always long-term); "VARIOUS" for a block
     bought on several dates, still split short / long between Parts; for a short sale, the date the delivered
     property was acquired (`i8949.pdf p. 6`).
   - **(c) Date sold or disposed of** — trade date; for an expired purchased option, the expiration date and
     "EXPIRED" in column (d) (`i8949.pdf p. 6`; `i1040sd.pdf p. 6`).
   - **(d) Proceeds** — the proceeds shown on the 1099-B / 1099-DA / 1099-S; if none was received, net proceeds
     (gross less selling expenses). Proceeds higher than shown → the correct amount. A 1099 for a mere return of
     principal on a savings bond or CD → the amount in both (d) and (e) (`i8949.pdf p. 6`).
   - **(e) Cost or other basis** — the basis shown when it was reported to the IRS (even if wrong — fix it in (g),
     code B); the correct basis for a noncovered security with no or unreported basis; adjusted for improvements,
     depreciation, nondividend distributions, splits, OID, market discount elected currently, bond premium, gift /
     inheritance rules, Schedule A (Form 8971) consistent basis, compensatory option income (`i8949.pdf p. 6–7`).
     "If you don't use the actual cost, attach an explanation of your basis."
   - **(f) Code(s)** — "If more than one code applies, enter all the codes that apply in alphabetical order (for
     example, "BOQ"). Don't separate the codes by a space or comma." (`i8949.pdf p. 7–8`)
   - **(g) Adjustment** — negative amounts in parentheses; with several codes, the net adjustment (`i8949.pdf p. 8`).
   - **(h) Gain or (loss)** — "subtract the cost or other basis in column (e) from the proceeds (sales price) in
     column (d). Then take into account any adjustments in column (g)" (`i8949.pdf p. 11`).
4. **Line 2 — Totals** of (d), (e), (g), (h) → Schedule D line 1b (box A or G), line 2 (box B or H) or line 3 (box C
   or I). "The total of the amounts in column (h) of line 2 of all your Forms 8949 should equal the amount you get by
   combining columns (d), (e), and (g) on the corresponding line of Schedule D" (`i8949.pdf p. 13`).

### Part II — Long-term (page 2)

5. Same layout with **box D / J**, **box E / K**, **box F / L**; "Do not use box F to report digital asset
   transactions. Use box L." Line 2 totals → Schedule D line 8b (D or J), line 9 (E or K), line 10 (F or L)
   (`f8949.pdf p. 2`; `i8949.pdf p. 4`).
6. "You don't need to complete and file an entire copy of Form 8949 (Parts I and II) if you can check a single box to
   describe all your transactions." (`i8949.pdf p. 4`)

### Column (f) codes and the column (g) rule for each (`i8949.pdf p. 8–11`)

| Code | When | Column (g) |
|---|---|---|
| **B** | Basis on the 1099-B / 1099-DA is incorrect | Box B/H/E/K: correct basis in (e), 0 in (g). Box A/G/D/J: reported basis in (e), the Worksheet for Basis Adjustments result in (g) (reported − correct, positive if the reported basis was too high) |
| **C** | Disposed of collectibles | 0 (row reported normally; feeds the 28% worksheet) |
| **D** | 1099-B box 1f / 1099-DA box 1h accrued market discount | Worksheet: −(smaller of gain or accrued discount), reported as interest; 0 if the discount was included currently |
| **E** | Selling expenses, option premiums or digital asset transaction costs not reflected on the 1099-B / 1099-DA / 1099-S | Expenses / premiums paid as a negative; premiums received as a positive |
| **H** | Main-home gain partly or fully excluded | Excluded gain as a negative |
| **L** | Nondeductible loss other than a wash sale (personal-use property, related party, loss not allowed per 1099-B box 7 / 1099-DA box 5) | The nondeductible loss as a positive |
| **M** | Several transactions on one row (Exception 2 or the special provision) | 0 unless another code needs an amount |
| **N** | Received the 1099 as a nominee | Gain as a negative / loss as a positive so (h) = 0 |
| **O** | Any other adjustment (incl. contingent payment debt instrument worksheet, QOF section 1231 rows) | As figured |
| **P** | Nonresident sale of a partnership interest with a U.S. business | Regulations section 1.864(c)(8)-1 adjustment |
| **Q** | QSB stock — section 1202 exclusion | Exclusion as a negative |
| **R** | Postponed gain (rollover of QSB stock gain, ESOP sale) | Postponed gain as a negative |
| **S** | Section 1244 loss beyond the ordinary-loss maximum | Ordinary loss claimed on Form 4797, as a positive |
| **T** | 1099-B box 2 / 1099-DA box 6 type of gain is wrong | Row placed in the correct Part; 0 if no other adjustment |
| **W** | Nondeductible wash-sale loss | Disallowed loss as a positive; if box 1g / 1i is wrong, the correct amount, and a statement when it is less |
| **X** | DC Zone or qualified community asset exclusion | Exclusion as a negative |
| **Y** | Gain previously deferred in a QOF, now recognized | Deferred gain as a positive |
| **Z** | Electing to defer gain invested in a QOF | Deferred gain as a negative (own row: column (a) = QOF EIN, (b) = investment date, (c)–(e) blank) |
| (none) | No adjustment | Leave (f) and (g) blank |

### Worksheets kept with records (`i8949.pdf p. 11–12`)

- **Basis Adjustments in Column (g)** — line 1 reported basis, line 2 correct basis; line 3 = line 2 − line 1 as a
  negative (g) when the correct basis is larger; line 4 = line 1 − line 2 as a positive (g) when it is smaller.
- **Accrued Market Discount Adjustment** — line 3 = max(0, proceeds − basis); line 4 = accrued market discount;
  line 5 = smaller of 3 and 4 → negative (g) and interest income.
- **Contingent Payment Debt Instrument Adjustment** — a gain is entirely ordinary (negative (g), code O, interest); a
  loss is ordinary up to prior OID inclusions less net negative adjustments (positive (g)).

### Exceptions to one row per transaction (`i8949.pdf p. 4–5`)

- **Exception 1** — Form 8949 not required for broker-reported sales with basis reported, no box 1f / 1g (1099-B) or
  1h / 1i (1099-DA) adjustment, Ordinary box unchecked, no adjustment needed, no QOF deferral or termination, and not
  collectibles: totals go straight to Schedule D line 1a / 8a. "If you qualify to use Exception 1 and also qualify to
  use Exception 2, you can use both."
- **Exception 2** — an attached statement with the same columns; one summary row per broker on Form 8949 with "see
  attached statement" in (a), (b) and (c) blank, code **M** (plus any other codes). Not available for a QOF deferral.
  "Don't enter "Available upon request" and summary totals in lieu of reporting the details". If e-filing without
  separate rows, mail Form 8949 (or statements) with Form 8453 (`i8949.pdf p. 5`).
- **Special provision** — certain corporations, partnerships, dealers; "Individual taxpayers aren't eligible except in
  rare circumstances" (`i8949.pdf p. 4`).

### Holding period (`i8949.pdf p. 2`)

"To figure the holding period, begin counting on the day after you received the property and include the day you
disposed of it." Inherited property is long-term. "If box 2 on Form 1099-B or box 6 on Form 1099-DA is blank and code
X is in the "Applicable checkbox on Form 8949" box … your broker doesn't know whether your gain (or loss) is short term
or long term. Use your own records to determine whether your gain (or loss) is short term or long term."

## 8. Married filing jointly / separately

- **Joint:** both spouses' transactions on one or more Forms 8949 (separate or combined); all totals on the one
  Schedule D (`i8949.pdf p. 1`).
- **Separate:** each spouse reports only their own transactions on their own Form 8949. A statement issued to the
  other spouse, or to a child, is not reported (a child's sale is on the child's return; Form 8814 covers only
  interest, dividends and capital gain distributions).

## 11. Worked example

**Taylor Sample** (000-00-0021), single.

| Statement / event | Part, box | (d) | (e) | (f) | (g) | (h) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1099-B short-term, basis reported, 1g wash sale 300 | I, A | 2,500 | 3,000 | W | 300 | (200) |
| 1099-B short-term, basis reported as 900, correct basis 100 | I, A | 1,000 | 900 | B | 800 | 900 |
| 1099-DA short-term, basis not reported, own records basis 1,000 | I, H | 1,200 | 1,000 | | | 200 |
| 1099-B long-term, noncovered, box 1e blank, own records basis 4,000 | II, E | 7,000 | 4,000 | | | 3,000 |
| Main home, 1099-S proceeds 320,000, basis 100,000, selling expenses 20,000 not on the 1099-S, 200,000 excludable | II, F | 320,000 | 100,000 | EH | (220,000) | 0 |

Line 2 totals → Schedule D:

- Part I box A: (d) 3,500, (e) 3,900, (g) 1,100, (h) **700** → line 1b.
- Part I box H: (d) 1,200, (e) 1,000, (h) **200** → line 2.
- Part II box E: (d) 7,000, (e) 4,000, (h) **3,000** → line 9.
- Part II box F: (d) 320,000, (e) 100,000, (g) (220,000), (h) **0** → line 10.

Check: for box A, (d) − (e) + (g) = 3,500 − 3,900 + 1,100 = 700 = Σ(h).
