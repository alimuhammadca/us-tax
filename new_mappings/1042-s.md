## 1. What this statement is

Form 1042-S, Foreign Person's U.S. Source Income Subject to Withholding (**2025**, `docs/IRS-Forms/f1042s.pdf` p. 2,
Copy B), is filed by a withholding agent for U.S.-source income paid to a **foreign person** (nonresident alien,
foreign entity) and the tax withheld under chapters 3 / 4. Filer instructions: `i1042s--2025.pdf`.

**How a filer of a Form 1040 who received one is treated.** The form is aimed at nonresidents: "every nonresident alien
individual … with U.S. income … must file a U.S. income tax return … all others file Form 1040-NR" (`f1042s.pdf` p. 3,
U.S. Income Tax Filing Requirements). A nonresident's return (Form 1040-NR) is outside this app, which produces Form 1040
only. A Form 1040 filer can still hold a 1042-S — a resident alien (green card or substantial presence test), or a
nonresident / dual-status alien married to a U.S. citizen or resident who elected to be taxed as a resident
(`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 8). For that filer:

- the **income** in box 2 is part of gross income ("all income you received … that isn't exempt from tax, including any
  income from sources outside the United States", `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 9) and goes on the Form 1040 line for its
  kind, which box 1's income code names (`f1042s.pdf` p. 3, 5 — e.g. 01 interest → line 2b, 06 dividends → line 3b, 16
  scholarship or fellowship grants, 17-20 compensation → line 1, 15 pensions / annuities, 23 other income); treaty
  benefits may apply (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 8, "you may qualify for certain tax treaty benefits");
- the **tax withheld** is a payment on **Form 1040 line 25c**: "Include on line 25c any … Tax withheld that is shown on
  Form 1042-S, Form 8805, or Form 8288-A. To assist in processing, attach the form to your return to claim a credit for
  the withholding" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 39). Box 10 is the total credit: "the combined amounts reported in box 7a
  …, box 8 …, and box 9" (box 9 is a negative, parenthesised amount) (`i1042s--2025.pdf` p. 29).

**Whose statement it is.** "13e Recipient's U.S. TIN, if any", `recipientTIN` (`MAP:145`; `UI:632`), seeded with the
person tab's SSN on a new entry (`us-tax-ui/src/app/service/statement-entry-state.service.ts:28-34`). Family check
`StatementSsnRules.java:83`.

**Person filter (the D-1a-1 question).** Not applicable: **no compute path reads any Form 1042-S key** (grep of
`microservices\` finds the mapper, extraction `GenericFieldMapper.java:559-561, 1370-1575`, the SSN rule and the catalog).
Line 25c is built from W-2G box 4 and Form 8959 line 24 only (`TRCS:49822-49851`); the design note at `TRCS:49642-49643`
lists 1042-S as out of scope ("4 OOS K-1/1042-S/8805/8288-A per G4 deferred"), as does `outstanding.md:3680`.

**Storage.** Entity `Se1042S` / table `se_form_1042_s`, one row per statement (`MAP:96-181`); amounts have no Notes
columns. UI `syncPdfToForm` `UI:583-668` uses `parseAmountAndNotes`: the shared parser drops a typed leading minus
(**D-ui-1**), and box 9 is drawn with printed parentheses (`UI:432`, `kind: 'parenthetical'`) so the user types the
figure without a sign — box 9 is stored positive, i.e. as the amount repaid. Every replica cell reaches a mapper key.

## 3. Checkboxes and codes

- **Box 1 income codes** (`f1042s.pdf` p. 3, 5): 01-05 interest, 06-08 / 34 / 40 / 52 dividends, 15 pensions,
  annuities, alimony, insurance premiums, 16 scholarship or fellowship grants, 17-20 compensation, 23 other income,
  24 QIE capital-gain distributions, and others. None is read.
- **Boxes 3a / 4a exemption codes, 12b-12c / 13f-13g / 15b-15c / 16d-16e status codes, 13j LOB code** — informational;
  stored.
- **AMENDED, 7b, 7c, 7d, 15** checkboxes — stored.

## 4. Questions the app asks with this statement

None. Nothing in the app asks about residency status for the filer's own return (beyond the dual-status / nonresident
spouse answers carried on Form 1040), so a 1042-S on file raises no flag.

## 5. Differences from the IRS instructions

New:

**D-1042-s-1 — tax withheld on Form 1042-S is not credited on Form 1040 line 25c.**
IRS: "Include on line 25c any … Tax withheld that is shown on Form 1042-S" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 39); box 10 is the
recipient's total withholding credit (`i1042s--2025.pdf` p. 29).
Code: `totalWithholdingCreditAmount` (and 7a / 8 / 9) is read nowhere; line 25c is W-2G box 4 + Form 8959 line 24
(`TRCS:49822-49851`). Already logged as deliberately out of scope (`TRCS:49642-49643`; `outstanding.md:3680`;
`lines\25abcd.md` "Deferred OOS (Gap E)"), but the screen accepts the statement with no warning.
Effect: **silent wrong amount** — payments, and so the refund, understated by the withholding. Severity **medium**
(rare for a Form 1040 filer; the whole withholding is lost when it happens).

**D-1042-s-2 — the income on Form 1042-S is not reported and nothing prompts for it.**
IRS: gross income includes all non-exempt income (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 9); a resident-alien filer reports it on the
line for its kind (box 1 code, `f1042s.pdf` p. 3, 5), subject to any treaty benefit (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 8).
Code: `grossIncomeAmount` and `incomeCode` are read nowhere; no advisory points the user to the Wages / Interest /
Other-income forms where the amount must be typed. Effect: **silent** — income omitted unless the user retypes it on the
right form. Severity **low** (rare; the amount may be treaty-exempt).
