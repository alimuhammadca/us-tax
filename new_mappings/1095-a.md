# Form 1095-A — Health Insurance Marketplace Statement

Statement form id `1095-a`. Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` =
`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`; `MAP` =
`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/statement/Form1095AMapper.java`; `UI` =
`us-tax-ui/src/app/forms/form-1095-a.component.ts`. Related analysis reused here: `new_knowledge\forms\7206.md`
(Pub. 974 coordination with the self-employed health insurance deduction), `new_mappings\w-2.md` (D-w-2-5, W-2 code FF
QSEHRA), `new_knowledge\1a.md` (D-1a-1, the household-wide statement list).

This statement feeds no income line. It feeds **Form 8962** (Premium Tax Credit), whose result goes to **Schedule 3
line 9** (net premium tax credit → Form 1040 line 31) or **Schedule 2 line 1a** (excess advance payment repaid →
Form 1040 line 17). Its link to the income section is the other direction: Form 8962 line 2a starts from Form 1040
line 11a and adds back tax-exempt interest, nontaxable social security and the Form 2555 exclusions (§4 below).

---

## 1. What this statement is

The Health Insurance Marketplace sends Form 1095-A to the person it identified at enrollment as the expected tax filer
for a qualified health plan bought through the Marketplace — one form per policy. "This Form 1095-A provides
information you need to complete Form 8962 … You must complete Form 8962 and file it with your tax return … if any
amount other than zero is shown in Part III, column C … or if you want to take the premium tax credit"
(`docs/IRS-Forms/f1095a.pdf` p. 3). "Do not attach to your tax return. Keep for your records" (`f1095a.pdf` p. 2;
also `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 5: "Include Form 8962 … (Don't include Form 1095-A.)").

IRS instructions used:

- Recipient: "Instructions for Recipient" — `docs/IRS-Forms/f1095a.pdf` p. 3 (2025 form, "Created 6/5/25", p. 2).
- Filer: `i1095a.pdf` p. 1-3 (2025).
- Where it goes: `i8962_2025.pdf` (Form 8962: p. 2 VOID / CORRECTED; p. 5 SLCSP, MEC, employer coverage; p. 14-17
  lines 10-23; p. 18-19 Part IV allocation situations); `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 5 (reconcile APTC), p. 11 (who must
  file, item 5), p. 111 (Schedule 2 line 1a), p. 117 (Schedule 3 line 9).

**Whose statement it is.** Line 5 "Recipient's SSN", stored as `recipientSSN`. "You are the recipient because you are
the person the Marketplace identified at enrollment who is expected to file a tax return" (`f1095a.pdf` p. 3, line 4).
The SSN "may show only the last four digits" (`f1095a.pdf` p. 3; `i1095a.pdf` p. 1). The new-entry pre-fill puts the
person tab's SSN under `recipientTIN` / `employeeSSN` / `beneficiarySSN` / `shareholderTIN`
(`us-tax-ui/src/app/service/statement-entry-state.service.ts:33`), none of which this mapper reads, so `recipientSSN`
starts blank and is typed or extracted. Form 1095-A is not offered on a dependent's tab
(`us-tax-ui/src/app/service/statement-selection.service.ts:133-138` — "the parent's").

**Household / person filter (the D-1a-1 question).** The mapper lists every 1095-A of the account for the year — the
whole household (`MAP:85-94`). Compute loads the list once (`TRCS:3271`) and:

- **Single / MFJ / HOH / QSS:** no person filter; every 1095-A in the account is summed into one Form 8962
  (`TRCS:12892-12934`). On MFJ each entry is tagged taxpayer or spouse only for the Part V year-of-marriage worksheets
  (`TRCS:12898-12900`). Summing all is right for one tax family (the IRS adds column A and C across Forms 1095-A,
  `i8962_2025.pdf` p. 16-17) — except column B (D-1095-a-2).
- **MFS:** a leg drops entries whose `recipientSSN` equals the other spouse's SSN (`TRCS:6193-6207`); entries with a
  blank or truncated SSN, or a dependent's SSN, stay on **both** legs. A policy the couple shared but which was issued
  to the other spouse cannot be allocated on this leg (D-1095-a-5).
- **Head-of-household split legs** (married, filing separately as HoH): not filtered at all, because the filter keys
  on the status text "Married filing separately" (D-1095-a-6).
- **Dependent's own return:** the household personal forms are dropped (`multireturn/DependentOwnFormScoper.java`
  class comment), so there is no `premium-tax-credit-taxpayer` form and `computeForm8962` returns null at its first
  gate (`TRCS:12685-12688`) — no 1095-A reaches a dependent's return. Matches the IRS (the PTC for a dependent's
  coverage belongs to the taxpayer who claims the dependent; `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 111).
- **Household-SSN check:** `1095-a` / `recipientSSN` is in the registry (`StatementSsnRules.java:86`); an SSN of
  nobody in the household raises the blocking `STATEMENT_DOES_NOT_BELONG_TO_FAMILY` (`TRCS:13850-13915`; blank is not
  checked, `:13901`). A truncated SSN or a former spouse's 1095-A is blocked (D-1095-a-7).

**Storage.** `MAP` — parent keys `:118-159`, Part II `coveredIndividuals` `:169-189`, Part III `coverageMonthly`
`:191-207`; entity `Se1095A` / `se_1095_a`, children `Se1095ACoveredIndividual` / `se_1095_a_covered_individual` and
`Se1095ACoverageMonthly` / `se_1095_a_coverage_monthly`. Line 33 totals are flat columns on the parent, sent and
returned nested under `annualTotals` (`MAP:151-158`, `:246-253`). UI: every input in `UI:113-433` (form view) and
`UI:463-600` (statement view) binds a key the mapper saves; no box shown is lost. The statement view always presents
12 month rows in calendar order (`UI:1262-1283`). Upload extraction: `GenericFieldMapper.java:549-557`
(`postProcess1095A`, `splitCoverages` places Part III rows by table position). Amount inputs are
`type="number"` / bound directly, so the shared-parser leading-minus issue (D-ui-1) does not touch this form, and no
1095-A amount is negative.

## 5. Differences from the IRS instructions

Reused: **D-w-2-5** (W-2 code FF QSEHRA not read — the monthly PTC is not reduced or barred);
**D-7206-*** (Pub. 974 coordination with the self-employed health insurance deduction; see `7206.md`);
**D-1095-c-1** (employer coverage offers on Form 1095-C and the affordability answer never limit the credit);
**D-1095-b-1** (other minimum essential coverage months never limit the credit).

New:

**D-1095-a-1 — VOID and CORRECTED are ignored.**
IRS: "Do not use the information on the Form 1095-A with the 'VOID' box checked or the previously received Form 1095-A
to complete Form 8962"; with CORRECTED, "Do not use the information on the original Form 1095-A"
(`i8962_2025.pdf` p. 2; `f1095a.pdf` p. 3).
Code: `void` / `corrected` are saved (`MAP:125-127`) and read by no compute code; every entry is summed
(`TRCS:12892-12934`). A voided form still adds premium, SLCSP and APTC; an original plus its corrected copy is counted
twice. Effect: **silent wrong amount** (credit or repayment). Severity: **medium**.

**D-1095-a-2 — Column B (SLCSP) is added across all Forms 1095-A.**
IRS: "If individuals in your coverage family enrolled in separate policies in the same state … The Marketplace should
have entered the same SLCSP premium … on each Form 1095-A. Enter the amount from column B of only one Form 1095-A—do
not add the amounts from each form" (`i8962_2025.pdf` p. 16; same rule for line 11, p. 15). Adding is right only for
policies in different states or the months before a marriage (p. 16-17).
Code: `sumColB[i] = sumColB[i].add(colB)` for every entry (`TRCS:12927-12930`); `marketplaceIdentifier` (the state)
is never read. Example: MFJ, parents on one policy ($1,000 / month) and children on another ($600), same state, each
form showing the family SLCSP $1,500, monthly contribution $200: IRS column (e) = min(1,600, 1,500 − 200) = $1,300;
app = min(1,600, 3,000 − 200) = $1,600 — $300 a month, **$3,600 a year of excess credit** (Schedule 3 line 9) or
under-repayment. Effect: **silent overstated refundable credit**. Severity: **high** (two policies for one family is
common).

**D-1095-a-3 — A blank or zero column B is not flagged.**
IRS: column B "may be wrong or blank or may report your applicable SLCSP premium as -0- … you must determine your
correct applicable SLCSP premium" (`i8962_2025.pdf` p. 5; "Missing or incorrect SLCSP premium", p. 14).
Code: a missing B reads as 0, so column (d) and (e) are 0 for the month (`TRCS:12956-12959`); no advisory exists among
the PTC flags (`TRCS:6243-6345`, all advisories). Effect: **silent understated credit / overstated repayment** for a filer with no
APTC (the case where the IRS says B is often blank). Severity: **medium**.

**D-1095-a-4 — Form 8962 is skipped silently when the inputs do not line up.**
IRS: Form 8962 is required "if any amount other than zero is shown in Part III, column C" (`f1095a.pdf` p. 3); a return
must be filed when advance payments were made (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 11, item 5).
Code: `computeForm8962` returns null, logging only, when the PTC form is missing, `claimsOrReceivedPtc` is not Yes, or
no 1095-A is on file (`TRCS:12685-12697`). `receivedAny1095A`, `uploadedAll1095AStatements` and
`confirmAllHealthCoverageDocumentationUploaded` are never read by compute (only `PremiumTaxCreditMapper.java`), and the
UI's completeness check does not require an uploaded 1095-A (`form-premium-tax-credit.component.ts:1537-1550`). So a
1095-A with APTC and an unanswered / "No" PTC form, or "Yes" with no 1095-A, gives a return with no Schedule 2 line 1a
repayment and no flag — unlike other statements (e.g. `OTHER_INCOME_MISSING_UPLOADED_1099_G`). Effect: **silent
understated tax** (repayment omitted). Severity: **high**.

**D-1095-a-5 — MFS: a shared policy issued to the other spouse cannot be allocated.**
IRS (Allocation Situation 2): spouses married at year end and filing separately "must equally allocate (50% to each
spouse)" the policy amounts; without Exception 2 each enters "0.50" in column (g) and repays that APTC
(`i8962_2025.pdf` p. 19); the example: "50% of the $4,000 APTC ($2,000) is allocated to Melissa … Melissa must add this
amount to her APTC" although the form went to one of them (p. 17). "The recipient of Form 1095-A should provide a copy
to other taxpayers as needed" (p. 2).
Code: the MFS leg removes every entry whose `recipientSSN` is the other spouse's (`TRCS:6197-6206`) before the Part IV
percentages are applied (`TRCS:12893-12921`), so the non-recipient has no entry to allocate 50% of. Line 8 (spouse's
SSN) is not consulted. Conversely, entries with a blank, truncated or dependent's SSN stay on both legs. Effect:
**silent understated repayment** on the non-recipient's leg (and double counting for the unfiltered entries).
Severity: **medium**.

**D-1095-a-6 — Head-of-household split legs are not filtered by person.**
IRS: a married person living apart may file HoH and take the PTC (Exception 1); shared-policy amounts are allocated
"0.50" for premium and APTC, the SLCSP being that of one's own coverage family; otherwise each return uses its own
family's Forms 1095-A (`i8962_2025.pdf` p. 19).
Code: the filter runs only when the leg's status is "Married filing separately" (`TRCS:6197`, `isMfsReturn`
`TRCS:3548-3549`), yet an HoH-split leg carries `mfsOtherSpouseSsn` (`multireturn/MfsFormScoper.java:850-856`, `:861`).
Both spouses' 1095-As are summed on each HoH leg. Effect: **silent double credit / double repayment** across the two
returns. Severity: **medium** (HoH-split households with Marketplace coverage).

**D-1095-a-7 — A truncated or former spouse's recipient SSN blocks the return.**
IRS: the furnished form may "truncate the social security number … showing only the last four digits"
(`i1095a.pdf` p. 1; `f1095a.pdf` p. 3); after a divorce the Marketplace may send the form to the former spouse, whose
copy the filer needs for Part IV (`i8962_2025.pdf` p. 2, Allocation Situation 1 p. 18).
Code: `normalizeSsn` keeps the digits ("XXX-XX-1234" → "1234", `TRCS:53713-53719`), which match nobody, so
`STATEMENT_DOES_NOT_BELONG_TO_FAMILY` blocks compute (`TRCS:13892-13915`); the same happens for a former spouse's SSN.
The help text says to "Enter SSN with or without dashes as printed" (`UI:1367`). Leaving the SSN blank avoids the
block (blank is skipped, `TRCS:13901`) but then MFS / Part V attribution is lost. Effect: **BLOCKING** flag on a correct
entry. Severity: **medium**.

**D-1095-a-8 — Covered individuals (Part II) are never read.**
IRS: Part II lists the people covered and their coverage dates; when someone enrolled is outside the tax family, or the
coverage family changed, the filer must allocate (Part IV) or use a different SLCSP (`f1095a.pdf` p. 3;
`i8962_2025.pdf` p. 14, p. 19).
Code: `coveredIndividuals` is saved (`MAP:169-189`) and read by no compute code; nothing compares the covered SSNs with
the taxpayer, spouse and dependents. Effect: no advisory; the amount is only as right as the user's own Part IV entries
(**silent**, unverified in size). Severity: **low**.

**D-1095-a-9 — Part III is read by row position; line 33 is not read.**
IRS: lines 21-32 are January-December; line 33 is their total and feeds Form 8962 line 11 (`f1095a.pdf` p. 3;
`i8962_2025.pdf` p. 15).
Code: row *i* is taken as month *i* (`TRCS:12903-12904`) and `monthName` is ignored; at most 12 rows are read. The
statement view and the extractor always save calendar order (`UI:1262-1283`; `GenericFieldMapper.java` `splitCoverages`),
but the form view lets the user add rows in any order (`UI:335-342`, `:1286-1296`), and the Part IV start/stop months and
the Part V pre-marriage window then hit the wrong months. Totals typed on line 33 with no monthly rows reconcile nothing
(`annualTotals` unread). Effect: **silent**, only in those cases. Severity: **low**.

Not supported without an id: a 1095-A for a catastrophic plan or stand-alone dental plan "aren't entitled to take a
premium tax credit" (`f1095a.pdf` p. 3) — not asked (rare; Marketplaces do not issue them, `i1095a.pdf` p. 1). Schedule A
medical premiums are not reduced by the PTC (`i8962_2025.pdf` p. 2) — not checked in this pass.
