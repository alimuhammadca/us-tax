# Form 1095-C — Employer-Provided Health Insurance Offer and Coverage

Statement form id `1095-c`. Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` =
`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`; `MAP` =
`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/statement/Form1095CMapper.java`; `UI` =
`us-tax-ui/src/app/forms/form-1095-c.component.ts`. Related: `new_mappings\1095-a.md` (Form 8962),
`new_mappings\1095-b.md` (minimum essential coverage), `new_knowledge\forms\7206.md` (D-7206-5).

This statement feeds no line of the return. Its Part II tells a Marketplace enrollee whether an employer offer made
them ineligible for the premium tax credit (Form 8962 → Schedule 3 line 9 / Schedule 2 line 1a): "This information may
be relevant if you purchased health insurance coverage for 2025 through the Marketplace and wish to claim the premium
tax credit on Schedule 3, line 9 … If you don't wish to claim the premium tax credit for 2025, you don't need the
information in Part II of Form 1095-C" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 5). Enrollment (line 16 code 2C, Part III) also bears
on the self-employed health insurance deduction (Schedule 1 line 17 / Form 7206).

---

## 1. What this statement is

An applicable large employer (ALE) sends Form 1095-C to each full-time employee: Part II reports, month by month, the
coverage offered (line 14), the employee's monthly cost for the cheapest self-only minimum-value coverage (line 15) and
safe-harbor / enrollment codes (line 16); Part III lists the people enrolled when the plan is self-insured.
"Do not attach to your tax return. Keep for your records" (`docs/IRS-Forms/f1095c.pdf` p. 1). "The information on
line 14 relates to eligibility for coverage subsidized by the premium tax credit for you, your spouse, and
dependent(s)" (`f1095c.pdf` p. 2).

IRS instructions used:

- Recipient: "Instructions for Recipient" — `docs/IRS-Forms/f1095c.pdf` p. 2 and p. 4 (form face p. 1, Part III p. 3;
  2025, "Created 5/21/25").
- Filer: `i109495c.pdf` (Instructions for Forms 1094-C and 1095-C, 2025) p. 7 (truncation), p. 11 (Plan Start
  Month), p. 12-14 (line 14), p. 14 (line 15), p. 14-15 (line 16 codes, line 17).
- Use on the return: `i8962_2025.pdf` p. 1 (family-member affordability), p. 5 ("Employer-sponsored coverage",
  affordability 9.02%); `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 5.

**Whose statement it is.** Part I line 2, "Social security number (SSN)" of the employee, stored as `employeeSSN`.
"For your protection, this form may show only the last four digits of your SSN" (`f1095c.pdf` p. 2; `i109495c.pdf`
p. 7). A new entry is pre-filled with the person tab's SSN under `employeeSSN`
(`us-tax-ui/src/app/service/statement-entry-state.service.ts:33`). Not offered on a dependent's tab
(`us-tax-ui/src/app/service/statement-selection.service.ts:133-138`).

**Person filter (the D-1a-1 question).** Not applicable to any amount: compute never loads `1095-c` entries (outside the
mapper the id appears only in `StatementFormCatalog.java:25`, `StatementSsnRules.java:88`,
`GenericFieldMapper.java:518-530` (extraction) and `UserDataBulkDelete.java:159`). The mapper lists every 1095-C of
the account (`MAP:81-91`). The only compute effect is the household-SSN check (D-1095-c-2).

**Storage.** `MAP` — parent keys `:113-141`, Part II rows `partIIMonthlyRows` `:154-169`, Part III
`partIIICoveredIndividuals` `:171-198`; entity `Se1095C` / `se_1095_c`, rows `Se1095CPart2MonthlyRow` /
`se_1095_c_part2_monthly_row` and `Se1095CPart3CoveredIndividual` / `se_1095_c_part3_covered_individual`. Every key the
UI binds has a mapper column; no typed value is lost. Part II is entered as rows of `period` ("All 12 Months" or a month
name) with the four line values (`UI:222-262`); codes are free text (placeholder "1A, 1B, 1C, …", "2A, 2B, 2C, …")
with no validation against the code list. Line 15 uses `p-inputNumber` (not the shared text parser, so D-ui-1 does not
apply; the amount is never negative).

## 3. Checkboxes and codes

**Line 14** (`f1095c.pdf` p. 2; `i109495c.pdf` p. 12-14). 1A qualifying offer (employee cost ≤ 9.5% as adjusted of the
FPL, plus spouse and dependents); 1B offer to employee only; 1C employee and dependents; 1D employee and spouse; 1E
employee, spouse and dependents; 1F offer not providing minimum value; 1G not full-time but enrolled in self-insured
coverage; 1H no offer (or not MEC); 1J / 1K spouse offered conditionally; 1L-1U individual coverage HRA variants (1R
not affordable, 1S not full-time); 1I, 1V-1Z reserved. For the PTC, the months coded with a minimum-value offer to the
person matter only when the offer is also affordable (line 15 test); 1F / 1H do not bar the credit.
Destination: none — not read (D-1095-c-1).

**Line 16** (`i109495c.pdf` p. 14-15). 2A not employed; 2B not full-time; **2C enrolled** in the coverage offered; 2D
limited non-assessment period; 2E multiemployer relief; 2F / 2G / 2H affordability safe harbors (W-2, FPL, rate of pay);
2I reserved. Only 2C affects the employee's PTC (`f1095c.pdf` p. 4). Destination: none — not read (D-1095-c-1).

## 4. Questions the app asks with this statement

On the Premium Tax Credit form: "Did you or your spouse have a job-based health insurance offer that cost more than
about 9% of your household income for self-only coverage?" (`employerCoverageNotAffordable`,
`us-tax-ui/src/app/forms/form-premium-tax-credit.component.ts:503-515`; help text says 9.02%, `:1223-1226`; required
for the form to be complete, `:1544`). Compute copies the answer to the Form 8962 output field
`line10EmployerCoverageAffordability` (`TRCS:12823`) and reads it nowhere else
(`output/Form8962OutputMapper.java:77`, `:179` only) — the answer changes no amount. The self-employed deductions
form asks for employer-plan months itself (`new_knowledge\forms\7206.md` §2).

## 5. Differences from the IRS instructions

Reused: **D-7206-5** — the Schedule 1 line 17 deduction ignores employer-plan eligibility months unless all 12 months
are ticked on the self-employed form; line 14 offers and line 16 code 2C / Part III enrollment are not consulted.

New:

**D-1095-c-1 — An affordable employer offer, or enrollment in employer coverage, never limits the premium tax credit.**
IRS: "you are considered eligible for MEC under the plan for a month only if the offer of coverage met a minimum
standard of affordability and provided … minimum value … The coverage offered by your employer is generally considered
affordable for you if your share of the annual cost for self-only coverage … is not more than 9.02% of your household
income … if you or your family member enrolls in employer-sponsored coverage for a month, you or your family member is
considered eligible for employer-sponsored coverage for that month" (`i8962_2025.pdf` p. 5); an individual eligible
for such MEC "is not in your coverage family for that month. Therefore, you cannot take the PTC for that individual's
coverage" (same page). Exception: no bar when the Marketplace, given accurate information, determined the offer
unaffordable and allowed APTC (same page).
Code: no compute path loads `1095-c`; Form 8962 monthly rows come from the 1095-A only (`TRCS:12892-12960`). The one
related answer, `employerCoverageNotAffordable`, is stored as an output flag only (`TRCS:12823`) — answering "No"
(the offer **was** affordable) still yields the full credit for every Marketplace month. Example: single filer,
household income $40,000, employer self-only cost $200 / month (6% — affordable, code 1E, minimum value), enrolled
in a Marketplace plan with no APTC for all 12 months, SLCSP $500 a month: IRS — no PTC for any month; app —
the full 12-month credit on Schedule 3 line 9. Effect: **silent overstated refundable credit** (or understated
repayment). Severity: **medium** (the Marketplace-determination exception covers many APTC enrollees, but not a filer
who claims the credit only at filing or whose offer arose mid-year).

**D-1095-c-2 — The household-SSN check can block a return on a statement that is never used.**
IRS: line 2 "may show only the last four digits of your SSN" (`f1095c.pdf` p. 2; `i109495c.pdf` p. 7).
Code: `1095-c` / `employeeSSN` is in the registry (`StatementSsnRules.java:88`); a truncated SSN ("XXX-XX-1234" →
"1234", `TRCS:53713-53719`) matches nobody and raises the **blocking** `STATEMENT_DOES_NOT_BELONG_TO_FAMILY`
(`TRCS:13892-13915`). The tab pre-fill (full SSN) avoids it unless the user types what is printed or uploads a scan.
Nothing reads the statement, so the block protects no amount. Effect: **BLOCKING** flag. Severity: **low**.
