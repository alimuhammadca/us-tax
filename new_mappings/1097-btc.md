## 1. What this statement is

Form 1097-BTC, Bond Tax Credit (**Rev. April 2025**, Copy B — form copy `se-statements/f1097btc.pdf` p. 2; not in
`docs\IRS-Forms\`), is sent by the issuer of a tax credit bond (or its agent, or a pass-through entity distributing the
credit) and reports the bond credit allowed to the holder for each month of the year. "You may be entitled to claim a
credit against your income tax liabilities, subject to certain limitations under section 54A(c) … For more information,
see Form 8912" (`f1097btc.pdf` p. 3, Instructions for Recipient).

Where it goes (`i8912.pdf`):

- **Form 8912 Part III line 13** — "Column (b1) Enter the Form 1097-BTC issuer's federal identification number … Column
  (b2) Enter the unique identifier shown in box 2b … Column (c) Enter the amount shown in box 1 of any Form 1097-BTC you
  receive" (`i8912.pdf` p. 3); line 14 total → Part I line 1 (p. 2-3). Fiscal-year filers use boxes 5a-5l (p. 3).
  Form 8912 → Schedule 3 line 6k.
- **Interest income** — "The current year credit on line 4 … is deemed to be a payment of qualified stated interest …
  and as such is treated as taxable interest income" (`i8912.pdf` p. 4) → Form 1040 line 2b.
- **Carryforward** — CREB credits (box 3 code 101) and pre-10/4/2008 QZAB credits cannot be carried forward (Form 8912
  line 3 / line 12 instructions, summarised in `CF:11-19`).

**Whose statement it is.** "RECIPIENT'S TIN", `recipientTIN` (`MAP:105`), seeded with the person tab's SSN on a new
entry (`us-tax-ui/src/app/service/statement-entry-state.service.ts:28-34`). Family check `StatementSsnRules.java:84`.

**Person filter (the D-1a-1 question).** The statement's **amounts are never read** — the credit is typed per person on
the Bond credit form (`bond-credit-taxpayer` `taxpayer1097BtcEntries` / `bond-credit-spouse` `spouse1097BtcEntries`,
`TRCS:46374-46390`; spouse rows only on a joint return). The statement list (unfiltered, `TRCS:6992`) is used only to
look up the **box 3 code** of a typed Part III row by its unique identifier, else by the issuer EIN when exactly one
statement has it (`CF:78-98`). A dependent's 1097-BTC therefore cannot add a credit; at worst its box 3 code could be
matched to a typed row with the same identifier (the row identifies the bond, so this is correct).

**Storage.** Entity `Se1097Btc` / table `se_form_1097_btc` (`MAP:95-129`). UI `syncPdfToForm` `UI:243-273`
(`parseAmountAndNotes`; no box is negative, D-ui-1 not relevant). Every replica cell has a mapper key. Upload extraction:
`GenericFieldMapper.java:687-689`, layout `:1353`.

## 3. Checkboxes and codes

- **Issuer checkboxes** — informational, stored.
- **Box 2a** — C / A / O; informational.
- **Box 3** — 101 (CREB) / 199 (other): 101 is read for the carryforward split (above).

## 4. Questions the app asks with this statement

The Bond credit form asks "Do you claim a bond credit?" (`claimsBondCredit`; Form 8912 is skipped when No,
`TRCS:46362-46366`) and, per person, a list of "Form 1097-BTC" rows (issuer name, EIN, unique identifier, credit amount)
plus "bonds held directly" rows (Part IV). An unused 2024 carryforward with "No" gets advisory
`FORM_8912_CARRYFORWARD_NOT_CLAIMED` (`TRCS:6996-7000`).

## 5. Differences from the IRS instructions

None new. Box 1 and the identifiers are retyped on the Bond credit form instead of being read from the uploaded
statement, with no comparison between the two; a mismatch is a usability gap (the IRS asks for the box 1 amount, and the
filer types it), not a rule deviation.
