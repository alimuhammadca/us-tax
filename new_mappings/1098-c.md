# Form 1098-C — Contributions of Motor Vehicles, Boats, and Airplanes

Statement form id `1098-c`. Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` =
`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`. No earlier deviation ids exist for this
statement; all ids below are new.

---

## 1. What this statement is

A charity (the **donee**) that receives a car, boat or airplane with a claimed value of more than $500 files Form 1098-C
and gives Copies B and C to the **donor** as the §170(f)(12) contemporaneous written acknowledgment; for a vehicle
claimed at $500 or less it may give Copy C only, with box 7 checked (`i1098c.pdf` p. 1). Form revision used: **Form
1098-C (Rev. April 2025)**, Copy C (`se-statements/f1098c.pdf` p. 3; the blank form is not in `docs/IRS-Forms` or
`public/irs`, so this copy — the one the UI replica was traced from — is used for captions).

IRS instructions used:

- Donor: "Instructions for Donor" — `se-statements/f1098c.pdf` p. 4 ("You must attach Copy B of Form 1098-C to your
  income tax return in order to take a deduction for the contribution of a qualified vehicle with a claimed value of more
  than $500"; boxes 1-7).
- Filer: `i1098c.pdf` p. 1-2 (Rev. 11-2019, the current edition; boxes 1-7).
- Where it goes: `i1040sca_2025.pdf` p. 10 (Schedule A line 12, gifts other than by cash: "If the amount of your
  deduction is more than $500, you must complete and attach Form 8283"; "Contribution of motor vehicle, boat, or airplane
  … The organization may use Form 1098-C"); `i8283.pdf` p. 7 (vehicle rules: deduct "the smaller of the vehicle's FMV …
  or the gross proceeds", exceptions; Section A column (b) vehicle checkbox / VIN), p. 8 (column (c) description).

**Whose statement it is.** The donor in "DONOR'S TIN", stored as `donorTIN` and mirrored to `recipientTIN`
(`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/statement/Form1098CMapper.java:106-108`, load `:142-144`). The
wrong-person check uses `donorTIN` (`StatementSsnRules.java:92`): a TIN outside the household blocks
(`STATEMENT_DOES_NOT_BELONG_TO_FAMILY`). A box-7 Copy C carries no donor TIN (`i1098c.pdf` p. 1 "do not … enter the
donor's TIN"), so such an entry is never checked.

**Storage.** Mapper `Form1098CMapper.java`; entity `Se1098C` / table `se_form_1098_c`. The list call returns every
1098-C of the account for the year — the whole household (`Form1098CMapper.java:71-80`). UI:
`us-tax-ui/src/app/forms/form-1098-c.component.ts` — `syncPdfToForm` (`:261-293`) copies every box into the model;
every saved key has a mapper column and every mapper key is on the screen. No UI box is lost. Upload extraction
(`field-mappings.json` key `1098-c`, `prebuilt-document`) maps only CORRECTED; the boxes are read by a label-anchored
cell layout (`GenericFieldMapper.java:564-572`, rows from `:1600`).

**Person filter (the D-1a-1 question).** None. `sum1098cGrossProceeds` totals box 4c over every 1098-C in the account and
drops the cap if any one of them has box 5a or 5b checked (`TRCS:15918-15933`, list loaded `TRCS:3127`, passed
`:5068` → `:14915`). On MFS the other spouse's 1098-C, and on a dependent's own return the parents', set or lift the cap
on the filer's vehicle deduction (**D-1098-c-1**).

## 4. Questions the app asks with this statement

On **Standard deductions** (Schedule A): "donated vehicle" amount (`charitableVehicleDonationAmount`, `TRCS:16321`) —
the claimed value; the 1098-C caps it. If the claim is above $500 and no 1098-C box 4c / 5a / 5b is on file, the blocking
(overrideable) `CHARITABLE_VEHICLE_1098C_REQUIRED` fires (`TRCS:16338-16369`). On **Form 8283**: the donation rows
(donee, `isVehicle`, `vehicleVin`, description, date, cost, FMV) — typed, never filled from the 1098-C.

## 5. Differences from the IRS instructions

**D-1098-c-1 — the 1098-C cap is figured for the whole household, not per donor and per vehicle.**
IRS: the deduction for *a* donated vehicle is "the smaller of the vehicle's FMV on the date of the contribution or the
gross proceeds received from the sale of the vehicle, unless an exception applies" (`i8283.pdf` p. 7) — vehicle by
vehicle, and only the donor's own gifts.
Code: `sum1098cGrossProceeds` adds box 4c of every 1098-C in the account and returns ZERO (no cap) if any entry has 5a /
5b (`TRCS:15918-15933`); there is no `donorTIN` filter; the typed vehicle amount is one total (`TRCS:16321`).
Effect: **silent wrong amount** — on MFS the other spouse's sale caps (or the other spouse's 5a / 5b uncaps) the filer's
vehicle; with two vehicles, one kept by the charity (5a) lifts the cap on one that was sold, and two sales of $500 or less
are capped at their total instead of $500 each. Severity: **low** (vehicle gifts are rare; two in one household rarer).

**D-1098-c-2 — box 7 ($500 maximum) is never read.**
IRS: "If this box is checked, your deduction in most cases equals the smaller of $500 or the donated vehicle's FMV"
(`f1098c.pdf` p. 4); a box-7 Copy C has boxes 4a-5c blank (`i1098c.pdf` p. 1).
Code: `maxDeduction500Applies` has no reader. A box-7 1098-C has no 4c / 5a / 5b, so `sum1098cGrossProceeds` returns
null — treated as "no 1098-C on file" (`TRCS:16343`): a claim above $500 raises `CHARITABLE_VEHICLE_1098C_REQUIRED`,
whose message says no Form 1098-C is on file; if the user overrides it, the full claimed value is deducted.
Effect: **blocking (overrideable), then silent overstatement on override**. Severity: **low**.

**D-1098-c-3 — box 6b goods and services are not subtracted from the vehicle gift.**
IRS: "Box 6b … Generally, the amount of your charitable contribution is reduced by the value of the goods and services
provided" unless 6c's intangible-religious-benefit box is checked (`f1098c.pdf` p. 4).
Code: `goodsAndServicesValueAmount` / `providedGoodsOrServices` have no reader; the only quid-pro-quo reduction is for
cash gifts (`TRCS:16311-16314`).
Effect: **silent overstatement** of Schedule A line 12 unless the user types the net value. Severity: **low**.

**D-1098-c-4 — no Form 8283 is required or produced for a vehicle claimed above $500.**
IRS: "If the amount of your deduction is more than $500, you must complete and attach Form 8283" (`i1040sca_2025.pdf`
p. 10); for a vehicle, Section A column (b) is checked and the 1098-C attached (`i8283.pdf` p. 7).
Code: the Form 8283 gate excludes the vehicle amount — "The vehicle amount is excluded from the test because it has its
own §170(f)(12) substantiation gate and is reported in Section A regardless" (`TRCS:16380-16386`) — but nothing reports
it: Form 8283 rows come only from the typed Form 8283 form (`TRCS:7845-7856`), and `charitableVehicleDonationAmount` has
no other reader.
Effect: **missing attachment** (no flag); the deduction amount is unaffected. Severity: **low-medium** (the return is
incomplete for any vehicle gift above $500 unless the user also fills Form 8283).

Unverified: whether a vehicle typed both as `charitableVehicleDonationAmount` and as a Form 8283 row with an FMV is
counted twice on Schedule A line 12 — the Form 8283 intake feeds Schedule A adjustments (`TRCS:16304-16306`); not traced
here.
