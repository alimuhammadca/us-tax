# Form 1098-E — Student Loan Interest Statement

Statement form id `1098-e`. Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` =
`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`. Line-level analysis already done in
`new_knowledge\10.md` (Schedule 1 line 21); its deviation ids D-10-2 (household sum → non-overrideable block on MFS /
dependent's own return, coordinator-verified) and D-10-3 (typed + statement double count) are reused, not re-derived.

---

## 1. What this statement is

A lender, loan servicer, government unit or school that received $600 or more of student loan interest from an
individual in the year sends Form 1098-E to the **borrower** (`i1098et--2025.pdf` p. 1-2, "Specific Instructions for
Form 1098-E"). Form version used: **Form 1098-E (2025)**, Copy B (`docs/IRS-Forms/f1098e.pdf` p. 3).

IRS instructions used:

- Borrower: "Instructions for Borrower" — `docs/IRS-Forms/f1098e.pdf` p. 4 ("You may be able to deduct student loan
  interest that you actually paid in 2025 … you may not be able to deduct the full amount of interest reported").
- Filer: `i1098et--2025.pdf` p. 2 (box 1 — includes capitalized interest and origination fees for loans made on or after
  1 September 2004; box 2).
- Where it goes: `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 98-99 (Schedule 1 line 21 and the Student Loan Interest Deduction Worksheet:
  "You can take this deduction only if … You paid interest in 2025 on a qualified student loan … Your filing status is
  any status except married filing separately … You, or your spouse if filing jointly, aren't claimed as a dependent";
  "A qualified student loan is any loan **you took out** to pay the qualified higher education expenses for … Yourself or
  your spouse … Any person who was your dependent when the loan was taken out"; worksheet line 1 "Don't enter more than
  $2,500"). Pub. 970 is not in `docs\books`.

**Whose statement it is.** The person in "BORROWER'S TIN", stored as `borrowerTIN`
(`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/statement/Form1098EMapper.java:113`). There is no `recipientTIN` alias
on this form. The wrong-person check uses `borrowerTIN` (`StatementSsnRules.java:90`): a TIN that is not the taxpayer,
spouse or a dependent raises the blocking `STATEMENT_DOES_NOT_BELONG_TO_FAMILY`. The UI does **not** offer 1098-E on a
dependent's tab — "student-loan interest — flow to the claiming parent"
(`us-tax-ui/src/app/service/statement-selection.service.ts:133-136`) — so a student's 1098-E is entered on a parent's tab.

**Storage.** Mapper `Form1098EMapper.java`; entity `Se1098E` / table `se_1098_e`. The list call returns every 1098-E of
the account (`uid`) for the year — the whole household (`Form1098EMapper.java:69-80`). UI:
`us-tax-ui/src/app/forms/form-1098-e.component.ts` — `syncPdfToForm` (`:373-409`) copies every displayed box into the
model; every saved key has a mapper column and every mapper key is on the screen except `borrowerSuffix`, which has no
box on the form (the name is one box). No UI box is lost. Upload extraction: Azure `prebuilt-tax.us.1098E`
(`us-tax-be/src/main/resources/field-mappings.json`, key `1098-e`), post-processed into the same keys
(`GenericFieldMapper.java:512-516`, `postProcess1099E` `:5904`).

**Person filter (the D-1a-1 question).** None. Line 21 adds `studentLoanInterestReceivedAmount` of every 1098-E in the
account (`TRCS:3257`, `:32787`, `:32973`) with no `borrowerTIN` check on any return type. On MFS legs and dependents'
own returns this is D-10-2 (non-overrideable block). On single / joint / HOH / QSS returns a 1098-E whose borrower is a
dependent (or whose TIN is blank) is deducted on the parent's line 21 (**D-1098-e-1**, new).

## 4. Questions the app asks with this statement

On the Income adjustments form (`income-adjustments-taxpayer` / `-spouse`, listed in `new_knowledge\10.md` §2):
"Student loan interest" (`studentLoanInterestDeductionLine21`) — its help text says "The deductible amount should come
from your Form 1098-E" (`us-tax-ui/src/app/forms/form-income-adjustments-taxpayer.component.ts:1683`), which is what makes
D-10-3 a double count. The dependent questions (`someoneCanClaimYou` / `someoneIsClaimingYou` on Standard deductions)
gate the deduction (`TRCS:32994-32998`).

Dead inputs: the engine still reads `received1098E` / `uploaded1098E` (gate `INCOME_ADJUSTMENTS_MISSING_UPLOADED_1098_E`,
`TRCS:33315-33322`) and `imported1098EBox1StudentLoanInterestTotal` (`TRCS:32973`), but no mapper stores any of them
(grep of `us-tax-be/src/main/java`), so the gate never fires and the imported total is always null. Harmless — the
statement list is read directly.

## 5. Differences from the IRS instructions

Reused:

| Id | Box | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| D-10-2 | 1 | Every 1098-E in the account counts: on an MFS leg the other spouse's, on a dependent's own return the parents', make line 21 > 0 and raise a **non-overrideable** blocker (the filer's own 1098-E on MFS can be cleared only by deleting the statement) |
| D-10-3 | 1 | Typed line 21 amount + 1098-E box 1 are added, though the help text tells the user to type the 1098-E figure — overstated deduction (capped at $2,500) |

New:

**D-1098-e-1 — a dependent's (or blank-TIN) 1098-E is deducted on the parent's single / joint / HOH return.**
IRS: "A qualified student loan is any loan you took out to pay the qualified higher education expenses for … Yourself or
your spouse … Any person who was your dependent when the loan was taken out" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 98-99) — the
filer must be the borrower; the 1098-E goes to the borrower (`i1098et--2025.pdf` p. 1-2).
Code: `sumStatementAmount(entries1099E, "studentLoanInterestReceivedAmount")` (`TRCS:32973`) has no `borrowerTIN` filter;
the wrong-person check passes any household TIN (`StatementSsnRules.java:90`); and the UI routes a student's 1098-E to the
parent's tab on purpose (`statement-selection.service.ts:135`).
Effect: **silent wrong amount** — a parent deducts interest on the child's own loan (up to $2,500), which the IRS does not
allow the parent; the child, being claimed, cannot deduct it either. Same root cause as D-1a-1. Severity: **medium-low**.

**D-1098-e-2 — box 2 (pre-September 2004 loans) not supported.**
IRS: "If checked, indicates that loan origination fees and/or capitalized interest are not included in box 1 for loans
made before September 1, 2004. See Pub. 970 for how to figure any deductible loan origination fees or capitalized
interest" (`f1098e.pdf` p. 4).
Code: `box1ExcludesOriginationFeesOrCapitalizedInterestPre2004` is never read (grep of `us-tax-be/src/main/java`).
Effect: **silent understatement** possible unless the user types the extra amount on line 21 (no prompt). Severity:
**low** (loans over 21 years old).
