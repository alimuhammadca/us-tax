# Form 1098-Q — Qualifying Longevity Annuity Contract Information

Statement form id `1098-q`. Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` =
`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`.

---

## 1. What this statement is

The **issuer** of a qualifying longevity annuity contract (QLAC) held in a qualified plan, 403(b), eligible 457(b) or
traditional IRA files Form 1098-Q and gives Copy B to the **participant** every year from the first premium until the
participant reaches 85 or dies, "to report the status of the contract" (`docs/IRS-Forms/f1098q.pdf` p. 4;
`i1098q.pdf` p. 3 "Who Must File"). Form revision used: **Form 1098-Q (Rev. April 2025)**, Copy B
(`docs/IRS-Forms/f1098q.pdf` p. 3).

IRS instructions used:

- Participant: "Instructions for Participant" — `docs/IRS-Forms/f1098q.pdf` p. 4 ("Prior to annuitization, the value of any
  QLAC held by your plan or traditional IRA … is not included when calculating the required minimum distribution (RMD)";
  boxes 1a-5l).
- Filer: `i1098q.pdf` (Rev. April 2025) p. 1-2 (premium limitation — "For 2025, the limitation amount is $200,000. This
  amount is adjusted for inflation …"), p. 3 (who files), p. 4 (boxes).

**Scope.** Out of scope by project decision (`CLAUDE.md`, "Out of Scope", added 2026-09-11, sc_00320): Form 1098-Q is an
issuer-filed information return with no line on Form 1040 or any schedule. Its only effect on a return is indirect —
the QLAC value left out of the RMD base, which changes the Form 5329 Part IX shortfall — and the app handles that through
a typed field (below). Nothing is missing for the 2025 return.

**Whose statement it is.** The participant in "PARTICIPANT'S TIN", stored as `participantTIN` and mirrored to
`recipientTIN` (`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/statement/Form1098QMapper.java:106-108`). The
wrong-person check uses `participantTIN` (`StatementSsnRules.java:91`). No compute path reads the statement, so there is
no person filter to get wrong.

**Storage.** Mapper `Form1098QMapper.java`; entity `Se1098Q` / table `se_form_1098_q`. List: every 1098-Q of the
account for the year (`Form1098QMapper.java:71-80`). UI: `us-tax-ui/src/app/forms/form-1098-q.component.ts` —
`syncPdfToForm` (`:280`) copies every box, including the 12 month / day pairs, into the model; every saved key has a
mapper column and every mapper key is on the screen. No UI box is lost. Upload extraction (`field-mappings.json` key
`1098-q`, `prebuilt-document`) maps CORRECTED and the issuer TIN; the boxes are read by a label-anchored cell layout
(`GenericFieldMapper.java:599`, rows incl. `:1995`).

## 4. Questions the app asks with this statement

None on the statement itself. The traditional IRA RMD inputs on the IRA form — prior-year-end balance
(`rmdPriorYearEndIraBalance`) and the QLAC value to leave out (`rmdQlacValue`) — feed the Form 5329 Part IX shortfall
(`TRCS:42933-42960`; history 2026-10-17 "Traditional IRA required minimum distribution computed (outstanding.md A61 …)").

## 5. Differences from the IRS instructions

None. Every box is stored and none reaches the return, which matches the IRS: the form has no Form 1040 destination.

Note (not a code issue): `CLAUDE.md` gives the 2025 QLAC premium limit as $210,000 (Notice 2024-80); the IRS copies in
`docs` say "For 2025, the limitation amount is $200,000. This amount is adjusted for inflation …" (`i1098q.pdf` p. 1-2)
and "cannot exceed $200,000" (`f1098q.pdf` p. 4). Notice 2024-80 is not in `docs\books`, so the $210,000 figure is
unverified here; the app does not use either number.
