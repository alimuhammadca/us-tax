# Form 1098 — Mortgage Interest Statement

Statement form id `1098`. Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` =
`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`. Earlier analysis reused here, not
re-derived: D-8-8 (`new_knowledge\8.md`, box 4 not person-filtered), D-sch-e-5 (`new_knowledge\forms\schedule-e.md`,
a linked 1098 on a property with personal-use days) and the V385 rental link (`history.md` 2026-10-24 "Form 1098 linked
to a rental (Schedule E line 12) …").

---

## 1. What this statement is

A lender (bank, mortgage servicer, government unit, cooperative housing corporation) that received at least $600 of
mortgage interest (including certain points) on one mortgage in the year sends Form 1098 to the **payer of record** —
the borrower. On this form the borrower is the "PAYER/BORROWER" and the lender is the "RECIPIENT". Form revision used:
**Form 1098 (Rev. April 2025)**, Copy B (`se-statements/f1098.pdf` p. 3, title; the blank form is not in
`docs/IRS-Forms` or `public/irs`, so this copy — the one the UI replica was traced from — is used for captions).

IRS instructions used:

- Payer/borrower: "Instructions for Payer/Borrower" on the back of Copy B — `se-statements/f1098.pdf` p. 4 (boxes 1-11).
- Filer: `i1098--2025.pdf` p. 5-6 (boxes 1-11; box 5 "To see if the applicability of this provision has been extended …").
- Where each box goes: `i1040sca_2025.pdf` p. 8 (lines 8, 8a — "Enter on line 8a mortgage interest and points reported
  to you on Form 1098"; "Refund of overpaid interest … don't reduce your deduction by the refund. Instead, see the
  instructions for Schedule 1 (Form 1040), line 8z"; "More than one borrower … you can only deduct your share"), p. 9
  (line 8c points not reported on Form 1098; **line 8d "Reserved for future use"**); `i1040se.pdf` p. 7 (Schedule E
  line 12 "If you have a mortgage on your rental property, enter on line 12 the amount of interest you paid … to banks or
  other financial institutions"; "Points … must be deducted over the life of the loan"); `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 92
  (Schedule 1 line 8z: "Reimbursements or other amounts received for items deducted in an earlier year, such as … home
  mortgage interest. See Recoveries in Pub. 525"); `p525.pdf` p. 24 (mortgage interest refund), p. 28 (nonitemized
  deduction recoveries).

**Whose statement it is.** The borrower in "PAYER'S/BORROWER'S TIN", stored as `payerBorrowerTIN` and mirrored to the
app-wide key `recipientTIN` (`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/statement/Form1098Mapper.java:107-108`,
`:140-142`). The lender's TIN is `recipientLenderTIN` — deliberately not `recipientTIN`. Form 1098 is the one statement
type **deliberately left out** of the wrong-person check (`StatementSsnRules.java:42-44, 110-111`): "a co-owner who is
not on this return may still deduct the mortgage interest they paid (Pub. 936)" — so a 1098 with any TIN never blocks.
The UI offers Form 1098 on every person tab, dependents included (not in `DEPENDENT_INAPPLICABLE_FORM_IDS`,
`us-tax-ui/src/app/service/statement-selection.service.ts:133-139`).

**Storage.** Mapper `Form1098Mapper.java`; entity `Se1098` / table `se_form_1098`. The list call returns every 1098 of
the account (`uid`) for the year — the whole household (`Form1098Mapper.java:71-80`). UI:
`us-tax-ui/src/app/forms/form-1098.component.ts` — the template binds `pdfRaw[…]` and `syncPdfToForm` (`:223-248`)
copies every box into the saved model; every saved key has a mapper column and every mapper key is on the screen. No
UI box is lost. Upload extraction (`field-mappings.json` key `1098`, `prebuilt-document`) maps only CORRECTED; the boxes
are read by two label-anchored cell layouts into the same keys
(`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/GenericFieldMapper.java:574-584`, `:1720-1815`).

**Person filter (the D-1a-1 question).** Three readers, three behaviours:

- **Box 4** (Schedule 1 line 8z): no filter at all — every 1098 in the account, on every kind of return (`TRCS:3053-3056`).
  D-8-8 records it for single / joint / HOH / QSS; the same loop also adds the other spouse's refund on an MFS leg and
  the parents' refund on a dependent's own return (**D-1098-1**).
- **Box 1 → Schedule E line 12**: only the 1098 the user picks for a rental property (`Form1098RentalLink.java:44-48`);
  no TIN check, by design (the user chose it).
- **Box 1 → Schedule A advisory**: 1098s whose borrower TIN is the taxpayer's or (not MFS) the spouse's, plus blank-TIN
  1098s (`Form1098RentalLink.java:103-105`, `filerTins` `:127-134`, called `TRCS:3557-3560`).

## 4. Questions the app asks with this statement

- **Rental income** form, per property (V385): "Which Form 1098 is for this property?" (`form1098EntryId`) and "Share of
  that Form 1098 for this property (%)" (`form1098SharePct`) — `Form1098RentalLink.java:25-26`; flags
  `FORM_1098_LINK_NOT_FOUND`, `FORM_1098_RENTAL_INTEREST_DIFFERS`, `FORM_1098_POINTS_ON_RENTAL` (all advisory).
- **Standard deductions** form (Schedule A): "Home mortgage interest paid" (`homeMortgageInterestPaid`, help text "Enter
  qualified home mortgage interest from Form 1098 … Box 1"), "Home mortgage points paid" (`homeMortgagePointsPaid`),
  home-equity interest and proceeds, average acquisition-debt balance(s), origination date, seller-financed interest
  (line 8b) — all typed; nothing is copied from the 1098.
- **Refund and Amount Owed** form: "Did you itemize deductions on your 2024 return?", 2024 itemized total, 2024 mortgage
  interest deducted — gate and limit the box 4 recovery (`new_knowledge\8.md` §2).

## 5. Differences from the IRS instructions

Reused:

| Id | Box | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| D-8-8 | 4 | Box 4 refund summed with no person filter on single / joint / HOH / QSS (a dependent's 1098 lands on the parent's 8z) |
| D-sch-e-5 | 1 | A linked 1098 on a property entered as whole-year totals goes to line 12 in full; the personal-use-days part is neither removed nor sent to Schedule A |

New:

**D-1098-1 — box 4 refund: no filter on MFS / dependent's-own returns either, and never split among co-borrowers.**
IRS: "Each borrower is entitled to deduct only the amount each borrower paid … Each borrower may have to include in
income a share of any amount reported in box 4" (`f1098.pdf` p. 4); a recovery is income only "in your income … up to
the amount of the deduction that reduced your tax" (`p525.pdf` p. 28) — i.e. the filer's own deduction.
Code: `TRCS:3053-3056` adds `refundOfOverpaidInterestAmount` of every 1098 in the account; there is no `scopedFilerSsn` /
`mfsOtherSpouseSsn` check and no share. Both MFS legs therefore report the whole household's refund, and a dependent's
own return reports the parents'. The 2024 limit (`priorYearMortgageInterestDeducted`) caps it only when entered.
Effect: **silent wrong amount** (overstated 8z) on MFS / dependent's own returns / co-owned loans. Extends D-8-8 to the
return types D-8-8 does not name. Severity: **low** (box 4 refunds are uncommon).

**D-1098-2 — a box 4 refund on a rental loan is treated as an itemized-deduction recovery.**
IRS: interest on a rental loan is deducted on Schedule E (`i1040se.pdf` p. 7) — a deduction taken "when you were
figuring your AGI"; for such a recovery "you must generally include the full amount of the recovery in your income"
(`p525.pdf` p. 28, Nonitemized Deduction Recoveries), whether or not the filer itemized.
Code: the box 4 amount is zeroed when the 2024 itemizing answer is No and otherwise capped by the 2024 itemized excess
(`TRCS:3060-3077`), with no regard to whether the 1098 is linked to a rental property (`form1098EntryId`).
Effect: **silent wrong amount** — a standard-deduction filer's refund of rental mortgage interest is left out of income.
Severity: **low**.

**D-1098-3 — box 6 points shown on Schedule A line 8c instead of 8a.**
IRS: "Enter on line 8a mortgage interest and points reported to you on Form 1098" (`i1040sca_2025.pdf` p. 8); line 8c is
"Points Not Reported on Form 1098" (p. 9).
Code: the single typed points field `homeMortgagePointsPaid` is printed on the Schedule A preview as
`line8c_points_not_reported_on_form_1098` (`us-tax-ui/src/app/forms/form-tax-return-schedulea.component.ts:226`); the
backend output has no 8a / 8c split (`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/model/output/ScheduleA.java:19-20`).
Effect: **cosmetic** — line 8e and the deduction are unchanged; the 1098-reported points sit on the wrong line.
Severity: **low**.

**D-1098-4 — the Schedule A advisory compares box 1 with one typed field only.**
IRS: line 8a = interest and points reported on Form 1098, your share (`i1040sca_2025.pdf` p. 8).
Code: `FORM_1098_INTEREST_NOT_ON_SCHEDULE_A` compares unlinked box 1 with `homeMortgageInterestPaid` only
(`Form1098RentalLink.java:112-117`); interest typed as home-equity interest (`homeEquityLoanInterestPaid`,
`TRCS:16190`) raises a false advisory, and box 6 points are never compared. A blank-TIN 1098 counts as the filer's.
Effect: **advisory only** (non-blocking; amount unaffected). Severity: **low**.

Not given an id: Schedule A is filled only from typed fields (owner decision, history 2026-10-24: "a 1098 may be for a
co-owner's loan or a second home, which the form cannot tell"); boxes 2 / 3 are replaced by typed average balances and
dates, which is what Pub. 936's limit needs.
