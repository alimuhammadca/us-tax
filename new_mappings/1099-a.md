# Form 1099-A — Acquisition or Abandonment of Secured Property

Statement form id `1099-a`. Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` =
`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`. Related analysis:
`new_knowledge\forms\schedule-f.md` (D-sch-f-6, 1099-A → Schedule F line 5b), `new_knowledge\forms\8949.md`,
`new_knowledge\forms\4797.md`.

---

## 1. What this statement is

A lender that acquires an interest in property that secured its loan (foreclosure, execution sale, deed in lieu), or
that learns the property was abandoned, sends Form 1099-A to the borrower. Copy B is the borrower's copy. Form revision
used: **Form 1099-A (Rev. April 2025)** (`docs/IRS-Forms/f1099a.pdf` p. 3, title).

IRS instructions used:

- Borrower: "Instructions for Borrower" — `docs/IRS-Forms/f1099a.pdf` p. 4: "You may have reportable income or loss
  because of such acquisition or abandonment. Gain or loss from an acquisition is generally measured by the difference
  between your adjusted basis in the property and the amount of your debt canceled in exchange for the property or, if
  greater, the sale proceeds. If you abandoned the property, you may have income from the discharge of indebtedness …
  Losses on acquisitions or abandonments of property held for personal use are not deductible."
- Filer: `i1099ac.pdf` (Rev. April 2025) p. 1-2 (boxes 1-6), p. 3 (coordination with Form 1099-C).
- Where it goes: Form 8949 / Schedule D for a capital asset; Form 4797 for business property ("Use Form 4797 to report
  … The sale or exchange of: 1. Real property used in your trade or business", `i4797.pdf` p. 1; "Dispositions of
  property as a result of foreclosure proceedings", p. 11); Schedule F line 5b for a forfeited CCC loan ("This amount
  may be reported to you on Form 1099-A", `i1040sf.pdf` p. 4).

**Whose statement it is.** The borrower is "BORROWER'S TIN", stored as `borrowerTIN`. The UI's new-entry seed does not
fill it (`us-tax-ui/src/app/service/statement-entry-state.service.ts:28-34` fills `recipientTIN` and similar keys only),
so a hand-typed 1099-A starts with a blank borrower TIN. A TIN of nobody in the household raises the blocking
`STATEMENT_DOES_NOT_BELONG_TO_FAMILY` (`us-tax-be/.../microservices/StatementSsnRules.java:77`; `TRCS:13885-13915`).

**Storage.** Mapper `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/statement/Form1099AMapper.java` (save keys
`:96-122`, list `:70`); entity `Se1099A` / table `se_1099_a`. UI `us-tax-ui/src/app/forms/form-1099-a.component.ts`,
`syncPdfToForm` `:284-310` — boxes 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, lender / borrower blocks and CORRECTED are saved; every saved key has a
mapper column (box 3 is "Reserved for future use" and is not shown). The UI never sends `lenderTelephone` (the mapper
column stays empty; the phone stays inside `lenderNameAddress`). Upload extraction: `GenericFieldMapper.java:696-698`
(`postProcess1099A`).

**What compute does with it.** Nothing is added to income from a 1099-A. It is used only by an advisory cross-check,
`crossCheckSpecialtyCapitalStatements` (`TRCS:11129-11174`, called `:3373`), which matches each 1099-A by its box 1
date against the hand-entered Form 8949 rows of the Capital gain / loss forms (`capital-gain-loss-taxpayer` /
`-spouse`, field `manualForm8949Transactions`) and raises non-blocking advisories:

- `FORM_1099A_NO_SALE_ENTERED` — no 8949 row on that date (`TRCS:11155-11161`);
- `FORM_1099A_AMOUNT_REALIZED_MISMATCH` — the row's proceeds differ from the amount realized, which the code takes as
  box 2 (not personally liable) or box 4 (personally liable; box 2 if box 4 is blank) (`TRCS:11150`, `:11162-11167`);
- `FORM_1099A_RECOURSE_SHORTFALL` — personally liable and box 2 > box 4: the difference may be canceled-debt income
  (`TRCS:11168-11173`).

**Person filter (the D-1a-1 question).** None — every 1099-A of the household is compared with both spouses' 8949 rows.
Because the result is advisory only, this has no amount effect.

## 4. Questions the app asks with this statement

None on the statement itself. The disposition must be entered by the user: a capital asset on the Capital gain / loss
form (Form 8949 row), business or rental property on the Business property sales form (Form 4797), a CCC forfeiture on
the Farm (Schedule F) form, and any canceled debt on the Other incomes form (line 8c, with the Form 982 questions).

## 5. Differences from the IRS instructions

Reused: **D-sch-f-6** — a 1099-A for a forfeited CCC loan is not routed to Schedule F line 5b (`new_knowledge\forms\schedule-f.md`).

New:

**D-1099-a-1 — the foreclosure cross-check looks only at Form 8949 rows.**
IRS: a foreclosure of business or rental real property is a disposition reported on Form 4797 ("The sale or exchange of
… Real property used in your trade or business", `i4797.pdf` p. 1; foreclosure dispositions, p. 11), and a forfeited CCC
loan is reported on Schedule F line 5b (`i1040sf.pdf` p. 4).
Code: `crossCheckSpecialtyCapitalStatements` builds its list only from `manualForm8949Transactions` of the two Capital
gain / loss forms (`TRCS:11131-11134`) and matches by date (`TRCS:11152-11154`); the Business property sales (Form 4797)
entries, the Farm form and imported 1099-B rows are not searched.
Effect: **cosmetic / misleading advisory** — a filer who correctly reported a rental-property foreclosure on Form 4797
still gets `FORM_1099A_NO_SALE_ENTERED` saying "no Form 8949 sale is entered", which invites entering the same
disposition a second time on Form 8949 (that would be a silent double count). No amount is computed from the 1099-A
either way. Severity: **low**.

Not given an id (unverified): the amount realized for a personally-liable debt is taken as box 4 even when box 4 is more
than box 2. The borrower instructions measure it as "the amount of your debt canceled in exchange for the property or,
if greater, the sale proceeds" (`f1099a.pdf` p. 4); Pub. 4681's Table 1-1, which the code comment cites, is not in
`docs\books\`, so the exact rule for FMV above the debt could not be checked. Advisory only.
