## 1. What this statement is

A broker or barter exchange sends Form 1099-B to the recipient (and the IRS) for sales of stocks, bonds, options,
commodities and other securities, for regulated futures / foreign currency / section 1256 option contracts (boxes
8–11), and for barter exchange income (box 13). "Brokers and barter exchanges must report proceeds from (and in some
cases, basis for) transactions to you and the IRS on Form 1099-B" (`f1099b.pdf p. 5`). Sales go to Form 8949 /
Schedule D (box 1d: "Report this amount on Form 8949 or on Schedule D (Form 1040) (whichever is applicable)",
`f1099b.pdf p. 5`); box 11 goes to Form 6781 ("Include this amount on your 2025 Form 6781", `f1099b.pdf p. 7`).

Sources: Copy B and Instructions for Recipient on the 2025 form copy `docs\IRS-Forms\f1099b.pdf` (Copy B p. 4,
recipient instructions p. 5 and p. 7); filer instructions `i1099b--2025.pdf`; `i8949.pdf`; `i1040sd.pdf`;
`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf`.

**Whose statement it is.** The statement belongs to the person in "RECIPIENT'S TIN" (`recipientTIN`). The mapper
lists every 1099-B of the account for the tax year — the whole household (`MAP:75-84`). Compute does filter by TIN,
but with catch-alls:

- **Sales (Form 8949):** `belongsToEitherCapitalPerson` (`TRCS:26243-26283`) keeps a 1099-B whose TIN is the
  taxpayer's or the spouse's, drops the other spouse's on MFS (`mfsOtherSpouseSsn`), and keeps **any other TIN
  (a dependent's, or blank)** when the taxpayer or spouse answered Yes to capital activity. This is the D-1a-1 pattern
  and is already recorded as **D-7a-1**.
- **Box 8–11 (Form 6781 → Schedule D):** grouped by TIN (`Form6781Compute.java:35-57`) and filtered by TIN on
  Schedule D (`TRCS:25295-25306`) — a dependent's box 11 stays off Schedule D; only the preview lists it (D-6781-6).
- **Box 4 (line 25b):** filtered by TIN only on MFS and dependent's-own returns (`TRCS:49762-49763`, `:49800`,
  helper `:51808-51823`). On single / joint / head-of-household returns every household 1099-B counts — including a
  dependent's (**D-1099-b-1**, same root cause as D-1a-1).
- **Boxes 14–16 (Schedule A line 5a):** filtered only on MFS (`TRCS:4324-4325`, `:8556-8571`) (**D-1099-b-2**).
- Belonging check: `STATEMENT_DOES_NOT_BELONG_TO_FAMILY` (blocking) fires for a `recipientTIN` that is nobody in the
  household (`StatementSsnRules.java:62`; `TRCS:13891-13915`).
- Community property (MFS / separate returns): proceeds, basis, wash sale and box 4 are split by
  `CommunityProperty.withCommunityPropertySplit` (`TRCS:3128-3135`); boxes 8–11 are not (D-6781-7).

## 4. Questions the app asks with this statement

On the Capital gain/loss form (`capital-gain-loss-taxpayer` / `-spouse`), see `new_knowledge\7a.md` section 2:

- "Did you have any capital gain or capital loss activity…?" (`hadCapitalGainOrLoss`) — the person gate that lets a
  1099-B with a dependent's / blank TIN through (D-7a-1).
- Statement checklist `received1099BOr1099Da` / `uploaded1099BOr1099Da` → blocking `MISSING_UPLOADED_1099_B_OR_1099_DA`
  when received but none saved; `CAPITAL_STATEMENT_UPLOAD_REQUIRED` (`validateCapitalStatementGating`
  `TRCS:26357-26431`).
- NUA employer stock: "Which sale (Form 1099-B)" `nuaStockSale1099BEntryId` + NUA amount and plan cost → the 1099-B row
  is split long / short (`TRCS:23686-23696`, `splitNuaStockSales` `:24951-25023`); flag `NUA_SALE_1099B_NOT_FOUND`.
- ISO shares: `isoStockSale1099BEntryId` + `isoSharesAmtBasis` → Form 6251 line 2k from box 1e (`TRCS:4140-4170`;
  UI `form-capital-gain-loss-taxpayer.component.ts:512-516`); advisory `ISO_AMT_BASIS_SALE_NOT_FOUND`.
- QOF: `hasQofDeferralOrTermination`, `qofForm8997AcknowledgedExternallyFiled` (Form 8997 flag).
- No question covers box 5 basis, box 7, box 1f, box 2 Ordinary, box 3 Collectibles, box 13 or a column (g) correction
  to a broker row (D-8949-3).

## 5. Differences from the IRS instructions

Referenced (recorded elsewhere): **D-7a-1** (dependent's / blank-TIN 1099-B on the parent's Form 8949), **D-7a-5**
(boxes 1f, 2 Ordinary, 3 Collectibles not read), **D-7a-8** (any 1099-B rules out Exception 1), **D-8949-1**
(term-less sale dropped), **D-8949-2** (box 7), **D-8949-3** (no column (g) on a broker row), **D-8949-4** (blank box
1e = 0), **D-8949-8** (rounding), **D-sch-d-2** (collectibles), **D-6781-1** (no straddle / hedge adjustment),
**D-6781-6** (6781 preview lists every 1099-B), **D-6781-7** (community property box 11 not halved).

New:

| ID | IRS rule | What the code does | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **D-1099-b-1** | Box 4 is the recipient's withholding: "Include this amount on your income tax return as tax withheld" (`f1099b.pdf p. 7`); line 25b takes withholding on "a 2025 Form 1099 … you received" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 39`) | `filterWithholdingEntriesForScopedFiler` is a no-op unless the return is MFS or a dependent's own (`TRCS:51808-51813`; scoped SSN stamped only by `MfsFormScoper.java:790, 856` / `DependentOwnFormScoper.java:176`). Same root cause as D-1a-1; also applies to 1099-DA box 4 | Refund overstated (withholding claimed twice); silent; medium (backup withholding is uncommon) |

On a single / joint / HOH return a dependent's box 16 is added; on the dependent's own return the parents' box 16 is added | Itemized deduction overstated; silent; low (box 16 on a 1099-B is rare; only when itemizing) |
| **D-1099-b-3** | Box 13 bartering is income: "Generally, you report this income on Schedule C" (`p525.pdf p. 20`; `f1099b.pdf p. 7`) | `barteringAmount` is saved (`MAP:164`) and read nowhere; no flag.
