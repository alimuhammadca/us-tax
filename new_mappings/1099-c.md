# Form 1099-C — Cancellation of Debt

Statement form id `1099-c`. Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` =
`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`. Line-level analysis already done in
`new_knowledge\8.md` (Schedule 1 line 8c) and `new_knowledge\forms\982.md` (Form 982 exclusions); their deviation ids

## 1. What this statement is

A federal government agency or an "applicable financial entity" sends Form 1099-C when it cancels a debt of $600 or
more, or when an identifiable event deems the debt canceled. Copy B is the debtor's copy. Form revision used:
**Form 1099-C (Rev. April 2025)** (`docs/IRS-Forms/f1099c.pdf` p. 3, title).

IRS instructions used:

- Debtor: "Instructions for Debtor" on the back of Copy B — `docs/IRS-Forms/f1099c.pdf` p. 4.
- Filer: `i1099ac.pdf` (Rev. April 2025) p. 3 (coordination with Form 1099-A), p. 6 (boxes 1-7).
- Where it goes: `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 91 (Schedule 1 line 8c); `i982.pdf` p. 1-3 (exclusions).

Debtor rule (`f1099c.pdf` p. 4): "If a creditor has discharged a debt you owed, you are required to include the
discharged amount in your income, even if it is less than $600, on the 'Other income' line of your Form 1040 … However,
you may not have to include all of the canceled debt in your income. There are exceptions and exclusions, such as
bankruptcy and insolvency."

**Whose statement it is.** The debtor is "DEBTOR'S TIN", stored as `debtorTIN`. The UI's new-entry seed fills only
`recipientTIN` / `employeeSSN` / `beneficiarySSN` / `shareholderTIN`
(`us-tax-ui/src/app/service/statement-entry-state.service.ts:28-34`), so a 1099-C typed by hand starts with a blank
debtor TIN. A TIN of nobody in the household raises the blocking `STATEMENT_DOES_NOT_BELONG_TO_FAMILY`
(`us-tax-be/.../microservices/StatementSsnRules.java:74`; check `TRCS:13885-13915`).

**Storage.** Mapper `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/statement/Form1099CMapper.java` (save keys
`:90-119`, list `:66`); entity `Se1099C` / table `se_1099_c`. The list returns every 1099-C of the account for the year.
UI: `us-tax-ui/src/app/forms/form-1099-c.component.ts` — `syncPdfToForm` (`:238-268`) saves every box 1-7, the
creditor and debtor blocks and CORRECTED; every saved key has a mapper column. The UI shows no VOID box (the mapper's
`void` column stays empty; VOID is a Copy A item anyway). Upload extraction: `GenericFieldMapper.java:704-706`
(`postProcess1099C`).

**Person filter (the D-1a-1 question).** Does not arise today: no amount box is read by compute (D-8-2). The list is
used only to count entries for the upload gate (`TRCS:3256`, `:33437-33442`). When box 2 is wired in, it will need a
`debtorTIN` filter; the Form 982 exclusions are already per-spouse-sensitive (D-982-1).

## 5. Differences from the IRS instructions

Reused:

| Id | Box | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| D-8-2 | 2 | Canceled debt never read; line 8c is typed only, while the UI promises box 2 flows automatically (high) |
| D-982-1 | 2 (exclusion) | Title 11 "Yes" on either spouse's form excludes all canceled debt of both spouses |
| D-982-2 | 2 (exclusion) | Qualified farm debt exclusion not limited by tax attributes + business basis |
| D-982-3 | 2 (exclusion) | QRPBI exclusion not limited; excess not added back |
| D-982-4 | — | Minimum tax credit never reduced |
| D-982-5 | 2 (exclusion) | QPRI uncapped when loan balance / qualified part are not entered |

New:

**D-1099-c-1 — a foreclosure reported on Form 1099-C alone (boxes 4, 5, 7) gets no deemed-sale check.**
IRS: a creditor that cancels a debt in connection with a foreclosure or abandonment "may file Form 1099-C only. You will
meet your Form 1099-A filing requirement for the debtor by completing boxes 4, 5, and 7 on Form 1099-C" (`i1099ac.pdf`
p. 3); for the debtor, box 7 means "You may have income or loss because of the acquisition or abandonment"
(`f1099c.pdf` p. 4).
Code: the A60 cross-check that tells the user a foreclosure is a sale and compares the Form 8949 proceeds with the
amount realized reads only `1099-a` entries (`crossCheckSpecialtyCapitalStatements`, `TRCS:11129-11174`, called
`:3373`); `fairMarketValueOfPropertyAmount` and `debtorPersonallyLiable` of a 1099-C are read nowhere.
Effect: **no advisory** — the user who received only a 1099-C for a foreclosure is not reminded to report the
disposition on Form 8949 / Form 4797; any gain is silently missing unless entered by hand. Severity: **low**.
