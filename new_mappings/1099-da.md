## 1. What this statement is

2025 is the first year of Form 1099-DA. A broker that effects a sale of a digital asset sends it to the customer: "If,
in 2025, you used a broker to effect the sale of a digital asset, your broker should send you a Form 1099-DA … In
2025, your broker has the option to report your basis … but is not required to do so … you must report gain or loss
from the transaction" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 6`). Sales go to Form 8949 boxes G–L (or Schedule D line 1a / 8a under
Exception 1) (`i8949.pdf p. 1, 3–4`). The filer must also answer the Form 1040 digital-asset question "whether or not
you received a Form 1099-DA" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 16`).

Sources: filer instructions `i1099da--2025.pdf` (box instructions p. 7–10); recipient instructions on the form copy
`docs\IRS-Forms\f1099da.pdf` (Copy B p. 4, Instructions for Recipient p. 5). **Note:** the copy in `docs\IRS-Forms\`
(and the field map in `us-tax-ui\public\irs\f1099da_*`) is the **2026** edition; its box numbering matches the box
list in `i1099da--2025.pdf p. 7–10`, which is used for the 2025 meaning of each box.

**Whose statement it is.** "RECIPIENT'S TIN", saved as `copyOther_recipient_tin` and read back as both
`copyA_recipient_tin` and `copyOther_recipient_tin` (`MAP:160`, `:233`). The mapper lists every 1099-DA of the account
for the tax year — the whole household (`MAP:82-90`).

- **Sales:** `belongsToEitherCapitalPersonByKey(entry, "copyA_recipient_tin", …)` (`TRCS:25040`, helper
  `:26252-26283`) — taxpayer / spouse TIN kept, other spouse dropped on MFS, **any other or blank TIN kept** when the
  taxpayer or spouse answered Yes: **D-7a-1** (a dependent's 1099-DA on the parent's return).
- **Belonging check:** the registry names the wrong key, so a stranger's 1099-DA is never flagged (**D-1099-da-2**).
- **Box 4:** dropped entirely on MFS / dependent's own returns (**D-1099-da-3**); on other returns every household
  1099-DA counts (same as **D-1099-b-1**).
- **Box 16:** not read (**D-1099-da-4**).
- Community property: box 1f, 1g, 1i and box 4 split (`TRCS:3136-3140`).

## 4. Questions the app asks with this statement

Capital gain/loss form (`new_knowledge\7a.md` section 2): `hadCapitalGainOrLoss` (person gate — D-7a-1);
`received1099BOr1099Da` / `uploaded1099BOr1099Da` → blocking `MISSING_UPLOADED_1099_B_OR_1099_DA`
(`TRCS:26423-26431`); QOF screening and Form 8997 acknowledgment. The Form 1040 digital-asset question is a separate
yes / no on the Digital assets form (`digitalAssetsYes` / `hadDigitalAssetActivity`, `TRCS:14364-14382`) and is not
checked against the 1099-DAs (D-1099-da-5). No question asks for a missing basis, holding period, box 5 or box 11c.

## 5. Differences from the IRS instructions

Referenced: **D-7a-1**, **D-7a-5** (1h, Ordinary), **D-7a-8**, **D-8949-1** (no row when box 6 and a date are blank —
routine for 2025 1099-DAs and for the aggregate stablecoin / NFT method), **D-8949-2** (box 5), **D-8949-3**,
**D-8949-4** (blank 1g = 0), **D-8949-8**, **D-1099-b-1** (box 4 of a dependent's 1099-DA on the parent's line 25b on
single / joint / HOH returns — same code).

New:

| ID | IRS rule | What the code does | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **D-1099-da-1** | "Applicable checkbox on Form 8949. Indicates where to report this transaction" (`f1099da.pdf p. 5`); codes G / H / J / K / Y (`i1099da--2025.pdf p. 7`) | The UI shows and saves `copyOther_applicable_checkbox_on_form_8949` (`UI:93`, `:639`) and extraction fills it, but `Form1099DaMapper` has no key and `Se1099Da` no column (`MAP:126-196`) — the typed value is discarded and the box is blank on reopen | UI-bound field with no backend column; compute would not read it anyway; cosmetic; low |
| **D-1099-da-2** | Each statement belongs to its recipient | The family-belonging registry checks `recipientTIN` for `1099-da` (`StatementSsnRules.java:63`), but 1099-DA entries carry only `copyA_recipient_tin` / `copyOther_recipient_tin` (`MAP:233`); every 1099-DA is skipped as "no TIN" (`TRCS:13900-13904`). A 1099-DA of someone outside the household is then added to the taxpayer's Form 8949 through the catch-all (`TRCS:26276-26282`) with no `STATEMENT_DOES_NOT_BELONG_TO_FAMILY` | Another person's sale on this return; silent; low (needs a mis-filed statement) |
