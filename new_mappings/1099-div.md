# Form 1099-DIV — Dividends and Distributions

Statement form id `1099-div`. Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` =
`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`. Line-level analysis already done in
`new_knowledge\2a.md`, `3a.md`, `3b.md`, `3c.md`, `7a.md`, `forms\8814.md`, `forms\schedule-b.md`, `forms\schedule-d.md`;
their deviation ids are reused here. Shared deviations D-1099-int-1 … D-1099-int-3 are defined in
`new_mappings\1099-int.md`.

---

## 1. What this statement is

A corporation, mutual fund (RIC), REIT or broker sends Form 1099-DIV to each person it paid $10 or more of dividends or
other distributions on stock, or $600 or more in a liquidation (or from whom it backup-withheld / for whom it paid
foreign tax). Copy B is the recipient's. Form revision used: **Form 1099-DIV (Rev. January 2024)**
(`docs/IRS-Forms/f1099div.pdf` p. 1, title).

IRS instructions used:

- Recipient: "Instructions for Recipient" — `docs/IRS-Forms/f1099div.pdf` p. 5 (every box).
- Filer: `i1099div.pdf` p. 4-5 (Rev. January 2024).
- Where each box goes: `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 25 (line 2a, line 3a), p. 26 (line 3b), p. 31 and p. 33 (line 7a,
  Exception 1), p. 39 (line 25b), p. 115 (Schedule 3 line 1); `i1040sb.pdf` p. 2 (Schedule B Part II);
  `i8995.pdf` p. 3 (qualified REIT dividends); `i6251.pdf` p. 3 (Form 6251 line 2g); `i8949.pdf` p. 5 (nondividend
  distributions); `i1040sd.pdf` p. 4 (liquidations); `i1040sca_2025.pdf` p. 3 (Schedule A line 5a).

**Whose statement it is.** "RECIPIENT'S TIN", stored as `recipientTIN`; pre-filled from the active person tab on
creation (`us-tax-ui/src/app/service/statement-entry-state.service.ts:28-34`).

**Storage.** Mapper `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/statement/Form1099DivMapper.java`; entity
`Se1099Div` / table `se_1099_div`, state rows `Se1099DivStateInfo` / `se_1099_div_state_info`. Listing returns every
1099-DIV of the account for the year (`Form1099DivMapper.java:74-83`). UI
`us-tax-ui/src/app/forms/form-1099-div.component.ts` — `syncPdfToForm` (`:368-415`) saves every box (box 11 is the FATCA
checkbox; box 5 is sent as `section199ADividendsAmount`, stored in `section_199a_dividends_amount`,
`Form1099DivMapper.java:148`). Every saved key has a column; no UI box is lost. Upload extraction: Azure
`prebuilt-tax.us.1099DIV` (`field-mappings.json` key `1099-div`; `GenericFieldMapper.java:725-728`).

**Person filter (the D-1a-1 question).** Loaded with the community-property split of boxes 1a, 1b, 2a-2d and 4
(`TRCS:3119-3126`). Every income path keeps a statement for a person only when `recipientTIN` is that person's SSN
(`belongsToPerson`, `TRCS:36021-36040`): line 3a / 3b (`TRCS:19942`), line 2a box 12 (`TRCS:19280-19285`), box 2a-2d
(`TRCS:24774-24782`), box 5 (`TRCS:17543-17548`). So a dependent's 1099-DIV is **not** added to the parent's lines; it
is routed to the parent's Form 8814 (`TRCS:24420-24428`) — see D-3c-1 … D-3c-3. Remaining person problems: blank TIN
(D-3b-3, D-2a-3, D-7a-1), a person whose dividend or capital form is not Yes (D-3b-2, D-7a-2), box 4 (D-1099-int-1),
box 7 (D-1099-int-2), box 16 (D-1099-int-3). A TIN of no household member blocks (`STATEMENT_DOES_NOT_BELONG_TO_FAMILY`,
`TRCS:13891-13910`; `StatementSsnRules.java:61`).

## 4. Questions the app asks with this statement

- Dividend income form (`dividend-income-taxpayer` / `-spouse`, `new_knowledge\3b.md` §2): "Did you have dividend
  income…" (`hadDividendIncome` — the per-person gate behind D-3b-2), hand-entered dividends, nominee amounts, the
  qualified-dividend exceptions (holding period, short sales, payments in lieu …, `new_knowledge\3a.md`).
- Capital gain/loss form (`new_knowledge\7a.md` §2): "Did you receive Form 1099-DIV with capital gain
  distributions?", "On any of your 1099-DIVs, are amounts filled in for … box 2b, 2c, 2d?", capital gain
  distributions not on a statement, nominee box 2a amount to subtract
  (`us-tax-ui/src/app/forms/form-capital-gain-loss-taxpayer.component.ts:1507-1549`). There is no question for box 3,
  9 or 10 (D-1099-div-1).
- QBI deduction form: "received / uploaded 1099-DIV with section 199A dividends" (`TRCS:17352-17353`) — the gate of
  D-1099-div-2.
- Foreign tax credit form: `hasForeignTaxCredit`, `claimsSimplifiedException`, `simplifiedForeignTaxesOverride`.
- Child's dividends: Form 8814 on the dependent (`new_knowledge\3c.md`).

Reused:

| Id | Box | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| D-3b-2 | 1a, 1b, 5, 13 | A person whose dividend form is not Yes loses all his or her 1099-DIVs, no flag |
| D-3b-3 / D-2a-3 | all amounts | Blank TIN → "the taxpayer's" on every return of the household |
| D-3b-4 | 1a | Schedule B line 5 does not foot to line 6 when Form 8814 / PFIC items apply |
| D-3c-1 / D-3c-2 / D-3c-3 | child's 1099-DIV | Form 8814 built with no election; box 4 withholding does not stop it; MFS double reporting |
| D-7a-1 / D-7a-2 / D-7a-8 | 2a-2d | Blank TIN; person without a capital form loses box 2a; Exception 1 ruled out by others' statements |
| D-7a-7 | child's 2b-2d | Form 8814 reads only box 2a; the 28% / 1250 / 1202 parts are not carried to the parent's worksheets |
| D-8814-2 / D-8814-4 | child's 13 / 12 | Child's PAB dividends never reach Form 6251; exempt-interest dividends left off Form 8814 line 1b |
| D-2a-4 | 12 | Box 12 not split for community property on MFS |
| D-sch-b-5 / D-sch-b-6 | 1a | Preview shows 15 dividend rows; layout labels |
| D-1099-int-1 | 4 | Dependent's backup withholding on the parent's line 25b (defined in `1099-int.md`) |
| D-1099-int-2 | 7 | Foreign tax not attributed to a person (defined in `1099-int.md`) |
| D-1099-int-3 | 16 | State withholding to Schedule A line 5a without a person filter except on MFS (defined in `1099-int.md`) |

New:

**D-1099-div-1 — nondividend (box 3) and liquidation (boxes 9, 10) distributions are never read and nothing prompts for
the taxable part.**
IRS: box 3 "Shows a return of capital. To the extent of your cost (or other basis) in the stock, the distribution
reduces your basis and is not taxable. Any amount received in excess of your basis is taxable to you as capital gain"
(`docs/IRS-Forms/f1099div.pdf` p. 5); "Enter the name of the payer of any taxable nondividend distributions in column (a)
on Part I with box C or box I checked or Part II with box F or box L checked" (`i8949.pdf` p. 5); liquidation
distributions are capital transactions — "Amounts received by shareholders in corporate liquidations. See Pub. 550"
(`i1040sd.pdf` p. 4).
Code: `nondividendDistributionsAmount`, `cashLiquidationDistributionsAmount` and
`noncashLiquidationDistributionsAmount` are written and read back by the mapper (`Form1099DivMapper.java:143, 155, 157`)
and referenced nowhere else in `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/`; the capital form has no question
about them. Effect: **silent** — correct when the distribution is within basis (nothing to report; the user must
still lower basis for a later sale), **understated capital gain** when it exceeds basis or when a liquidation completes
with a gain / loss, unless the user knows to add a Form 8949 row by hand. Severity: **low** (most box-3 amounts are
within basis; liquidations are rare).

**D-1099-div-2 — section 199A dividends (box 5) are dropped unless a QBI form is answered Yes.**
IRS: "Individuals … use Form 8995 to figure the QBI deduction if: • You have QBI, qualified REIT dividends, or qualified
PTP income or loss" (`i8995.pdf` p. 1); qualified REIT dividends are "reported to you on Form 1099-DIV, line 5"
(`i8995.pdf` p. 3).
Code: box 5 is read only inside `collectQbiInputsForPerson` (`TRCS:17370-17377`, sum at `:17533-17550`); the whole QBI
computation returns null when neither QBI form says `hadQualifiedBusinessIncomeInputs` and there is no Schedule C QBI
(`TRCS:17034-17049`). No advisory reads `section199ADividendsAmount` (the only other reference, `TRCS:19957`, is
summed into `DividendPersonTotals` and not used for a flag). Effect: **silent wrong amount** — Form 1040 line 13a is
missing the 20% REIT-dividend deduction for a filer who uploads a 1099-DIV with box 5 but never opens the QBI form
(the user's tax is overstated). Severity: **medium** (box 5 is common on fund 1099-DIVs; the deduction is 20% of it).
