# Form 1099-G — Certain Government Payments

Statement form id `1099-g`. Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` =
`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`; `CP` =
`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/CommunityProperty.java`. Line-level analysis already done in
`new_knowledge\8.md` (Schedule 1 lines 1, 7, 8z), `forms\schedule-f.md` and `1099-int.md` (withholding); their
deviation ids are reused here rather than re-derived.

## 1. What this statement is

A federal, state or local government unit sends Form 1099-G to report unemployment compensation, state or local income
tax refunds, credits or offsets, RTAA payments, taxable grants, agricultural payments and CCC-loan market gain. Copy B
is the recipient's copy. Form revision used: **Form 1099-G (Rev. March 2024)**, the revision in force for 2025
(`docs/IRS-Forms/f1099g.pdf` p. 4, title).

IRS instructions used:

- Recipient: "Instructions for Recipient" on the back of Copy B — `docs/IRS-Forms/f1099g.pdf` p. 5.
- Filer: `i1099g--2024.pdf` p. 2 (boxes 1-11) — the Rev. March 2024 booklet; `i1099g.pdf` is the December 2026
  revision and is not used.
- Where each box goes: `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 88-89 (Schedule 1 line 1 and line 7), p. 92 (line 8z, RTAA), p. 39
  (Form 1040 line 25b); `i1040sf.pdf` p. 4 (Schedule F lines 4a / 4b, 6a); `i1040sca_2025.pdf` p. 3 (Schedule A
  line 5a).

**Whose statement it is.** The recipient is "RECIPIENT'S TIN", stored as `recipientTIN`. A new 1099-G created on a
person tab is pre-filled with that person's SSN (`us-tax-ui/src/app/service/statement-entry-state.service.ts:28-34`).
A TIN of nobody in the household raises the blocking `STATEMENT_DOES_NOT_BELONG_TO_FAMILY`
(`us-tax-be/.../microservices/StatementSsnRules.java:65`; check `TRCS:13885-13915`; a blank TIN is not checked).

**Storage.** Mapper `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/statement/Form1099GMapper.java` (save keys
`:100-136`, state rows `:139-157`, list `:70-79`); entity `Se1099G` / table `se_1099_g`, state rows `Se1099GStateInfo` /
`se_1099_g_state_info`. The list returns every 1099-G of the account (`uid`) for the year — the whole household.
UI: `us-tax-ui/src/app/forms/form-1099-g.component.ts` — the template binds `pdfRaw[...]` (`:28-65`) and
`syncPdfToForm` (`:243-280`) copies every box into the saved model; every saved key has a mapper column and every
box of Copy B is on the screen, **except** the unnumbered "blank box next to box 9" for refund interest
(D-1099-g-3). Payer telephone is split off the last line of the payer block (`:249-255`). Upload extraction: Azure,
post-processed by `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/GenericFieldMapper.java:717-722, 736-739`.

**Person filter (the D-1a-1 question).** It differs box by box:

- **box 2** (Schedule 1 line 1): no person filter on single / joint / head-of-household / QSS returns — a
  dependent's 1099-G refund lands on the parent's line 1 (**D-8-8**). On MFS the other spouse's 1099-Gs are dropped
  (`CP:612` `withoutOtherSpouse`; community-property states split them, `CP:613-617`).
- **box 4** (line 25b): filtered only on MFS / dependent's-own returns (**D-1099-int-1**, `TRCS:49802`,
  `:51808-51823`).
- **box 11** (Schedule A line 5a): filtered only on MFS (**D-1099-int-3**, `TRCS:8537-8538, 8553-8573`).
- **box 1** is never read (D-8-1), so the question does not arise for it except through the community-property shift
  (D-1099-g-4).

## 4. Questions the app asks with this statement

On the Other incomes form (`other-incomes-taxpayer` / `-spouse`; full list in `new_knowledge\8.md` §2):
"received 1099-G" / "uploaded 1099-G" (`received1099G`, `uploaded1099G` — blocking
`OTHER_INCOME_MISSING_UPLOADED_1099_G` when received but none on file, `TRCS:33427-33434`); the "uploaded every
statement" confirmation (`OTHER_INCOME_STATEMENT_CONFIRMATION_REQUIRED`, blocking, `TRCS:33418-33425`); typed
unemployment `unemploymentCompensationLine7` (the UI tells the user box 1 is applied automatically and to type only
amounts not on a 1099-G — 8.md row 11); repayments `unemploymentRepaymentAdjustment`; typed line 1
`taxableStateLocalRefundsLine1`. The 2024 itemizing questions that drive the refund worksheet are on the Refund and
Amount Owed form (`priorYearItemizedDeductionsClaimed`, `priorYearTotalItemizedDeductions`,
`priorYearStandardDeductionAvailable`, `priorYearStateLocalTaxesBeforeCap`, `priorYearFilingStatus`,
`priorYearTaxableIncome`). Contributions to a governmental unemployment / paid-family-leave program are not asked
(D-8-12).

## 5. Differences from the IRS instructions

Reused (analysed at line level; see the cited file):

| Id | Box | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| D-8-1 | 1 | Unemployment compensation never read; line 7 is typed only, while the UI says box 1 is applied automatically (high) |
| D-8-5 | 5, 6, 7 | RTAA, taxable grants and agriculture payments never read |
| D-8-6 | 2 | A typed line 1 refund is added on top of the box 2 amount |
| D-8-7 | 3 | Year of the refund not read; the Pub. 525 exceptions are never detected |
| D-8-8 | 2 | Box 2 not filtered by person on single / joint / HOH / QSS returns (dependent's refund on the parent's line 1) |
| D-8-12 | 1 | Governmental UC / paid-family-leave contributions not asked |
| D-sch-f-6 | 7 | 1099-G farm payments not routed to Schedule F |
| D-1099-int-1 | 4 | Box 4 withholding of a dependent credited on the parent's line 25b |
| D-1099-int-3 | 11 | State withholding on Schedule A line 5a not filtered by person except on MFS |

New:

**D-1099-g-1 — box 8 (trade or business) is ignored; the refund goes to Schedule 1 line 1.**
IRS: "If this box is checked, the amount in box 2 is attributable to an income tax that applies exclusively to income
from a trade or business … If taxable, report the amount in box 2 on Schedule C or F (Form 1040), as appropriate"
(`f1099g.pdf` p. 5; filer rule `i1099g--2024.pdf` p. 2).
Code: `tradeOrBusinessIncome` is saved (`Form1099GMapper.java:133`) and read nowhere in `microservices\` outside the
mapper; box 2 always enters the line 1 path (`TRCS:3024-3040`, `:32146-32166`), where the itemized-deduction worksheet
can even zero it for a filer who did not itemize — but a business tax was deducted on Schedule C / F, not Schedule A.
Effect: **silent wrong amount / wrong line** — income on line 1 instead of Schedule C / F (no self-employment tax on
it), or dropped by the worksheet. Severity: **low** (business-only state income taxes are rare).

**D-1099-g-2 — box 9 market gain is never read.**
IRS: "Shows market gain on CCC loans … See the Schedule F (Form 1040) instructions" (`f1099g.pdf` p. 5); line 4a
includes "Market gain from the repayment of a secured Commodity Credit Corporation (CCC) loan"; "if you didn't report
the CCC loan proceeds under the election, you must report the market gain on line 4b" (`i1040sf.pdf` p. 4).
Code: `marketGainAmount` is saved (`Form1099GMapper.java:134`) and never read by compute; the Schedule F path reads only
1099-PATR (`new_knowledge\forms\schedule-f.md` §2). D-sch-f-6 covers box 7, not box 9. Effect: **silent
under-reporting** unless the farmer types it on the farm form; no advisory. Severity: **low**.

**D-1099-g-3 — refund interest in the blank box next to box 9 has no field.**
IRS: "the payer may include interest of less than $600 in the blank box next to box 9 on Form 1099-G. Regardless of
whether the interest is reported to you, report it as interest income on your tax return" (`f1099g.pdf` p. 5;
`i1099g--2024.pdf` p. 2).
Code: the UI has no input for that box (`form-1099-g.component.ts:61-65`) and the mapper no column. Effect: **silent
under-reporting** of line 2b if the user does not type it on the Interest income form. Severity: **low**.

**D-1099-g-4 — community-property MFS: line 7 can go negative.**
IRS: line 7 reports the unemployment compensation in box 1 (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 89); on community-property MFS
each spouse reports his or her share (Form 8958 line 12).
Code: line 7 never contains box 1 (D-8-1) — `line7Gross` = typed amounts only (`TRCS:32316`). `CP.split1099G` still
computes a shift from box 1: for the filer's own 1099-G it **subtracts** the share moved to the other spouse
(`CP:621-634`, `:629`: `if (filer.equals(tin)) { shift = shift.subtract(moved) … }`), and `TRCS:32318-32320` adds that shift
to `line7Gross`. With nothing typed (as the UI instructs), the filer's line 7 is negative (e.g. a $10,000 box 1 at 100%
community → about −$5,000 instead of +$5,000), and it flows into line 10 unfloored (`TRCS:32341-32343`, `:32580`,
`:32691`). The other spouse's leg gets +$5,000. Effect: **silent wrong amount** (income understated by the full box 1
on the recipient's leg). Severity: **medium** (rare situation, but sign-reversed income with no flag). Fixing D-8-1
removes it.
