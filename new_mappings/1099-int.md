# Form 1099-INT — Interest Income

Statement form id `1099-int`. Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` =
`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`. Line-level analysis already done in
`new_knowledge\2a.md`, `2b.md`, `10.md`, `forms\8814.md`, `forms\schedule-b.md`; their deviation ids are reused here
rather than re-derived.

## 1. What this statement is

A bank, broker, government agency or other payer sends Form 1099-INT to each person it paid at least $10 of interest
(or from whom it backup-withheld, or for whom it paid foreign tax). Copy B is the recipient's copy. Form revision used:
**Form 1099-INT (Rev. January 2024)**, the current revision for 2025 (`docs/IRS-Forms/f1099int.pdf` p. 1, title).

IRS instructions used:

- Recipient: "Instructions for Recipient" on the back of Copy B — `docs/IRS-Forms/f1099int.pdf` p. 5 (boxes 1-10) and
  p. 7 (boxes 11-17, nominees).
- Filer: `i1099int.pdf` p. 2-4 (Rev. January 2024; this one booklet covers 1099-INT and 1099-OID).
- General: `i1099gi--2025.pdf` p. 11 (VOID / CORRECTED).
- Where each box goes on the return: `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 25 (lines 2a / 2b), p. 39 (line 25b), p. 95
  (Schedule 1 line 18), p. 115 (Schedule 3 line 1); `i1040sb.pdf` p. 1-2 (Schedule B Part I); `i6251.pdf` p. 3
  (Form 6251 line 2g); `i1040sca_2025.pdf` p. 3 (Schedule A line 5a).

**Whose statement it is.** The recipient is the person in "RECIPIENT'S TIN". Stored as `recipientTIN`. When a 1099-INT
is created on a person tab, the UI pre-fills that person's SSN (`us-tax-ui/src/app/service/statement-entry-state.service.ts:28-34`).

**Storage.** Mapper `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/statement/Form1099IntMapper.java`; entity
`Se1099Int` / table `se_1099_int`, state rows `Se1099IntStateInfo` / `se_1099_int_state_info`. The list call returns
every 1099-INT of the account (`uid`) for the year — the whole household, not one person (`Form1099IntMapper.java:74-83`).
UI: `us-tax-ui/src/app/forms/form-1099-int.component.ts` — the template binds `pdfRaw['boxN']` and
`syncPdfToForm` (`:276-319`) copies every box into the saved model; every saved key has a mapper column, and every
mapper key is shown on the screen. No UI box is lost. Upload extraction (Azure `prebuilt-tax.us.1099INT`) maps to the
same keys (`us-tax-be/src/main/resources/field-mappings.json`, key `1099-int`; post-processing
`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/GenericFieldMapper.java:717-734`).

**Person filter (the D-1a-1 question).** Compute keeps a 1099-INT for a person only if `recipientTIN` equals that
person's SSN (`belongsToPerson`, `TRCS:36021-36040`), so a dependent's 1099-INT (child's SSN) is **not** added to a
parent's line 2a / 2b — D-1a-1 does not repeat for the income boxes. It does repeat for:

- a **blank** recipient TIN — counted as "the taxpayer's" on every return of the household (D-2a-3, D-2b-2);
- **box 2** (Schedule 1 line 18): no person filter at all (D-10-1);
- **box 4** (line 25b): no person filter on single / joint / head-of-household / QSS returns (**D-1099-int-1**, new);
- **box 6** (foreign tax credit): no person filter (**D-1099-int-2**, new);
- **boxes 15-17** (Schedule A line 5a): filtered only on MFS (**D-1099-int-3**, new).

A TIN that belongs to nobody in the household raises the blocking `STATEMENT_DOES_NOT_BELONG_TO_FAMILY`
(`TRCS:13891-13910`; rule `us-tax-be/.../microservices/StatementSsnRules.java:60`). So D-2b-3 ("filled TIN matching
neither filer is dropped with no message") is narrower than its wording: it is silent only when the TIN is a household
member who is not a filer on this return (for example the spouse's SSN on a joint return where `spouse.ssn` is blank).

A dependent's 1099-INT is read instead by the parent's Form 8814 (`TRCS:24406-24419`) — see `new_knowledge\3c.md` and
`forms\8814.md`.

## 5. Differences from the IRS instructions

Reused (analysed at line level; see the cited file for the full row):

| Id | Box | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| D-2a-3 / D-2b-2 | all amounts | Blank recipient TIN → counted on every return of the household (both MFS legs, every dependent's own return) |
| D-2a-4 / D-2b-7 | 1, 3, 8, 13 | Community property MFS split covers boxes 1, 3, 8 only; box 13 premium not split |
| D-2b-3 | TIN | Filled TIN of a household member who is not a filer on this return is dropped silently (a non-household TIN blocks — see §1) |
| D-2b-4 | 10 | Market discount never read |
| D-2b-6 | 11, 12 | Premium netted inside the payer line; does not trigger Schedule B and is not shown as "ABP Adjustment" |
| D-2b-8 | 1, 3 | Rounding per person before the total (±$1) |
| D-10-1 | 2 | Early withdrawal penalty summed for the whole household (other spouse's on MFS, a dependent's on a parent's return, the parents' on a dependent's own return) |
| D-8814-2 | 9 | A child's private activity bond interest never reaches the parent's Form 6251 |
| D-3c-1 / D-3c-2 | child's 1099-INT | Form 8814 built without an election; box 4 backup withholding of the child does not stop it |
| D-sch-b-5 / D-sch-b-6 | 1 | Schedule B preview shows 14 payer rows; adjustment labels not the IRS ones |

New:

**D-1099-int-1 — box 4 backup withholding of a dependent is credited on the parent's return** (also 1099-OID box 4 and
1099-DIV box 4).
IRS: "If you received a 2025 Form 1099 showing federal income tax withheld on dividends, taxable or tax-exempt interest
income … you received, include the amount withheld in the total on line 25b" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 39) — the
withholding goes with the income, which is the child's (a child's custodial-account income is the child's,
`p17.pdf` p. 57-58, as cited in `new_knowledge\2b.md`). A child with withholding cannot even be put on Form 8814:
"There was no federal income tax withheld from the child's income" (`i8814.pdf` p. 1).
Code: line 25b sums `federalIncomeTaxWithheldAmount` of every 1099-INT / 1099-OID / 1099-DIV in the account
(`TRCS:49795-49820`); `filterWithholdingEntriesForScopedFiler` returns the list unchanged when neither
`scopedFilerSsn` nor `mfsOtherSpouseSsn` is set (`TRCS:51808-51813`) — i.e. on every single, joint, head-of-household
and QSS return. The income boxes of the same statement are dropped by `belongsToPerson` (`TRCS:36027-36033`).
Effect: **silent wrong amount** — the parent's line 25b (and refund) is overstated by the child's backup withholding; if
the child also files his or her own return (which filters by the child's SSN), the same withholding is claimed twice.
Secondary: on MFS / dependent's-own returns the filter is positive (`:51818`), so a **blank-TIN** statement's withholding
is dropped on every leg while its income is counted on every leg (D-2b-2) — inconsistent, no flag. Also a 1099-DIV whose
income is skipped because the person's dividend form is not Yes (D-3b-2) keeps its withholding. Severity: **medium**
(backup withholding on a child's account is uncommon, but the error is a refund overstatement with no warning).

**D-1099-int-2 — foreign tax on the simplified (no Form 1116) path is not attributed to a person** (also 1099-DIV
box 7).
IRS: the credit without Form 1116 requires that "all of that income and the foreign tax paid on it were reported to you"
and that "the total of your foreign taxes wasn't more than $300 (not more than $600 if married filing jointly)"
(`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 115) — your (and on a joint return your spouse's) foreign taxes only.
Code: `computeForm1116` sums `foreignTaxPaidAmount` of every 1099-INT and 1099-DIV in the account with no TIN check
(`TRCS:57079-57089`; lists passed from `TRCS:6528-6531`), and uses that total as the default credit and for the
$300 / $600 test (`TRCS:57094-57120`). The Form 1116 qualified-dividend adjustment likewise reads every 1099-DIV
(`TRCS:56961-56973`).
Effect: **silent wrong amount** on MFS (the other spouse's foreign tax) and on a parent's return (a dependent's foreign
tax); the inflated total can also wrongly fail the $300 / $600 test and push the filer to the full Form 1116 path. The
`simplifiedForeignTaxesOverride` field lets a user correct it by hand. Severity: **medium**.

**D-1099-int-3 — state tax withheld (box 17) reaches Schedule A line 5a without a person filter except on MFS** (also
1099-OID box 14, 1099-DIV box 16, and the other statements in `STATE_WITHHOLDING_FORMS`).
IRS: line 5a includes "State and local income taxes withheld from your salary during 2025" (`i1040sca_2025.pdf` p. 3) —
your own withholding (on MFS, see Pub. 504, `i1040sca_2025.pdf` p. 1).
Code: `withScheduleALine5a` reads every listed statement in the account and filters by TIN only when `mfsTaxpayerSsn` is
passed, which is only on MFS (`TRCS:4324-4325`, `:8560-8573`); on MFS a blank-TIN statement still counts (`:8570`).
Effect: **silent wrong amount** — on a parent's itemized return a dependent's state withholding is deducted; on a
dependent's own itemized return the parents' is. Same root cause as D-1a-1, shared by W-2, W-2G, 1099-R, -G, -B, -MISC,
-NEC, -K. Severity: **low** (itemizers only; dependents rarely have state withholding on a 1099-INT).
