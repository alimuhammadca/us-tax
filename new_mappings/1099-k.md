# Form 1099-K — Payment Card and Third Party Network Transactions

Statement form id `1099-k`. Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` =
`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`. Line-level analysis already done in
`new_knowledge\forms\schedule-c.md` and `new_knowledge\8.md` (Schedule 1 entry space); IRS rules for the entry space
in `new_lines\8.md` §6.0. Their deviation ids are reused here.

---

## 1. What this statement is

A payment settlement entity — a card processor (merchant acquirer) or a third party settlement organization such as a
payment app or online marketplace — sends Form 1099-K to each payee whose payment card / third party network
transactions it settled. Copy B is the payee's copy. Form revision used: **Form 1099-K (Rev. March 2024)**, still the
current revision for 2025 (`docs/IRS-Forms/f1099k.pdf` p. 4, Copy B). For 2025 a 1099-K is required only when the
payee's business transactions are "more than $20,000 and the total number of your transactions is more than 200"
(`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 7, p. 88); the filer instructions still describe the $600 threshold (`i1099k--2024.pdf`
p. 1), which the 1040 instructions supersede for 2025.

IRS instructions used:

- Payee: "Instructions for Payee" on the back of Copy B — `docs/IRS-Forms/f1099k.pdf` p. 5.
- Filer: `i1099k--2024.pdf` (Rev. March 2024, in force for 2025) p. 4 (boxes 1a-8).
- General: `i1099gi--2025.pdf` p. 11 (VOID / CORRECTED).
- Where it goes: `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 39 (line 25b), p. 88 (Schedule 1 entry space; personal items at a gain →
  Form 8949), p. 89 / 91 (not on lines 8a-8z unless not self-employment), p. 101 (Schedule 1-A tips); `i1040sc.pdf`
  p. 5 (Schedule C line 1); `i1040sca_2025.pdf` p. 3 (Schedule A line 5a).

**Whose statement it is.** The payee is the person in "PAYEE'S TIN", stored as **`payeeTIN`** (not `recipientTIN`,
`Form1099KMapper.java:147`). A new entry created on a person tab is seeded with `recipientTIN` only
(`us-tax-ui/src/app/service/statement-entry-state.service.ts:28-34, :56`), which this mapper ignores — so a 1099-K
typed by hand has a blank payee TIN unless the user fills it (D-1099-k-2). The form is **not offered on a
dependent's tab** (`us-tax-ui/src/app/service/statement-selection.service.ts:133-134`; D-1099-nec-4).

**Storage.** Mapper `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/statement/Form1099KMapper.java`; entity `Se1099K`
/ table `se_1099_k`, state rows `Se1099KStateInformation` / `se_1099_k_state_information` (payload key
`stateInformation`, `:198-200`). The monthly boxes are stored as `month01Amount` … `month12Amount` and bridged to the
screen keys `january5aAmount` … `december5lAmount` (`:45-54`, `:165-188`, `:264-287`). On load the mapper also emits
box 4 under the standard key `federalIncomeTaxWithheldAmount` (`:260-262`) so the line 25b helper can read it. The
list call returns every 1099-K of the account (`uid`) — the whole household (`:98-108`).
UI: `us-tax-ui/src/app/forms/form-1099-k.component.ts` — model `:244-275`, `syncPdfToForm` `:321-370` copies every
box; every box on the screen has a mapper column. The screen has a CORRECTED checkbox but no VOID one, so `void` is
always saved false (cosmetic). Upload extraction (Azure `prebuilt-tax.us.1099K`) maps to the same keys
(`us-tax-be/src/main/resources/field-mappings.json`, key `1099-k`; post-processing
`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/GenericFieldMapper.java:778-787`, `postProcess1099K` `:4804-4817`).

**Person filter (the D-1a-1 question).**

- **Box 1a (Schedule C):** filtered — the auto-fill adds only statements whose `payeeTIN` (falling back to
  `recipientTIN`) equals the person's SSN (`sumScheduleCStatements`, `TRCS:28940-28951`); a blank TIN is skipped. A
  dependent's 1099-K produces a Schedule C on the dependent's own return (`TRCS:677-693`, `:709-711`).
- **Box 4 (line 25b):** no person filter on single / joint / head-of-household / QSS returns — **D-1099-int-1** (same
  root cause; `TRCS:49804`). On MFS / dependent's-own returns the filter does find `payeeTIN`
  (`extractStatementRecipientTin`, `TRCS:51786-51800`).
- **Box 8 (Schedule A line 5a):** filtered only on MFS (**D-1099-int-3**) — and even on MFS the filter does not know
  `payeeTIN`, so every household 1099-K counts (**D-1099-k-1**).

A TIN that belongs to nobody in the household raises the blocking `STATEMENT_DOES_NOT_BELONG_TO_FAMILY`
(`TRCS:13891-13910`; rule `StatementSsnRules.java:75`, field `payeeTIN`).

## 4. Questions the app asks with this statement

- **Business income** form: blank gross receipts on a single business are filled from 1099-NEC + 1099-K
  (`TRCS:26925-26927`); returns and allowances (refunds, which box 1a includes) are typed on the same form.
- **Other incomes** form: "Personal items sold at a loss or reported in error on Form 1099-K (disclosure only)"
  (`form1099KPersonalItemsDisclosureAmount`) — the Schedule 1 entry space, kept out of lines 9 / 10
  (`new_knowledge\8.md` §4.0). Not compared with box 1a.
- **Capital gains** (Form 8949) — a personal item sold at a gain must be typed there; nothing points to it from the
  1099-K.
- **Additional deductions (Schedule 1-A)** — the screen says 1099-K tips are read from uploaded statements; they are
  not (D-1099-nec-2).

## 5. Differences from the IRS instructions

Reused:

| Id | Box | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| D-1099-nec-1 | 1a | With no business entered, box 1a is not read and nothing flags it (business, personal-gain or entry-space amounts all left to the user) |
| D-sch-c-3 | 1a | Auto-fill takes the full box 1a; personal items / errors entered in the Schedule 1 entry space are not subtracted, so they are also Schedule C income |
| D-1099-int-1 | 4 | Backup withholding of every household 1099-K credited on single / joint / HOH / QSS returns |
| D-1099-int-3 | 8 | State withholding to Schedule A line 5a filtered only on MFS |
| D-1099-nec-2 | — | Tips included on a 1099-K are never read, though the Schedule 1-A screen says they are |
| D-1099-nec-4 | all | 1099-K not offered on a dependent's tab although the dependent's own Schedule C reads it |

New:

**D-1099-k-1 — on an MFS return the other spouse's 1099-K state withholding goes on Schedule A line 5a.**
IRS: line 5a is "State and local income taxes withheld from your salary during 2025" (`i1040sca_2025.pdf` p. 3) —
your own.
Code: on MFS, `withScheduleALine5a` skips a statement whose TIN is another person's, but looks for the TIN only under
`STATEMENT_PERSON_TIN_KEYS` = `employeeSSN`, `recipientTIN`, `recipientTin`, `recipientIdNumber`, `winnerTin`,
`winnerTIN` (`TRCS:8542-8543`); a 1099-K keeps its TIN in `payeeTIN`, so `tin` stays null and the statement is
counted (`:8562-8573`). (The line 25b filter includes `payeeTIN`, `TRCS:51789-51790`, so withholding is attributed
correctly there.)
Effect: **silent wrong amount** — on each itemizing MFS leg, every household 1099-K box 8 is deducted (twice across
the two returns). Severity: **low** (MFS itemizers with state withholding on a 1099-K).

**D-1099-k-2 — a hand-entered 1099-K starts with a blank payee TIN.**
IRS: the form belongs to the payee whose TIN is shown (`f1099k.pdf` p. 5, "Payee's taxpayer identification number").
Code: the new-entry seed sets `recipientTIN` / `employeeSSN` / `beneficiarySSN` / `shareholderTIN`
(`statement-entry-state.service.ts:28-34`), never `payeeTIN`; `Form1099KMapper` reads only `payeeTIN` (`:147`). If the
user does not type it: the Schedule C auto-fill and its mismatch advisory skip the statement (`TRCS:28944-28946`); a
dependent's 1099-K never triggers the dependent's Schedule C (`:709-711`); box 4 still counts on every non-scoped return
and is dropped on every MFS / dependent's-own leg (`:51818`); box 8 counts on every return including both MFS legs.
No flag says the TIN is missing.
Effect: **silent** — the business receipts are not auto-filled (a filer relying on the auto-fill reports none of
them). Severity: **low** (uploaded forms get the TIN from extraction; the screen does show the empty TIN box).
