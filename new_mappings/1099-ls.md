# Form 1099-LS (`1099-ls`) — every box and where it goes

`MAP` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/statement/Form1099LsMapper.java`. `UI` =
`us-tax-ui/src/app/forms/form-1099-ls.component.ts`. Read with `1099-sb.md` (the two are matched by policy number).

## 1. What this statement is

Form 1099-LS, Reportable Life Insurance Sale (**Rev. April 2025**, Copy B — form copy `se-statements/f1099ls.pdf` p. 3;
not in `docs\IRS-Forms\`), is sent by the **acquirer** of a life insurance contract in a reportable policy sale (§6050Y)
to each payment recipient (the seller). "An acquirer of a life insurance contract … in a reportable policy sale under
section 6050Y must give this form to you for payments made to you" (`f1099ls.pdf` p. 4, Instructions for Payment
Recipient). Filer instructions: `i1099ls.pdf` (Rev. 4-2025). The issuer of the policy separately sends the seller Form
1099-SB showing the investment in the contract (box 1) and surrender value (box 2) — see `1099-sb.md`.

**Where it goes.** The recipient instructions (`f1099ls.pdf` p. 4) and filer instructions say only what the boxes show;
none of the documents in `docs\books\` says how the seller reports the sale. The app follows Rev. Ruls. 2009-13 and
2020-05 (cited in its code, `TRCS:11125-11127`): gain = amount received (1099-LS box 1) − investment in the contract
(1099-SB box 1); the part up to the inside build-up (1099-SB box 2 − box 1) is ordinary income (Schedule 1 line 8z), the
rest capital gain (Form 8949 / Schedule D). Those rulings are **not** in the source set, so this split is recorded as
**unverified** against `docs\books\`.

**Whose statement it is.** "PAYMENT RECIPIENT'S TIN", `paymentRecipientTIN` (`MAP:107`, falls back to `recipientTIN`
`:108`); the screen mirrors it into `recipientTIN` (`UI:197`), which is seeded on a new entry
(`us-tax-ui/src/app/service/statement-entry-state.service.ts:28-34`). Family check `StatementSsnRules.java:81`.

**Person filter (the D-1a-1 question).** No amount reaches any return. The only compute use is a set of advisories
(`crossCheckSpecialtyCapitalStatements`, `TRCS:11194-11232`) that reads every 1099-LS of the household (`TRCS:11139`)
with no TIN test, and checks "entered" against the taxpayer's / spouse's Other incomes line 8z list and Form 8949 rows.
A dependent's (or, on MFS, the other spouse's) policy sale therefore produces advisories on the wrong return —
**cosmetic**, non-blocking.

**Storage.** Entity `Se1099Ls` / table `se_form_1099_ls` (`MAP:96-118`). UI `syncPdfToForm` `UI:188-205`
(`parseAmountAndNotes`; box 1 is never negative, D-ui-1 not relevant). Every replica cell has a mapper key. Upload
extraction: `GenericFieldMapper.java:683-685`, layout `:3034-3077`.

## 3. Checkboxes and codes

CORRECTED only — stored.

## 4. Questions the app asks with this statement

None on the screen. The income must be typed: the ordinary part as a Schedule 1 line 8z item on Other incomes
(`otherIncomeItems8z`), the capital part as a Form 8949 row on the Capital gain/loss form (the advisory looks for a row
whose description contains "insurance", `TRCS:11223-11226`).

## 5. Differences from the IRS instructions

None recorded. The sale's income is not computed from the statement — the app advises and the filer types it — and the
split the advisory proposes rests on revenue rulings that are outside `docs\books\` (unverified, not a deviation).
