# Form 1099-LTC — Long-Term Care and Accelerated Death Benefits

Statement form id `1099-ltc`. Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` =
`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`. Line-level analysis already done in
`new_knowledge\forms\8853.md` (Form 8853 Section C); its deviation ids are reused here rather than re-derived.

---

## 1. What this statement is

An insurance company or viatical settlement provider sends Form 1099-LTC for payments under a long-term care insurance
contract and for accelerated death benefits. **Copy B goes to the policyholder**, who reports it; Copy C goes to an
insured who is not the policyholder "for information only because these payments are not taxable to you"
(`docs/IRS-Forms/f1099ltc.pdf` p. 5). Form revision used: **Form 1099-LTC (Rev. April 2025)** (`f1099ltc.pdf` p. 2,
title).

IRS instructions used:

- Policyholder: "Instructions for Policyholder" — `f1099ltc.pdf` p. 3: "Generally, amounts received under a qualified
  long-term care insurance contract are excluded from your income. However, if payments are made on a per diem basis,
  the amount you may exclude is limited. The per diem exclusion limit must be allocated among all policyholders who own
  qualified long-term care insurance contracts for the same insured. See Pub. 525 and Form 8853."
- Filer: `i1099ltc.pdf` (Rev. April 2025) p. 2 (boxes 1-5).
- Where it goes: Form 8853 Section C (lines 14-26) → Schedule 1 line 8e (`i8853.pdf` p. 8-10; `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf`
  p. 91, "Enter the total of the amounts from Form 8853, lines 8, 12, and 26").

**Whose statement it is.** The policyholder — "POLICYHOLDER'S TIN", stored as `policyholderTIN`. Compute matches on
`recipientTIN` first and falls back to `policyholderTIN` (`TRCS:10691-10692`); the mapper never stores a `recipientTIN` for this form, so in practice the policyholder TIN decides. The UI's new-entry seed fills
`recipientTIN` (and other keys) with the person's SSN, but the 1099-LTC screen saves only `policyholderTIN`
(`us-tax-ui/src/app/service/statement-entry-state.service.ts:28-34`; `form-1099-ltc.component.ts:244-282`), so a
hand-typed 1099-LTC normally has its policyholder TIN only if the user types it. A TIN of nobody in the household raises
the blocking `STATEMENT_DOES_NOT_BELONG_TO_FAMILY` (`us-tax-be/.../microservices/StatementSsnRules.java:78`).

**Storage.** Mapper `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/statement/Form1099LtcMapper.java` (save keys
`:96-131`, list `:70`); entity `Se1099Ltc` / table `se_1099_ltc`. UI `us-tax-ui/src/app/forms/form-1099-ltc.component.ts`,
`syncPdfToForm` `:244-282` — every box (1-5, date certified), both name blocks and CORRECTED are saved, and every saved key
has a mapper column. No VOID box (Copy B has none). Upload extraction: `GenericFieldMapper.java:795-800` (policyholder
and insured blocks).

**Person filter (the D-1a-1 question).** Positive filter on the policyholder TIN for Form 8853 lines 17 and 24
(`sum1099LtcBox1`, `TRCS:10685-10700`), so a 1099-LTC of a dependent or of the other spouse is not counted on this
person's form. A **TIN-less** 1099-LTC is counted for every person whose Form 8853 is computed — both spouses
(**D-8853-8**). On MFS the spouse's Form 8853 income is dropped by the other-income step (`TRCS:32054`).

## 3. Checkboxes and codes

| Item | Values | Meaning (`f1099ltc.pdf` p. 3) | Destination |
|---|---|---|---|
| Box 3 Per diem | checked | Paid on a periodic basis regardless of expenses — exclusion limited ($420 a day, `ReferenceData.LTC_PER_DIEM_LIMIT`) | Box 1 → Form 8853 line 17 |
| Box 3 Reimbursed amount | checked | Reimbursement of actual expenses | Box 1 → Form 8853 line 24 |
| Box 4 | checked / blank | Qualified LTC contract | Not read (line 18 typed) |
| Box 5 | Chronically ill / Terminally ill + date | Certification of the insured | Not read (line 16 typed) |

## 4. Questions the app asks with this statement

On the Archer MSA / LTC (Form 8853) form (`msa-ltc-taxpayer` / `-spouse`; full list in `new_knowledge\forms\8853.md`
§2): name and SSN of the insured (14a / 14b), qualified-contract per-diem part (18), accelerated death benefits (19),
days in the LTC period (21), costs of qualified services (22), reimbursements (24), terminally ill (16). Line 15
("did anyone else receive per-diem payments for this insured") is not asked (D-8853-6).

## 5. Differences from the IRS instructions

Reused (see `new_knowledge\forms\8853.md` §8):

| Id | Box | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| D-8853-6 | 1 | One insured and one LTC period per person; line 15 never asked, so the $420-a-day limit is never shared with other policyholders of the same insured |
| D-8853-8 | TIN | A 1099-LTC with no TIN is counted on each spouse's Form 8853 (double-counted on a joint return). Aggravated by the UI: the screen saves only `policyholderTIN`, which the new-entry seed does not fill, so a hand-typed 1099-LTC is TIN-less unless the user types it |
| D-8853-7 | — | Form 8853 preview prints blank; the filed form is not produced |
| D-8-13 | — | The line 8e advisory says the app does not compute Form 8853 although it does |

New: none.
