# Form 1099-MISC — Miscellaneous Information

Statement form id `1099-misc`. Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` =
`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`. Line-level analysis already done in
`new_knowledge\8.md`, `1h.md`, `1d.md`, `forms\schedule-c.md`, `forms\schedule-e.md`, `forms\schedule-f.md`; their
deviation ids are reused here rather than re-derived.

---

## 1. What this statement is

A payer sends Form 1099-MISC for rents, royalties, prizes and other income, medical and health care payments, crop
insurance proceeds, attorney gross proceeds and similar payments of $600 or more (royalties $10), or when it
backup-withheld. Copy B is the recipient's copy. Form revision used: **Form 1099-MISC (Rev. April 2025)**
(`docs/IRS-Forms/f1099msc.pdf` p. 4, Copy B). In this revision **box 14 is "Reserved for future use"**
(`f1099msc.pdf` p. 5) — excess golden parachute payments moved to Form 1099-NEC box 3 (`i1099mec--2025.pdf` p. 1).

IRS instructions used:

- Recipient: "Instructions for Recipient" on the back of Copy B — `docs/IRS-Forms/f1099msc.pdf` p. 5.
- Filer: `i1099mec--2025.pdf` (Rev. April 2025) p. 1 (What's New), p. 4 (2nd TIN not., box 1), p. 5 (boxes 2-3),
  p. 6 (boxes 4-8), p. 7 (boxes 9-18).
- General: `i1099gi--2025.pdf` p. 11 (VOID / CORRECTED).
- Where it goes: `i1040se.pdf` p. 5-6 (Schedule E lines 3 / 4); `i1040sc.pdf` p. 5; `i1040sf.pdf` p. 4 (crop
  insurance); `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 24 (line 1d Medicaid waiver), p. 39 (line 25b), p. 89 / 91 (Schedule 1 other
  income, Olympic prize money 8m), p. 92 (8z), p. 101 (Schedule 1-A tips), p. 114 (Schedule 2 line 17h);
  `i1040sca_2025.pdf` p. 3 (Schedule A line 5a).

**Whose statement it is.** The recipient is the person in "RECIPIENT'S TIN", stored as `recipientTIN`
(`Form1099MiscMapper.java:120`). A new entry created on a person tab is seeded with that person's SSN
(`us-tax-ui/src/app/service/statement-entry-state.service.ts:28-34`). Unlike 1099-NEC / 1099-K, the form is offered on
a dependent's tab.

**Storage.** Mapper `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/statement/Form1099MiscMapper.java`; entity
`Se1099Misc` / table `se_1099_misc`, state rows `Se1099MiscStateInfo` / `se_1099_misc_state_info` (payload key
`stateLocalInfo`, `:166-168`). The list call returns every 1099-MISC of the account (`uid`) for the year — the whole
household (`:76-85`).
UI: `us-tax-ui/src/app/forms/form-1099-misc.component.ts` — the template binds `pdfRaw[...]`, `syncPdfToForm`
(`:301-341`) copies boxes 1-12, 15-18, FATCA and CORRECTED into the model. **Not saved:** the screen's boxes "13a Cash
tips", "13b TTOC" and "14 Overtime compensation" (`:77-79`) — they are not on the 2025 form and have no model key or
mapper column — and the payer's room / state / country / ZIP inputs (`:37-42`; only name, street and city are joined
into `payerNameAddress`, `:304-306`) — **D-1099-misc-1**. The mapper columns `box3IsStrikeBenefits` (`:134`, D-1h-1)
and `excessGoldenParachutePaymentsAmount` (`:153`, the pre-2025 box 14; written only by upload extraction) have no
screen control. There is no VOID checkbox on the screen. Upload extraction (Azure `prebuilt-tax.us.1099MISC`) maps to
the same keys (`us-tax-be/src/main/resources/field-mappings.json`, key `1099-misc`, `Box14` →
`excessGoldenParachutePaymentsAmount`; post-processing `GenericFieldMapper.java:717-722, :741-744, :4913-4924`, which
splits box 17 into `state` + `payerStateId`).

**Person filter (the D-1a-1 question).**

- **Boxes 1-3, 5-11, 15 (income):** never read by compute, so no filter question — except box 3 tagged as strike
  benefits, summed for the whole account with no TIN check (`sumStrikeBenefitsFrom1099Misc`, `TRCS:42030-42047`,
  list `:39984-39988`) — D-1h-13 (and D-1h-1: the tag cannot be set).
- **Box 4 (line 25b):** no person filter on single / joint / head-of-household / QSS returns — **D-1099-int-1** (same
  root cause; `TRCS:49805`).
- **Box 16 (Schedule A line 5a):** filtered only on MFS — **D-1099-int-3**.

A TIN that belongs to nobody in the household raises the blocking `STATEMENT_DOES_NOT_BELONG_TO_FAMILY`
(`TRCS:13891-13910`; rule `StatementSsnRules.java:67`).

## 4. Questions the app asks with this statement

- **Other earned income** form: strike benefits — "mark … on the 1099-MISC entry — we'll add it automatically", with
  a typed fallback (`new_knowledge\1h.md`, D-1h-1).
- **Employment** form: "treated as a contractor" household employer with withholding → must match a 1099-MISC /
  1099-NEC payer, else the blocking `WITHHOLDING_ON_CONTRACTOR_HOUSEHOLD_FIRM_NEEDS_1099_STATEMENT_*`
  (`TRCS:3528-3529`, `:14141-14190`).
- **Medicaid waiver** form — statement picker copies box 3 / 6 into a row (D-1d-3).
- **Rental / royalty (Schedule E)**, **Business income (Schedule C)**, **Farm income (Schedule F)**, **Other incomes
  (Schedule 1 line 8)** — all typed; none reads a 1099-MISC box (D-8-4, D-sch-c-4, D-sch-f-6, D-1099-misc-2).
- **Additional deductions (Schedule 1-A)** — the screen says 1099-MISC tips are read from uploaded statements; they
  are not (D-1099-nec-2).

## 5. Differences from the IRS instructions

Reused:

| Id | Box | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| D-8-4 | 1, 2, 3 | Rents, royalties and other income never read; no flag; box 4 withholding still credited |
| D-1h-1 | 3 | Strike-benefit tag cannot be set from the UI and is reset on save; the form tells the user not to retype it |
| D-1h-13 | 3 | Tagged strike 1099-MISC summed for the whole account, no recipient filter |
| D-sch-c-4 | 3 (business), 5, 6, 7 (and 11) | Business boxes never read for Schedule C, not in the auto-fill or the mismatch check |
| D-sch-f-6 | 9 | Crop insurance proceeds stored, never read |
| D-1d-3 | 3, 6 | A Medicaid-waiver 1099-MISC row reaches line 1d / 8s only with the election |
| D-1099-int-1 | 4 | Backup withholding of every household 1099-MISC credited on single / joint / HOH / QSS returns |
| D-1099-int-3 | 16 | State withholding to Schedule A line 5a filtered only on MFS |
| D-1099-nec-2 | — | Tips on a 1099-MISC never read, though the Schedule 1-A screen says they are |

New:

**D-1099-misc-1 — the screen shows boxes that are not on the 2025 form, and their values are discarded.**
IRS: Rev. April 2025 Copy B has box 13 "FATCA filing requirement" and box 14 with no caption; "Box 14. Reserved for
future use" (`f1099msc.pdf` p. 4-5).
Code: the screen shows "13a Cash tips", "13b TTOC" (two inputs) and "14 Overtime compensation"
(`form-1099-misc.component.ts:77-79`); `syncPdfToForm` never reads `cash_tips`, `ttoc_1`, `ttoc_2` or
`overtime_compensation` (`:301-341`), the model has no such keys (`:231-256`) and the mapper has no such columns. The
payer's room / state / country / ZIP inputs (`:37-42`) are likewise dropped (`:304-306`).
Effect: **cosmetic** for a genuine 2025 form (those boxes are blank) — but a user who types tips or overtime there
(believing the app reads them for Schedule 1-A) loses them with no message; the payer address is saved incomplete.
Severity: **low**.

**D-1099-misc-2 — box 8 substitute payments in lieu of dividends or interest are not reported.**
IRS: "Box 8. Shows substitute payments in lieu of dividends or tax-exempt interest received by your broker on your
behalf as a result of a loan of your securities. Report on the 'Other income' line of Schedule 1 (Form 1040)"
(`f1099msc.pdf` p. 5); line 8z is for "any taxable income not reported elsewhere" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 92).
Code: `substitutePaymentsAmount` is stored (`Form1099MiscMapper.java:142`) and read nowhere (grep of
`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/`); no advisory.
Effect: **silent wrong amount** if the user does not retype it as an 8z item — income understated. Severity: **low**.

**D-1099-misc-3 — box 15 nonqualified deferred compensation (failed §409A) is neither income nor taxed.**
IRS: "Box 15. Shows income as a nonemployee under an NQDC plan that does not meet the requirements of section 409A …
Report this amount as income on your tax return. This income is also subject to a substantial additional tax to be
reported on Form 1040" (`f1099msc.pdf` p. 5); Schedule 2 "Line 17h … This income should be shown in box 12 of Form
W-2 with code Z, or in box 15 of Form 1099-MISC. The tax is 20% of the amount required to be included in income plus
an interest amount" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 114).
Code: `nonqualifiedDeferredCompAmount` (`Form1099MiscMapper.java:155`) is read nowhere; Schedule 2 line 17h takes only
the typed `schedule2Line17hSection409aAdditionalTax` (`TRCS:43256`, `:43316-43317`); no advisory links the two.
Effect: **silent wrong amount** — the income is missing from AGI and the 20% tax from line 17h unless both are typed.
Severity: **low** (rare).
