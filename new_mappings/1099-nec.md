# Form 1099-NEC — Nonemployee Compensation

Statement form id `1099-nec`. Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` =
`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`. Line-level analysis already done in
`new_knowledge\forms\schedule-c.md`, `1g.md`, `1d.md`, `8.md`; their deviation ids are reused here rather than
re-derived.

---

## 1. What this statement is

A business sends Form 1099-NEC to a person it paid $600 or more for services as a nonemployee (fees, commissions,
prizes for services, a working interest in oil and gas), or from whom it backup-withheld. Copy B is the recipient's
copy. Form revision used: **Form 1099-NEC (Rev. April 2025)** (`docs/IRS-Forms/f1099nec.pdf` p. 4, Copy B).

IRS instructions used:

- Recipient: "Instructions for Recipient" on the back of Copy B — `docs/IRS-Forms/f1099nec.pdf` p. 5.
- Filer: `i1099mec--2025.pdf` (Rev. April 2025; one booklet for 1099-MISC and 1099-NEC) p. 9 (2nd TIN not.),
  p. 10 (box 1, golden parachute in box 1), p. 11 (boxes 2-7).
- General: `i1099gi--2025.pdf` p. 11 (VOID / CORRECTED).
- Where each box goes: `i1040sc.pdf` p. 5 (Schedule C line 1); `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 24 (line 1d Medicaid waiver),
  p. 39 (line 25b), p. 89 / 91 (not on Schedule 1 lines 8a-8z unless not self-employment), p. 101 (Schedule 1-A
  qualified tips), p. 114 (Schedule 2 line 17k); `f8919.pdf` p. 2 (misclassified employee); `i1040sca_2025.pdf`
  p. 3 (Schedule A line 5a).

**Whose statement it is.** The recipient is the person in "RECIPIENT'S TIN", stored as `recipientTIN`
(`Form1099NecMapper.java:121`). A new entry created on a person tab is seeded with that person's SSN as
`recipientTIN` (`us-tax-ui/src/app/service/statement-entry-state.service.ts:28-34, :56`). The form is **not offered on
a dependent's tab** (`us-tax-ui/src/app/service/statement-selection.service.ts:133-134`, comment "out of scope
app-wide"), although compute supports a dependent's own Schedule C (D-1099-nec-4).

**Storage.** Mapper `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/statement/Form1099NecMapper.java`; entity
`Se1099Nec` / table `se_1099_nec`, state rows `Se1099NecStateInfo` / `se_1099_nec_state_info`. The list call returns
every 1099-NEC of the account (`uid`) for the year — the whole household (`Form1099NecMapper.java:77-87`).
UI: `us-tax-ui/src/app/forms/form-1099-nec.component.ts` — the template binds `pdfRaw[...]` and `syncPdfToForm`
(`:217-249`) copies every box into the saved model; every box on the screen has a mapper column. Two small losses:
the per-state `…Notes` text of boxes 5 and 7 is not copied back (`:243-248`, cosmetic), and the mapper column
`isTipIncome` (`:137`) has **no** screen control (D-1099-nec-2). Upload extraction (Azure `prebuilt-tax.us.1099NEC`)
maps to the same keys (`us-tax-be/src/main/resources/field-mappings.json`, key `1099-nec`; post-processing
`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/GenericFieldMapper.java:717-722, :746-749, :4930-4937`, which splits
box 6 into `state` + `payerStateId`).

**Person filter (the D-1a-1 question).**

- **Box 1 (Schedule C):** filtered — the Schedule C auto-fill adds only statements whose `recipientTIN` equals the
  person's SSN (`sumScheduleCStatements`, `TRCS:28924-28953`). A dependent's 1099-NEC is not added to a parent's
  Schedule C; on the dependent's own return a synthetic one-business Schedule C is injected when a 1099-NEC / 1099-K
  carries the dependent's SSN (`TRCS:677-693`, `dependentHasSelfEmploymentStatements` `:701-716`). A blank TIN is
  skipped (`:28934`).
- **Box 1 tips (Schedule 1-A):** filtered by TIN (`sum1099NecTipsForSsn`, `TRCS:54804-54816`), but unreachable
  (D-1099-nec-2).
- **Box 4 (line 25b):** no person filter on single / joint / head-of-household / QSS returns — **D-1099-int-1** (same
  root cause; 1099-NEC is one of the lists, `TRCS:49803`).
- **Box 5 (Schedule A line 5a):** filtered only on MFS — **D-1099-int-3** (1099-NEC is in `STATE_WITHHOLDING_FORMS`,
  `TRCS:8537-8538`).

A TIN that belongs to nobody in the household raises the blocking `STATEMENT_DOES_NOT_BELONG_TO_FAMILY`
(`TRCS:13891-13910`; rule `us-tax-be/.../microservices/StatementSsnRules.java:66`).

## 4. Questions the app asks with this statement

- **Business income** form (`business-income-taxpayer` / `-spouse`): one row per business; a blank "gross receipts"
  on a single business is filled from the person's 1099-NEC + 1099-K (`TRCS:26925-26927`); "operated for profit" No →
  hobby, Schedule 1 line 8j (`new_knowledge\forms\schedule-c.md`). The business form does not mention the 1099-NEC,
  and the Employment form tells the user a 1099-NEC "is not supported by this application (Schedule C)"
  (`form-employment-taxpayer.component.ts:112-117`) — stale since Schedule C is computed (feeds D-1099-nec-1).
- **Employment** form: "treated as a contractor" household employer with withholding → must match a 1099-NEC /
  1099-MISC payer (`TRCS:14141-14190`); a hint when a 1099-NEC is on file and no W-2
  (`form-employment-taxpayer.component.ts:120-125, :239-241`).
- **Uncollected wages (Form 8919)** — typed; the 1099-NEC is not linked (D-1g-3).
- **Medicaid waiver** — statement picker copies box 1 into a row (D-1d-3, `new_knowledge\1d.md`).
- **Additional deductions (Schedule 1-A)** — "Tip amounts are read from your uploaded statements (W-2, 1099-NEC,
  1099-MISC, and 1099-K)" and a hand-entry field "Tips from 1099-NEC, 1099-MISC, or 1099-K not already in uploaded
  statements" (`form-additional-deductions.component.html:64, :156`) — see D-1099-nec-2.

## 5. Differences from the IRS instructions

Reused:

| Id | Box | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| D-1099-int-1 | 4 | Backup withholding of every household 1099-NEC (a dependent's included) credited on single / joint / HOH / QSS returns |
| D-1099-int-3 | 5 | State withholding to Schedule A line 5a filtered only on MFS |
| D-1g-3 | 1 | 1099-NEC from a firm listed on Form 8919 is auto-filled into Schedule C gross receipts — the pay is counted twice |
| D-sch-c-5 | 1 | Auto-fill fills the single business even if it is a statutory-employee or hobby activity |
| D-1d-3 | 1 | A Medicaid-waiver 1099-NEC row reaches line 1d / 8s only with the election |

New:

**D-1099-nec-1 — 1099-NEC box 1 (and 1099-K box 1a) is lost when no business is entered.**
IRS: "If the amount in this box is SE income, report it on Schedule C or F (Form 1040) … and the recipient/partner
completes Schedule SE" and, if not SE income, "report the amount shown in box 1 on the 'Other income' line (on
Schedule 1 (Form 1040))" (`f1099nec.pdf` p. 5); "If you received one or more Forms 1099-NEC, be sure line 1 includes
amounts properly shown on your Forms 1099-NEC" (`i1040sc.pdf` p. 5).
Code: box 1 is read only inside `computeScheduleC`, per business (`TRCS:26908-26914`: `if (businesses == null ||
businesses.isEmpty()) continue;` comes before `sumScheduleCStatements`); the reconciliation advisory is set only
inside that loop (`:27054-27056`). No other path reads `nonemployeeCompensationAmount` (grep; the 1-A path needs
`isTipIncome`). The Employment form tells the user the 1099-NEC "is not supported by this application (Schedule C)"
(`form-employment-taxpayer.component.ts:115`). Box 4 of the same statement is still credited on line 25b.
Effect: **silent wrong amount** — a person who uploads a 1099-NEC and does not also open the Business income form
gets no Schedule C, no Schedule 1 line 3, no SE tax, and still the withholding credit. Same for a 1099-K with no
business. Severity: **medium** (common gig-income case; IRS matching would produce a CP2000).

**D-1099-nec-2 — 1099-NEC tips cannot be marked, and the screen says they are read automatically.**
IRS: "You may be able to claim a deduction for qualified tips paid to you in 2025 that are included on Form W-2, Form
1099-NEC, Form 1099-MISC, Form 1099-K, or reported directly by you on Form 4137" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 101).
Code: uploaded 1099-NEC tips count only when the entry's `isTipIncome` is true (`TRCS:54809`); no control in
`us-tax-ui` sets `isTipIncome` (grep: no occurrence outside `.bak` files), and `Form1099NecMapper.java:137` writes
`PayloadCoercion.bool(data, "isTipIncome")` — null — on every save. 1099-MISC and 1099-K are never read for tips. The
Additional deductions form says "Tip amounts are read from your uploaded statements (W-2, 1099-NEC, 1099-MISC, and
1099-K)" and labels the hand-entry field "… not already in uploaded statements"
(`form-additional-deductions.component.html:64, :156`).
Effect: **silent wrong amount** — a self-employed tipped worker who follows the screen leaves the tips out of the
hand-entry field, and Schedule 1-A line 5 (Form 1040 line 13b) is $0 for them. Same pattern as D-1h-1 (strike
benefits on 1099-MISC). Severity: **medium**.

**D-1099-nec-3 — box 3 excess golden parachute payments: the 20% excise tax is not computed.**
IRS: "Box 3. Shows your total compensation of excess golden parachute payments subject to a 20% excise tax"
(`f1099nec.pdf` p. 5); "If you received a Form 1099-NEC, the tax is 20% of the EPP shown in box 3. Enter this amount
on line 17k" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 114).
Code: `excessGoldenParachutePaymentsAmount` is stored (`Form1099NecMapper.java:132`) and never read; Schedule 2 line
17k comes only from W-2 box 12 code K (`TRCS:43350-43361`, called `:5459`).
Effect: **silent wrong amount** — Schedule 2 line 17k (and total tax) understated by 20% of box 3. Severity: **low**
(rare).

**D-1099-nec-4 — 1099-NEC / 1099-K are hidden on a dependent's tab although the dependent's own return supports them.**
IRS: a child's earnings are the child's (`p17.pdf` p. 10, as cited in `new_knowledge\1h.md`); SE income goes on the
earner's own Schedule C / SE (`f1099nec.pdf` p. 5).
Code: `DEPENDENT_INAPPLICABLE_FORM_IDS` drops `1099-nec` and `1099-k` from the dependent tab ("out of scope
app-wide", `statement-selection.service.ts:133-134`), while compute builds a dependent Schedule C from household
statements carrying the dependent's SSN (`TRCS:677-693`). The only way in is to add the statement on a parent's tab
(seeded with the parent's SSN) and overwrite the TIN.
Effect: **cosmetic / usability** — no wrong amount if the TIN is retyped; if it is not, the child's income lands on
the parent's Schedule C. Severity: **low**.
