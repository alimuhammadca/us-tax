# Form 1099-PATR (`1099-ptr`) — every box and where it goes

The app's statement form id is **`1099-ptr`** (not `1099-patr`): `StatementFormCatalog.java:72`. Paths are relative to
`C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`. `MAP` =
`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/statement/Form1099PatrMapper.java`. `UI` =
`us-tax-ui/src/app/forms/form-1099-ptr.component.ts`.

---

## 1. What this statement is

Form 1099-PATR, Taxable Distributions Received From Cooperatives (**Rev. April 2025**, `docs/IRS-Forms/f1099ptr.pdf`
p. 3, Copy B), is sent by a cooperative to each patron to whom it paid patronage dividends, nonpatronage
distributions, per-unit retain allocations or redemptions, or to whom it passes through a §199A(g) deduction or
credits.

IRS instructions used: "Instructions for Recipient" on the form copy (`f1099ptr.pdf` p. 4): "Generally, if you are an
individual, report any amounts shown in boxes 1, 2, 3, and 5 as ordinary income, unless nontaxable, on your income tax
return … Amounts shown in boxes 7, 8, and 9 are information needed to compute your section 199A(a) deduction." Where
the amounts go: Schedule F lines 3a / 3b (`i1040sf.pdf` p. 4, "If you received distributions from a cooperative in
2025, you should receive a Form 1099-PATR. On line 3a, show your total distributions from cooperatives … Don't include
as income on line 3b patronage dividends from buying personal or family items, capital assets, or depreciable
assets"); Schedule C line 6 ("Amounts you received in your trade or business as shown on Form 1099-PATR",
`i1040sc.pdf` p. 6); box 4 → Form 1040 line 25b (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 39); patrons of a specified agricultural or
horticultural cooperative must use Form 8995-A (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 34; patron reduction and pass-through §199A(g)
deduction, `i1040sf.pdf` p. 1).

**Whose statement it is.** "RECIPIENT'S TIN", `recipientTIN` (`MAP:118`), seeded with the person tab's SSN on a new entry
(`us-tax-ui/src/app/service/statement-entry-state.service.ts:28-34`). Family check `StatementSsnRules.java:72`.

**Person filter (the D-1a-1 question).**

- **Boxes 1, 2, 3, 5 (income)** — filtered by **exact** match of `recipientTIN` to the taxpayer's (and, not MFS, the
  spouse's) SSN (`TRCS:27525-27526`, callers `TRCS:4032-4035`). A dependent's 1099-PATR is not put on a parent's return
  — but a statement with a **blank** TIN is not put on anyone's, silently (**D-1099-ptr-1**).
- **Box 4 (line 25b)** — filtered only on MFS / dependent's-own returns (`TRCS:49810`,
  `filterWithholdingEntriesForScopedFiler` `TRCS:51808-51823`): on single / joint / HOH / QSS returns a dependent's (or a
  blank-TIN) 1099-PATR withholding is credited — **D-1099-int-1** (already recorded).

**Storage.** Entity `Se1099Ptr` / table `se_1099_ptr` (`MAP:106-151`). UI `syncPdfToForm` `UI:218-253` uses
`parseAmountAndNotes` (no box can be negative, so **D-ui-1** does not bite). Upload extraction:
`us-tax-be/.../GenericFieldMapper.java:719, 756`.

## 4. Questions the app asks with this statement

None on the statement screen. Routing depends on the farm / business forms: number of farms, accounting method, and
whether Schedule F line 3a / 38a or Schedule C line 6 was typed (typed values win; advisories
`FORM_1099PATR_ADDED_TO_SCHEDULE_C_*`, `FORM_1099PATR_SCHEDULE_C_LINE6_SHORT_*`, `FORM_1099PATR_INCOME_NOT_REPORTED_*`,
`FORM_1099PATR_FARM_MISMATCH_*`, all non-blocking, `TRCS:27552-27605`). The QBI form's "cooperative patron" question
blocks the return (`TRCS:17357, 17448-17454`). The advisory for a patron with no farm and no business names only
Schedule F and Schedule C — not Schedule 1 line 8z for taxable distributions of a non-business patron.

## 5. Differences from the IRS instructions

Already recorded and only referenced: **D-sch-f-5** (`new_knowledge\forms\schedule-f.md`: the fill sets line 3b = the
full total, including patronage dividends on personal / capital / depreciable purchases that `i1040sf.pdf p. 4` keeps
off 3b; the Schedule C line 6 fill at `TRCS:27547` shares the root), **D-1099-int-1** (box 4 of a dependent credited on
non-MFS returns).

New:

**D-1099-ptr-1 — a 1099-PATR with a blank (or mistyped) recipient TIN is dropped without a word.**
IRS: boxes 1, 2, 3, 5 are income of the recipient (`f1099ptr.pdf p. 4`). Code: `withPatrOnScheduleF` sums only entries
whose `recipientTIN` equals the person's SSN (`TRCS:27525-27526`); a blank TIN matches nobody, so `patr` stays 0 and the
method returns before any advisory (`TRCS:27532`). The family check skips blank TINs (`TRCS:13899-13902`). Box 4 of the
same statement is still credited on a non-MFS return (`TRCS:49810`, `:51810-51813`). The TIN is seeded on a new entry
but can be cleared. Effect: **silent wrong amount** — income omitted while its withholding is claimed. Severity **low**.

**D-1099-ptr-2 — §199A boxes 6, 7 and 13 are not used; the patron rules are not supported.**
IRS: box 6 "Shows your share of the cooperative's section 199A(g) deduction passed through to you"; box 7 "If there is
an amount in this box and the amount is related to a trade or business for which you are claiming a section 199A(a)
deduction, you are required to compute a reduction to your deduction per section 199A(b)(7)" (`f1099ptr.pdf p. 4`);
"Distributions from a cooperative that are included in a patron's qualified business income and are identified on
Form 1099-PATR as qualified payments are subject to the patron reduction. A cooperative's section 199A(g) deduction
passed through to a patron … is included in the patron's qualified business income deduction" (`i1040sf.pdf p. 1`);
Form 8995 cannot be used by a patron of a specified cooperative (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 34`).
Code: no compute path reads `section199agDeductionAmount`, `qualifiedPayments199ab7Amount` or `specifiedCoop` (grep of
`microservices\`); farm QBI is computed (`TRCS:4073`) with no patron reduction; only a user who answers the QBI form's
patron question is stopped (`TRCS:17448-17454`, blocking).
Effect: when the question is not answered — **silent wrong amount** both ways (box 6 deduction missed; box 7 reduction
not applied, overstating QBI where the business has W-2 wages); when answered — **BLOCKING** (return cannot be
produced). Severity **low-medium**.

**D-1099-ptr-3 — boxes 6-9 show blank after reload and later edits are ignored.**
The screen reads and writes `section199AgDeductionAmount`, `qualifiedPayments199Ab7Amount`,
`section199AaQualifiedItemsAmount`, `section199AaSstbItemsAmount` (+ `…Notes`) (`UI:208-211`, `:245-248`); the mapper
returns the lowercase keys on load (`MAP:200-207`) and, on save, prefers the lowercase key (`MAP:136-143`), which the
reloaded model still carries (`us-tax-ui/src/app/directives/statement-form.directive.ts:155`). So after a reload the four
boxes display blank and any new value typed there is discarded in favour of the first saved value. Effect: **cosmetic**
today (no compute reads them). Severity **low**.
