# Form 1099-Q — Payments From Qualified Education Programs (Under Sections 529 and 530)

Statement form id `1099-q`. Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` =
`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`. Line-level context: `new_knowledge\8.md`
(Schedule 1 line 8z). No earlier deviation ids exist for this statement; the analysis below is new.

---

## 1. What this statement is

A qualified tuition program (QTP, "529 plan") or the trustee of a Coverdell education savings account (ESA) sends Form
1099-Q for each distribution. Copy B is the recipient's copy — the recipient may be the designated beneficiary (the
student) or the account owner (box 6). Form revision used: **Form 1099-Q (Rev. April 2025)**
(`docs/IRS-Forms/f1099q.pdf` p. 2, title).

IRS instructions used:

- Recipient: "Instructions for Recipient" — `f1099q.pdf` p. 3: "Nontaxable distributions from CESAs and QTPs are not
  required to be reported on your income tax return. You must determine the taxability of any distribution. See Pub.
  970 …"; "an additional 10% tax may apply to part or all of any amount included in income from the CESA or QTP. See
  Form 5329."
- Filer: `i1099q.pdf` (Rev. April 2025) p. 2 (boxes 1-6), p. 2-3 (box 7 FMV and distribution codes).
- Where it goes: Schedule 1 line 8z, "Taxable distributions from a Coverdell education savings account (ESA) or a
  qualified tuition program (QTP). Distributions from these accounts may be taxable if (a) in the case of distributions
  from a QTP, they are more than the qualified higher education expenses of the designated beneficiary in 2025 …; and
  (b) they were not included in a qualified rollover. Nontaxable distributions from these accounts don't have to be
  reported … This includes rollovers and qualified higher education expenses refunded to a student from a QTP that were
  recontributed to a QTP with the same designated beneficiary generally within 60 days" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf`
  p. 92-93); the 10% additional tax on Form 5329 Part II → Schedule 2 line 8 (`i5329.pdf` p. 5-6;
  `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 112).
- Coordination with the education credits: "Any qualified expenses used to figure the education credits cannot be taken
  into account in determining the amount of a distribution from a Coverdell ESA or a qualified tuition program …
  that is excluded from gross income" (`i8863_2025.pdf` p. 4); "An education credit can be claimed in the same year the
  beneficiary takes a tax-free distribution … as long as the same expenses aren't used for both benefits"
  (`i8863_2025.pdf` p. 5). Pub. 970 (the worksheets) is not in `docs\books\`.

**Whose statement it is.** "RECIPIENT'S TIN", stored as `recipientTIN`, pre-filled with the person-tab SSN on a new entry
(`us-tax-ui/src/app/service/statement-entry-state.service.ts:28-34`). A TIN of nobody in the household raises the
blocking `STATEMENT_DOES_NOT_BELONG_TO_FAMILY` (`us-tax-be/.../microservices/StatementSsnRules.java:68`).

**Storage.** Mapper `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/statement/Form1099QMapper.java` (save keys
`:97-127`, list `:71`); entity `Se1099Q` / table `se_1099_q`. UI `us-tax-ui/src/app/forms/form-1099-q.component.ts`,
`syncPdfToForm` `:209-242`: boxes 1-6, payer / recipient blocks, CORRECTED and **VOID** go into the model; the mapper
has no `void` column (D-1099-q-6). **Box 7** (FMV / distribution code) is not on the screen at all (D-1099-q-3). Upload
extraction: `GenericFieldMapper.java:717-722, 760-763` (`postProcess1099Q`, `recover1099QBox3FromOcr`).

**How it is computed.** `computeQtpTaxableEarnings` (`TRCS:8493-8533`), called per person at `TRCS:4307-4312`
(spouse skipped on MFS):

1. Runs only if the person's Other incomes form has "529 plan or Coverdell ESA: qualified education expenses"
   (`qtpQualifiedEducationExpenses`) and the person's SSN is known; otherwise returns null — nothing is computed and
   the user's own line 8z rows stand (`TRCS:8496-8500`).
2. Sums box 1 and box 2 (negative earnings floored at 0) of the 1099-Qs whose `recipientTIN` equals the person's SSN,
   skipping box 4a trustee-to-trustee and box 4b QTP-to-Roth-IRA transfers (`TRCS:8503-8509`).
3. Adjusted qualified expenses = expenses − tax-free assistance (`qtpTaxFreeEducationAssistance`) − credit expenses,
   floor 0. Credit expenses come from the education-credits students whose `studentTinLine21` equals **the 1099-Q
   recipient's SSN**: AOTC up to $4,000, LLC up to $10,000 of the student's net expenses (`TRCS:8513-8528`).
4. Taxable = 0 if adjusted expenses ≥ box 1 total, else earnings × (box 1 − adjusted expenses) ÷ box 1
   (`TRCS:8529-8530`). The 10% base = earnings × (box 1 − expenses before the scholarship / credit reductions) ÷ box 1,
   capped at the taxable amount (`TRCS:8531-8533`).
5. The taxable amount becomes a line 8z item "Taxable distributions from a qualified tuition program / Coverdell ESA
   (Form 1099-Q)" (`withQtpLine8zItem`, `TRCS:8640-8650`).
6. Form 5329 Part II: line 5 += the taxable total of both spouses; line 6 += taxable − 10% base, plus any typed
   `form5329Line6ExceptionAmount`; line 8 = 10% of line 7 (`TRCS:4342-4345`, `:5401-5422`) → Schedule 2 line 8.

**Person filter (the D-1a-1 question).** Positive filter on `recipientTIN` (`TRCS:8504`): a dependent's 1099-Q is not
counted on the parent's return, and the other spouse's is not counted on MFS. D-1a-1 does not repeat. A **blank** TIN
is dropped on every return (D-1099-q-4).

## 5. Differences from the IRS instructions

**D-1099-q-1 — the education-credit expenses are not removed when the 1099-Q is issued to the account owner.**
IRS: "Any qualified expenses used to figure the education credits cannot be taken into account in determining the amount
of a distribution from a Coverdell ESA or a qualified tuition program … that is excluded from gross income"
(`i8863_2025.pdf` p. 4); the distribution is compared with "the qualified higher education expenses of the designated
beneficiary" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 92).
Code: credit expenses are taken only from the student whose `studentTinLine21` equals the 1099-Q's `recipientTIN`
(`TRCS:8517-8528`). When the plan pays the account owner (a parent; box 6 checked) and the parent claims the AOTC for
the child, the child's TIN never equals the parent's, so `creditExpenses` = 0 and the same expenses both earn the credit
and shelter the 529 earnings.
Effect: **silent wrong amount** — line 8z (and the Form 5329 Part II base) understated; e.g. $10,000 distributed to the
parent with $4,000 earnings, $10,000 of tuition, AOTC on $4,000: IRS taxable = $4,000 × (10,000 − 6,000) ÷ 10,000 =
$1,600; app = $0. Severity: **medium** (paying the owner and claiming the AOTC in the same year is a common pattern).

**D-1099-q-2 — a 1099-Q with the expenses question left blank produces nothing and no flag.**
IRS: "You must determine the taxability of any distribution" (`f1099q.pdf` p. 3); taxable earnings go on line 8z
(`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 92).
Code: `computeQtpTaxableEarnings` returns null when `qtpQualifiedEducationExpenses` is blank (`TRCS:8496-8500`); no
advisory names the 1099-Q (no flag code mentions QTP / 1099-Q in `TRCS`). The question sits under "Show less common
situations" on the Other incomes form.
Effect: **silent under-reporting** when the distribution exceeded the expenses and the user typed nothing. Severity:
**low–medium** (same pattern as D-8-11 for the 1099-QA).

**D-1099-q-3 — box 7 (Coverdell ESA FMV and distribution codes) cannot be entered.**
IRS: "For CESA distributions … the payer/trustee is not required to report amounts in boxes 2 and 3. Instead, the
payer/trustee may report the fair market value of the CESA as of December 31 … in box 7. To figure your earnings and
basis, use the Coverdell ESA—Taxable Distributions and Basis worksheet in Pub. 970" (`f1099q.pdf` p. 3; filer rule
"leave boxes 2 and 3 blank. Do not enter zero. Instead, you must report the fair market value … in box 7",
`i1099q.pdf` p. 2); codes 4 (disability) and 5 (death) mark exceptions to the 10% tax (`i1099q.pdf` p. 3; `i5329.pdf`
p. 6).
Code: no box 7 input or column (`form-1099-q.component.ts` binds `box1` … `box6` only; `Form1099QMapper.java:97-127`).
With boxes 2 / 3 blank, earnings sum to 0 (`TRCS:8508`) and the taxable amount is always 0; the 5a-5c checkboxes are not
read, so no ESA-specific path exists; disability / death exceptions depend on the user typing the Form 5329 line 6
amount, whose help text speaks only of ABLE accounts (`form-other-incomes-spouse.component.ts:1225-1227`).
Effect: **silent under-reporting** for an ESA reported by FMV; 10% over-charged if a death / disability exception is not
typed. Severity: **low**.

**D-1099-q-4 — a 1099-Q with a blank recipient TIN is ignored silently.**
IRS: the recipient's taxable earnings are reported regardless (`f1099q.pdf` p. 3).
Code: `!ssn.equals(normalizeSsn(getString(e, "recipientTIN")))` skips it (`TRCS:8504`); the household TIN check also
skips blank TINs (`TRCS:13900-13904`). Effect: **silent under-reporting**, no flag. A typed 1099-Q is pre-filled with
the person's SSN, so this mainly affects an upload whose TIN was not read or a cleared field. Severity: **low**.

**D-1099-q-5 — 60-day rollovers / recontributed refunds are not asked, and all of a person's 1099-Qs share one ratio.**
IRS: nontaxable distributions include "rollovers and qualified higher education expenses refunded to a student from a
QTP that were recontributed to a QTP with the same designated beneficiary generally within 60 days"; the test is per
designated beneficiary (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 92-93); box 2 earnings are not included in income if "rolled over to
another qualified education program or to an ABLE account, within 60 days" (`f1099q.pdf` p. 3).
Code: only box 4a / 4b transfers are excluded (`TRCS:8505-8506`); the person's 1099-Qs are pooled into one box 1 / box 2
total against one expenses figure (`TRCS:8503-8509`), even when they are for different beneficiaries (a parent who is
the recipient for two children's plans).
Effect: **silent wrong amount** (over-taxed for a 60-day rollover; misallocated when beneficiaries differ). Severity:
**low**.

**D-1099-q-6 — the VOID checkbox shown on the screen is not stored.** The UI renders VOID and copies it to `model.void`
(`form-1099-q.component.ts:29`, `:212`); `Form1099QMapper` has no `void` column. VOID is a Copy A item
(`i1099gi--2025.pdf` p. 11). Effect: **cosmetic**. Severity: **low**.

Not given an id (unverified): Form 5329 Part II is built as **one** form from both spouses' QTP and ABLE amounts
(`TRCS:4342-4345`, `:5401-5422`), whereas each spouse who owes the tax files his or her own Form 5329 (`i5329.pdf`
p. 1); the total tax on Schedule 2 line 8 is the same. On a dependent's own return the parent's AOTC students are on the
parent's Education credits form; whether `educationCreditsTaxpayer` there is the parent's or the dependent's form was not
traced, so the credit coordination for a 1099-Q issued to the student was not verified.
