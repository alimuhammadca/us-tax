# Form 1099-SA — Distributions From an HSA, Archer MSA, or Medicare Advantage MSA

Statement form id `1099-sa`. Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` =
`us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`. Line-level analysis already done in
`new_knowledge\forms\8889.md` (HSA) and `new_knowledge\forms\8853.md` (Archer MSA / MA MSA); their deviation ids are
reused here rather than re-derived.

---

## 1. What this statement is

The trustee or custodian of a health savings account (HSA), Archer MSA or Medicare Advantage (MA) MSA sends Form 1099-SA
for each distribution. Copy B is the recipient's copy. Form revision used: **Form 1099-SA (Rev. April 2025)**
(`docs/IRS-Forms/f1099sa.pdf` p. 2, title).

IRS instructions used:

- Recipient: "Instructions for Recipient" — `f1099sa.pdf` p. 3: "File Form 8853 or Form 8889 with your Form 1040 or
  1040-SR to report a distribution from these accounts even if the distribution isn't taxable. The payer isn't required
  to compute the taxable amount of any distribution."
- Filer: `i1099sa--2025.pdf` p. 2-3 (boxes 1-5).
- Where it goes: HSA → Form 8889 line 14a (`i8889.pdf` p. 8, "These amounts should be shown on Form 1099-SA, box 1") →
  lines 16 / 17b → Schedule 1 line 8f / Schedule 2 line 17c (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 91); Archer MSA → Form 8853 line
  6a; MA MSA → Form 8853 line 10 → Schedule 1 line 8e (`i8853.pdf` p. 5-7; `i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 91).

**Whose statement it is.** "RECIPIENT'S TIN", stored as `recipientTIN`; pre-filled with the person-tab SSN on a new entry
(`us-tax-ui/src/app/service/statement-entry-state.service.ts:28-34`). The statement is not offered on a dependent tab
(`us-tax-ui/src/app/service/statement-selection.service.ts:134-137`). A TIN of nobody in the household raises the
blocking `STATEMENT_DOES_NOT_BELONG_TO_FAMILY` (`us-tax-be/.../microservices/StatementSsnRules.java:70`).

**Storage.** Mapper `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/statement/Form1099SaMapper.java` (save keys
`:96-124`, list `:70`); entity `Se1099Sa` / table `se_1099_sa`. UI `us-tax-ui/src/app/forms/form-1099-sa.component.ts`,
`syncPdfToForm` `:200-231`: boxes 1-5, payer and recipient blocks, CORRECTED and **VOID** are put in the model; the
mapper has no `void` column, so a VOID check is lost (D-1099-sa-2). Upload extraction: `GenericFieldMapper.java:717-722,
765-767`.

**Person filter (the D-1a-1 question).** Every read is a positive filter on `recipientTIN` = the person's SSN
(`TRCS:10178-10190`, `:10200-10220`, `:10228-10242`, `:10659-10671`), so another person's 1099-SA is never counted —
D-1a-1 does not repeat. A 1099-SA with a **blank** TIN reaches no form (D-8889-9; the same `norm != null &&
!norm.equals(tin)` test drops it for Archer / MA MSA, `TRCS:10666`).

## 5. Differences from the IRS instructions

Reused:

| Id | Box | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| D-8889-6 | 3, 4 | Nonspouse-beneficiary FMV at death (box 4) and codes 5 / 6 not used |
| D-8889-9 | 1, 5, TIN | A 1099-SA with no box 5 check or no TIN reaches no form |
| D-8889-8 | — | Form 8889 preview prints blank; the required form is not produced |
| D-8853-2 | 3 | Archer MSA 20% tax: no automatic code 3 / 4 (or age-65) exception |
| D-8853-7 | — | Form 8853 preview prints blank |

New:

**D-1099-sa-1 — HSA code-2 earnings are reported through Form 8889 line 16 (Schedule 1 line 8f) instead of "Other
income".**
IRS: Form 8889 line 14b includes "any excess contributions (and the earnings on those excess contributions) included on
line 14a that were withdrawn by the due date" (`i8889.pdf` p. 8); the earnings are reported separately: "include the
earnings in 'Other income' on your tax return" (`i8889.pdf` p. 8; `f1099sa.pdf` p. 3, box 2).
Code: `sum1099SaCode2ExcessWithdrawal` returns principal = box 1 − box 2 and earnings = box 2; only the principal is
added to line 14b, so the earnings stay in 14c → line 16 → line 8f, and are then subtracted from the 20% base
(`TRCS:9957-9958`, `:10200-10220`; `new_knowledge\forms\8889.md` §4 step 12).
Effect: **cosmetic / line placement** — the same amount of income and no 20% tax, but Form 8889 lines 14b / 14c / 16 and
Schedule 1 lines 8f / 8z differ from the IRS layout. Severity: **low**.

**D-1099-sa-2 — the VOID checkbox shown on the screen is not stored.**
The UI renders VOID and copies it to `model.void` (`form-1099-sa.component.ts:29`, `:203`); `Form1099SaMapper` has no
`void` column (`:96-124`). VOID is a Copy A item only (`i1099gi--2025.pdf` p. 11) and is not used anywhere. Effect:
**cosmetic** — the check disappears on reload. Severity: **low**.
