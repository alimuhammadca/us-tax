## 1. What this statement is

Form 1099-SB, Seller's Investment in Life Insurance Contract (**Rev. April 2025**, Copy B — form copy
`se-statements/f1099sb.pdf` p. 3; not in `docs\IRS-Forms\`), is sent by the **issuer** (insurance company) of a life
insurance contract to the seller when it learns of a reportable policy sale or a transfer to a foreign person (§6050Y)
(`f1099sb.pdf` p. 4, Instructions for Seller). Box 1 is the issuer's estimate of the seller's investment in the contract
(§72(e)(6)): "the aggregate amount of premiums or other consideration paid for the contract, less the aggregate amount
received under the contract, to the extent that such amount was excludable from gross income" (`i1099sb.pdf` p. 2).
Box 2 is the cash surrender value.

**Where it goes.** As for 1099-LS: nothing in `docs\books\` says how the seller reports the sale; the app's advisory uses
box 1 as basis and box 2 − box 1 as the ordinary (inside build-up) part, per revenue rulings outside the source set
(**unverified**; see `1099-ls.md` §1).

**Whose statement it is.** "SELLER'S TIN", `sellerTIN` (`MAP:107`, falls back to `recipientTIN` `:108`); the screen
mirrors it into `recipientTIN` (`UI:196`), seeded on a new entry. Family check `StatementSsnRules.java:82`.

**Person filter (the D-1a-1 question).** Same as 1099-LS: the 1099-SB is read only to pair with a 1099-LS of the same
policy number in an advisory (`TRCS:11196-11202`), household-wide, no TIN test — cosmetic.

**Storage.** Entity `Se1099Sb` / table `se_form_1099_sb` (`MAP:96-118`). UI `syncPdfToForm` `UI:187-203`
(`parseAmountAndNotes`; no negative box). Every replica cell has a mapper key; the VOID box exists on Copy A only.
Upload extraction: `GenericFieldMapper.java:679-681`.
