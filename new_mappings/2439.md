# Form 2439 (`2439`) — every box and where it goes

Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`.
`MAP` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/statement/Form2439Mapper.java`. `UI` =
`us-tax-ui/src/app/forms/form-capital-statement.component.ts` (Form 2439 has no screen of its own; it is the shared
"capital statement" screen with the replica at `UI:98-…`, fields in `capital-statement-configs.ts:19-103`).

---

## 1. What this statement is

Form 2439, Notice to Shareholder of Undistributed Long-Term Capital Gains, is sent by a regulated investment company
(RIC, e.g. a mutual fund) or real estate investment trust (REIT) that kept long-term capital gains instead of paying them
out and paid tax on them for the shareholder. The current revision is **Rev. November 2021** (still current; its
instructions say "the 2021 form or the applicable line on the current year form") — `docs/IRS-Forms/f2439.pdf` p. 3
(Copy B). Copy B says "Attach to the shareholder's income tax return for the tax year that includes the last day of the
RIC's or REIT's tax year", and the 2025 Form 1040 instructions say "Attach Forms W-2 and 2439 to Form 1040 or 1040-SR"
(`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf` p. 67).

IRS instructions used: "Instructions for the Shareholder" on the form copy (`f2439.pdf` p. 4 and p. 6); filer
instructions on the same copy (`f2439.pdf` p. 2, 8); "Undistributed Capital Gains" in `i1040sd.pdf` p. 6; the 28% Rate
Gain Worksheet (`i1040sd.pdf` p. 11) and Unrecaptured Section 1250 Gain Worksheet (`i1040sd.pdf` p. 12).

**Whose statement it is.** "Shareholder's identifying number", saved as `shareholderTIN` (`MAP:116-119`; the screen's
key `shareholderTin` is accepted as a fallback). A new entry is seeded with the person tab's SSN under `shareholderTIN`
(`us-tax-ui/src/app/service/statement-entry-state.service.ts:28-34`). Family check: `StatementSsnRules.java:105`
(`STATEMENT_DOES_NOT_BELONG_TO_FAMILY`, blocking; a blank TIN is skipped, `TRCS:13899-13902`).

**Person filter (the D-1a-1 question).** Split:

- **Box 1a / 1b / 1d (Schedule D)** — filtered: `belongsToCapitalPersonByKey(entry, "shareholderTIN", …)`
  (`TRCS:25278-25280`, rule `TRCS:25512-25534`) keeps an entry only if its TIN is this person's SSN; a dependent's
  2439 is therefore **not** put on a parent's Schedule D. A **blank** TIN falls to `ownerRole` (always "taxpayer",
  `MAP:104-105`) — so it goes to the taxpayer on every MFS leg and every dependent's own return (**D-7a-1**, already
  recorded). The person's capital form must be saved / answered Yes or the amounts are dropped (`TRCS:24763-24768`,
  **D-7a-2**).
- **Box 2 (Schedule 3 line 13a)** — **not filtered at all**: `applyForm2439CreditToSchedule3` adds box 2 of every Form
  2439 in the account (`TRCS:48223-48242`, called `TRCS:7036`; list loaded unfiltered `TRCS:3142`). This is the D-1a-1
  pattern and worse — it applies on MFS and dependent's-own returns too (**D-2439-1**, new).

**Storage.** Entity `SeForm2439` / table `se_form_2439`, one row per statement; amounts are stored as **text**
(`MAP:121-127`, parsed at compute by `parseAmount`, `TRCS:52507-52535`, which accepts "-", "(…)", "$" and commas).

**UI.** The replica is the default view (`_pdfView = true`, `UI:599`); `syncPdfToForm` (`UI:660-680`) copies every
replica cell into the model. Three problems: the screen binds `shareholderTin` / `ricReitEin` but the mapper emits
`shareholderTIN` / `ricReitEIN` on load (`MAP:140-141`), and the tax-year cells (`calendarYear`,
`taxYearBeginning…`, `taxYearEnding…`, `UI:666-670`) have no mapper column (**D-2439-2**, **D-2439-3**). Amounts are
plain text fields (not `parseAmountAndNotes`), so **D-ui-1** does not apply; no box can be negative anyway. Upload
extraction: `GenericFieldMapper.java:654-656`, layout `:2399-2445`.

## 3. Checkboxes and codes

VOID and CORRECTED only — both stored, neither read. No codes.

## 4. Questions the app asks with this statement

On the Capital gain/loss form (`capital-gain-loss-taxpayer` / `-spouse`): "received Form 2439" (`receivedForm2439`) and
"uploaded it" (`uploadedForm2439`) — if received is Yes and no 2439 is uploaded, the blocking
`MISSING_UPLOADED_FORM_2439` fires (`TRCS:26433-26440`); a person-level "has Form 2439 undistributed gains"
(`hasForm2439UndistributedCapitalGains`, `TRCS:24811`) also forces Schedule D. No other question changes where a box
goes.

## 5. Differences from the IRS instructions

Already recorded and only referenced: **D-7a-1** (blank-TIN 2439 → taxpayer on every MFS leg / dependent return),
**D-7a-2** (capital form not saved → boxes 1a-1d dropped; never read on a dependent's own return).

New:

**D-2439-1 — box 2 credit is taken from every Form 2439 in the household, on every return.**
IRS: the credit goes with the gain — "Include on Schedule 3 (Form 1040), line 13a, the tax paid as shown in box 2 of
Form 2439" in the same paragraph that puts box 1a on *your* Schedule D (`i1040sd.pdf p. 6`); box 2 "is the tax paid by
the RIC or REIT on the undistributed long-term capital gains shown in box 1a" (`f2439.pdf p. 4`).
Code: `applyForm2439CreditToSchedule3` loops over the unfiltered list (`TRCS:48230-48236`; list `TRCS:3142`), while box
1a of the same statements is person-filtered (`TRCS:25278-25280`). Consequences: a dependent's 2439 (child's SSN) gives
the parent the refundable credit without the gain; on married filing separately each spouse's 2439 is credited on
**both** returns (counted twice); a dependent's own return gets the parents' credits; a person whose capital form was
never saved gets the credit but not the gain (D-7a-2). The presence test that forces Schedule D (`TRCS:23747`) is
equally unfiltered (cosmetic).
Effect: **silent wrong amount** — refundable credit overstated (line 31 / refund). Severity **medium**.

**D-2439-2 — the shareholder TIN (and the RIC's EIN) cannot be changed after the first save.**
The screen binds `shareholderTin` / `ricReitEin` (`UI:651, 673`; `capital-statement-configs.ts:47, 54`), the mapper
returns `shareholderTIN` / `ricReitEIN` on load (`MAP:140-141`), and the loaded value stays in the model
(`us-tax-ui/src/app/directives/statement-form.directive.ts:155`). After a reload the TIN box shows blank; whatever the
user types goes in `shareholderTin`, but the mapper reads `shareholderTIN` first (`MAP:116-119`, `:111-115`), so the old
value — often the seeded person-tab SSN — is kept. Effect: **silent** — a 2439 entered from the wrong person tab cannot
be re-attributed, so boxes 1a-1d stay on (or off) the wrong person's Schedule D. Severity **low-medium**.

**D-2439-3 — the tax-year cells are typed (and extracted) but never saved.**
`calendarYear`, `taxYearBeginningMonthDay`, `taxYearBeginningYear`, `taxYearEndingMonthDay`, `taxYearEndingYear` are on
the replica (`UI:146-162`) and filled by extraction (`GenericFieldMapper.java:2431-2440`), but `applyParent` has no such
keys (`MAP:102-128`) — the typed value is lost. The IRS uses that year to decide which return the 2439 belongs to (Copy
B, `f2439.pdf p. 3`). Effect: **cosmetic** (no compute reads it; a fiscal-year RIC's 2439 for the wrong year is not
caught). Severity **low**.
