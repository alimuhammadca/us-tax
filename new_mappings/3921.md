`MAP` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/statement/Form3921Mapper.java`. `UI` =

## 1. What this statement is

Form 3921, Exercise of an Incentive Stock Option Under Section 422(b) (**Rev. April 2025**, `docs/IRS-Forms/f3921.pdf`
p. 3, Copy B), is sent by the employer (or its transfer agent) for each transfer of stock to an employee who exercised
an incentive stock option (ISO). It reports no income: "You must recognize (report) gain or loss on your tax return for
the year in which you sell or otherwise dispose of the stock. Keep this form and use it to figure the gain or loss …
When you exercise an ISO, you may have to include in alternative minimum taxable income a portion of the fair market
value (FMV) of the stock" (`f3921.pdf` p. 4, Instructions for Employee).

Where it matters on the 2025 return:

- **Form 6251 line 2i** — "you must generally include on line 2i the excess, if any, of (1) the fair market value …
  over (2) the amount you paid for the stock … If you acquired stock by exercising an ISO and you disposed of that stock
  in the same year, the tax treatment under the regular tax and the AMT is the same, and no adjustment is required …
  Form 3921. If you received a Form 3921, it may help you figure your adjustment" (box 4 × box 5 − box 3 × box 5,
  worked example) — `i6251.pdf` p. 4; the same rule in `p525.pdf` p. 12.
- **AMT basis** — "Increase your AMT basis in any stock acquired through the exercise of an ISO by the amount of the
  adjustment … See the instructions for line 2k" (`i6251.pdf` p. 4).
- **Sale** — gain or loss on Form 8949 / Schedule D in the year of sale; a disqualifying disposition makes part of it
  wages (`p525.pdf` p. 12).

Filer instructions: `i3921.pdf` (Instructions for Forms 3921 and 3922).

**Whose statement it is.** "EMPLOYEE'S TIN", `employeeTIN` (`MAP:119`). Not seeded on a new entry (the seed carries
`employeeSSN`, not `employeeTIN`: `us-tax-ui/src/app/service/statement-entry-state.service.ts:28-34`). Family check
`StatementSsnRules.java:104`.

**Person filter (the D-1a-1 question).** `computeIsoAmtAdjustmentLine2i` skips an entry whose TIN is neither the
taxpayer's nor (not MFS) the spouse's SSN (`TRCS:36445-36449`; spouse SSN nulled on MFS at `TRCS:6378-6380`). So a
dependent's 3921 is not put on a parent's return. A **blank** TIN is counted on every return — both MFS legs and every
dependent's own return (**D-3921-3**).

**Storage.** Entity `SeForm3921` / table `se_form_3921`; boxes 3-5 are decimals (`MAP:129-134`).

**UI.** The replica is the default view (`_pdfView = true`, `UI:694`; replica `UI:274-350`); `syncPdfToForm`
(`UI:849-886`) rebuilds the model from it, reading boxes 3, 4 and 5 with **`parseFloat`** (`UI:867-869`) — see
**D-3921-2**. Every replica cell reaches a mapper key; the suffix is placed before the last name on display and dropped
on save (`UI:818, 855-857`; cosmetic). VOID is not on the replica (the hidden form view has it). Upload extraction:
`us-tax-be/.../GenericFieldMapper.java:594-596` (`postProcess3921`).

## 3. Checkboxes and codes

CORRECTED (and VOID) only; stored, not read.

## 4. Questions the app asks with this statement

None on the 3921 screen. Compute reads a key `disqualifyingDispositionSameYear` (`TRCS:36428-36430`) that no screen and
no mapper column provides (**D-3921-1**). The advisory `AMT_ISO_EXERCISE_ADJUSTMENT_APPLIED` (non-blocking,
`TRCS:36462-36470`) tells the user the adjustment applies only if the shares were held past year-end. The later sale and
its AMT basis are entered on the Capital gain/loss form (`TRCS:4141-4171`).

## 5. Differences from the IRS instructions

New:

**D-3921-1 — a same-year sale of the ISO shares cannot be told to the app, so the AMT adjustment is always added.**
IRS: "If you acquired stock by exercising an ISO and you disposed of that stock in the same year, the tax treatment
under the regular tax and the AMT is the same, and no adjustment is required" (`i6251.pdf` p. 4; `p525.pdf` p. 12).
Code: the only exclusion is `disqualifyingDispositionSameYear` on the 3921 entry (`TRCS:36428-36430`), but
`Form3921Mapper` has no such column (`MAP:108-139`) and no UI sets it (only unit tests do). Every 2025 exercise is
therefore added to line 2i; the advisory says the adjustment "does not apply" after a same-year sale, but the user's only
remedy is to delete the statement. Effect: **silent wrong amount** (advisory only) — AMTI and possibly AMT overstated.
Severity **medium**.

**D-3921-2 — boxes 3, 4 and 5 typed with "$" or a thousands comma are saved wrong.**
`syncPdfToForm` converts the three replica cells with `parseFloat` (`UI:867-869`): "1,000" shares becomes 1, "$25.00"
becomes `NaN` (sent as null). The screen's own instruction tells the user to type amounts as printed, e.g. "$1,234.56"
(`UI:41`). With a null box the entry is skipped (`TRCS:36453-36455`); with "1,000" the spread is multiplied by 1. Other
statement screens use `parseAmountAndNotes` (cf. D-5498-1, same pattern). Effect: **silent wrong amount** — Form 6251
line 2i understated or dropped. Severity **medium**.

**D-3921-3 — a Form 3921 with no employee TIN is counted on every return of the household.**
The person check only rejects a TIN that is present and different (`TRCS:36445-36449`); a blank TIN is added on both
MFS legs and on each dependent's own return. The adjustment belongs to the person who exercised (`i6251.pdf` p. 4;
comment at `TRCS:6372-6376`). The TIN is not seeded on a new entry (above), so a blank TIN is plausible. Effect:
**silent wrong amount** (AMTI overstated on the other returns). Severity **low**.

D-ui-1 does not apply (this screen does not use the shared parser, and no box is negative).
