`MAP` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/statement/Form3922Mapper.java`. `UI` =

## 1. What this statement is

Form 3922, Transfer of Stock Acquired Through an Employee Stock Purchase Plan Under Section 423(c) (**Rev. April 2025**,
Copy B — form copy `se-statements/f3922.pdf` p. 2; this copy is not in `docs\IRS-Forms\`), is sent by the employer (or
transfer agent) when it records the first transfer of legal title of ESPP shares bought at a discount (or at a price not
fixed at grant). It reports **no income for 2025 by itself**: "No income is recognized when you exercise an option under
an employee stock purchase plan. However, you must recognize (report) gain or loss on your tax return for the year in
which you sell or otherwise dispose of the stock. Keep this form and use it to figure the gain or loss" (`f3922.pdf`
p. 3, Instructions for Employee).

What the boxes are used for — **only in the year the shares are sold** (`p525.pdf` p. 12-13):

- **Qualifying disposition** (holding periods met): ordinary income (compensation) = the lesser of FMV at grant (box 3)
  minus the option price (box 8 when the price was not fixed at grant, otherwise the price fixed at grant), and the sale price minus
  the amount paid (box 5); the rest is capital gain on Form 8949 / Schedule D. "Your basis is equal to the option price"
  plus the ordinary income included.
- **Disqualifying disposition**: compensation = FMV at exercise (box 4) minus price paid (box 5), normally on the W-2.
- **No AMT adjustment**: the Form 6251 line 2i rule covers ISOs only (`i6251.pdf` p. 4; `p525.pdf` p. 12 "Alternative
  minimum tax (AMT). For the AMT, you must treat stock acquired through the exercise of an ISO …").

Filer instructions: `i3921.pdf` (Instructions for Forms 3921 and 3922).

**Whose statement it is.** "EMPLOYEE'S identification number", `employeeTIN` (`MAP:109-110`, falls back to
`recipientTIN`); the screen mirrors it into `recipientTIN` (`UI:207`), which is seeded with the person tab's SSN on a
new entry (`us-tax-ui/src/app/service/statement-entry-state.service.ts:28-34`). Family check `StatementSsnRules.java:106`.

**Person filter (the D-1a-1 question).** Not applicable: **no compute path reads any Form 3922 key** (grep of
`microservices\` finds only the mapper, extraction `GenericFieldMapper.java:586-588`, the SSN rule and the catalog).

**Storage.** Entity `Se3922` / table `se_form_3922` (`MAP:98-127`). UI `syncPdfToForm` `UI:199-220` uses
`parseAmountAndNotes`; no box can be negative (D-ui-1 not relevant). Every replica cell has a mapper key.

## 3. Checkboxes and codes

CORRECTED only — stored.

## 4. Questions the app asks with this statement

None. A sale of ESPP shares is entered from its Form 1099-B and Form 8949 rows on the Capital gain/loss form, and the
compensation part normally arrives on the W-2; the app does not compute the ESPP ordinary-income amount or the basis
adjustment from Form 3922.

## 5. Differences from the IRS instructions

None recorded. Form 3922 carries no 2025 return amount; its use is in the sale year, where the app relies on the typed
sale (basis and any compensation are the filer's to enter). Not deriving the qualifying-disposition compensation /
basis from boxes 3, 5 and 8 is a missing convenience, not an IRS deviation, and is noted here only.
