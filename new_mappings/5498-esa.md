## 1. What this statement is

Form 5498-ESA, Coverdell ESA Contribution Information, is filed by the trustee or issuer "for each person for whom you
maintained any Coverdell education savings account (ESA) during 2025"; "If no reportable contributions were made for
2025, no return is required" (`i5498e--2025.pdf p. 1`). It is furnished to the beneficiary by April 30, 2026
(`i5498e--2025.pdf p. 2`) — often after the return is filed.

It is **informational**. Box 1: "Do not deduct these amounts on your income tax return"; "If the total contributions
made to all your Coverdell ESAs for the calendar year shown on this form exceeded $2,000, you must, by May 31 of the
following year, withdraw the excess, plus earnings, or you may owe an additional tax. You must keep track of your
Coverdell ESA basis (contributions and distributions)" (Instructions for Beneficiary, `se-statements/f5498e.pdf p. 3`).

Form copy: the only one on disk is `se-statements/f5498e.pdf` (**Rev. December 2026**; not in `docs\IRS-Forms\` or
`us-tax-ui\public\irs\`), page 2 Copy B, page 3 Instructions for Beneficiary. It has the same two boxes the 2025 filer
instructions define (`i5498e--2025.pdf p. 2`: "Box 1. Contributions (Other Than Amounts in Box 2)", "Box 2. Rollover
Contributions"), so the captions below are the 2025 ones.

Where a box can matter on a 2025 return:

- **Form 5329 Part V** (beneficiary's return) — "Additional Tax on Excess Contributions to Coverdell ESAs. Complete this
  part if the contributions to your Coverdell ESAs for 2025 were more than is allowable"; line 31 "Excess contributions
  for 2025"; line 33 "6% (0.06) of the smaller of line 32 or the value of your Coverdell ESAs on December 31, 2025 …
  Include this amount on Schedule 2 (Form 1040), line 8" (`docs/IRS-Forms/f5329.pdf p. 2`). "Your contribution limit is
  the smaller of $2,000 or the sum of the maximum amounts the contributor(s) to your Coverdell ESAs are allowed to
  contribute. The maximum contribution may be limited based on the contributor's modified AGI"; "Don't include rollovers
  in figuring your excess contributions" (`i5329.pdf p. 7-8`). Box 1 is the contribution figure; box 2 is excluded.
- **Form 1099-Q** distributions — box 1 contributions are the beneficiary's Coverdell basis (Instructions for
  Beneficiary above); the 1099-Q itself reports the basis of the distribution, so 5498-ESA is a record only.
- Box 2 confirms a tax-free rollover of a Coverdell distribution ("Generally, any amount rolled over … is not taxable",
  `f5498e.pdf p. 3`).

**Whose statement it is.** "BENEFICIARY'S TIN", `beneficiaryTIN` (`MAP:115-116`, falls back to `recipientTIN`), returned
also as `recipientTIN` (`MAP:150`). The screen surfaces a seeded `recipientTIN` in the beneficiary box and mirrors it
back (`UI:211`, `UI:251`). Upload extraction copies `beneficiaryTIN` into `recipientTIN`
(`us-tax-be/.../GenericFieldMapper.java:625-630`). Family check: `StatementSsnRules.java:96` (`STATEMENT_DOES_NOT_BELONG_TO_FAMILY`).
The beneficiary is usually a child (a dependent).

**Person filter (the D-1a-1 question).** Not applicable: **no compute path reads any Form 5498-ESA key** — a grep of
`us-tax-be\src\main\java\com\ustax\` for every saved key finds only the mapper, the entity `Se5498Esa`, extraction
(`GenericFieldMapper.java:625-630`, layout `:2151`), the SSN rule (`StatementSsnRules.java:96`) and the catalog
(`StatementFormCatalog.java:51`). A child's 5498-ESA therefore cannot leak onto a parent's return.

**Storage.** Entity `Se5498Esa` / table `se_form_5498_esa`, one row per statement (`MAP:97-130` save, `:132-165` load).

**UI.** Replica view; `syncFormToPdf` `UI:207-233`, `syncPdfToForm` `UI:235-263`, amounts through `parseAmountAndNotes`
(`UI:237`). Every replica cell has a mapper key, and every mapper key is on the screen (or derived: `trusteeIssuerNameAddress`
`UI:247`, `beneficiaryCityStateZip` `UI:259`). No box is negative, so D-ui-1 does not apply.

## 3. Checkboxes and codes

None besides CORRECTED.

## 4. Questions the app asks with this statement

None on the 5498-ESA screen, and no other form asks for Coverdell contributions or an excess. Coverdell **distributions**
are handled from Form 1099-Q on the other-incomes form (taxable earnings → Schedule 1 line 8z, `TRCS:8639-8646`; the 10%
Form 5329 Part II tax, `TRCS:43012-43015`) — see `new_mappings\1099-q.md`.

## 5. Differences from the IRS instructions

New:

**D-5498-esa-1 — Form 5329 Part V (6% tax on excess Coverdell ESA contributions) is not supported.**
IRS: "If the contributions to your Coverdell ESAs for 2025 were more than is allowable or you had an amount on line 33
of your 2024 Form 5329, you may owe this tax" (`i5329.pdf p. 7`); line 33 "Additional tax. Enter 6% (0.06) of the
smaller of line 32 or the value of your Coverdell ESAs on December 31, 2025 … Include this amount on Schedule 2 (Form
1040), line 8" (`f5329.pdf p. 2`).
Code: no key of this statement is read; `Form5329` has no Part V field (`us-tax-be/.../model/output/Form5329.java:8-16`);
the Schedule 2 line 8 sum omits it (`TRCS:42994-43016`); no intake form asks for the excess, the prior-year line 32
carryover or the year-end value. Nothing warns when box 1 exceeds $2,000.
Effect: **silent wrong amount** — the 6% tax (and its carryover) is missing on the beneficiary's own return. Severity
**low** (the trustee usually refuses or returns excess contributions; the tax falls on the beneficiary's return, which is
usually a child's).
