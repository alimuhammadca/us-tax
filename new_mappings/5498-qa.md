## 1. What this statement is

Form 5498-QA, ABLE Account Contribution Information. "Any state, or its agency, or instrumentality that establishes and
maintains a qualified ABLE program must file, for each ABLE account, a Form 5498-QA … with the IRS on or before June 1,
2026", and "must also furnish a statement to the designated beneficiary by March 15" (`i1099qa--2025.pdf p. 3`).

It is **informational**. Box 1: "Do not deduct these amounts on your income tax return. The total amount contributed to
your ABLE account may not exceed the annual cumulative limit … If amounts contributed to your ABLE account are greater
than the annual contribution limit, the excess contributions and the earnings on those contributions must be returned to
the contributors by the date your tax return is due (including extensions), or you may owe a penalty"
(Instructions for Beneficiary, `se-statements/f5498qa.pdf p. 3`). The 2025 limit: contributions "can't exceed $19,000"
plus, for an employed designated beneficiary, up to the lesser of compensation or the one-person poverty line ($15,060
continental U.S., $18,810 Alaska, $17,310 Hawaii) (`i1099qa--2025.pdf p. 1`).

Form copy: the only one on disk is `se-statements/f5498qa.pdf` (**Rev. December 2026**; not in `docs\IRS-Forms\` or
`us-tax-ui\public\irs\`), page 2 Copy B, page 3 Instructions for Beneficiary. Boxes 1-7 match the 2025 filer
instructions (`i1099qa--2025.pdf p. 3-4`); **box 8 "Trump account to ABLE rollover" exists only on the Rev. 12-2026
copy** — the 2025 instructions stop at box 7.

Where a box can matter on a 2025 return:

- **Form 5329 Part VIII** (designated beneficiary's return) — line 50 "Excess contributions for 2025"; line 51
  "Additional tax. Enter 6% (0.06) of the smaller of line 50 or the value of your ABLE account on December 31, 2025.
  Include this amount on Schedule 2 (Form 1040), line 8" (`docs/IRS-Forms/f5329.pdf p. 2`). "Total contributions
  (including contributions from a section 529 account) made to your ABLE account for 2025 may not exceed $19,000 plus …
  Don't include ABLE rollovers or program-to-program transfers in figuring your excess contributions"; no tax if the
  program returns the excess with its net income by the return due date (`i5329.pdf p. 9`). Box 1 is the contribution
  figure, box 4 the year-end value.
- **Form 8880 line 1** — "Traditional and Roth IRA contributions, and ABLE account contributions by the designated
  beneficiary for 2025. Do not include rollover contributions" (`f8880.pdf p. 1`; who qualifies, `p. 2`). Box 1 counts
  every contributor's money, so only the beneficiary's own part goes on line 1 — box 1 is an upper bound, not the figure.

**Whose statement it is.** "BENEFICIARY'S TIN", `beneficiaryTIN` (`MAP:114-115`, falls back to `recipientTIN`), returned
also as `recipientTIN` (`MAP:158`). The screen surfaces a seeded `recipientTIN` in the beneficiary box and mirrors it
back (`UI:221`, `UI:267`). Upload extraction copies `beneficiaryTIN` into `recipientTIN`
(`us-tax-be/.../GenericFieldMapper.java:617-622`). Family check: `StatementSsnRules.java:97`.

**Person filter (the D-1a-1 question).** Not applicable: **no compute path reads any Form 5498-QA key** — a grep of
`us-tax-be\src\main\java\com\ustax\` for every saved key finds only the mapper, the entity `Se5498Qa`, extraction
(`GenericFieldMapper.java:617-622`, layout `:2067-2082`, `:2131`), the SSN rule (`StatementSsnRules.java:97`) and the
catalog (`StatementFormCatalog.java:52`).

**Storage.** Entity `Se5498Qa` / table `se_form_5498_qa`, one row per statement (`MAP:96-138` save, `:140-182` load).

**UI.** Replica view; `syncFormToPdf` `UI:217-249`, `syncPdfToForm` `UI:251-285`, amounts through `parseAmountAndNotes`
(`UI:253`). Every replica cell has a mapper key, and every mapper key is on the screen (or derived: `issuerNameAddress`,
`beneficiaryCityStateZip`). No box is negative, so D-ui-1 does not apply.

- **Box 5** — account opened this year; stored only.
- **Box 6** — A: eligibility under §529A(e)(1)(A), SSDI (Title II); B: §529A(e)(1)(A), SSI (Title XVI); C: disability

  6 respiratory, 7 other (`i1099qa--2025.pdf p. 4`). Stored as free text; no return effect.

## 4. Questions the app asks with this statement

None on the 5498-QA screen. The Saver's credit form asks "ABLE account contributions you made as designated beneficiary"
(taxpayer and spouse) for Form 8880 line 1. ABLE **distributions** come from Form 1099-QA (Schedule 1 line 8q,
`TRCS:32488-32490`; Form 5329 Part II) — see `new_mappings\1099-qa.md`. No form asks for an ABLE excess contribution.

## 5. Differences from the IRS instructions

New:

**D-5498-qa-1 — Form 5329 Part VIII (6% tax on excess ABLE contributions) is not supported.**
IRS: "If the contributions to your ABLE account for 2025 were more than is allowable, you may owe tax" (`i5329.pdf p. 9`);
line 51 "Enter 6% (0.06) of the smaller of line 50 or the value of your ABLE account on December 31, 2025. Include this
amount on Schedule 2 (Form 1040), line 8" (`f5329.pdf p. 2`).
Code: no key of this statement is read; `Form5329` has no Part VIII field (`us-tax-be/.../model/output/Form5329.java:8-16`);
the Schedule 2 line 8 sum omits it (`TRCS:42994-43016`); no intake form asks for the excess. Nothing warns when box 1
exceeds $19,000.
Effect: **silent wrong amount** — the 6% tax is missing. Severity **low** (a qualified ABLE program must refuse
contributions above the limit, `i1099qa--2025.pdf p. 1`, and returned excess carries no tax, `i5329.pdf p. 9`).

Box 8 holding a field the 2025 form does not have is harmless (stored only) and is not a deviation.
