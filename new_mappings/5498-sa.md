`MAP` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/statement/Form5498SaMapper.java`. `UI` =

## 1. What this statement is

Form 5498-SA, HSA, Archer MSA, or Medicare Advantage MSA Information, is filed by the trustee for each health savings
account (HSA), Archer MSA or Medicare Advantage (MA) MSA, reporting contributions, rollovers and the year-end value.
"Note: Don't attach Form 5498-SA to your income tax return. Instead, keep it for your records" (Instructions for
Participant). It is due to the participant by June 1, 2026 for 2025 (filer instructions `i1099sa--2025.pdf` p. 3-4) —
often after the return is filed.

Form copy: the only one on disk is `se-statements/f5498sa.pdf` (**Rev. December 2026**; not in `docs\IRS-Forms\`), pages
2 (Copy B) and 3 (Instructions for Participant). Its boxes 1-6 are the same as the 2025 boxes defined in the 2025 filer
instructions (`i1099sa--2025.pdf` p. 4: Box 1 "Employee or Self-Employed Person's Archer MSA Contributions", Box 2
"Total Contributions Made in 2025", Box 3, Box 4 "Rollover Contributions", Box 5 "Fair Market Value", Box 6
"Checkbox"), so the captions below are the 2025 ones.

Where the figures are used on the 2025 return:

- **HSA** — Form 8889 line 2: "Include on line 2 only those amounts you, or others on your behalf, contributed to your
  HSA for 2025 … Do not include employer contributions (see line 9) or amounts rolled over from another HSA or Archer
  MSA … Also, do not include any qualified HSA funding distributions (see line 10)" (`i8889.pdf` p. 4). Box 2 includes
  employer contributions and funding distributions and "is provided for IRS use only" (`f5498sa.pdf` p. 3), so it is
  **not** line 2 as such.
- **Archer MSA** — box 1 "Shows contributions you made … to your Archer MSA. You may be able to deduct this amount"
  (`f5498sa.pdf` p. 3) → Form 8853 line 2 ("Include on line 2 contributions you made to your Archer MSA in 2025",
  `i8853.pdf` p. 3).
- **MA MSA** — contributions by the SSA "aren't includible in your gross income nor are they deductible"
  (`f5498sa.pdf` p. 3).

**Whose statement it is.** "PARTICIPANT'S TIN", `participantTIN` (`MAP:115`, falls back to `recipientTIN` `:116`); the
screen mirrors it into `recipientTIN` (`UI:280`), seeded with the person tab's SSN on a new entry
(`us-tax-ui/src/app/service/statement-entry-state.service.ts:28-34`). Family check `StatementSsnRules.java:95`.

**Person filter (the D-1a-1 question).** Not applicable: **no compute path reads any Form 5498-SA key** (grep of
`microservices\` finds the mapper, extraction `GenericFieldMapper.java:633-635`, the SSN rule and the catalog). Confirmed
in `new_knowledge\forms\8889.md` ("Form 5498-SA — stored only; line 2 is typed") and `new_knowledge\forms\8853.md`
("Form 5498-SA — not read").

**Storage.** Entity `Se5498Sa` / table `se_form_5498_sa` (`MAP:96-139`). UI `syncPdfToForm` `UI:263-298`
(`parseAmountAndNotes`; no box is negative, D-ui-1 not relevant). Every replica cell has a mapper key.

## 3. Checkboxes and codes

- **Box 6** — HSA, Archer MSA, MA MSA: kind of account; stored only.

## 4. Questions the app asks with this statement

None on the 5498-SA screen. HSA contributions are typed on the HSA form (Form 8889 line 2 "HSA contributions you made",
`contributionsMadeLine2`; employer contributions come from W-2 box 12 code W), Archer MSA contributions on the Archer MSA
form (Form 8853). See `new_knowledge\forms\8889.md`, `8853.md`.

## 5. Differences from the IRS instructions

None new. Not reading Form 5498-SA follows the IRS ("keep it for your records"; box 2 and 3 "for IRS use only"); how
the typed figures are used is reviewed under Form 8889 / 8853 (D-8889-1 … D-8889-10 in `new_knowledge\forms\8889.md`).
There is no cross-check between box 1 / box 5 and the typed Archer MSA contribution / year-end values — a usability gap,
not a rule deviation.
