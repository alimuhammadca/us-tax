`MAP` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/statement/Form5498Mapper.java`. `UI-5498` =

## 1. What this statement is

Form 5498, IRA Contribution Information (2025 — `docs/IRS-Forms/f5498.pdf`), is filed by the IRA trustee or issuer for
each IRA it maintained in 2025, reporting contributions, rollovers, conversions, recharacterizations, the December 31
value and the next year's RMD. It is **not filed with the return**; the participant keeps it ("You should keep copies of
your income tax return, and Forms W-2, 8606, and 5498", `p590a.pdf p. 53`; `i8606.pdf p. 8` lists Forms 5498 among the
records that prove basis). Timing: the December 31 value and RMD statement is due to the participant by February 2,
2026, but the contribution information "must be provided by June 1, 2026" (`i1099r--2025.pdf p. 24`) — often after the
return is filed, so the return's figures normally come from the filer's own records.

IRS instructions used: "Instructions for Participant" on the form copy (`f5498.pdf p. 4`); filer instructions
`i1099r--2025.pdf p. 20-26` (Specific Instructions for Form 5498; boxes `p. 24-26`).

**Whose statement it is.** "PARTICIPANT'S TIN", `participantTIN` (`MAP:277`); family check by
`StatementSsnRules.java:94` (`STATEMENT_DOES_NOT_BELONG_TO_FAMILY`, `TRCS:13891-13913`). The mapper lists every 5498 of
the account (`MAP:232-241`).

**Person filter (the D-1a-1 question).** Not applicable: **no compute path reads any Form 5498 key** — a grep of every
saved key outside the mapper / extraction finds only the SSN rule (`StatementSsnRules.java:94`) and TIN validation
(`TinValidator.java`). A dependent's 5498 therefore cannot leak onto a parent's return.

**Storage.** Entity `SeForm5498` / table `se_form_5498`, one row per statement (`MAP:259-325`).

**UI.** Replica view (`UI-5498:1274`); `syncPdfToForm` (`UI-5498:1521-1569`) copies every replica box into the model
(`UI-5498:1278`). Every model key has a mapper column and every mapper key is on the screen. Amounts are converted with
`parseFloat` (D-5498-1). Upload extraction reads the form by position (`FORM_5498`,
`us-tax-be/.../GenericFieldMapper.java:1201-1286`; `postProcess5498` `:3577`).

## 3. Checkboxes and codes

- **Box 7** — IRA, SEP, SIMPLE, Roth IRA: kind of IRA; stored only.
- **Box 11** — RMD for 2026 required; stored only.
- **Box 13c** (`f5498.pdf p. 4`): a disaster / combat-zone postponement code (e.g. FD for a federally designated
  disaster; EO13239, EO12744, PL115-97 … for combat zones), **PO** (rollover of a qualified plan loan offset — pairs with
  a 1099-R code M), **SC** (self-certified late rollover). None is read.
- **Box 14b**: QR (qualified reservist), DD (qualified disaster), BA (birth or adoption), EP (emergency personal
  expense), DA (domestic abuse victim), TI (terminally ill). None is read; the matching repayment worksheets are not
  supported (D-4b-6).
- **Box 15b**: A–H asset types (A stock not readily tradable … H more than two types). Informational.

## 4. Questions the app asks with this statement

None on the 5498 screen, and no other screen reads it. The figures the return needs are typed where they are used:
Income adjustments (IRA deduction, traditional / Roth contributions, prior-year excess), IRA income (year-end value,
prior-year balance for the RMD, rollovers, conversions), Form 8880 (contributions). See `new_knowledge\10.md`,
`4b.md`, `forms\8606.md`.

## 5. Differences from the IRS instructions

Not reading Form 5498 is not itself a deviation: the IRS does not require the return to use it, and the contribution
copy often arrives after filing (`i1099r--2025.pdf p. 24`). The figures are retyped, and errors in those typed figures
are already recorded (D-10-6 deduction not compared with contributions; D-8606-2 / -3 / -4 basis and year-end value).
One observation: the screen invites the user to enter boxes 1, 5 and 10 that the app then ignores, with no pointer to
where those figures must be entered — a usability gap, not an IRS deviation.

New:

**D-5498-1 — amounts typed with a thousands separator or a "$" are saved wrong.**
`syncPdfToForm` converts every amount with `parseFloat` (`UI-5498:1546-1567`): "5,000" is saved as 5, "$5,000" as
`NaN` (sent as null). Other statement screens use `parseAmountAndNotes` (e.g. `form-1099-r.component.ts:280`). The name
is also rebuilt lossily (middle names dropped, suffix placed before the last name: `UI-5498:1488`, `:1535-1537`).
**Cosmetic** today (no compute reads Form 5498) but the stored record is wrong. Severity **low**.
