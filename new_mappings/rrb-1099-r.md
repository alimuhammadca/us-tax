# Form RRB-1099-R (`rrb-1099-r`) — every box and where it goes

Paths are relative to `C:\us-tax`. `TRCS` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/TaxReturnComputeService.java`.
`MAP` = `us-tax-be/src/main/java/com/ustax/microservices/statement/FormRrb1099RMapper.java`. `UI-RRB` =
`us-tax-ui/src/app/forms/form-rrb-1099-r.component.ts`. Line-level analysis: `new_knowledge\5a.md`, `5b.md`; their ids
are reused.

## 1. What this statement is

Form RRB-1099-R, Annuities or Pensions by the Railroad Retirement Board, is issued by the RRB (not the IRS) for the
non-social-security-equivalent part of tier 1 (NSSEB), tier 2, vested dual benefits and supplemental annuities. The
social-security-equivalent part is on Form RRB-1099 (line 6a, a separate statement).

Sources: the form copy `docs/IRS-Forms/1099rrb.pdf` (an RRB sample dated **2016** — the only copy in the source set;
boxes 1-12 with box 10 "Rate of Tax", box 11 "Country", box 12 "Medicare Premium Total"); its Copy B legend: "Report
this income on your federal tax return. If this form shows federal income tax withheld in box 9 attach this copy to your
return." The 1040 instructions say only "If you received a Form RRB-1099-R, see Pub. 575 to find out how to report your
benefits" (`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 28`); Pub. 575 is not in `docs/books`. Pub. 525 says tier 1 above the social security
equivalent, tier 2 and vested dual benefits "are treated as pension or annuity income and are taxable under the rules
explained in Pub. 575" (`p525.pdf p. 35`) — i.e. lines 5a / 5b. Line 25b: "railroad retirement benefits" withholding
(`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 39`).

The app's live replica follows a **newer layout than the 2016 copy**: boxes 1-9 the same, box 10 = Medicare premium total,
no rate-of-tax or country box (`UI-RRB:320-390`; extraction grid `GenericFieldMapper.java:2319-2343`). That layout could
not be checked against an IRS-source copy (none in the source set) — **unverified**.

**Whose statement it is.** Box 2, "Recipient's identification number", saved as `recipientIdNumber` (`MAP:106`). The
new-entry seed pre-fills `recipientTIN` / `employeeSSN` / … but not `recipientIdNumber`
(`us-tax-ui/src/app/service/statement-entry-state.service.ts:28-34`), so an RRB-1099-R created on a spouse or dependent
tab carries no owner until box 2 is typed (D-5a-1). RRB forms are deliberately left out of the family SSN check
(`StatementSsnRules.java:110-111`; reason given at `:31-33`: box 2 "not always an SSN") — see D-rrb-1099-r-1.

**Person filter.** Lines 5a / 5b: `belongsToPersonRrb1099R` (`TRCS:35933-35950`) — a filled box 2 must equal the
taxpayer's / spouse's SSN (digits only, `normalizeSsn` `TRCS:53713-53719`), a blank box 2 goes to the taxpayer (D-5a-1).
This runs on every filing status. Line 25b: filtered only on MFS / dependent's-own returns (`TRCS:49797`,
`filterWithholdingEntriesForScopedFiler` `:51808-51823`, which reads `recipientIdNumber` `:51789`) — a dependent's
withholding is credited on the parent's return (same mechanism as **D-1099-int-1**, extended here). MFS community
property: box 7 and box 9 split (`CommunityProperty.splitRrb1099R`, `CommunityProperty.java:574-581`).

**Storage.** Entity `SeRrb1099R` / table `se_rrb_1099_r`, one row per statement, list = whole household (`MAP:69-78`).

**UI.** Replica view only (`_pdfView = true`, `UI-RRB:765`); `syncPdfToForm` (`UI-RRB:890-912`) copies the replica into
the model (`UI-RRB:772-797`). Every live box has a mapper column. The model also carries `payerFederalId`,
`recipientNameAddress`, `rateOfTax`, `country` (`UI-RRB:776-795`) with **no mapper column** — they are bound only in the
old replica hidden behind `*ngIf="false"` (`UI-RRB:243-318`), so no user can type them today; nothing is lost.

## 3. Checkboxes and codes

Only CORRECTED and DUPLICATE (stored only). Box 1's payee code identifies the payee to the RRB and does not route
anything in the app or on the return.

## 4. Questions the app asks with this statement

On the Pension / annuity income form: "received RRB-1099-R" / "uploaded RRB-1099-R" (blocking
`MISSING_UPLOADED_RRB_1099_R`, `TRCS:22878-22886`), the Simplified Method / General Rule inputs (age, starting date,
cost — box 3 is read directly when a method runs), rollover (RRB is added back after the rollover subtraction — not
rollover-eligible), PSO. See `new_knowledge\5a.md`, `5b.md`.

## 5. Differences from the IRS instructions

Reused: **D-5a-1** (blank box 2 → taxpayer of every computed return; seed does not pre-fill box 2), **D-1099-int-1**
(withholding of another household member credited; extended to RRB-1099-R box 9), D-5b-6 / D-5b-8 / D-5b-10 when a
basis-recovery method is used.

New:

**D-rrb-1099-r-1 — an RRB-1099-R whose box 2 is not the taxpayer's or spouse's SSN silently drops out of lines 5a / 5b,
while its box 9 withholding is still credited.**
IRS: the annuity is the recipient's pension income (`p525.pdf p. 35`) and the withholding goes with it on line 25b
(`i1040gi_ty2025.pdf p. 39`).
Code: RRB forms are excluded from the family SSN check (`StatementSsnRules.java:110-111`) on the ground that box 2 is
"not always an SSN" (`:31-33`), yet `belongsToPersonRrb1099R` treats a filled box 2 as an SSN and keeps the statement
only when the digits equal the taxpayer's or spouse's SSN (`TRCS:35939-35945`). A box 2 typed with a claim number, a
masked SSN ("XXX-XX-1234") or a typo matches nobody: the income is skipped on lines 5a / 5b, while
`filterWithholdingEntriesForScopedFiler` passes the whole list unchanged on single / joint / HOH / QSS returns
(`TRCS:51810-51813`), so box 9 is credited on line 25b. No flag fires. Effect: **silent understatement** of income with
the withholding still claimed. Severity **medium** (the RRB-1099-R normally shows the recipient's SSN; the mismatch needs
a typing or masking difference).
