The second check is self-consistency — feed the resulting MAGI back through 8.5% and the contribution amount must reproduce the deduction. Both beat comparing a headline figure, and both identify *which* answer is wrong rather than merely that two differ. Pins: `Sc00387SqaScenarioTest`.
