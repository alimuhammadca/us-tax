**★ The EIC is READ FROM A TABLE, not computed from a formula.** The instructions say to look the amount up in $50 brackets, so the printed value governs and the phaseout formula is only a description of it. Pin controls on the **table's own bracket boundaries** (2025: 23,300–23,350 pays the full 4,328; 23,350–23,400 pays 4,324) rather than on a formula result that may agree by coincidence at one midpoint. sc_00383's AGI landed in an exact printed row — `40,000 40,050 | 0 1,663 3,640 4,534` (p.55) — so no judgement was involved at all.

**★ §152(c)(4) is not an AGI contest when a parent is in play.** Under §152(c)(4)(C) a non-parent is barred **outright** whenever a parent can and does claim the child; the AGI comparison only opens if **no** parent claims, and then the non-parent additionally needs an AGI above the highest parental AGI. A scenario that dangles a higher-AGI grandparent is testing whether you apply the priority rule or reach for the tiebreaker arithmetic — the higher AGI is a decoy.
