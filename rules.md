


**★ Schedule 3 line 6l takes a negative Form 8978 line 14 as a POSITIVE amount.** 6l ("Amount on Form 8978, line 14") sits under *6 Other nonrefundable credits*, so a net DECREASE in tax from a BBA push-out is entered there as a credit, not as a signed adjustment — `roundMoney(amt.abs())` — and line 16 is left untouched. A positive line 14 goes the other way, onto line 16 box 3 as "Form 8978". ★ **Both wrong routings produce a complete, plausible return** that differs only on the tested line, so when a scenario supplies only one sign, test the mirror. Pins: `Sc00386SqaScenarioTest`.

**★ A pushed-out partnership adjustment is recomputed at the REVIEWED YEAR's rates, and is a tax rather than income.** §6226 does not re-open the reviewed year: the partner recomputes what that year's tax would have been, and pays the delta on the current return. Applying the current year's brackets to a prior-year adjustment is the natural mistake and yields a different number (2023 Single: 10% to 11,000, 12% to 44,725, 22% to 95,375, 24% to 182,100). The adjusted income never enters the current year's AGI — **AGI is the control** that tells the two readings apart.

**★ The line-16 box-3 write-ins all sit behind a parent gate, and the failure is silent.** `hasForm8978Adjustment`, `hasForm8621PficTax`, and the 962/ECR/965INC equivalents each gate their own amount; seed the amount without the gate and line 16 quietly returns the plain regular tax with no flag — often with no balance due at all, so the return looks finished. Two consecutive scenarios (385, 386) each cost a run to this. [[feedback_child_list_gated_by_parent_boolean]]
