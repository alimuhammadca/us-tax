


**★ Never use Playwright's `check()` on a framework-CONTROLLED checkbox.** `check()` clicks and then *verifies* the box reads checked, retrying until timeout. Against `<input [checked]="isSelected(...)" (change)="toggle(...)">` — where the DOM state is owned by Angular and the handler navigates — it retries against a re-rendered node and burns the whole test timeout, reporting only "Test timeout exceeded". Click the **label/card** instead: it forwards the click and is the real user path. The statements picker (`.selection-card`) is exactly this shape. ★ The tell is in the call log: *"click action done → navigations have finished"* followed by a hang means the click worked and the **verification** is what is looping. Diagnosed 2026-09-21 after this read as a flake for months.

**★ After changing a computed VALUE, sweep the tests by value — not just by field name.** The EIC `FLOOR → HALF_UP` fix needed 8 pin corrections across 5 specs. Grepping `earnedIncomeCredit` found six and missed two, because the survivors were a **rendered UI-text** pin (`toHaveText(/\$307\.00/)`) and a **derived delta** (`$649 − $307 = $342`) — neither mentions the field. Also sweep stale *comments*: one still read `$306`, a generation older than the assertion beside it. And verify each candidate against the primary source before touching it — `toBe(1811)` looked like a sibling but was genuinely unaffected (exact 1811.25, floor = round).
