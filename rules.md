


**★ Two different error modes across two attempts is evidence of flakiness, not a defect.** A deterministic bug does not change its symptom between retries. sc-adjacent example: `line1h:135` failed in-suite first with a disabled Save button and then with `Unable to create 1099-r statement entry via API` - different layers entirely. Read BOTH attempts' errors from the JSON reporter before deciding, and weigh reproducibility: 0-in-6 failures in isolation against ~1-in-4 under full-suite load means contention, not logic. ★ **Do not patch a flake you cannot make fail** - contrast `statement-recipient-ssn-autofill`, where the `check()` call log named the cause exactly and a real fix followed. An unreproducible flake belongs in the record as open, not behind a speculative wait that will look like a fix and hide the next symptom.
